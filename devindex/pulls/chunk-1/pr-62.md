Resolves #60

The Pages site now ships `dist/development` beside `dist/production`, and each build serves the app from its own entry under the `/devindex/` mount.

A build's entry (`dist/<env>/apps/devindex/index.html`) sits two levels below its workers. A worker resolves `basePath: '../../../../'` against its own script, so under a mount the index fetch climbs past the site; at an origin root it is clamped, which is why the build works locally. Each build's entry now gets what the site root's entry already had:
- a `<base>` at its build directory (`../../`);
- that build's `neo-config.json` with `basePath: '../../'`, `workerBasePath: './'` and `isGitHubPages`.

`assembleSite()` now:
- takes the workspace root;
- ships each build in `ENTRIES` whole;
- refuses a workspace missing any of them;
- lists the entries in `deploy-receipt.json`.

`entryPage()` generalizes the former `rootEntry()` by its base. The site root stays production's entry.

dist/esm and dev mode moved to #61. They need the bump to `neo.mjs` 13.2.0 (its esm build rewrites the engine imports this 13.1 build leaves in `node_modules`) and neomjs/neo#19430 (a workspace's dev-mode worker resolves the index inside `node_modules/neo.mjs`).

Evidence: L2 (headless Chromium on the assembled site under a mount, plus the assembly spec) → L2 required by #60's ACs. Residual: the live read on neomjs.com after the deploy and the Portal's `dist_dev`/`dist_prod` rows. Residual-Owner: #61, which stays open past this merge.
