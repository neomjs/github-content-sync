Resolves #644

The Create door's foot fade now follows the door's scroll state instead of the door's existence: it paints while the door can still scroll down and leaves at the end; a door that holds everything shows none. Stylesheet only, as one shared idiom: the `scroll-fade` mixin (`resources/scss/src/apps/agentos/shared/_scroll-fade.scss`, written here and landed first by #730 with identical bytes, so this diff only includes it) makes the scrolling element a `scroll-state` container and gates its own `::after` on `@container scroll-state(scrollable: bottom)`; the Create door includes it (`@use '../shared/scroll-fade' as *; @include scroll-fade;`), the memory-candidate lists already do with their own panel colour. The fade's look is unchanged; no measurement, no class, no JavaScript. Measured first, as the ticket asked: in Playwright's Chromium 156 the container's own `::after` does query its scroll state (0 for a door that cannot scroll, 1 at the top of an overflowing door, 0 at its end), so the ticket's "move the fade to a child element" caveat does not apply; the installed shell's Electron 43 (Chromium 150) is past the feature's 133 floor.
