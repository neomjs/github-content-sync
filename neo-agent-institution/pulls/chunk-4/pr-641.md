Resolves #640

Wake routes is one of the four panes the rail parks (`Container#getPreservedItemIds`), and `getWakeRoutesPane()` still resolved through `getReference`. That reference answers only the projected tree. So once the pane was parked, two things missed it:
- `loadWakeRoutes`' snapshot push;
- the Reconnect re-drive, `reconnectFleet` calling `getWakeRoutesPane()?.onRefreshClick()`.

The revealed pane then showed the stale snapshot. The accessor now resolves the parked pane by its declared id: `this.vesselPane('wakeRoutes') || Neo.get(this.getPaneDeclaration('wakeRoutes')?.id) || null`. That is #634's rule for Detail and the engine's own (`Workspace#releaseDeclaredPanes`). The JSDoc names the parked phase.

Intake: self-authored carve (same session). Prescription checked: `apps/agentos/view/fleet/cockpit/VesselContainer.mjs#getWakeRoutesPane` owns the concern. Every Wake routes push routes through it, and the component manager is the engine's lookup for declared panes.

Evidence: L2 (real-cockpit unit arms over the dock's own park and reveal operations, red on dev's accessor) → L2 required (the ticket's three unit ACs). Residual: none.
