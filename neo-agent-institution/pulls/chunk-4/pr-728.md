Wires the Brain's additive `cleanupUnresolved` and `failureReason` lifecycle fields (`neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1082`) through the Institution roster projection, the `Start fleet` / `Stop fleet` plans, and the seat card's `#controlStatus` line. Evidence: L3 (unit + visual dark/light golden suites) → L4 required (AC-4 post-merge installed Row 1 walk). Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: #12.
