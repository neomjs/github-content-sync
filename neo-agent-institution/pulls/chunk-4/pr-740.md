Resolves #734

🌿 A slow Fleet can no longer pass for a failed one.

`awaitFleetReady` turned "the Fleet is slow" into "the Fleet failed" after a fixed 15 s, and nothing ever attached the transport afterwards. On 2026-10-19 every boot of the installed FM with seats running took 18–19 s, because recovery re-proves the running seats against a loaded Memory Core. The roster stayed empty while the Fleet itself served. Now the product boots have no deadline while the Fleet child lives. Past 15 s they log `HARNESS_BRAIN_FLEET_STARTING …` once and keep waiting, and when the child answers, the boot settles `up` and the cockpit's existing liveness poll loads the roster. An exited child, a typed plane refusal or an unloadable wire contract still fails the boot. The smoke keeps its 15 s bound. Quit aborts a wait still pending (`fleetReadyAbort`), so teardown never hangs on a Fleet that never answers.

Evidence: L2 (unit, fake child and readiness seam) → L3 required (the installed boot with seats running). Residual: the installed boot attaches the roster at ~18 s. Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#736.
