Resolves #654

One `Start fleet` press now sends its seats in waves of two instead of all at once: `FleetStartPlan.waves` groups the eligible records in roster order, `FleetBatchController.executeStartFleetBatch` sends the next wave once the previous wave's intents answered (settled, refused, or timed out on the adapter's honesty bound), and between waves the chrome line reads `N started · P pending · M excluded` — a seat the batch has not sent yet is `pending`, never `rejected`. A newer batch, a replaced Fleet or a destroyed controller retires the batch between waves with no further intent. `startFleetWaveSize` (default 2, documented on the controller) sets the wave; `1` is one by one. The stop batch keeps its single wave. The burst that left every Claude Desktop seat without Memory Core and Knowledge Base on the first real fleet start is bounded in the cockpit; the admission proof's own limit stays Brain #964.

Evidence: L2 (unit — the pure helpers and the batch loop against a recording bridge) → L4 required (AC-4: an installed fleet start on the next candidate). Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: #12.
