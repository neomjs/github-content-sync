Resolves #733
Part of #732

When a step in `executePlan` fails after `quit` has stopped the Fleet Manager and before the swap `rename` moves the installed bundle, the installer now releases its exclusion, reopens the unchanged bundle it quit (`open -a <installed.path>`) before printing the error, and throws the five-line operator message from Clio's `AC-3` design read (`#733#issuecomment-6286842685`). `STOPPED_UPDATE_STEPS` is the sole classifier, mapping all eight pre-swap failure kinds (the three `beforeMutation` checks: `bundle-users`, `planned-slot`, `continuity-guard`, plus the five post-quit step types: `custody`, `stage`, `verify`, `runtime`, `remove`) to their fixed product-word `Reason:` line with the verbatim step error on the indented line below and their step-specific `Next:` action. Refusals before `quit` keep their existing single-line message and reopen nothing; if reopening fails, that error is reported with the resolved slot path and the installer still exits non-zero.
