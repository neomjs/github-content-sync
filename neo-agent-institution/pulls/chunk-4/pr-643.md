Resolves #618

The fleet button reads the plan it would run: `Start fleet · 4` while anything eligible is down, `Stop fleet · 8` when the up fleet is all that is left, the plain `Start fleet` with its reason on the title when no plan can be computed (the roster unobserved, every runtime source unwired, every row excluded) — never a count the partition did not produce. The label is written on settled rosters only: the liveness layer's one admit path now ends in `onRosterSettled`, which the batch layer overrides; a batch in flight keeps the last words and disables the button, so a press mid-cascade is impossible rather than ignored.

The fleet-wide stop is a two-press in the design read's frame (#618 comment 6074063707): the first press renders the plan where the batch summaries already live — `Stop fleet · 8 seats: Ada, Clio, … · 3 excluded`, the names in the grid's own order, every exclusion's reason on the title — and the chip becomes the second press, `Stop fleet · press again`, the stop icon kept; nothing is sent. The arm lasts until that press (one stop intent per planned seat through the card's own verb, the summary `N stopped · M excluded`), a settled roster whose plan differs (the label returns to its plan, nothing sent), or ten seconds. Start keeps one press.

Six commits on the branch: the plan half (`partitionFleetStop`, `describeFleetButton`, the summary's verb), the two-press batch, the split of the batch layer into `FleetBatchController` (the controller back under the 1000-line bar), this binding, a comment cleanup for the source-comment archaeology guard (ticket and review references out of the durable comments; two older ones in the control spec's header went with them), and — round 1, Emmy's RA-1/RA-2 — the confirmation bound to its Fleet: the arm records the bridge's profile and the roster store at the first press, the liveness layer's roster retirement ends in `onRosterRetired` (the batch layer drops the arm), and the second press re-partitions the roster as it is now before anything is sent — a replaced target, a retired roster, a differing plan or a seat whose own action is pending withdraws the confirmation with nothing sent. The chip keeps its `fm-fleet-start` class: the stylesheet and the journeys name it, and renaming it is churn this PR declines.

Evidence: L3 (unit on the real controller's prototype with the bridge faked; the NL journey over the fixture Fleet wire; one golden per state on the sample roster given lifecycle evidence) → L3 required (AC-1 to AC-4 are labels, a hold, a wire journey and goldens).
