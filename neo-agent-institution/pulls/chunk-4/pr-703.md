Resolves #596

The Activity head now chooses the A2A population it reads: `mailbox` (the viewer's own mailbox, as before), `involves me` or `all A2A`. The two observer modes send the closed selector over the Brain's canonical observer contract, page through its `a2a.continuation`, and open a message row read-only in the mailbox's shared detail. What the feed holds belongs to one admission (`observation.admissionKey`): another viewer, plane, mode or policy retires rows, counts, continuation and the open message, and late answers land nowhere. The owner logic lives in a new `AgentOS.util.ActivityObserver` beside `FleetAdmission`; the controllers only relay.

Evidence: L2 (unit fences under mutation, and the journey rendered in a real browser over the landing fixture's canonical observer answers, in CI) → L3 required for the installed walk on a real plane and a busy population (the ticket's Post-Merge Validation). Residual: the installed journey, Residual-Owner: #490.

## Deltas from ticket
- The ordinary mailbox read stays the default third mode: its first page is also the roster's lane source (`readHeldA2ASnapshot`), so observer-only modes would empty the lane chips. While an observer mode is in view, those chips age from the last mailbox read. Out of scope, noted in defect-note `MESSAGE:0085a9ef-5a6e-4214-a4cc-1f84bd4fbe0c`.
- `all A2A` asks for `memorySharing: 'team'`, so a deployment with a narrower policy answers `clamped` and the note says so.
- An empty-then-refilled store paints stale pooled rows when the refill beats the list's update (reproduced on the merge-base). A switch or admission change therefore retires the feed's state but replaces its rows in one Store transaction, and a cold stream shows none of them meanwhile. The engine race is out of scope, noted in defect-note `MESSAGE:b46a3373-a4cc-4968-bacd-453b84b565ab`.
- `TargetBinding.clearActivity` now owns every activity retirement (profile, mode, admission) and resets the older-page fences, so `activityHistoryProfileId` is gone.
- A new population reads from its newest row; message rows take the pointer in observer modes. Rows open by pointer only, since the list's rows are not focusable.
