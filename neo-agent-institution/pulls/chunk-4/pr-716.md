Resolves #476

The Agent Detail's Thought stream pane reads the shown seat's newest public turn summaries through the Fleet's `fleetRecentTurns` verb (neomjs/neo-agent-brain#792): a Store-bound list of `RecentTurn` records, newest first — the turn's age and the word for a weaker summary over the derived line — with the pill aged from the read's `capturedAt`, and every no-row state in words: a wired empty page, a plane that clamps peer turns, the producer's own `unavailable` with its detail, a read the bridge did not answer, a bridge without the verb (the unobserved pill, its title naming the verb), a seat the roster names no identity for. The read is the cockpit's one owned reader (`util/RecentTurnsRead.mjs`, the operator inbox's shape over the open-work read's laws: a generation fence, in-flight accounting on the liveness cadence, typed unavailable envelopes, profile retirement through `TargetBinding`) plus a seat binding: one read when a seat is first shown, one per cadence pass (60 s) while it stays shown, nothing without a seat; the answer lands on the detail as one reactive config, and the pane's body (`detail/ThoughtStreamBodyContainer.mjs`, the repo pane's shape) renders it only for the seat it was asked for. The intake record and the sharpening it settled are in the ticket body's `## Intake` section.
