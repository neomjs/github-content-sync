Resolves #475

A host-file effect whose receipt was interrupted and whose result the host no longer shows stays `reconcile-required` through every re-check, and the card offered `re-check` alone. The card now remembers the operator's own re-check of such a row and offers `start a fresh run` as its quiet second chip: the first press says what a fresh run leaves behind, the second sends `setupEvaluate({fresh: true})`, the broker binds a new record for the same target, and the lede names the run it retired, which stays on disk as history. A read that refuses with no live row left offers the same exit beside its consent line. The witness row keeps the Brain's exits. The preset choices under Other choices moved into their own `PresetCards` class: the door had crossed the apps file-size bar (1041 lines; 981 now).

Evidence: L3 (the pinned Brain's own answers on a scripted host — the card's unit arms, the e2e through the page shell, the visual goldens) → L3 required (AC-1 … AC-4). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: #351.
