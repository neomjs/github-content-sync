Resolves #749

The roster card reads each word the Fleet writes with the control it obliges, instead of rejecting every word outside `running` / `stopped` / cleanup-owing `failed` as a contradiction: `stopping` stays up with a pending-stop control, a cleanup-free `failed` and an `unavailable` seat are off and startable with the Fleet's own reason, the read-only `exited` is off and named as the exit it was; `unknown` and any word outside `neo-agent-brain/fleet-contract` stay `invalid`, and the CARD-word pass-through is gone. The fleet start plans a failed or unavailable seat with `re-checks: <reason>` and prints that reason once. The Brain pin moves to the `dev` head `9668697b`, which carries #1201's exported words (neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1202).

Evidence: L2 (unit arms on the pure mapping and the plan, red first at the head) → L2 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-3); AC-4 needs L3 (captures on the smoke profile) and AC-5 the installed FM. Residual: AC-4, AC-5, Residual-Owner: #732.
