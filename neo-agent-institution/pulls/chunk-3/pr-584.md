Resolves #573

An installed shell can now move its seats from the root it adopted to the Brain's default, `~/.neo-ai/agents`, once and for the whole installation.
- **Consent** asks the Brain for a dry run of `moveSeatHomes` against this installation's registry and roots, and persists only the inputs of the plan it was shown (`seat-root-move.json`). A refused consent names its code beside its sentence, for the System view in #582.
- **The boot** settles a consented move before the first-launch choice and before the Fleet child. It rules out every other writer, re-plans, runs the Brain's one-shot move, reads the registry back, and only then commits by writing the root record with origin `moved`. The old folders are then archived under the old root.
- **The declared Brain pin moves** to Brain `dev` `a8dd1ae4` (neomjs/neo-agent-brain#901), where a moved seat starts at its new root with its path pins re-derived.

Evidence: L2 (unit specs over real temp roots with a scripted Brain, plus a composed arm that runs the real Brain's plan, move and restore in a real Brain root) → L2 required (AC-2 to AC-7 name units and the composed arm). Residual: the installed move on the operator's machine, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-brain#571.
