Resolves #436

An instance switch now retires the operator's own inbox and identity, so the south pane never shows one instance's mail under another instance's name, and Reconnect re-reads both. This is #181's rule applied to the one pane it did not cover. FM v1 row 2's pre-sitting audit found it ([#335](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-institution/issues/335#issuecomment-5951975107)).

- **`TargetBinding.retireOperatorMailbox`** sits beside the roster, activity and graph-scene retires. When the bridge in hand names another profile than the one that answered the identity:
  - the identity, the window, the posture and the last send's outcome leave together;
  - the read fence advances;
  - the pane reads `unobserved`.
- **The cockpit `Controller`** stamps `operatorProfileId` when an identity lands. Both operator loads retire first, and an identity answer lands only while the profile that asked is still the bridge in hand.
  - `loadOperatorIdentity` answers `'bound'`, `'held'` or `null`. `null` covers nothing landing: no verb, a throw, a refusal, or a stale profile.
  - A send that settles after a switch keeps its result for the caller, but its outcome never lands in the new pane and the new inbox is not re-read.
  - One `bridgeProfileId` getter is the origin every settlement compares against.
- **A `reconnectFleet` override on `Controller`** re-drives both after the inherited ten surfaces, which keeps the base `LivenessController` unaware of a subclass's reads.
  - An identity that binds anew reads its own first window: `OperatorContainer#afterSetRecord` fires on `null` → record.
  - A `'held'` identity fires nothing under `Neo.isEqual`, so the override reads its first window.
  - A stale or failed answer reads nothing, even from an older Reconnect that settles after the switch.

Evidence: L2 (unit, red-first; visual). Residual: none. Row 2's sitting (step 5 of its script) is the installed witness of the rule as a whole.
