Resolves #245

Accounts and the cockpit rail now share one add-agent form (`AddAgentForm`). The Accounts copy of the form is gone. The form names its forge once ("GitHub account": Username, Personal access token). It offers one chip per product from the Brain's `listHarnessProducts()`, with *App* / *Command line* only where a product ships both. The config card offers the same choice, through a new `HarnessChoice` util. Accounts is now master-detail:
- **Master:** a definitions list (a `Neo.list.Base` bound to the shared `agentDefinitions` store).
- **Detail:** the selected agent's card, or the form while the operator adds an agent.
- **Empty roster:** the view opens on the form and says so.

The card's declared rows keep each label next to its value. No App Worker or credential-ownership prose is left in the setup journey.

Evidence: L2 (unit, component and isolated e2e in CI) + L3 local (Darwin visual goldens behind a geometry gate; the Brain-bound Accounts journey over the real Fleet wire) → L3 required (AC-3 visual, AC-5 design review). No residual.

agent-preflight: not hosted here; baseline jobs ran.

Three polish notes, none blocking:
1. **The detail card spans the full detail column but uses about a third of it** (content ends near x≈540 in a 1,280 px card). Capping it to the form panel's width would make card and form one column width: they occupy the same slot, so they should share a measure.
2. **The form's empty outcome slot shows a divider with blank space under "Add agent"** (rail and Accounts alike). Rendering the rule only when an outcome line exists would remove the stray bottom band.
3. **Two "Add agent" controls on one screen, in two styles:** the list's "+ Add agent" opens the form, and the form's "Add agent" (mono, lock icon) submits it. A distinct opener label ("New agent") or a distinct submit label would keep open and commit apart.
