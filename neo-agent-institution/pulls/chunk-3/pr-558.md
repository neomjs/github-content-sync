Resolves #557

🌿 Home's first line counts the merges that wait for the operator and says why the operator's questions cannot be counted yet, so what waits for the operator can no longer drown in the team's history.

Home leads with the operator's own line, above everything.

- **Merges.** The merge half reads the queue the cockpit already fills (#483's `FleetAwaitingMerge`, from the open-work read's `awaitingMerge`), so a PR merged anywhere leaves the count without any message. The merge count is a button: one click opens the fleet head's merge queue, with no new view.
- **Questions.** The questions half is the source's own `questions` block, which `OpenWorkRead.questions` projects beside `openWork`. Today's wire carries none, so it reads `unsupported` with the source's reason, `questions are not listed yet`. When Brain #860 deploys and the read lists a person's open Tasks, the line follows without an edit to the view.
- **What the line may claim.** "nothing waits for you" needs both axes complete and observed zero, and a stale observation keeps its age (`nothing waits for you as of 12m ago`). A partial queue's zero reads `your merges could not all be read`, and a partial count reads `at least 5 merges wait for you · some could not be read`, its reason in the title either way. Any axis with something to say lights the line, so a silent merge read never hides known questions.
- **Silence.** A missing verb, a failed read, or a packaged shell without a plane earns no pixels.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms over the pure line, the source projection, the queue opener and Home's provider wiring; a visual arm in the real app that lands each state, then clicks the merge count). #551 keeps AC-2/3/4 behind Brain #860 and the installed AC-6 (row 4's walk, #490).
