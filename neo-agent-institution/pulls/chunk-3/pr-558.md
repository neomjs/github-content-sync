Resolves #557

🌿 Home's first line counts the merges that wait for the operator and says why the operator's questions cannot be counted yet, so what waits for the operator can no longer drown in the team's history.

Home leads with the operator's own line, above everything. The merge half reads the queue the cockpit already fills (#483's `FleetAwaitingMerge`, from the open-work read's `awaitingMerge`), so a PR merged anywhere leaves the count without any message. The questions half has no producer until Brain #859 lists a person's open Tasks. Until then it reads `your questions are not listed yet`, with its reason in the title, never a 0. A read that fails reads `could not be read` instead. "nothing waits for you" is said only when both halves answered zero, and a stale queue reads `5 merges as of 12m ago`.

A missing verb or a failed read earns no pixels, which is the merge button's rule (`OpenWorkRead.unavailable`'s silent coverages). Only the producer's own `unavailable` is named. The line is hidden for a packaged shell without a plane, and the Home field keeps its marks off it.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms over the pure line and Home's provider wiring; a visual arm in the real app with a landed open-work answer). #557 carries no installed check; #551 keeps its AC-6 (row 4's installed walk, #490), and its AC-2/3/4 behind Brain #859.
