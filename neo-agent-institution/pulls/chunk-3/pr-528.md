Resolves #509

🌿 The Observatory's side panel can no longer hold a section to a box: the open section takes the panel's height, a collapsed one still says what it holds, and no title is cut.

This delivers parts 1–3 of #509 as the design seat read and approved them ([5973633722](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-institution/issues/509#issuecomment-5973633722)). Part 4, the remembered splitter, is #527.

- **One section open at a time.** View sits first and static, so the lens controls never move. Below it come Team, Nodes and Selected. The open one takes the panel's remaining height. A collapsed one keeps its head and its fact (`Team · 5 of 6`, the selected node on one line). Heads are ghost buttons with a caret and `aria-expanded`.
- **The defaults follow the read.** Team opens when the read lists peers, otherwise Nodes. Selecting a node opens Selected, unless the viewer is browsing Nodes; then the collapsed Selected head follows the keys live.
- **Nothing reads in a box.** The 132 px Team clamp is gone (All lists every peer). Node titles wrap whole, and the selected node's title wraps.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms and visual goldens on the fixture cockpit at this head) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-4). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#485

## Deltas from ticket

- Not the engine accordion: section heads are buttons and sections flex, because the panel's sections are heterogeneous containers. The intake comment [5971869821](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-institution/issues/509#issuecomment-5971869821) records why, and the design read accepted it.
- "Show all" is the existing All toggle.
- The default follows the read (Team with peers, otherwise Nodes), not the last used section (decision 1), so nothing persists.
- Titles wrap whole rather than to two lines. At this width a 90-character title outgrows two lines, and the ticket's own "no ellipsis" wins over the two-line estimate. The Fix line is amended to match.
- Part 4 moved to #527 (decision 4). #509's Fix and AC-3/AC-4 now point there.
