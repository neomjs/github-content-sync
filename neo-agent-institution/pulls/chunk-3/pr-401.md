Resolves #399

Both screenshot configs now compare at a per-pixel threshold of 0.03, declared once in `test/playwright/screenshotThreshold.mjs`. Under Playwright's 0.2 default a dark card on a dark page counts as unchanged, so a geometry change on the cockpit's surfaces could pass its golden. Each config keeps its own pixel budget.
