Resolves #554

When the compose selection contracts, `RecipientChipList#createItems` destroys each trimmed chip. Its stored vnode kept naming the chip until the row's own update landed, so a cockpit update walking the row in that window threw `util.VNode.getVnode: Component not found` (`OperatorComposeControlsNL`'s intermittent failure). The trim now destroys with `destroy(true, true)`: the engine's `VNodeUtil.unlinkRetiredReferences` keeps the chip's node, unnamed, until the update removes it. It runs silently because `createItems` sends that update itself.

Evidence: L2 (unit: the row's stored vnode after a real selection contract through the form) + L3 (the full NL battery on the dev-server build) → L4 not required: the change is one engine call inside the row, with no installed-shell surface of its own.
