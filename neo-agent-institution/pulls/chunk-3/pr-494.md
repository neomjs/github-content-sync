This is the fixture half of row 2's walkthrough. `CockpitStateWalkthroughNL.spec.mjs` provokes six states in turn on one mounted cockpit against a real Fleet server: cold, unreachable, live, stale, one source failing and degraded. In each state it:
- reads every census surface the page carries: the banner, the switcher, the roster and the activity feed;
- asserts the words against the state's census cell;
- attaches them as that state's receipt.
