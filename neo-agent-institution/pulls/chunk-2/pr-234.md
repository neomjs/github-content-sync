Resolves #230

The cockpit gains an "Observatory" pane beside "Route graph": the Golden Path route as a 3D scene the canvas worker draws with WebGL2 — the items on a helix by rank, their citations on rings around them, the route as a beaded line in the signal; a current route in colour, a withheld one dim, any other currency a cleared surface. A frame is owed only to a change (a scene, the surface, the theme, the camera): idle, the worker draws nothing. Drag orbits, the wheel zooms, the head names the node under the pointer and shows the gesture hint otherwise. The pure `ObservatorySceneLayout` declares the scene shape a Brain feed (neomjs/neo-agent-brain#533) lands in through the one `setScene` entry; today it derives the scene from the Golden Path envelope and nothing else. The FM ink mirror the two canvas-worker renderers share moves into `fmPalette.mjs`.

Change class: capability (`feat`). agent-preflight: not hosted here; the baseline jobs run.

Evidence: L2 (unit: the layout spec and the shipped-document arms; NL e2e: the scene lands, drag orbits, wheel zooms, idle draws nothing, withheld keeps the scene, degraded clears it; visual: five goldens across both skins) → L3 required (AC-5: the operator orbits the real route in the team shell, after #228). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: #10.

## Deltas from ticket
- The gesture hint (`drag orbits · wheel zooms`) stands in the head's hover slot while no node is under the pointer — the pane's one affordance for a first-time viewer.
- The camera fits the scene to the surface's tighter axis on every new scene and resize until the pointer takes it (`camera.touched`); the ticket named no framing rule.
- `getStats()` on the renderer and `readStats()` on the host are the specs' read; no DOM probe.
- The route's ribbon is beads — round points sampled along each leg — rather than instanced quads: enough for a route of items, and the multiscale leaf (H3-e) revisits the ribbon at scale.

### 🔬 Depth Floor

**Challenge — the declared scene shape cannot express a partial answer, and this is the coupling I flagged before reviewing.** `ObservatorySceneLayout` returns `{currency, empty, nodes, edges, route}` and documents that "a Brain scene feed lands in the same shape". But a *bounded neighbourhood* feed — which is what #533 is specified to deliver — is budget-capped by definition, and the whole point of its `completeness: 'complete' | 'truncated'` field is that a truncated scene must not render as though it were the whole graph. As declared, this shape has nowhere to put that: a scene cut at `maxEdges: 200` and a scene containing every edge produce **pixel-identical output**, and the pane's currency line above the canvas says nothing about it, because currency is about the *route* being current/withheld/degraded, not about the neighbourhood being partial.

That is the silent-wrong-answer shape I have been filing tickets about all session — a viewer cannot distinguish "this is the graph" from "this is a budget cut of the graph" — and it is much cheaper to fix now, while the only producer is a total one, than after #533 lands and a second producer exists. My recommendation, and I will carry it: add `completeness` (and a `counts` object, which the e2e arm already reads out of `getStats()` anyway) to the declared shape, defaulting to `'complete'` in `fromGoldenPath` and `empty()`. Then the pane can render a "partial" affordance, and #533's feed has a slot to land in rather than a field to add under pressure. **I will take that in #533 rather than ask you to re-head this** — the field is inert until my feed exists, and the pane is what the operator wants today.
