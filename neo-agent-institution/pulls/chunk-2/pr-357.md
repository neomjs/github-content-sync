Resolves #349

The Activity head now states what the feed holds. It reads `newest 50 · older on scroll`, then `all N shown` once the mailbox answers no older row, or `newest 1,000 · older not kept` at the ring's cap, with `· M dropped` when the ring evicted. The per-source populations move out of the head into a line under it, `sources · mailbox · 36 / 24h · 412 total`, so a mailbox total never reads as the feed's size.

When the list mounts its last held row, the stream asks for the next older page. `ReadingSurfacesController.loadActivityHistory` reads it from the mailbox lane alone: `fleetActivity({limit: 50, offset, slots: ['a2a']})`, where the offset is the mailbox rows held. It reads one page at a time, and `FleetAdmission.admitActivityHistory` lands the page through the store's merge, fenced to the profile it was read for. Nothing is asked at a full ring, after the mailbox answered an empty page, or after a page that added nothing, until the store moves.

Depends on neomjs/neo-agent-brain#636 (approved). Before it, the Brain's activity read passes no offset to the mailbox and answers every lane, so a page adds nothing and the reads stall. Merge #636 first.

Evidence: L2 (the NL battery drives the real app and a real buffered list; the landing fixture serves the bridge's `fleetActivity` from a 120-message mailbox) → L2 required (the ticket defines AC-2's falsifier in the NL battery). The same scroll against a live plane is Post-Merge Validation, once a Brain with #636 runs.

## Deltas from ticket
- **The cap stays at the store's 1000, and a full ring asks nothing.** At the cap, a page would be the very tail the ring evicts, so the head says `older not kept`. New live arrivals still evict the oldest rows, and `· M dropped` counts them.
- **The jump to newest is the existing `N new events ↑` button.** It already appears while the reader is scrolled into history and returns the list to index 0.
- **The sources line has no `pr —` entry.** The PR lane reports no count rows, and a dash would claim a slot its source does not fill. The line prints the composer's count rows as they are, labelled as sources.
- **One head golden, not two.** `all N shown` changes only the retention cell's words, which the NL arm and `container.spec` pin. A second golden would re-record on every head change without guarding more geometry.
- **The offset needs a Brain change after all.** The ticket expected none for the A2A lane. Brain #636 forwards `offset` to the mailbox and reads only the lanes a request names.
