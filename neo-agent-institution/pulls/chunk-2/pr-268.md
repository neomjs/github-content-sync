Resolves #267

The two navigation corrections from the operator's #10 ruling. The keeper nav keeps its six destinations, routes and order, but shows only the icons: upright, at the icon scale, each tab speaking its label as a tooltip. The label stays the tab's accessible name, clipped rather than removed. On the right rail, each item rests on its own panel-ground chip, so the engine's 2px gap reads as cockpit ground between chips instead of a word space inside a label. The three states take three grounds (panel, panel-2 on hover, line with the signal ink when revealed), so hovered and open never share a picture. Both changes are token values and app-side rules; nothing in the engine changes.

Evidence: L3 (the icon-rail contract asserted in a real browser; the rail and nav rendered and compared in both skins) → L3 required (AC-1/AC-2 need a rendered browser). Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket
The resting chip uses `--fm-panel` rather than the ticket's `--fm-rail`. `--fm-rail` sits one hex step above the dark ground, and the first render still ran the items together. With the chip on panel, hover moves up to panel-2 and the revealed tab to the line tone, keeping the three states distinct.
