Resolves #208

The theme switch was the last control in the chrome on the engine's touch scale: a 49 × 48 box with 1 px of air in the 50 px shell band, heavier than the logo (30) and the instance chip (21) beside it and 1.5× the bar's controls beneath. `.agent-top-toolbar` now re-values the four `--button-*` scale vars the engine's button reads at the element (height 32, min-width 32, padding 0, radius 6 — the #199 mechanism), so the switch is a 32 px square centered in the band, its glyph centered, its right edge on the gutter line with Start fleet below. Nothing on `.agent-button`, the band keeps its 50 px, the instance switcher keeps its own chip geometry.

Evidence: L3 (DOM rects in the browser at two widths; the visual suite alone) → L3 required for AC-1. No residual.

agent-preflight: not hosted here; the shared PR baseline runs.

Built on #207's branch (the two viewport goldens carry both the switch and the segmented bar) and opened against `dev` while #207 awaits its merge: the diff carries `e42683e` until then, after which this PR is the one commit `0bedd1e`.
