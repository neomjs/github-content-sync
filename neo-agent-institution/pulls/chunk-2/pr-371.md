Resolves #370

The macOS app gets its own icon: a navy tile taken from the brand blue `#3E63DD`, with the Neo mark's exact paths in white at 60% of the tile's width. Until now `mac.icon` was the full-bleed brand logo. macOS wrapped it in a light system tile, where the N filled about 80% of the width and its white fill vanished into the background.

`harness/assets/app/neoAppIcon.svg` follows Apple's macOS grid:
- a 1024 canvas with an 824 rounded tile (radius 185.4) and a soft drop shadow;
- the tile gradient `#1c2c5f` → `#0d1535`;
- the two ribbons centred on the mark's own point of symmetry.

The mark keeps its geometry. The white single-colour rendering is the one that stays legible on a dark tile; with the outline kept, the blue edges swamp the white at 32 px and 16 px. The in-app logo is unchanged.

Evidence: L3 (electron-builder 26.15.3's own icon toolset makes the `.icns`, and macOS renders it through `NSWorkspace`) → L3 required for AC-1 to AC-3. Residual: AC-4 (the operator's rebuilt app in the Dock), Residual-Owner: #12.
