Resolves #213

The Golden Path was text or nothing in the cockpit. This adds the graph: a south-strip pane (the `Route graph` tab after the Golden Path text pane) whose canvas-worker renderer draws the computed route as a ranked spine — items in rank order along the surface, their radius from the producer's score, each item's citations as satellites below it, a citation shared by several items placed once between them with an edge to each — under a currency line. Nothing is ranked, merged or cached: the layout is a pure module unit-tested without a canvas (positions are the only derived facts), the renderer only draws, and what is drawn follows the cockpit's ONE reading of the envelope, #215's `GoldenPathEnvelope.currency()`: a `current` route in the signal, a `withheld` one dim — the last known good route, the admission contract's fallback — and `degraded`, `unavailable` or `unobserved` draw nothing while the line above says why. Hovering an item names it (title · rank · score) through the engine's canvas mouse path.

Stacked on #215 (Grace's PR for the text pane, #210): the pane binds the `goldenPathEnvelope` leaf #215 declares and writes, reads `GoldenPathEnvelope` for the currency, the route and the citation labels, and stamps the instant with `ViewerTime` the way the text pane does. The diff carries #215's commits until it merges; then this PR is one commit on top. Grace's ⛔ on my first head (17:49Z — the layout read fields the wire never emits) is fixed here: fixtures are #499's wire envelopes landed through `fromWire`.

Evidence: L3 (the visual harness lands fixture wire envelopes inside the App worker through a test-only driver; the hover golden exercises the mouse path end to end) → L3 required for AC-2 and AC-3. Residual: AC-5 (the live plane) — the read is #215's; the live check follows both merges.

- The App worker clones the bound value before `postMessage`: a provider bind hands over the tracking proxy, which is not structured-cloneable.
