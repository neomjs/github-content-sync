Resolves #386

On macOS the Fleet Manager's main window drops the gray native title bar. The traffic lights sit in the cockpit's own top bar, as in Claude and Codex, and that bar drags the window. A browser renders the cockpit as before.

- `harness/main.mjs`: on macOS only, `createHarnessWindow` adds `titleBarStyle: 'hiddenInset'`, `titleBarOverlay: true` and `trafficLightPosition {x: 16, y: 18}`. Popups and the credential prompt keep their native frames.
- `Viewport.scss` `.agent-top-toolbar`:
  - left padding `max(9px, env(titlebar-area-x, 9px))`;
  - `-webkit-app-region: drag`, with `no-drag` on its `.neo-button`s (the instance switcher, the theme button).
- The visual stamp follows the SCSS blob. Every golden passes unchanged.

Evidence: L3 (the real Electron 43.5.0 shell, driven by window-server input) → L4 required for AC-4 (the packaged app). Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: #7.
