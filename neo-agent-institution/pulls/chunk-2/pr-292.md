The left-side FM nav's active destination now has a **ground**, not just a brighter icon.

`resources/scss/src/apps/agentos/Viewport.scss`, one file, +9/−1.

## What was actually wrong

The icon rail shipped with this state:

```scss
--tab-button-glyph-color-pressed   : var(--fm-ink);   /* active = brighter glyph  */
--tab-button-background-color      : transparent;     /* resting                   */
--tab-button-background-color-hover: var(--fm-panel);  /* hover — the ONLY ground   */
```

There was **no pressed background at all**. So the active tab had a brighter icon on nothing, and because hover owned the only surface on the rail, **hovering the active destination read as less selected than hovering an inactive one.** The nav had no way to say "you are here" that survived a pointer.

## The fix

```scss
--tab-button-glyph-color-pressed      : var(--fm-signal);
--tab-button-background-color-active  : color-mix(in srgb, var(--fm-signal) 12%, transparent);
```

Three grounds for three states — transparent at rest, the panel lift on hover, a low-percentage signal wash for active. That is the same three-ground separation the cockpit's right rail got in #268, and the same signal-tint shape `apps/workstation/Viewport.scss:58` already uses for its own nav, which is the precedent #267 names.

## Why I checked the token rather than trusting it

A `color-mix` against an undefined variable is invalid at computed-value time and **silently falls back to nothing** — the change would have looked committed and done nothing. So I resolved it: `--fm-signal` is defined in **both** skins at the same scope as the `--fm-panel` this block already used:

- `theme-neo-dark/apps/agentos/Viewport.scss:49` → `#5eead4`
- `theme-neo-light/apps/agentos/Viewport.scss:56` → `#0f766e`

So the wash is a real colour in both skins.

## One correction I owe, from this morning

I raised a merge-gate question on #268 because I could not resolve the rail tokens — they are in **this** repo's theme, and I had been looking in the engine's. With the values in hand: on the dark rail (`#0e131a`) the active ground `#262f3d` is the lightest of the three; on the light rail (`#e9edf3`) the active `#d3dae4` is the darkest. The ordering is monotone in **contrast against the rail**, not absolute lightness — so the active chip does outrank resting in both skins, and my "hue-less hairline" worry was an artefact of me assuming lightness. Grace answered that by measurement and she was right.

## What I have not done

**I have not looked at this rendered.** No golden was re-recorded and no app was run, so what I have is a verified token chain and a structural argument, not a picture. The three grounds are distinct in both skins by construction, but "nice pressed state" is a judgement only the operator can make, and the one thing I would check first in the render is whether the active teal band reads as clearly stronger than the hover lift — the two differ by **chroma**, not by lightness, which is deliberate (it keeps the band in the chrome's voice rather than competing with the keeper-view) but is the part most worth a second opinion.

Per #278, a change to committed visual baselines re-dirties every open FM PR on merge, so I have deliberately left the goldens alone rather than add churn to Ada's and Grace's in-flight PRs. If the look is right, the golden re-record belongs in this PR; tell me and I will run it.

`Resolves` nothing — this is the pressed half of the icon rail from the operator's in-session request, which is the authority for it, not a new ticket.
