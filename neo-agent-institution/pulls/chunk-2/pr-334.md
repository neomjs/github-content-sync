Resolves #333

🌿 A selected node can no longer lead with its id, and a work item or a session can no longer keep its evidence more than a step away.

The strip leaves. The side panel reads Team, View, Nodes and the selected node.
- **View:** names each control by the question it answers: Roadmap or Hubs wells, Attention, Golden Path, Messages, Outside wells. A withheld route reads withheld in its own control.
- **The selected node:**
  - leads with its label and kind, then what the scene carries about it: state as last ingested, attribution, last activity, and route rank on the route;
  - groups its relations by type and direction under counted headers;
  - keeps its id behind Copy.
- **Open:** routes through `AgentOS.util.GraphNodeSource`. A canonical work item opens its GitHub page, a session or summary opens its Memories drill in the cockpit, and any other node names its kind and says it has no source view.

Stacked on #332: its two commits lead this branch, because the View rows and the selection's actions use #332's `.fm-pane-actions` verb scale. Merge #332 first, and this branch rebases to its one commit.
