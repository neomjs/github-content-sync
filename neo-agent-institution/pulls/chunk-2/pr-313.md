Resolves #310

🌿 *An agent's inbox can no longer pass for a centre of gravity.*

The Observatory's default geography is now density wells: the read's 48 best-connected nodes (never a leaf) attract, each node joins the hub it reaches first, and it sits at its hop distance around that hub. Mail (messages, their broadcast sentinels and the relations routing them) stays out of the scene until the **Mail** toggle brings it in. The nodes in no well (no edge, or no path to a hub) sit in an outer **Halo**, cut into sector clusters so the far level of detail draws them on the shell, and that toggle hides them. The head names what the view hid ("1 mail node hidden · 2 in the halo"). Neither toggle hides a route seed, so the route never skips an item. No wire change: `ObservatorySceneLayout.fromGraphScene` gains `{geography, mail, halo}` options whose defaults keep today's communities scene bit for bit, and the view opts in.

Evidence: L2 (unit, Neural Link e2e and Darwin visual goldens in the local harness, on synthetic fixtures) → L4 required for AC-6 (installed FM, the operator's viewer, cold saved-plane launch). Residual: AC-6, Residual-Owner: #312.

## Deltas from ticket
- **A hub needs two relations.** With a fixed 48, a small graph made every leaf a one-node well. Now a graph with no centre puts all of its nodes in the halo; the NL fixture that loses `pr-101` forms 0 wells.
- **The layout's defaults stay today's scene.** The view passes density, mail off and halo on; every existing layout and canvas arm holds unchanged.
- **Halo sectors are clusters of their own** (`haloFrom` on the wire), so the engine's far centroids sit on the shell, never one bright centroid at the centre; the canvas inks them faint and small.
- **Every tunable is a class config** (operator review). `ObservatorySceneLayout` is a singleton whose geometry, search bounds, mail kinds and types, wells and halo are configs, every step a method; `canvas/Observatory`'s node sizes, community tones and LOD threshold are configs, and `hsl`/`hueOf` are methods. `Neo.overwrites` or a subclass reaches every value. Each method body diffs equal to the original except where it reads a config.
- **The toggles end the selection strip.** In the head, Mail and Halo clipped the read's line in the withheld state ("freshness-sla-brea"). Below it, the line keeps the head's whole width, and only the gesture hint yields, as before this PR. The engine's ghost skin colours a button's label itself, so a toggle's pressed state never showed; the Route toggle already had this before this PR. The shared `fm-observatory-toggle` style now re-binds the engine's ghost variables.
- **A view filter never hides a route seed** (Euclid's review). With Halo off, a seed in no well keeps its place on the shell. With Mail off, a mail seed stays. So the drawn route never joins two ranks across a hidden one.
- **The head counts mail as mail.** "N mail nodes hidden" names what the Mail toggle removes, messages and their broadcast sentinels; "messages" miscounted a sentinel.
- **Expectations follow the new default:** the NL e2e and visual head lines gain "· 2 in the halo" or "· 3 in the halo", and the 100k arm asserts the 48 wells instead of "most of the 64 generated groups".
- **On the synthetic 100k fixture (64 planted groups, no real hubs), density renders as a diffuse ball**, and topology communities separate the planted groups better. The default follows the real graph (D#19317 §5, where communities give a featureless sphere of inbox wells), which is why AC-6 is the deciding check.
