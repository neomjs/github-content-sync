Resolves #365

🌿 *The field can no longer write between the hero's lines.*

Home's canvas measures the hero's lines, their rects and their font sizes, relative to the canvas and one frame after anything moves them. After links, motes and ripples, the renderer erases the field under each line. The erase is full at the line's box and fades out over 0.4 em of that line's font size. The haze then fills in beneath (`destination-over`), and the marks go on top, so no mote or link sits on a glyph and no mark is dimmed.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms on the renderer and the host) + L3 (an e2e arm in Chromium, Home goldens re-recorded on Darwin) → L3 required (AC-2 and AC-3 are browser facts).

Based on `dev` since #368 merged (d058a63), which is merged in at 7f090dd. Beyond #368, that merge brings only README text.

## Deltas from ticket

- The feather is 0.4 em of each line's font size, not a fixed width (Grace's review), so the host reads each line's font size with its rect.
- `getStats().quiet` lists the rects (`{x, y, width, height, fontSize}`, canvas-relative) rather than counting them, so the e2e driver can hold them against the lines. #365's AC-1 now says "lists", and its Contract Ledger records the payload, freshness and failure semantics.
- The e2e reader switch lands `shellPlaneConfigured: false` through the visual suite's `homeState` driver, the way the ViewportController publishes it for a packaged shell without a plane.
- The plane line appears only in the cold golden, which is dark. A connected plane hides it, so no light golden shows it.
