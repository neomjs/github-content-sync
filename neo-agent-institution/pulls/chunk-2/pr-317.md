Resolves #308

🌿 *System can no longer hand the operator a decision the daemons own.*

v4 rebuilds the System design from its questions, following the operator's direction of 2026-09-28: Agent OS is self-diagnostics and self-healing. It has three bands, in this order:
- **Self-healing:** each condition reads as the ADR 0025/0026 loop, *diagnosed → acted → now*. Where healing stops, the view names the daemon work and its issue; nothing asks anyone to act.
- **Maintenance:** the heavy lane's *now · waiting · next*, with progress only where the plane measures it.
- **Containers:** one plain line each, with the raw detector fields one click down.

The frames use our plane's data from 2026-09-28, and they show what the loop does today. The diagnosis counts Chroma at 84% of its 16 GiB limit while it uses 70%; the other 2.2 GiB is reclaimable file cache. So the actuator has asked for a raise three times an hour, and each is refused at the top of its band (ADR 0026 §2.8).

Evidence: L2 (the rendered design in headless Chromium, both skins, 1280 and 390 px, reduced motion) over captured real data. Design-only; no runtime claim.
