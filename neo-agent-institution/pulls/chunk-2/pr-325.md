## Deltas from ticket
- **AC-3 lost its registry arm, and the ticket body says why.** No registry field holds a colour, and hues hashed from the whole wheel collide on the roster (`@neo-gpt`, `@neo-preview` and `@tobiu` within 7°, `@neo-gemini-pro` and `@neo-opus-ada` 5° apart). So `fmPalette.PEER_HUES` is eight places outside the gold, each identity keeps its hashed place, and `peerHues` probes the peers shown together to free ones. A stored colour whose only writer is that default adds nothing until the agent-setup view lets the operator choose one, which the ticket keeps out of scope.
- **Design call for review: the lens and the heat colour a route's seeds too.** With the seeds kept gold, three of the five lensed nodes in the fixture were indistinguishable from any seed. The route stays drawn by its path and its ranks, but the engine inks a path from its seeds' colours, so under an overlay the path carries them (`observatory-pane-lens.png`, `observatory-pane-heat.png`). The alternative, a gold path over recoloured seeds, needs a path colour in `Neo.canvas.GraphScene`.
- An empty Team list says so: `Team · no peer in this read`.
- The head's line keeps its width while the gesture hint shows, and gives way to a hovered node. A withheld route already pushed the hint out of the head (`observatory-pane-withheld-light.png`), and with both overlays on, the lens and heat clauses would too.
