Resolves #382

The cockpit's tab headers dragged into a layout that never answered: the engine's in-window drop feedback — the preview renderer, the drop-indicator menu and the gesture controller that feeds them — is a consumer-composed tier, and the cockpit composed none of it (measured headless before the fix: zero indicator chips, no `DragAffordances`, `DropIndicators` or `Participation` instance in the App Worker). The cockpit now declares a dock host under its control bar that holds the projected shell and the two persistent overlays, `VesselContainer` composes `DragAffordances` over them and routes the projection's three cross-zone drag seams to it, and one skin rule makes the host the overlays' containing block. A held tab header shows the indicator menu with the hovered chip active, and releasing on it commits one dock operation through the cockpit's own reducer. The operator's shell observation and the served cockpit were the same absence; this closes both.

Evidence: L2 (headless Chromium: the red-first e2e arm + the cockpit's dock, pop-out, multi-window and rail witnesses + the visual tier) → L3 required for AC-4 (the installed Electron shell after the next repackage). Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: #382's Post-Merge Validation (the operator's installed receipt), with the FM v1 milestone's repackage as its carrier (#380).

agent-preflight: not hosted here; baseline `pr-body` + `substrate-size` run in CI.
