Resolves #358

🌿 The packaged Brain's backups now live where removing the plane cannot reach them, so a clean start can no longer take the restore with it.

`buildPackagedBrainEnv` takes `backupRoot` apart from `dataRoot` and throws without one. The product boot writes bundles to `<userData>/backups`, beside `<userData>/brain`. Before this change they went to `<userData>/brain/backups`, beneath the plane they exist to restore (ADR 0019 §10.9). The packaged smoke keeps its disposable bundles inside its throwaway isolation root, and the call site says why.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms over the Brain's own path resolution, red on `dev`) → L2 required for AC-1 to AC-3. The installed build's first bundle is Post-Merge Validation. Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket

- #358's AC-2 was corrected before implementation. A sibling of the smoke's isolation root would escape the throwaway root into a shared temp parent, so the smoke keeps its disposable bundles inside it, the exemption §10.9 grants disposable bundles.
- `harness/README.md`'s packaged-profile paragraph gains the beside-the-plane clause.
