Resolves #374

🌿 *A blank field's reason can no longer write over the next field's name.*

The Add agent form zeroes its fields' margins to ride its own gap, and the engine draws a field's reason line below the field's fixed-height box. So a blank required username's "Required" (213–226 px) landed on the *Working repo* field's floating label (215–226 px). An invalid field now takes 16 px below it. That covers the 16.3 px reason line and the 5.4 px the next label floats above its box, once the form's 10 px gap is counted. Valid fields never move.

Evidence: L3 (a CI e2e arm reading the served cockpit in Chromium, and the Darwin pane golden) → L3 required. No residuals.

## Deltas from ticket

- The reason line is in flow, not absolute: `errorPositionAbsolute` defaults to `false`, and the form never sets it. The overlap comes from the field's fixed `height`, which the line overflows. The ticket's first fix applies either way.
- The form's computed gap is 10 px, not the 8 px its SCSS comment claimed. The comment I touched now names no number, and the arm reads the computed gap.
- The arm lives in e2e rather than the visual suite, so CI runs it on every PR. No new golden: the arm pins the geometry, and a valid form's picture is unchanged.
