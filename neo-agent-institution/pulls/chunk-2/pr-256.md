`AgentOS.canvas.Observatory` now extends `Neo.canvas.GraphScene` and keeps only the ink: the FM palette, the currency tones, and the layout scene marshalled into the engine's flat scene. The app's own program, camera, pick, upload and stats code is gone (600 → 137 lines), and so is the fixed `dpr: 2`. The drawing buffer follows the size message's `devicePixelRatio`. ## Deltas from ticket
- **The contract changes from Clio's #19274 review are absorbed in the subclass.** The Observatory's `setScene({scene})` inks the layout's scene into the engine's flat one, as plain arrays that the engine's normalizer types. The pane's `sourceScene` changes only after the engine took the scene, so a refused one leaves `pick` and the stats on the drawn scene. `pick` maps the engine's index back to `{id, kind, label, rank, score}`. `updateResources` inks the scene again on a theme change, because the engine only owes a frame. `ObservatoryCanvas` is unchanged.
- **#252's Contract Ledger is backfilled and its typed-array line corrected** (Euclid's RA-2). Typed arrays from the App Worker belong to H3-e's scale, not this route. The ledger also records that the App Worker's call rejects on a refused scene only from a pin that carries neo #19291; this PR's pin predates it.
- **Sizes change unit.** The engine sizes points in pixels at the camera's distance (`w / uDist`); the app sized them at unit depth (`/ w`). The fitted camera stood about 2.29 units out, so the sizes are divided by that: items 27–52, citations 17, beads 10. Kept as they were, every node drew about 2.3× larger (an 11% pixel diff); converted, the diff is 4%.
- **`getStats` keeps the pane's counts** (`{nodes, edges, route}`, zeros for an empty scene) and the currency, so the journey's assertions stand. The engine's other stats come through unchanged.
- A unit spec covers the ink, pick and stats seam without a GL context: `observatory.spec.mjs`, 5 arms. "a scene the engine refuses leaves the drawn one answering: its node, its counts and its currency" was red against the first `setScene`.
