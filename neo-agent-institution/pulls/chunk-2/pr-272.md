Resolves #271

Slice 1 of #258 (#271 is its sub-issue; #258 stays open with all its ACs for slice 2): the Observatory draws the Golden Path's bounded graph neighbourhood instead of the route alone, with one canonical selection over it. Stacked on #270 (the Brain and engine pins); its two commits drop out of this diff when it lands.

- **Read and leaf.** The cockpit reads `fleetGraphScene` beside the Golden Path read, on its own fence, into its own `graphSceneEnvelope` provider leaf. A graph read never writes `goldenPathEnvelope`. `ClosedShape` (moved out of `GoldenPathEnvelope` unchanged) lands both envelopes in their declared shapes. `GraphSceneEnvelope.describe` is the scene's line: capability, capture, holdings, and `complete` or `partial, budget …`. It never shows a total or a continuation.
- **Scene.** `ObservatorySceneLayout.fromGraphScene` (the first commit) replaces the route-only `fromGoldenPath`, which is retired. Positions depend only on ids, route order and edges. Seeds keep their route slot and carry their rank as a cue, and only the feed's edges become lines.
- **Selection.** Selection is one origin-qualified id, never a draw index. A click selects and an orbit does not; a read that still holds the id keeps it; a read that lost it clears it and the strip says why. The strip names only what the feed carries: label, kind, qualified id, rank, and relations by type, where a missing type counts as `unspecified`.
- **Renderer.** Ink steps down by hop, and the signal is spent only while the read is current. A selection fades the nodes outside its neighbourhood. `locate` is added as the inverse of `pick`.
