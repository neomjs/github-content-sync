Resolves #210

The cockpit's south strip gains a **Golden Path** tab. It renders the `fleetGoldenPath` envelope (neomjs/neo-agent-brain#499) in three parts:
- **Currency line first**, in the producer's words.
- **Route items next**, in the producer's order, taken from a pane-local Store.
- **Provenance last.**

The pane synthesizes nothing and ranks nothing.

The currency states:
- **current:** the admission admits the route, the route is `fresh`, and it has not expired.
- **withheld:** the route is shown as the last known good route, with the producer's reason (`freshness-sla-breached`, `route-expired`, `route-stale`).
- **degraded:** the route file is missing or invalid.
- **unavailable:** the verb is not wired, or the read failed.

**One truth for every Golden Path pane.** The envelope is the cockpit provider leaf `goldenPathEnvelope`. This is the contract the FM lead set for #213's graph pane, which binds the same leaf.
- `GoldenPathEnvelope` (util) declares the leaf's closed shape once. It lands every read into that shape and holds the one currency derivation.
- The shape is closed because of how `setData` handles objects. It drills them into leaf paths, and the ancestor rebuild stops at a `null` block: a route written as `null` then reads `null` forever. A key the next envelope omits keeps its old value.
- So every landing writes every declared key.

Two responsibilities leave the cockpit files that sat at the 1,000-line bar (Controller 998, Container 999):
- the catch-up and Golden Path owners, which become the new `ReadingSurfacesController` layer;
- the perspective share beat's logic, which moves into `CockpitPerspectives` with thin wrappers kept.

Evidence: L3 (the real shell in Chromium, the pane driven through the cockpit's own write over the Neural Link, goldens on Darwin) → L3 required for AC-1 and AC-3. Residual: AC-2, Residual-Owner: #9 (the live plane needs #499 merged and the Institution's Brain bump).

## Deltas from ticket
- **The four states.** The ticket named current / last-known-good / withheld / unavailable. The admission contract has two outcomes: admitted, or not admitted with the `last-known-good` fallback. So "last known good" is how a withheld route is presented, and the fourth state is a **degraded** source.
- **Citations instead of reasons.** The ticket says "the reasons as written", but `computed-route.v1` items carry `citations`. They are shown by label, up to 3, then as an overflow count.
- **The provider leaf** above, instead of an owner-held snapshot, because two panes read the envelope.
  - A torn-out pane keeps its bind. Bindings are created once at `initConfig` and write `Neo.get(componentId)`; reparenting never re-binds them.
  - An envelope bound mid-construction is applied once the pane's Store exists. That guard replaces the awaited fallback the first head used.
- **The extractions** above. The size gate requires them ("extraction along responsibility seams, never line golf"). The view-topology laws rule out a data module in `view/` or `util/`.
- **Visual baselines.**
  - Re-rendered: `cockpit-default-shell`, `cockpit-intermediate-720` and `cockpit-review-1280`, which carry the stream tab strip. The new tab sat under the diff threshold, so they passed without it.
  - Reverted: two unrelated drifts, `accounts-config-surface` and `system-view-cold`.
  - `baseline-inputs.json` is re-stamped.
- **Not re-rendered: `FleetNavFamilyPin`'s south golden.**
  - It is already red on dev: 100 px differ at d93f7db. The active-tab indicator under ACTIVITY is gone, and the strip's bottom border sits 1 px lower than in the 09-18 golden.
  - This PR adds the GOLDEN PATH label on top, for 209 px in total.
  - Re-rendering here would bless the indicator loss, which is the FM owner's call. A defect-note is sent.
