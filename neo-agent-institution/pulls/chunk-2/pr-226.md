Resolves #225

When the plane refuses a plane-attach fleet child, the shell now says why and offers the way back. Refusals include an identity mismatch, an unreachable plane, and no viewer identity. Until now it said "fleet: Brain is not ready". A stored record reads as configured, so the plane card never came back on its own.

- **The refusal is typed.**
  - `fleetReadyOrPlaneRefusal` wraps the fleet's readiness wait.
  - A plane-attach child that exited before ready becomes `planeRefusal`, with `code: 'plane-refused'`.
  - It quotes the child's last line once the child's output has closed, because the line can still be in flight at `exit`. That wait is bounded.
  - The quote is truncated to 240 characters, and dropped if it carries a known secret (the fleet bearer or the plane bearer).
  - A timeout with the child still alive, or any other mode, keeps its own error.
  - The harness uses its own plan and the child's words; it parses no Brain wording.
- **The lifecycle names it.** `bootFailureCause` maps both typed refusals, and `CAUSE_SEVERITY` ranks `plane-refused` with `organism-beside-plane`.
- **The banner says it:** *plane refused · The plane refused this shell — connect it again · <the child's line>*. It carries `action: 'connect-plane'`, so the bar's button reopens the card (#222's `showPlaneSetup`).
- **The env composition is named.** `startBrainChild`'s JSDoc now says a fragment can add or override an inherited variable, never remove one. This was @neo-preview's note from the #224 and neomjs/neo#19234 reviews.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms from the refusal through the lifecycle to the banner; the wait's drain proven with a line that lands after `exit`) → L3 required for AC-4 (a real refused attach on the team machine). Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: #12.
