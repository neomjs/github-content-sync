### 🔬 Depth Floor

**Challenge:** `partialActivityEvents` refuses the whole partial page when any remaining row is unrecognized. The event types and sources are checked against the Institution's pinned Brain contract, and the confidence values are hard-coded with no contract source. Today Brain `dev` and the pin list identical event types, so nothing is refused. But the Brain ships ahead of the pin: the first new event type a healthy source emits would refuse every partial page containing it, and the real A2A rows beside it would go stale. That is the blackout this ticket fixes, returning under skew. The `wired` path never applies these checks. Filtering unrecognized rows out (admitting the recognized ones, and counting or logging the drop) keeps "never manufacture activity" for the unknown rows without hiding the known ones. Your three refusal arms stay green under a filter, since none of them has a valid row. Non-blocking; your call whether it lands here or as a follow-up.
