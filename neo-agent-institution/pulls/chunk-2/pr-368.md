Resolves #366

🌿 *No gesture can now leave the field emptier than it found it.*

Ripples and the pointer's parting now move where a mote is drawn, through a per-frame drawn buffer, never where the mote is. The field is seeded across its whole wrap domain at the surface's density, so the current carries as many motes in as out.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms step the real renderer headless) + L3 (Home goldens re-recorded in Chromium) → L3 required (AC-1 and AC-2 are render facts). No residuals; the installed hidden-window check is #367's.

## Deltas from ticket

- `field.push` and `ripple.push` were velocities. They become `field.part`, the share of `reach` the pointer clears as a core, and `ripple.lift` in px. One `drawn` buffer (`placeMotes()`) now serves links, motes and marks, replacing three per-pair parallax computations.
- `moteMin` and `moteMax` now clamp a visible target, not the count on any one frame, and the total scales it to the wrap domain: 160 at 900×600, 229 at the cap, 238 at the floor. The new `moteCap` (320) caps the total on every surface, and a surface without area holds none. #366's Contract Ledger records these keys.
- The pointer's lasting hole was a suggestion, not a measured defect. In a probe the old pointer left the 250 px region at its control count, because its push kept the motes inside that region. So AC-1's pointer half pins the structural property, state untouched, rather than a count that could not tell old from new.
- The ticket's installed hidden-window AC moved to #367, which carries the engine pin past neomjs/neo#19338.

## Test Evidence

- Behavior probe on both renderers (a throwaway spec, not committed), measuring state plus parallax so it means the same on each. The click hole is 20 against 20 on the new renderer and 3 against 23 on the old. Visible after 180 s: 182 new, 125 old.
- At bf8015e: the renderer spec 6/6 and Home visual arms 2/2, with the stamp check clean. A per-second probe of AC-2's run reproduces the review's 175–208 range exactly.

**`lift: 22`: keep.** The ring carries the gesture. At 400 ms it is a thin teal stroke at radius ≈ 104 px, and by 800 ms it is at ≈ 210 px and fading. Motes on its front ride out 10–15 px and settle behind it, and their links stretch with the drawn positions without tearing. No frame shows a hole behind the wave, the visual counterpart of AC-1's click arm.

**`part: 0.6`: works, and the rim is worth one more look.** At full strength the pointer holds a clean clearing of about 80 px, and 500 ms after it leaves the field has mostly closed again. The clearing's edge is where the motes pile up. A mote at distance `d < reach` is drawn at `0.4·d + 78`, so everything inside 130 px is packed into the 78–130 px annulus: density rises to about 2.5× right at the rim, then drops to 1× outside it. In the frames the pressed cluster on the lower rim reads slightly like a wall rather than a bow wave. Two cheap options, if the next look agrees:
- `part: 0.5`: a 65 px clearing and a 2× step at the rim.
- An eased falloff, so the displacement's slope meets 1 at the rim and the density steps nowhere. A quadratic `(1 − d/reach)²` curve needs `part ≤ 0.5` to stay monotonic, because above that, motes near the pointer would cross.

This spot of the seed was sparse at rest, so treat the wall reading as a hypothesis. A frame pair at 0.6 and 0.5 over a denser spot would settle it.
