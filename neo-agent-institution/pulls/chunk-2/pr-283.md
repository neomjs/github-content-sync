Resolves #249

The live roster reconcile read membership from the filtered view. With the density fold armed, the idle tier lives only in the store's unfiltered twin, so two things went wrong. A folded resident the snapshot dropped was never removed, and the fold kept counting ghosts. A folded resident the snapshot still carried missed `store.get`, was treated as a joiner, and never took its row. `LivenessController#reconcileRoster` now finds a known resident in the view first (where hydrating also writes the twin), and in the twin only when the view hides it. It takes the census of departures from the twin.

Evidence: L2/L3 (a unit arm over a real filtered `FleetRoster`; the NL shrink arm in a real browser, with a control on dev's reconcile) → L3 required for AC-2.

## AC Evidence

|---|---|
| AC-1 | `rosterStore.spec` › "a fleet that shrinks under the idle fold…": 20 residents, the roster Controller's `tierRank` fold armed (view 6, twin 20), then a 7-resident snapshot. The twin shrinks to 7; the folded `idle-0`, still in the snapshot, keeps its record instance and takes its row; the view still folds it. Red on dev (twin stays 20) |
| AC-2 | `FleetGridScaleNL`'s shrink arm drops `test.fail` and passes: "+14 idle", then a 6-resident answer un-folds and titles "Fleet · 6 agents". Control: the same arm with dev's `reconcileRoster` fails |

## Deltas from ticket
- **No filter suspension.** The ticket's second half asked for a removal path that reaches filtered-out records. The engine has one since neomjs/neo#18855 (2026-09-18): a keyed `remove` mirrors its input keys into the unfiltered twin, so `store.remove(agentId)` reaches a hidden row. Only the census was wrong.
- **The lookup half.** Found at intake: a folded resident still in the snapshot was re-added as a joiner instead of taking its row. A mutation reverting only the lookup turns the arm red on that assertion (`lastActivityAt` stays `null`).

### 🧠 Graph Ingestion Notes

*   **`[RETROSPECTIVE]`**: **A filtered view is not a census, and the difference only appears when the filter is doing its job.** The defect was not "idle rows were mishandled" — it was that membership was read from a *view* while the records being counted lived in the twin the view hides. With no filter active the two are the same collection, which is exactly why the seed's replace path and the first landing were unaffected and the bug survived until a 20-resident fleet folded. The transferable shape: **any code that answers "what exists?" must decide whether it means "what is visible" or "what is present", and the two coincide right up until a filter, a fold, or a permission hides something.** A census written against a view is a latent bug that no small-scale test can surface. The related discipline, also visible here: the lookup and the census are *two* reads of the same question, and fixing one without the other produces a fix that passes a count assertion and still duplicates a record — which is why the arm asserts `toBe(folded)`, the instance, and not merely that a row exists.
