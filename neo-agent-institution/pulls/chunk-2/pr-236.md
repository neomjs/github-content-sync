Resolves #235

The credential window now fits its content. It opened at a fixed 260 px, while the document needs 264 px at the shipped wording (the lede wraps to two lines). As a macOS sheet over the cockpit, the operator's case, the page scrolled and Cancel / Connect sat cut off. The window's own preload now reports the body's natural height once, on load, and main sets the content size to it, clamped to 200…720. `useContentSize` makes the size the page's, with or without a title bar. No page script is added.

Evidence: L3 (the Electron probe below, a real modal sheet over a parent window) → L3 required for AC-1.

agent-preflight: not hosted here; baseline jobs ran.
