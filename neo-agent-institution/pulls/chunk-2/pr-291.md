Resolves #288

The observatory now lays out every graph read in communities and draws the Golden Path route as an overlay the pane's toggle removes. A large read engages `GraphScene`'s level of detail, and wheel zoom and drag rotation already worked. A 100k-node fixture lands through the live read's own write and proves all of it in the FM window. The look follows Clio's 3D proof of concept: nodes are dust that adds up on the dark skin, so a community glows, and the route is a gold line.
- **Communities:** Louvain over the read's own edges, deterministic in id order. They sit on a golden-angle sphere, and each node sits at a point its id draws inside its community's ball.
- **Positions** depend only on ids and edges, so the route never moves a node.
- **Scale:** the scene crosses to the canvas worker once per read, as typed arrays by node index (`ObservatorySceneLayout.wire`). It carries no node object or id: `pick` answers an index, and the App Worker resolves it against the scene it holds. At 100k that clone takes about 1 ms, where node objects took 218–268 ms (491–533 ms at 244k). A selection or overlay change re-inks in the worker without the scene crossing again. The pane reads the provider leaf through a closed-shape projection instead of a JSON round trip. Its lists keep to a budget, plus at most one row for a selection beyond it.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms over the layout, the renderer, the pane and the canvas routing) + L3 (`FleetObservatoryNL` in the real FM window, including the 100k scale arm) → L3 required for AC-2, AC-3 and AC-5.
