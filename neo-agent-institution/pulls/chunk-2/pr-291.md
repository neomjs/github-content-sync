Resolves #288

The observatory now lays out every graph read in communities and draws the Golden Path route as an overlay the pane's toggle removes. A large read engages `GraphScene`'s level of detail, and wheel zoom and drag rotation already worked. A 100k-node fixture lands through the live read's own write and proves all of it in the FM window.
- **Communities:** Louvain over the read's own edges, deterministic in id order. They sit on a golden-angle sphere, and each node sits at a point its id draws inside its community's ball.
- **Positions** depend only on ids and edges, so the route never moves a node.
- **Scale:** a selection or overlay change re-inks in the canvas worker without the scene crossing again. The pane reads the provider leaf through a closed-shape projection instead of a JSON round trip, and its lists keep to a budget.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms over the layout, the renderer, the pane and the canvas routing) + L3 (`FleetObservatoryNL` in the real FM window, including the 100k scale arm) → L3 required for AC-2, AC-3 and AC-5.

## Deltas from ticket
- **Louvain, not label propagation.** LPA with a smaller-label tie rule flooded 4 groups into 2; with seeded ties it fragmented 400 nodes into 50. Louvain recovers the groups, finding 63 of 64 at 100k with a minimum purity of 0.78; its resolution limit merges one pair.
- **LOD from 2,000 nodes.** `GraphScene` draws a clustered scene's far level as centroids, so a 16-node read would show blobs and nothing pickable. Smaller reads draw every node.
- **Design at scale, found by looking at the 100k captures.** At that scale, grey steps and 12-unit nodes read as fog, so:
  - communities take muted golden-angle hues from the signal's hue;
  - nodes shrink with density, from 12 to 3;
  - the route is a finer ribbon (the renderer's own `sceneStyle`) and keeps its colour under a selection;
  - the Route toggle is the roster's quiet ghost verb.
