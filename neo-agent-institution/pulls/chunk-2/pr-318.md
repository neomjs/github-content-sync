Resolves #309

🌿 *Catch Up can no longer ask before it tells, or pass off a failed read as a quiet day.*

v2 redefines Catch Up from the returning reader's questions, following the operator's direction of 2026-09-28: purpose and content first, visual design after. Catch Up has one job, "I was away: what happened that I should know, and where do I look?", for the operator and for peers. Home, the Observatory, System and Activity keep their own questions (§3). The pane reads top to bottom:
- **the window**, as a sentence;
- **Landed**: merged pull requests in their own titles, newest first, with dependency bots folded into one line;
- **Decided**: work closed without landing (decision records and graduations are not read yet);
- **In the team's words**: the memory summary, labelled as a summary, last;
- **the close**, explicit, at the end.

The frames use real reads from 2026-09-28, and they show why the installed pane looks empty. Its anchor forgets every reader when the Fleet service restarts. Its pull-request read covers only `neomjs/neo`, and on our plane it cannot sign in to GitHub. Its summary misnamed two of the day's merged fixes.

Evidence: L2 (the rendered design in headless Chromium, both skins, 1280 and 390 px) over captured real data. Design-only; no runtime claim.

- **Dependency bots fold into one line.** They made 12 of today's 20 merges. This is a grouping by author, not a ranking.
- **The partition moves** from the head to the summary, the only source that partitions.
- **The baseline's npm-overrides check is folded in** (`386f8c4`). Since #316 bumped monaco-editor to 0.57.0, the `monaco-editor > dompurify ^3.4.13` override no longer holds a floor, and the check reds every Institution PR on current dev. The rule is gone, and the lock resolves dompurify 3.4.15 from monaco's own range.
