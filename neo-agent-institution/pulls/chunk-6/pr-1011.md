Resolves #1010

ADR 0038 §2.2 gains fact 5, the identity a request acts as. It is a protected relation from the provider subject to one `AgentIdentity`. The fact carries its two writers, the first-admission and refusal rules, and the authority an administrative bind needs. §2.5.1 row 3 now cites fact 5 for a seat's binding, and a dated header row records the amendment with its three Brain leaves. This is decision authority only; nothing here claims runtime delivery.

## Deltas from ticket

- Fact 5 states the administrative bind's authority: binding an existing identity needs explicit administrative or cutover authority over that identity, and seat operation never suffices. This comes from Sophie's peer-role read, recorded as neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1812 AC-11. It applies §2.3's separation of the Fleet and content grant families.
- The count words follow the new fact. The heading, the intro, the session-intake recipe and the retrieval hint now say five. Fact 1 and the binding negative ACs name the acting identity.
- The amendment is a dedicated header row, the pattern of the 10-13 and 10-26 amendments, instead of a sixth entry in the long `Amended` cell.
