Resolves #959

Home's operator line now ends with a quiet `141 expired unanswered`: the asks that expired without the operator's word inside the producer's window. The Mailbox gains an `expired · 141` chip and the list behind it. One block feeds both. `OpenWorkRead.questions` carries the producer's `expired` count leaf-complete, so the provider's deep merge never keeps an old count. The expired view lists the producer's rows in the producer's order, and it shows only the expired read's own answer. An answer without `since` comes from a plane without Brain #1691; it stays `not read yet` and is never shown as the open list under the wrong name.

Evidence: L3 (the visual suite renders Home and the Mailbox in a local browser from driver-landed reads, both skins; the unit specs run in CI) → L3 required (AC-1's Look is a rendered observable). Residual: AC-6, Residual-Owner: #951.

## Deltas from ticket
- **The waiting counts carry no accent in code.** They inherit the line's ink with a dotted underline. So the expired run is quieter by its underline instead: none at rest, solid on hover, in the line's own colour. For Clio's design read.
- **The chip stays while its own list is in view**, even if the count falls to zero, so the pressed chip never vanishes under its list ([intake](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-institution/issues/959#issuecomment-6868346568)).
- **One navigation path.** Home fires `questionsOpen` with `{view}` instead of a new `expiredOpen`. `openOperatorQuestions` and `showQuestions` (renamed from `showOpenQuestions`) take the view.
- **Outside the Fix list:**
  - `TargetBinding.retireOperatorMailbox` clears the expired read on a profile switch.
  - `AgentMailbox.sortersOf` keeps the producer's order.
  - The Viewport provider's `questions` block starts leaf-complete.
- The empty line reads `no ask expired this week` as written; the intake proposes the window's date instead.

**Design read of the three Look points, after the merge.** Read from the three Home baselines on `dev` (`home-operator-expired.png`, `-light.png`, `-nothing.png`).

1. **The expired run: dim it, and give it its siblings' dotted underline.** As built it has the line's own ink and no underline at rest. Two things follow in the frame: "141" is the largest number on the line and the eye lands on it first, and it is the one link of three that does not look like one ("5 questions" and "2 merges" are dotted, it is bare until hovered). Quiet belongs to the ink, the affordance to the underline: the secondary ink (`--fm-ink-dim`, if it holds 4.5 : 1 on Home's ground in both skins) with the same dotted underline as the two counts before it.
2. **The chip stays while its own list is in view, even at zero: yes.** A pressed control that vanishes under its own list is worse than "expired · 0".
3. **The empty line: take the window's date.** "no ask expired since 7 Nov" is true for any window; "this week" is true only when the window happens to be one.

1 and 3 are two style lines and one string. Where they ride is the builder's call; nothing here reopens #959.
