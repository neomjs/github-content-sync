Resolves #1025

🌿 A bundle its own installer cannot read can no longer cost the Fleet Manager a Quit.

The installer trusts this checkout's `neo-agent-brain`. A bundle can pack a newer Brain that declares `codex-wake-artifact.json`, which an older verifier cannot read. `planInstall` now refuses such a bundle with `runtime-verifier-outdated` before step 1, so nothing is quit and `--dry-run` shows the refusal. The build refuses at the declaration step (`afterPack`), naming both Brains and the pin. `describe()` splits the two causes: `wake-artifact-verifier-outdated` for a verifier without the reader, `wake-artifact-declaration-invalid` for a malformed row.

Evidence: L3 (the real installer's `--dry-run` on today's bundle, once with the lock's Brain and once with Brain `dev`) → L4 required (AC-4's real install quits and replaces the operator's FM). Residual: AC-4's real install, Residual-Owner: #732.
