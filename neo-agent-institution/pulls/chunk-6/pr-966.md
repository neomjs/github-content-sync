## Deltas from ticket

- Implemented all 3 `@neo-fable-clio` `AC-1` build conditions from [`#958#issuecomment-6870970709`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-institution/issues/958#issuecomment-6870970709): `Select` (`customizable: false`) matched to `26 px` height, `12 px` `--fm-text-body`, and quiet `▾` caret; `--fm-signal` teal focus border on both `.neo-input-wrapper:focus-within` / `.neo-focus` and `.neo-selectfield-input:focus`; single count in the head (`Nodes · <matched> of <total>`) with `first 500 listed` added to the detail line only when `matched > listed`.
- Used Neo's trailing-edge `static delayable = {emitFilterChange: {type: 'buffer', timer: 120}}` (rather than `type: 'debounce'`, which fires synchronously on the leading edge in `Neo.util.Function.debounce`) so rapid typing fires zero `fillNodeList` passes during keystrokes and flushes synchronously on `ArrowDown`, clear, or kind selection.
- Gated `extraId` retention in `ObservatoryContainer#syncLists` on `ObservatoryNodeList.matchesNode(scene.nodes[index], me.nodeFilter)` so a canvas-selected node outside `listBudget` is suppressed while excluded by an active filter (keeping zero-match states at `0` rows) and re-appended once the filter clears; added `await settle()` after `details.click()` in `FleetObservatoryNL.spec.mjs:573` so canvas `ResizeObserver` height updates settle before `places()` is sampled.
