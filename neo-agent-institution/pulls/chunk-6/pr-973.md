Resolves #972

🌿 The split can no longer describe a row its list keeps out of sight.

The Observatory's split now shows the selected row whenever it shows the selection. Entering the node list from the Nodes head, which includes #958's query field, reveals the row it focuses. Nodes opening over a selection reveals it once Selected and its relations have settled. Reveals queue, so two never add their deltas. A new read reveals nothing, so it never pulls the list away from the rows the viewer is reading, and Escape from Selected still returns to the row at the held `scrollTop`.

Evidence: L3 (Darwin Chromium NL arm on the fixture plane, real key events and DOM geometry; unit arms in CI) → L4 required (AC-5, the installed walk). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: #485.

Prescription checked: `apps/agentos/view/fleet/goldenpath/ObservatoryNodeList.mjs` owns the concern. The engine Navigator's `scrollIntoView` keeps no context row and would move `scrollTop` on Escape, and the pane only decides when to ask.

## Deltas from ticket

- `enterFromHead` and the reveal in `syncSections` wait through `trap()`. If the pane is destroyed mid-wait, the wait rejects with `Neo.isDestroyed` instead of reaching a list whose fields `destroy()` removed. An arm in `observatoryContainer.spec.mjs` destroys the pane during an entry from the head; without the trap it logged a `TypeError`.
- A unit arm in `observatoryNodeList.spec.mjs` pins the queue: the second reveal starts after the first has scrolled, and a failed one does not stop the next. It is red against `dev`'s source.
- `ObservatoryContainer.mjs` is three lines under its ceiling, so the next responsibility it takes needs a seam cut first.
