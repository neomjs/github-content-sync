Resolves #898

The Golden Path pane stops calling a route `current` after it expires. Currency is measured against the viewer's clock, the pane re-derives its facts row on every liveness pass, and the cockpit re-reads the route at the route's own expiry. Before this, a left-open pane re-read nothing and kept the read-time word.

Evidence: L2 (unit specs in CI, red on dev and green here) + L3 (the Neural Link e2e in headless Chromium, local) → L4 required for AC-7 (the installed app left open across two producer passes). Residual: AC-7, Residual-Owner: #479.

- `GoldenPathEnvelope`: `currency(envelope, now)` and `withheldReason(envelope, now)` treat `expiresAt <= now` as expired (`isExpired`). `currentUntil()` says until when a route stays current. A cockpit-side `read` block, which the wire never carries, marks a failed re-read: `afterFailedRead()` keeps the landed route withheld with the read's reason, or lands unavailable when there is none.
- The pane takes `now_` from the liveness pass (`onLivenessTick`, beside the Mailbox's), re-applies its facts row without a new envelope, and touches the currency title only when it changes.
- `LivenessCadence`: a `goldenPath` read (cap 1, on the tasks interval) and `reschedule()`, which sets a due time either way (`expedite()` only moves one earlier). `writeGoldenPath()` reschedules to a current route's expiry, otherwise one interval out.
- `loadGoldenPath()` reads one at a time: the cadence, Refresh, Reconnect and the admission recovery share the read on the wire, and only the wire's own settle frees the next one. `TargetBinding.retireGoldenPath()` retires the landed route and fences the read in flight when the bridge answers for another profile. That read keeps the wire until it settles, and a request meanwhile reads anew once it has, so one read stays in flight across profile switches. A failed first read lands as unavailable, the old profile's late answer lands nowhere, and a read that settles after teardown touches nothing and starts none.

## Deltas from ticket
- AC-4 needed a place for the cockpit's own observation, so the envelope's closed shape gains `read: {state, reason}`, cleared by every landing. Writing it into `admission` would have attributed the failure to the producer.
- AC-3 needed `LivenessCadence.reschedule()`: `plan()` reschedules at `now + interval` and `expedite()` only moves earlier, so on the tasks interval alone an hour-long route would be re-read every 120 s, the ticket's own Avoided Trap.
- "A failed re-read" is the cockpit's own fallback: the verb is absent or the read threw. A producer answer of `unavailable` or `degraded` still lands as the producer said.
- Reconnect stays uncapped for every other seam, but the Golden Path joins a read already on the wire. A wire that never settles holds the pane at its last landing (a timed-out wait lands as a failed read) until it settles. A profile switch waits for the old profile's wire the same way: until it settles, the new profile's pane stays unobserved.
- The e2e and visual fixtures keep their 2026-07-05 instants and now expire in 2099: the older-day form shows no year, so every golden renders as before.
