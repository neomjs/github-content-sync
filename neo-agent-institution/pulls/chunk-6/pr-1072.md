Resolves #1071

`.github/workflows/merge-comment.yml` calls neomjs/neo's merge-comment workflow at the merge commit of neomjs/neo#22369, `c991ccb090`. A merged pull request from outside the team now gets the Engine's comment here too: the author's standing across the three repositories, curated issues to take next, and the badge. Like `claim-responder.yml`, the caller owns only the `pull_request_target` trigger and the token; the filter, the concurrency and the words stay in the Engine. `mergeCommentWorkflow.spec.mjs` pins the caller's contract beside the claim responder's spec.

**Operator step before the merge:** add `.github/workflows/merge-comment.yml` to this repository's `pull_request_target` Actions policy (neomjs/neo#21069). Enforcement begins on 2 December.

Evidence: L2 (the contract spec runs in CI; the pinned ref's `workflow_call` is read at `c991ccb090`) → L2 required for AC-1 and AC-2; AC-4 needs a real merged outside pull request. Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo#20892.

CI jobs: +1 — a called workflow needs a job of its own (`uses:` takes one per job), so it cannot join `claim-responder.yml`'s. It starts only when a pull request closes, and the called job is skipped unless an outside pull request merged.
