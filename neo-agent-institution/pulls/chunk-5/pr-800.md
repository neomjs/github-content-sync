Resolves #790

The Activity stream's rows take keyboard focus. ArrowUp and ArrowDown move it, Enter opens the focused message beside the list in an observer mode, and Escape closes it.

The open message is the list's one selection. `activity/SelectionModel.mjs` (sibling of the roster's model) takes the selection only for a message row, and a click on the open row closes it. The list's Navigator turns Enter into a click on the focused row, so the keys open exactly what the pointer opens.

The open row wears the roster card's tint, with its hairline shifted toward the signal. The keyboard ring sits inside the row.

The container's hand toggle (`onListItemClick`, `selectedEventId`) is deleted in favour of the selection, which the engine re-applies across the buffered pool's recycling.

**Owner's call (selection, not focus only):** as a selection, the open row carries `aria-selected` and the selected paint through the engine: `createItem`, plus `restoreSelection` after every pool rebind. Focus only would need a hand-kept open class on recycled rows. The mailbox mode reads with selection off, because it opens nothing in the stream.

**AC-3 (Tab order):** the head's chips, then the rows as one stop, then out. A row's chips are not controls (spans with no verb), so nothing inside a row is a stop of its own. The spec pins that no row holds an `a`, a `button` or a `[tabindex]`.

Evidence: L2 (red-first e2e in a real browser, computed paint in both skins) → L3 required (AC-4, the operator's walk on the installed app). Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: #505.

## Deltas from ticket
- The ticket says the Navigator never subscribes without focusable rows. It does subscribe (`list/Base#afterSetMounted`), and it makes the first row tabbable. The red was ArrowDown: no other row had a `tabIndex`, so focus never moved.
- The row pads its text one 4px step inline (`--fm-space-1`), so the open tint and the keyboard ring clear the glyphs. Before the step, the ring cut the first digit of the time (row and text both at x=60). With it, the text sits at 64 and the ring at 60–62, in both skins. The stream's goldens are re-captured for that step.
- The unit arms for the open path move from `itemClick` to the selection model's click path, plus one arm: another message moves the detail to it, and Escape closes it.

- **Challenge**: In `apps/agentos/view/fleet/activity/SelectionModel.mjs:29-36`, `close()` calls `me.deselectAll()` followed by `me.view.fire('select', {records: [], selection: me.items})`, whereas `apps/agentos/view/fleet/activity/Container.mjs` calls `selectionModel.deselectAll()` directly at three internal reset sites (`afterSetAdapterState` at line `392`, `afterSetMode` at line `438`, and `applyDetail` at line `536`). Because each of those three container sites immediately calls `me.applyDetail()` (or runs inside `applyDetail()`), calling `deselectAll()` directly avoids a re-entrant `select -> onListSelect -> applyDetail` hop. However, unlike `apps/agentos/view/fleet/roster/SelectionModel.mjs:113-121` (which overrides `deselect` and `deselectAll` so any deselection emits the empty `select` envelope), an external caller invoking `list.selectionModel.deselectAll()` directly rather than `list.selectionModel.close()` would clear `aria-selected` without hiding `stream-detail` until the next `applyDetail()`. Non-blocking because `SelectionModel#close()` is the public close verb and all three container reset paths call `applyDetail()` synchronously.
