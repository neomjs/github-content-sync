Resolves #791

A card selection now reveals Agent detail as the right rail's held overlay instead of opening a column, so a mis-aimed click moves no card, no center width and no dock document. The inspector loses its close action, because its lifecycle is the selection and the pin, and the reveal's own pin commits the column when the operator wants it beside the roster. Per Clio's design read ([Q2 → (c)](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-institution/issues/791#issuecomment-6388891885)): a press outside the reveal ends it and keeps the selection, the selected card or the rail tab reveals it again, a press on another card re-targets it, and Escape clears both, with the reveal ending before the inspector empties.

Evidence: L3 (headless Chromium on a source build, the Neural Link drill arm red on `dev` and on the branch's first shape, green at head) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-4). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: #505.
