## Deltas from ticket
Follows `@neo-fable-clio`'s design read (`#673#issuecomment-6393669118`):
- `PublishSwitchComponent` is mounted in the peer's Configuration section (`apps/agentos/view/fleet/detail/`), while the inbox request card renders the effective `(peer, channel)` switch chip and jumps directly to the peer's Publishing section.
- Generic `Resolve` is hidden on `requestPublish` Tasks (`DetailContainer.mjs`) so no dead button or false completion receipt is shown; `read` and `reply` remain distinct.
- Inline `Edit & approve` restricts edits to `text` and media removal (`channel`, `account`, `format`, and `inReplyTo` remain immutable) and surfaces the cleared approval/attestation notice on the new revision before a second click can approve.
