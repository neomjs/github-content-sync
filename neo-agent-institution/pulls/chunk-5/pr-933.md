Resolves #929

The product bar gains a Nightshift toggle left of the theme switch. It shows the plane's operator presence record, which the roster read now carries into the Viewport provider (`operatorPresence`, leaf-complete; `mode: null` is unknown, never present). A click while away switches it off. A click while present opens three spans (until switched off, 8 hours, 12 hours), and a span sends one `setOperatorPresence` with its `until`. The toggle shows the record the verb answers, then the cockpit re-reads the roster: that read's generation fence drops an answer read before the click. Each write takes its own generation too, so an answer overtaken by a later click, or sent through a bridge the cockpit has since left, lands nowhere. A write that does not land leaves the record as it was, and the toggle's title names why in fixed words (the remote error text never renders). A target switch retires the record and the toggle's last refusal, whether or not the roster was wired.
