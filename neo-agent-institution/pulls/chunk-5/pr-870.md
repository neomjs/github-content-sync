Resolves #843

A PAT the operator brings to the plane card no longer relaunches the Fleet Manager. Main hands the fleet child its plane bearer on a credential pipe (`harness/planeCredentialPipe.mjs`, fd 3, the protocol neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1463 serves) instead of its environment. `attachPlane()` probes the PAT as before, then sends it over that pipe, and stores `plane.json` only once the child answers `adopted`. A refusal, or a PAT the plane names as another account, leaves the binding and the record as they were, and the card names both accounts. So does a running child whose pipe has closed: it keeps serving the PAT it bound, and the card says a reopen puts the new one to use. A boot that the plane refused for its PAT runs again in this process with the new one. Only a shell that can do neither (no plane yet, another plane, or a plane from the environment) still relaunches. In the cockpit, the Connect door's `connected` re-drives every seam, and a reconnect asks `verifyPlane()` again while a credential cause stands, so "pat refused" clears on the shell's answer at once instead of at the next 300 s re-check.

Evidence: L3 (a real fd-3 socketpair between main's pipe end and a child spawned by `startBrainChild`; the carrier contract over the real Brain's class rule at Brain `690dc8df`; the Brain entry's own fd-3 end-to-end spec in neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1463) → L4 required (AC-5: refuse → Connect → `live` with no relaunch, walked on an installed candidate). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: #516.
