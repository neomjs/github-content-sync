Resolves #878

🌿 Zoomed all the way in, the Observatory can no longer show unnamed dust around its target.

The wire carries one name per node from its layout label, and none where the label is only the node's id. `ink()` hands the names to `GraphScene` as `names`, in the wells' ink. The Observatory's `nearStyle` sets a 7 px floor and a 200 px name width. Closer than `lod.near`, every node draws as a mark and the nodes nearest the target carry their names. Above the band nothing changes: the fitted frame and the frame on the well are byte-identical before and after. The engine half is neomjs/neo#20312 (PR neomjs/neo#20317). `dev`'s lock already carries it: dependabot's #882 resolves the Engine at `28fc096649`. So this PR changes no lock, and CI resolves the Engine at `dev` regardless.

Evidence: L3 (local unit, NL and visual runs, plus rendered frames read by the design seat) → L3 required (AC-3, AC-4). No residuals.
