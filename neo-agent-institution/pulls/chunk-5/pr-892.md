Resolves #891

🌿 A release that ships on a weekend no longer waits until Monday to reach the Institution.

`.github/dependabot.yml` moves both ecosystems, npm and github-actions, from `interval: daily` (Monday to Friday) to `interval: cron` with `cronjob: "30 13 * * *"`. That runs every day at 13:30 UTC, the hour the scheduled job already used, so the organization's Dependabot runs stay staggered on the shared runners. A two-line header note says why. Majors, cooldown (`neo-agent-skills` excluded) and grouping are unchanged. It's the twin of neomjs/neo#20607 and neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1519.
