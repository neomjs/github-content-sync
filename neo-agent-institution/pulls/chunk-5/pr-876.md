Resolves #875

Home's field now follows a reader switch however long its render takes. `applyState` measured the hero's lines on a 30 ms timer, so a render that took longer landed after the read, and the field stayed quiet under lines that were gone until the next resize. That is the HomeField AC-2 failure on #874's first CI run. `applyState` now renders the lines' container over its whole subtree and measures once that render has landed. No hidden line's own render is awaited, since that would wait for a mount. An unmounted Home skips, because its canvas measures when it mounts.

Evidence: L2 (e2e: the Home field driver's rect check in a headless browser, with the App worker's vdom batches held by a test driver) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-3). Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket

- `measureQuiet` keeps its 30 ms as a coalescing wait, and its docblock now says so.
