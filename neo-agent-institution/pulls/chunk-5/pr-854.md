Resolves #844

Related: #424, #516, #843, neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1409

Records plane outages that fail and recover across cockpit reads (`FleetAdmission.recordOutageStart` / `recordOutageRecovery`) and service restarts `<= 60 s` detected between answered deployment-state reads from `services[].startedAt` (`DeploymentStateRead.detectServiceRestartOutage`) onto `StateProvider.data.planeOutageNote` (`{reason, recoveredAt, startedAt}`), rendering a product-word recovery note on `SpineBanner` (`Plane outage HH:MM–HH:MM — recovered`) with the retained diagnostic reason and UTC ISO receipt window on `title` and `ariaLabel`. When the spine is otherwise `live`, the note renders under `kind: 'live'` (preserving `instanceState: 'ok'`) with `action: 'dismiss-outage'` (`Dismiss` button clearing `planeOutageNote`); when the stream is `partial` (as in Row 5's H2 walk where PR lanes are unconfigured), the product-word note appends to the `feed partial` lead and its retained reason + ISO window append to `title`/`ariaLabel`, clearing when `Recheck` is clicked or expiring automatically after `OUTAGE_NOTE_TTL_MS` (5 min) on subsequent cadence reads.

Change class: `restoration` (`agent-preflight`: not hosted here; baseline `checkAppFileSizes` + `checkVisualBaselines` + `unit-institution` ran).

Evidence: L1 (unit/spec ceiling in PR checkout) → L4 required (AC-3 installed Row 5 H2 re-walk receipt). Residual: AC-3, Residual-Owner: #516.

- **Route 2 data source (`neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1409` / PR #1413):** `projectDeploymentServiceForFleet` now projects `services[].startedAt` from `inspect.currentRun.startedAt` (leaving `inspect` off the wire), and `DeploymentStateRead.detectServiceRestartOutage` reads only `services[].startedAt` and bounds the outage window from `min(startedAt)` to `max(startedAt)`.
- **Note dismissal and 5-minute expiry (`dismiss-outage`, `recheck`, `OUTAGE_NOTE_TTL_MS`):** On a `live` spine, `SpineBanner.deriveSpineBanner` sets `action: 'dismiss-outage'` (`Dismiss` button in `ControlToolbar.SPINE_ACTIONS`). On a `feed partial` spine (whose action is `recheck`), clicking `Recheck` in `SpineAction.run` also clears `planeOutageNote` while re-driving the fleet, and `FleetAdmission.expireOutageNote` + `SpineBanner.formatShortOutage` automatically retire any note once `OUTAGE_NOTE_TTL_MS` (`300000` ms / 5 min) has elapsed so an un-dismissed note never lingers indefinitely or across days.
