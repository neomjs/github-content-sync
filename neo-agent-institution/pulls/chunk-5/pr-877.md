Resolves #839

🌿 *The Observatory's wells can no longer be read as unlabeled dust: each one carries its name on the canvas and a row in Wells.*

The Observatory names its density wells where they lie and gives them a section of their own:
- **Labels on the canvas.** The wire carries the wells. `ink()` labels them largest first at 11–15 px through the engine's label layer (neomjs/neo#20288), each sitting `lift` above its hub, never over it. Labels keep their ink under a selection, the lens or the heat: they name the geography, not the focus.
- **Wells apart from the halo.** Well nodes are sized from the wells' own count, and the halo's dust takes the softer `lineSoft` (`ground` added to `fmPalette`). The two are visibly different treatments in one frame.
- **A Wells section** (`ObservatoryWellsContainer`, `ObservatoryWellList`) sits between Team and Nodes and opens only when chosen. A row flies the camera to its well (`ObservatoryCanvas.flyTo`) without moving a node, and the row stays marked.
- **The wheel comes 10× closer** (operator's read, 11-05: *"zooming in is not enough"*). `zoom.min` goes 0.5 → 0.1 and `lod.near` 0.55 → 0.35: step (a) of Clio's zoom ladder on #312. Rendering nodes differently up close is neomjs/neo#20312.

Evidence: L2 (unit + the Observatory NL arms on 11-04 + visual goldens) → L4 required (AC-1: the operator names three wells on the installed build). Residual: AC-1, Residual-Owner: #485.
