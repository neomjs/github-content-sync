## Deltas from ticket
- **Two behaviors the arms surfaced** (ticket Fix 5 and 6, added to the body before this PR): `Container#onDockPaneReturn` reveals the inspector again when it comes home to its rail, and `CockpitPerspectives#revealsFromRail` keeps a vesseled inspector from being revealed. Without the second, a restore while away cached a placeholder under `detail`. On the return, `Rail#syncRevealPane` saw the same item id and kept the placeholder.
- **AC-3 corrected in the ticket:** as filed, it said an outside press ends the selection. #791's design and `FleetCockpitDrillNL` keep the selection.

The head stays intrinsic-height with no body overlap, which is what the receipt guards. A note for the views' design read: at 271 px, "Change id…" truncates and the handle wraps at its hyphen.

Before re-stamping, I design-read the six goldens that were already stale on dev, and all six are current now (Test Evidence).
