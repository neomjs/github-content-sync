Resolves #789

A selected Memories row now wears the roster card's selection language — the 8 % signal tint and the 24 % signal border mix — instead of a hard 2 px ring drawn by two mechanisms (a recolored border plus an inset shadow). The roster's outer selection shadow is deliberately not carried: the pooled grid cell's `overflow: hidden` clips it into faint side halos cut flat at the card's bottom, and a grid row is not a card on a surface, so the selected card and turn set `box-shadow: none`. A turn keeps its authored 2 px signal stripe on the left (`border-left-color: var(--fm-signal)`), which the border mix would otherwise repaint. The uneven top and bottom edges the operator saw did not reproduce in the fixture (the old ring was a uniform 2 px, the card 4 px inside its cell); with the ring gone there is no surface left for them.

Evidence: L3 (the visual suite: computed-style assertions red-first, goldens, a side-by-side read against a selected roster card) → L3 required. Residual: none; the operator's installed check is post-merge, Residual-Owner: #505.
