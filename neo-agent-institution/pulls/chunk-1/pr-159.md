Resolves #128

The roster drops its fixed 126 px row. `roster/List.mjs` sets `itemHeight: null` and `pluginAnimateConfig: {measureItemHeight: true, minItemWidth: 410}`, so the engine's Animate plugin measures the rendered cards and **every card takes the tallest one's height** (engine pin 11 carries that, neomjs/neo#18886). The calm roster goes from 126 px cards to 104 px cards, equal, and all three rows now fit the default shell — the third was clipped before.

Evidence: L3 (full visual runs and the Neural Link battery on this machine, every re-rendered golden read by eye) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-3). Residual: none.

agent-preflight: not hosted here; the baseline jobs run in CI. Declared class: `capability` → `feat` (the roster's height becomes content-driven).

## Deltas from ticket

- **AC-1 was reworded on the ticket.** Its first wording ("no reserve taller than the padding") predates @tobiu's ruling of 2026-09-18 that cards in a grid have the same height. The 30 px work-row reserve on unobserved seats is still there, deliberately: without it the whole roster re-pitches (≈ 74 → 104 px) the moment the first agent starts working. His call; one SCSS line either way.
- **Option (c)** — lifting the card surface against the pane ground — is not in this PR. After the captures I think the equal, tighter cards read as cards without it; also his call.
- `AgentCardSynthesisRenderNL.spec.mjs` hard-coded the old row (`10 + count × 136`) to size its capture. It now waits until every item carries ONE inline height and sizes the capture from the rendered column — the spec follows the geometry instead of restating it.
- The consumer-side deletion the engine PR named: the `126` anatomy constant and its docblock are gone.
