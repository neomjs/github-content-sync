Resolves #69

Related: #10 (parent), #63 / PR #71 (the same design pass, the activity rows), PR #70 (the witness battery this spec joins)

The empty fleet is the product's first-run moment, and its one affordance failed it twice: the "Add your first agent" CTA was the roster container's LAST vbox item after the `flex: 1` list, so an empty roster parked it at the pane bottom; and its label and plus glyph rendered in the theme's reversed button ink — the Engine colours `.neo-button-text` / `.neo-button-glyph` from `--button-text-color` / `--button-glyph-color`, which the SCSS's `color: var(--fm-ink)` on the button element never reaches. The list and the CTA now share one card-region box (the CTA centered over it on both axes — the design ruling's "real button in the card region", literally), and the CTA carries the family plate and ink through the button's own token channel in every state the Engine paints — rest, hover and press: background, border, label and glyph, fourteen tokens, no element-level paint left to lose on touch. A new Neural Link witness boots the cockpit against a zero-row Fleet roster and reads placement, then colour at rest, under hover and under press.

Evidence: L3 (the witness against the mounted cockpit, both themes; the two theme screenshots read) → L3 required (a first-paint rendering contract). Residual: none in scope. Named, not touched: in the light theme the app-shell title "Agent OS" is nearly invisible against the header (the shell's title colour, a different surface) — ledgered with the other design-pass residuals on #10 (https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-institution/issues/10#issuecomment-5501089367) so the disclosure outlives this body.
