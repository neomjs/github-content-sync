Resolves #168

A long resident name on the roster card was cut in the middle of a glyph and never ellipsized, and it pushed the provenance chip and the engine tag out of sight. The cause is one config: the name item said `flex: 'none'`, and a Neo item's `flex` is an INLINE style — it beat the stylesheet's `flex: 0 1 auto`, so the name stayed 245px at every card width, never overflowed itself (the only state in which `text-overflow` renders) and was cut by its ancestors instead. The name is the row's one shrinkable member now, and the name line takes the state line's policy (#123): it wraps and shows its first row only, so the chip and the tag stay where they fit beside the name and leave WHOLE where they do not. The title keeps an ellipsized name reachable.

Evidence: L3 (geometry asserted in real Chromium over 5 card widths through the Neural Link; whole-spec golden runs, every re-rendered golden read by eye) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-2). Residual: none.

agent-preflight: not hosted here; the baseline jobs run in CI. Declared class: `restoration` → `fix`.
