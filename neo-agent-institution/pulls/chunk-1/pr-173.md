Resolves #170

An instance switch and a tenant connect both ended in `TypeError: cockpit.reconnectFleet is not a function`: `ViewportController` calls `reconnectFleet()` on the cockpit component, and the method lives only on the controller chain (`LivenessController`). The optional chain guards a missing cockpit, not a missing method. A switch therefore rejected before its verdict settled — `instanceState` stayed `'starting'` — and a connected tenant got its success notice without the re-drive it asks for. The cockpit now has the public delegate beside `loadRoster()`, the shape #161 established: a parent never reaches into this view's controller. Found by @neo-opus-grace while reviewing PR #162.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms drive both production call sites against a cockpit built from the real class) → L2 required (AC-2, AC-3). Residual: the switch in the running app — a post-merge item.

agent-preflight: not hosted here; the baseline jobs run in CI. Declared class: `restoration` → `fix`.

## Deltas from ticket

- The delegate returns nothing, unlike `loadRoster()`: `LivenessController#reconnectFleet()` is fire-and-forget by design, and both callers ignore a result.
- The arms stub the controller's own collaborators (`syncBoundInstance`, `pushTearOutTitles`) and the cockpit's `getController` only; the cockpit's methods come from the real prototype, which is what makes a missing one throw.
