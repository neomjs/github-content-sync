The memories pane renders its session summaries and turns through engine grids, and the engine's data-table skin painted through the card design: the cell lattice (`border-bottom` / `border-right` / the first cell's `border-left`), the even-row stripe, the hover tint and the row-model selection band — a second frame around every card, ruled gaps that read as slabs, and a purple band behind the clicked card although the surface has no selection semantics (the drill fires from the card click). The head ran without gaps: the pane was authored for the pinned drawer whose host owned the rhythm, and in the docked south register (and the vessel window) no host supplies any. Both observations are the operator's dogfooding read of 2026-09-12; both verified against the render and the sources.

What lands: the registers block of `memories/Container.scss` (the block that already collapses the header toolbar) resets the engine chrome at the skin layer — the container frame, the cell lattice, the even-row stripe, the hover tint and the row-selection paint, the colors as the engine's own `--grid-*` custom properties re-bound per surface, the structural 1 px lattice and the cell padding overridden by a four-class rule (the engine's is three; per-class CSS load order is not a contract, specificity is). The pane root carries the panel rhythm the roster root declares too (`padding: var(--fm-space-4); gap: var(--fm-space-3)`), so the head, the meta line, the registers and the actions rail sit on one rhythm in both hosts. Coverage: a memories-pane golden in both skins at the 720 band with the rhythm and the register frame asserted on computed styles (red-first against the old CSS), and a grid-chrome arm in the memories Neural Link witness on the live cards (cell background, borders, padding; the engine-marked row paints no band).

Evidence: L2 (unit-free surface: the visual arm's computed-style assertions + goldens, the NL witness's chrome arm on real cards) → L2 required. Residual: the engine instantiates a `RowModel` for a null `selectionModel` (`grid.Body#beforeSetSelectionModel` → `ClassSystemUtil.beforeSetInstance(null, RowModel)`), so a consumer cannot opt out of selection at pin 7 — the mark's paint is neutralized here, its class stays. Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo#18626.
