## What

1. **Every drill line carries its label** (operator catch: an unlabeled headline next to labeled siblings reads as a mystery third thing) — `summary` / `response` / `prompt` / `thought` all labeled; nothing unlabeled remains.
2. **The turn title is the derived `miniSummary`** (operator direction + measurement: thoughts run 900–2,100 chars this session, responses have no upper bound — raw prose can never be the headline; backfilled miniSummaries measure 120–250 chars and read as genuine titles). The weighted title line is labeled `summary` and carries its **own provenance pill**: `derived` (backfill pipeline) — the drill-head's `authored` pill now explicitly covers only response/prompt/thought, so the provenance grammar does not blur upward. While a turn awaits backfill (`summaryFallback: true`), the pill reads `raw excerpt` and the line renders dim — honest truncation, never dressed as a derived summary.

Until it lands, the drill renders the labeled prose contract without the summary line — no placeholder.
