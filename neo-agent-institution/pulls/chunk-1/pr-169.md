Resolves #123

The roster card's state line had no fit policy: every member kept its full text, so the badge, the band and the telltale ran past the identity column and were cut mid-word at its edge, where the lifecycle verbs begin — not only at the narrow bands, but at the roster's own 410px column floor, where a single-axis telltale ended 47px past the line. The line is ONE row now, and the fit is the content's own: it wraps and shows its first row only, so a member the row cannot hold leaves WHOLE, from the end — never cut, never under the verbs. The item order is therefore the priority (dot · word · telltale · band · beacon words · badge): the exception follows the word and leaves last. Two members get a narrow form the stylesheet picks by the line's width: the telltale's axis initials (`w` · `t` · `w·t`) below a 508px line, the badge's number below 283px; text, title and aria keep the words in both forms.

Evidence: L3 (geometry asserted in real Chromium over 11 card widths × 7 rows through the Neural Link; full visual runs alone; every re-rendered golden read by eye) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-5). Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket

- The forms ride **data attributes** (`data-mark`, `data-count`) rendered by `::before`, not second spans: the element's text stays the whole phrase at every width, so text readers and the existing arms see one truth and only the stylesheet knows the form. The words retract with `font-size: 0` — the cockpit bar's own marks-with-titles idiom.
- The wrap is the layout's `wrap: 'wrap'` config and the line is `flex: 'none'`: the identity vbox stretched the line (inline `flex: 1 1 0%`), which would have defeated the one-row height.
- The state word's line box gives the first row its full height, so every card's name and state word now sit at ONE height: before, a card without a chip had a 10px line and its name sat 3px lower than its neighbour's. That is the whole diff of the three visual goldens.
- On 271–300px cards with two 44px verbs and a `rate-limited` word, even the two-axis mark leaves (the line is 95–124px; a one-axis mark stays from 289px). The ticket's Out of Scope names the cause: the verb pair.
- Noticed, not touched here: a long resident name is cut without an ellipsis at the narrow widths (identical in the old goldens) — filed and fixed as #168, stacked on this branch.
