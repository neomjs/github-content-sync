Resolves #182

A retired roster card now leaves with its vnode reference: `onStoreMutate` destroys it with `updateParentVdom` (silent — the store's `load` rebuilds right after), so the `li`'s stored vnode stops naming a component the registry no longer knows. The fresh card a returning key gets under the SAME id is then diffed against that node and patched — one card per `li` — instead of being inserted beside the old node. Measured live before the change: 18 cards for 9 records after the cockpit's first live admission (the seed's 9 same-key cards kept), 29 for 11 after an instance switch; after it: 9 for 9, and 11 seed cards with zero previous-instance cards after a switch on the combined head with #184. The roster container also takes its `store` as a CONFIG the way its list does — and owns what it creates (`ownedStores`: retired on replacement and on the grid's destroy; an injected instance is never touched; `null` stays `null`).

## Deltas from ticket
- The fix is one argument, not a pool redesign: the retirement's `destroy(true, true)` keeps the pool's "never re-key a card" rule intact and lets the engine's own `unlinkRetiredReferences` do the work.
- `roster/List.mjs` — `beforeSetStore` keeps the base instantiation contract and drops the base list's unconditional `oldValue?.destroy()`: this list owns no store, a re-seat only unbinds, so a provider-owned store replaced through the container survives.
- `roster/Container.mjs` — `beforeSetStore` instantiates a plain config (the container took an instance only; `value?.on` threw on a config) and the container OWNS what it creates: `ownedStores`, retired on replacement and on `destroy()`; an injected store is never destroyed by the grid, and the children (list, health bar) only unbind on a re-seat. Contract Ledger row on #182. Unit arm: config → owned → replacement → destroy → clean remount under the same fixed id; injected → survives.
- The unit tier cannot show the doubled node (no mounted vnode there): its arm pins the mechanism (the `destroy` contract); the component tier holds the DOM through a test-only writer driver (`rosterRefill.driver.mjs`, loaded into the App worker via `loadModule`, parameters in the module URL — the worker exposes no remote for a store mutation).
