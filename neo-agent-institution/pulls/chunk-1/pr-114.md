Resolves #21

The rail gains **System** — the connected instance's engine room, a keeper-view beside Fleet. It reads one thing: the `fleetDeploymentState` picture (neomjs/neo-agent-brain#314) the cockpit's liveness owner lands on the Viewport provider through the fleet wire, and renders it as plane cards (one per compose service: the folded status word, memory pressure, service class, restart churn, the container-health decision), three maintenance lanes (heavy-maintenance posture, the backup lane's retry phase with its reason codes, the snapshot's own freshness) and one head line naming which instance is diagnosed. Honest states are first-class: never answered says so, a stale picture keeps its cards and dates them, an unavailable or unsupported wire is one reason for the whole view, and the logs region names the Brain verb it is waiting for. Observe-only by declaration — no card carries a verb.

Every landed picture carries its provenance — the `profileId` of the bridge that answered it and `observedAt`, the reader's clock at landing. The view judges the held picture against the bound profile: a picture another instance answered is foreign and renders as nothing observed for this instance; a failed current read makes a same-scope picture *last known* rather than *current*, and its age (and every row's) keeps moving from the observation anchor instead of freezing at the wire's number — on every observation, and on the read owner's cadence tick while its reads hang at the cap (the view has no clock of its own; the owner is the clock).

Wire side: the twin `FLEET_WIRE_METHODS` gains `fleetDeploymentState`; `installFleetBridge` stamps `Error.fleetWireState` (the validated envelope state, so an older server's `unsupported-method` reads as an answer); `AgentOS.util.DeploymentStateRead` is the read owner's seam on the liveness fences and caps. The picture and its connection observation are declared leaf-complete on the **Viewport** provider, the owner's tick instant `systemTickAt` beside them (the sibling keeper-view binds all three) — a block declared `null` would stop the provider's leaf bubble on its first real answer, which the real-provider witness pins; the same leaf merge is why a landing always carries its stamp explicitly.

Evidence: L2 (unit — the real parent/child provider pair, the real bridge and loader including the instance-switch control, the System view under a Viewport-shaped provider with a pinned clock; a visual golden of the cold view rendered locally) → L3 required (AC-2's live-plane rendering needs the containers rebuilt with neomjs/neo-agent-brain#325, merged `dd2109f` and inert on the running plane until then).
