The cockpit declares itself. `panes` — one component config per stable dock item id: module, provider bindings, string handlers, `header.text` as the title, `autoHidden` on the rail tools — and `zones` — the SSOT §01 arrangement with its explicit node ids — replace the hand-built `CockpitDockDocument`, the eleven-case `resolveDockReference` switch and the `dockModel` seed in `construct()`. The engine lowers the declaration in `onAfterConstructed` (`Authoring.fromZones`), captures a config per pane with a runtime id, instantiates a pane the first time the projection needs it and parks one that leaves the tree (`retainDeclaredPane` rides `paneDeclarations`): the inspector keeps its instance across a perspective switch and a reveal/hide cycle, where the old path released the revealed pane on every restore and rebuilt it on the next reveal (measured in the 2026-09-12 demo). Parking is scoped to the rail (`getPreservedItemIds` → the inspector and the invoked tools): the south reading surfaces carry engine grids, and a grid remounted after its store emptied while the pane was parked repaints its previous row pool beside the new rows (defect-noted; `FleetMemoriesNL` measured store 1 / DOM 3), so those panes rematerialize from their seeds as before until the grid remount lands — the override names its retirement trigger. What stays the cockpit's, in `resolvePane` over the engine's declared-instance answer: the vessel stand-in (an owned or in-flight item is never stolen back by a restore) and the first-creation seeds (`seedPane`: the owner-held snapshots a pane reopens on, the selected resident, the inspector's stores, the shell-owned window toggles, and the listener scope a vesseled pane needs — a pane in its own window has no controller above it). The first projection is the LOWERED document's, made once after the engine seeded it (the cockpit is the app's main view; its resident panes exist at boot); the presets (`CockpitPresets.create(document)`) and the projected perspective list derive from that same document.
