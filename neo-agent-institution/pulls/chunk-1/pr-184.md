Resolves #181

Retained cockpit truth now belongs to the profile whose answer produced it. The roster and the activity feed record the answering `bridge.profileId` — the rule `DeploymentStateRead` already follows — and a read through a bridge bound elsewhere first retires the previous profile's rows: the roster returns to its seed through the store's own `load()`, the feed empties, the surface reads `sample`, the roster-derived consumers re-snapshot. The new profile's first live answer is then a first admission, and its failure never shows another instance's residents as `stale`; a same-profile failure keeps `stale`, unchanged. The seam is `apps/agentos/util/TargetBinding.mjs`. To keep the liveness owner under the 1000-line bar, the Brain-health read moved beside it as `util/BrainHealthRead.mjs` in the `DeploymentStateRead` shape (the controller keeps `loadBrainHealth` / `applyBrainHealth` as its handles): 993 → 960 lines.
