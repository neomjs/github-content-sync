# Contributors choose bounded help and keep independent review

Terminal predicate: An outside contributor can choose a bounded repair on the original PR, keep accurate credit, and receive independent approval of the resulting head; maintainer prerequisites reach a capable owner, each repository activates only against its receipts, and the finite trial read records a retain, narrow or stop decision with every residual owned.

## Problem scope

A contributor’s useful revision can still lead to another round for a small settled repair, or to repeated requests to absorb the project’s base-branch changes. The existing authorship rule allows mechanical maintenance but reserves content to the author; its review-count/discussion-size polish threshold measures cost rather than consent. Meanwhile a maintainer who helps can be missing from the author-family verdict, and an outside PR’s integration action can have no native seat to wake.

The evidence and selected design are [Discussion neo#21005](https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/21005), including [Ada’s independent step-back](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/21005#discussioncomment-19631613). Its census is observational, not a claim about what authors prefer. The actor-hour proxy was falsified by the published control; the stricter claim-before-work baseline is an author’s own issue comment before opening, not a responder reply.

This needs coordinated changes to the shared rules, public guidance, Brain approval/routing consumers and activated delivery. One PR cannot honestly deliver that outcome. The claim workflow and measurement remain Mnemosyne’s accepted stream under neomjs/neo#20892; this parent owns the assistance policy and its operational prerequisites. The existing neomjs/neo-agent-skills#14 moves governance enforcement and explicitly excludes policy rewrites in that migration; neomjs/neo-agent-skills#302 governs execution fidelity, a different outcome.

## Intended solution

**Choice and responsibility.** Approve a correct first PR; otherwise give one complete actionable review and let the author make the first meaningful content revision unless help is requested sooner. An optional invitation checkbox does not authorize an edit. The first content-assistance push on each PR requires an affirmative reply accepting the named scope; subsequent repairs stay within that agreement, and withdrawal/new intent stops it. A substantial replacement is not disguised as polish. Retarget a useful residual on the original PR before replacing it; acknowledge retained commits accurately and never invent merge credit.

**Independent acceptance.** A seat that pushes a content repair does not approve that PR. Its canonical family joins the author/disclosed families for the cross-family predicate and all relevant readers; incomplete evidence cannot certify independence. The existing reviewer-family demand budget, ordinary Round-2 contract, ticket authority and human-only merge gate remain intact.

**Executable maintenance.** Mechanical work has one correct form from named inputs, no author-intent choice and verified output. Base churn belongs to a maintainer where an authorized route works; at most one contributor rebase request is the ceiling. Merge base updates into the contributor’s existing branch without rewriting their commits. Record old/new heads and checks, hand back with `git pull --ff-only`, stop on local divergence, and check the repair after the next author push. Brain must actually route outside integration and head-bound CI classification; a content repair returns to an actionable author handback. A refused operation retains its named maintainer owner until a supported Task handoff is accepted and the result is verified.

**Time and evidence.** A dependency date needs an available author action and a named blocked dependency. It is at least three working days from the latest legitimate action-adding handback, stated with date/timezone; repeated unchanged actions do not reset it. Our waits do not count. Ordinary check-ins, agreed return dates and existing claim expiry remain distinct. Recommitment after release does not silently reset a date or displace an accepted continuation.

Offers, refusals, completed content pushes and release notices have stable, provenance-checked hidden records. Offers alone do not count as assists; author responses and actual commit/check outcomes remain separately evidenced. Changed dates/withdrawals are new comments. A push receipt preserves accepted scope plus the consent reply’s author, ID and timestamp, not just a deletable link. Contributor-facing receipts stay on GitHub; private Task access is not required.

**Delivery and activation.** Engine guidance starts first; Brain, Institution and Skills have explicit parity before their expanded policy activates. Every routable seat must demonstrate the required operation under current effective configuration, or routing must actually exclude unproven seats. A family representative or stored setting is insufficient. Branch integration also needs its actual PR/head receipt. Existing permission parity stays with neomjs/neo#20925, and the integration producer stays under neomjs/neo-agent-brain#759; native dependencies connect their necessary leaves.

Record activation per repository after source, published/loaded consumers and execution receipts. Announced dates on neomjs/neo#20119 and neomjs/neo#20828 stand. No E3 notification replays a pre-activation close. The E1 instruction backfill has its own frozen baseline and guarded-authority checks.

Sophie stewards this policy outcome and records its retain, narrow or stop decision on this parent after neomjs/neo#21024’s day-14 read, including the loaded-byte retention/retirement condition in neomjs/neo-agent-skills#399 AC4. Beneficiary acceptance is the actual scoped path in neomjs/neo-agent-skills#401 AC3, including credit and independent approval, or its explicit zero-uptake/unavailable-witness record. Zero uptake alone is not successful assistance; any stop/narrow closeout keeps residuals with owners and revalidation triggers. The [entry-seeded closeout matrix](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-skills/issues/398#issuecomment-6898418077) governs the later evidence reconciliation. Mnemosyne accepted the contributor-program workflow/parity and measurement stream. The trial’s day 14 starts at the first E1 backfill or first scoped assistance offer, whichever occurs first, even with zero uptake or an execution gate still blocked. Path-specific enable/use dates remain visible. E1’s effect review waits for 75 curated-issue openings after full coverage, with a forecast published when backfill ends and uncertainty reported; day 14 supplies no E1 effect verdict. The program read separates completion, return, effort, rebase requests, defects, objections, invitation-box behaviour and claim/release outcomes; it claims no causality from a small cohort. Unwanted edits, lost work, permission bypass or an attributable correctness regression stop the expansion pending correction and renewed decision.

## Out of scope

Changing badge rungs, claim limits/expiry, the first-approve-worthy rule, author competence thresholds, core merge authority, or the wider Brain identity-history architecture. No default content takeover, automatic body writer, permanent seat capability table, new top-level skill or approval-wrapper service. An existing permissions ruling is not a new grant.

## Avoided traps

- A checked template or edit-access flag substituted for scoped consent, or an offer counted as a completed assist.
- A rebase-method base update rewriting contributor commits before a promised `--ff-only` handback.
- A maintainer certifying content they added while their family is invisible.
- A documented default without a holder-to-wake path or executable route.
- A fixed read date that starts only after uptake and therefore never starts.
- Copying the Discussion into loaded skills; the policy implementation replaces existing text with measured near-net-zero load and base-aware lint.
- Closing this parent from a PR or from a count of closed children; beneficiary acceptance and the recorded trial decision are the closeout.
