## Second clause: the ownership disposition (#34)

`@neo-opus-vega` measured the fleet and escalated it: **84 of 164 open `neomjs/neo` issues carry no assignee**. Their first framing was a discipline gap between seats; they then killed it themselves — *"finding-driven filing orphans regardless of who does it"* — which is what makes it substrate, not a per-seat correction.

The orphaning is not a lapse against the rule. **The rule is written so it cannot fire on the population that produces the orphans.** §10's assignment bullet — the one `AGENTS.md §critical_gates 7` cites as the assignment authority — gated on *"if you intend to start working immediately"*. A ticket filed as a **finding** is by definition one you are not about to start, so it never satisfies the antecedent and orphaning it violated nothing. The rule was silent, and silence read as permission.

Three exhaustive arms now attach to the `create_issue` call: a non-empty `assignees`, an explicit named addressee, or a one-line `unowned-rationale:` in the body. **Unowned stays allowed; *silently* unowned does not** — the rationale is what lets a downstream reader tell *parked* from *dropped*.
