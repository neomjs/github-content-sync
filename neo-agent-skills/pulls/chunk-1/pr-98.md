Resolves #97

The external-contributor register rule already existed. `references/pr-review-guide.md` §1 has said *"For External/First-Time Contributors: Start with positive reinforcement. Acknowledge their effort"* for months. This PR is not a second copy of it — it is the slot it had nowhere to land in.

## The measurement

Five reviews, one contributor (`@sloemo01`), three PRs in `neomjs/neo` on 2026-09-19/20:

| PR | reviewer | template | literal "thank" | ecosystem entry point |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| `neomjs/neo#19017` | @neo-opus-grace | **micro** | ❌ | ❌ |

All three slot-bearing reviews open with a personal address. Neither slotless micro-review does. `assets/pr-review-template.md:21` carries `**Peer-Review Opening:**` with *"Thanks for putting this together!"* as its own example text; the micro asset was 603 bytes of `Class → Verdict → Glance → Findings`.

**What this sample can and cannot establish (corrected under RA-2).** Read the reviewer column: all three full-form reviews are @neo-opus-ada's and both micro-reviews are mine. Form and reviewer covary perfectly, so the table sizes the gap and cannot isolate its cause — "the missing slot caused the missing greeting" and "these two maintainers open differently" fit it equally well. The earlier framing here claimed the former. The slot stands as **the intervention being tried**, which is all the existing §1 register rule needs to justify it; no new experiment is required for this PR, and the honest test is the sunset condition below.

My `neomjs/neo#19017` review is still the sharpest single data point, whatever the sample can carry: submitted 25 minutes after reading the operator's correction, consciously intending to honour it, containing praise and a next-issue pointer and neither the words of thanks nor an entry point. Intent plus a fresh explicit instruction was not enough on its own.

## The routing inversion

§6.4 is emphatic that micro is *"a rule, not a permission — a MECHANICAL PR **gets** this shape… at ANY size"*. A curated `good first issue` is mechanical by construction: bounded, test-only or docs, no new abstraction. **So the better we scope a newcomer ticket, the more certainly its review lands on the one template with no person in it.** The routing rule is correct on its own axis; rigour-scaling and person-scaling were riding one switch. §6.4 now says so in one sentence.

## Substrate-Load Audit

`references/pr-review-guide.md` is IN-SCOPE for `turn-memory-pre-flight`, so its placement decision tree is recorded here rather than assumed.

- **Step 1 — universal, every turn?** No. The rule fires only when a review is being composed for an external contributor. It does not belong in `AGENTS.md`.
- **Step 2 — a specific lifecycle event or workflow?** **Yes, and the tree terminates here.** The event is PR review; the owning workflow is `pr-review`. Both edits land inside that skill — the guide it already loads and the asset it already emits. Steps 3–5 (edge-case atlas, harness-local, none-of-the-above) are therefore not reached and are inapplicable by construction, not by preference.
- **Harness-loader mutation:** none. The mechanical pre-flight commands (`.codex/hooks.json`, `codex-context.mjs`, `readlink .claude/CLAUDE.md`) exist to catch double-loading through a harness entry point. This diff edits no loader, no router, no manifest entry and no symlinked root file, so those probes have nothing to read here. Stating that explicitly rather than skipping them silently.
- **Load-effect (on-demand, not per-turn):** +1660 B against `4253d70`, paid only when a seat loads `pr-review` — which it does when it is already about to spend a review's worth of tokens. Nothing is added to the turn-loaded layer. The cost is symmetric in the wrong direction for maintainer PRs: omitting the `Opening` from an emitted review does **not** remove those bytes from the loaded template, which is exactly why the slot is one line and conditional rather than a section.
- **Sunset:** `#97` carries it — this slot retires when a body lint enforces the opening, or when a dedicated external-contributor review path lands, whichever is first.
