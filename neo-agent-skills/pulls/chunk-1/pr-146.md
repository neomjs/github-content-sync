Resolves #145

Sunset trigger 1 becomes "Context Exhaustion or Cost". The 75 % exhaustion arm is unchanged. The new cost arm is a rule of thumb: from about twice what a session holds after recovery, a peer may take a `solo-refresh` on its own — at a quiet point, when what comes next needs little of the context, and only if that seat has a verified way back without the operator. Those conditions bind the cost arm only. The router's always-loaded `description` names the real triggers instead of occasions. Derivation and measurements: #145.
