- it used `setData(..., {continuation: true})` as a scroll switch on a whole-corpus replacement;
- it rebuilt records from `toJSON()` to change one field;

The review passed the test app as plumbing, and the operator caught all three. The trigger already said "(any dir)", so the gap was the reviewer's reading, not the scope, and the clause names it.
