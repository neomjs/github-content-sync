Resolves #169

A reviewer with Required Actions and an unspent round now withdraws an approval by submitting `CHANGES_REQUESTED`: GitHub's own `reviewDecision` stops reporting the approval, the review counts as the family's round (guide §6.3), and an ordinary Round 2 clears it. The `[MERGE_HOLD]` / `[RE_REVIEW_HOLD]` token stays where no round is justified: the family's round is spent, or the head moved and no defect is established yet. A hold is not a round, so its clearing review takes the full template, the form neomjs/neo#19574 left undecidable.
