Resolves #438

🌿 *A review of release notes can no longer approve on facts alone.*

Release notes and posts now meet their skill on three paths that need no task recognition. A ticket for one names its skill on its first screen (ticket-create §1b). Intake never exempts that read, even on a self-authored ticket (the carve). A review of a diff under `.github/RELEASE_NOTES/**` or `learn/blog/**` loads the same bar (pr-review §7.5.6). `release-notes` now opens its bar on the impact questions and takes the blog bar's whole-artifact verdict, cold read and undersell flavors by pointer. Its truth rule becomes the blog's: anchors while drafting, one fact check at the final version. The blog cold read gains the share question.

Evidence: L1 (static contract: five reference texts; skill lint and the full skills suite) → L1 required (AC-1 to AC-6 are reference text). Residual: AC-7, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo#22265 (the 14.1 notes ticket, whose body will name `release-notes`).
