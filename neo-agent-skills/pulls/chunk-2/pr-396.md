Resolves #395

Step 8 of the session-sunset workflow now opens with a precondition: before the continuity ping, the session stops every background task it started, or lets it finish, and the ping says that none is left. Without it, a task that finishes after the ping re-invokes the sunsetted session beside the fresh one. That happened on 2026-11-15 (neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1739): Grace's sunset left three subagents running, and her woken old session then sent two handover amendments and a GitHub comment while its successor was already working.

Step 10 now also says why it follows Step 8: its save closes the receipt's turn, so it comes after the ping, and again after any addendum.

Evidence: L1 (static: the skill-corpus lint and the package tests pass; a procedure line has no runtime arm) → L4 required (AC-5: the next Claude sunset after background work shows no turn from its old session). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1739.
