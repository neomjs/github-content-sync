Resolves #231

🌿 Closing is a maintainer act now, and what can no longer close with it is an intent that is still valid.

The terminal verdicts in epic-resolution §4 now carry the reviewer's terminal action. A retire verdict closes the epic as not planned, with its superseding links, once every live clause has an owner; the epic's open subs re-parent or close the same way. Missing subs are filed by the owners who accept them. This removes the operator-only hedge, left over from the retracted §0-merge parallel (neomjs/neo#10698, then neomjs/neo#11102 and neomjs/neo#11229, which fixed only the completed close). The §5 template now names the terminal action taken. Every not-planned close of an issue or PR now runs an intent check. A changed architecture retires the prescription, not the intent, so the closing comment names each still-valid intent and its surviving home. Closing another author's artifact takes two agreeing peers. The authority is the operator's ruling of 2026-10-28, and the safeguard is the operator's idea, chosen and scoped here (#231 Context).

## Deltas from ticket

The intent check lives in `decision-substrate-sweeps.md`, not in `ticket-intake` §4 as first drafted, because the corpus lint refused `ticket-intake-workflow.md` at 25,127 B against its 25,000 B budget. Same-turn reconciliation is where `ticket-intake` §4 already sends closes, so the check fires on the same path. Two agreeing peers apply only to another author's artifact, where the closing risk sits; a universal two-peer rule would tax the frequent, reversible own closes. §6's close FYI drops "recommendation", since the review now closes.
