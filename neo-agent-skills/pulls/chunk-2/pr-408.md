Resolves #363

Adds a `README.md` opening skill-count guard to `scripts/lint-skill-corpus.mjs` (`§3c`) and six fixture cases to `scripts/test-lint-skill-corpus.mjs`. The check scans the opening above the first `## ` heading, requires both `<N> skills` and `<M> of the <N> skills`, verifies every matched denominator `<N>` against `skills.manifest.json` (`40`), rejects subset counts exceeding total skills, and emits file-and-line diagnostics (for example `README.md:25` and `README.md:27`).

Evidence: L1 (corpus lint + 52-case `test-lint-skill-corpus.mjs` + 23-suite `npm test` + version/archaeology guards) → L1 required (AC-1..AC-3 lint & test invariants).

Micro-review eligible: mechanical — single opening-count guard in `scripts/lint-skill-corpus.mjs` plus six boundary fixture arms in `scripts/test-lint-skill-corpus.mjs`.
