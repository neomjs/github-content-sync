Resolves #417

After a rebase or dev merge of an approved PR, the author posts the `git range-diff` as a delta note. The author requests no one unless reviewed content changed. This follows the operator's 2026-11-17 ruling: in the census on #417, none of the last 100 merged neo PRs drew changes requested after a rebase-only change. On an outside contributor's PR, any author push after approval re-enters review. `ci-green-review-routing.md` §4 carries the rule where the re-request is decided. `merge-hold-tokens.md` tells a reviewer that neither hold token applies to a maintainer's mechanical rebase. No public behavior change, no migration.
