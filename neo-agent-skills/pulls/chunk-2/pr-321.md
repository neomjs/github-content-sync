Resolves #320

The guide lint moves here from the Brain as `neo-agent-skills-guides`, plus a `Guides` step in the shared `PR baseline` job. The step is off unless a caller sets `guides: true`, and `guides_skip` names the rules to drop. A repository can now lint its `learn/` guides at its pinned baseline tag. Without this, it had to check out the Brain's `dev` (neomjs/neo#20597, under neomjs/neo#20328).

The step lives in `PR baseline`, not in the `guards` list. That job already installs this package's guards at the workflow's own tag, so the lint runs exactly the pinned release. A `guards` entry would need the bin in the consumer's own install, and the Engine pins 0.1.90.

The port keeps the Brain lint's rules and output, with three changes:
- the root defaults to the working directory, not the script's checkout;
- `--script-root` (repeatable) and `--openapi-root` make the data roots explicit. The Brain's `--root` mode used to merge its own scripts in silently;
- `--skip` drops a rule, and a name no rule answers to exits 2.

Evidence: L2 (contract suites, plus a parity run of the Brain lint and this port on the same trees) → L3 required for AC-4 (the published 1.2.0 manifest). Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo#20597.

**What this deletes downstream:** the Brain's 651-line `ai/scripts/lint/lint-guides.mjs` and its 338-line spec, which retire under a Brain ticket when the Brain moves to the bin. Also the Engine's `lint-guides.yml`, with its Brain checkout, under neomjs/neo#20597. Until then, this is the second copy.

## Deltas from ticket

- The contract suite also refuses a `--root`, `--script-root` or `--openapi-root` in the step, and a `${{ }}` in its shell. The skip list is the only narrowing the step accepts, and it comes from the caller's declared input.
- `skill-corpus.yml` gains the contract step and the tarball assertions: the bin ships, its test does not.
- **Every rule reads a guide in one linear pass** (`e54f994`). The guides are pull-request input, and the job judging them is a required context. CodeQL flagged three regular expressions that rescan from every repetition: the fence, the LR node id and the link pattern. Measuring found four more quadratic paths: a line number counted from the top of the file per finding, a declaration searched for in the whole prefix of every token, and inline code re-counted before every token. The Brain's lint carries all of them today.
- Reading fences by line also fixes an inherited off-by-one. With a blank line right after the fence, every finding in that block was reported one line early.

### 🔬 Depth Floor

- **Challenge**:

This preserves 1:1 parity with the Brain lint's `[^#]*$` regex while making the check linear (`O(N)`), and works for comma-separated Mermaid `classDef` declarations (`fill:#3498db,stroke:#282829,color:#1234` because `fill`/`stroke`/`color` repeat before each `#`). However, a multi-token CSS shorthand property with two all-numeric 4/6/8-digit hex colors and no intermediate property name (e.g., `scrollbar-color: #123456 #654321;` or `border-color: #1234 #5678;`) will have `segment === '123456 '` for the second token and flag `#654321` as `ticket-id`. Non-blocking (inherited grammar and realistic guide CSS uses alphanumeric hex or single-color declarations).
