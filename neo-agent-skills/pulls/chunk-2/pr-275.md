Resolves #274

Trigger 1 of the sunset skill no longer gates on an absolute token count. Exhaustion is over 75 % of the seat's own window, or context-pressure signals. Below that, continuing is the default, because a fresh session repays its recovery first. A `solo-refresh` stays the seat's call only at a quiet point before a semantically distant lane (or a wait past the prompt cache), and never before the operator has answered once after the opening prompt. The `clear_session` clause is gone: no seat clears its own session. The wake plane's 250k deferral is named as wake-delivery policy, never a sunset trigger. This is the operator's ruling of 2026-11-05, quoted on #274.

## Slot rationale (§1.1) and load effect

- **Trigger 1 (`session-sunset-workflow.md:18`): `rewrite`.**
  - The *Gate* arm, a fixed 200k/250k obligation, is retired.
  - *Exhaustion* returns to the window share #145 kept, read on the seat's own window.
  - *Cost* keeps #145's permission and adds the ruling's two conditions: semantic distance, and an operator answer.
  - It drops "an operator-free way back … only recommends (trigger 4)". Without a self-clear, that clause would send every Claude seat back to asking whether to sunset.
  - Loads only behind the skill's trigger. A miss costs the operator's channel plus a 130k+ re-recovery. DISCIPLINE-ONLY: a seat reads its own gauge.
- **Self-clear sentence (`:11`): `rewrite`,** from instruction to prohibition. MACHINE-ENFORCEABLE-CANDIDATE: a harness hook could refuse a self-clear. The skill was the instruction's only source, so this removes the cause.
- **`description` (router + manifest mirror): `rewrite`,** 203 → 189 B.
- **Load effect:** the Map (`SKILL.md`, loaded when the skill fires) shrinks 14 B, and the Atlas (`session-sunset-workflow.md`, read on a sunset) shrinks 121 B. No turn-loaded substrate changes.
