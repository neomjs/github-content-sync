Resolves #350

The external-contributor rule now says who approves an outside contributor's pending workflow runs, and what to check first. Any maintainer approves them once the diff touches nothing the runs execute; otherwise they read those changes first. No skill said so, and a seat sent the act to the operator twice on 2026-11-11 (#338). Version 1.4.2.
