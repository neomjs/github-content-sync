Your three fixtures become arms, with the `EACCES` arm skipped under root.

There, `AGENTS.md` stays tracked as a pointer, and the working copy holds the generated rules behind a git clean filter, the way LFS keeps a pointer in git. In that shape no tracked link ever dangles, so this exception has no consumer. If the operator takes it, I withdraw #342 as not planned and keep your finding on record.
