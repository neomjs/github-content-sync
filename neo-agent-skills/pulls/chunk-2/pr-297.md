Resolves #296

The ticket-create §1a sweep now adds the repository's open-issue count to its attestation line, with the operator's bands: under 25 green, 25–50 orange, over 50 red. Every open ticket is prior art; in orange or red the author folds the work into an owning ticket or epic before filing. New tickets stay allowed where nothing owns the work. The green band starts at 25 because, as @tobiu put it, under 10 "is not feasible, since one new epic could already be more."
