This gives the outbound reference a slot for what a post is for. `outbound-post.md` §0 "The Job" asks for six fills before drafting:
- the reader's action, and where its number shows;
- the link in view;
- a trailer instead of the article;
- the level in line one;
- an ending on the action;
- replies that link where their answer continues.
