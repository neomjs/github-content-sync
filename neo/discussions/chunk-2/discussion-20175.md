### 3. Concrete Falsifiers & Code Anchors for Option E (`src/gl/avatarShaders.mjs`)

       c += fibreResponse(n, v, normalize(uLightDir1), base.rgb, uHairParams) * uLightColor1;
       c += fibreResponse(n, v, normalize(uLightDir2), base.rgb, uHairParams) * uLightColor2;
