# Dock Layouts for multi-window dashboards: the benefits post, with the film

## Context

The operator's release-day goals (2026-10-11) ask for a new blog post that carries the 13.2 film, explains what Dock Layouts give a **multi-window dashboard**, shows screenshots, and gives code examples, in two stages: benefits first, implementation details later. This ticket is stage 1. Stage 2 gets its own ticket when this one merges.

The 13.2 post `learn/blog/a-workspace-that-can-leave-its-window.md` (`#9854`, 2026-10-09) already tells *why it works*: one SharedWorker heap, one Group transaction, the real-window war story. It has no film and one code sample. This post answers a different reader's question: **what does my dashboard's user get, and how little do I write to give it to them?**

## The Problem

- **Angle:** a dashboard user arranges the workspace the way their work is shaped, across screens, and nothing they arrange is lost. Panes tear out into real OS windows and come home as themselves, fold into edge rails, maximize, come back as a named perspective, and Undo walks all of it. The builder declares panes and zones; the dock owns the rest.
- **Audience:** cold readers who build data-dense dashboards (operations, monitoring, analytics, trading), developers and architects. No Neo knowledge assumed.
- **Sources to mine (nothing invented):** `learn/guides/uibuildingblocks/DockLayoutsFeatures.md` (what users can do, and what the dock refuses), `DockLayoutsAdoption.md`, `DockLayoutsWindows.md`, `DockLayoutsStateAndPersistence.md`, `learn/tutorials/DockLayoutsFirstLayout.md`, the Dock chapter of `.github/RELEASE_NOTES/v13.2.0.md` (`#14800`), the film (`#15252`), and the running dashboards: `examples/dashboard/choreography` (the portal's Dock Layouts card) and `apps/workstation`.

## The Architectural Reality

Docs only. The post lives in `learn/blog/<slug>.md` and is registered by hand in `apps/portal/resources/data/blog.json` (2026 node). The SEO surfaces are generated by the data-sync pipeline and stay untouched. Images are hosted in `neomjs/pages` under `resources_pub/website/blog/<slug>/` (precedent: `website/blog/jsonblueprints/`); release-note frames under `website/release-notes/v13.2.0/` are reused where they fit. A YouTube video embeds as a cover image linked to the video (precedent: `learn/blog/json-blueprints-and-shared-workers.md`).

## The Fix

One post: thesis, the dashboard user's problem, the film, what each dock feature gives that user (with screenshots of running dashboards), a short code taste copied from a live tutorial or example, honest bounds, one question and one next step. Deep code goes to stage 2.

I filed #19603 for the same post 42 seconds after this one and closed it as a duplicate. Two points from it that this body does not carry, as proposals for you to take or leave:

- **A screenshot that spans two OS windows.** The multi-window benefit is the one a single-window frame cannot show; one of the three screenshots could be a main window and a torn-out popup side by side, the pane live in the popup.
- **Name the bounds in the post.** The browser's popup permission (a tear-out needs it), and SharedWorkers are same-origin, so the windows of one app share one App Worker only on one origin.
