# Unify Dock and Neural Link undo on the group cursor

## Context

The Engine already exposes Neural Link begin/commit/undo/redo through a private `TransactionService` keyed by writer/session, while ordinary dock UI gestures bypass it. `DockService#buildDockReverse` creates one-holder snapshots and a second dock-side undo authority. D#18224 resolved that these must be migrated, not supplemented.

## The Problem

Two histories over one heap disagree under interleaving: writer A can undo an older whole-document snapshot over a newer human B mutation. A Group cursor must reverse B before A regardless of origin. Deleting the whole legacy sessions map in the first dock increment would also break non-dock commands before their compensation path exists.

## The Architectural Reality

All dock origins—human gesture, Workstation action, Neural Link dock operation, restore—target one Group. Neural Link commands select a Group and operate its latest cursor, not one writer's private latest entry. Non-dock command execution remains on the legacy service until migrated; mixed batches are refused.

## The Fix

Route dock begin/commit/undo/redo/list/save/replay into `manager.Transaction`; delete `DockService#buildDockReverse` and dock-side `recordUndo`; preserve non-dock sessions temporarily; return structured Group/cursor results through the existing tool surface. The delivered cut must be net-negative across replaced undo code.

## Ordinary popup admission

The all-dock-origin cut includes ordinary tear-out birth: admit a full Workspace and its semantic document through the Group claim before the first committed pop-out, then record that transition through the same Group history. A documentless reservation is provisional native-window admission only; it must not survive as the committed popup representation. Preserve the live pane subtree, compensate failed admission, and delete the replaced bare-vessel document/promotion ownership in the same cut. Placement consumes the admitted document in #18313; the complete user journey is proved in #18316. This supplies #18311's already-planned popup-as-Workspace prerequisite without changing its extraction scope.

## Accepted Group lifecycle boundary

Per D#18150 [step 3](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/18150#discussioncomment-18289325) and its [terminal mapping](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/18150#discussioncomment-18293826), this leaf accepts admissions, connection cache, vessel ownership and retirement directly into the settled Group. The `tearOutAdmissions`, `tearOutConnects`, `tearOutPanes` and `tearOutRetirements` state and corresponding lifecycle methods must leave Workspace with their teardown; forwarding wrappers or a renamed intermediate dock owner do not discharge the cut. #18311 retains pane-handoff choreography and the native identity witness. Transaction stays generic, with dock participant and native effect seams supplied by its consumers. Accepted by @neo-gpt as #18314 owner; implementation remains open until the deletion and behavior are proved.

The same cut owns the remaining Workstation `vesselWorkspaces` owner-lookup consolidation against Group participant identity, as required by #18318 AC-4. Full popup documents already alias their Workspace owner; the remaining lookup must not become a second participant-membership authority. Euclid retains final consumer integration alongside Emmy's NativeLifecycle contribution. This is accepted work, not a completed acceptance claim.
