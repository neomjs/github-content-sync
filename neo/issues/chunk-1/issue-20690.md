# The examples section opens on families, not on a build

## Context

The 13.5 gate asks that a stranger finds each component family's examples as an overview in which every example names what it proves (`ROADMAP.md`). #20500 delivered the first family as a fifth tab beside four build tabs; its review announced the next step for the second family: families become the navigation and the build a selector. The steward's map on #20498 ([comment](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20498#issuecomment-6757406109)) carries the decision, the family table and the counts. This leaf is the structure every family leaf waits for.

## The Problem

Measured on dev `62f75736ba` in a real Chromium at 1440×900: `/examples` opens on the tab `dist/prod` and shows 44 cards in one column, two on the first screen. The tab bar reads `dist/prod`, `dist/esm`, `dist/dev`, `Dev Mode`, `Grid & Table`: four answers to "which build" before the one answer to "what can it do". Five more families (116 demonstrations under `examples/`, 103 of them without a catalog record) have no place to go. Adding each as one more hand-written tab would also collide: the tab list lives in three places that are kept in step by hand, so parallel family pull requests would edit the same lines.

## The Architectural Reality

- `apps/portal/view/examples/TabContainer.mjs:43-92`: five items. Four differ only in `catalogMode` and `environment`; the fifth filters the same store by `family` (lines 82-86).
- `apps/portal/view/examples/TabContainerController.mjs:18-21` routes `/examples` and `/examples/{itemId}`; line 29 `tabItems` mirrors the item order by hand; lines 46-57 resolve a list by a reference naming convention and default to `dist_prod`.
- `apps/portal/view/examples/List.mjs:35` `catalogMode` and `:41` `environment` are non-reactive and encode the same build twice; lines 125-128: the cover fallback hardcodes `fa-table`.
- `resources/scss/src/apps/portal/examples/List.scss:286`: the three-column captioned layout is keyed to `.portal-examples-grid-overview`.
- `apps/portal/model/Example.mjs`: `family` is one string; nothing marks a showcase record.
- `Portal.view.shared.TabContainer` gives the section its left tab bar with `Neo.tab.plugin.Overflow`; the News section carries seven tabs in it.
- Specs: `test/playwright/unit/apps/portal/view/examples/TabContainerController.spec.mjs` (tab order, 46 identities, the 21 family identities, the established card order) and `test/playwright/e2e/portal/ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs` (the four mode selections).
- Design authority: "One overview page per family on the portal at the kitchen-sink standard" (#20498, Intended solution shape 1).

## The Fix

1. **Tabs are Featured plus the families that have records**, in this order: Featured, Grid & Table, Forms, Multi-Window, Calendar, Components, State & Data. Title, route, icon and order of a family come from one place. A family without records renders no tab, and its route opens Featured.
2. **Featured is the established showcase:** the 27 records that are not family-only (`workstation` through `liquid-glass`, and `dock-choreography`), marked on the record, in catalog order, in today's one-column layout.
3. **Every family tab uses the captioned three-column layout of `/examples/grid`.** The cover fallback shows the family's icon.
4. **The build becomes one control at the foot of the tab bar** (production by default; ESM, the development build, dev mode). It sets the build for every list: `catalogMode` becomes reactive and `environment` derives from it. `/examples/dist_prod`, `/examples/dist_esm`, `/examples/dist_dev` and `/examples/devmode` keep resolving: Featured in that build.
5. **The three featured records without a home family get their captions here:** `devindex`, `covid-dashboard`, `portal`.
6. **The gate becomes a spec:** a record with a `family` and without `feature` or `lookFor` fails the unit spec.

Removed by this change: the four hand-written build tabs, the hand-kept `tabItems` mirror and the duplicate `environment` config.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] On dev the tab bar reads Featured, Grid & Table. `/examples/forms` opens Featured until Forms has records.
- [ ] One control selects the build for every list, production by default; no build tab remains; the four mode routes resolve as the ledger says.

- [ ] `devindex`, `covid-dashboard` and `portal` carry `feature` and `lookFor`, each checked in the running application.
- [ ] Unit: a family record without `feature` or `lookFor` fails; the tab order has one source.

- [ ] Post-merge, not this pull request: the family routes enter `seo.json` and the sitemap with #20688.

## Out of Scope

New family records and their captions (the family leaves under #20498); covers (#20679); a search field or grouping inside a family; the home's cards linking to families (#20682); sitemap and `seo.json` entries.

## Avoided Traps

- One hand-written tab item per family in `TabContainer.items`, `tabItems` and the spec: five parallel family pull requests would collide on the same lines.
- Dropping the build modes: maintainers verify builds through them. They move, they do not go.
- A new page or card design for families: `/examples/grid` is the standard and the section's tab bar stays.
- Changing the showcase order: it is delivered intent (#19640, #19947), and the review of #20675 restored it once.
