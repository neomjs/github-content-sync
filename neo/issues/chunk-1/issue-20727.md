## The Problem

Measured on `dev` at `56a333fbac`:

1. **Populated `/examples/<family>` routes are absent from `apps/portal/resources/data/seo.json`, `sitemap-core.xml`, `llms.txt`, and `getContentRouteObjects()`:**
   - `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json` currently has `21` published records with `family: 'grid-table'` (making `/examples/grid` a live published family tab), and subsequent leaves under #20498 will populate `forms`, `multi-window`, `calendar`, `components`, and `state`.
   - Neither `buildScripts/docs/seo/seoJson.mjs#getSeoJson` nor `buildScripts/docs/seo/generate.mjs#collectAllRoutes` reads `familyDefinitions` and `examples.json`, so `/examples/grid` (and future populated `/examples/<family>` routes) has no entry in `seo.json`, `sitemap-core.xml`, `llms.txt`, or `getContentRouteObjects()`.
2. **Unpopulated `/examples/<family>` routes and legacy build routes emit self-referential canonical URLs in `Portal.service.Seo` instead of canonicalizing to `/examples`:**
   - When a user or crawler visits `/#/examples/forms` (while `forms` has 0 records in `examples.json`) or `/#/examples/dist_prod`, `TabContainerController#onExamplesRoute` (`apps/portal/view/examples/TabContainerController.mjs:88–92`) falls back to the `/examples` (`Featured`) tab.
   - However, `Portal.service.Seo#getMetadata` (`apps/portal/service/Seo.mjs:99–113`) pops path segments to `lookupRoute === '/examples'` and returns `route: normalizedRoute` (`'/examples/forms'` or `'/examples/dist_prod'`), causing `#updateDocumentHead` to set `<link rel="canonical" href="https://neomjs.com/examples/forms">` and `og:url` to the unpopulated/legacy URL rather than `https://neomjs.com/examples`.

## The Architectural Reality

- `apps/portal/view/examples/TabContainer.mjs:40–48` (`familyDefinitions`) is the single ordered source of truth for family identity (`family`), title (`title`), and route (`route`). Because `TabContainer.mjs` imports UI classes and calls `Neo.setupClass`, Node build scripts (`buildScripts/docs/seo/seoJson.mjs`) can extract `familyDefinitions` directly from `TabContainer.mjs`'s AST via `acorn` (already a `devDependency` used by `buildScripts/util/check-*.mjs`) without duplicating the table or booting the runtime.
- Pairing `familyDefinitions` (`definition.family !== null`) with non-hidden records in `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json` (`!record.hidden && record.family === definition.family`) determines which `/examples/<family>` routes are currently published, so adding records for `forms`, `multi-window`, `calendar`, `components`, or `state` under #20498 automatically adds their `seo.json`, `sitemap-core.xml`, `llms.txt`, and `getContentRouteObjects()` entries on regeneration.
- In `Portal.service.Seo#getMetadata`, returning `route: '/examples'` when a nested `/examples/*` route falls back to `lookupRoute === '/examples'` aligns the browser's `<link rel="canonical">` and `og:url` (`https://neomjs.com/examples`) with `TabContainerController#onExamplesRoute`'s Featured tab fallback while preserving `route: normalizedRoute` for other section fallbacks (such as `/news/tickets/123`).

## The Fix

1. **Derive published `/examples/<family>` metadata in `buildScripts/docs/seo/seoJson.mjs` (`extractExampleFamilyDefinitions`, `getPublishedExampleFamilies`, + `getSeoJson`) and regenerate `apps/portal/resources/data/seo.json`:**
   - Parse `familyDefinitions` from `apps/portal/view/examples/TabContainer.mjs` via `acorn` (throwing on missing, non-array, or non-literal/incomplete AST entries, and accepting `examplesPath`, `examplesTabContainerPath`, and `familyDefinitions` options for isolated unit-test fixtures) and match each non-Featured family (`definition.family !== null`) against non-hidden records in `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json`.
   - Exclude `route.startsWith('/examples/')` from `existingSeo` preservation in `getSeoJson` so unpopulated or withdrawn family routes never linger, and emit `{title: 'Neo.mjs ${definition.title} Examples', description, schemaType: 'CollectionPage'}` for every family with `records.length > 0`.
   - Regenerate `apps/portal/resources/data/seo.json` (adding `/examples/grid`).
2. **Include published `/examples/<family>` routes via `collectExampleFamilyRoutes` (`buildScripts/docs/seo/seoJson.mjs`, re-exported and consumed by `buildScripts/docs/seo/generate.mjs`):**
   - Emit `{category: 'example-family', filePath: examplesPath, id: definition.route, name: '${definition.title} Examples'}` for each populated family route so `getContentRoutes` (`/#/examples/grid`), `getContentRouteObjects` (`{route: '/examples/grid', clientSideRoute: '/#/examples/grid'}`), `getSitemapXml` (`https://neomjs.com/examples/grid` at `<priority>0.9</priority>`, dated by `examples.json`), and `getLlmsTxt` (listed under `## Demo Apps and Examples`) stay synchronized with `examples.json`.
3. **Canonicalize fallback `/examples/*` sub-routes to `/examples` in `Portal.service.Seo#getMetadata` (`apps/portal/service/Seo.mjs`):**
   - When an unlisted `/examples/*` route falls back to `lookupRoute === '/examples'`, return `route: '/examples'` so `#updateDocumentHead` sets `canonicalUrl` and `og:url` to `'https://neomjs.com/examples'`.
4. **Add unit test coverage in `test/playwright/unit/buildScripts/docs/seo/Generate.spec.mjs` and `test/playwright/unit/apps/portal/service/Seo.spec.mjs`:**
   - Verify populated vs unpopulated family handling in `getSeoJson`, `getContentRoutes`, `getContentRouteObjects`, `getSitemapXml`, `getLlmsTxt`, and `Portal.service.Seo`.

## Out of Scope

- Authoring new catalog records for `forms`, `multi-window`, `calendar`, `components`, or `state` in `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json` (owned by the family leaves under #20498).
- Middleware route table and bot fallback for `/examples/<family>` (delivered in `neomjs/middleware-v2#59` / `PR #60`).

## Avoided Traps

- **Duplicating `familyDefinitions` in `buildScripts/docs/seo/`:** Hardcoding a second copy of the family list would violate #20690 `AC-6` (*"the tab order has one source"*) and drift whenever `TabContainer.mjs` is updated.
- **Emitting `sitemap-core.xml` or `seo.json` entries for empty families (`0` records):** Empty families render no tab and fall back to Featured (`/examples`); indexing them before their records land would create soft-404 / duplicate pages in Search Console.
