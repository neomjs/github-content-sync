# Updates under a yielded ancestor wait on a flight they never touch

## Context

Design authority: `learn/guides/fundamentals/AsymmetricUpdates.md`, Best Practices: "When to use Independent Updates (Default) … **Performance Isolation**: When one component is very heavy (e.g., a Data Grid) and you don't want to block the UI of lighter components." The same guide scopes merging to a parent and child "both updating at the same time".

This is the result of a post-merge challenge of PR `#20743` (`#20741`), asked for by the operator on 2026-11-11. That PR sets `needsVdomUpdate = true` when `VdomLifecycle#updateVdom` yields. I ran one counting spec, unchanged, on the PR's parent `c7e1974483` (base) and on its merge commit `5f407d6956` (head), in the unit harness. A batch is one `VdomHelper.updateBatch` call.

## The Problem

**What `#20743` improves, measured. Keep it.**

| Scenario | base | head |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| S1: an item moves across sibling containers while a third sibling is in flight, then both containers update | 4 batches, 5 deltas, `removeNode` + `insertNode` | 2 batches, 4 deltas, one `moveNode` |
| S3: a child asks again during its flight, its parent and a sibling update | 4 batches, 4 payloads, 4 deltas | 2 batches, 4 payloads, 4 deltas |
| S4: ten rounds of the same | 30 batches, 30 deltas | 20 batches, 30 deltas |
| S9: two children update back to back with overlapping flights; the parent asks for one depth-1 update | lands after 152 ms, when the test stops the children: it starves | lands after 0.6 to 1.9 ms |
| Grid with content-sized rows, mount to rest | 18 batches, 115 deltas, 40 cells measured | 9 batches, 104 deltas, 70 cells measured |

**What it costs.**

| Scenario | base | head |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| S2: a parent asks for a depth-1 update while one leaf is in flight; five other leaves then update | 7 batches; the five land at 3 ms, while the first flight is still in the air | 2 batches, same 7 payloads and 7 deltas; none of the five lands before the first flight does (19 ms with a 16 ms hold) |
| S8: the first flight never settles; the root asks for one depth-1 update; a sibling, a cousin two levels down another branch, and that branch update | all three land | none lands; all three sit in `VDomUpdate.mergedCallbackMap` under the root |
| S7: `await root.set({})` on a yielded component | settles at 1.3 ms | settles at 17.8 ms, with the deferred flight |
| Ten renders of a covering ancestor that change nothing in a content-sized grid | 0 measure passes | 10 passes of `measureRowHeights` and `measureAheadRows`, all in memory: no main-thread call, no batch |

The blocking flight failing (S5) or its component being removed (S6) releases the parent on both trees; no flag stays set.

1. **Head-of-line blocking.** Once any ancestor has asked for an update while any descendant is in flight, every later update below that ancestor boards its batch and waits for that flight. The updates share no DOM node with it.
2. **A wider freeze on a wedge.** A flight whose reply never comes used to stop one component and its ancestors' own updates. Now it stops the whole subtree of every ancestor that asks for an update, up to the window. The watchdog reports it after 5 s and releases nothing.
3. **`set()` settles later** on a yielded component, even when it changed nothing that renders.
4. **No test holds any of this**, in either direction. The starvation of S9, which `#20743` fixes, has no spec either.

## The Architectural Reality

- `VDomUpdate#registerInFlightUpdate` registers a flight on every ancestor, and `VdomLifecycle#isChildUpdating` yields on any of them. There is no depth check.
- Since `#20743` a yielded component carries `needsVdomUpdate = true`. `mergeIntoParentUpdate` then takes every descendant: `canMergeUpdate` returns `true` for any depth and distance.
- The unconditional yield is load-bearing. `Container#afterSetNeedsVdomUpdate(false)` clears every descendant's flag when the container's payload is built, at any depth. A scratch prototype that let a depth-1 parent fly beside a flight it does not collide with lost a child's second request that way: in S3 the second text never rendered.
- The wedge watchdog in `VDomUpdate` comes from `#12946`. Its ticket named a recovery hook; what ships is the report.
- `Abstract#set` and `layout.Base#set` return `promiseUpdate()` whenever the flag is set.
- `grid.View#afterVnodeSync` guards with `deltas &&`. `executeVdomUpdate` hands `syncVnodeTree` an array for every update render (`response.deltas || []`), so the guard only tells a first render apart.
- The guide was not touched by `#20743`.

## The Fix

Outcomes; the mechanism belongs to intake.

1. **A flight that the watchdog reports parks no other component.** A candidate that fails no flight: when the watchdog fires, the ancestors that wait on it drop their flag and their parked descendants update on their own, as they did before `#20743`. A late reply then still finds its waiters.
2. **Specs for both sides of the trade:** S9 (the parent does not starve), S8 (after the watchdog, the others land), S2 (what waits and for how long, as the contract of record), S7 (when `set()` settles), and an operation budget for the content-sized grid's mount (batches, deltas, measured cells).
3. **The guide says what a yield does** to the updates below it.
4. **`grid.View#afterVnodeSync`** loses the dead guard or reads the deltas it is given.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] With a flight that never settles, updates of components that do not collide with it land again once the watchdog has reported it; unit-tested with a shortened `watchdogThreshold`.
- [ ] A unit spec holds S9: one parent update lands while two children update with overlapping flights.
- [ ] Unit specs hold S2 and S7 as written contracts, with the numbers a reader needs (what lands before the blocking flight, what after).
- [ ] A unit spec holds the content-sized grid's mount budget: batches, deltas and measured cells, with the final row geometry.
- [ ] `learn/guides/fundamentals/AsymmetricUpdates.md` describes the yield: which updates board the waiting ancestor's batch, and that isolation ends there until it departs.
- [ ] `grid.View#afterVnodeSync` has no guard that is always true.
- [ ] *(Browser)* One E2E arm shows S8 across the worker round trip, or the body says why the unit harness is the ceiling.

## Out of Scope

- Reverting `#20743`. It fixes a split move that is red in the browser on its base, and the starvation of S9.
- The larger question: should a depth-1 parent yield behind a flight it does not collide with at all. Answering yes to isolation needs scope-aware flag clearing in `Container#afterSetNeedsVdomUpdate` first. That is a design question for the operator or a sandbox, not this leaf.
- Failing a wedged flight from the watchdog, with a late reply to discard.

## Avoided Traps

- **Merging only what collides with the waiting ancestor:** it restores isolation and brings back the starvation of S9 for a depth-1 parent.
- **Yielding only on a collision, today:** it loses updates through the flag clearing above (prototype, S3).
- **Reading "fewer batches" as "cheaper for the user":** S2 sends the same payloads in fewer messages and lands five of them later.
