# List the five 2026 Medium stories in the Portal's Medium tab

## Context

The Portal's News section has a Medium tab fed by `apps/portal/resources/data/medium_blog.json`. The operator on 2026-11-10, after publishing the fifth Medium story of this autumn: "we also ignored apps/portal/resources/data/medium_blog.json quite a bit."

## The Problem

The file's newest entry is id 73, dated Nov 20, 2025, and the file last changed on 2026-01-13. The author's Medium profile lists five stories after that entry, read on 2026-11-10. A visitor of the Medium tab sees a list that ends a year ago, while the newest pieces are the ones we send readers to.

| Published | Title | Publication |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 12, 2026 | Multi-window web apps without syncing state | ITNEXT |
| Oct 31, 2026 | The main thread is the wrong place for a data grid | ITNEXT |
| Nov 05, 2026 | Persistent memory for AI coding agents: no prompt stuffing | ITNEXT |
| Nov 07, 2026 | Context compaction recovery for AI coding agents: six checks, not a summary | ITNEXT |
| Nov 10, 2026 | JSON.stringify is not safe inside a script tag: the JSON-LD XSS and its one-line fix | none at the time of reading |

## The Architectural Reality

- `apps/portal/resources/data/medium_blog.json`: 73 entries, newest first, each with `author`, `authorImage`, `date` (`Mon DD, YYYY`), `id`, `image`, `name`, `provider`, `publisher`, `selectedInto`, `type`, `url`.
- `apps/portal/view/news/medium/List.mjs` builds a card's image as `<imageBasePath>/blog/<record.image>` and the author image as `<imageBasePath>/blogAuthor/<record.authorImage>`, with `imageBasePath` pointing at `neomjs/pages` `main`. A path with a folder therefore works as `image`.
- All five covers are already in `neomjs/pages` under `resources_pub/website/blog/`: `multi-window-dashboards/desk.png`, `grid-off-the-main-thread/devindex-dark.png`, `persistent-memory-for-ai-coding-agents/index-and-archive.png`, `context-compaction-recovery-for-ai-coding-agents/summary-checks-ledger.png`, `json-ld-xss-json-stringify-in-a-script-tag/one-element-three-nodes.png`.
- The card crops its image from the centre and lays title and author over the bottom left (blog guide §5, "Portal Medium card").
- #9852 (closed as not planned) concerned copying old Medium posts into `learn/blog`; its closing comment keeps the Medium index as the archive. Nothing there excludes new entries.
- Design authority: n/a — calls no existing behavior wrong; the list is data that was not kept up.

## The Fix

Add five entries, ids 74 to 78, at the top of the file in the existing shape. `author` and `authorImage` follow the file's convention, the Medium account that published the story, as entry 73 does. `url` takes the share links the operator supplied.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: the file parses and holds 78 entries; the five new ones come first, newest first, with every field of the existing shape.
- [ ] AC-2: each new entry's title, date and publication match the story on Medium, and its `url` opens that story.
- [ ] AC-3: each new card's image loads on the Medium tab, and the receipt shows the five cards at desktop and at phone width, with what the crop leaves of each cover.
- [ ] AC-4: the 73 existing entries are unchanged.

## Out of Scope

- Whom a card names as author when an AI maintainer wrote the story. Each story's byline names its writer; the card's author field follows the Medium account today, and changing that is a design decision for the whole list.
- A generator or sync for this file.
- The empty `publisher` of entry 73.
