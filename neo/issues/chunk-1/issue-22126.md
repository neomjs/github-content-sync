# Lock scheduler event resize gestures to their row and time axis

Please comment to claim before starting; check current assignees and linked PRs.

## Context

Observed by `@tobiu` during manual `14.0.0` scheduler testing (`2026-11-24`): grabbing an event's left or right resize handle (`.neo-resizable-start` / `.neo-resizable-end`) allows moving the floating preview vertically across rows and switching the booking's `resourceId` inside the same resize gesture. While both mutations apply cleanly without throwing, coupling cross-row reassignment into a 1D edge-resize handle produces an unfamiliar UX where natural vertical pointer drift silently moves an event onto a neighboring resource row.

## The Problem

Whole-bar drag (`resizeEdge: null`) and edge-handle resize (`resizeEdge: 'start' | 'end'`) share a single `EventDragZone` pipeline. Once `#20907` (`32fb6cb32e`) enabled `useProxy: true` by default on `Neo.scheduler.Container`, two leaky behaviors surfaced during handle resizes:

1. **Visual vertical proxy drift:** The floating `.neo-scheduler-drag-preview` tracks `data.clientY` vertically instead of staying anchored to the resized bar's row.
2. **Cross-row `resourceId` reassignment during resize:** Vertical pointer wobble across a row boundary resolves the hovered `.neo-grid-row`'s resource and commits `updates.resourceId` on `mouseup` alongside the new `startDate`/`endDate`.

## The Architectural Reality

- `Neo.scheduler.EventDragZone#getDragProxyStyle` (`src/scheduler/EventDragZone.mjs:87-91`) unconditionally computes `top: `${(data.clientY ?? 0) - (this.offsetY || 0)}px``, even when `this.resizeEdge` is `'start'` or `'end'`. By contrast, `Neo.gantt.EventDragZone#getDragProxyStyle` (`src/gantt/EventDragZone.mjs:225-227`) pins `top: `${this.dragElementRect.top}px``.
- `Neo.timeline.draggable.EventDragZone#dragMove` (`src/timeline/draggable/EventDragZone.mjs:298-300`) calls `this.resolveTarget(data)` unconditionally, `#datesFor` (`src/timeline/draggable/EventDragZone.mjs:186`) adopts `target?.resourceId` inside the `resizeEdge === 'start'` and `resizeEdge === 'end'` branches (`:190-205`), and `#dragEnd` (`:244-246`) commits `updates.resourceId = pending.resourceId`.
- `Neo.timeline.draggable.EventDragZone#getMainThreadConfigs` (`src/timeline/draggable/EventDragZone.mjs:443-453`) sends unconditional `moveHorizontal: true, moveVertical: true` instead of locking the cross-axis when `this.resizeEdge` is active (compare `Neo.calendar.view.week.EventDragZone`, `src/calendar/view/week/EventDragZone.mjs:84`, and `#2250` limiting resize handles to their own column).
- Design authority: operator UX confirmation during `14.0.0` manual testing (`2026-11-24`) + sibling precedent in `Neo.gantt.EventDragZone#getDragProxyStyle` (`src/gantt/EventDragZone.mjs:227`) and Calendar Week column-locked resize (`#2250`).

## The Fix

Constrain resize-handle gestures (`Boolean(this.resizeEdge)`) to 1D resizing along the active `orientation` while keeping whole-bar moves (`resizeEdge: null`) free to reschedule and switch resource rows:

1. **`src/timeline/draggable/EventDragZone.mjs`:**
   - When `this.resizeEdge` is `'start'` or `'end'`, preserve `snap.resourceId` in `datesFor` (and skip cross-row `resolveTarget(data)` in `dragMove`).
   - In `getMainThreadConfigs()`, lock the perpendicular axis during resize (`moveHorizontal: me.orientation === 'horizontal' || !me.resizeEdge`, `moveVertical: me.orientation === 'vertical' || !me.resizeEdge`).
2. **`src/scheduler/EventDragZone.mjs`:**
   - In `getDragProxyStyle(next, data)`, when `this.resizeEdge` is set, pin `top: `${this.dragElementRect.top}px`` (falling back to `(data.clientY ?? 0) - (this.offsetY || 0)` only for whole-bar moves or synthetic unit calls without `dragElementRect`).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Dragging `.neo-resizable-start` or `.neo-resizable-end` horizontally while moving the pointer vertically across neighboring `.neo-grid-row` tracks keeps the floating `.neo-dragproxy` pinned to the event's own row `top` and preserves the event's original `resourceId` both mid-gesture (`dragMove` preview) and on `mouseup` (`eventChange` / store record).
- [ ] Dragging the bar body (`resizeEdge === null`) across rows continues to move the preview vertically and reassign `resourceId` as before.
- [ ] Unit tests in `test/playwright/unit/timeline/EventDragZone.spec.mjs` and `test/playwright/unit/scheduler/Container.spec.mjs` cover `resizeEdge` cross-row pointer payloads, proxy `top` pinning, and perpendicular `getMainThreadConfigs` flags.
- [ ] Real-pointer E2E in `test/playwright/e2e/scheduler/DragAndResize.spec.mjs` (failing on base before the fix) pulls a resize handle diagonally into another van's row and verifies both mid-gesture status/proxy position and committed `resourceId` stay on the original van.

## Out of Scope

- Adding a new config to re-enable diagonal resize-plus-row-switch (no consumer use case identified).
- Changes to `Neo.gantt.EventDragZone` (already pins `top` and does not reassign task rows on resize) or `Neo.calendar.view.week.EventDragZone`.

## Avoided Traps

- **Filtering `updates.resourceId` only in `dragEnd`:** Leaving `resolveTarget` and `clientY` active during `dragMove` would still show the floating preview and live status bar (`examples/scheduler/dragAndResize/Viewport.mjs:368`) jumping onto neighboring vans mid-resize, then snapping back on release. Lock both preview geometry and candidate `resourceId` from `dragStart`/`dragMove` onward.
