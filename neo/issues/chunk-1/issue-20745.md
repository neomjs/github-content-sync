# A keyboard cannot expand or collapse a row of a tree grid

## Context

A grid whose first column is a tree column presents itself as a tree grid: the grid carries `role="treegrid"` (`src/grid/Container.mjs:394`), and every parent row carries `aria-expanded` and `aria-level`. The only way to expand or collapse a row is a mouse click on the small toggle in front of the label. Someone who works with the keyboard, with or without a screen reader, is told that a row is collapsed and has no key that opens it.

## The Problem

Measured on `dev` `c7e1974483` in `examples/grid/tree` (the development server, Chromium, a 1024 × 768 viewport):

| Step | `aria-rowcount` | `aria-expanded` of the `src` row |
|---|---|---|
| Page loaded | 8 | `true` |
| Click on the row's "Type" cell: the row is selected and focus is in the grid view | 8 | `true` |
| ArrowLeft, then Enter, then Space | 8 | `true` |
| Click on the row's toggle (control: the measurement sees a collapse) | 4 | `false` |
| Click on the row's "Type" cell, then ArrowRight, then Enter | 4 | `false` |
| ArrowDown (control: keys reach the grid, the selection moves to the next row) | 4 | `false` |
| Click on the toggle again | 8 | `true` |

The keys arrive, and none of them changes the row.

To see it: `npm run server-start`, open `http://localhost:8080/examples/grid/tree/index.html`, click a folder row and press ArrowLeft.

## The Architectural Reality

- The toggle is a plain `div` (`src/grid/column/component/Tree.mjs:71`) with one listener, a `click` delegate (`:95`). Its handler `onToggleClick` (`:195`) calls `store.expand(record)` or `store.collapse(record)` and fires `expand` or `collapse` on the grid container.
- `Neo.grid.View` is the grid's single key registry (`src/grid/View.mjs:87`), and the view's selection model adds its keys there. `Neo.selection.grid.RowModel#register` adds `Down` and `Up` (`src/selection/grid/RowModel.mjs:146`). The cell models add `Left` and `Right` to move between cells (`src/selection/grid/CellModel.mjs:196`). Under the row model, `Left` and `Right` have no handler.
- No selection model knows about expanding. At `c7e1974483`, `git grep -n -i -E "expand|collapse" -- src/selection/grid` prints nothing (control: the same command with `toggle` as the pattern prints 13 lines).
- The store already has what a key needs: `Neo.data.TreeStore#expand` (`src/data/TreeStore.mjs:463`) and `#collapse` (`:249`) take a record, and a record carries `collapsed` and `isLeaf` (`src/data/TreeModel.mjs:33`, `:45`). The container knows whether it is a tree grid (`isTreeGrid`, `src/grid/Container.mjs:193`).
- The [WAI-ARIA treegrid pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/treegrid/) names the keys: with focus on a row, Right Arrow expands a collapsed row and Left Arrow collapses an expanded one.

Design authority: the tree guide says the tree column maps its ARIA fields to the rows so that the grid is "fully accessible to screen readers at any scale" (`learn/guides/datahandling/TreeStore.md`, section "The Technical Reality: Why This Matters"). A row that announces `aria-expanded="false"` and cannot be opened without a mouse falls short of that sentence.

## The journey, before and after

- **Before:** select a folder row. ArrowUp and ArrowDown move the selection, and every other key does nothing. Opening the folder needs a mouse.
- **After:** select a folder row, by a click or by arriving on it with ArrowUp or ArrowDown. ArrowRight opens it and ArrowLeft closes it. Nothing else changes: no new tab stop, nothing new for the mouse, and no change in a grid without a tree column.

## The Fix

In `Neo.selection.grid.RowModel`:

1. Register `Left` and `Right` beside `Down` and `Up` in `register()`, and remove them in `unregister()`.
2. Add `onKeyDownRight(data)` and `onKeyDownLeft(data)`. When `canNavigate(data)` passes, the grid is a tree grid and exactly one row is selected: Right expands that row if its record is a collapsed parent, and Left collapses it if its record is an expanded parent. In every other case the key does nothing.
3. Keep one copy of the expand and collapse logic. Move the store call and the `expand` / `collapse` event out of `onToggleClick` into one method on `Neo.grid.Container`, which owns `isTreeGrid` and fires both events, and call it from the click delegate and from both keys.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: In a tree grid under the row model, with a collapsed parent row selected, ArrowRight expands it: its children are among the store's visible items, and the grid container fires `expand` with `{record}`, as a click on the toggle does.
- [ ] AC-2: With an expanded parent row selected, ArrowLeft collapses it, and the container fires `collapse` with `{record}`.
- [ ] AC-3: Both keys do nothing on a leaf row, on a parent row that is already in the requested state, with no row or more than one row selected, in a grid without a tree column, and while `canNavigate(data)` is false. The selection stays as it was in each of these cases, and ArrowUp and ArrowDown work as before.
- [ ] AC-4: The store call and the event live in one method, and `onToggleClick` and both key handlers call it.
- [ ] AC-5: A unit spec covers AC-1 to AC-3 and passes with `npm run test-unit -- <your spec>`. `test/playwright/unit/selection/RowModelKeypressClick.spec.mjs` shows how to drive the row model with a mock view. That spec and `test/playwright/unit/grid/TreeRowAria.spec.mjs` still pass.
- [ ] AC-6: The accessibility section of `learn/guides/datahandling/TreeStore.md` names the two keys in one sentence.
- [ ] AC-7: The pull request says what you saw in `examples/grid/tree`: a folder row closes on ArrowLeft and opens on ArrowRight, with its `aria-expanded` value before and after.

## Out of Scope

- Reaching the rows with Tab. In the example, Tab cycles through the three header buttons and never enters the rows (the grid view has `tabindex="-1"`). That is a separate gap and deserves its own issue.
- The cell selection models. There, Left and Right move between cells and Enter starts an edit, so the pattern's Enter rule needs a design decision first.
- Moving the selection to the parent row or to the first child with the same keys.
- `Neo.tree.List`.
- A role, a name or a tab stop for the toggle.

## Avoided Traps

- **Making every toggle a focusable button.** It adds one tab stop per row, and the pattern operates the row, not the icon.
- **Copying `onToggleClick` into the model.** Two copies of the store call and the event drift apart.
