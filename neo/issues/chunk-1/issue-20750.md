# Claim responder auto-assigns first-in-line claims with a 1-issue cap

## Context

On `2026-11-10` and `2026-11-11`, three curated `good first issue` threads exposed the gap left when `.github/workflows/claim-responder.yml` replies `"first in line"` without assigning the claimant:

- `#20676`: `@DevEtaiGabbai` claimed it at `2026-11-10T19:18:19Z` (`IC_kwDODSospM8AAAABk-82PA`) and got the automatic `"first in line"` reply, while `assignees` stayed `[]` for over 7 hours. At `2026-11-11T02:35:54Z` (`IC_kwDODSospM8AAAABlG_KLQ`), `@Vijay431` (an account spraying identical `<!-- gfi -->` comments across 7 issues in 5 orgs within 23 minutes) also claimed `#20676`.
- `#20717`: `@Vijay431` posted the same `<!-- gfi -->` template 1 minute 53 seconds earlier (`02:34:01Z`, `IC_kwDODSospM8AAAABlG9chA`), receiving `"first in line"` and holding `#20717` in `earlierClaim()` for 7 days while `assignees` remained `[]`.
- `#20736`: `@fahim1252375-maker` claimed it at `02:01:39Z` and opened PR `#20746` at `02:32:51Z`, while `#20736` still showed `assignees: []` in GitHub's issue list.

As noted on `#19201` (`IC_kwDODSospM8AAAABW4t8bg`), contributors discover `good first issue` tickets through GitHub's own overview and issue lists, which display `assignees` — not comment threads. Operator direction (`@tobiu`, `2026-11-11`): automatic replies work well; only automatic assignment is missing to highlight that a ticket is claimed, while keeping the upfront expectation that the first approve-worthy PR wins if someone grabs a ticket and never sends a PR.

## The Problem

`#19202` deliberately omitted `issues.addAssignees` so manual maintainer confirmation would screen spray accounts. In practice, leaving `first in line` unassigned produces three concrete defects without preventing single-ticket spray holds, and adding auto-assignment naively introduces a fourth (`@neo-opus-grace`, `IC_kwDODSospM8AAAABlHhWkw`):

1. **Invisible holds in GitHub's issue lists (`no:assignee`):** Claimed tickets show no avatar in GitHub's issue lists or `good first issue` overviews until a maintainer session runs hours later, drawing duplicate human and agent work (`#20708` on `#20673`, `#20676`).
2. **Self-contradicting alternative suggestions:** `.github/workflows/claim-responder.yml:75-78` populates `candidates` from `repo:${owner}/${repo} is:issue is:open label:"${label}" no:assignee -linked:pr`. Because `first in line` leaves `assignees: []`, `pickAlternatives()` (`buildScripts/util/claimResponse.mjs:138-148`) treats already-claimed unassigned issues as free and can advertise them under `"open and nobody holds them"`.
3. **Unreleasable spray holds:** Even without auto-assignment, `earlierClaim()` (`buildScripts/util/claimResponse.mjs:160-182`) holds `#20717` for 7 days on `@Vijay431`'s comment, and has no path for a maintainer's 1-click GitHub unassign to release the claim (`CONTRIBUTING.md:130`).
4. **Auto-assignment without 7-day expiry (`IC_kwDODSospM8AAAABlHhWkw`):** `holders()` previously counted every `issue.assignees` entry unconditionally. Once `claim-responder` auto-assigns a claimant, a silent outside assignee without an open PR would hold the issue past `CLAIM_WINDOW_DAYS` (`7` days) instead of expiring like the comment claim it confirmed (`CONTRIBUTING.md:134-135`).

## The Architectural Reality

- `buildScripts/util/claimResponse.mjs:14-15, 160-317`: pure reply-decision module (`earlierClaim`, `holders`, `renderReply`, `decideReply`).
- `.github/workflows/claim-responder.yml:47-95`: runs `actions/github-script@v9` on `issue_comment: [created]` with `permissions: issues: write` (`.github/workflows/claim-responder.yml:17`). On public GitHub repositories, `POST /repos/{owner}/{repo}/issues/{issue_number}/assignees` (`github.rest.issues.addAssignees`) succeeds with `issues: write` once a non-collaborator has commented on the issue.
- **Cross-repo sparse-checkout callers:** `neomjs/neo-agent-brain/.github/workflows/claim-responder.yml:49,82` and `neomjs/neo-agent-institution/.github/workflows/claim-responder.yml:49,82` sparse-checkout `buildScripts/util/claimResponse.mjs` from `neomjs/neo@dev` and pass `decideReply(...)` directly to `createComment({..., body})`. `decideReply()` MUST therefore keep returning `String|null`.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md:128-135`: documents the claim rule, 7-day activity window, handoff after 7 silent days, and first-approve-worthy-PR merge policy (`#19195`, `#20616`).

Design authority: `CONTRIBUTING.md:128-135` (`#19195`, `#20616`), operator direction (`@tobiu`, `2026-11-11`) superseding `#19202`'s non-assignment constraint, and `#19202` owner read (`@neo-opus-grace`, `IC_kwDODSospM8AAAABlHhWkw`) preserving the 7-day expiry on outside-contributor assignments.

## The Fix

1. **`buildScripts/util/claimResponse.mjs`:**
   - Export `decideClaim({comment, issue, comments, events, openPullRequests, candidates, heldByClaimant, repoUrl})` returning `{assign: Boolean, body: String, assignedBody?: String, unassign?: String[]} | null`, while keeping `decideReply(options)` returning `String|null` (`decideClaim(options)?.body || null`) so cross-repo callers stay unbroken.
   - **Single-curated-issue cap (`heldByClaimant`):** When `held.length === 0` on the target issue, check `heldByClaimant` (other open curated issues in the same repo currently assigned to `claimant`). If non-empty, return `assign: false` and a polite reply naming the existing assigned issue(s) and asking the contributor to finish or step back from that issue before claiming another, plus `CONTRIBUTING.md` footer and `replyMarker(claimant)`.
   - **7-day outside-assignee expiry (`staleAssignees` / `holders`, `IC_kwDODSospM8AAAABlHhWkw`):** Export `staleAssignees(...)` and skip lapsed outside-contributor assignees in `holders()`. An outside-contributor assignee (identified from outside-human issue/comment authorship, never team `COLLABORATOR`/`MEMBER`/`OWNER` or self-assigned team members without outside comments) with no open PR into `repository` holds only within `CLAIM_WINDOW_DAYS` (`7` days) of their latest comment or `assigned` event on the issue. Team assignees keep unconditional holds.
   - **Auto-assign + stale handoff + upfront expectation wording:** When `held.length === 0` and `heldByClaimant` is empty, return `assign: true`, `unassign: staleAssignees(...)` (original-case logins of any lapsed outside assignees on the issue), `assignedBody` (confirming `"Assigned to you — you can start now"` and noting the 7-day active window + that the first approve-worthy PR wins if another lands first or a claim goes quiet), and `body` (the `"first in line"` fallback if `addAssignees` does not stick or a caller uses `decideReply`).
   - **Maintainer unassign release (`events`):** In `earlierClaim()`, treat a prior claim comment by `login` as released when `events` contains a later `{event: 'unassigned', assignee: {login}}` at or before the current comment timestamp (just like a withdrawal comment), so a maintainer's 1-click GitHub Unassign immediately frees the issue.
2. **`.github/workflows/claim-responder.yml`:**
   - Fetch `events` (`github.paginate(github.rest.issues.listEvents, {owner, repo, issue_number: issue.number, per_page: 100})`) and `assignedSearch` (`repo:${owner}/${repo} is:issue is:open assignee:"${comment.user.login}"`, filtered by `curatedLabel(item) && item.number !== issue.number`).
   - Call `decideClaim(...)`. When `decision.assign` is `true`, call `github.rest.issues.addAssignees({owner, repo, issue_number: issue.number, assignees: [comment.user.login]})` inside `try/catch`; when `addAssignees` confirms `comment.user.login` in `data.assignees`, select `decision.assignedBody` and, if `decision.unassign.length > 0`, call `github.rest.issues.removeAssignees` to clear any lapsed outside assignee(s) (falling back to `decision.body` without removing anyone if `addAssignees` fails or drops the user).
3. **`CONTRIBUTING.md` (`§3` item 3):**
   - Update item 3 to state that a first-in-line claim on a curated issue (one open curated issue at a time) is automatically assigned by the reply, that a maintainer can unassign accounts that spray claims, and keep the 7-day window + first-approve-worthy-PR rule explicit.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **AC-1:** `decideClaim()` returns `assign: true`, `unassign: []`, and `assignedBody` (naming the 7-day active window and first-approve-worthy-PR rule) when a claimant is first in line on an unassigned issue and holds no other open curated issue in the repo, while `decideReply()` continues returning `String|null` for cross-repo callers.
- [ ] **AC-2:** When `heldByClaimant` contains another open curated issue number, `decideClaim()` returns `assign: false` and tells the claimant which issue they already hold instead of assigning a second one.
- [ ] **AC-3:** `earlierClaim()` releases an earlier claim when followed by an `unassigned` issue event for that claimant (`events`), matching withdrawal behavior.
- [ ] **AC-4:** `.github/workflows/claim-responder.yml` fetches issue events and the claimant's open assigned issues, calls `github.rest.issues.addAssignees` when `decision.assign` is `true`, calls `github.rest.issues.removeAssignees` when `decision.unassign` is non-empty after verified assignment, and posts `assignedBody` only when `data.assignees` includes the claimant (falling back to `decision.body` otherwise).
- [ ] **AC-5:** `CONTRIBUTING.md` §3 item 3 documents automatic assignment on first-in-line curated claims (one open curated issue at a time), maintainer unassignment for claim sprayers, and `npm run check-relative-links` + `npm run test-unit -- test/playwright/unit/buildScripts/util/claimResponse.spec.mjs` pass.
- [ ] **AC-6:** Outside-contributor assignees without an open PR into `repository` expire after `CLAIM_WINDOW_DAYS` (`7` days) from their latest comment or `assigned` event on the issue: at 6 days quiet the assignee still holds in `holders()`, at 8 days quiet (with no open PR) they do not hold and `decideClaim()` returns `assign: true, unassign: [staleLogin]`, while an open repo PR or team-member assignment continues holding past 8 days (`IC_kwDODSospM8AAAABlHhWkw`).

## Avoided Traps

- **Changing `decideReply()`'s return type from `String|null` to an object:** `neo-agent-brain` and `neo-agent-institution` sparse-checkout `neomjs/neo@dev` and pass `decideReply()` straight to `issues.createComment({..., body})`.
- **Expiring team-member self-assignments or contributors with an open PR:** Team assignees (`MEMBER`/`COLLABORATOR`/`OWNER`, including self-assigned seats with zero issue comments) and outside contributors with an open PR targeting `repository` must never be treated as lapsed.
- **Using `label:"good first issue" label:"help wanted"` in GitHub issue search:** GitHub search ANDs multiple `label:` qualifiers; filter `assignee:${login}` results with `curatedLabel(item)` in JS instead.
- **Claiming `"Assigned to you"` or removing a prior assignee without checking `addAssignees` response:** GitHub REST `POST .../assignees` returns HTTP `201` with `assignees: []` if an unassignable user is silently ignored; verify `data.assignees` before choosing `assignedBody` or calling `removeAssignees`.

### @neo-opus-grace - 2026-11-11T03:13:21Z

**Owner read (#19202): the shape holds, with one gap. Auto-assignment drops the 7-day expiry.**

`#19202`'s no-assign rule was a spray screen, and the operator's direction supersedes it. The cap is the better screen, and keeping `decideReply()` as `String|null` keeps the Brain and Institution callers safe.

**The gap.** Today a first-in-line claim stays a comment, and `earlierClaim()` lets it lapse after `CLAIM_WINDOW_DAYS` of its claimant's silence (`claimResponse.mjs:175`). Once the reply assigns, that claim becomes an assignee. `holders()` counts an assignee unconditionally (`:206-209`), so a claimant who goes quiet holds the issue until a maintainer unassigns by hand. Meanwhile every later claimant is told "it's assigned to X", which contradicts `CONTRIBUTING.md`'s seven days.

Take #20717 under this PR: the sprayer is auto-assigned and never returns. The issue drops out of `no:assignee` and stays out until someone notices. Today it frees itself on day 8.

**Suggested rule:** an assignment the responder made expires like the claim it confirmed.
- **`holders()`:** an outside-contributor assignee whose last word on the issue (a comment, or their open PR) is older than `CLAIM_WINDOW_DAYS` holds nothing. Team assignees keep today's unconditional hold.
- **`decideClaim()`:** for a claim on such an issue, the decision answers "first in line" and carries `unassign: [stale login]` beside `assign`. Alternatively it only names the lapse, if moving an assignment without a maintainer is a step the operator has not taken. Either way, the reply must not say "taken" for a claim `CONTRIBUTING.md` calls lapsed.
- **Arm:** an assignee whose last comment is 8 days old does not hold against a new claim, and one 6 days old does.
