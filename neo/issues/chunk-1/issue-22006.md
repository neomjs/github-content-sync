# The Trees tiles take their capture size from their examples

## Context

`buildScripts/docs/captureExamples.json`'s tile list has been frozen since #21908 (16:46Z, 22 Nov). Per `catalogTiles()` in `buildScripts/docs/captureExamples.mjs`, "a new tile needs its record, never an entry in the manifest", captured at its component's own size. #21983 (merged 17:47Z the same day) still added two sized rows: `{"path": "examples/tree", "width": 300, "height": 350}` and `{"path": "examples/treeAccordion", "width": 300, "height": 270}`. Euclid's review of #21997 removed the same kind of rows there; these two are the remainder, mine.

## The Problem

The tile size now lives in two places: each example's own config, and a manifest row that overrides it at capture time. The examples render the tree list at 320 × 420 and the tree accordion at 320 × 350, while the published crops (neomjs/pages#182) come from the rows. A recapture from the catalog alone would not reproduce them.

## The Architectural Reality

- `buildScripts/docs/captureExamples.mjs`: `tileList()` validates the frozen rows; `catalogTiles()` adds every other `kind: 'component'` record at its own size; `captureTile()` applies a row's `width` / `height` to the stage component before measuring.
- `examples/tree/MainContainer.mjs` (tree list 320 × 420, playlist 320 × 260) and `examples/treeAccordion/MainContainer.mjs` (320 × 350).
- #21997 shows the shape: the rows went, and the wizard took the row's height in its own code.

## The Fix

Remove the two rows. Where the example's own size crops worse than the row did, set that size in the example's own code (as #21997 did for the wizard), checking that the phone stage still fits. Recapture both tiles with their light partners onto `neomjs/pages`, and verify the published digests.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] No `examples/tree` or `examples/treeAccordion` row in `buildScripts/docs/captureExamples.json`.
- [ ] Both tile pairs on Pages are reproduced by the stock camera from the catalog records, digests listed on the pull request. `[L4-deferred — Pages main digest verification once the companion neomjs/pages PR merges; tracked on #21140]`
- [ ] At 390 × 844 both examples still fit their stage.

## Out of Scope

Rows that predate the freeze (#21908's own list). The Trees examples' behavior.
