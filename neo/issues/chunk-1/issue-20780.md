# Without the VDom worker, meta, auto-mounts and the paint hook break

## Context

Found on 2026-11-11 at `5012129e70`, while measuring the reply path for Discussion #20751 (the VDOM update pipeline). `Neo.config.useVdomWorker: false` runs `Neo.vdom.Helper` inside the App worker (`src/DefaultConfig.mjs` calls it experimental). In that configuration the App worker sends the deltas to main itself, and three things differ from what happens for a VDom worker's reply.

## The Problem

1. **An update's `meta` never reaches main.** During a scroll of `examples/grid/bigData` (120 frames, 35 update batches) all 35 batches leave `Helper.updateBatch` with a `meta` entry for the grid view, and 0 of 35 calls of `Neo.main.DeltaUpdates#update` receive one. With the VDom worker it is 35 of 35. Same on SharedWorkers.
2. **`Neo.vdom.Helper.create({autoMount: true, …})` mounts nothing.** A node created this way is in the DOM 300 ms later with the VDom worker and absent without it, on dedicated and on shared workers.
3. **`afterExecuteVdomUpdate` runs before the paint.** The hook's JSDoc promises "after Main Thread DOM delta paint"; without the worker it runs before the deltas are even sent. Content-sized grid rows schedule their measurement from it and measure the old layout.

The e2e spec `test/playwright/e2e/grid/ContentSizedRowsExample.spec.mjs` (15 tests) passes 15 with the worker and 4 with `useVdomWorker: false` as the checkout's default. With 1 and 3 repaired in a scratch patch it passes 7.

1 and 2 were read in a real browser without a source change: a wrapper around `Neo.main.DeltaUpdates.update` counts the calls whose argument carries `meta`, and `useVdomWorker: false` is routed into the page's `neo-config.json`. The spec is `Pipeline.spec.mjs` in the first comment of Discussion #20751 (tests `grid bigData` and `autoMount outside the lifecycle`, `VP_ARM=2t`).

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/mixin/VdomLifecycle.mjs:540-557`: the `afterExecuteVdomUpdate` loop comes first, then `if (!Neo.config.useVdomWorker && response.deltas?.length > 0) {await Neo.applyDeltas(me.windowId, response.deltas)}`. `response.meta` is dropped there; `src/worker/App.mjs:125-131` sends `{action: 'updateVdom', deltas}`.
- With the worker the reply passes main whole and before the App worker sees it: `src/worker/Manager.mjs#onWorkerMessage` hands its payload (deltas and meta) to `Main#onUpdateVdom`, `DeltaUpdates#update` fires `update` with it, and the reply is forwarded once the deltas are applied.
- Readers of `data.meta` on main: `src/main/addon/GridRowScrollPinning.mjs:176-183` (`if (!meta || !deltas) return`, so `workerScrollTop`, `scrollEpoch` and the scroll corrections never update) and `src/main/addon/ListMotion.mjs:115-118`. Producers: `src/grid/View.mjs:984` (`getVdomUpdateMeta`) and `src/list/plugin/Animate.mjs:206`.
- Users of the hook: `src/grid/Row.mjs:93` and `src/grid/Body.mjs:383` ("Triggered after an update of this row landed in the DOM … Content-sized rows measure what it rendered"), `src/grid/column/component/Tree.mjs:106`.
- Auto-mount: with the worker, `src/worker/Manager.mjs:566-580` turns a `create` reply that carries `autoMount` into an `insertNode` delta. Without the worker `Helper.create` is a local call and nobody sends that delta. `VdomLifecycle#initVnode` covers its own case (`:905-918`, from `#8134`); `src/component/Helix.mjs:779` calls `Helper.create` with `autoMount: true` directly and relies on main.
- `Design authority:` the JSDoc of `VdomLifecycle#getVdomUpdatePayload`: "This allows the App Worker to send contextual state (e.g., the specific `scrollTop` value the VDOM was calculated against) alongside the VDOM deltas to the Main Thread."

## The Fix

1. In the two-thread path of `VdomLifecycle#executeVdomUpdate`, send the deltas together with the reply's `meta`, and send them before the `afterExecuteVdomUpdate` loop. A message `{action: 'updateVdom', deltas, meta}` is enough: `Main#onUpdateVdom` queues the message object itself. A scratch prototype that does exactly this reads 35 of 35 for the meta.
2. Give a `Helper.create` caller outside the lifecycle the insert it relies on: one helper for "create, then send the insert when there is no VDom worker", used by `initVnode` (in place of its inline block) and by `Helix`.
3. One unit spec per defect, all with `useVdomWorker: false`: an update of a component that implements `getVdomUpdateMeta()` sends the meta with its deltas; `afterExecuteVdomUpdate` runs after `Neo.applyDeltas` has resolved; `Helper.create` with `autoMount` sends one `insertNode` delta.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] With `useVdomWorker: false`, an update whose payload carries `meta` reaches `Neo.main.DeltaUpdates#update` with that `meta` (unit spec).
- [ ] With `useVdomWorker: false`, `afterExecuteVdomUpdate` runs after the deltas were applied (unit spec).
- [ ] With `useVdomWorker: false`, `Neo.vdom.Helper.create` with `autoMount: true` mounts its node (unit spec on the `insertNode` delta), and `Helix` uses the shared helper.
- [ ] Nothing changes with `useVdomWorker: true`: the unit and the component tier stay green.
- [ ] JSDoc on every touched method.

## Out of Scope

- Making the two-thread configuration the default, or any other option of Discussion #20751.
- The eight tests of `ContentSizedRowsExample.spec.mjs` that still fail with `useVdomWorker: false` after repairs 1 and 3, all on "the grid settles within 600 frames". Their cause is not read; it gets its own ticket once someone reads it.
- `postMountUpdates` of the string renderer, which neither configuration passes for an auto-mount.

## Avoided Traps

- A second message for the meta. Main's listeners read `meta` from the same object as the deltas, in the `update` event that precedes their application.
