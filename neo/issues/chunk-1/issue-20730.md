# Remove the star plea from the JSON-LD post and date its leaf to the publish day

## Context

The post `learn/blog/json-ld-xss-json-stringify-in-a-script-tag.md` merged on 2026-11-10 with #20718. The operator read its closing section after the merge: "we should probably remove 'A star tells us this piece was worth your time.' => begging is not the right angle."

## The Problem

1. **One sentence asks for a favour.** The closing list offers three actions: look at the engine, run `npx neo-app@latest`, read the review rules. Each gives the reader something. The sentence after the first link asks the reader to pay for the article with a star and names our benefit as the reason. It sits in the last lines of the piece, where the reader decides what to do next.
2. **The leaf's date may be off.** The leaf in `blog.json` is dated `2026-11-12`. The rule from #20718's post-merge list is that the leaf carries the publish day, and the operator started the Medium version on 2026-11-10.

## The Architectural Reality

- `learn/blog/json-ld-xss-json-stringify-in-a-script-tag.md:166` on `dev` at `6ec1e183a0`: `- **Look at the engine:** [github.com/neomjs/neo](https://github.com/neomjs/neo). A star tells us this piece was worth your time.`
- `apps/portal/resources/data/blog.json`, the leaf `blog/json-ld-xss-json-stringify-in-a-script-tag`, `"date": "2026-11-12"`.
- The blog guide's rule for a call to action (`blog-authoring-guide.md` §1) is the reader's next action with its link. The link and the action stay.
- Population on `dev` (`git grep -i -w star -- 'learn/blog/*.md'`): five files mention a star, two in a call to action. The other one, `one-repository-per-reader.md:79`, gives the reader a reason of their own ("to follow what we build next") and is not touched.
- Design authority: n/a — calls no existing behavior wrong; a copy decision by the operator, quoted above.

## The Fix

- Remove the sentence. The list item ends on the repository link.
- Set the leaf's `date` to the day the Medium version went out, if that day is not 2026-11-12.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: the sentence is gone and the item reads `- **Look at the engine:** [github.com/neomjs/neo](https://github.com/neomjs/neo).`; the post renders at `#/news/blog/json-ld-xss-json-stringify-in-a-script-tag` with its three actions.
- [ ] AC-2: the leaf's `date` equals the day the Medium version was published, with the receipt for that day in the PR; `blog.json` parses. If the publish day is 2026-11-12, the file is unchanged and the PR says so.
- [ ] AC-3: nothing else in the post changes.

## Out of Scope

- The LinkedIn and X drafts of #20718: neither asks for a star.
- Calls to action in other posts.
- The Medium version, which the operator edits himself.

## Avoided Traps

- Replacing the plea with another argument for a star. Whether a piece earned one is the reader's call.
