2. **#22114 as an observed flow** in §1 (`19889367`): the drag proxy's mount waits on App → VDom worker → Main, and hosted runners missed the tear-out boundary.
3. **The Phase-1 gate observables** (⚠6) and the I3 guard (⚠4) in §2's migration: the 18-shard e2e matrix in the two-thread arm, the matched-repeat hosted tear-out comparison, a SharedWorker multi-window pop-out spec, and capturing `windowId` before the diff.
4. **The removal inventory** is #22196's two marked blocks: 31 engine files and 3 cross-repository consumers (⚠2). The record links them rather than copying them, and names the guard that keeps external `myApps.json` entries for `vdom` building.
