# Dock progressive details inside the 2x2 lead example card footer

## Context

On `/examples` (`Overview`) and every group page at desktop container widths (`@container (min-width: 1100px)`), the first demonstration card spans `2 × 2` (`grid-column: span 2; grid-row: span 2`). Operator (`@tobiu`, `2026-11-25`) noted that while the `2 × 2` card scale reads well, its bottom info-bar inherits roughly double the height of `1 × 1` cards while showing the same brief caption — leaving the text looking lost above empty surface — and when the `2 × 2` card is focused/selected, `.portal-example-more` slides out over the `16:9` cover at `1 × 1` scale while the tall footer below remains half-empty. Operator selected **Option 3** (`"option 3 wins"`): dock `.portal-example-more` (`lookFor`, `scope`, and `Desktop · Mobile`) permanently inside the `2 × 2` lead card's footer zone at rest with modest editorial typography, and omit the cover slide-over on focus/selection. Curated `good first issue` / `help wanted` labels are omitted because this operator-directed design enhancement is being delivered immediately.

## The Problem

With `1 × 1` cover height $C_1 = \frac{9}{16}W_1$, footer $F_1 \approx 98\text{px}$, column gap $G_x = 24\text{px}$, and row gap $G_y = 13.5\text{px} = \frac{9}{16}G_x$, two stacked `1 × 1` cards span $2C_1 + 2F_1 + 13.5\text{px}$ while the `2 × 2` card's uncropped `16:9` cover spans $2C_1 + 13.5\text{px}$, leaving $2F_1 \approx 196\text{px}$ below the cover (`grid-template-rows: auto 1fr` in `List.css:254`). Because every `1 × 1` progressive detail block (`.portal-example-more`) stays collapsed at `0fr` inside `.preview-image-wrap` until `.neo-selected` (`List.css:320-379`), the `2 × 2` lead card wastes ~100–120px of vertical footer space at rest and then occludes its own hero cover on selection.

## The Architectural Reality

- `apps/portal/view/examples/List.mjs:300-324` already renders `.portal-example-more` (`lookFor`, `scope`, and `.portal-example-meta` `Desktop · Mobile`) as the third child of `.content` after `.preview-image-wrap` and `.portal-example-footer` on every non-tile card, and all 22 lead cards across `Overview` and the 20 card-bearing groups carry non-empty `lookFor` copy (`74–176` chars).
- `resources/css/src/apps/portal/examples/List.css:61-79` scopes the `2 × 2` lead card under `@container (min-width: 1100px)` (`&.portal-examples-list-lead > .neo-list-item:first-child`), while `List.css:311-380` scopes multi-column `.portal-example-more` overlay rules under `@container examples-list (min-width: 704px)`.
- `test/playwright/e2e/portal/ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs` (`expectCardLayout` at lines `167-227` and selection geometry at lines `1387-1398`) currently assumes `.portal-example-more` is collapsed at rest (`height < 1`) and opens inside `.preview-image-wrap` even on `isLead`.

Design authority: Operator `@tobiu` (`2026-11-25`, selecting Option 3 while preserving `#22049`'s uncropped `16:9` cover ratio invariant).

## The Fix

1. **`resources/css/src/apps/portal/examples/List.css`:** Under `@container (min-width: 1100px)` for `&.portal-examples-list-lead > .neo-list-item:first-child` (placed after the `@container examples-list` rules so it governs both resting and `.neo-selected` states):
   - Switch `.content` to `grid-template-rows: auto auto 1fr` so `.preview-image-wrap` keeps its exact `16:9` frame (`row 1`), `.portal-example-footer` renders immediately below (`row 2`, stepped-up padding `14px 18px 12px`, `.neo-title` `21px`, `.portal-example-feature` `15px`), and `.portal-example-more` docks permanently in `row 3` (`grid-column: 1; grid-row: 3; grid-template-rows: 1fr; max-height: none; opacity: 1; backdrop-filter: none; background: var(--portal-examples-list-surface); border-top: 1px solid var(--portal-examples-list-edge); padding: 11px 18px 14px; pointer-events: auto`), with `.portal-example-look-for` (`13.5px`, `--portal-examples-list-text`) and `.portal-example-scope` (`12.5px`, `--portal-examples-list-text-muted`) readable at rest and invariant across hover/selection.
   - Keep `.neo-selected` on the `2 × 2` lead card purely perimeter-ring + `Open live` cue illumination (`0.0px` geometry delta and `100%` unoccluded `16:9` cover).
2. **`apps/portal/view/examples/List.mjs`:** Update `@summary` / class JSDoc on `createItemContent` to reflect that the `2 × 2` lead card docks `.portal-example-more` permanently below `.portal-example-footer` while `1 × 1` multi-column cards retain the `<= 40%` cover disclosure band.
3. **`test/playwright/e2e/portal/ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs`:** Update `expectCardLayout` and the `#21676` selection test so `isLead` verifies permanent docked `.portal-example-more` below `.portal-example-footer` (`100%` unoccluded `16:9` cover at rest and when `.neo-selected`, all copy fitting inside card bounds across `Overview` and every group page including `Containers & Layouts` and `Tabs`), while `1 × 1` cards continue to assert closed-at-rest + `<= 40%` in-cover open-on-select.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **AC-1:** At `@container (min-width: 1100px)` (`1440 × 900`), every `2 × 2` lead card (`Overview` and group pages) keeps its exact `16:9` uncropped cover and renders `.portal-example-more` (`lookFor`, optional `scope`, and optional `Desktop · Mobile` pill) permanently docked below `.portal-example-footer` inside the card bounds with zero overflow.
- [ ] **AC-2:** Focusing or selecting (`.neo-selected`) a `2 × 2` lead card draws the selection ring and lights `Open live` without sliding any overlay onto `.preview-image-wrap` (`100%` unoccluded cover) and with `0.0px` displacement of `title`, GitHub link, `Open live`, and neighboring cards.
- [ ] **AC-3:** `1 × 1` multi-column cards (`>= 704px` list width) and single-column / phone cards (`< 704px` / `<= 640px`, where the lead card is `1 × 1`) preserve their existing progressive `.portal-example-more` behavior unchanged.
- [ ] **AC-4:** `ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs` passes across `Overview` and group pages, accompanied by `1440 × 900` before/after (`dev` vs head) frames of `Overview`'s `2 × 2` lead card at rest and selected in `neo-dark` and `neo-light`.

## Out of Scope

- Changing `16:9` cover aspect ratios or re-capturing screenshots (`#21942`, `#22049`).
- Left-rail `"Start here · 12"` and `"Search 170 examples…  /"` copy updates (delivered in a separate sibling ticket).

## Avoided Traps

- **Shrinking the `2 × 2` footer to `1 × 1` height:** Forces `.preview-image-wrap` to `~14:9` and crops `~19%` off the right edge of `1280 × 720` covers (`#22049`).
- **Inflating title/feature font sizes alone:** Leaves single-line descriptions stranded at the top of a `728px`-wide bar and retains the mismatched `12.5px` cover slide-over on focus.
