# Establish one-question-one-page owner table for duplicate learn/ guides

## Context

Sub-issue of `#20498` implementing **Row 4 (`guides-duplicate-questions`)** of the three-reader `learn/` map ([`#20498#issuecomment-6697670393`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20498#issuecomment-6697670393)).

- **Ownership disposition:** `handoff: @neo-fable-mnemosyne` (self-selected for Monday per `MESSAGE:bd85fdb3-9e70-479d-95ac-7264e6504596`; left unassigned until Monday pickup).

## The Problem

Across `learn/benefits/body/`, `learn/gettingstarted/`, `learn/guides/fundamentals/`, `learn/guides/coreengine/`, `learn/guides/datahandling/`, `learn/guides/userinteraction/`, and `learn/javascript/`, the same core engineering questions are answered on multiple overlapping pages without a clear owner:
- **Config system (`4` pages):** `learn/benefits/body/ConfigSystem.md` (`27.7 KB`), `learn/guides/fundamentals/ConfigSystemDeepDive.md` (`20.4 KB`), `learn/guides/coreengine/ConfigSystem.md` (`7.5 KB`), and `learn/gettingstarted/Config.md` (`4.4 KB`, Jul 2025).
- **Instance / component lifecycle (`2` pages):** `learn/guides/fundamentals/ComponentLifecycle.md` and `learn/guides/coreengine/Lifecycle.md`.
- **Extending classes (`3` pages):** `learn/guides/fundamentals/ExtendingNeoClasses.md`, `learn/javascript/Classes.md`, and `learn/javascript/ClassSystem.md`.
- **Shared bindable data / State Providers (`2+` pages):** `learn/guides/datahandling/StateProviders.md`, `learn/benefits/body/StateProviders.md`, and `learn/guides/coreengine/` overlap.
- **Events (`2` pages):** `learn/guides/userinteraction/CustomEvents.md` and `learn/guides/userinteraction/DomEvents.md` vs `coreengine/`.
- **Group-level overlap:** `"The Core Engine"` (`6` pages, Mar 2026) restates `"Fundamentals"` (`12` pages) at lower depth.

Design authority: `#20498` + `#20498#issuecomment-6697670393` (Row 4 `guides-duplicate-questions`) + `.agents/skills/guide-authoring/references/guide-authoring-bar.md` §0–§2.

## The Architectural Reality

- Both human readers and the Knowledge Base (`neo-mjs-knowledge-base`) suffer when four pages compete to answer *"how does the reactive config system work?"* at different depths and dates.
- Per `#20498#issuecomment-6697670393` Row 4, merging pages before agreeing on an explicit **owner table** (`one question → one canonical page`, plus explicit role separation where a Benefits pitch page vs. a Deep-Dive Guide serves a distinct reader) risks losing unique content or breaking inbound links.

## The Fix

1. **Deliverable 1 (This Ticket — The Owner Table on the Record):**
   - Produce and record the canonical **Question-to-Page Owner Table** covering all duplicate clusters (`config`, `lifecycle`, `extending classes`, `state providers / bindable data`, `events`, and `"The Core Engine"` vs. `"Fundamentals"`).
   - For each page in a cluster, specify its exact disposition:
     - **Canonical Owner** (absorbs unique technical depth and examples from duplicates),
     - **Distinct-Reader Companion** (e.g., `benefits/body/ConfigSystem.md` as the evaluator pitch vs. `guides/fundamentals/ConfigSystemDeepDive.md` as the implementation reference, with explicit cross-links), or
     - **Fold & Retire from `learn/tree.json`** (naming the target owner page and the inbound links to update).
2. **Subsequent Merge Leaves:** Individual page merges execute as follow-up PRs against the recorded owner table, keeping the stronger telling per paragraph (`#20518` guard).
