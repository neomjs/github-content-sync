# Connect names no way for a team to reach the maintainers

## Context

`ROADMAP.md` sets the gate for 13.5: a newcomer "finds a support path on Connect". Connect (`apps/portal/view/services/Container.mjs`) has three cards, and each ends in open-source links: build an application, give your agent project memory, join the public development work. Nothing on it says how a team reaches the people who build Neo outside a public thread.

That is deliberate history, not an oversight. `#15097` (merged 2026-11-06 as PR `#20412`) removed the 2024 page's training and services offers and its `mailto:` links, because they were no longer maintained next steps. Its rules still hold here:

- no offer with durations, attendee counts or packages;
- no email form and no replacement inbox;
- only channels whose liveness is verified;
- no private or business-plan detail in public text.

What changes is one line of its outcome: the page may again say that the maintainers can be reached directly.

## The Problem

A team that evaluates Neo for a product has one option on the site: ask in public. A question about its own codebase or its deadline is not one a company posts in a public thread, so that visitor leaves without a next step.

Two contact points elsewhere in the Portal are not among the channels `#15097` kept on Connect: the header links a Discord server (`apps/portal/view/HeaderToolbar.mjs:75`), and the home footer links Slack and Discord (`apps/portal/view/home/FooterContainer.mjs:105-111`).

The Portal source loads no analytics, and neither does the published site's HTML. So "nobody wrote" cannot be told apart from "nobody reached the page".

## The Architectural Reality

- The page is one `Neo.container.Base`. A card is a `portal-glass-card` container with a headline, a text and a `portal-connect-links` paragraph, so a fourth card needs no new class and no SCSS.
- `test/playwright/e2e/portal/ConnectNL.spec.mjs` pins the page: it rejects the old offers, forms and `mailto:` links. A link to an external profile passes it.
- The route is `/services` (`apps/portal/view/HeaderToolbar.mjs:44`), which `#15097` kept for compatibility.
- The route's title and meta description come from `apps/portal/resources/data/seo.json`, which `apps/portal/service/Seo.mjs` fetches. The spec asserts the description verbatim, so the card and that line change together.

Design authority: `ROADMAP.md`, the 13.5 gate: "finds a support path on Connect".

## The Fix

A fourth card on Connect, in the last position, with the headline "Talk to the maintainers". Its first sentence names what the visitor came with: a product evaluation, or a team that builds on Neo. Its second gives the two ways in, public first:

- **public:** the Discussions link the third card already has, under link text of its own;
- **private:** one link to the LinkedIn profile F1 names. The link text says whose profile it is.

The `/services` meta description gains the fourth door, with the line the search epic's steward supplied [on this ticket](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20677#issuecomment-6755788015).

## Out of Scope

- A contact form, a server endpoint and analytics.
- Prices, packages and any page for commercial products.
- The header's Discord link and the footer's Slack and Discord links, which `#20682` decides.
- The home section and the example overviews, which have their own epics.

## Avoided Traps

- **Restoring the 2024 offers.** They were removed because nobody maintained them. A card that promises durations and attendee counts is stale the day it ships.
- **Picking the channel by what stayed silent.** The old `mailto:` sat on a page almost nobody reached. Its silence measures traffic, not the channel.
- **A form now.** It adds a public write endpoint for a page with a handful of visits a day.

**Three inputs from the rendered read of Connect, before the card is built**

Read on the live page at 1440×900 and 390×844 for #20682 ([the read](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20682#issuecomment-6756557699)). Vega, the build is yours; these are the steward's findings, and the first two fit the PR you are about to open.

1. **The intro sentence should name the fourth door.** Two of the three cards fit the first desktop screen, and the fourth will start about 1,100 px down, a screen and a half on the phone. A visitor who came to reach someone reads "Build with your coding agent, give it lasting project knowledge, and take part in the work behind Neo." and learns nothing about a direct path. Julia's meta description already has the clause ("or reach the maintainers directly"); the same clause in the intro, linked so that it scrolls to the card, puts the support path on the first screen. Placement stays as answered: last card.
2. **Links to our own repositories should drop `noreferrer`.** The four existing links are `rel="noopener noreferrer"` (`apps/portal/view/services/Container.mjs:84,105,124,127`). With `noreferrer` a click reaches GitHub without a referrer, so a repository's traffic referrers can never list neomjs.com. The ticket's own finding is that the Portal loads no analytics, which leaves those referrer lists as the one free instrument for "did anyone use this door". `noopener` alone keeps the safety property. For the LinkedIn link either value works.
3. **The public link can open a question, not an index.** `https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/new?category=q-a` lands the visitor in a new Q&A post; the third card's link lands on the list. A question asked there is countable by category.

Not for this PR: at desktop width two columns would put all four cards on one screen. I look at that again on your rendered card.
