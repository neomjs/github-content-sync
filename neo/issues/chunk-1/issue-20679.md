# Give the 17 cover-less Grid and Table example cards real covers

## Context

A leaf of #20498, cornerstone 1 of Engine 13.5 (`ROADMAP.md`). Its first leaf, #20500 (PR #20675), puts 21 captioned Grid and Table demonstrations on the Portal. Seventeen of them have no screenshot and render the intended fallback: a grey gradient, a table icon and the word Grid or Table. The steward's render read of that PR at `c8034978b8` ([review 5834593429](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/pull/20675#pullrequestreview-5834593429)): the captions carry the overview, the covers do not. At 1440 px its first screen shows five grey cards out of six. The epic's standard is a kitchen sink, where every example shows itself.

Depends on #20500: the catalog `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json` exists on `dev` once PR #20675 merges. Capturing can start before that; the catalog edit lands after.

## The Problem

A stranger evaluating the grid sees seventeen near-identical cards. The caption says what an example proves and nothing shows it.

Count receipt: `examples.json` at `c8034978b8`, parsed and filtered on `family === 'grid-table'`: 21 records, 4 with an `image` (`grid-big-data`, `grid-issues`, `grid-tree-big-data`, `table-filtering`), 17 with `"image": null`, listed in the frame table below.

## The Architectural Reality

- `Portal.view.examples.List#imageBasePath` (`apps/portal/view/examples/List.mjs`) serves a record's `image` from `neomjs/pages` `main`, `resources_pub/website/examples/`: by relative path on the deployed site, from `raw.githubusercontent.com` everywhere else. A new cover is invisible in a local Portal until Pages holds it.
- The engine keeps the originals in `apps/portal/resources/images/examples/devmode/`, 1280×720 JPEG since 13.2 (`workstation-13-2.jpg` 89 KB, `grid-issues-13-3.jpg` 197 KB), and Pages publishes identical bytes. Precedent: `#19640`, `#19947`. The originals ship in the npm package and are copied into every dist build, hence the byte budget below.
- `.preview-image` paints the cover with `background-size: cover` at the default position and `background-blend-mode: overlay` over a grey gradient (`resources/scss/src/apps/portal/examples/List.scss`). Measured on the overview at `c8034978b8`: a card is 445×400 at 1440 px, 468×400 at 1024 px and 328×400 at 390 px. The frame is anchored top-left and cropped on the right: 63 % of its width shows at 445 px, 46 % at 328 px. The caption block starts 100 to 216 px below the card's top and covers what lies under it.
- The fallback (`.portal-example-placeholder`) shows while `image` is empty and stays for future entries.

## The Fix

One 1280×720 cover per example below, captured from the running example, so that the card shows what its caption names.

- **Composition:** the telling detail sits in the top-left third of the frame (the left half, the upper third). The card crops the right and the caption covers the bottom; a proof placed bottom-right shows nothing on a phone.
- **State:** the feature in action, never the untouched default view.
- **Theme:** the dark theme where the example offers a switch (the white caption and the overlay blend read best on it), otherwise the example's default.
- **Files:** `devmode/<record id>-13-5.jpg`, JPEG, at most 150 KB each.
- **Order of work:** the `neomjs/pages` pull request first (public repository, same bytes under `resources_pub/website/examples/devmode/`); once it is merged, a local Portal on the engine branch shows the covers and the card-size read is possible before the engine pull request merges.
- **Recapture:** the pull request records per cover the URL, viewport, theme and interaction, as a small capture script or as a table. No generator is required.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] All 17 records carry an `image`; each original exists under `apps/portal/resources/images/examples/devmode/`, is 1280×720, JPEG and at most 150 KB.
- [ ] Each cover shows the state its row of the frame table names, with the detail in the top-left third, in the dark theme where the example offers one.
- [ ] A unit arm fails when a family record's `image` has no original in the engine tree (today one arm checks the Issues cover alone).
- [ ] The pull request embeds the overview at 1440 px and at 390 px with the covers in place, and lists URL, viewport, theme and interaction per cover.
- [ ] The companion `neomjs/pages` pull request is linked and carries identical bytes; both bodies list the checksums.
- [ ] No caption changes, no new example; the four existing covers stay.

## Post-Merge Validation

After the Pages deployment and the Engine publication the public overview shows 21 covers. Whoever merges the Pages pull request records the served result under #20498.

## Out of Scope

Changing `imageBasePath` or where covers are served from; a cover generator in the build; recapturing the four existing covers; covers for other families; caption copy and the scrim under captions on image cards (both PR #20675).

## Avoided Traps

- A frame of the example's untouched default view: most of these examples open on a plain grid that looks like the next one.
- The proof in the lower or the right part of the frame: the caption covers the one and the card crops the other.
- A light-theme frame under the overlay blend: it greys out.
- A mock-up or a composited image: a cover is a capture of the running example.
