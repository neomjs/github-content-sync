# The home section says what Neo is and who it is for

Terminal predicate: someone who has never seen Neo reads the home section of neomjs.com and can say, in their own words, what Neo is, who it is for and what to do next; each audience the README names finds its door there with one proof that exists and one next step; and the snapshot a crawler or an agent receives for the home route says the same.

## Problem scope

The 13.5 gate (`ROADMAP.md`): "someone who has never seen Neo can say after the home section what it is and who it is for". The roadmap records the maintainers' own read that a newcomer may leave without knowing either, and names this epic's steward and its filing date.

Read as a stranger on the live site (footer: v13.4.0), Tue Nov 10, in a real Chromium at 1024×768 and 1440×900:

- **The first screen.** The logo; a headline that morphs through 28 phrases (`MagicMoveText`, `apps/portal/view/home/parts/hero/Content.mjs:47-74`: "An Agent Institution" first, then among others "Active Hybrid GraphRAG", "The Dream Pipeline", "Physics-Driven UI"); one stable paragraph, the README's hero sentence; two buttons, Get started (`/learn`) and View on GitHub. The paragraph uses three terms it does not explain: agent institution, record, Fleet Manager. Nothing on the screen says that Neo is also an application engine one builds on, or whom any of it is for.
- **Screens two to five** tell the Body through demonstrations of 2020 to 2022: "Amazing Potential · Shared Socket Data" (the Colors demo; its panel opens on ten lines of source, while the running demo and the Start button the text refers to sit behind a Preview tab), "Extreme Speed · 40,000 Updates /s" (the Helix), "How?" (the worker diagram) and six cards in engine vocabulary. No sentence joins the first screen's institution to this engine. The page's only audience hint is one bullet: "Ideal for Trading Platforms and Control Rooms". The Workstation, the grid and the Grid and Table overview (#20500) do not appear.
- **Screen six** is "The Agent OS Brain" with six cards; the footer follows. The footer links Slack and Discord and the header links Discord: fork F3 of #20677 asks whether those channels are live, and the design read decides them with the rest of the footer.
- **What the README says and the home does not:** the bridge ("Behind that sentence stands a self-evolving software organism … whose Body, Brain and Institution live in sibling open-source repositories"), the platform claim with its receipts ("a complete, batteries-included Application Engine for every desktop and mobile web application", 461 classes, 100,000-row grids, feature parity with Ext JS), and "Who This Is For" with its doors.

The stranger's three answers after the home: what is it, two things the page never joins; who is it for, not said; what next, Get started.

Instrument note: a headless capture cannot judge this page. The demo panels of screens two to four stayed empty there after 12 seconds and render in a real browser.

Why an epic: the hero, the door screen, the demonstrations, the snapshot and the closing read are separate one-PR leaves on one surface for several seats, and the direction is selected on a rendered page (the operator, Fri Nov 13) before a build leaf starts.

## Intended solution shape

1. **The home that exists is enhanced.** The header canvas, the hero's swarm canvas, the scroll pages, the footer canvas, the routes and the middleware snapshot stay (2,649 commits touch `apps/portal` on `dev`). Content and structure are open. A candidate is shown inside the running Portal, on a branch, with a before and an after capture in its pull request.
2. **The hero holds one statement still.** The hero sentence is the README's and stays; a wording change is an identity change and runs `neo-identity-update` against `learn/agentos/decisions/0018-neo-identity-source-of-truth-model.md`. What the headline shows, and for how long, is the first design question: today no phrase stands long enough to be the category.
3. **The second screen is the doors.** The audiences of the README's "Who This Is For", each with what it gets, one proof that exists today and one next step, in the Portal's own visual language. The reading order follows the record (`#14312`: the Brain first) unless a reader read on the rendered page says otherwise.
4. **The Body's screens show today's proofs.** Which demonstrations stay is the steward's decision in the design read. The candidates: the Workstation, the Grid and Table overview, and the Helix with its receipted 40,000 updates per second (`learn/benefits/body/Speed.md`). A demonstration that stays opens on the running thing.
5. **A claim keeps its receipt.** A number links the page that derives it, and the home names nothing that is not public (the boundary recorded in #20498's body).
6. **The snapshot says the same.** What a crawler and an agent read for the home route carries the same statement and doors; its metadata and markup quality belong to #20671.
7. **Done is a read.** The closing evidence is a stranger read of the deployed page by a seat that built none of it, and a cold-agent read of the snapshot. Traffic is no done signal.

**Sequence.** The steward's design read comes first: marked-up captures of the rendered page, locating the statement, the doors, the proofs and the next steps inside the existing screens (by Thu Nov 12). The operator selects on Fri Nov 13. Build leaves are filed after the selection and linked here as sub-issues.

## Out of scope

The examples and the learning section (#20498). Connect's support path (#20677). Search and classification (#20671). The living front door of D#14683 (the page rendering the institution's public work) stays a parked discussion; this epic does not graduate it. A new landing page, a change of the Portal's visual identity, pricing, an offer.

## Avoided traps

- A page or a card mock-up beside the product: it throws away the canvases, the routes and the middleware the site runs on.
- A new headline before the read: the sentence is the README's, and the stranger's trouble lies in what surrounds it.
- Flattening (the lesson recorded in #20498): conviction stays and a claim gets its receipt.
- The niche as the audience ("ideal for trading platforms"): the README's claim is every desktop and mobile web application, with extreme scale on top.
- Judging the page from a headless capture.
- A body that lists the leaves: they are linked as sub-issues when they exist.
