# Collapsing a planning Task Board column frees no width

## Context

The operator, 2026-11-24, testing `neomjs.com/dist/production/examples/dashboard/planning/index.html` (v14.0.0): collapsing the Task Board's `Ready` column shrinks it to a narrow strip, but `Active` and `Done` keep their positions and widths. The freed width stays empty: *"what is the point when collapsing a task board column does not free any space?"*

## The Problem

A collapsed column exists to give its width to the columns that are still open. In the planning dashboard it gives nothing. In the operator's frames, `Active`'s left edge sits at the same x before and after `Ready` collapses. The same holds at phone width, where the board lays out two columns per row.

## The Architectural Reality

- `resources/css/src/examples/dashboard/planning/Viewport.css:72-82` turns the board into `display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(3, minmax(0, 1fr))` and frees open columns with `width: auto`. `:117-130` does the same at `max-width: 650px` with `repeat(2, minmax(0, 1fr))`. Both shipped in v14.0.0, introduced by #22013.
- `resources/css/src/board/Column.css` gives `.neo-board-column-collapsed` `width: var(--neo-board-column-collapsed-width)`. In the grid, the collapsed column shrinks inside a track that keeps its full `1fr`, so nothing reflows.
- `Neo.board.Container` lays columns out as a flex row (`layout: {ntype: 'hbox', align: 'stretch'}`, `src/board/Container.mjs:141-143`). The example's `display: grid` overrides it.

Design authority: "a collapsed column keeps its header and count and hides its body" (`src/board/Column.mjs` class description), at `--neo-board-column-collapsed-width` (`board/Column.css`). `board/Container.css` states the consequence for a row of columns: "every lane, open, narrowed by its own collapsed columns". A collapsed column narrows its row; the planning example keeps the row as wide as before.

## The Fix

In `resources/css/src/examples/dashboard/planning/Viewport.css`, drop the grid and keep the board's flex row:
- Open columns share the width: `flex: 1 1 0; min-width: 0; width: auto`.
- A collapsed column keeps the board's collapsed width and does not grow.
- At `max-width: 650px`, wrap the row (`flex-wrap: wrap`) with open columns on a half-row basis, so a collapsed column's neighbour in its row takes the freed width.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] At 1600×1000, collapsing `Ready` narrows it to the collapsed width, and `Active` and `Done` take the freed width: `Active` moves left, and `Done`'s right edge stays at the board's content edge. Expanding restores equal widths.
- [ ] On the phone layout (390×844, `Task Board` tab), collapsing a column widens its row neighbour instead of leaving the gap empty.
- [ ] `test/playwright/e2e/dashboard/SharedPlanning.spec.mjs` asserts both, red on `dev` and green on the branch; the PR body embeds before/after frames.

## Out of Scope

- An engine-level "columns fill the board" mode in `Neo.board.Container`. One example needs it today.
- Other examples' board layouts.

## Avoided Traps

- Keeping the grid and sizing tracks per column with `:has()` / `:nth-child()`: track counts are per example, and the engine's own flex row already does this.
