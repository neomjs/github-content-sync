# ROADMAP: the next Engine cut ships after its known defects

## Context

Engine v14.0.0 shipped on 2026-11-23 ([release notes](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/releases/tag/14.0.0)). `ROADMAP.md` still reads `## Next: Engine 14 — The Front Door`, the pre-release plan.

The operator, 24 Nov: "we did imho not update the roadmap post v14. while we don't know yet if minor or patch release, we could start with adding important tickets in there, to ensure the known defects get addressed". Earlier the same evening, with #22121 as the example, he called open bugs without a resolving PR known bugs we would ship, "so imho release blockers".

## The Problem

The roadmap's next section describes a release that has already shipped. The defects found since the cut have no place in the plan: 10 open issues carry `bug`, three of them have no owner, and only one bug has a fix branch in flight (#21974).

## The Architectural Reality

- `ROADMAP.md` holds the next-release scope only (its own header). The line table and the re-read paragraph stay as they are.
- `.agents/skills/update-roadmap`: the next section **replaces** the shipped one; the roadmap gives cornerstones and rationale, with the live set queried rather than listed; the deferred set is explicit; stewards self-select.
- Design authority: the `ROADMAP.md` header ("The roadmap holds the next-release scope only") and the operator's 24 Nov direction quoted above.

## The Fix

In `ROADMAP.md`:
- The shipped-history line links v14.0.0 instead of v13.4.0.
- `## Next: Engine 14 — The Front Door` is replaced by the next cut:
  - **Thesis:** no known defect ships. The version follows from what lands, and the operator decides it at the cut.
  - **Gate:** every open `bug` on a shipped Engine or Portal surface is fixed on `dev`, or deferred in the section with its reason.
  - **Live set:** the open-`bug` query. A dated table groups the known defects by surface, with an owner per issue or a visible "open".
  - **Also in the section:** the still-open v14 epics beside the gate, the deferred set, how the cut runs, and session intake.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `ROADMAP.md`'s next section names the follow-up cut, its gate and the live query. No Engine 14 pre-release text remains as "Next".
- [ ] Every issue open with `bug` at merge time appears in the dated table, with its owner or "open".
- [ ] The shipped-history line links v14.0.0.
- [ ] The deferred set is explicit.

## Out of Scope

- Fixing the bugs: each one has its own issue.
- Creating the next milestone or closing `v14.0` (#14, 6 open items). That needs a milestone write, which this seat's tooling was refused before.
- The Brain and Institution lines.
