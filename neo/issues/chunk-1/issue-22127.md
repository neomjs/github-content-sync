# Keep trailing single Portal example cards at 1×1 column width

Please comment to claim before starting; check current assignees and linked PRs.

## Context

On the live v14.0.0 Portal (`Complete Apps`, `#/examples/apps`), `On Air at Eight` (`apps/portal/resources/data/examples/apps.json:165-187`) stretches across all three desktop columns (`grid-column: 1 / -1`) as a horizontal banner while the three cards directly above it (`Multi-Step Account Form`, `Live Finance Desk`, `Multi-Window Email Client`) render as standard `1×1` cards. Operator visual review on 2026-11-24 confirmed the trailing card should stay a standard `1×1` card in column 1 rather than flexing across the row.

## The Problem

`#22024` / PR `#22025` added a `3n + 4` final-row rule (`4`, `7`, or `10` visible demonstration cards) so groups ending with one card on their last row fill all three columns. In practice on `Complete Apps` (`11` records in `apps.json`, reduced to `10` when `Portal App` hides itself in the matching build via `List.mjs:447`), `Workers & Platform` (`4`), `Lists & Galleries` (`7`), and `Multi-Window` (`10`), the horizontal stretch introduces four defects:

1. **Inverted visual hierarchy & ~67% empty surface:** `GroupPage` already leads with a `2×2` hero card (`Workstation` at index 0). Stretching whichever card sorts last into a `~1,000 px` row gives the tail card hero width while leaving the `2fr` right pane (`~670 × 190 px`) ~80% empty dark background with `Open live` stranded `400+ px` from the title — amplified when `.neo-selected` draws the green selection ring around the empty expanse.
2. **`+16 px` column-seam drift:** The outer list uses `grid-template-columns: repeat(3, minmax(0, 1fr))` with `column-gap: 24px` (`(W - 48px) / 3` per card), whereas `.content` inside the stretched card uses `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr) minmax(0, 2fr)` with `gap: 0` (`W / 3`). That makes the stretched card's cover `16 px` wider (and proportionally taller at `16:9`) than column 1 directly above it, visibly misaligning the vertical border against the grid seam above.
3. **`48 px` container-query mismatch (`1052–1099 px` list width):** `List.css:61` switches to 3 columns at `@container (min-width: 1100px)` on `examples-list` (inside `GroupPage`'s `24 px × 2` horizontal padding), while `List.css:81` fires the `3n + 4` full-row rule at `@container examples-group-page (min-width: 1100px)` (outside that padding). When `examples-group-page` is `1100–1147 px` wide (`1364–1411 px` viewport with the `264 px` sidebar), the list is still a **2-column** grid (`10` cards = 5 full pairs with zero orphans), yet the `3n + 4` rule fires anyway — stranding card `#9` alone on row 5 and stretching card `#10` across row 6.
4. **Build-mode layout jump:** Switching the build selector on `Complete Apps` so `Portal App` becomes visible (`10 → 11` cards) abruptly collapses `On Air at Eight` from a full-width horizontal row back to a `1×1` card.

## The Architectural Reality

- `resources/css/src/apps/portal/examples/List.css:81-108`: `@container examples-group-page (min-width: 1100px)` applies `grid-column: 1 / -1` and side-by-side `.content` / `.portal-example-footer` / `7.5cqi` `.portal-example-more` overrides to `.portal-examples-group-page &.portal-examples-list-lead > .neo-list-item:nth-last-child(1 of .neo-list-item):nth-child(3n + 4 of .neo-list-item)`.
- `resources/css/src/apps/portal/examples/GroupPage.css:3`: declares `container-name: examples-group-page;` solely for `List.css:81` (verified via repo-wide search across `apps/`, `resources/`, and `test/`).
- `test/playwright/e2e/portal/ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs:180-181,197-198,219-222`: `expectCardLayout` carries the `wide` / `beside` branch added in `#22052` to accommodate `#22025`'s side-by-side final-row card.
- `apps/portal/view/examples/ListModel.mjs#stepRow`: already resolves vertical `ArrowDown` / `ArrowUp` targets from live card bounding rectangles, so a trailing `1×1` card in column 1 remains directly reachable from column 1 above it (and via fallback from columns 2–3).

Design authority: "Non-tile cards separate .preview-image-wrap (unoccluded at rest and on hover, with a hard-light reflection) from .portal-example-footer" (`apps/portal/view/examples/List.mjs:265-268`; operator visual direction on 2026-11-24 superseding `#22024`'s full-row stretch while preserving `#21140` / `#22049`'s 3-column grid, `2×2` lead card, and `16:9` cover invariant).

## The Fix

1. Remove the `@container examples-group-page (min-width: 1100px)` lone-row override block in `resources/css/src/apps/portal/examples/List.css:81-108` and the now-unused `container-name: examples-group-page;` line in `resources/css/src/apps/portal/examples/GroupPage.css:3` (rebuilding compiled CSS themes if applicable).
2. Simplify `expectCardLayout` in `test/playwright/e2e/portal/ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs` (`L180-181`, `L197-198`, `L219-222`) so every non-tile demonstration card — including trailing `3n + 4` cards — asserts the standard `1×1` docked footer below an unoccluded `16:9` cover, and add/update a `1440×900` E2E assertion (red on `dev` base, green with fix) verifying that `Complete Apps` / `Forms & Fields` (`3n + 4` cards) render the final card at single-column width aligned with column 1 (`Math.abs(lastCard.width - column1Card.width) < 1.5`) while `ArrowDown` still reaches and selects the final card.

Decision Record impact: none.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] On desktop (`1440×900` and `1366×768`), `3n + 4` demonstration groups (`Complete Apps`, `Forms & Fields`, `Workers & Platform`, `Lists & Galleries`, `Multi-Window`) render their trailing final card at standard single-column (`1×1`) width aligned with column 1, with `.portal-example-footer` docked below an unoccluded `16:9` cover and zero `1052–1099 px` two-column orphan blowout.
- [ ] The `2×2` lead card (`#22049`), two-column (`1280×720`), phone (`390×844`), and component tile grids preserve their existing geometry, and `ArrowDown` still selects and focuses the trailing card with `.portal-example-more` details opening in-frame (`<= 22.5cqi`).
- [ ] `test/playwright/e2e/portal/ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs` removes the `wide`/`beside` carve-out, fails on `dev` (`On Air at Eight` / `Forms & Fields` final card currently spanning `> 0.9` list width), and passes on the branch; PR body embeds before/after full-tab frames of `Complete Apps` showing the trailing row.

## Out of Scope

- Changing catalog membership, ordering, or `Portal App` self-hiding (`List.mjs:447`).
- Arrow-key traversal between demonstration cards and component tiles (`#22121`) or compact tile glass hover (`#22122`).

## Avoided Traps

- **Fixing only the `24 px` column-gap math and `examples-list` container query while keeping the `1 / -1` horizontal banner:** Fixes seam alignment and the `1366 px` 2-column bug, but leaves the core hierarchy inversion and ~67% empty dark surface intact.
- **Stretching the lead card to `2×3` to absorb the extra card:** Already falsified in `#22024` / `#22049` (crops the `16:9` lead cover).
