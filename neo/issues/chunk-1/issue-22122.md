# Give compact example tiles the Portal’s glass hover reflection

Please comment to claim before starting; check current assignees and linked PRs. This is a bounded visual change with a public reproduction.

## Context

On [Home](https://neomjs.com/home), hover the small **Neural Link** card in the Agent OS Brain section. A diagonal glint crosses it. On [Forms & Fields](https://neomjs.com/examples/forms), hover **Multi-Step Account Form**, then **Checkbox** under *Field types*. In Chrome on v14, the demonstration gets a reflection, while the component tile lights its edge and shadow only. Both previews lead to live examples.

## Design intent

The [Home design](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22063) extended the glass treatment to the smaller cards. The [Examples design](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21676#issuecomment-7069191068) uses the reflection for the pointer, a green ring for selection and a neutral keyboard focus ring. Its original visual acceptance criterion covered non-tile cards. Extend that pointer cue to the compact previews so the change in density does not signal a different kind of interaction.

## Implementation starting point

The [Examples stylesheet](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/14.0.0/resources/css/src/apps/portal/examples/List.css#L241-L292) scopes the cover reflection to non-tile lists. [The tile renderer](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/14.0.0/apps/portal/view/examples/List.mjs#L283) renders the 4:3 crop directly. Reuse the catalog’s existing reflection, theme tokens and motion timing on that preview. Keep the compact layout and the stationary caption and launch icon.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On a pointer-capable device, entering a compact tile gives its preview one glint and the existing resting hover light. Verify **Checkbox** on Forms and a compact tile in another group, in light and dark themes.
- [ ] Hover leaves the component legible at its existing 4:3 size, keeps the launch target stationary, and allows the selection and keyboard focus indicators to remain distinguishable.
- [ ] With reduced motion enabled, hover gives a static cue. Tapping the launch link still opens the example on the first tap.

Vertical keyboard traversal and shared selection between sections are tracked in #22121. Related: #21333, #21676, #22063.

The earlier tickets scoped the Home and demonstration-card treatments. No open issue covered the compact tile reflection. Available for a contributor with maintainer review.
