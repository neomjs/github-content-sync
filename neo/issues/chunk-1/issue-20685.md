# ROADMAP: the home and search cornerstones enter the 13.5 table

## Context

`ROADMAP.md`'s 13.5 section says a cornerstone enters the table "once it has an epic and one self-selected steward", and names two as on the way: the home section and search discoverability. Both conditions now hold for both:

- #20682, "The home section says what Neo is and who it is for": steward Clio, milestone v13.5, filed 2026-11-10.
- #20671, "Search engines and agents find and classify neomjs.com": steward Julia, milestone v13.5, filed 2026-11-09.

## The Problem

The roadmap is the session intake for contributors and agents. It still tells them that two cornerstones have no epic, so it points nobody at the two epics or their leaves. The gate's third clause, "finds a support path on Connect", has no anchor in the section either, although `#20677` delivers it.

## The Architectural Reality

- `ROADMAP.md`, section "Next: Engine 13.5 — The Front Door": the cornerstone table has two rows (`#20498`, `#20667`); the paragraph "Not yet in the table" and its two bullets follow it; "Carried in the milestone" names `#20664`.
- Each epic body opens with a `Terminal predicate:` line. The table's "Done signal" column summarizes that line for rows 1 and 2.
- The `update-roadmap` workflow's core rule: cornerstones are linked, the full work set is the milestone's items, and nothing is listed in prose that the milestone already holds.

Design authority: `ROADMAP.md`, "A cornerstone enters it once it has an epic and one self-selected steward."

## The Fix

In `ROADMAP.md` only:

- Two rows join the cornerstone table: the home section (`#20682`, Clio) and search discoverability (`#20671`, Julia). Each done signal summarizes its epic's terminal predicate.
- The "Not yet in the table" paragraph and its two bullets go, since nothing is waiting.
- "Carried in the milestone" gains the support path on Connect, with `#20677`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The cornerstone table has four rows. Rows 3 and 4 link `#20682` and `#20671`, name Clio and Julia as stewards, and carry a done signal that agrees in substance with the epic's `Terminal predicate:` line.
- [ ] The "Not yet in the table" paragraph and both bullets are gone, and no other sentence of the section says a cornerstone is waiting for an epic or a steward.
- [ ] "Carried in the milestone" names the support path on Connect and links `#20677`.

## Out of Scope

- The epics' own bodies. `#20682`'s Related line points at the paragraph this removes; its steward updates it.
- The gate sentence, the deferred set and the cadence paragraph.

## Avoided Traps

- **Copying each terminal predicate into the table verbatim.** That is a second copy, and it drifts the first time an epic's predicate is sharpened. The row summarizes and links.
