# Grid: a hidden pre-render measures rows ahead of mounting

## Context

This is leaf L3 of epic #19658, blocked by #19662 (in-place measurement). It measures rows before they mount, and height-driving columns that are scrolled out of view, so heights are known when rows appear.

## The Problem

In-place measurement only sees mounted rows and columns. Two cases go unmeasured:
- rows entering at wheel speed (41–71 per second on bigData, D#19645 probe 6);
- a height-driving column that is horizontally unmounted.

The estimate for unseen rows needs refining as measurements arrive.

## The Architectural Reality

- **The precedent:** `MagicMoveText.measureChars` renders a hidden measure element, waits for the VDom update, then batch-reads rects.
- **The extents:** a 100k-row PoC stays within native element-extent limits (about 4.8M px at mixed heights). Scaled scrolling is out of scope.
- **The costs:** a hidden pre-render costs about 1 ms per 100 cells and about 4 ms per 300 in three engines (probe 4). The existing scroll-to-fresh-rows pipeline takes 50–80 ms (probe 6).

## The Fix

- **The hidden subtree:** a `visibility: hidden` subtree inside the grid's own DOM and style scope, never `display: none` or detached, since those have no layout box. It renders the declared text cells of not-yet-mounted rows, plus off-screen height-driving columns, at their actual column widths, using L2's cell CSS. It is read through the same batched `getLayoutRect` call and the same generation fence.
- **Measure-ahead:** measure the rows that lie about one pipeline-latency of scroll beyond the overscan window, and refine further rows in idle time.
- **Estimates:** unseen rows use the mean of measured heights, never in place of a known height. The total extent is called estimated while unseen rows remain.
- **Font arrival:** the main thread's `document.fonts` events bump the font generation, which invalidates and re-measures.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A hidden-measured cell and the same cell measured displayed give equal heights at fractional widths; this is a spec.
- [ ] A height-driving column that is horizontally unmounted still drives its row's height; this is a spec.
- [ ] Font arrival invalidates and re-measures, and no height measured with the fallback font survives; this is a spec.
- [ ] At ordinary wheel speed, rows mount with measured heights. The share of rows mounted at the estimate is recorded in the PR.
- [ ] Unseen rows use the measured mean, and known heights are never replaced by it.

## Out of Scope

Scroll correction with pinning (L4), `AnimateRows` (L5), the 100k journey and integrated throughput acceptance (L6).

## Avoided Traps

- `display: none` or detached nodes, which measure 0.
- A general measurement service in v1.
- Measuring before fonts are ready without invalidating afterwards.
