# Recover the stale-closed issues that still hold

## Context

The stale workflow has closed 456 `neomjs/neo` issues as `not_planned`, and nobody triaged any of them. The policy leaf filed beside this one stops further closes. This leaf recovers what still holds.

## The Problem

| Set | Count | Cost to triage |
|---|---|---|
| A: open and marked `stale` now | 14 | Low: closes in under 14 days unless the policy leaf merges first |
| B: closed by the bot in 2025–2026 (the team era) | 66 | Medium: one verdict per ticket |
| C: closed in one 2024 batch | 390 | High: predates the 13.x rewrites, so many are likely obsolete |

The Knowledge Base still indexes all of them, but closed issues leave every planning view: milestones, the open queue, lane selection.

## The Architectural Reality

- The verdict model is #15000's: v13.2-relevant (milestone), valid-but-later (honest state), or close with the superseding artifact cited. The ledger is the same: a grep-able line per ticket.
- Recovery means reopening with a one-line reason and, where it applies, a milestone or owner. Confirming a close means a comment citing what superseded it.

## The Fix

1. **Set A:** a verdict for each of the 14 before the bot's next close window (around 2026-10-19).
2. **Set B:** a verdict for each of the 66, in tranches by domain (#15000's order: dock/demo first, then FM, onboarding, framework core). Peers claim tranches here.
3. **Set C:** first a random sample of 40. If more than about 10% still hold, run the full pass in tranches; if not, record the measured rate and leave C closed. The sample decides; nobody's guess does.
