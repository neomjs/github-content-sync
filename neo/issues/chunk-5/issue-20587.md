# The off-socket Neural Link adapter fails six window specs

## Context

#20451 retires the Brain-gated e2e tier. Every Neural Link spec now runs off-socket in the engine tier, or `LOCAL_ONLY` in `buildScripts/util/e2eCiSelection.mjs` states why it stays local (PR #20584). On 2026-11-08 a local off-socket probe ran the 42 window-driving specs: 37 files were clean, and they move into the tier. Six window specs fail on the adapter and pass over the Bridge. With the Brain tier gone, no CI job runs them. This ticket owns their `LOCAL_ONLY` entry.

## The Problem

Measured off-socket, locally, on one worker:

- **Re-boot.**
  - `workstation/DockSplitterZoneIdNL` "3 consecutive boots" fails 3/3 off-socket and passes 2/2 over the Bridge (#20461). After a re-boot the dock host never becomes visible: the adapter's SharedWorker handle does not survive the re-boot.
  - `workstation/WorkstationResetSurvivesReloadNL` (2 arms) and `workstation/WorkstationTopologyGroupsNL` (4 arms) fail after `page.reload` with `neuralLink.connectToApp(...)`: "the page's App Worker … did not initialize Neo.ai.Client within 30000 ms".
  - `workstation/WorkstationFiveBeatNL` (2 arms) times out in its `boot()`, which waits 30 s for `.workstation-tour-play` after re-navigating.
- **Closed bound page.** `realworld/EmailMultiWindowNL` closes its source page (`page.close({runBeforeUnload: true})`). Then: "Tearing down "neuralLink" exceeded the test timeout of 90000ms".
- **Topology.** `neural-link/WindowOps` compares a drag receipt with `popupWindow.innerRect.x` from `get_window_topology`. The value is `undefined`: a `toBeCloseTo` matcher error at line 158.

Observation versus inference:

- The re-boot cause is measured on `DockSplitterZoneIdNL` only. The other three re-boot specs share its symptom, an App Worker that no longer answers after a reload, but not yet a proven cause.
- A SharedWorker app keeps its App Worker across `page.reload`, so a reload does not reset worker state (#18770). That bears on the re-boot specs but does not decide their cause.
- The `EmailMultiWindowNL` and `WindowOps` causes are not established.

## The Architectural Reality

- `test/playwright/dedicatedWorkerNeuralLink.mjs` is the adapter. Its `get_window_topology` reads `Neo.manager.Window.items` in the App Worker, then adds the windows of `Neo.apps` that have no item, and those come without `innerRect`. An item gets `innerRect` only from `Neo.manager.Window#onWindowConnect` (`src/manager/Window.mjs:181`), and only when the connect carried `windowData`.
- `test/playwright/sharedWorkerAppWorker.mjs` (`attachPageSharedAppWorker`) holds the page's SharedWorker handle, the one a re-boot invalidates.
- `test/playwright/externalBrainSelection.mjs` lists the eligible specs (`ADAPTER_ELIGIBLE_SPECS`). `buildScripts/util/e2eCiSelection.mjs` holds `RUN_PATHS`, `LOCAL_ONLY` and the duration table the shards balance by.

## The Fix

In the adapter:

1. After a re-boot (reload or re-navigation), re-attach to the page's App Worker instead of keeping the first handle.
2. Release a binding whose page closed, instead of waiting on it at teardown.
3. Find why the `WindowOps` popup reaches the topology without `innerRect`, and make the off-socket topology carry it.

Then, for each spec green on a hosted run:

- move it from `LOCAL_ONLY` into `ADAPTER_ELIGIBLE_SPECS`, and into `RUN_PATHS` for `workstation/`;
- give it a duration in `e2eEngineDurations.json`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `DockSplitterZoneIdNL` "3 consecutive boots" passes off-socket 3/3 locally, red first: 0/3 on the base.
- [ ] Each of the six specs passes off-socket on two hosted engine-tier runs and leaves `LOCAL_ONLY`. A spec that cannot keeps an entry that states its measured cause.
- [ ] Each adapter change has an arm that fails without it.

## Out of Scope

- The Bridge and Brain-service classes of `LOCAL_ONLY`. They test the Brain itself and stay local.
- Rewriting Brain-service specs to the off-socket SDK.
