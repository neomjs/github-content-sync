# A group header drag slides its neighbours and carries its column cells

## Context

The operator's manual retest of `examples/grid/headerGroups` on `dev` (`36e14fa4ac`, after #20630) found that a group header drag works but feels unlike every other drag in the product. The recording shows Q2 dragged after Q3. The proxy is the bare "Q2" label bar, the headers and columns underneath stay put until the release, and then everything jumps at once.

A leaf column drag, a tab drag and an ungrouped header drag all behave differently: the dragged item's neighbours slide aside live as it passes them, and a column drag's proxy carries the column's body cells. The operator: "for tabs as well as non-grouped headers, the not dragged headers slide then drag-moving. this part is missing. also, when dragging a column header, … the drag proxy includes column cells. missing too."

## The Problem

#20627 asked for a drop-time design, and #20630 shipped it: "the group row and the leaf row re-project once on drop". The slot logic is right (never inside another group, never out of its region, scrolled or not). What it lacks is the feedback the leaf drag gives: a live preview of the new order, and a proxy that shows what is moving.

## The Architectural Reality

- **Leaf drag:** `src/draggable/grid/header/toolbar/SortZone.mjs`, extending `src/draggable/container/SortZone.mjs`. `onDragMove` calls `switchItems` once the pointer crosses a neighbour's threshold; that's the live slide. The header zone builds its proxy with the column's body cells cloned in (`body.getColumnCells(dataField)`, the proxy-cell block around lines 161–231). Groups are already respected there: a leaf stays inside its group, and a release outside it parks (`isOutsideGroup`, `setReorderParked`).
- **Group drag:** `src/grid/header/GroupRow.mjs` runs its own gesture on the base `Neo.draggable.DragZone`, with `useProxyWrapper: false`. The proxy is the cloned group cell. The slot comes from `GroupRow.getGroupDropOrder` over `GroupRow.getUnits`. The commit is `columns.move` per leaf, then `grid.Container#onColumnsMutate`, then `Toolbar#measureAfterUpdate`. The zone registers under the row, and the cells turn draggable only once that registration lands.

## The Fix

The group drag gets the leaf drag's feedback, without a second gesture engine:

1. **Live preview.** While a group cell drags, its run of leaves moves as one block among the region's top-level units. A neighbouring unit (a group, or an ungrouped column) slides aside when the block passes its centre, with the leaf zone's switch animation. The group row re-projects with the leaves.
2. **Proxy payload.** The proxy shows the group cell over its leaf headers and their body cells, the way a leaf proxy shows its column.
3. **Commit = preview.** The release keeps the previewed order with no second jump. Escape slides everything back.

Reuse before adding: either give the header `SortZone` a unit mode that switches a run of items, or extract its switch and cell-proxy code into helpers that both drags call. `GroupRow.getUnits` / `getGroupDropOrder` keep owning which slots are legal.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Dragging Q3 toward Q2 slides Q2's leaves and body columns aside live once Q3's proxy passes Q2's centre. The release commits that order with no further movement (e2e on `examples/grid/headerGroups`: mid-drag, Q2's first leaf header has moved right by Q3's width before the release).
- [ ] The group proxy shows the group cell, its leaf headers and their rendered body cells (e2e: mid-drag, the proxy holds Jul, Aug and Sep header buttons and the first row's Jul cell value).
- [ ] The existing #20627 contract holds: never inside another group, never out of its region, scrolled grids, Escape mid-drag restoring the original order (the HeaderGroups e2e group arms keep passing).
- [ ] A leaf drag, in a group or ungrouped, is unchanged (the HeaderGroups leaf arms keep passing).
- [ ] The operator's manual pass on `examples/grid/headerGroups` accepts the gesture.

`Prescription checked: src/draggable/grid/header/toolbar/SortZone.mjs (at #20632's ffa5c47ecf) — owns the concern`

At that head the header zone already does everything the group drag lacks:
- live switching with body cells (`swapNeighbours` moves a header and its column's cells);
- group-aware switch targets (`getSwitchTarget` / `getSwitchExtent` move an ungrouped header past a whole group in one step);
- the group edge (`groupOverflow` / `moveToGroupEdge`);
- the cancel restore (inherited from `container/SortZone`).

A second engine beside it would fork all of that.

**Plan:** a unit mode in that zone. A group cell's drag starts the region toolbar's zone with the group's run of headers as one unit, and every neighbour it passes is a whole unit (a group, or an ungrouped header), so it can't land inside a group. The proxy holds the group cell over its leaf headers and their cells. The drop commits the previewed order.

**Deletion this enables:** GroupRow's own gesture goes, namely the base-DragZone setup, the start fence, `moveGroup` and `getGroupDropOrder`. GroupRow keeps the projection and hands the gesture to the zone, so one drag engine serves leaves and groups.

Base: branched from #20632's head and rebased onto dev once #20632 merges. Vega is consulted before any edit that touches her `groupOverflow` / `moveToGroupEdge` seams.
