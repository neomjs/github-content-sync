# Lists & Galleries part 2: drag reorder, the animated list, three tiles

Owned: a leaf of #21367 until its lock, Sun 22 Nov 18:00 CET. Part 2 of Lists & Galleries; part 1 is #21454 (PR #21458).

## Context

#21367's row for Lists & Galleries keeps `list/animate`, renews `component/carousel` with images, `list/buffered` (its count in the stage) and `list/circle` (fits its stage), and adds reorder by drag, in a list and in a card grid. #21140's target is 5 to 7 cards; part 1 keeps four. Measured on `dev` 9f8be2ddb7 at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844:

- `list/animate`, a `Neo.container.Viewport` with a card grid of 15 portraits, has no catalog record. At 390 its toolbar runs off the right edge: "Is online" is cut, "Measured rows" and "Order by row" are off screen.
- `component/carousel` shows three text reviews in a 500 × 500 box, no image; at 390 the box is wider than its 368-px stage. Its record (`carousel`, family `lists`) has `image: null`, so it does not publish.
- `list/buffered` holds 5,000 records (`examples/list/buffered/MainStore.mjs:14`); its stage shows ten rows and no count, so nothing says the list is buffered. No record.
- `list/circle` is 1000 × 1000 by default: wider than its 904-px stage at 1440 and its 368-px stage at 390. No record.
- No example reorders a list by drag. `Neo.draggable.list.SortZone` (#21078) has one consumer, the Web Desktop's launcher (`src/desktop/Launcher.mjs`).

## The Problem

A visitor to Lists & Galleries sees galleries only. The examples that show what a list does (animate, buffer, reorder by drag) are unpublished or missing, and the three workbench entries that could be tiles open on a stage that cuts them or says nothing about them.

## The Architectural Reality

Design authority: #21367's row above; #21140's design 4: "A *demonstration* has a story and data, and gets a cover card. A *component* entry is one component in the workbench, and gets a compact tile"; and `Neo.draggable.list.SortZone`'s JSDoc: "Reorders a store-bound list's records by drag and drop, in any layout", where "The store needs a sorter on `sortField` with `reapplyOnRecordChange: true`."

- `Neo.list.Base#draggable` with `dragZoneConfig: {module: SortZone}` makes a list sortable (`src/list/Base.mjs:68-81`); `sortDirection: 'horizontal'` serves rows of cards.
- `list/buffered`, `list/circle` and `component/carousel` extend `Neo.examples.ConfigurationViewport`, so they are workbench entries; `list/animate` is not. A tile is a record with `kind: 'component'` whose crop comes from the tile recipe's path list in `example-captures.json` (#21333); a record turns `kind: 'component'` in the pull request that gives it its crop.
- Records: `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json`; recipes: `example-captures.json`; `seo.json`; covers and crops ship in a `neomjs/pages` pull request. Count specs: `MainContainerController.spec.mjs`, `ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs`.

## The Fix

- New `examples/list/reorder`: a list and a card grid, each reordered by drag through `SortZone` on its own store (a sorter on `order` with `reapplyOnRecordChange: true`). A card.
- `list/animate`: its toolbar wraps at 390. A card.
- `component/carousel`: image slides from the portraits `list/animate` and the gallery already load from `resources/examples/` (no new binaries), in a box that fits its stage. Its record turns into a tile.
- `list/buffered`: the stage names how many records the list holds and how many rows it renders. A tile.
- `list/circle`: sized to its stage. A tile.
- Records, recipes, SEO entries, covers and crops; the count specs follow. Six cards after this leaf.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: In `examples/list/reorder`, a dragged list item or grid card lands where it is dropped and the store's order follows; an e2e spec drags in both.
- [ ] AC-2: At 390 × 844 none of the five pages cuts a label or a control.
- [ ] AC-3: The carousel's slides show images.
- [ ] AC-4: The buffered list's stage names its record count and its rendered rows.
- [ ] AC-5: The circle list fits its stage at 1440 × 900 and at 390 × 844.
- [ ] AC-6: `list-reorder` and `list-animate` publish as cards with covers taken after their content is drawn; the carousel, the buffered list and the circle list publish as tiles; the count specs follow.

## Out of Scope

- Reorder by drag inside `list/animate`: its sort buttons own that store's order.
- Engine changes beyond what an AC needs; a fault found in `SortZone` becomes its own leaf under #21367.
- Renaming the COVID-era classes of part 1 (`coronaGallery`, `coronaHelix`): after the cut.

## Avoided Traps

- Drag reorder added to `list/animate`: a drag order and a field sort would fight over one store's order.
- Hand-made covers for the three workbench entries: a tile takes its crop from the recipe (#21140, design 4).
