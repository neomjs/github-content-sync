# Buttons, Menus & Toolbars shows a context menu, a breadcrumb and a paging toolbar working

Owned: a leaf of #21367 until its lock, Sun 22 Nov 18:00 CET. The group's renewal, after its defects (#21430 merged, #21434 in review).

## Context

#21140's target for Buttons, Menus & Toolbars is 4 cards; it shows none. The rows on #21142 ([comment](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21142#issuecomment-6980244557)) renew `menu/context`, `toolbar/breadcrumb` and `toolbar/paging`, add an application toolbar whose items arrive as JSON, and leave `button/effect`, `menu/panel` and `serverside/toolbarItems` without a card. Measured on `dev` 87e0ae1bc4 at 1280 × 720:

- `examples/menu/context` is the page `test/playwright/e2e/rendering/ContextMenuNL.spec.mjs` drives: a dark workspace that says "Right-click anywhere in this workspace", a "Focusable outside target" button and "Last action: none". It tests the menu; it does not show what a context menu is for.
- `examples/toolbar/breadcrumb` follows a tree of buttons (Home, Accessibility, Forms › Form 1 › Page 2) that navigate nowhere.
- `examples/toolbar/paging` shows zero rows: its store reads a remote API (`remotes-api.json`).
- `examples/grid/serverSide` (#21384) already runs `Neo.toolbar.Paging` against the seeded `SimulatedConnection`, and it publishes in Grid & Table with a cover.

## The Problem

A visitor who opens Buttons, Menus & Toolbars finds no card. The context menu's page is a test harness, the breadcrumb walks a tree of placeholder buttons, and the paging toolbar pages through nothing. Rebuilding `toolbar/paging` on #21189's simulated server would copy `grid/serverSide`, which already shows that toolbar paging real rows.

## The Architectural Reality

Design authority: #21367's terminal predicate, "every label drawn, focus where the page promises, nothing cut at 390 px, no card that opens on an error", and #21140's card count.

- Records: `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json`; a record joins another group through `alsoIn` and counts once, in its home group. Capture recipes: `apps/portal/resources/data/example-captures.json`; images ship in a `neomjs/pages` pull request.
- `Neo.menu.List` opens as a context menu from a `contextmenu` DOM event; its submenus come from nested `items` (#21434 is the tree-store path).
- `Neo.toolbar.Breadcrumb` renders the path to its `activeKey` from a store of records with `parentId`.
- The catalog count specs follow every card: `test/playwright/unit/apps/portal/view/examples/MainContainerController.spec.mjs` and `test/playwright/e2e/portal/ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs`.

## The Fix

- A new `examples/menu/contextFiles/`: project files in a list; a right-click on a file selects it and opens a context menu (Open, Duplicate, Move to › folders, Star, Delete) whose actions change the list, and a right-click on the free space offers New file. `examples/menu/context` stays as the spec's harness.
- `examples/toolbar/breadcrumb` renewed on a real hierarchy, the engine repository's folders: the page shows the current folder's description and its subfolders; a subfolder goes one level down, a crumb goes back up, and the hash route holds the place. (A `Neo.tree.List` beside it was dropped: its folder click toggles the folder rather than navigating.)
- `grid-server-side` gets `alsoIn: ['buttons']`, which shows the paging toolbar in this group without a second copy of the simulated server.
- Records, recipes and covers for the two new cards; the count specs follow.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: In `examples/menu/contextFiles`, a right-click on a file selects it and opens its context menu at the pointer; each action changes the list as its label says; Escape closes the menu.
- [ ] AC-2: In `examples/toolbar/breadcrumb`, a subfolder shows its path in the breadcrumb and lists its own subfolders; a crumb returns to that ancestor; a reload keeps the place.
- [ ] AC-3: At 390 × 844 neither page cuts a label or a control.
- [ ] AC-4: Buttons, Menus & Toolbars shows the two cards with covers taken after their content is drawn, and `grid-server-side` as a card from Grid & Table.
- [ ] AC-5: The catalog count specs follow.

## Out of Scope

- The application toolbar whose items arrive as JSON: a contributor leaf, filed beside this one.
- The split button and menu tree tiles: the tile lane (#21329, #21333) takes them once #21430 and #21434 are merged.
- Deleting `button/effect`, `menu/panel`, `serverside/toolbarItems` or `toolbar/paging`: they get no card; their folders stay.

## Avoided Traps

- A second simulated server for `toolbar/paging`: it would copy `examples/grid/serverSide/SimulatedConnection.mjs` to show the same toolbar.
- Renewing `menu/context` in place: the e2e spec drives that page and its markers.
