# Dock Pane Pop-out says what it proves, with content in its panes

## Context

#21140's target renews `dashboard/tearOut` in the Dashboard & Docking group: "↻ its claim said on screen, with content". My rows of 18 Nov on #21142 (`issuecomment-6980130976`) read it: "its claim (a pane leaves the window and keeps what you typed) is nowhere on screen; the panes are empty but for one 'Draft' field". Band order on #21140 (`issuecomment-7006670970`): this leaf follows #21465.

Ownership: a maintainer lane in #21140's roadmap table, due before the lock (Sun 22 Nov 18:00 CET), so no contributor labels.

## The Problem

On `dev` at `e2272a9f2b` the record `dock-tear-out` has `image: null`, so the group shows no card for it (`apps/portal/model/Example.mjs`: a record without a cover publishes no card). Its Queue, Metrics and Alerts panes hold only their own names, and nothing on the page says what to try. A visitor who opens it sees four labels and a text field.

## The Architectural Reality

- `examples/dashboard/tearOut/Workspace.mjs` is a `Neo.dashboard.dock.Workspace` with four panes (`queue`, `metrics`, `alerts`, `notes`), `enableDockTearOutLifecycle: true` and a cross-window sort group; `Viewport.mjs` mounts it, or stays an empty `tear-out-target` in a `?tearout=<id>` window.
- `test/playwright/e2e/dashboard/DockTearOutDefaultRoute.spec.mjs` pins the Notes tab, its pop-out action, `?tearout=notes`, and that the page's first `input` is the Notes draft.
- The cover pattern is #21465's: a recipe in `example-captures.json` (family `dashboard` is declared), a capture with `captureExamples.mjs --family dashboard`, the image in a `neomjs/pages` pull request.

## The Fix

1. The Queue, Metrics and Alerts panes show a small support desk: open tickets as a list bound to a store of records, three figures, recent alerts. No input precedes the Notes field.
2. A line over the workspace says what the example proves and what to do: type a draft in Notes, pop the pane out, close its window, find the draft back.
3. A recipe and a cover for `dock-tear-out`, so its card publishes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: Each of the four panes shows content, and a line over the workspace states the claim and the steps.
- [ ] AC-2: The group page shows a Dock Pane Pop-out card with a cover taken by its recipe.
- [ ] AC-3: `DockTearOutDefaultRoute.spec.mjs` passes unchanged, and the catalog specs pass with the new counts.

## Out of Scope

- A new sortable KPI dashboard (`Neo.dashboard.Container`): the Colors dashboard already shows that engine path, and the pull request lists it in Dashboard & Docking through `alsoIn` instead (decision on #21140, `issuecomment-7007761183`).
- Engine changes to the tear-out lifecycle; #21455 and #21375 own its open defects.
