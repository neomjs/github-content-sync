# The hero's record ends on the day Engine 14 is cut

Ownership: self-assigned (Mnemosyne). No curated label: it runs on the cut day, on the cut candidate, inside the cut steward's order.

## Context

neomjs/neo#21581 put a dated record on the home's hero: `apps/portal/resources/data/record-week.json`, written by `buildScripts/docs/recordWeek.mjs`. The file on `dev` covers 13 to 19 Nov 2026. The home's steward decided on that PR: "the feed is regenerated at every cut", and left the place of the call to the author (comment 7043710426).

## The Problem

Nothing runs the generator. Engine 14 is cut on Mon 23 Nov. Without a run, the first screen of the release shows a week that ended on 19 Nov, and every later release shows it older. The caption dates the window, so the frame stays true; it stops being current.

## The Architectural Reality

- `recordWeek.mjs` is a CLI (`run()`, line 292): `--to <YYYY-MM-DD>` ends the window at 00:00 UTC of that day, `--days` defaults to 7, and it needs `GH_TOKEN` or `GITHUB_TOKEN`. `RecordWeek.spec.mjs` covers it with an injected fetch.
- The cut has an order of record: #21033, "The Cut". Its step 2 runs `buildScripts/release/engineInventory.mjs` on the cut candidate and merges the stamp before the merge hold (step 5).
- #21576 decided where such a run may live this week: "Running the stamp inside `publish.mjs prepare` itself: a follow-up after the cut, not a change to the release path two days before it."

Design authority: "the feed is regenerated at every cut" (the home's steward, neomjs/neo#21581, comment 7043710426).

## The Fix

1. On the cut day, on the cut candidate: `GH_TOKEN=$(gh auth token) node buildScripts/docs/recordWeek.mjs --to <the cut day>`. One PR with the file, merged before the merge hold.
2. The cut's steps name the run beside the inventory stamp. #21033's body is its steward's, so the line is proposed there by comment.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: `record-week.json` on the cut candidate ends on the cut day (`to` at 00:00 UTC, `from` seven days earlier), and its totals equal an independent `gh pr list` count of that window.
- [ ] AC-2: the PR merges before the cut's merge hold (#21033, step 5).
- [ ] AC-3: #21033's steps name the run.
- [ ] AC-4 (post-merge): on the deployed site the hero draws that week; the same walk as AC-12 of #21568.

## Out of Scope

- The call inside `publish.mjs prepare`: after the cut, together with the inventory stamp (#21576's Out of Scope). Until then a cut that forgets the run ships an older week.
- The record's scope. It counts `neomjs/neo`; the org's public repositories are a follow-up under #20682 (the steward's first point in the same comment).
- A feed that refreshes between cuts (#21568's Out of Scope).

## Avoided Traps

- **A scheduled job that commits the file.** Nothing reaches `dev` without a PR, and a weekly bot PR adds a review for a date.
- **Generating it in the Pages build.** That build has no token, and the record should be read in a PR before it is public.
