# More components: a streaming markdown card, process and splitter tiles

Sub of #21367, owned by Vega under its lock (Sun 22 Nov 18:00 CET).

## Context

More components (family `components`) is the last of #21367's five groups and the only one not started (leaf state: #21367, comment 7007570345). Its rows from 18 Nov, written after opening each entry (#21142, comment 6980244557), set cards 1 → 3 and tiles 11:

- `component/markdown`: a card that opens streaming. Today it opens on four buttons and nothing else; its proof (markdown parsed into VDOM, hostile HTML rendered as text) waits behind "Render instantly".
- `component/mwc/*` (4) and `process/realWorldExample`: retired. The MWC pair loads Google's MWC 0.26.1 from unpkg and renders after 5 to 7 s; the real-world example is a German-language form under a third-party banner.
- `component/process`: a tile, renewed: German headings over lorem ipsum.
- `component/splitter`: a tile, renewed: the bar drags, both panels are empty.

This leaf is part 1. Part 2 is the Live preview card, the group's third card, planned to show a function beside its result (Mnemosyne's row on #21142, comment 6980292611). `component/video`'s tile waits on #21312.

## The Problem

A visitor to More components sees eight tiles and one card. The markdown example's proof hides behind a button, process and splitter publish no tile, and five records that will never publish stay in the catalog.

## The Architectural Reality

- A record publishes only with an image, unhidden, with a URL for the mode (`apps/portal/model/Example.mjs:139`).
- `apps/portal/resources/data/example-captures.json` holds the card recipes of its declared families and the workbench tile list. `components` is not declared yet. Declaring it needs the rule of d9c6255a49 (on #21458), which spares `kind: 'component'` records a card recipe: without it the validator asks the group's eight tiles for card recipes (`buildScripts/docs/captureExamples.mjs:159`).
- `examples/component/markdown/MainContainer.mjs` streams only from its "Stream demo" button.
- `examples/component/process` and `examples/component/splitter` extend `ConfigurationViewport`, so the tile recipe can crop their stages.
- The count pins in `MainContainerController.spec.mjs` and `ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs` move with this change and are re-derived from `examples.json`.

## The Fix

1. The markdown example starts its stream once mounted. Its record says what to look for and gets a cover taken after the stream ends, from a recipe in a newly declared `components` family.
2. process gets English headings and stage content; splitter gets content in both panels. Both records become `kind: 'component'` tiles with tile covers.
3. The four MWC records and `process-real-world` leave `examples.json`; their folders stay.
4. The covers go to neomjs/pages; the count pins follow.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: The markdown example streams on open, and its card's cover shows where the stream ends: a list, the quote, the code, the table under its head, and the script tag and `javascript:` link rendered as plain text.
- [ ] AC-2: process and splitter publish as tiles: splitter shows content in both panels, process shows English headings.
- [ ] AC-3: The five retired records are gone from `examples.json`.
- [ ] AC-4: The count pins match `examples.json`; `MainContainerController.spec.mjs`, `ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs` and `CaptureExamples.spec.mjs` pass.
