# A torn-out pane can come back to its window while its popup stays open

Owned by Vega (the tear-out lane, beside #21375).

## Context

Row 1 of `test/playwright/e2e/colors/tearOutMatrix.spec.mjs` failed on CI four times on 19 Nov (#21410, #21412, #21436, #21445), and once in 10 local runs with the release moved outside the window. In every failing run the Grid pane, torn out into its popup, is back in the colors app's main window shortly after pointer-up, while the popup still shows it:

```
Error: the main window after the drop: no chart text leaves its svg
+   "\"Occurrences in grid cells\" at 47,543 17×175, its svg at 34,527 1212×160",
```

The failing CI job (135375311683) and its green rerun (135393511768) log the same receipt (`acquiredAtStep: 8, proxyRight: 1508`), and `dev` passed 10 of 10 local runs: the gesture is the same, the outcome races. In the CI trace the main window shows two panes at pointer-up and three about 220 ms later; its error context lists a Grid pane in the main window.

## The Problem

Someone who drags a pane out of a dashboard and lets go soon after its popup appears can end up with the pane in both windows: the popup keeps its DOM while the source window renders it again. Which window owns the widget is then undefined.

## The Architectural Reality

Design authority: row 1's contract at the drop, "Pointer-up outside the source window is the DETACH terminal: the popup persists as a standalone OS window."

- `Neo.dashboard.Container#openWidgetInPopup` stages the widget in `detachedItems`; the widget moves into the popup only when the popup's app connects (`#onWindowConnect` → `mainView.add`).
- `Neo.draggable.container.SortZone#processDragEnd` moves the dragged component back into its owner when `isWindowDragging` is false and the component is still in `owner.items`; `#checkWindowBoundary` restores the layout on a re-entry.
- `Container#onWindowDisconnect` reintegrates a detached widget when its popup disconnects outside a drag.

Which of the three returns the pane is not traced yet: the colors app's App worker is a SharedWorker, so its `onDragBoundaryEntry` and `onWindowDisconnect` logs never reach the page console the spec reads.

## The Fix

Trace first: a receipt the spec can read for each of the three paths, so a failing run names the one that fired. Then make a detach terminal leave the pane in exactly one window.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-3: Row 1 asserts again that the main window's panes refill its area and that no chart text leaves its svg (the arm #21453 removes); 20 of 20 local runs pass.
