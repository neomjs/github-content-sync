# The phone hero draws its strip when a week outgrows the 3px pattern

## Context

The v14 cut-day hero record in [#22082](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/pull/22082) contains 459 eligible merged PRs for 16–22 November. The preceding record contained 301. The largest daily column grew from 69 to 92 squares. Each square represents one merged PR. [Independent census](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21586#issuecomment-7092999603).

## The Problem

At 390 × 844 on cut candidate `aa41c1f139`, fresh identical Portal CSS and the same browser draw the old week’s seven-day strip but leave the new week blank. The App Worker holds the correct new caption and the renderer reports all 459 items, `mode: 'none'` and `strip: null`. The existing `HomeHeroRoundTableNL` phone case fails. At 1440 × 900 the new record draws normally.

## The Architectural Reality

`Neo.canvas.RoundTable.layout()` checks the space below the statement, fits one column per day and descends through the configured square sizes. In this phone scene the canvas is 390 × 786 and the bottom of the buttons is 721.125 px in canvas coordinates. At 92 items on the busiest day, even the 3 px square with its 1 px gap fails the minimum clearance. The page already has the source data. The missing output is the strip and its caption.

Design authority: #21568 and #21602 established one square per PR, the dated caption and separation from the statement. The release steward selected a smaller standalone pattern after the existing layouts fail. The strip’s author and the Home steward have the exact phone frames for their final look. [Release coordination](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21033#issuecomment-7095200969).

## The Fix

Preserve every existing layout that fits. When no existing pattern can display the strip, try a smaller standalone pattern while keeping seven day groups, one square for each recorded PR and the caption. Keep the cut-day data PR separate. Home spacing and typography keep their present design for this release.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] At 390 × 844 the actual 459-item record renders a seven-day strip and dated caption clear of the statement, with all 459 items represented in the settled frame.
- [ ] The working 301-item phone strip and existing wider table/strip layouts retain their geometry. A scene too small even for the fallback still declines to draw.
- [ ] Browser evidence includes the failing cut-day frame and the corrected frame on identical data. A regression test preserves this case without committing a duplicate of the release data.
- [ ] The visual handoff includes desktop and phone, normal and reduced motion. The design reader and a cross-family reviewer disposition the bounded change before the operator’s physical-test window.

## Out of Scope

Aggregating PRs, modifying the generated counts, changing the seven-day window, redesigning the phone statement layout, or publishing the cut. #21586 owns the generated file.
