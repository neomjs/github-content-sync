# The phone Home Features scroller squeezes its six cards into 50 px tracks

## Context

#22079 (merged for v14 as `04adf91d10`) moved the Home lower cards onto `.portal-glass-card`. On a 390 × 844 phone, Emmy measured the six Features tracks at **50 px** each, with the first card 50 × 1,172 px, where the base had 167–196 px tracks (#22079 `issuecomment-7093798254`). The card text has no horizontal room. This is the Home page of Engine 14, "The Front Door", so it must be fixed before the cut.

## The Problem

At 700 px and below, the Features row is a horizontal scroller: `grid-template-columns: repeat(6, 1fr)` with `overflow-x: scroll` (`resources/css/src/apps/portal/home/parts/Features.css`). A `1fr` track's minimum is the card's automatic minimum size. #22079 gives `.portal-content-box` `min-width: 0` (`resources/css/src/apps/portal/Viewport.css`), which makes that minimum zero, so the six tracks divide the viewport instead of scrolling.

## The Architectural Reality

The scroller owns its track sizing. The card's `min-width: 0` is right for the shared grid card, and the 3- and 2-column desktop grids do not depend on it.

## The Fix

Size the phone scroller's tracks from their cards: `repeat(6, minmax(min-content, 1fr))`. A `min-content` minimum uses the card's content size whatever `min-width` the card sets. The card rule and the desktop grids stay as they are.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] At 390 × 844, the six Features tracks are content-wide again (base 167–196 px) and the row scrolls horizontally.
- [ ] At 1440 × 900, the three 368 px columns are unchanged.
- [ ] All six cards keep the glass card.

## Out of Scope

- Other Home rows and the About cards from #22079.
