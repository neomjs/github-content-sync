# Engine 14 release notes: the final pass at the cut head

## Context

Iteration 5 (#21776, PR #21777) wrote `.github/RELEASE_NOTES/v14.0.0.md` against `dev` `7cd0d6618e` (21 Nov, 06:31Z). On 21 Nov the operator directed that the notes get no more iterations, only one final pass at the cut head. He merged the last approved pass between 05:53Z and 06:01Z on 23 Nov (`dev` `8e649faff9`). The merge hold starts at 07:00 UTC (#21033 `issuecomment-7093193157`), and this pass is step 4 of the cut.

## The Problem

The note still carries iteration 5's banner and two "Pending the operator" designation lines. Its counts were taken on 21 Nov: 486 merged PRs since 13.4.0, 89 outside merges, 371 routes and 312 core URLs, 128 examples. The 175 commits on `dev` since `7cd0d6618e` are not in it, and among them is the web desktop tear-out (#22067).

## The Architectural Reality

- `buildScripts/release/publish.mjs prepare --version 14.0.0` requires this exact file.
- `prepare` writes `seo.json` into the release commit (#21809) and stamps the note's SEO dates (#21810). The SEO lines are therefore read from a regeneration at the cut head (#21033 step 3), never from the tracked file alone.
- The release-notes workflow §7 is the checklist (`.agents/skills/release-notes/references/release-notes-workflow.md`).

## The Fix

One PR to `.github/RELEASE_NOTES/v14.0.0.md`:
1. Remove the banner and fill the designations. *Release Type* is a major release, *Engine 14 — The Front Door*; *Stability* is Stable (#21032 `issuecomment-7093192843`). The operator's merge of this PR is his confirmation gate.
2. Fold the merges after `7cd0d6618e` into their chapters, from Sophie's mining digest on #21032. Anchor every claim.
3. Take the counts at the cut head, after the cut-day data PRs merge: PRs and issues since 13.4.0 (GitHub search, timestamped), outside-contributor merges, the SEO lines from the regenerated `seo.json`, and the catalog.
4. Run §7: render beside 13.4.0, the numbers sweep last, then `prepare --rehearse`.
