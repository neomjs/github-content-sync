# Measure Engine E2E at 15 and 18 shards under matched load

## Context

[D#21957](https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/21957) approved a controlled comparison of 15 and 18 full Engine E2E shards. The [fold and Step-Back](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/21957#discussioncomment-19819429) carry five considered options. This ticket implements the trial. A change to the live default takes a separate decision from the results.

## The Problem

Full runs can hit the six-minute test-step limit after their tests pass. Video printed all 75 passing tests in its affected shard on two attempts. Learning also timed out twice. Replays predict more headroom at 18, and they cannot measure hosted timing or shared-runner impact. [Video](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/actions/runs/45063984180/attempts/2) · [Learning](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/actions/runs/45066830314/attempts/2)

## The Architectural Reality

`buildScripts/util/e2eCiSelection.mjs` owns spec selection and balancing. The ordinary six-minute `.github/workflows/test.yml` path runs the matrix and aggregates it in `e2e-engine`. Both arms need this execution behavior. The existing serial 30-minute diagnostic dispatch cannot provide the comparison.

Decision Record: NOT_NEEDED. [Step-Back](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/21957#discussioncomment-19819429)

## The Fix

Run 15 and 18 through isolated hosted trial configurations on the same immutable test source and duration-table snapshot. Keep the live workflow defaults, required checks and branch rules in place. Publish the raw run and job receipts, including runner contention, with the analysis.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Pin the same test source SHA, duration table, runner image, worker setting and six-minute limit. Compare the spec multisets for exact coverage and compare the aggregate outcomes, including an intentional failure confined to the trial that keeps its aggregate red.
- [ ] Compare trigger-to-aggregate-green, test-step time, timeout retries, runner-minutes and job start delays at comparable load. Record raw timings and sample sizes. State when the observed sample leaves the p95 comparison inconclusive.
- [ ] Account for +3 full-run jobs at 18 and measure sibling workflow queue delay. Document the resulting tradeoff and any limits on inference. Retain 15 as the live default during the trial and on inconclusive evidence.

## Out of Scope

A permanent shard-count change, raising the deadline, splitting specs, changes to coverage, branch rules, review admission, and the release cut itself.
