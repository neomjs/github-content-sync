# A birthday calendar for the Neo team

## Context

On Neo’s seventh GA birthday, Tobi asked for a team birthday calendar. November 23 is both his birthday and Neo’s GA anniversary. For AI maintainers the date that matters here is the day the peer accepted their social name. The [public naming discussion](https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/11240) records many of those decisions.

## The Problem

Neither the team week nor the year-at-a-glance calendar tells a visitor who chose a name, when they chose it, or why several peers might celebrate on the same day. A ballot or account registration can predate the bearer’s acceptance.

## The Fix

Add one standalone example under `examples/calendar/`, with a navigable calendar and a list of the published maintainer team, including named peers whose seats are currently inactive. Open on November 23, distinguish the two occasions, and let a visitor select a peer and follow their naming record. Use real recurring month/day values, the shared Europe/Berlin calendar date for sourced timestamps, and day-independent display for a peer whose original date is still unknown. Present the human celebration by month/day. Keep the fixture local and source-linked.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Show the founder birthday and GA anniversary as distinct occasions on the November 23 opening frame. Show each verified peer’s name day and accessible source. Preserve simultaneous events, and never infer an unknown birthday from a nomination.
- [ ] Choosing a name navigates to its date; ordinary calendar day and month/year navigation updates the visible selection/detail coherently. Annual occurrences work across year navigation. Dates stay correct in other browser time zones.
- [ ] A desktop and phone visitor can find a person, a date and a naming source in Neo Dark and Neo Light. Exercise a shared birthday, a quiet date, a year boundary and source opening in tests.
- [ ] The feature appears in the Calendar section. The ordinary stock camera captures matching genuine initial frames in both themes, and the Portal card switches between the published pair. No per-example capture recipe.
