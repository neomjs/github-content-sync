# A dialog shown without an animation target stays off-screen

handoff: @neo-gpt-emmy. Her design ask on the #21942 backfill found it, and whether the examples' half rides that backfill or this ticket's pull request is her call. No curated labels: the work is dated by the examples' lock, Mon 23 Nov 07:00 UTC.

## Context

The design ask: do the two editor examples (`examples/scheduler/eventEditor`, `examples/gantt/taskEditor`) carry their story on the frame a visitor lands on? They do not. At card width both read as a plan, like their sibling cards. A blind read of eight unlabelled frames at 445 × 250 graded the plan frames 4, 4, 5 and 5 of 10 as catalog cards ("good pictures and wrong cards") and the same examples with their editor open 6, 7, 5 and 6; the reader picked the open editor in all four cases. #21140's body already rules it: an example opens on its telling state, set in its own code, "a zoom preset, an open editor, a selection".

Writing that into the examples' own code found the defect below.

## The Problem

`openEventEditor(id)` for a record without a rendered bar shows the modal mask and leaves the dialog at `left: -10000px; top: -10000px`. A visitor sees a blurred, dimmed page; focus sits in the invisible Title field.

![the scheduler example after openEventEditor at mount: the mask, no dialog](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/f775b8f56f6a4ef18fcd230b595a52838e4129d3/review-evidence/21140/editors-landing/stranded-under-the-mask.jpg?raw=true)

Measured headless on `dev` at `0b9a737401`, 960 × 540, Neo Dark and Neo Light, no console error in any run:

| Call | Bar id | `.neo-dialog` rect |
|---|---|---|
| `openEventEditor(id)` in the viewport's `afterSetMounted` (scheduler) | `findBarId()` returns `null`: the rows are not rendered yet | x −10000, y −10000, 440 × 291 |
| `ensureEventEditor().openForRecord(record)` 1500 ms after mount (Gantt) | the bar is rendered, no id is passed | x −10000, y −10000, 440 × 291 |
| `openEventEditor(id)` 1500 ms after mount (scheduler) | resolved by the container, animated show | x 260, y 125: centred |
| a real double-click on the bar (both) | from the event, animated show | x 260, y 125 |

So this is not timing: a show without an animation target is parked. An application reaches it whenever it opens the editor for a record whose bar is not rendered: at start, right after adding a record, or for an event outside the rendered range.

## The Architectural Reality

- `.neo-floating` parks every floating component off-screen with `left: -10000px; top: -10000px` (`resources/css/src/component/Base.css:23`).
- `Neo.dialog.Base` reverts that position when it has no animation target, in an override named `render(mount)` (`src/dialog/Base.mjs:1128`). The override came with #5982; #4963 is the same symptom a year earlier.
- #7147 (commit `1dced6c8c8`, 31 Jul 2025) renamed the lifecycle method `render` to `initVnode`. The dialog's override kept the old name. On `dev` no class in the dialog's chain (`Panel`, `container.Base`, `component.Base`, `component.Abstract`, `mixin.VdomLifecycle`) defines `render(mount)`, and the only call of it in `src` is the override's own `super.render(mount)`.
- The plain path is `Neo.component.Base#show` → `me.startMountFlight(me.initVnode(true))` (`src/component/Base.mjs:2022`).
- `Neo.timeline.EventEditor#openForRecord` sets `animateTargetId = barId || null` and calls `show()` (`src/timeline/EventEditor.mjs:724`); `Neo.scheduler.Container#openEventEditor` passes `barId || findBarId(record.id)` (`src/scheduler/Container.mjs:1291`).

Design authority: "Opens the shared {@link Neo.timeline.EventEditor} bound to `eventIdOrRecord`. Returns `null` when {@link #editable} is `false` or the record cannot be resolved." (`@summary` of `openEventEditor`, `src/scheduler/Container.mjs:1285`). A resolvable record opens; a rendered bar is no condition. The dead override states the same intent: "If there is no animation target, we need to ensure that the initial offscreen positioning from .neo-floating gets reverted".

Mechanism verified: with the override renamed to `initVnode(mount)` and returning `super.initVnode(mount)`, the first two rows of the table land at x 260, y 125 in both themes. Not measured: other `dialog.Base` subclasses. The mechanism does not depend on the editor.

## The Fix

One pull request:

1. `src/dialog/Base.mjs`: the off-screen revert runs on the path `show()` takes today. The scratch patch renames the override; whether it belongs in `initVnode` or in `show()` is the builder's decision.
2. Two unit tests, red on `dev`: a dialog without `animateTargetId`, first shown through `show()`, carries no off-screen `left` and `top`; `openEventEditor` on a record without a rendered bar shows the editor.
3. Both examples open their editor in the viewport's `afterSetMounted`, on a record whose bar stays in view beside the centred dialog at 960 × 540. The scratch patch picks "Root canal" (the Surgery row, below the dialog) and "Design the inner pages" (below the dialog's lower right corner, with its arrow to "Review the designs"). The pick is the builder's; the rule is that the edited bar is not under the dialog. The blind reader's second complaint was a form that hides the item it edits.
4. The two catalog records' `lookFor` sentences start from the open editor. The cards' cover pairs are the new landing frames, captured with an English locale so that the form's dates match the plan's labels (the frames below show `24.11.2026` beside "Tue, Nov 24": this machine's locale).

Left: today's opening frames. Right: the landing frames of the scratch patch, no interaction.

![four rows at card width: opening frame and landing frame, scheduler dark and light, Gantt dark and light](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/f775b8f56f6a4ef18fcd230b595a52838e4129d3/review-evidence/21140/editors-landing/board-opening-vs-landing.jpg?raw=true)

The scratch patch (3 files, +27 −2) is [`editors-open-on-load.diff`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/f775b8f56f6a4ef18fcd230b595a52838e4129d3/review-evidence/21140/editors-landing/editors-open-on-load.diff); two frames at full size lie beside it. The builder applies, reworks and owns it. The reader saw the frames with the bar under the dialog; the right-hand frames here have had no fresh read yet.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A `Neo.dialog.Base` without `animateTargetId`, first shown through `show()`, appears on screen (unit test, red on `dev`).
- [ ] `openEventEditor(id)` and `openTaskEditor(id)` for a record without a rendered bar show the editor centred (unit test, red on `dev`).
- [ ] Both examples land on their open editor without interaction: at 960 × 540 in Neo Dark and Neo Light the dialog is on screen, focus is in Title, the edited record's bar is visible outside the dialog, and the console is clean.
- [ ] Cancel, Escape and the close action leave the plan as it is today; a double-click and Enter open the editor again.
- [ ] The `lookFor` sentences of `scheduler-event-editor` and `gantt-task-editor` match the landing state.
- [ ] Look: the design seat reads the four landing frames at card width on the pull request; the cover pairs on `neomjs/pages` are those frames.
- [ ] Post-merge: both cards of the catalog show the editor.

## Out of Scope

- The modal mask's strength in Neo Dark. The reader's first complaint about the editor frames was "a blocked screen": the mask is as strong in dark as in light. A theme-level look, after the cut.
- Time fields on a day-level Gantt task (`00:00` twice in the form).
- An anchored or non-modal editor.
- #21928, the dialog example's own scene.

## Avoided Traps

- **A delay in the example.** `timeout(1500)` before the call makes the frame land and leaves the defect for the next application that opens an editor on an unrendered record.
- **A cover staged by steps.** The old covers opened the editor through a capture recipe and typed an invalid end. The landing frame shows a real record with valid values.
- **A fixed dialog position in example code** to keep the bar visible. The record is chosen instead.
