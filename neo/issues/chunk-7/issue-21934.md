# Examples merged without their catalog record get one

## Context

On 22 Nov the team builds about 25 example leaves at once (the map is in #21140's body, "Open rows"). Until #21856 has merged, an example's pull request edits no catalog file, so that no two of them collide: its record stands as text in the pull request body. Those examples are merged and on no page until someone carries their records into the catalog. This is that pass, as #21613 was for the wave of 20 Nov.

## The Problem

A leaf's criterion names a card, a tile or a cover in the catalog. A pull request that merges before the split cannot meet it, and without one owner for the records each author would open a second pull request on the same group files, hours before the lock.

## The Architectural Reality

- After #21856 the catalog is one file per group under `apps/portal/resources/data/examples/`; an example pull request that merges after it carries its own record and needs nothing here.
- **Widened on 22 Nov, 14:42Z.** #21856 has merged, and a second kind of record waits: until #21908 has merged, the capture guard refuses a new covered record in a recipe family, so such an example's pull request cannot carry its record either (reported by Grace at 13:59Z for the sortable dashboard and by Emmy at 14:35Z for board and Gantt records). Those records belong to this pass if their example merges before #21908. If #21908 lands first, each rides its own pull request.
- The steward's order (#21033): #21856 merges as soon as it is approved and green.
- A cover is the frame a visitor lands on, shot at 960 × 540 through #21908's landing capture, and merged on `neomjs/pages` before its record.

## The Fix

One pull request, opened once #21856 has merged and again before the lock if more examples have landed: for every example merged without a record, take the record from its pull request body, add it to its group's file with `order`, `alsoIn` and the cover's file name, and regenerate `seo.json`. The pull request lists the leaves it publishes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every example of the 22 Nov wave that is merged on `dev` by Mon 23 Nov 06:00 UTC has its record in its group's file, or is named in the pull request with what it waits for.
- [ ] Each record's cover is merged on `neomjs/pages` and loads on the card.
- [ ] The catalog's specs pass, and `seo.json` equals the generator's output.
- [ ] The look: the group pages that gained a card, at 1440×900 in Neo Dark, on the pull request.

## Out of Scope

Building or renewing an example. Records of examples that merge after #21856 and can ride their own pull request.

## Waiting records (state of 22 Nov, 14:42Z)

- The sortable dashboard: its record stands as text in #21921 (Grace).
- The sales pipeline (#21898) and the Gantt task tree (#21922): complete candidate records in their bodies, paired covers live on `neomjs/pages`. Emmy offered to carry these two entries here, with `order`, `seo.json` and the card read.
