# theme-base: bind button's FloatingAction and Segmented files (18 variables) to roles

Owned by @neo-gemini-julia. Sub-issue of #22484 (`theme-base` package moves, Step 6 of #22295).

## Context

Completes the `button` package's `theme-base` move under #22484 / #22295 (`ADR 0044`). The `button` pilot (#22351) moved `resources/css/theme-base/button/Base.css` (`156` variables), after which #22415 (`PR #22417`, `FloatingAction.css`, `17` variables) and #22234 (`Segmented.css`, `1` variable) landed per-scheme copies in `resources/css/theme-neo-light/button/` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/button/`. Until those two files move to `resources/css/theme-base/button/`, `Global.css`-only role themes (`#22359` / `PR #22438` Material pair), `create-theme --base theme-neo`, and `.neo-theme-scope` subtrees lack `--neo-floating-action-button-*` and `--neo-segmented-button-border-radius`.

## The Problem

- `resources/css/theme-neo-light/button/FloatingAction.css` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/button/FloatingAction.css` contain `17` identical role-bound declarations across two legacy per-scheme files instead of one `[class*="neo-theme-"]` file in `resources/css/theme-base/button/FloatingAction.css`.
- `resources/css/theme-neo-light/button/Segmented.css` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/button/Segmented.css` duplicate `--neo-segmented-button-border-radius: 999px` as a raw literal instead of binding `var(--neo-radius-full)` (`9999px`) once in `resources/css/theme-base/button/Segmented.css`.
- Leaving either file in `theme-neo-{light,dark}/button/` blocks Step 8 deletion of the two legacy Neo folders and leaves `Neo.button.FloatingAction` and `Neo.button.Segmented` unstyled on role-only themes.

## The Architectural Reality

- `resources/css/src/button/FloatingAction.css` reads `17` `--neo-floating-action-button-*` variables; `resources/css/src/button/Segmented.css` reads `--neo-segmented-button-border-radius` on `.neo-segmented-button.neo-toolbar` and `> .neo-button`.
- `resources/css/theme-light/button/{FloatingAction,Segmented}.css`, `resources/css/theme-dark/button/{FloatingAction,Segmented}.css`, and `resources/css/theme-cyberpunk/button/{FloatingAction,Segmented}.css` all already declare explicit per-theme values in `neo.theme` above `neo.base`, so moving Neo's copies into `theme-base` introduces zero classic or Cyberpunk changes.
- `Design authority:` `learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md` (Decisions 3, 4, 5 & 7) and #22295 Rules 1, 3, 4, 13, 14 & 16.

## The Fix & Binding Table (Rule 13)

Create `resources/css/theme-base/button/FloatingAction.css` and `resources/css/theme-base/button/Segmented.css` on `[class*="neo-theme-"]`, and delete `resources/css/theme-neo-{light,dark}/button/{FloatingAction,Segmented}.css`.
