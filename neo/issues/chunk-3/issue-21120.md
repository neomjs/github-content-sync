# The Components examples get their covers

## Context

Pull request #21105 catalogs the visual components, canvas and wrapper examples: 31 records of family `components`, 25 of them without a cover. The six covers it keeps were made for the 13.4 list. On 16 Nov 2026 the operator read that tab and graded the covers in its previews 1 of 10 (#21119 carries his words and the measurements). The catalog records of #21105 are not at fault: its ticket put covers out of scope.

A leaf of #20498, cornerstone 1 of Engine 14 (`ROADMAP.md`), cut on Mon 23 Nov.

## The Problem

Count receipt, `examples.json` at #21105's head `3896a82534`: 31 records with `"family": "components"`; 6 carry an `image` (`helix`, `offscreen-canvas`, `covid-helix`, `covid-gallery`, `gallery`, `liquid-glass`), 25 carry `null`.

With #21119 a record without a cover renders no card, so the largest family would open on six cards. And those six do not hold either: `helix.png` and `offscreenCanvas.png` show a browser's tab strip and address bar, and all six are whole-window shots that turn to texture at card width.

## The Architectural Reality

- #20679 delivers the capture script, the recipe shape and the frame rule: 1280×720, the example's own surface, legible at 445 px, no browser or OS chrome. This ticket adds recipes and changes no script.
- The records: `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json`, family `components`. A record's `feature` line says what the example proves; its `scope` line says what a wrapper or chart example needs from outside.
- The bytes go to `neomjs/pages`, `resources_pub/website/examples/devmode/`.
- Design authority: the frame rule of #20679 and the operator's read quoted on #21119, for the six covers this ticket replaces.

## The Fix

One recipe and one cover per record of the family: 25 new, 6 re-shot. For each, run the example, bring it to the state its `feature` line names, and capture it by #20679's rule. The caption is the brief: a cover shows what its caption claims. An example whose point is motion gets the control that drives it in the frame.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every record of family `components` carries an `image` that has a recipe, and the script reproduces all of them in one run. A record whose example needs a key or an unreachable service keeps `image: null`; the pull request names it.
- [ ] Each cover obeys #20679's frame rule and shows what the record's `feature` names.
- [ ] **Look:** the pull request shows all covers on one sheet at card width (445 px), the re-shot six beside the covers they replace. The design seat reads the sheet before review.
- [ ] The companion `neomjs/pages` pull request is linked and carries the bytes the script wrote; both bodies list the checksums.
- [ ] No change to a record beyond `image`.

## Out of Scope

The script and the frame rule (#20679); the card (#21119); the records #20694 will add; captions.

## Avoided Traps

- A cover of the example's default view, when the caption names an action.
- The six old covers kept because they exist.
