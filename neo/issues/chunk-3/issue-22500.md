# grid: separate row hover from selection across all selection models

Owned by `@neo-gemini-julia` for immediate Engine `14.1` fix execution; curated contributor labels omitted. Sub-issue of #22484 (`epic-review` Greenlight: [#22484#issuecomment-7231775093](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22484#issuecomment-7231775093)), repairing a visual & specificity regression introduced in #22389 (`PR #22431`).

## Context

Flagged by `@tobiu` during merge review of `table`'s sibling `theme-base` move (`PR #22454` / #22442), with explicit direction (`2026-11-28`) that row hover must look clean in Theme Neo and stay clearly separated from selection across all six `grid` selection models (`CellModel`, `ColumnModel`, `RowModel`, `CellColumnModel`, `CellRowModel`, `CellColumnRowModel`).

## The Problem

1. **`1.00:1` hover-vs-companion-selection collision (`resources/css/theme-base/grid/Body.css:5,10,14`):** In #22389 (`PR #22431`), `--neo-grid-cell-background-color-hover`, `--neo-grid-cellrowmodel-row-cell-background-color-selected`, and `--neo-grid-cellmodel-column-cell-background-color-selected` were all bound to `var(--neo-color-background-accent-subtle)` (`#ecf2ff` light · `#142457` dark) because `theme-neo/Global.css` only exposed two accent surface roles (`accent-subtle` and `selected`), making every hovered row paint the **exact same colour (`1.00:1`)** as the non-active companion cells of a selected row (`CellRowModel`, `CellColumnRowModel`) or selected column (`CellColumnModel`).
2. **Hover overwrites `.selected-column-cell` in `CellColumnModel` and `CellColumnRowModel` (`resources/css/src/grid/Body.css:97` vs `:126-135`):** `.neo-grid-body .neo-grid-row .neo-grid-cell.selected-column-cell` has specificity `(0, 4, 0)`, whereas `.neo-mouse .neo-grid-row:not(.neo-selected):hover .neo-grid-cell` has specificity `(0, 5, 0)`. Once companion column selection (`background-selected`, `accent-200/800`) steps above hover (`background-accent-subtle`, `accent-100/900`), hovering an unselected row downgrades `.selected-column-cell` to hover unless guarded with `:not(.neo-selected):not(.selected-column-cell)`.

## The Architectural Reality

- `Design authority:` `learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md` §2 Decision 3 (*"A state is a grade away"*), Epic #22295 Rules 5 & 15 (*"where no role fits, a role enters with the move that reads it"*), and `@tobiu` review direction on `PR #22454` (`2026-11-28`).
- Matching `14.0.0`'s three-step `theme-neo-dark/grid/Body.css` hierarchy (`purple-900` `#182449` hover → `purple-700` `#253974` companion row → `purple-600` `#304384` active cell) and sibling #22442 (`PR #22454`), `grid` binds to the **three-grade opaque accent ladder** declared in `resources/css/theme-neo/Global.css`:
  1. **Tier 1 — Row hover (`--neo-grid-cell-background-color-hover` → `var(--neo-color-background-accent-subtle)` = `accent-100` `#ecf2ff` · `accent-900` `#142457`):** `100%` opaque over pinned `.neo-locked-start` / `.neo-locked-end` cells (`z-index: 5`), `17.07:1 · 13.20:1` with `text-default`.
  2. **Tier 2 — Companion row/column & `RowModel` (`--neo-grid-cellmodel-column-cell-background-color-selected`, `--neo-grid-cellrowmodel-row-cell-background-color-selected`, `--neo-grid-rowmodel-cell-background-color-selected` → `var(--neo-color-background-selected)` = `accent-200` `#c4d6ff` · `accent-800` `#233c8f`):** `1.30:1 · 1.49:1` step above hover, `13.11:1 · 8.83:1` with `text-default`.
  3. **Tier 3 — Active selected cell (`--neo-grid-cellmodel-cell-background-color-selected`, `--neo-grid-cellrowmodel-cell-background-color-selected` → `var(--neo-color-background-selected-strong)` = `accent-300` `#9cb9ff` · `accent-700` `#304fb8`):** `1.33:1 · 1.39:1` step above companion selection (`1.73:1 · 2.08:1` above hover), `9.84:1 · 6.33:1` with `text-default`.
- Guarding `.neo-mouse .neo-grid-row:not(.neo-selected):is(:hover, .neo-hover)` with `.neo-grid-cell:not(.neo-selected):not(.selected-column-cell)` preserves both `.neo-selected` and `.selected-column-cell` across all six `Neo.selection.grid.*` models with `0` classic or Cyberpunk `theme-equality` differences.

## The Fix

1. **`resources/css/theme-neo/Global.css` & `buildScripts/util/check-theme-palette.mjs` (+ `check-theme-palette.spec.mjs`):** Declare `--neo-color-background-selected-strong: light-dark(var(--neo-palette-accent-300), var(--neo-palette-accent-700))` and its two `CONTRAST_PAIRS` floors (`4.5:1` `text-default`, `1.1:1` step on `background-selected`, matching #22442).
2. **`resources/css/src/grid/Body.css`:** Narrow `.neo-mouse .neo-grid-row:not(.neo-selected)` `:hover, &.neo-hover` target to `.neo-grid-cell:not(.neo-selected):not(.selected-column-cell)`.
3. **`resources/css/theme-base/grid/Body.css`:** Bind companion column & row (`--neo-grid-cellmodel-column-cell-background-color-selected`, `--neo-grid-cellrowmodel-row-cell-background-color-selected`) to `var(--neo-color-background-selected)` and active cell (`--neo-grid-cellmodel-cell-background-color-selected`, `--neo-grid-cellrowmodel-cell-background-color-selected`) to `var(--neo-color-background-selected-strong)`.
4. **`test/playwright/externalBrainSelection.mjs` & `test/playwright/e2e/grid/SelectionModesExample.spec.mjs`:** Mark `'grid/SelectionModesExample.spec.mjs'` adapter-eligible so the dedicated-worker Neural Link adapter runs in `e2e-engine` CI, and assert the distinct opaque fills (`restBg`, `hoverBg`, `companionBg`, `activeBg`) and unbroken selection across all six models in Neo Light and Neo Dark.

## Out of Scope

- Step-10 role migration of classic or Cyberpunk `grid` sheets.

## Avoided Traps

- **`background-neutral-hover` (`rgb(255 255 255 / .05)` layered over `#0e0f13`):** Composites to `#191a1e`, a low-chroma olive-grey that clashes with Theme Neo's `267°–270°` slate/indigo palette; `accent-100/900` → `accent-200/800` → `accent-300/700` keeps hover in the clean `267°` accent family at `100%` opacity.
- **`color-mix(in srgb, var(--neo-color-background-neutral-hover), var(--neo-color-background-surface))`:** CSS Color 4 interpolates alpha (`0.525`), leaving pinned `.neo-locked-start` / `.neo-locked-end` columns translucent.
- **Omitting `:not(.selected-column-cell)` on hovered `.neo-grid-cell`:** `.neo-mouse` hover `(0, 5, 0)` outranks `.selected-column-cell` `(0, 4, 0)` and downgrades column-selection crosshairs in `CellColumnModel` and `CellColumnRowModel` to hover fill.
