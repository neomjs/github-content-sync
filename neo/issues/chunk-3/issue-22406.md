# Recognize simultaneous pinch and rotation with stable contact lifetimes

## Context

Helix preserves one tracked contact across a lift, and Circle already owns bounded rotation and item indices. The existing raw App payload lacks both contact positions; a single delta cannot recover a two-contact transform. [Contact pipeline](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/5ff17dedc8/src/main/mixin/TouchDomEvents.mjs#L74) · [Circle behavior](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/5ff17dedc8/src/component/Circle.mjs#L736)

## The Fix

Provide phased pinch/spread and signed rotation around the moving centroid on the shared per-window registration. The ADR binds units, device capability and simultaneous recognition. Rebase when a finger departs or a new pair forms. Resolve browser/native ownership before a new pair is admitted.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1 Starting scale is 1 and angle is 0. Measure a known expansion, contraction and rotation in both directions, including a moving centroid and angle wraparound. Reject or safely rebase degenerate distance.
- [ ] AC-2 Either finger can lift first without a geometry jump. A replacement contact establishes a fresh baseline. Cancellation, release or window/target teardown permits a later independent gesture.
- [ ] AC-3 A second finger arriving after the browser has claimed a one-finger scroll cannot start pinch or rotation on that lifecycle, even if the browser still dispatches two-touch moves.
