# Forms, Multi-Window, Calendar, Scheduler and Gantt examples get their covers

## Context

Three family overviews are on `dev` with most of their cards cover-less. On 16 Nov 2026 the operator read the examples overview and graded its previews 5 of 10 and their covers 1 of 10 (#21119 carries his words and the measurements). #21119 changes the card: a record without a cover renders no card. #20679 delivers the capture script and the frame rule. This ticket gives the three families their covers.

A leaf of #20498, cornerstone 1 of Engine 14 (`ROADMAP.md`), cut on Mon 23 Nov.

## The Problem

Count receipt, `examples.json` on `dev` `2404340dfa`:

| Family | Records | With a cover | Without |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `forms` | 24 | 3 | 21 |
| `multi-window` | 12 | 7 | 5 |
| `calendar` | 4 | 1 | 3 |

29 records show an icon on a slab today and would show nothing after #21119: Forms would open on three cards.

#21116 moves `gantt-basic` and `scheduler-basic` out of `calendar` into their own families, `gantt` and `scheduler`, and publishes a family's route only once one of its records has a cover. Both records stay in this ticket's 29, and their two covers are what put `/examples/gantt` and `/examples/scheduler` on the site.

Twenty more covers are in use outside Grid and Table and Components: the 11 above and nine on records without a family (`webdesktop`, `devindex`, `tab-container`, `state-provider`, `dialog-drag-drop`, `covid-dashboard`, `portal`, `realworld`, `realworld-v2`). They were made one by one for the 13.4 list, as whole browser windows in mixed themes and scales, and nobody has read them against the frame rule.

## The Architectural Reality

- #20679 delivers the capture script, the recipe shape and the frame rule: 1280×720, the example's own surface, legible at 445 px, no browser or OS chrome. This ticket adds recipes and changes no script.
- The records: `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json`. A record's `feature` line says what the example proves.
- A Multi-Window example runs on a SharedWorker and opens further windows; its cover is one window's surface, and the recipe says which.
- The bytes go to `neomjs/pages`, `resources_pub/website/examples/devmode/`.
- Design authority: the frame rule of #20679 and the operator's read quoted on #21119, for any cover in use that this ticket replaces.

## The Fix

1. **29 new covers:** one recipe and one cover per cover-less record of the five families. `gantt-basic` and `scheduler-basic` come first, in a pull request of their own if the rest would hold them: the two routes of #21116 wait on them. For each, run the example, bring it to the state its `feature` line names, capture it by #20679's rule.
2. **20 covers read:** each cover in use outside Grid and Table and Components is held to the frame rule at the width its card shows it, then kept or re-shot. A re-shot cover gets a recipe; a kept one gets a reason.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every record of the families `forms`, `multi-window`, `calendar`, `scheduler` and `gantt` carries an `image` that has a recipe, and the script reproduces all of them in one run. A record whose example needs a key or an unreachable service keeps `image: null`; the pull request names it.
- [ ] Each new cover obeys #20679's frame rule and shows what the record's `feature` names.
- [ ] The pull request lists the 20 covers in use with kept or re-shot and one reason each; none that shows browser or OS chrome is kept.
- [ ] **Look:** the pull request shows one sheet per family at card width (445 px), re-shot covers beside the ones they replace. The design seat reads the sheets before review.
- [ ] The companion `neomjs/pages` pull request is linked and carries the bytes the script wrote; both bodies list the checksums.
- [ ] No change to a record beyond `image`.

## Out of Scope

The script and the frame rule (#20679); the card (#21119); the Components family (#21120); Grid and Table (#20679); captions; the records #20696 will add.

## Avoided Traps

- A form field's cover in its empty default state: the caption names validation, a picker or a format, and the cover shows that.
- A Multi-Window cover of one idle window: the point is the second window, so the frame shows the moved or shared part.
- A cover kept because re-shooting an application is work: the Featured tab is the first screen a stranger sees.

**The look of the first screen: two passes, four cold reads (design seat, Mon 16 Nov).** Earlier versions of this comment carried the first pass's recipes and then one viewport for every cover. Neither held; what stands is below.

![The examples' first screen: covers in use, first pass (cropped regions), second pass (whole surfaces at 960 px)](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/0029b8f6bfb018bce26462a411dd2560d348b9b9/review-evidence/20498/first-screen-three-passes.jpg?raw=true)

`/examples` at 1440×900 on the branch of #21124. Candidate covers are swapped in at the browser; no file changed. These four cards are what a stranger and the operator grade first.

**The cold look.** Two readers without session context, each given an unlabelled pair in swapped order and asked how much the page makes them want to open an example (1 to 10):

| Pair | Engineer | AI agent |
|---|---|---|
| covers in use → first pass, a 640 to 800 px region cut from the 1280 px layout | 4 → 7 | 7 → 5 |
| covers in use → second pass, the whole example rendered at a 960×540 viewport | 5 → 7 | 4 → 7 |

The first pass split the readers. The agent preferred the covers in use ("all the crops read as widget snippets, not applications"), and both called the Workstation crop broken ("clipped mid-word", "looks like a rendering accident"). The second pass wins with both, at 7. That is a step, not the bar.

**Rules for every cover of this ticket and of #20679:**

1. **Text arrives on the card at 0.64 of its size or more.** On the 615 px Featured card the whole 1280 px layout (0.48) read as "illegible smears" and the 960 px viewport (0.64) as readable. A family card is 445 px wide, so a record shown in a family tab is captured at 695 px of layout or less; 960 px holds only for a record shown on Featured alone.
2. **The frame ends where the example ends.** The whole example at that viewport, with its own margins and scrollbars, where it lays out; a region only where it does not, ending on component borders. A cut label reads as a rendering accident.
3. **The telling part sits in the upper 60 %**, because the caption takes the rest. The top-right 64×44 px stay calm: the card's window badge sits there and collided with a Workstation button in both readers' eyes.
4. **A state where the default says too little** (the TreeGrid at rest is a flat list of folders).
5. **Scale is shown, not captioned.** Both readers said the 100,000 rows rest on the caption alone. A row number with five digits, or the example's own counter, belongs in the frame.
