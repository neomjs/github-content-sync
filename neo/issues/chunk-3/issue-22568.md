# Bind Scheduler’s ten theme variables to shared roles

Part of #22484. The Scheduler needs one shared binding sheet for Neo, Material and generated themes.

## Context

`resources/css/src/scheduler/Container.css` reads ten Scheduler variables. The Neo pair define them in their two `scheduler/Container.css` files, and each legacy theme also has its own values. The Material composition census found nine unresolved Scheduler reads in each scheme. The scheduler’s drag preview mounts under the body and needs the same category paint as its source bar.

## The architectural reality

The Basic example uses colors for recording rooms. Depot and other examples use the same hooks for category swatches. Workstation uses amber for In progress, violet for Review, and emerald for Done. The corresponding Board edges and Scheduler progress fills read the same tokens. Timeline owns the shared header CSS in `#22533`.

Design authority: [ADR 0044](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/dev/learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md), its categorical revalidation trigger, and the package rules in #22295. The ADR’s default reuse maps violet to accent. On the combined Timeline `ea529035ac` and Workstation product palette `5f2d9c21e4` heads, that reuse gives Ready and Review exactly the same computed fill and edge in both schemes. In standalone Neo Dark its edge measures 2.85:1 over its fill. Direct info clears the contrast floor and paints the category blue. The two opaque mixes from the first proposal conflict with ADR 0044 decision 4. The design seat can choose the direct info pair or name categorical roles if the surface needs to retain violet. [Binding guard](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22571)

## The fix

Put all ten declarations in `resources/css/theme-base/scheduler/Container.css` and remove the two Neo copies. Below is the measured proposal. The violet rows are provisional until the design seat records the categorical decision.

| Variable | Proposed binding | Readers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--neo-scheduler-divider-border-color` | `--neo-color-border-default` | Locked resource divider |
| `--neo-scheduler-bar-emerald-background` | `--neo-color-background-success-subtle` | Booking and drag preview fills, category swatches |
| `--neo-scheduler-bar-emerald-border-color` | `--neo-color-border-success` | Booking and preview edges, swatches, Workstation Done column and progress fills |
| `--neo-scheduler-bar-amber-background` | `--neo-color-background-warning-subtle` | Booking and drag preview fills, category swatches |
| `--neo-scheduler-bar-amber-border-color` | `--neo-color-border-warning` | Booking and preview edges, swatches, Workstation In progress column and progress fills |
| `--neo-scheduler-bar-violet-background` | `--neo-color-background-info-subtle` | Booking and drag preview fills, category swatches |
| `--neo-scheduler-bar-violet-border-color` | `--neo-color-border-info` | Booking and preview edges, swatches, Workstation Review column and progress fills |
| `--neo-scheduler-drag-preview-box-shadow` | `0 5px 18px var(--neo-color-shadow)` | Body-rooted booking preview |
| `--neo-scheduler-bar-handle-border-radius` | `2px` | Resize grip geometry |
| `--neo-scheduler-drag-preview-border-radius` | `--neo-radius-small` | Booking preview geometry |

With direct info, the Basic category edge measures 4.37:1 Light and 5.52:1 Dark. On the combined Workstation heads, Review uses hue `240` and Ready hue `184`. The Review edge measures 4.37:1 and 6.62:1, and its text over painted progress reads 11.89:1 and 9.34:1. In the Planning view, Board edges and Scheduler edges match for In progress, Review and Done. The role-based amber edge in Basic Light rises from 2.86:1 to 4.53:1.

In a disposable compiled sheet, Material resolves all nine former gaps per scheme. Material and a fresh Neo-derived theme loaded the shared sheet and drew the actual booking and body-rooted move and resize previews in both schemes. The preview preserved its source fill, edge and 4px radius, then returned the booking to its initial geometry on Escape. Material’s three sampled edges measured at least 3.95:1 and the booking labels at least 6.65:1. The Depot legend uses those same three edge hooks.

The exact proposed palette guard at `ff6eb2128f` accepts both direct Scheduler roles. It reports the previous fill and edge mixes under `[mix]`. [Palette guard](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/pull/22573)

Decision Record impact: applies ADR 0044’s package move and exercises its categorical revalidation trigger. The direct-role alternative is measured. Preserving violet would need the design seat’s role decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The design seat records the violet decision on this leaf, with Ready and Review visually distinct on the real Workstation palette and with both category fills and edges measured.
- [ ] The shared sheet resolves all ten hooks in Neo and a fresh generated Neo-derived theme. The Material composition resolves all nine previous gaps. Run the theme build and equality check, including Classic and Cyberpunk values inside a Neo root.
- [ ] Render both schemes of Scheduler Basic and Workstation Planning. Run the existing `BasicNL` move and resize tests, verify the canceled booking stays unchanged, and check a category swatch against its bar in a real Scheduler example. On Workstation, confirm Board status edges match Scheduler edges and measure label contrast over the progress paint.

## Out of scope

Timeline and Gantt CSS, the Workstation product palette itself, and changes to Scheduler layout or gestures.

## Avoided traps

Accent reuse collapses Ready and Review on Workstation. Direct info changes the example’s established violet category to blue. Keeping the booking shadow’s existing geometry preserves its shape while each theme supplies the shadow color.
