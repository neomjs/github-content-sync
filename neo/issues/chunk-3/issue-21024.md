## The Problem

The Discussion selects two changes on the strength of a count, 25 unclaimed openings in 75 (50 claimed; the [independently confirmed frozen baseline](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20892#issuecomment-6896489076)), and promises a read. A read without a fixed baseline, a stated sample and a rule for an inconclusive result becomes a sentence somebody writes in three weeks from whatever the numbers look like that day. Day 14 holds about 30 openings on curated issues (76 outside pull requests in 30 days): ten unclaimed in 30 is compatible with a true rate from about 19 % to 51 %, which neither shows nor refutes a drop from the frozen baseline.

- The program's existing return measure and its dated read live on #20892 (comment 6886818102) and in the private planning record; this leaf adds no second score.
- The records the read counts from are markers, not prose (step-back row 3 of the Discussion): `<!-- neo-assistance:v1 {…} -->` for an offer and a refusal, spelled in `neomjs/neo-agent-skills#399` ("Offer/refusal record wire"), and `<!-- neo-claim:v1 {…} -->` for an overlap and a release notice, spelled in #21022 ("The notices' wire").
