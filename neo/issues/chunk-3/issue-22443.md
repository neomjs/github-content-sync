# theme-base: bind container's 5 files (17 variables) to roles

Owned by `@neo-gemini-julia` on the Engine `14.1` design-system critical path (`#22295` Step 6, row `container`); carries no contributor labels because active Step 6 implementation starts immediately in this session and unblocks `#22359` (`--neo-viewport-background-color`).

## Context

Step 6 row `container` of Epic #22295 ([Step 6 map](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295#issuecomment-7217835670); Rule 20 canvas pointer in `#22295`), filed and built directly under Epic #22295 Rule 13 (`17` declarations / `16` unique custom properties across `5` files, `100%` role-bound with `0` literals and `0` uncovered forks). Unblocked on `dev@c91d3f482a` now that `#22350` (`theme-neo/Global.css`) and `#22351` (`PR #22420`) have merged.

## The Problem

Measured across `resources/css/theme-neo-light/container/**` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/container/**` on `dev@c91d3f482a` (`5` CSS files: `Viewport.css` `1`, `Base.css` `2`, `Panel.css` `3`, `Accordion.css` `3`, `AccordionItem.css` `8` = `17` custom-property declarations across `16` unique names per theme, comments stripped; positive control `--neo-viewport-background-color`):

1. **Missing shared canvas & container base for new themes (`#22359`, `create-theme --base theme-neo`):** Until `container` moves into `theme-base`, any role-only theme without legacy per-widget `container/{Viewport,Base,Panel,Accordion,AccordionItem}.css` copies lacks `--neo-viewport-background-color`, `--neo-container-background-color`, `--neo-container-color`, `--neo-panel-*`, and `--neo-accordion-*`.
2. **Duplication & retiring `--neo-sem-*` reads:** All `17` declarations are duplicated across `theme-neo-light` and `theme-neo-dark`; `12` declarations in `theme-neo-dark` still read retiring `--neo-sem-*` tokens (`--neo-sem-color-bg-neutral-default`, `--neo-sem-color-surface-neutral-highlighted`, `--neo-sem-color-text-neutral-subdued`, etc.).
3. **Raw colour literals & Neo Light `#292929` accordion-title inversion:** `theme-neo-light/container/Accordion.css` paints an inverted `#292929` dark banner with `#fff` text while `theme-neo-dark` uses `surface-neutral-default`, and `AccordionItem.css` holds raw `0 4px 16px rgba(0 0 0 / .1)` shadow literals in both schemes (violating ADR 0044 I1).

## The Architectural Reality

- `Design authority: "Every component variable is declared once, in theme-base, bound to roles."` (`learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md` §2 decision 4; I1; Epic #22295 Rules 1–20).
- **Cascade & desktop popup transparency (`resources/css/src/desktop/PopupViewport.css:3-10`):** `.neo-viewport.neo-desktop-popup-viewport` and its `.neo-container` descendant rule sit in `neo.rules` above `neo.base`, so their `--neo-container-background-color: transparent` glass override continues to outrank `[class*="neo-theme-"]` in `theme-base/container/Base.css`.
- **Standalone per-file loading (`Accordion.css` & `AccordionItem.css`):** Both `src/container/Accordion.css:13` and `src/container/AccordionItem.css:5,64,80,81` read `--neo-accordion-item-border-radius`; keeping the `var(--neo-radius-small)` declaration in both `theme-base/container/Accordion.css` and `AccordionItem.css` preserves dynamic single-class stylesheet loading.
- **Classic & Cyberpunk isolation (`dev@c91d3f482a`):** `theme-light` and `theme-dark` declare all `5` files (`17` declarations) in `neo.theme` above `neo.base`; `theme-cyberpunk` (`PRE_ROLE_THEMES`) declares `container/Panel.css` (`3` declarations), and binding `--neo-accordion-item-header-height` and `--neo-accordion-item-border-radius` to scale roles (`--neo-control-height-large`, `--neo-radius-small`) rather than raw `px` literals keeps `theme-cyberpunk` at `0` computed-style differences before Step 10.

## The Fix

Create `resources/css/theme-base/container/{Accordion,AccordionItem,Base,Panel,Viewport}.css` (`5` files) declaring all `17` entries (`16` unique variables) once under `[class*="neo-theme-"]`, bound `100%` to roles, and delete `resources/css/theme-neo-light/container/**` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/container/**` (`10` files).

## Out of Scope

- Moving `theme-light`, `theme-dark`, or `theme-cyberpunk` `container` sheets onto roles (Step 10).
- `src/Global.css`'s `--neo-global-*` variables (Step 6 `global` row, Rule 20).

## Avoided Traps

- **Hardcoding `50px` or `5px` literals in `theme-base/container/AccordionItem.css`:** would alter `theme-cyberpunk` (which has no `AccordionItem.css` override before Step 10); binding to `var(--neo-control-height-large)` and `var(--neo-radius-small)` keeps `theme-cyberpunk` untouched while putting `Accordion` on the shared shape/control scales.
- **Breaking desktop popup transparency:** verified `src/desktop/PopupViewport.css:3-10` sits in `neo.rules` above `neo.base`, keeping `.neo-desktop-popup-viewport .neo-container` transparent.
