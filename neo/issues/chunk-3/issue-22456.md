# Let the example form cards and workbench paint their container backgrounds

## The problem

Checkout and signup put the form in a real container, but the card stays transparent when an app changes its container background. The example workbench uses the same unresolved variable on its stage and inspector. The stock page can mask the defect when the canvas and card happen to share a color.

## The architectural reality

Design authority: [Clio’s Checkout decision](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/pull/22438#issuecomment-7230729803) puts the outer card on the native container surface, the groups on Fieldset’s raised surface, and the controls on the field surface. `resources/css/src/container/Base.css` already paints `--neo-container-background-color`. All four affected elements carry `.neo-container`; the inspector is a Panel, which inherits Container. The two form cards also declare a Panel border on plain Containers. On the normal routes neither page requests Panel’s sheet. The computed border is `0px none` in both Neo Light and Neo Dark, where canvas and card share a fill. A headerless `Neo.container.Panel` extends Container and uses its existing items/layout path; its stylesheet owns the edge.

The four example rules read `--panel-background-color`, with no declaration in the engine sheets at `ab98a1fb22`.

On both form routes, Chromium computed a transparent card and sampled the viewport color at an exposed card pixel after setting the local container variable to `rgb(72, 81, 192)`. Removing only the overriding declaration produced the requested pixel in Neo Light and Neo Dark. The workbench’s stage and inspector also honored the local container value after removing their overrides.

## The fix

Delete the four redundant background declarations from Checkout, validation and ConfigurationViewport. Use a headerless Panel for each form card so its native stylesheet supplies the intended edge, and remove the two example-owned border declarations. The existing component paints the surface. The container migration #22443 owns the shared role binding.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the shipped Checkout and signup examples, a local `--neo-container-background-color` paints the exposed card in both Neo schemes. The screenshot and computed color agree.
- [ ] The configuration workbench stage and inspector honor that same component variable.
- [ ] All four reads are removed. On both form pages the native Panel paints a visible 1px edge in both Neo schemes while retaining the existing children and layout.
