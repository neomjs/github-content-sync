# The examples section becomes Neo's kitchen sink

Terminal predicate: a first-time visitor opens Examples on the released Portal and comes away informed, excited and convinced: every group of the engine is in reach from every examples page, each published example is current, captioned and shown at its best, the component examples run in a workbench that makes their configs explorable, and two readers without session context grade the first screen and one group page at 9 of 10 or better.

## The target, Wed 18 Nov 2026

One section for every seat that builds or reviews under this epic: the design, what each of the 22 groups shows at the cut, and the open lanes. It replaces the per-group targets that stood in comments (map v2, the slate's parts) and the overview of [pass 2](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21130#issuecomment-6936132087).

### Design

1. **One shell.** On the desktop the group list (`GroupIndex`, presentation `list`) stands at the left of every examples route, `/examples` included: the find field, "All examples", Apps, then five bands ("The list" below). On the phone the crumb and its sheet stay as built.
2. **A list entry navigates.** It opens the group's page at its own route and filters nothing: a group route is a search landing with its own title, Back works, and a link can be shared.
3. **`/examples` is "Start here".** The headline with the count, the lead as built (it keeps the sentence on workers), the action, the build control, then the twelve featured demonstrations as cards, each with its group's name. The first card is the lead and takes two columns and two rows; a group page gives its first demonstration the same weight. No group cards and no band sections at any size. The banded index shows on the phone only, under the cards: on the desktop the list is the index. `featured: true` selects a record, the catalog's order places it.
4. **Two kinds of entry.** A *demonstration* has a story and data, and gets a cover card. A *component* entry is one component in the workbench, and gets a compact tile: the component itself on the dark theme's surface, enlarged to fill the tile, and its name. The record says which (`kind: 'component'`; a demonstration is the default). A tile needs no hand-made cover: one recipe captures every workbench stage's component by its own rectangle. Visitor text says "every config live", not "workbench".
5. **Headers.** The list has its five bands. A group page has one header per kind when it holds both ("Demonstrations", then "Field types" or "Components"), and none when it holds one. No other grouping in Engine 14.
6. **Density.** Three card columns beside the list at 1440×900, six tiles per row. This replaces the two-column rule of #21241, which was mine and derived from a legibility measure: a cover is made to read at 340 px (one feature, cut tight), the grid is not widened to fit a cover.
7. **Every cover and crop is taken in the dark theme,** and a title says what the example shows, never a class name ("Date field", not `form.field.Date`). A card and a tile show at rest that they open a running example (a small cue on the caption), every count line says "live examples", and the multi-window badge carries a word: the open half of #21131, folded into the Shell lane.

8. **A find field.** The list answers "what is in this family"; it cannot answer a question that cuts across families (drag and drop in every area, everything with a date) or a visitor who knows a name and not our group for it. So: one field at the head of the list, and on the phone at the head of the sheet and of the entry page.
   - It filters as the visitor types. The content area shows the matches as cards and tiles under their group's name; the list shows each group's number of matches and dims a group without one. Clearing the field returns to the page that was open.
   - It searches an entry's title and caption (`feature`, `lookFor`), the titles of its home group and of every group in its `alsoIn`, and an optional `keywords` list on the record. Titles alone would miss "drag" on a card named "Sprint Board".
   - "/" focuses it, Escape clears it and returns focus. The query stays out of the route in this first step, so typing writes no history.
   - A capability visitors ask for by name keeps a curated cross-list in the list (Drag & Drop today): curation is complete and ordered, the field covers what nobody curated.

**One cold read of the board** (a front-end engineer who evaluates component libraries, unlabelled frames): the entry page 8 against 5 as built, Forms & Fields 7 against 3. What keeps both from a 9 is in the design now (a lead card, the workers sentence, tiles that show one recognisable control, band headers that can be read) or is content: covers in one theme, cut tight. The reader also asked, unprompted, for a filter over all entries: design 8, the Find lane, in Engine 14's scope. That read saw the board's first version; the one above carries these edits and the re-cut list, and has not been read cold.

**Start here, in order:** the 100,000 × 200 grid · Workstation · the tree grid at scale · Dock Choreography · the multi-window COVID app · GitHub Issues Grid · Studio Bookings (scheduler) · Community Garden Plan (Gantt) · Sprint Board · Calendar · Web Desktop · Helix.

## Problem scope

**The operator's direction, Mon 16 Nov 2026** (relayed here; recorded the same day in three comments on #20498). The examples section was meant kitchen-sink-like from the start. Begin with the inventory of what the engine has to offer, then choose the meaningful ones. Engine 14 needs many new examples; several existing ones are old; the grids need more. The component examples look like a storybook, and that part is to be upgraded into something like a storybook for Neo. As it stands on `dev`, the section blocks the Engine 14 cut. His measure is the one for a blog post: a first-time visitor is informed, excited and convinced.
