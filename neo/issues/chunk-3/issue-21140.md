# The examples section becomes Neo's kitchen sink

Terminal predicate: a first-time visitor opens Examples on the released Portal and comes away informed, excited and convinced: every group of the engine stands on one overview, each published example is current, captioned and covered, the component examples run in a workbench that makes their configs explorable, and two readers without session context grade the first screen and one group page at 9 of 10 or better.

## Problem scope

**The operator's direction, Mon 16 Nov 2026** (relayed here; recorded the same day in three comments on #20498). The examples section was meant kitchen-sink-like from the start. Begin with the inventory of what the engine has to offer, then choose the meaningful ones. Engine 14 needs many new examples; several existing ones are old; the grids need more. The component examples look like a storybook, and that part is to be upgraded into something like a storybook for Neo. As it stands on `dev`, the section blocks the Engine 14 cut. His measure is the one for a blog post: a first-time visitor is informed, excited and convinced.

**What the record shows** ([the inventory](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20498#issuecomment-6918253093), every runnable entry rendered once at dev `a29510a067`): 139 examples and 15 apps. The catalog publishes 72 of them, 26 with a cover. Six groups have no record at all, trees among them. 66 of the 139 examples are a small component beside a hand-written config panel on a white page. Two readers without session context grade today's first screen 5 of 10 and the released one 3 and 2 ([the first look](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21130#issuecomment-6918809237)).

**Why an epic, and why a new one.** #20498 owns this outcome and is saturated: 31 sub-issues, and its learning half is a different stream. Its examples half failed in one specific way. Preferences and decisions were written into comments and into two Discussions that never graduated; no leaf carried them; the baseline shipped instead. A trace of 16 Nov over both Discussions, nine tickets, two pull requests and `dev` found these with no ticket:

- the groups overview with a breadcrumb sibling switcher, the operator's preference of 10 Nov (Discussion #20742). The rotated rail had been decided about twenty hours earlier and was never reopened;
- the steward's own rule of 10 Nov that the rail is replaced if eight tabs do not fit ([comment](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20498#issuecomment-6782048768)). The measurement of 11 Nov said they do not fit, and nothing followed;
- Layouts as a group, and an `alsoIn` list for a record that belongs to two groups, proposed three times;
- the publication bar of Discussion #20742 (an example resets, works at phone width, in both themes, by keyboard), while nine of 28 Featured records have no proof line;
- the buried field types and the missing demonstrations;
- the examples' configuration panel, on the operator's word that it "could get enhanced a LOT" (Discussion #20744, no comment since 10 Nov);
- which examples Engine 14 shows at all. No slate exists.

Several coordinated leaves, three of them looks that must precede their builds, one gate: that is an epic.

## Intended solution shape

Four streams toward one gate. A leaf is one pull request, or one decision with its evidence on the ticket. A look comes before its build.

1. **The shell.** `/examples` opens on an overview of the groups of [map v2](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20498#issuecomment-6918253093): 22 groups in four bands. Groups are data, a record can list the other groups it belongs to, and from a group page every other group stays in reach. How a visitor moves between groups is undecided (the switcher he preferred, an upright list, today's rail) and is settled by a first-click read on the real Portal.
2. **The slate.** One decision per group: which examples stay as they are, which are renewed, which retire, and which new ones Engine 14 needs, the grids first. Every new or renewed example becomes its own leaf. The publication bar of Discussion #20742 is applied here, per example.
3. **The workbench.** `examples/ConfigurationViewport.mjs` carries 66 examples, so one change to it changes how all of them look and what a visitor can do in them. A first slice for Engine 14: a stage that shows the component at a readable size on the theme's surface, an inspector, a reset, a theme switch. Then the product surface Discussion #20744 describes: state in the URL, the config as code, events, sizes. An integration of Storybook itself (#5010) is not this.
4. **Publication.** Every published example has a record, a caption and a cover made by recipe. No card without a cover, and a group publishes with its first covered record.

**The gate** is [proposed on #20498](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20498#issuecomment-6918379055) and read at the v14 check on Fri 20 Nov. What a no-go does to the cut is the operator's and the release holder's call.

**Ownership.** Leaves are claimed, never assigned. The steward is the design seat: the looks, the slate and the gate's read are hers; a build belongs to whoever claims it.

**Authority.** This epic is operator-directed. Discussions #20742 and #20744 are inputs, not its source: neither has graduated. The one leaf that would build an option #20742 has not decided, #21130, stays a provisional reservation until the first-click read or the operator's word.

## Out of scope

- The learning section: it stays with #20498.
- The engine families themselves (board, scheduler, Gantt: #20874). Their examples enter the slate.
- A commercial feature in a caption or an example before 1 Dec (the rule of #20498).
- The Portal's home (#20682) and the crawler surfaces (#20671), beyond the group routes.

## Avoided traps

- **A Discussion as the only holder of a decision.** That is how the overview was lost for six days. A preference gets a leaf on the day it is recorded, as a reservation if it is undecided.
- **Deciding the navigation from the day's tab count.** Seven tabs on 10 Nov, ten on 16 Nov, 22 groups in the map.
- **A placeholder where a cover is missing.** #21119 removed it; a group simply appears later.
- **Whole-window covers of the configuration viewport.** They read as empty. A cover shows one feature at a readable size, in one theme.
- **New examples before the slate.** A new example names the question it answers and why no existing one can (Discussion #20742).
- **Splitting Grid and Table.** The gate of 7 Nov counts twenty-plus in one family, and its route is the one crawlers know.
