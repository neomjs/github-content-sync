# Engine 14 release notes, mined and iterated to the cut

Terminal predicate: Engine 14 is published by `publish.mjs` with release notes a reader can follow: named chapters on what the release delivered, every claim anchored to its primary, the counts regenerated at the cut head, and the window's outside contributors named.

## Problem

Engine 14 is the front-door release (`ROADMAP.md`, *Next: Engine 14 — The Front Door*; milestone v14.0), cut on Monday 2026-11-23. `publish.mjs prepare` refuses to cut without `.github/RELEASE_NOTES/v<version>.md`. At 2026-11-15 16:1xZ no notes file, branch, ticket or claim existed.

The window since 13.4.0 (published 2026-11-09T13:58:43Z) is not small. A live GitHub count at 16:17Z, six days in, finds 153 merged pull requests and 140 closed issues. In the same window:
- a timeline, a scheduler and a Gantt chart reached `dev`, and the board is due by Thu Nov 19 (#20874);
- the engine opened seams for packages that live outside its repository (#20667);
- the Portal's discoverability, its example overviews and its home section are cornerstones in work (#20671, #20498, #20682);
- outside contributors merged their work: #20892 counts 26 accounts with a merge in the window (its comment `issuecomment-6897057793`).

"The Front Door" names the gate. It must not shrink the story (release-notes §1, rule 4).

**Why an epic, not one ticket.** The release-notes protocol makes notes multi-iteration by design. 13.4.0 folded its epic into one leaf (#20496's closing comment) because its headline chapter was the open count, which moved until the gate; written once at the gate, those notes were one PR. Engine 14 is planned as a major, and most of its chapters are on `dev` already, so they can be mined while the arcs are fresh. Iteration 1 merges early behind its banner, mining leaves refine the chapters, and the final iteration (banner off, counts at the cut head, designations recorded) gates `publish.mjs prepare`. Each of these is a one-PR leaf, linked here as a sub-issue.

## Intended solution

- **The staging note** `.github/RELEASE_NOTES/v<version>.md`, iterated on `dev` under `.agents/skills/release-notes` (§1–§7). The version is the one the Nov 20 reading fixes on #20874; 14.0.0 is planned. The bar is the precedent set, read from GitHub Releases: 13.0.0, the last major, with its five hero chapters; 12.1.0, 11.24.0, 11.23.0 and 11.22.0; and 13.4.0, the previous note, whose sections the new one keeps or drops by a decision recorded here (§7).
- **A census first.** Before iteration 1, each cornerstone's steward is asked what will be on `dev` at the cut and what stays open, and the answers are recorded on this epic. A chapter is written only from what the census and the mining show.
- **Scope from the corpus** (§2): `buildScripts/release/analyzeClosedSinceRelease.mjs` with 13.4.0's publish time as the cutoff, re-run against live GitHub at the cut head.
- **Chapters from Memory Core mining per arc** (§3). The candidates are the five cornerstones and the outside contributions. An arc without mining behind it is a candidate, not a chapter.
- **The outside contributors** are named by script from #20892's program: accounts outside the team with a merge in the window, each with its pull requests.
- **Every claim anchored** (§4). Designations: release type major for 14.0.0, stability Stable. The operator left 13.4.0's designations to the steward on 2026-11-09, so these stand unless the operator says otherwise, and the final iteration records them.
- **Public wording** follows cornerstone 2 (#20667): no consumer of the hosting seams is named.

## Avoided traps

- **A changelog dump with a title on top.** Grouped tables support the story; they never replace it (§5).
- **A single-pass PR.** The v13.1 first draft shipped environment errors and one-lens narration (§1).
- **Counting from the engine's own content mirror.** It no longer exists (#19159); the corpus is the only source.
- **A chapter that reports an open cornerstone as finished.** A cornerstone still open at the cut is told by what it delivered and what it still owes.
