## Context

On 16 Nov 2026 the examples section's rail was measured while its first screen was read for covers (headless Chromium against the branch of #21124). The rail is the left tab bar every Portal section shares. #20690 moved the example families into it and put the build control at its foot. Five tabs publish on `dev`. #21129 adds Scheduler, Gantt and Board, and Components and State & Data publish with their first covered record (#21120, #20696).

A leaf of #20498, cornerstone 1 of Engine 14 (`ROADMAP.md`), cut on Mon 23 Nov.

## The Problem

A rotated tab needs its label's width plus 46 px (font `600 16px Source Sans 3`; the constant is 45.6 to 46.3 px across the five rendered tabs).

| Tab | Height | Source |
|---|---:|---|
| Featured | 107 px | measured |
| Grid & Table | 129 px | measured |
| Forms | 89 px | measured |
| Multi-Window | 142 px | measured |
| Calendar | 108 px | measured |
| Scheduler | 115 px | from the same font metrics |
| Gantt | 84 px | from the same font metrics |
| Board | 87 px | from the same font metrics |
| Components | 134 px | from the same font metrics |
| State & Data | 130 px | from the same font metrics |
| The build control | 156 px | measured |

Ten tabs need 1,125 px, with the build control 1,281 px. The rail is the viewport's height minus the 58 px header:

| Viewport | Rail | Families shown of ten |
|---|---:|---|
| 1920×1080 | 1,022 px | 7 (computed) |
| 1440×900 | 842 px | 5 (computed) |
| 1366×768 | 710 px | 4 (computed) |
| 1280×720 | 662 px | 3 (observed today with five families: Multi-Window and Calendar sit behind the overflow control) |

The computed rows reserve the build control and 48 px for the overflow control, the size read off the 1280×720 frame. No row sits within 39 px of a threshold.

At 1280×720 a stranger reads Featured, Grid & Table, Forms and a three-dot button. Nothing says that Calendar, Scheduler, Gantt, Board, Multi-Window, Components or State & Data exist. `Neo.tab.plugin.Overflow` keeps the active tab visible, so a visitor who lands on `/examples/gantt` sees Gantt and loses another family instead.

## The Architectural Reality

- `apps/portal/view/shared/TabContainer.mjs`: `tabBarPosition: 'left'` and `Neo.tab.plugin.Overflow` on the header toolbar, for News and Examples (#20599).
- `apps/portal/view/examples/TabContainer.mjs`: `familyDefinitions` is the one ordered source of family, title, route and icon; `buildModes` feed the control at the rail's foot (`'Build: ' + …`, #20690).
- `src/tab/plugin/Overflow.mjs`: headers beyond the bar's main-axis extent collapse behind one control; the active and the focused header are protected.
- Styles: `resources/scss/src/apps/portal/shared/TabContainer.scss`, `resources/scss/src/apps/portal/examples/List.scss`.
- Design authority: "One overview page per family on the portal at the kitchen-sink standard" (#20498, Intended solution shape 1). The rail's capacity was never decided. #20690 kept "the section's tab bar" as an avoided trap of that leaf, and #20599 put "the bar's visual design: rotated labels, icons" out of scope while noting that "each new tab still costs height".

## The Fix

1. **The look first, on this ticket, before any build** (the design seat, by Tue 17 Nov): the running Portal with the groups of map v2 at 1440×900, 1280×720 and 390×844, every state as a whole first screen beside the released 13.4 page.
   - **The overview page:** `/examples` as a groups overview (the operator's recorded preference, Discussion #20742), laid out in the four bands of map v2, beside today's Featured page.
   - **A group page, three ways:** the breadcrumb with a sibling switcher (his preference); an upright group list beside the cards; today's rotated rail as the control. A horizontal strip is out: 22 labels do not fit one line.

If the switcher is picked, the breadcrumb and route constraints named in Discussion #20742 (its "Source constraints" section and Julia's comment) are part of this build: the breadcrumb's key types and route normalisation, its disabled last crumb, and a routed button whose menu fires both.

## Out of Scope

The cards and covers (#21124, #20679, #21120, #21121); which families exist (#21129, the map on #20498); the rail of News and Learn; a search field or grouping inside a family; what a family with one example shows on its page.

## Avoided Traps

- **Moving the build control only.** It returns 156 px, one family.
- **Shorter labels or icons only.** #20599 named it: each new tab still costs height.
- **One tab for Calendar, Scheduler, Gantt and Board.** Their routes are separate search landings (#21116).
- **A new navigation for every Portal section.** The measurement is the examples' ten families; News has seven tabs and its overflow control was the answer there.
- **Deciding the navigation from the day's tab count.** Ten tabs were the count of 16 Nov; the map has 22 groups, and the next one is a line in `familyDefinitions`.
- **Scrolling the rotated rail.** #20599 rejected it: a scroll without an affordance leaves hidden tabs undiscovered.

**What keeps it from a 9, named by both readers**

1. Proof and scope belong on the same screen: every group above the fold, and the applications in view. One anchor for the eye, not eleven equal tiles.
2. The tiles are texture. Small, light and dark mixed, and several read as toys (a lorem-ipsum tree, a cube, a clock, an empty calendar). Each needs a cover that shows its one feature at a readable size, in one theme.
3. The numbers do not reconcile: "150" against 79 that can be counted, and "curated" where a number should stand.
4. "Build: Production" is unexplained in the best place of the page.
5. No action that tests the claim ("open the 100,000 × 200 grid"), and no cue that a card opens live (#21131).
6. The thin groups show: Scheduler 1, Gantt 1, Calendar 2.
7. Phone: the groups have to be in reach within the first 844 px, and the applications row needs a cue that it scrolls.

The engineer's first point is larger than this page: nothing on it says what Neo.mjs is.
