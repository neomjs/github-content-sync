# Give every released-vs-dev difference a verdict before the 14.1 cut

Owner: Ada (`@neo-opus-ada`), holder of the 14.1 bug gate (#22364). Split from #22448: that leaf delivers the comparison runner, and this one delivers the verdicts the cut needs from it.

## Context

#22448's runner (`npm run theme-release-compare`) loads every theme-Neo surface on neomjs.com and on a local `dev` build, in light and dark. It reports each element whose computed style differs, with the custom properties that resolve to the `dev` value. Its first full run, on `dev` `4ffe11d572`, gave 190 runs (surface × scheme):
- 164 differ;
- 22 are equal;
- 4 failed to load, the two surfaces new on `dev` (`button/floatingAction`, `grid/orderReviewDesk`).

A refined second run is on `dev` `3a767aa484`. It reads text colour where it paints, adds list items, and ignores neutral re-tints no reader sees.

## The Problem

Most differences are decided step-6 moves. Some are regressions nobody has named: the operator found the plain-button border (#22451) by eye, after equality and the `dev` frames had passed it. Without a verdict per difference, the cut cannot tell the two apart.

## The Architectural Reality

- Verdict sources: #22295's rules 1–24, the design seat's rulings on each row's leaf, and the step-6 row tickets (#22484's subs).
- The report's "Resolves from" column names the role or component variable behind each `dev` value, which points to the row that moved it.
- Known verdicts are already listed in #22448's body: the button border is `broken`, fixed by #22452; the dialog title, bars, edge and scrim are `intentional`; the dialog footer is `broken`, #22463; the form cards are `intentional`, #22459.

## The Fix

Triage the latest run on the cut candidate, surface by surface. Group rows by the variable they resolve from, so one decided row covers every surface it reaches. Each group gets one of two verdicts:
- `intentional`, citing its decision (a rule, a row ticket or a design-seat ruling);
- `broken`, citing a filed `bug` that joins #22364.

The verdict table lives in a comment here and is kept current per run.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: Every differing row of the run on the cut candidate carries a verdict: `intentional` with its decision, or `broken` with a filed bug on #22364.
- [ ] AC-2: A run after the fixes shows no `broken` row left without an owner.

## Out of Scope

- The runner itself (#22448).
- Gating CI on the report.

**Explained, not bugs.** Every surface whose frames differ by more than 5% of their pixels is accounted for. Most move with the rows above (raised bars, grid headers, no zebra). The rest:
- the dialog scrim (rule 19) darkens the whole page in three examples;
- frames caught at different moments: worker/canvas animates, calendar/year's year view sat at another scroll position (light: January against November), the Portal hero was mid-fade, grid/streaming had streamed a different number of rows;
- list/reorder gained its keyboard hint (#22250, PR #22302);
- component/wrapper/mermaid renders no diagram on `dev`. The export lacks `dist/mermaid.mjs`, which `npm run bundle-browser-deps` builds (and Legit's `dist/monaco`). That is the export's gap, not the release's.
