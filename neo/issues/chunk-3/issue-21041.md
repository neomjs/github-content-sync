# A release-day post and its outbound tell Engine 14, seven years on

## Context

Engine 14 is cut on Monday 2026-11-23, seven years to the day after [the public release announcement](https://codeburst.io/neo-mjs-the-public-release-announcement-1519255bc8f9) (`ROADMAP.md`, *Next: Engine 14 — The Front Door*). Its scope is set to carry momentum, and the release counts outside contributors, aiming at 75 or more (#20892). The notes are #21032 and the cut record is #21033. Both put outbound posts about the release out of scope, and no post is planned for the release day. This ticket is that post: the writing, with its outbound drafted beside it.

## The Problem

- **Nobody hears about a release that nobody tells.** Grace's reading of the 13.2 window (2026-10-14) found that the work succeeded and its distribution failed. Her advice was to tell the story rather than announce a framework release.
- **What a stranger can now do is new.** A board, a timeline, a scheduler and a Gantt chart join the component families on one shared time axis (#20874). So a team that builds a planning or tracking tool no longer has to leave the engine. The front door work (#20682, #20498, #20671) is where that stranger arrives.
- **The post has a hard date.** neomjs.com serves the published npm package, so the post reaches the site only if it is on `dev` before `publish.mjs prepare` runs for 14.0.0. Its lock is Sunday 2026-11-22, 18:00 CET (`blog-post` §5).
- **The contributor goal needs a door.** A reader who wants to help should land on the open `good first issue` list. The contributor count comes from #20892's script, through the notes.

## The Architectural Reality

- The post is `learn/blog/<slug>.md`, registered by hand in `apps/portal/resources/data/blog.json`. `apps/portal/sitemap.xml` and `apps/portal/llms.txt` are generated by `buildScripts/docs/seo/generate.mjs` and never hand-edited.
- The cover (2400 × 1260) and the images live in `neomjs/pages` under `resources_pub/website/blog/<slug>/` and merge before the post.
- The outbound drafts (X, LinkedIn) are written with the post, in the same pull request, per `.agents/skills/blog-post/references/outbound-post.md`.
- The post's numbers come from the notes (#21032) and the cut record (#21033). It invents none of its own.

## The Fix

1. Mine the window, then pick one reader: a team building a planning or tracking tool, on its own records.
2. Open on that job. Show the four families on one time axis with one diagram or capture. Close with a Try-It that runs from a fresh `npx neo-app` workspace, and with the contributor's next step.
3. Draft the X and LinkedIn posts with it.
4. Get two reviews, one from another model family and one read as a stranger. Run one consolidated fact check at the final version by a seat that did not draft. The operator approves last.
5. Merge on `dev` before the cut.

## Out of Scope

- The release notes (#21032) and the cut (#21033).
- The distribution plan: channels, timing and reach live in the team's private planning repository, as #13383 set.
- Films.

## Avoided Traps

- **A changelog with a date.** The notes hold the full account. The post tells one reader's job and links the notes.
- **A product-name headline.** It is accurate, and invisible to a stranger (`blog-post` §3, flavour 6).
- **A post written after the cut.** It would reach neomjs.com only with the next npm version.
