# Guard that theme-base values read roles only (ADR 0044 decision 4)

## Context

Step 6 of #22295 moves each package's component variables into `theme-base`, bound to roles. Two rows filed on 28 Nov bound a variable to a mix of two roles, and every guard and all of CI passed them:

- #22565 (Board): `--neo-board-column-background: color-mix(in oklab, var(--neo-color-background-surface), var(--neo-color-background-inverse) 5%)`
- #22511 (dashboard, mine): `--neo-dock-proxy-border-color: color-mix(in srgb, var(--neo-color-border-accent) 38%, var(--neo-color-border-default))`

A reviewer caught the first by reading the ADR ([review 6096911227](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/pull/22565#pullrequestreview-6096911227)); the second is my own. About twenty package rows are still open under #22484.

## The Problem

ADR 0044 decision 4 holds a component variable to roles, with one exception: a role mixed with `transparent`. Rule 5 of #22295 keeps raw colour out of `theme-base` (I1). No check reads the shape of a `theme-base` value:

- `check-theme-palette` refuses a role declared outside a `Global.css` and measures role pairs, but does not read what a component variable holds.
- `theme-census` reads `theme-base` only to resolve reads (`theme-census.mjs:278-283`). Its per-theme `composition` counts each theme's own sheets, and `compositionOf` calls any value with one role read a `role`, so a two-role mix, or a role mixed with a raw colour, counts as a clean binding. I1 says the census reports a raw colour in a component variable; for `theme-base` it reports nothing.

A two-role mix is a colour no role names, so the palette check cannot measure the pairs it forms. That measurability is the reason decision 4 gives for "roles only".

## The Architectural Reality

Design authority: ADR 0044 decision 4: "Every component variable is declared once, in `theme-base`, bound to roles. […] One exception to "roles only": a component may mix a role with `transparent` for a translucent treatment of its own, as the desktop window's glass does. A second reader makes it a role." I1 adds: "Keywords such as `light`, `dark` and `transparent` are not raw colour values, so `--neo-color-scheme` and a component's own translucent mix pass."

- **Owner:** `buildScripts/util/check-theme-palette.mjs` holds the measurable rules and already reads every component sheet for misplaced roles (`[scope]`). It runs in `pr-baseline.yml` with the other theme guards. `themeCss.mjs` holds the shared readings.
- **Precedent:** `check-theme-radius-literals.mjs` reads `radiusLiterals` from `themeCss.mjs`, which `theme-census` reports too. It fails on anything outside an `ALLOWED` list whose entries carry their reasons and fail once they no longer match (#22353).
- **On `dev` at `0e9fa32f2c`:** no raw colour in `theme-base`, and all eight `color-mix()` values mix one role with `transparent`: Button's ripple, Range's thumb shadow inside a shorthand, the grid and table scrollbar thumbs, and AnimatedCurrency's two flashes.

## The Fix

1. `themeCss.mjs` gains one reading of a declaration value's colour sources. It distinguishes role or palette reads (with the lengths and keywords around them, as in `0 2px 8px var(--neo-color-shadow)`), one read mixed with `transparent`, a `color-mix()` of two colour sources (two reads, or a read and a raw colour), and a raw colour.
2. `check-theme-palette.mjs` fails a `theme-base` component variable that holds a two-source mix (`[mix]`) or a raw colour (`[raw]`), naming the file, the variable and the value. An `ALLOWED` list keeps listed colour values with their reasons and fails an entry that no longer matches; rule 26's Monaco canvas adds the first entry when #22493 lands.
3. `theme-census` reports `theme-base`'s composition from the same reading, which closes I1's report for the shared layer.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: In a fixture `theme-base` sheet, the palette check fails both two-role mixes quoted in Context and a raw colour (`#0a7a6e`), each line naming the file, variable and value. It passes a role read, a role inside a shorthand, a role mixed with `transparent`, a `light-dark()` choice between two roles, a length and a keyword.
- [ ] AC-2: On `dev`, the check passes, reading every current `color-mix()` as one role with `transparent`.
- [ ] AC-3: An `ALLOWED` entry that no longer matches fails the run.
- [ ] AC-4: `npm run theme-census` reports `theme-base`'s composition, and the census spec pins the new kinds on its fixture.

## Out of Scope

- Rebinding the two values found: #22565 (its RA-1) and #22511 (its author's repair).
- A theme's own per-widget files (`neo.theme`), which hold values where its design language deviates, and the legacy themes' sheets.
- Structural rules in `src` (the radius guard owns their radii, and the census reports their colour literals), and `apps/` and `examples/` sheets.

## Avoided Traps

- **A new guard file.** The palette check is the measurability gate that decision 4's rule serves, and it already reads every component sheet.
- **Folding the census into a gate** (#22270): the census keeps reporting, and the palette check decides.
- **A CSS parser dependency** (#22270): the guards read comment-stripped text with regular expressions, and the spec pins the edge cases (nested `var()` fallbacks, `light-dark()`, shorthands).
