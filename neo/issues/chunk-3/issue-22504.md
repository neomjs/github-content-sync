# theme-base: bind dashboard's 3 files (23 variables) to roles

Owner: Vega (`@neo-opus-vega`). This is the `dashboard` row of #22484, the parent of #22295's step 6.

## Context

Step 6 declares each engine package's variables once in `theme-base`, bound to theme Neo's roles (`resources/css/theme-neo/Global.css`). `dashboard` has three sheets with 23 variables. They paint the dock while a drag runs: the preview of where a panel will land (accept, reject and signal), the arrival outline, the drag proxy, the drop chips and a detached window's placeholder.

## The Problem

`theme-neo-light/dashboard/` and `theme-neo-dark/dashboard/` declare the 23 as literals: a teal family (`#0d9488`, `#0f766e`, `#5eead4`), a rose for reject, slate surfaces, `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)` chip shadows and five radii. No role carries any of them, so neither a theme written against the roles nor a product palette reaches the dock. A theme generated with `--base theme-neo` falls back to the structural sheets' own blues where a reader has a fallback, and loses the signal, the proxy, the chips and the radii where it has none.

## The Architectural Reality

- Design authority: #22295's rules, with ADR 0044 behind them. Rule 1 binds by ending, and a name read by two or more properties is bound by hand or split by reader. Rule 5 keeps no raw colour in `theme-base`. Rule 16 keeps a length off the scale as a listed literal, or binds it to the nearest role with the difference declared. Rules 17 and 25 bind the released teal by its job, and accent marks what acts.
- Readers: `resources/css/src/dashboard/Container.css`, `dock/interaction/Preview.css` and `dock/window/VesselPlaceholder.css` read all 23. `accept-color` and `reject-color` are read as a border and as a fill, and `signal-color` as a border and inside two glows. No `.mjs` file reads any of them.
- `resources/css/src/apps/workstation/Workspace.css` mixes `--neo-dock-preview-reject-color` into Workstation's own reject surface.
- `theme-light`, `theme-dark` and `theme-cyberpunk` declare all 23, so the classic themes keep their values and need no `initial` lines.
- #22353 turned the dock's five literal radii into these variables and left binding them to `--neo-radius-*` to this move (its Out of Scope).

The ratios are each chip's strongest stroke pixel against its most common ground pixel, read from the rendered frame.

### For the acting design seat

1. **The two overlays.** The preview panel and the signal chip float over the dock's content at 94 %. Proposed: the panel reads `surface-raised`, since it floats like a bar, and the chip reads `surface`, since its released dark is the near-black canvas. Or should one role serve both?
2. **The chips' shadow.** Rule 5 removes the raw `rgba(0, 0, 0, …)`. The proposal keeps the geometry and reads `color-shadow`, so the alpha goes from 0.35 to 0.16 in light and 0.50 in dark. Or should the chips read `elevation-2`, as menus do?
