# Move the pinch and rotate recognizer into src/main/gesture

Owned by @neo-opus-grace: I offered it in the #22437 review thread and Euclid accepted. No curated labels, because this is owned work that waits on #22447's module contract.

## Context

#22437 put pinch and rotate on `Neo.main.addon.Gesture` as methods (dev `9dcc6c6d7c`): `trackTransform`, `queueTransformFrame`, `cancelTransformFrame`, the `#transformClaims` WeakMap, and a `contact` subscription in `construct`. Its two sibling recognizers are still open and use another shape:
- #22447 (tap/press) builds `src/main/gesture/TapPress.mjs`, a `Neo.core.Base` class registered with `Neo.setupClass`, which the addon creates with `Neo.create` and destroys (head `c4c09c21d2`).
- #22439 (pan/swipe) moves Translation into the same directory in the same shape (head `07f29e102d`).

Euclid, the author of all three, chose separate recognizer classes under `src/main/gesture/` for the family. Left inside the addon, the transform recognizer would be the one exception.

## The Problem

One seam would keep two shapes, and whoever adds the remaining families has to choose between them. My #22437 review also left two non-blocking content nits on this code, and both land here:
- The addon's class `@summary` names no recognizers.
- The rule for three or more contacts is unstated: scale and rotation follow the two earliest-admitted contacts, while `x`/`y` average all of them.

## The Architectural Reality

- **Owner:** `src/main/addon/Gesture.mjs` owns contact ownership, the claims, `publish()` and the lifetime rules. None of that moves. The transform recognizer only observes `contact` frames and publishes through the claim, and its state already lives apart in `#transformClaims`.
- **Precedent:** DragDrop keeps a helper beside its addon (`src/main/draggable/Resize.mjs`), and #22447 adds `src/main/gesture/` the same way, though as Neo classes where `Resize` is a plain one.
- **Gap:** the module contract (its configs and the contact observer route) lands with #22447, which is not on dev yet. Hence the blocker, #22404.

Design authority: n/a. This calls no existing behavior wrong; it changes placement only. Every event and payload stays as ADR 0045 decisions 2, 3 and 8 define them.

## The Fix

After #22447 merges, rebase on it (and on #22439 if that lands first), then:
1. Move the transform state and its three methods into `src/main/gesture/Transform.mjs`, on the contract #22447 lands for `TapPress`.
2. Have `Gesture.mjs` create it the way it creates `TapPress` (`Neo.create` with `doc` and `publish`; transform reads no policy, so no `settings`), subscribe the module's `track` to `contact` in place of its own `trackTransform`, as #22439 does for `Translation`, and destroy it. The owner gains no family branch: `TapPress`'s direct call is the one shape left to converge.
3. Point the unit spec at the module if the contract lets it be built without the addon; otherwise it keeps driving through `contact`.
4. Leave the component spec as the unchanged behavior witness.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: `src/main/gesture/Transform.mjs` holds the pinch and rotate recognizer on #22447's module contract. `Gesture.mjs` no longer declares `trackTransform`, `queueTransformFrame`, `cancelTransformFrame` or `#transformClaims`, and subscribes the module's `track` instead of its own `trackTransform`.
- [ ] AC-2: Behavior is unchanged. `test/playwright/component/main/GestureTransforms.spec.mjs` passes without an edit, and the unit coverage still passes: wraparound, degenerate vectors, membership rebase, coalescing and fresh-pair ids.
- [ ] AC-3: The class `@summary` of `Neo.main.addon.Gesture` names the recognizer modules it builds, and the module's `@summary` states the rule for three or more contacts.

## Out of Scope

- Changing any event, payload, phase or clock (ADR 0045 decisions 2, 3 and 8).
- Moving pan/swipe (#22439) or tap/press (#22447). Both are Euclid's.

## Avoided Traps

- Moving before #22447 merges. That would guess its module contract and conflict with both open PRs on `Gesture.mjs`.
