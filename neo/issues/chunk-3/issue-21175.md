# The Grid & Table group shows twenty demonstrations a buyer can check

Terminal predicate: a first-time visitor opens the Grid & Table group of the Portal's examples and finds twenty demonstrations of its own; each card opens on a frame worth reading, answers one question a buyer of a grid asks, and meets the bar of #21140 (it resets, works at phone width, in both themes and by keyboard); no card is a twin, a bench or a test fixture.

## Problem scope

The group is the first a grid buyer opens, and the gate of 7 Nov on #20498 asks it for twenty-plus demonstrations. The slate read all 21 entries on `dev` by opening and using each one ([#21142, part 1](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21142#issuecomment-6924949418)):

- Six entries show the same six team names.
- Three demonstrations exist twice, once on the grid and once on the table, and look identical.
- Two entries are test benches from 2019, and one is a test fixture that says so in its own source.
- Two look like a product (`grid/issues`, `grid/headerGroups`).

So the count is met on paper and the tab does not convince. The slate's verdict: keep 2, renew 12, retire 7, and add six examples for capabilities `dev` has and no entry shows (pinned columns, a filter row on the grid, the built-in cell types, CSV export, server-side data, a streamed load). That is 20 demonstrations of its own.

**Why an epic.** Nineteen leaves, each one example and one pull request, are read together as one tab on Fri 20 Nov and again at the cut. #21140 held 24 subs when this was filed; the slate adds a set like this one per group, so each group's leaves hang under a group epic and #21140 keeps the groups.

## Intended solution shape

- **The tab reads in the order of a buyer's questions:** does it look like a product (the issues grid), does it scale (big data, the tree at scale), can people work in it (two ways to edit, two filter rows, selection by keyboard), can it be shaped (grouped headers, pinned columns, cell types, row heights, banding, animated sort), does it reach a backend (server-side data, a streamed load, export), and what the table is for.
- **One leaf is one example:** its folder, its catalog record (`apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json`) and its cover. A renewed example keeps its folder and its record id. A new example is a sibling folder `examples/grid/<feature>/` after the model of `examples/grid/headerGroups`: its own viewport, a headline, one line that says what to try, a theme button.
- **Data reads as real.** No generated placeholder names, no random words, no header that names an implementation detail. The frame shows its own scale where scale is the point.
- **Every leaf carries a Look criterion:** the first frame at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844 goes on the pull request and is read by the design seat before code review. The cover is the last criterion and is shot with the producer of #20679.
- **Retiring is removing the record.** The folder stays wherever specs load it. A twin never counted as a demonstration (the Avoided Traps of #20500), so retiring does not wait for the new examples; the group reaches twenty when they are in.
- **Examples on the configuration viewport keep their host** until the workbench (#21143) replaces it.
- **Order of the build:** the big data grid, cell editing, the tree at scale, the country statistics, pinned columns and the grid's filter row first, for the read of Fri 20 Nov. Then selection, the row dialog, animated sorting, cell types and export. Then the rest.

## Out of scope

The other 21 groups (their own slate parts on #21142) · the card (#21119), the groups as data (#21141), the navigation (#21130) and the workbench (#21143) · the cover producer (#20679, on `dev`) · engine features `dev` does not have: row grouping, aggregation and pivot get no example and no caption that suggests them.

## Avoided traps

- **Counting twins, panes or modes** to reach twenty.
- **Calling value banding row grouping, or a view loaded from JSON a server-side row model** (both from #20500).
- **Renewing the locked-columns fixture.** Its geometry is a test contract for 14 spec files; it gets a new example beside it.
- **A leaf per finding.** One example is one pull request, whatever the read found in it.
