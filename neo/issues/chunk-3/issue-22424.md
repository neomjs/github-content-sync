# Bottom navigation on phones, the same destinations as a rail on wider screens

Unowned: open to any peer for 14.1. Check the assignee and linked PRs before claiming. A leaf of #22414 (mobile components).

## Context

Phone apps switch between three to five top-level destinations from a bar at the bottom edge. D#22259's audit names adaptive destination navigation: a bottom bar on compact screens, a rail or drawer on larger ones. Destination identity, Back and deep links stay stable across both.

## The Problem

`tab.Container` can place its tab bar at the bottom (`static tabBarPositions`, `src/tab/Container.mjs:32`, `tabBarPosition_`). It does not provide a phone navigation bar:
- the tabs own their views instead of following routes;
- it has no icon-over-label layout with an active indicator;
- the bar does not switch to a rail when the window widens.

## The Architectural Reality

- Buttons already navigate by `route` and mark the active destination with `ariaCurrent` (`src/button/Base.mjs`).
- Components get per-breakpoint configs through `responsive` (`plugin/Responsive.mjs`).
- The leaf decides between extending `tab.Container` and composing a toolbar of route buttons, and records why. The test is which keeps one destination identity across the bottom-bar and rail presentations.

## The Fix

A destination bar that:
- renders as a bottom bar on compact screens and a vertical rail on wider ones;
- shows icon and label per destination, with a badge and an active indicator;
- is driven by routes, so Back, deep links and reloads land on the same destination;
- sits inside the bottom safe area on phones.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Each destination is a route. Selecting one changes the route, and opening a deep link marks it active, with `aria-current="page"`.
- [ ] Back returns to the previous destination in both presentations.
- [ ] Crossing the breakpoint swaps bar and rail without losing the active destination or the view state.
- [ ] Keyboard: arrow keys move between destinations, Enter activates.
- [ ] The bottom bar respects the safe-area inset. A floating action button (#22415) sits above it, not on it.
- [ ] A phone example with frames at 390 px and at a wide width.

## Out of Scope

- Navigation drawers with nested sections.
- Gesture events (#22400).
