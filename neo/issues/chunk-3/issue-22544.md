# Bind LivePreview chrome to theme-base roles

## Context

LivePreview owns three CSS properties used by its shell, header toolbar and tab body. Those are the whole Engine `code` package row in the [Material coverage census](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22484#issuecomment-7233945527).

## The Problem

The two legacy Neo files supply all three values. A theme generated from consolidated `theme-neo` has the role palette with no shared LivePreview bindings.

## The Architectural Reality

[`src/code/LivePreview.css`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/76d743e589440a8381d1875a92b1a346a0a821cc/resources/css/src/code/LivePreview.css#L8-L34) reads the shadow on the preview shell, the fill on its header toolbar, and a single border colour on the header and tab body. The [existing equality fixture](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/76d743e589440a8381d1875a92b1a346a0a821cc/buildScripts/util/themeEqualityMatrix.mjs#L219-L225) samples those consumers. Classic sheets supply all three values. Cyberpunk uses the renderer without a LivePreview value sheet.

## The Fix

Move the three hooks into `theme-base/code/LivePreview.css` and retire the two legacy Neo copies. Compare the observed stock and generated paint in the actual runnable preview. Record the shell elevation decision with the design seat on this leaf.

| Hook | Binding | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--neo-live-preview-border-color` | `--neo-color-border-default` | Raised header separator and region edge, #22295 rules 4/18 |
| `--neo-live-preview-header-background-color` | `--neo-color-background-surface-raised` | Toolbar, rule 18 |
| `--neo-live-preview-container-box-shadow` | Proposed `--neo-elevation-2` | Compare the embedded card on the released and candidate views and obtain the design choice |

Prescription checked: `resources/css/theme-base/code` owns the preview value sheet. Decision Record impact: aligned with ADR 0044.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The three shared properties resolve in Neo Light and Dark and in a fresh generated Light and Dark pair.
- [ ] The LivePreview shell, tab header and body show the role bindings on the real example. Record intentional paint differences in whole released/candidate frames.
- [ ] Scoped theme equality, Classic and Cyberpunk parity, the theme guards and the existing LivePreview browser flow pass.

## Out of Scope

Monaco language and rendering changes, and the Learn/Docs wrapper bindings owned by #22534.
