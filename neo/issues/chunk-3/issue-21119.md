# An example card shows its cover whole and says less

## Context

The operator read the examples overview on 16 Nov 2026, from pull request #21105 and from `dev`. His grades: the preview design 5 of 10, the covers inside it 1 of 10. His words: "the entire preview design: HUGE text, no quality content", "the OLD VERSION looks 10x nicer", "here a 10/10 is mandatory".

The card he read is the glass-lip composition of #20724 (PR #20813). Euclid selected it on 12 Nov on my design read, under the operator's delegation. That read compared crops of one record against a black caption box. It never put a whole tab beside the 13.4 list it replaces. This ticket does.

A leaf of #20498, cornerstone 1 of Engine 14 (`ROADMAP.md`). Engine 14 is cut on Mon 23 Nov.

## The Problem

The page tells and does not show. Measured in Chromium at 1440×900 on the examples view as it stands on `dev` `2404340dfa`:

- **A card shows a quarter of its cover.** A family card is 445×499 px. The caption takes 249 px, exactly half, whatever its length. The cover is scaled to fill the whole card and anchored top-left, and the caption lies over its lower half: 27 % of the image is visible. On several covers made for the 13.4 list (`helix`, `offscreen-canvas`) that corner is the browser's own tab strip and address bar.
- **The text is as large as the picture.** Title 24 px, the proof line 17 px semibold, the action 15 px, 31 words per card on average.
- **46 of 76 records have no cover** (Grid & Table 17 of 21, Forms 21 of 24, Multi-Window 5 of 12, Calendar 3 of 4). Each renders a 48 px icon and the component's name on a navy slab. The first screen of Grid & Table is four quarter covers and two slabs. #21105 brings the count to 71 of 101.
- **Featured** is one column of 800 px with 28 cards of 400 to 546 px, half of each caption: two cards per screen.
- **The 13.4 list** paints each cover over the whole 800 px card with the title on it. It says nothing about what an example proves, and it looks like a product.

#20724 met every criterion it had: 288 card observations without overflow, contrast 11.41:1, "at least half the cover uncovered". None of them asked whether a stranger wants to open the example.

## The Architectural Reality

- `apps/portal/view/examples/List.mjs`: `createItemContent` (142-172) builds cover, placeholder and caption; `getBackgroundImageStyle` (231-241) paints the cover as a CSS background; `fallbackIconCls` (41) names the placeholder's glyph.
- `resources/scss/src/apps/portal/examples/List.scss`: `.content` with two equal rows (31-40), `.preview-image` at `inset: 0` with `background-size: cover` (42-49), the caption and its 22 px lip (51-73), the type sizes (75-141), the placeholder (143-157), the family grid (208-220). Palette aliases: `resources/scss/theme-neo-{dark,light}/apps/portal/examples/List.scss`.
- `apps/portal/model/Example.mjs:25` documents `image: null` as "selects the typographic fallback".
- `test/playwright/e2e/portal/ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs` carries #20724's docking and containment oracles; `test/playwright/unit/apps/portal/view/examples/TabContainerController.spec.mjs` the catalog counts.
- Covers are 1280×720 (precedents `#19640`, `#19947`); #20679 produces them.
- Design authority: the operator's read of 16 Nov 2026, quoted in Context. It replaces the treatment selected on 12 Nov under his delegation (#20724, "Selected treatment").

## The Fix

The look first, then one pull request.

1. **Candidates as frames, before any source change.** Rendered in the running Portal with the same six records, at 1440×900 and 390×844, dark and light, each beside the 13.4 list and the current card:
   - **Cover first:** the cover whole at 16:9 on top, never cropped and never under text; below it the title, one line that says what the example proves, the action in a quieter line, platforms and limits as one small line.
   - **Full bleed:** the 13.4 composition kept. The cover fills the card, title and proof line stand on a scrim at its foot; the action appears on hover and focus, and under the image on touch widths.
   - **Featured** as a showcase in both: larger cards, the cover leading.

   The operator reads the board and picks. His bar is 10 of 10; a candidate below it is not built.
2. **The picked card** in `List.mjs` and its styles: the cover whole, the type one step down the Portal's scale with one emphasis per card, no equal split.
3. **No cover, no card.** A record without a cover stays in the catalog and renders no card on an overview; a family's tab appears with its first covered record. The typographic fallback goes.
