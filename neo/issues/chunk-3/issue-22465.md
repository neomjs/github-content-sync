# theme-base: bind list's 2 files (16 variables) to roles

Sub-issue of #22295 (Step 6 `theme-base` package move for `list`). Owned by `@neo-gemini-julia` for immediate Engine 14.1 execution, so curated contributor labels are omitted.

## Context

Under [ADR 0044](learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md) Decision 4, Decision 5, and Migration Step 6 (governed by Epic #22295 Rules 1–23), each Engine package's Neo theme sheets (`resources/css/theme-neo-light/<pkg>/` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/<pkg>/`) move into a single role-bound `resources/css/theme-base/<pkg>/` sheet under `[class*="neo-theme-"]`, with both per-scheme Neo copies deleted.

The `list` package holds **2 files** and **16 custom properties** (`list/Base.css`: 15, `list/Circle.css`: 1). All 16 properties have live readers across `resources/css/src/list/{Base,Chip,Circle,ValuePicker}.css`, `resources/css/src/tree/{Accordion,List}.css`, `resources/css/src/draggable/list/DragZone.css`, `resources/css/src/app/content/{SectionsList,TreeList}.css`, and `resources/css/src/apps/legit/Viewport.css`.

## The Problem

1. **Selected list-item contrast failure (`ADR 0044 §5`):** `theme-neo-{light,dark}/list/Base.css` bind `--neo-list-item-color-selected` to `--neo-sem-color-text-primary-default` (`#1c60a0` · `#5472e4`) on `--neo-sem-color-surface-primary-background` (`#d6e1ff` · `#182449`), painting **`3.98:1`** in light and **`2.91:1`** in dark — below the `4.5:1` text floor in both schemes.
2. **Dark hover invisibility & old token reads:** `theme-neo-dark/list/Base.css` sets both `--neo-list-item-background-color` (`--neo-sem-color-bg-neutral-highlighted`, `#282c37`) and `--neo-list-item-background-color-hover` (`--neo-sem-color-surface-neutral-hover`, `#282c37`) to the identical colour over `--neo-list-container-background-color` (`#0d0f12`), giving dark list rows zero hover feedback while reading 10 legacy `--neo-sem-*` tokens per scheme.
3. **Raw colours in component sheets (`ADR 0044` I1):** `list/Base.css` hardcodes `#fff` (×2 in light) and `red` (`--neo-list-item-highlight-search-color` in both schemes, failing `4.5:1` on `background-selected`), and `list/Circle.css` hardcodes `rgba(62, 123, 134, 0.302)`.
4. **Hardcoded theme-folder path in `Chip.spec.mjs`:** `test/playwright/component/list/Chip.spec.mjs:111-115` constructs `/dist/development/css/theme-neo-{dark,light}/list/Base.css` directly instead of calling `themeSheetHrefs()`.

## The Architectural Reality

Verified on `origin/dev` (`2c6e9a4642`):
- `resources/css/src/list/Base.css` reads 13 of `list/Base.css`'s 15 variables; `--neo-list-border-color` is read by `resources/css/src/app/content/SectionsList.css:35`, and `--neo-list-item-border-color-active` is read by `resources/css/src/list/Chip.css:33`. Zero variables are unread.
- `resources/css/theme-{light,dark}/list/{Base,Circle}.css` re-declare 15 of the 16 variables in `neo.theme` (`--neo-list-item-background-color-active` reads a role so pre-role classic themes keep their initial fallback). `theme-cyberpunk` ships no `list/` files, so `resources/css/theme-cyberpunk/list/Base.css` sets `--neo-list-item-padding: initial` (`--neo-list-container-border: none` already matches `border`'s initial value) to preserve Cyberpunk's `0px` list/value-picker item padding unchanged until Step 10 (`#22461` / `#22445` precedent).
- `test/playwright/util/themeSheets.mjs` exports `themeSheetHrefs(themes, classNames)` so component specs load `Global.css`, `theme-base`, and per-theme overrides from `resources/theme-map.json` without hardcoding folder names.

## The Fix

Create `resources/css/theme-base/list/Base.css` and `resources/css/theme-base/list/Circle.css` under `[class*="neo-theme-"]`, preserve Cyberpunk's pre-Step-10 padding fallback in `resources/css/theme-cyberpunk/list/Base.css` (`--neo-list-item-padding: initial`), delete `resources/css/theme-neo-{light,dark}/list/{Base,Circle}.css`, and switch `test/playwright/component/list/Chip.spec.mjs` to `themeSheetHrefs([theme], ['list.Base', 'component.Chip'])`.

| the match's text | on `surface` | on `background-selected` | |
|---|---|---|---|
| `text-warning` (the row) | 6.69 · 9.69 | 4.78 · 5.01 | a status, which rule 23 excludes |
| `text-brand` | 6.19 · 14.04 | **4.42** · 7.26 | under the floor on a selected row in light. `brand-700` also sits 11° of hue from `success-700` at the same lightness, so it reads as success |
| `text-accent` | 4.98 · 7.05 | **3.55 · 3.64** | under the floor on a selected row |
| `text-strong` | 20.11 · 19.19 | 14.36 · 9.92 | passes, and cannot be told from the item's text (`text-default`: 18.37 · 17.08) |

With a fill the pair is the match's own: `text-strong` on `background-brand-subtle` is 19.10 · 14.25 on every row state. The fill stands 1.05 · 1.35 against `surface` and 1.33 · 1.44 against `background-selected`. It is carried by hue, as a `mark` is, and owes no 3:1.
