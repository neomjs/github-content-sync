# Replay bounded gesture contact traces in the Engine’s owning window

## Context

[EventSimulator](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/5ff17dedc8/src/main/addon/EventSimulator.mjs#L320) constructs real DOM Touch objects from JSON. [InteractionService](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/5ff17dedc8/src/ai/client/InteractionService.mjs#L234) already resolves target-window geometry. Repeating independent simulate_event calls leaves trace validation, coordinate conversion and interruption cleanup to each caller.

## The Fix

Provide the Engine interaction-service entry point for one bounded contact sequence and its readable presets. Reuse owning-window target resolution and materialization through EventSimulator. Handle timed stationary frames, simultaneous changes, staggered lifetimes and clean termination. Expose the engine provider, observed contact transitions, trust and terminal state.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1 Reject malformed identity transitions, non-finite coordinates, out-of-budget traces and unavailable targets before partial dispatch. Reuse the existing interaction ownership/concurrency behavior and refuse a demanded browser capability when absent.
- [ ] AC-2 Replay the [eight-event trace](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/22259#discussioncomment-19961766), a timed hold and a simultaneous transform. A timeout, unmount or window loss cleans up without a fabricated terminal success.
- [ ] AC-3 The same target-relative trace works in two windows without cross-window contact or coordinate contamination. Verify a separate app-state outcome and truthful provider receipt via the registered Engine RPC.
