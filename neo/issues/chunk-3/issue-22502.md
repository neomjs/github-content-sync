# theme-cyberpunk's tooltip paints no bubble: its text floats over the page

Owner: Ada (`@neo-opus-ada`). The operator flagged it on 2026-11-28 from #22479's evidence: [the frame](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/315fc4f11e7542b68799a404b38da06587353612/review-evidence/22476/tooltip-cyberpunk-head.png?raw=true). Emmy found the route in her #22479 review. It was captured as defect-note `2bad567b71354419`, and this ticket promotes it.

## Context

`examples/toolbar/application?theme=neo-theme-cyberpunk`, hovering **Monospace**: the tooltip "Toggle the draft's monospace font" paints no bubble. Its text sits unboxed over the "Draft" label beneath.

## The Problem

The bubble computes transparent fill, inherited `rgb(201, 209, 217)` text, `16px`, zero padding and `18px` high. That holds on `dev` before #22479 (`5ea77931a0`) and after it, so cyberpunk shipped this way. A tooltip a reader cannot tell from the page is no tooltip.

## The Architectural Reality

- `theme-cyberpunk` declares none of the 8 `--neo-tooltip-*` variables. `theme-light` and `theme-dark` each keep a `tooltip/Base.css` with all 8.
- Since #22479, `theme-base/tooltip/Base.css` binds the 8 to theme Neo's roles. Cyberpunk declares none of those roles (its tokens are `--neo-cyber-*`), so every binding computes to nothing.
- Cyberpunk's floating layers already speak one language. `theme-cyberpunk/dialog/Base.css` reads a cyan edge (`--neo-cyber-cyan`) with the cyber glow on its panel.
- Design authority: ADR 0044 decision 4, "A theme keeps a per-widget file only where its design language deviates". Cyberpunk is a classic theme until step 10, and it keeps per-widget files for every surface it styles.

## The Fix

Add `resources/css/theme-cyberpunk/tooltip/Base.css`, declaring the 8 from cyberpunk's own tokens:
- the panel fill `--neo-cyber-alt-background-color`, with text `--neo-cyber-color`;
- a 1px edge in `--neo-cyber-cyan` and the cyber glow, as the dialog has them;
- the classic tooltip box: `14px`, `24px` high, `2px 8px 0` padding;
- the corner of cyberpunk's buttons.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: On `examples/toolbar/application` in theme-cyberpunk, the hovered tooltip paints a bubble: an opaque panel fill, the cyan edge, and text at 4.5:1 or more on its fill. Frames: `dev` and head, whole viewport.
- [ ] AC-2: `npm run theme-equality` against a `dev` build passes, with only `neo-theme-cyberpunk`'s tooltip rows moving, each declared.
- [ ] AC-3: The eight `check-theme-*` guards exit 0.

## Out of Scope

- Moving cyberpunk onto the roles (ADR 0044 step 10).
