# theme-base: bind table's 3 files (31 variables) to roles

Owned by `@neo-gemini-julia` on the Engine `14.1` design-system critical path (`#22295` Step 6, row `table`); carries no contributor labels because active Step 6 implementation starts immediately in this session.

## Context

Step 6 row `table` of Epic #22295 ([Step 6 map](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295#issuecomment-7217835670)), filed and built directly under Epic #22295 Rule 13 (every variable in `table` follows Rules 1–19 and the settled `grid` twin rulings from [#22389#issuecomment-7227989668](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22389#issuecomment-7227989668) with zero uncovered forks). Blocked by #22389 (`PR #22431`), which lands the `--neo-color-background-selected` `accent-800` dark step and its `1.1:1` palette guard pair (Rule 15).

## The Problem

Measured across `resources/css/theme-neo-light/table/**` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/table/**` on `dev@c91d3f482a` (`3` CSS files: `table/Body.css` `13` vars, `table/Container.css` `13` vars, `table/header/Button.css` `5` vars = `31` custom-property declarations per theme, comments stripped; positive control `--neo-table-cell-background-color-hover`):

1. **Duplication & retiring `--neo-sem-*` reads:** All `31` declarations are duplicated across `theme-neo-light` and `theme-neo-dark`; `20` declarations in `theme-neo-dark` still read retiring `--neo-sem-*` tokens (`--neo-sem-color-surface-neutral-hover`, `--neo-sem-color-surface-primary-default`, `--neo-sem-color-text-neutral-reversed`, etc.).
2. **Classic-light `#5d83a7` header holdover & zebra drift:** `theme-neo-light/table/header/Button.css` still paints classic-light's `#5d83a7` slate-blue header bar with `#fff` text/glyphs and `rgb(28.85%, 41.31%, 53.11%)` ripple while `theme-neo-dark` uses `surface-neutral-highlighted`; `table/Container.css` paints `#f2f2f2` / `surface-neutral-highlighted` zebra stripes where `background-accent-subtle` hover in dark would step by hue alone (`1.01:1`, Rule 15 & `#22389` point 2).
3. **Raw colour literals & invisible dark custom scrollbar:** `--neo-table-cell-ismodified-color` holds raw `orange` in both schemes, and all four custom scrollbar colours in `table/Container.css` hold raw black `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05..0.4)` in both light and dark (making the scrollbar thumb near-invisible at `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)` on dark's `#0e0f13` surface and violating ADR 0044 I1 and Rule 4's `3:1` thumb boundary floor).

## The Architectural Reality

- `Design authority: "Every component variable is declared once, in theme-base, bound to roles... One exception to 'roles only': a component may mix a role with transparent for a translucent treatment of its own, as the desktop window's glass does."` (`learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md` §2 decision 4; I1; Epic #22295 Rules 1–19).
- **Sticky locked-column opacity invariant (`resources/css/src/table/Container.css:43-59,69-75` & `src/table/Body.css:31-35`):** `td.neo-locked` (`position: sticky; z-index: 10`) and `th` (`position: sticky; top: 0; z-index: 10`, `.neo-locked` `z-index: 30`) scroll over adjacent cells, and `.neo-table-row:hover .neo-table-cell` replaces `background-color`. Exactly like `grid/Body.css`, cell hover, rest, `even`, and selection fills MUST resolve to **opaque** colour roles (`background-surface`, `background-accent-subtle`, `background-selected`), and `th` / `.neo-table-header-button` keeps an opaque `background-surface-raised` bar (Rule 18).
- **Reader & classic/Cyberpunk coverage (`dev@c91d3f482a`):** All `31` variables have `>= 1` reader in `resources/css/src/table/{Body,Container,header/Button}.css` (`0` unread). `theme-light` and `theme-dark` declare all `3` files (`31` vars) in `neo.theme` above `neo.base`; `theme-cyberpunk` declares only `table/Container.css` (`1` var: `--neo-table-container-scrollbar-border-radius: 5px`) and sits in `PRE_ROLE_THEMES` (`check-theme-var-references.mjs:49`) until Step 10, so `theme-cyberpunk/table/{Container,Body}.css` re-declare the two non-role geometry literals (`--neo-table-container-cell-padding` and `--neo-table-cell-ismodified-size`) as `initial` (`--neo-table-header-button-background-image: none` already matches `background-image`'s initial value) to keep Cyberpunk's table paint unchanged until Step 10.

## The Fix

Create `resources/css/theme-base/table/{Body,Container,header/Button}.css` (`3` files) declaring all `31` variables once under `[class*="neo-theme-"]`, preserve `theme-cyberpunk`'s pre-Step-10 fallbacks in `resources/css/theme-cyberpunk/table/{Body,Container}.css` (`initial`), and delete `resources/css/theme-neo-light/table/**` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/table/**` (`6` files).

## Out of Scope

- Moving `theme-light`, `theme-dark`, or `theme-cyberpunk` `table` sheets onto roles (Step 10).
- Renaming example-owned custom properties in `examples/table/**` (Step 6 example-group row `examples/table`).

## Avoided Traps

- **Binding `--neo-table-cell-background-color-hover` to translucent `--neo-color-background-neutral-hover`:** `td.neo-locked` sits at `position: sticky; z-index: 10` (`src/table/Container.css:43-59`) and replaces `background-color` on `:hover`; a translucent hover leaks horizontally scrolled cell text underneath.
- **Keeping `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)` scrollbar thumbs in `theme-base`:** fails ADR 0044 I1 and renders dark-mode custom scrollbars nearly invisible against `#0e0f13`.
