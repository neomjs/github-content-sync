# theme-base: bind table's 3 files (31 variables) to roles

Owned by `@neo-gemini-julia` on the Engine `14.1` design-system critical path (`#22295` Step 6, row `table`); carries no contributor labels because active Step 6 implementation starts immediately in this session.

## Context

Step 6 row `table` of Epic #22295 ([Step 6 map](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295#issuecomment-7217835670)), filed and built directly under Epic #22295 Rules 1–19 and `ADR 0044` Decision 3, refined per operator review on `PR #22454` (`2026-11-28`) so row hover (`background-accent-subtle`), companion row/column selection (`background-selected`), and active cell selection (`background-selected-strong`) form an opaque three-grade accent ladder across all six selection models (matching `14.0.0`'s three-step `purple-900` → `purple-700` → `purple-600` hierarchy without sticky-column translucency or muddy neutral-hover induction).

## The Problem

Measured across `resources/css/theme-neo-light/table/**` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/table/**` on `dev@c91d3f482a` (`3` CSS files: `table/Body.css` `13` vars, `table/Container.css` `13` vars, `table/header/Button.css` `5` vars = `31` custom-property declarations per theme, comments stripped; positive control `--neo-table-cell-background-color-hover`):

1. **Duplication & retiring `--neo-sem-*` reads:** All `31` declarations are duplicated across `theme-neo-light` and `theme-neo-dark`; `20` declarations in `theme-neo-dark` still read retiring `--neo-sem-*` tokens (`--neo-sem-color-surface-neutral-hover`, `--neo-sem-color-surface-primary-default`, `--neo-sem-color-text-neutral-reversed`, etc.).
2. **Classic-light `#5d83a7` header holdover, zebra drift, sticky-hover translucency & two-role selection collapse:** `theme-neo-light/table/header/Button.css` still paints classic-light's `#5d83a7` slate-blue header bar with `#fff` text/glyphs and `rgb(28.85%, 41.31%, 53.11%)` ripple while `theme-neo-dark` uses `surface-neutral-highlighted`; `table/Container.css` paints `#f2f2f2` / `surface-neutral-highlighted` zebra stripes; `src/table/Body.css:31-35` (`&:hover .neo-table-cell`) has translucent hover in dark (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) that bleeds scrolled text through sticky `td.neo-locked` cells AND overwrites `.selected-column-cell` on hover; and having only two accent surface roles (`accent-subtle` and `selected`) forces opaque row hover to collide `1.00:1` with `CellRowModel` / `CellColumnModel` companion selection.
3. **Raw colour literals & invisible dark custom scrollbar:** `--neo-table-cell-ismodified-color` holds raw `orange` in both schemes, and all four custom scrollbar colours in `table/Container.css` hold raw black `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05..0.4)` in both light and dark (making the scrollbar thumb near-invisible at `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)` on dark's `#0e0f13` surface and violating ADR 0044 I1 and Rule 4's `3:1` thumb boundary floor).

## The Architectural Reality

- `Design authority:` `learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md` §2 decisions 3 & 4 (*"A state is a grade away... Every component variable is declared once, in theme-base, bound to roles"*), Epic #22295 Rules 5 & 15 (*"where no role fits, a role enters with the move that reads it"*), and `@tobiu` review direction on `PR #22454` (`2026-11-28`).
- **Three-Grade Accent Ladder (`resources/css/theme-neo/Global.css` + `buildScripts/util/check-theme-palette.mjs`):** `Table` and `Grid` paint three distinct opaque accent states over `background-surface` (`#f9fafb` · `#0e0f13`):
  1. **Row hover (`--neo-color-background-accent-subtle`, `accent-100` `#ecf2ff` · `accent-900` `#142457`):** `100%` opaque over sticky `td.neo-locked` cells, `17.07:1 · 13.20:1` with `text-default`.
  2. **Companion row/column & `RowModel` (`--neo-color-background-selected`, `accent-200` `#c4d6ff` · `accent-800` `#233c8f`):** `1.30:1 · 1.49:1` step above hover, `13.11:1 · 8.83:1` with `text-default`.
  3. **Active selected cell (`--neo-color-background-selected-strong`, `accent-300` `#9cb9ff` · `accent-700` `#304fb8`):** new role added in `theme-neo/Global.css`, guarded in `check-theme-palette.mjs` (`1.33:1 · 1.39:1` step above `background-selected`, floor `1.1:1`; `9.84:1 · 6.33:1` with `text-default`, floor `4.5:1`).
- **Selection-model hover guard (`resources/css/src/table/Body.css:31-35`):** Narrowing `&:hover .neo-table-cell` to `&:not(.neo-selected):hover .neo-table-cell:not(.neo-selected):not(.selected-column-cell)` preserves selected cells and `.selected-column-cell` crosshairs across all six `Neo.selection.table.*` models (`CellModel`, `ColumnModel`, `RowModel`, `CellColumnModel`, `CellRowModel`, `CellColumnRowModel`) with `0` classic or Cyberpunk `theme-equality` differences.
- **Reader & classic/Cyberpunk coverage:** All `31` variables have `>= 1` reader in `resources/css/src/table/{Body,Container,header/Button}.css` (`0` unread). `theme-light` and `theme-dark` declare all `3` files (`31` vars) in `neo.theme` above `neo.base` (`0` differences); `theme-cyberpunk` declares `table/Container.css` (`1` var: `--neo-table-container-scrollbar-border-radius: 5px`) plus `initial` on `--neo-table-container-cell-padding` and `--neo-table-cell-ismodified-size` in `theme-cyberpunk/table/{Body,Container}.css` (`0` differences).

## The Fix

Add `--neo-color-background-selected-strong` (`light-dark(var(--neo-palette-accent-300), var(--neo-palette-accent-700))`) and its two contrast pairs (`4.5:1` text, `1.1:1` step on `background-selected`), guard `src/table/Body.css` row hover with `:not(.neo-selected)` and `:not(.selected-column-cell)`, create `resources/css/theme-base/table/{Body,Container,header/Button}.css` (`3` files, `31` declarations), preserve `theme-cyberpunk`'s pre-Step-10 fallbacks (`initial`), and delete `resources/css/theme-neo-{light,dark}/table/**` (`6` files).

## Out of Scope

## Avoided Traps

- **Translucent `--neo-color-background-neutral-hover` (`rgb(255 255 255 / .05)`):** Replacing `background-color` turns sticky `td.neo-locked` columns see-through, and even when composited via `linear-gradient` over `#0e0f13` it paints `#191a1e` — a muddy, near-zero-chroma olive-grey next to Theme Neo's `267°–270°` slate/indigo palette.
- **Two-role accent collapse (`accent-subtle` hover + `accent-subtle` companion row):** Makes hovered rows `1.00:1` identical to `CellRowModel` and `CellColumnModel` companion selection; adding `--neo-color-background-selected-strong` (`accent-300` · `accent-700`) restores `14.0.0`'s three-grade accent hierarchy (`accent-100/900` → `accent-200/800` → `accent-300/700`).
- **Keeping `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)` scrollbar thumbs in `theme-base`:** fails ADR 0044 I1 and renders dark-mode custom scrollbars nearly invisible against `#0e0f13`.
