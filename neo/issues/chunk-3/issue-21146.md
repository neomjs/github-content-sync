# Covers for the remaining example groups, and one tile cover per group

## Context

A card without a cover is not shown (#21124), and the overview of #21140 shows one tile per group. Three leaves produce covers: #20679 (Grid & Table, with the capture script), #21120 (Components) and #21121 (Forms, Multi-Window, Calendar, Scheduler, Gantt). A leaf of #21140, for the Engine 14 cut on Mon 23 Nov.

## The Problem

- No leaf owns the covers of Tree, Tab Container, Containers & Layouts, Dashboard & Docking, Buttons/Menus/Toolbars, Lists & Galleries, Dialogs & Overlays, Charts/Canvas/Maps, State & Data, Functional Components and Workers & Platform. Their groups cannot publish.
- No group has a tile cover. In [the first look](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21130#issuecomment-6918809237) the tiles were crops of whole-window frames, and both readers named the result: "the thumbnails are texture, not proof", and "next to the app cards they look like toys". Light and dark frames were mixed.

## The Architectural Reality

- #20679's capture script: one recipe per record (route, state, region, device scale).
- The cover rule measured on #21121: text on a cover reads at 0.64 of its size or more; a slice reads as a widget, a whole example as an application; a cut label reads as broken.
- A group tile is about 213 px wide at 1440×900 and 171 px on a phone (the sketch of #21130).
- Most of these examples build on the configuration viewport; once its first slice is on dev, the stage is one capture rect for all of them.

## The Fix

1. **A recipe per record** of the groups above, added to #20679's script: the component in a telling state, at a scale where its text reads on a 445 px card.
2. **One tile cover per group:** the group's one feature, readable at 213 px, in the dark theme. Gantt bars with a dependency, a tree with open branches, a calendar with events.
3. The design seat reads each sheet before review.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every published record of the groups above has a cover made by a recipe in the script.
- [ ] Each of the bound groups has a tile cover, named by the group's definition.
- [ ] All tile covers are captured in one theme, and no tile shows an empty state or placeholder text.
- [ ] The design read of each sheet is on this ticket before the pull request asks for review.
- [ ] **Look:** the overview's first screen with the tile covers beside the first look's frame; two readers without session context grade the unlabelled pair.

## Out of Scope

The covers of #20679, #21120 and #21121; records; the examples' own content.

## Avoided Traps

- **Whole-window shots of the configuration viewport.** They are white pages with a small component.
- **Capturing before the workbench's first slice lands.** Every such cover would be re-shot. If that slice misses Thu 19 Nov, capture by region from today's viewport and say so.
