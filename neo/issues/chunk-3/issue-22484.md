# Every engine package makes its theme-base move

Terminal predicate: on `dev`, every engine package has made its `theme-base` move: its component variables are declared once under `resources/css/theme-base` and bound to roles, its two copies under `theme-neo-light` and `theme-neo-dark` are deleted, and a theme generated with `--base theme-neo` paints the widgets of every moved package.

Steward: the design seat (Clio; acting until Thu 3 Dec 21:00Z: Mnemosyne, who holds the assignment until then). This is step 6 of #22295, split out as its own parent.

## Problem scope

Step 6 of #22295 moves the engine's packages into `theme-base`, one pull request per package: 43 moves with rule 20's `global` row. Each row is a leaf of its own while it is in flight. On 28 Nov at 01:22Z #22295 held 26 subs, 11 of them rows, with five rows open and more than 30 not yet filed. Filed under that parent, the rows would take it past 50 subs.

A parent's subs share one outcome that can be checked. The rows share theirs, the predicate above. The other steps of #22295 end in different outcomes: the scope class, the old tokens and the two Neo folders, the short reference, the classic pair and Cyberpunk. So the rows get this parent, and #22295 keeps the steps.

Why an epic and no single ticket: 43 pull requests by several holders, each revertable by itself, gated by one set of rules.

## Intended solution

Nothing is decided here. This parent links what governs a move and copies none of it:

- **The rules:** "Rules every package move follows" in the body of #22295 (24 rules on 28 Nov), with ADR 0044 (`learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md`) behind them. A table that follows the rules is built without a read. For what no rule covers, the holder asks the design seat on the leaf, and the answer becomes a rule in that body.
- **A row:** its leaf carries the binding table as its body (rule 13). The holder is the leaf's assignee, and this parent's native sub-issue list is the registry of rows.
- **A move's gates:** the computed-style equality run with every moved value declared, the theme guards, a light and dark frame pair read against the released site (rule 4, #22448), and `WorkstationPaletteBridgeNL` green.
- **The 14.1 cut's gate** (#22295, "Open, each with its trigger"): a theme generated with `--base theme-neo` paints a hovered tab.

## Out of scope

- The steps of #22295 that follow the moves. They stay under #22295.
- The product palettes (#22426) and the Material pair (#22359).
- A new rule. Rules are written in the body of #22295.

## Avoided traps

- **A copy of the rules here.** Two bodies would drift; the rules changed five times on 27 and 28 Nov.
- **A sub list in this body.** The native sub-issue list is the registry.
- **Re-parenting the closed rows.** They stay under #22295 as its record. The open rows move here, and new rows are filed here.
