# theme-base: bind plugin's Popover and Resizable sheets (3 variables) to roles

Owned by @neo-gemini-julia (curated `good first issue` / `help wanted` omitted: active maintainer-owned 14.1 Step-6 package move). Sub-issue of #22484 (`theme-base` package moves, Step 6 of #22295).

## Context

Moves the `plugin` CSS package (`Popover.css` and `Resizable.css`, `3` variables total) into `resources/css/theme-base/plugin/` under #22484 / #22295 (`ADR 0044`). Until `plugin` moves out of `resources/css/theme-neo-{light,dark}/plugin/`, role-only themes (`#22359` / `PR #22438` Material pair and `create-theme --base theme-neo`) and `.neo-theme-scope` subtrees lack `--neo-popover-border-radius`, `--neo-popover-box-shadow`, and `--neo-resizable-color`, and Step 8 cannot retire the two legacy Neo folders.

## The Problem

- `resources/css/theme-neo-light/plugin/Popover.css` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/plugin/Popover.css` duplicate raw literals (`--neo-popover-box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgb(0 0 0 / 30%)`, `--neo-popover-border-radius: 4px`) instead of binding scale and elevation roles once in `resources/css/theme-base/plugin/Popover.css`.
- `resources/css/theme-neo-light/plugin/Resizable.css` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/plugin/Resizable.css` duplicate a raw hex literal (`--neo-resizable-color: #bbb`) instead of binding `--neo-color-text-muted` once in `resources/css/theme-base/plugin/Resizable.css`.
- `resources/css/theme-cyberpunk/plugin/Popover.css` declares `--neo-popover-border-radius: 4px` without `--neo-popover-box-shadow` (currently resolving to `none` in Cyberpunk), so adding `--neo-popover-box-shadow` in `neo.base` requires `--neo-popover-box-shadow: initial` in `theme-cyberpunk/plugin/Popover.css` to preserve zero Cyberpunk changes prior to Step 10.
- `test/playwright/component/apps/theme-equality/app.mjs` mounted `.neo-popover` without a `popover` attribute, leaving `src/plugin/Popover.css`'s `.neo-popover[popover]` rule unmatched during `theme-equality` runs, and `examples/popover/neo-config.json` omitted `neo-theme-neo-{dark,light}` despite `ConfigurationViewport` offering all four themes.

## The Architectural Reality

- `resources/css/src/plugin/` has three stylesheets:
  - `Popover.css` reads `--neo-popover-border-radius` and `--neo-popover-box-shadow` on `.neo-popover[popover]` (plus `--neo-global-background-color`, owned by #22444).
  - `Resizable.css` reads `--neo-resizable-color` on `.neo-resizable` FontAwesome drag/resize grip glyphs (`\f7a4`, `\f7a5`, `\f101`).
  - `PrefixField.css` reads zero custom properties and has no theme files.
- `resources/css/theme-light/plugin/{Popover,Resizable}.css` and `resources/css/theme-dark/plugin/{Popover,Resizable}.css` declare all three variables explicitly in `neo.theme` above `neo.base`.
- `Design authority:` `"Component variables are declared once, in theme-base"` (`learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md` Decision 4) and #22295 Rules 1, 3, 5, 13, 14, 17 & 18.

## The Fix & Binding Table (Rule 13)

Create `resources/css/theme-base/plugin/Popover.css` and `resources/css/theme-base/plugin/Resizable.css` on `[class*="neo-theme-"]`, add `--neo-popover-box-shadow: initial` to `resources/css/theme-cyberpunk/plugin/Popover.css`, delete `resources/css/theme-neo-{light,dark}/plugin/{Popover,Resizable}.css` (`4` files), add `popover: 'manual'` (`display: 'block'`) to `.neo-popover` in `test/playwright/component/apps/theme-equality/app.mjs`, and include `neo-theme-neo-{dark,light}` in `examples/popover/neo-config.json`.

## Out of Scope

- `resources/css/src/plugin/PrefixField.css` (reads zero theme variables) and `grid/header/plugin/Menu.css` (already in `theme-base/grid/header/plugin/Menu.css` via #22389).
- Migrating `theme-light`, `theme-dark`, or `theme-cyberpunk` `plugin/*.css` onto roles (Step 10 of #22295).

## Avoided Traps

- **Omitting `--neo-popover-box-shadow: initial` in `theme-cyberpunk/plugin/Popover.css`:** Cyberpunk's `Popover.css` only sets `--neo-popover-border-radius: 4px`, so without `initial` it would inherit `--neo-elevation-2` from `neo.base` before Cyberpunk defines roles in Step 10.
- **Unmatched `.neo-popover[popover]` in `theme-equality/app.mjs`:** Without `popover: 'manual'`, `.neo-popover` never matches `src/plugin/Popover.css`'s `.neo-popover[popover]` selector during computed-style equality runs.
- **Stale `dist/development/css` files after `git rm`:** Always clear `dist/development/css` before rebuilding themes for `theme-equality` (`defect-note` `df7f4c05`).
