# Bind Board’s three theme sheets to shared roles

## Context

A theme generated from `theme-neo` can render Board’s structure without its own component values. The Material package census leaves 14 unresolved Engine names from Board in each scheme. At `dev@0e9fa32f2c`, Board has 50 legacy properties with matching names in `Card.css`, `CardEditor.css`, and `Container.css` across the two Neo directories.

## The Problem

In the published 14.0.0 Board, the same WIP pigment paints the warning strip and the count badge’s background. Board also uses its page background as the Save and drag-stack foreground. Muting or remapping one palette value can lose the distinction between those states. A generated theme also needs the existing lane, card and editor values to resolve.

## The Architectural Reality

Design authority: “A role paints what its family owes.” The established package move in #22484 places component variables in `theme-base`. The Board package’s structural readers remain in `resources/css/src/board`. `Card`, `Column`, and `SortZone` carry the record and column accent inputs through rendering and drag. Workstation currently projects its own Board signal and panel values, which the move must preserve. [Binding rules](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295) · [Design record](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/0e9fa32f2c/learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md)

## The Fix

Move the three pairs to `resources/css/theme-base/board` and retire the six legacy Neo copies. Resolve the WIP strip with `border-warning`, its badge with `background-warning-subtle` and `text-warning`, and preserve the existing `--neo-board-wip-color` hook for its border readers. Give Save and the drag count the accent foreground role and the overdue glyph its inverse ink. Legacy overrides decline the three new defaults so their existing readers retain their colors. Preserve the Workstation signal and matching ink in its current bridge. The four coupled geometry lengths and the 6 px editor button radius remain measured literals.

## Contract Ledger

| Surface | Authority | Behavior | Fallback and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Board WIP, card, lane and editor styling | Board structural styles and the table above | Shared component values, separate warning strip and badge, existing role pairs | Real Board Basic, Card Editor, WIP Limits and Swimlanes in Neo and Material |
| Existing card and column accents | Card, Column, SortZone | Retain the actual record and column values, including detached drag rendering | Existing border and fill fallbacks where no record accent is provided |
| Classic, Cyberpunk and Workstation overrides | Current theme files and Workstation Viewport | Keep existing colors and palette ownership | Compare painted values and run the existing Workstation palette bridge |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] All 50 existing properties move into the three shared sheets. The 14 formerly unresolved Material Engine names resolve in both schemes, and no new unresolved Engine name appears.
- [ ] The published and candidate Board journeys show card selection, a real transition through WIP capacity, the populated editor and swimlanes. The candidate warning badge and Save foreground meet 4.5:1 in Neo and Material Light and Dark.
- [ ] Classic Light, Classic Dark and Cyberpunk keep their computed styles and stock Board appearance. The existing Workstation palette bridge passes in both skins and the pop-out.

## Evidence at intake

Chromium measured eight released Board cases and both Neo and Material proposals. The Board overlay reduced the Material count from 340 to 326 per scheme. WIP badge ink measures 6.20/7.45 in Neo and 4.86/5.49 in Material. Save measures 5.20/5.20 and 6.43/7.57. The three legacy schemes passed 120,528 equality comparisons with zero undeclared differences. Workstation’s four bridge cases passed.
