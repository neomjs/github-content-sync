# A Gantt example shows a project of 2,000 linked tasks

## Context

A leaf of #21346, the planning examples under #21140, and one of the three that the cut of Mon 23 Nov 2026 gets. The visitor's question: does the Gantt chart stay usable when the project has thousands of tasks?

No contributor labels: a maintainer builds it for the cut, and its first step is a profile whose result can change the scope.

## The Problem

`examples/gantt/basic` shows one phase with four tasks and two links. A real plan has hundreds to thousands of tasks, and scale is what a buyer checks first. The Gantt chart draws its task tree through the grid's buffered body; no example shows it under load.

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/gantt/Container.mjs`: `taskColumns_` (`:70`), `cascadeDependencies_` (`:36`), `dependencyCreation` (`:38`), `useWorkingTime_` (`:72`). The stores: `src/gantt/TaskStore.mjs` (a tree store) and `src/gantt/DependencyStore.mjs`.
- **Two facts from [the source check on #21346](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21346#issuecomment-6982869553):** the link layer indexes every task and is not windowed vertically (`src/gantt/DependencyLayer.mjs:412-486`); and a parent task shows its stored dates, nothing derives them from its children (`src/gantt/Container.mjs:16-17`). Nothing has profiled the chart at this size.
- `examples/gantt/basic/Viewport.mjs` (135 lines) is the template: a toolbar, the chart, its two stores. It is not edited.
- The catalog: `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json` (the record `gantt-basic` is the model); `publishes()` in `apps/portal/model/Example.mjs:133` drops a record without `image`.
- Specs for the family: `test/playwright/e2e/gantt/`.
- Design authority: n/a, a new example. Look, caption and cover follow the target in #21140's body.

## The Fix

1. **Profile first.** Put the data of step 2 into a scratch copy of the base example and measure first paint, expanding and collapsing a phase, a vertical scroll through all rows and a horizontal scroll. Post the numbers here. If it does not hold, file the engine ticket for the link layer with that reproducer and link it here as a blocker; the example then ships with the largest counts that do hold, or after the cut.
2. **A new folder `examples/gantt/bigData`:** a building project of 2,000 tasks in three levels (phases, work packages, tasks), with finish-to-start links inside a package and between packages, generated in the example from a fixed seed. The generator writes every parent's dates as the span of its children. The task count and the link count stand in the toolbar.
3. **It opens with the first phase expanded and the others collapsed,** so the first frame shows the tree, the bars and the links together.
4. **Editing stays on only if the profile shows that a task move with its cascade holds at this size.** Otherwise the example is read-only and says so; the pull request names which.
5. **One line says what to try,** for example "Expand a phase and scroll: the tree, the bars and the links keep up."
6. **A catalog record** `gantt-big-data` in the family `gantt`: a title that says what it shows ("A project of 2,000 tasks"), `feature` and `lookFor` in the form of its siblings, and a cover taken in the dark theme.

## Out of Scope

Engine changes: a defect the profile finds gets its own ticket, and so does deriving a parent's dates from its children. Working time, zoom levels and editing in the tree's cells: each is a candidate of its own in #21140. Loading from a server.

## Avoided Traps

- **Parent dates typed apart from their children.** The engine does not derive them, so a phase could end before its last task.
- **Counts the engine does not hold.** The numbers on screen are the ones that passed the profile.
- **A data file,** and **random data on each load:** the cover and the spec name first-frame content.
