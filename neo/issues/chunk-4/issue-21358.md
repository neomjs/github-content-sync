# The examples' platform groups show their proofs on the page

Terminal predicate: a first-time visitor opens Multi-Window, Workers & Platform, State & Data or Functional Components in the Portal's examples, and every card there proves its claim on the page itself: the page names what to try, the result shows without the console, nothing depends on a host the project does not run, and no card is the twin of another.

## Problem scope

These four groups carry what sets the engine apart: one app across several windows, work off the main thread, state that reaches every surface. The slate opened all 33 of their entries on `dev` `0128668721` at 1440×900 and 390×844 and used each by script ([this band's rows](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21142#issuecomment-6980292611)). What a visitor meets today:

- **The proof is not on the page.** Three entries answer their one action only in the console, `worker/task` and the functional button among them. The band's two strongest proofs, a canvas that keeps moving while the main thread is stopped and a dialog dragged between two windows, open as white pages that do not say what to try.
- **One API per page, not one question per card.** Eight `stateProvider/` entries each show one binding in the top third of an empty page; four of them are the same screen.
- **Hosts the project does not run.** The video example plays a clip from a third-party media host (#21312). The two canvas demos are old builds outside the repository, and one loads three third-party trackers. Colors had the same dependence and is the band's first delivered leaf (#21330).
- **Twins.** `multiWindowHelix` repeats the gallery's pop-out, `functional/templateComponent` is `nestedTemplateComponent` minus one button, `todoList/version1` is the same list from 2019.

**Why an epic:** nine renewals and six tiles across four groups, built by maintainers and by outside contributors, under one bar and in one order. The parent #21140 holds 33 subs and names "an epic to come" for this band.

## Intended solution shape

**The bar for a card of this band.** It answers one question a visitor brings ("does the UI stay alive under heavy work?", "where does state live?"). Its first screen names the one action to try. The result of that action is visible in the page, at 1440×900 and at 390×844. Its data is in the repository or generated in the example. Its record in `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json` says what it proves, in the visitor's words. A renewal rebuilds an existing folder; the band adds no example beyond the renewals.

**What each group shows at the cut.** The evidence per entry is in the rows; this is the target, not a list of tickets.

| Group | Cards | Tiles |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-Window | Kept: Workstation, the COVID app, the COVID gallery. Colors with its fallback (delivered). The shared dialog, renewed: dark, a dialog worth dragging, one line on what to do. Shown from other groups: Dock Perspectives, Dock Pane Pop-out, "One state, every surface", "Stop the main thread" | none |
| Workers & Platform | "Stop the main thread": the canvas demo rebuilt in the repository on the canvas worker. "Heavy work off the UI" from `worker/task`. The video move with a clip the project may show. "Ready before the click, and offline" from `preloadingAssets` on `Neo.worker.ServiceBase` | sitemap, unless More components takes it |
| State & Data | "One state, every surface" replaces `stateProvider/advanced` and absorbs the two-way, dialog and multi-window samples. The data pipeline with a payload that matters and a readout of where it was parsed. The todo list | none |
| Functional Components | none of its own; `apps/email` and the Live preview card join by `alsoIn` once they pass | define, host, nested template, button, nested components |

**An entry that gets no record stays in the repository while something loads it.** `Tables.md` loads `stateProvider/table`, an e2e spec loads `functional/nestedComponents`, the home loads `multiWindowHelix` until #21351 lands. "No record" is a catalog decision, never a folder deletion in the same change.

**Order.** For review by Fri 20 Nov: Colors (#21330, merged), the two functional examples without their test scaffolding, the shared dialog. Then "Stop the main thread", "One state, every surface", the todo list, heavy work, the pipeline, preload and offline. The video waits for #21312. What has not passed by the lock (Sun 22 Nov 18:00 CET) ships after the cut, as #21140 says.

**Who builds.** A leaf is filed when it is ready for a builder, and says for whom: the functional examples and the todo list are sized for an outside contributor; the worker, canvas and service-worker cards need a maintainer. Tiles publish through the recipe and the tile list of #21332 and #21333: a leaf of this epic fixes the example, not its tile.

**Open question, owner Mnemosyne** (investigation 3 of #21140): what the pipeline reports per stage, and whether an interceptor's input and output can be shown without engine work. It decides the scope of the pipeline card, and that leaf is filed after the answer.

## Out of scope

- The shell, Find, the tile recipe and the tile list (#21325, #21326, #21332, #21333), and every other band of #21140.
- The home page (#20682, #21351).
- An overview page per group: #20696 is closed as superseded, and #21140 asks that none be built.
- An example for `Neo.worker.HiddenTick`: a lead for a multi-window card after the cut.
- Removing a folder that a guide, a spec or the home still loads.

## Avoided traps

- **Cutting a part before listing who stands on it.** The slate's first verdict for Colors generated its data in the app; a blog post, a benefit page and the home stood on its WebSocket backend. The leaf became a fallback (#21330).
- **One card per API.** Eight state-provider pages each prove one binding to a reader who already knows the API. One card on real content answers the question a visitor has.
- **A proof that needs the console.** A reviewer with the dev tools open sees it; a visitor does not.
- **Nine leaves filed on one day into a red band.** A leaf without a builder is one more open issue. Each is filed when its builder, or its contributor-ready shape, exists.
