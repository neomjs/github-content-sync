# The desktop launcher and its apps tree move into src

## Context

Leaf of #21198. The demo's launcher (`apps/webdesktop/view/Launcher.mjs`, 190 lines), its tree (`model/App.mjs`, `store/Apps.mjs`) and the arrangement persistence in its controller (`readArrangement()`, `getArrangementKey()`, `onAppsRowsChange()`) are what any web desktop needs; only the `permission` field and filter are the demo's.

## The Problem

A consumer copies the launcher and loses its fixes. The persistence is tangled into the demo's sign-in: the arrangement must be restored before the new account's filter renders, and of two quick sign-ins the later one wins.

## The Architectural Reality

- Design authority: n/a — moves and generalizes existing behavior; calls none of it wrong.
- The launcher is a `list.Base` with `draggable.list.SortZone` (horizontal, within a group), keys Enter and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Left/Right, `dblclick` to open, and a single tap on touch devices (`Neo.config.hasTouchEvents`).
- `store/Apps.mjs` is a `TreeStore` sorted by `order`, with `arrange(arrangement)` and `getArrangement()`, and a per-instance copy of its catalog. Its `included` filter on `permission` is the demo's.
- Persistence today: `Neo.main.addon.LocalStorage`, keyed per account, written on `rowsChange` except while restoring.
- Styles: `resources/scss/src/apps/webdesktop/Launcher.scss`.

## The Fix

- `src/desktop/model/App.mjs` (`Neo.desktop.model.App`, `TreeModel`): `id`, `text`, `iconCls`, `color`, `description`, `order`, `handler`, `isHeader`. The demo subclasses it and adds `permission`.
- `src/desktop/store/Apps.mjs` (`Neo.desktop.store.Apps`, `TreeStore`): the `order` sorter, `arrange()`, `getArrangement()` and the catalog copy, with no filter. The demo subclasses it with its data and its permission filter.
- `src/desktop/Launcher.mjs` (`Neo.desktop.Launcher`, `list.Base`): icons, group headers, drag and keyboard reordering, an `openApp` event on Enter, double-click or a touch tap.
- Persistence behind `Launcher#arrangementKey`: `null` saves nothing; a key restores its arrangement and saves on each new order.
- `resources/scss/src/desktop/Launcher.scss` with the neo-dark and neo-light variables the demo has; the demo's launcher styles go. The other three themes are #21205.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: The three classes exist under `src/desktop/` with `@summary` JSDoc. The demo uses them through subclasses; `permission` and its filter exist only in the demo.
- [ ] AC-2: Unit specs pin: `arrange()` and `getArrangement()` round-trip; apps an arrangement omits keep their catalog place; a filter change leaves `order` untouched.
- [ ] AC-3: Unit specs pin persistence: a new `arrangementKey` restores before the launcher renders the store's next filter; of two quick key changes the later one wins; a failed read gives the catalog order; a restore saves nothing, and a reorder saves once.
- [ ] AC-4: The launcher's styles live under `resources/scss/src/desktop/` with the neo-dark and neo-light variables, and the demo keeps none of them.
- [ ] AC-5: The demo is unchanged for its user: `test/playwright/e2e/webdesktop/PhoneAndTouch.spec.mjs` and the demo's unit specs pass.
