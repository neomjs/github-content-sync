# One checkout form takes an address and a payment

Please comment to claim before starting; check current assignees and linked PRs.

## Context

#21140's content table, Forms & Fields, new for Engine 14: "＋ **Address and payment**: Country, ZipCode, Phone, Currency and IBAN in one checkout form." Lane epic #21329.

## The Problem

A visitor asks: do the fields know about countries, postal codes, phone numbers and bank accounts? No example answers it. Country, Currency, Phone and ZipCode appear only scattered over the form app's pages, and IBAN appears in no example or app.

## The Architectural Reality

- The field classes `src/form/field/Country.mjs`, `ZipCode.mjs`, `Phone.mjs`, `Currency.mjs` and `IBAN.mjs` exist, and their validation has unit coverage under `test/playwright/unit/form/field/` (`ZipCodeValidate`, `PhoneValidate`, `IBANValidate`, `Currency`).
- Which countries IBAN supports is decided in this leaf (Julia's source note on Discussion #20742).
- Design authority: n/a, because this is a new example and calls no existing behaviour wrong.

## The Fix

A new example under `examples/form/`: a checkout form with a shipping address (country, postal code, phone) and a payment (an amount in a currency, an IBAN). Validate as the visitor types, and have the postal code and the phone follow the chosen country where the fields support it. Add a record and a caption that names the question.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: All five field types are in one form, and each validates its input.
- [ ] AC-2: Where a field follows the country (postal code, phone), changing the country changes what it accepts.
- [ ] AC-3: The caption or the page says which countries the IBAN check covers.
- [ ] AC-4: The example works at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, in both themes, by keyboard.
- [ ] AC-5: A record with a caption, and frames at both sizes on the pull request.

## Out of Scope

- The sign-up form and the form app (sibling leaves under #21329).
