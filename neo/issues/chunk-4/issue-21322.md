# Charts, Canvas & Maps opens with its graph scene and its map

## Context

#21140's target (body of Wed 18 Nov) takes Charts, Canvas & Maps from 0 cards to 6 at the cut. The group publishes none today. The Component shelves rows on #21142 (comment 6980244557) opened and used every entry. Two entries stay as they are: the graph scene and the OpenStreetMaps map. Two records leave: `canvas`, which draws nothing by design, and `cesium-js`, which failed 3 runs of 3.

This leaf makes the group appear with its two keeps. The group has seven changed examples, so it splits in two, as #21140's order of work asks. The new demonstrations (Sparklines, Mermaid) and the renewed chart follow as their own leaf.

A leaf of #21140, cornerstone 1 of Engine 14 (`ROADMAP.md`), cut Mon 23 Nov. It is the Component shelves lane's first content, in review by Fri 20 Nov 12:00 CET.

## The Problem

- `examples.json` on `dev` `0128668721` holds five `charts` records: `canvas`, `graph-scene`, `cesium-js`, `open-street-maps` and `am-charts`. All five carry `image: null`, and `Portal.model.Example.publishes()` needs a cover, so the group shows nothing.
- `example-captures.json` declares only `grid-table`, `gantt` and `scheduler`. No recipe exists for `charts`.
- `canvas`: the stage stays empty; `examples/component/canvas/canvas.mjs` only logs that it loaded, and the record's own `lookFor` says the example "draws nothing on it yet".
- `cesium-js`: `Cesium is not defined` at `src/main/addon/CesiumJS.mjs:51` in 3 runs of 3, then the fly-to reads `camera` of undefined.

## The Architectural Reality

- Records: `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json`, one rule for a card in `Portal.model.Example.publishes()`, groups as data (#21141).
- Covers: `buildScripts/docs/captureExamples.mjs` reads recipes from `apps/portal/resources/data/example-captures.json` (`id`, `viewport`, `theme`, `state`, `ready`, `steps`, `after`, `region`) and writes bytes for `neomjs/pages` `resources_pub/website/examples/devmode/`, which `Portal.view.examples.List#imageBasePath` serves.
- Design authority: #21140 design 4 and 7. A demonstration gets a cover card, every cover is taken in the dark theme and cut tight on one feature, and it reads at 340 px.

## The Fix

1. `example-captures.json`: declare `charts`; one dark recipe for `graph-scene` (the clustered scene, its scale readable in the frame) and one for `open-street-maps` (the Iceland earthquake markers).
2. `examples.json`: an `image` for both; the `canvas` and `cesium-js` records leave the catalog. Their folders stay, with every spec that loads them.
3. A companion `neomjs/pages` pull request carries the two covers; both bodies list the checksums.
4. Every spec that pins published counts per group follows the new count.
