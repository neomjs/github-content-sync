# Forms & Fields, Calendar and the component tiles at the Engine 14 cut

Terminal predicate: on the released Portal, every workbench entry of the examples section shows as a tile of its own component, Forms & Fields opens with demonstrations that read as real forms above a tile for each of its field types, Calendar opens on today's team month at desktop and phone size, and the design seat's group-page read passes both groups.

## Problem scope

#21140's target (its body, 18 Nov) gives Forms & Fields four cards and its field types as tiles, Calendar one renewed card, and the whole section "80 cards and 61 tiles". The opened rows on #21142 ([Grace](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21142#issuecomment-6980130976), [Ada](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21142#issuecomment-6980411401)) found these on `dev` `0128668721`:

- 21 of the 23 Forms entries stand on the workbench, and each reads as an empty card.
- Ten field types have no entry: Country, Currency, Display, Hidden, IBAN, Password, Phone, Range, Search, ZipCode.
- The color list, the chip label and the form app's Save break in front of a visitor (#21324).
- The input masks hide under the name `fieldWithPrefix`.
- The form app fills a quarter of the frame, and on a phone its page list covers the form.
- The calendar opens on 21 July 2024 with placeholder events and has no phone layout.
- No workbench entry has a tile yet: there is no `kind`, no crop recipe and no tile list.

**Why an epic.** About eight pull requests across `neomjs/neo` and `neomjs/pages`, in a fixed order: the defects before the states template, the crop recipe before the tile records, the shell (#21325) before the tile list. They share `examples.json`, `example-captures.json` and `seo.json`, and serve one predicate. #21140's "Order of work" puts one ticket per pull request under a lane epic, as #21175 does for Grid & Table.

## Intended solution shape

- **Defects first** (#21324): a visitor must not meet a thrown list, a label over chips, or a Save that throws.
- **Tiles** (design 4 and 5 of #21140). One capture recipe crops each workbench stage's component by its own rectangle in the dark theme, and the bytes go to `neomjs/pages`. `Portal.model.Example` gains `kind: 'component'`, and a group page lists its component entries as tiles under the group definition's tile heading (`tileHeading`, which #21325 keeps on `forms`). A record becomes `kind: 'component'` in the pull request that gives it its crop. The seams today:
  - `buildScripts/docs/captureExamples.mjs:119` accepts only 640 × 360.
  - A `dark` recipe sets only the browser's colour scheme, while the workbench reads its theme from `theme` (`examples/ConfigurationViewport.mjs:411`).
  - `MainContainerController#publishGroups()` (`:96`) writes the group counts on every load and build switch, so per-kind counts belong there.
  - A free-size crop letterboxes unless the tile's surface is the stage's dark colour in both Portal themes.
- **The field states template.** One template shows a field in its states (empty, filled, invalid, disabled, read-only) on all 18 field workbenches, and the field types without an entry get one in the same template.
- **Calendar.** `calendar/basic` opens on today with a believable team month, names move and resize on screen, and gives the phone a day view. `calendar/weekview` leaves the catalog.
- **Demonstrations**, one pull request each with its look pair: the form app renewed, input masks under that name, a sign-up form that validates, and an address-and-payment form.
- **Every pull request** carries its first frame at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, dark. The design seat reads the group pages whole on `dev` on Sat 21 Nov.

## Out of scope

- The shell, Start here and Find (#21325, #21326).
- Other groups' renewals and cards, which are their lanes under #21140. Their un-renewed workbench entries do get tiles here.
- Calendar capabilities the event model lacks, such as recurrence and resources. The all-day row is #20883.
- The workbench's code view and its state in the URL (#21144).
- The email field's error layout (a defect-note of 18 Nov).

## Avoided traps

- **A cover card for a workbench entry.** It reads as an empty page (design 4).
- **A tile without its image.** It unpublishes the group (#21140's build notes).
- **One pull request per entry.** They would collide on `examples.json` and queue on few reviewers.
- **One states template written eighteen times,** once per field.
