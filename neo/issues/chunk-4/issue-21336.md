# The form app reads as a real multi-step form, and works on a phone

Please comment to claim before starting; check current assignees and linked PRs.

## Context

#21140's content table, Forms & Fields: "↻ Form App (`apps/form`: a real multi-step form, validated per step, with a phone layout)". It is one of the group's four demonstration cards. Lane epic #21329.

## The Problem

`apps/form` is the group's only complete form: 15 pages behind a page tree, *Validate page*, *Validate all pages* (the tree then marks each page), and *Save*. Its pages are named after field types ("CheckBoxes", "HiddenFields", "NumberFields"), so it reads as a test bench. At 1440 × 900 it fills the left quarter of the frame. At 390 × 844 the page tree covers the form. Save stores the values without any visible confirmation. (Save also throws today; #21324 fixes that first.)

## The Architectural Reality

- `apps/form/view/` holds the viewport, `FormContainer` with its controller, `FormPageContainer` and `pages/Page1.mjs` to `Page15.mjs`.
- Validation runs through `form.Container#validate()` and `isValid()`, and Save through `getSubmitValues()` (`src/form/Container.mjs:552`, `:321`, `:253`).
- `test/playwright/component/form/field/CheckBox.spec.mjs` references this app.
- Design authority: #21140's body (quoted above).

## The Fix

- Re-cut the pages into the steps of one real task, such as opening an account: personal details, address, preferences, review. Keep the field types it already shows, among them Currency, Hidden, Phone and ZipCode.
- Validate each step before *Next*.
- Fill the frame on a desktop, and fold the page list on a phone.
- Show the saved values after *Save*.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: The steps name a real task, and the field types the app shows today are still shown.
- [ ] AC-2: *Next* is blocked on a step with an invalid field, and the step says what is wrong.
- [ ] AC-3: At 1440 × 900 the form fills the frame; at 390 × 844 the page list folds away and the form is in view.
- [ ] AC-4: *Save* shows the submitted values to the visitor.
- [ ] AC-5: The CheckBox component spec still passes.
- [ ] AC-6: Frames at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, dark, before and after, are on the pull request.
