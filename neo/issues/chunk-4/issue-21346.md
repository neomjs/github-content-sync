# Scheduler, Gantt and Task Board show ten examples each

Terminal predicate: a first-time visitor opens the Scheduler, the Gantt or the Task Board group of the Portal's examples and finds ten demonstrations of that family, each answering one question a buyer of a planning component asks and each opening on a frame worth a cover; and every open leaf under this epic can be finished by a contributor from its own body.

## Problem scope

**The operator, 18 Nov 2026:** "task board, gantt charts, scheduler => gut feeling need 10+ examples each … it really relates to neo's inventory." On the structure, the same day: one epic for the three families, not one each, and tickets for the community "only if the scope is rock-solid". On 20 Nov he asked which new examples the groups get; they stood at two each.

**What exists (20 Nov).** Two examples per family: the base examples `examples/scheduler/basic`, `examples/gantt/basic` and `examples/board/basic`, built with the families themselves (#20874), and the cut's three: a year for 300 people (#21355), 2,000 tasks (#21356) and swimlanes (#21357).

**What the engine carries and no example shows** (config names; lines on each leaf, read on `dev` `8ed566ae7d`):

- `src/scheduler`, `src/timeline`: `barDraggable`, `resizableBars`, `editable` with an event editor, `minimumDuration`, `barRenderer`, `percentDoneField`, `resourceColumns`, `showNonWorkingTime`; on the time axis `nonWorkingDays`, `nonWorkingDates`, `snapMinutes`, `timeZone` and the zoom presets.
- `src/gantt`: `cascadeDependencies`, `dependencyCreation`, `useWorkingTime`, `taskColumns`, a task editor whose save cascades.
- `src/board`: `wipLimit`, `locked` and `collapsed` columns, `maxRenderedCards` with `overscan`, `columnsResortable`, card items, an inline title editor and a card editor, a selection model with ranges.

**Read against the source on 18 Nov 2026** ([Grace's check](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21346#issuecomment-6982869553)): 13 candidates are carried, 13 in part, 1 is missing. Its corrected wording is what the leaves build. Nothing was driven before a leaf was filed: the drive is each leaf's first step.

**Why an epic, and why one.** About 27 one-example leaves that share their rules: what makes a planning example, how its look is read, how its scope is made safe for a contributor. One body holds those rules once. This epic splits per family when it passes about 25 leaves; it holds 23.

## Intended solution shape

1. **The candidates are the rows Scheduler, Gantt and Task Board of the content table in #21140's body,** read off the config names above. A candidate is a lead.
2. **A leaf for contributors exists only for a driven candidate:** the capability was run on `dev` in the family's base example, and it works. A candidate that needs engine work becomes an engine ticket and waits there. **Leaves for maintainers are the exception** (the cut's three, and the nineteen of 20 Nov): they are filed on the source check, carry no contributor labels, and the drive is their first step.
3. **What makes a leaf rock-solid.** Every leaf fills the same frame, so that it can be finished from public context alone:
   - the visitor's question, in one sentence;
   - the config or class that answers it, with file and line;
   - the folder and the record's id, name and sentences;
   - the data: what the records are, how many, where they come from (shipped with the example, never a server);
   - the first frame in words: what is on screen at rest, and what the one named action shows;
   - the checks: no console error, 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, the keyboard where the capability has one, every theme of the base example;
   - the look: frames on the pull request, read cold by the reviewer's seat, with every affordance in its states and no control left in a default style.
   A leaf gets `good first issue` and `help wanted` only when every line of that frame is filled and its capability was driven.
4. **One example is one folder and one pull request, and that pull request edits no shared file.** The catalog's data, its two specs and the e2e selection list are edited by a publication ticket, once per wave (#21613 is the first), from the record each leaf carries as text. Reason: every examples pull request that edits those files makes the others stale (`#21539`), and three groups would get nineteen of them. A base example is the template; a new example does not edit it.
5. **Order.** An example ships with Engine 14 if it has merged by Sun 22 Nov 12:00 CET and #21613 publishes it before the lock at 18:00 CET. The cut waits for none of them. After the cut they land week by week, in the order of a buyer's questions: can people edit it by drag and in an editor, does it know working time, can it zoom, can a bar or a card be designed, does it update itself.
6. **Ten is the depth the operator named, not a quota.** A family that answers eight questions well does not get two fillers, and a candidate whose telling state would show an engine gap is held until the gap is closed.

## Out of scope

- The engine families and their theme files (#20874, #20879), and any capability the engine does not have yet: that is an engine ticket.
- A capability of a commercial package (the rule of #20498, until 1 Dec). Every candidate is a config of the public engine under `src/`.
- The renewal of the three base examples: rows of #21140, done with their group's catalog pass.
- Calendar depth: its event model has no recurrence and no resources (investigation 4 of #21140's roadmap).
- Loading from a server for these families: deferred in `ROADMAP.md`.

## Avoided traps

- **One epic per family today.** Three bodies with the same rules.
- **A contributor's leaf from a config name.** A contributor builds on a capability that turns out to need engine work, and the pull request cannot merge.
- **A curated label on a leaf with a blank in its frame.** The blank is where a contributor guesses.
- **A scale example that fetches.** Its data is generated in the example, with its count on screen.
- **A record in every example's pull request.** Nineteen pull requests would collide on three files at the merge gate.
- **An example about a capability with a visible gap.** It advertises the gap.
