# A Workstation cold-boot test hits the 30 s test timeout in hosted CI

## Context

`test/playwright/component/workstation/WorkstationBoot.spec.mjs:209`, "a cold boot retains the saved workspace and Reset brings its pane back", fails now and then in the components tier's third shard. It fails on pull requests that do not touch the Workstation, and it passes on a re-run of the same commit. Every sighting ends the same way: `Test timeout of 30000ms exceeded`, while the last step waits for the Feed pane after Reset.

| Sighting | Run | Diff | Re-run |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-08, #20585 at `13bf619871` | 42456080122 | not the Workstation | green |
| 2026-11-16, #21070 | 43780773980, job 133017283355 | adds `apps/webdesktop` and a catalog entry | requested |
| 2026-11-17, #21206 at `d506b174f5` | 44000812835, job 133764156006 | adds `src/board` and catalog data | green (attempt 2) |

Locally it passes 22 of 22 at 3.0–4.3 s per run: 3 on `a442f37f3e`, 3 on #21206's head, and 16 on `a442f37f3e` with 8 workers. Vega measured 13 of 13 at 2.3–2.4 s on the first sighting.

## The Problem

A red that clears on a re-run costs every author a diagnosis and a re-run, and it teaches the team to re-run reds unread. This one has done so three times in nine days. The log cannot say whether the pane came late or never came, because the test timeout cuts the last wait short.

## The Architectural Reality

- The test tears the Feed pane out into a popup, then polls until the persisted topology lists the vessel workspace under the Group's key (up to 30 s). It closes the popup and the root, cold-boots a new page with the same Group identity, and clicks "Reset to default". Reset is `apps/workstation/view/Workspace.mjs#resetTopology`, which `apps/workstation/view/TopologyToolbar.mjs` calls.
- The test's steps declare waits of up to 60 s (lines 83, 97, 98 and 245), but it runs under the component config's default 30 s test timeout. All three sightings were cut by that timeout during the last wait.

## The Fix

Discriminate first, then fix:

1. Read the failing run's artifact `test-results-components-44000812835-shard-3` (9 files, retained until 2026-12-01). Its error context holds the page snapshot at the timeout: a booted root without the Feed pane means Reset lost it; a root still booting means the runner was slow.
2. Slow: give the test a budget that covers its declared waits (`test.setTimeout`), so a loaded runner can finish. Lost: fix the Reset path for the ordering that drops the retained vessel workspace, and still align the budget.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The cause is named from the failing run's evidence: a slow runner, or Reset losing the pane.
- [ ] The test's own timeout covers the waits its steps declare.
- [ ] If Reset loses the pane, a test reproduces that ordering and the fix makes it pass.

## Out of Scope

Other component tests whose step waits exceed their test timeout, and runner capacity or sharding (#18916).
