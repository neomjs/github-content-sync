# Input masks get an example under their own name

Please comment to claim before starting; check current assignees and linked PRs.

## Context

#21140's content table, Forms & Fields: "↻ Input masks (`fieldWithPrefix`, under that name, both themes)". Lane epic #21329.

## The Problem

`examples/fieldWithPrefix` shows a strong capability under a name nobody searches for. Its five fields carry a phone number, a date, a MAC address, an alphanumeric code and a card number, and typed letters are refused where a mask wants digits. But:

- The stage is titled "Container · Neo.container.Base".
- It opens dark, unlike its sibling field entries.
- A label reads "MAC Adress".
- The phone pattern `+1 (___) ___-___-____` has 13 slots, while a US number has 10; typing 13 digits gives `+1 (491) 511-324-5678` with the valid mark.
- Nothing says what to type.

## The Architectural Reality

- `examples/fieldWithPrefix/MainContainer.mjs` puts `TextField`s with a `PrefixPlugin` (`pattern`, `slots`, `accept`) on the workbench. The inspector edits the first field's plugin.
- Design authority: #21140's body (quoted above).

## The Fix

- The entry and its record are named "Input masks". Its stage names it, and it opens in the theme of its siblings.
- Fix the label typo, and give the phone mask a real format.
- Add one line on what to type.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: The record and the stage say "Input masks".
- [ ] AC-2: The phone mask accepts exactly a real number's digits.
- [ ] AC-3: "MAC Address" is spelled right.
- [ ] AC-4: The page opens in the same theme as the other field entries, and works in both.
- [ ] AC-5: Frames at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, before and after, are on the pull request.

## Out of Scope

- The field states template and the other field entries (sibling leaves under #21329).
