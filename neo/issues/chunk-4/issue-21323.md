# The dock example's panes show an operations desk, not their names

## Context

A renewal row of the Dashboard & Docking group in the examples target ([#21140](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21140), its content table; the opened row on [#21142](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21142#issuecomment-6980130976)). The group's build is Grace's lane (A2A claim 13:39Z, 18 Nov). Under #21140's order of work, a rewrite is one pull request with its look pair on it.

## The Problem

`examples/dashboard/dock` (record `dock-layouts`) is the dock's playground: two declared perspectives (Operator, Review), Save Current and Delete Saved, a tab zone, splits, an edge region. Its perspectives, saving and tabs work, but each of its ten panes renders only its own name ("Strategy", "Swarm", "Terminal", "Logs", "Inspector", "Metrics", "Timeline", "Agents", "Alerts", "History"). At 1440 × 900 the first frame is four white rectangles with a word in each, and a perspective switch moves words around. A visitor cannot see why anyone would dock these panes, and a cover of it would show empty panes: #21316 holds this record's cover for that reason.

## The Architectural Reality

- `examples/dashboard/dock/MainContainer.mjs` (547 lines): the `panes` config (`:76`–`:85`) declares the ten panes; `strategy` is `ReloadablePane.mjs`, the other nine are plain components with `text`. The two perspectives (`:91`) share the pane ids `main-tabs`, `side-split`, `root-split` and the right edge region.
- The dock itself, `src/dashboard/dock/` (Workspace, projection, `PerspectiveLibrary`), is unchanged by this ticket.
- Specs that load this folder address panes by id: component `DockTabReorderGesture`, `DockSplitterPaint`; e2e `DragSensorDispatch`, `DockPinCollapseNL`, `DockAutoHideRevealNL`; unit `DockZoneModel`, `DockWorkspaceFirstProjection`, `ai/client/DockService`.
- Catalog record `dock-layouts` in `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json`.
- Design authority: #21140's content table, Dashboard & Docking: "↻ `dashboard/dock` (dark; panes with content, not their names)"; design 7: covers and crops in the dark theme.

## The Fix

1. **One desk, one data set.** The panes become views of one small agent operations desk on a generated data set held by the example's root state provider: a dozen agents with state and current task, their recent events, a few alerts, finished runs. Swarm and Agents list the agents, Metrics shows live counts, Logs and Terminal stream lines, Timeline the day's events, Alerts carries severities, History the finished runs, Strategy the plan, and Inspector the selected agent.
2. **A selection crosses panes.** Selecting an agent in Swarm shows it in Inspector, and the selection survives a tab drag to another zone and a perspective switch: the point of a dock is many views of one thing.
3. **Dark by default**, one line on the first frame that says what to try (drag a tab to another zone, switch perspectives).
4. Pane ids, the two perspectives and the saving stay as they are.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] At 1440 × 900 every pane in view shows content from the one data set; no pane shows only its name.
- [ ] Selecting an agent in Swarm shows it in Inspector before and after Inspector moves to another zone, and after a perspective switch.
- [ ] The ten pane ids and both perspectives are unchanged; the specs listed above pass (`npm run test-unit`, `npm run test-components` and `npm run test-e2e` on their paths).
- [ ] 390 × 844 shows a readable first frame of the default perspective.
- [ ] **Look:** the first frame at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, dark, beside today's frame on the pull request, read cold before code review.
- [ ] The record's `feature` and `lookFor` name the cross-pane selection; its cover is taken from the new state, or the pull request names the change that brings it.

## Out of Scope

The dock engine (`src/dashboard/dock/`); `dashboard/tearOut` and the sortable dashboard (their own tickets); `dashboard/choreography` and `dashboard/crossWindow` (kept as they are).

## Avoided Traps

- **Ten unrelated widgets.** One data set is what makes the panes one desk.
- **New pane ids or perspectives.** Eight spec files address them.
