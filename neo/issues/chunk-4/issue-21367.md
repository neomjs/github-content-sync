# The five component groups show their components working

Terminal predicate: a first-time visitor opens Buttons, Menus & Toolbars, Lists & Galleries, Dialogs & Overlays, Charts, Canvas & Maps or More components in the Portal's examples and sees each component work as its card says (every label drawn, focus where the page promises, nothing cut at 390 px, no card that opens on an error), with each group at the card count of #21140's target.

## Problem scope

These five groups hold 47 entries. On 18 Nov I opened and used each one; the rows are on #21142 (comment 6980244557), and their nine corrections are in the slate's body.

- **Six engine defects show on the cards themselves.** Each is captured as a defect-note:
  - a split button multiplies its menu (006590d3);
  - a click in a menu tree empties it (e71871ff);
  - the popover opens in the page's corner (0bd1ec00);
  - the amCharts pie draws no text, while its tooltip carries the data (a2375219);
  - Escape after a mouse open leaves focus on the body, though the dialog page promises to refocus the button (ed66cef6);
  - CesiumJS creates its viewer before its library has loaded (b408f593).
- **Some entries cannot stay:**
  - the canvas stage is empty by design;
  - CesiumJS failed 3 runs of 3;
  - Google Maps needs a key;
  - the four MWC wrappers load Google's 2021 web components from unpkg;
  - `button/effect`, `menu/panel` and `serverside/toolbarItems` are retired by the slate's corrections.
- **Some components have no example at all:** `Neo.component.Sparkline`, `Neo.component.wrapper.Mermaid` and `Neo.tooltip.Base`. No example reorders a list by drag (`Neo.draggable.list.SortZone`), and none builds an application toolbar.
- **Cards today against #21140's target:** Buttons 0 → 4, Lists 4 → 5–7, Dialogs 1 → 3, Charts 0 → 5, More components 1 → 3.

**Why an epic:** five groups, six engine fixes that each come before a renewal, and new examples, built in one order under one bar by maintainers and outside contributors. #21140, which holds 33 subs, names "an epic to come" for this band.

## Intended solution shape

| Group | Kept | Renewed (its defect fixed first where named) | New | Retired |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Buttons, Menus & Toolbars | `button/base`, `menu/list` | `button/split` and `menu/tree` (defects first), `menu/context`, `toolbar/breadcrumb` on a real hierarchy, `toolbar/paging` on #21189's simulated server | an application toolbar whose items arrive as JSON | `button/effect`, `menu/panel`, `serverside/toolbarItems` |

| Charts, Canvas & Maps | `component/graphScene`, OpenStreetMaps (#21322) | `charts` (its labels first) | Sparklines, Mermaid | canvas and CesiumJS for the cut (#21322), Google Maps |
| More components | none | `component/markdown` | none | the four MWC wrappers |

- **Order:** Charts, then Dialogs, Buttons, Lists, More components. Charts part 1 is in review (#21328). Part 2 brings the graph scene's cover, the pie's labels, Sparklines and Mermaid.
- **Engine defects:** each is a leaf of this epic, filed before its group's renewal, with the defect-note promoted on the leaf.
- **Covers:** a card publishes only with a cover taken after its content is drawn. A canvas scene needs a drawn signal on the DOM, as OpenStreetMaps got `data-rendered` in #21328.
- **Dates:** first content was in review before Fri 20 Nov 12:00 CET (#21328). The lock is Sun 22 Nov 18:00 CET; what misses it ships after the cut, as #21140 says.
- **Who builds:** Vega for the defects and the renewals. A leaf sized for an outside contributor says so: the tooltips and the application toolbar are candidates.

## Out of scope

- Component tiles and the tile recipe: #21329 (Ada), #21332, #21333.
- The examples shell (#21325) and the platform groups (#21358).

## Avoided traps

- Renewing a card before its engine defect is fixed: the card would demonstrate the defect.
- A cover taken before its content is drawn: the map in #21328 needed `data-rendered` before three runs agreed.
- Filing every leaf at once into a red band: a leaf is filed when it is ready for a builder.
