# The board's card editor keeps its contrast in cyberpunk and neo-light

Please comment to claim before starting; check current assignees and linked PRs.

## Context

Julia's approval of #21344 (the board in the cyberpunk, dark and light themes) named two contrast seams in the board's card editor. They sit outside that PR's tokens and were left as follow-up themes work. #21344 has merged; this ticket keeps the two seams.

## The Problem

1. **neo-light, the Save button.** Its label is `#f3f5f8` on `--neo-board-accent-color` `#0f9f8f` (`resources/css/theme-neo-light/board/Container.css:11`). That measures 3.02:1, under the 4.5:1 that WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 asks of normal-size text. The ratio is recomputed from the two hex values.
2. **cyberpunk, the editor's chrome.** In the editor frame of #21344 (`editor-cyberpunk.png`), the dialog header's icons (minimize, close) and the unfocused text fields' outlines read faint against `--cyber-bg-alt`. Their colours come from `Neo.dialog.header.Toolbar` and the form fields' cyberpunk theme files, not from the board's.

## The Architectural Reality

- Each component's theme file owns its tokens: `resources/css/theme-<theme>/board/Container.css` holds the board's `--neo-board-*`. The dialog header and the text fields read their own cyberpunk files under `resources/css/theme-cyberpunk/`.
- Design authority: WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3, Contrast (Minimum): 4.5:1 for text. SC 1.4.11, Non-text Contrast: 3:1 for the parts of a control that identify it, such as an icon or a field's boundary.

## The Fix

1. neo-light: darken the board's accent, or give the Save label its own colour, until the label reaches 4.5:1. Change the token in the board's theme file, so that every accent use stays consistent.
2. cyberpunk: give the dialog header's icon colour and the text field's unfocused border cyberpunk values that reach 3:1 against the surface behind them, in their own theme files.

To measure, open `examples/board/basic`, open a card's editor, and read the computed colours in the browser's dev tools. A contrast checker gives the ratio.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] In neo-light, the card editor's Save label measures at least 4.5:1 against its button.
- [ ] In cyberpunk, the editor's header icons and the unfocused field outlines measure at least 3:1 against their background.
- [ ] The pull request shows the editor before and after, in both themes.

## Out of Scope

- The cyberpunk selected-card fill, which #21344's drag read also noted: readers told the selected, dragged and dropped cards apart in every theme.
- The other themes, and any surface outside the card editor.
