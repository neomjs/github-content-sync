# A group page lists its component entries as tiles

## Context

Design 4 and 5 of #21140: "A *component* entry is one component in the workbench, and gets a compact tile … The record says which (`kind: 'component'`; a demonstration is the default)." A group page has "one header per kind when it holds both", with six tiles per row at 1440 × 900 (design 6). The Portal half stacks on the shell's pull request, #21325. Lane epic #21329.

## The Problem

A group page renders every record as a cover card. A workbench entry therefore reads as an empty page: Forms & Fields shows three white cards titled by class name.

## The Architectural Reality

- `Portal.model.Example#publishes()` is the one rule for a card: a cover, and a URL in the selected build.
- `GroupPage` renders the group's records through `List`.
- `MainContainerController#publishGroups()` (`apps/portal/view/examples/MainContainerController.mjs:96`) writes `count` and `crossCount` on each group on every load and build switch.
- #21325 keeps `tileHeading: 'Field types'` on the `forms` family definition.
- Design authority: #21140's body, design 4: "A tile needs no hand-made cover: one recipe captures every workbench stage's component by its own rectangle", and its build note: "A record becomes `kind: 'component'` in the pull request that gives it its crop, never before: a tile without an image would unpublish its group."

## The Fix

- `kind` on `Portal.model.Example`, defaulting to a demonstration.
- A tile list on the group page under the group's `tileHeading` (default "Components"); the headers show only when a group holds both kinds.
- Per-kind counts written by `publishGroups()`.
- A record for every workbench entry with `kind: 'component'` and its crop from the tile recipe as `image`.
- The Forms & Fields records #21140 marks ✕ leave the catalog: `form/field/picker`, `date/selectorContainer`, and `fields` once #21334's Range entry is on `dev`. Their folders stay (#21140: "the folder stays where a spec loads it").

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: A record with `kind: 'component'` renders as a tile showing its crop and its name; a demonstration still renders as a card.
- [ ] AC-2: A group holding both kinds shows "Demonstrations", then its tile heading; a group holding one kind shows no header.
- [ ] AC-3: A tile publishes under the same rule as a card, so it needs an image and a URL in the selected build. A group made only of tiles publishes once one tile passes.
- [ ] AC-4: The per-kind counts follow a build switch.
- [ ] AC-5: Six tiles per row at 1440 × 900, with frames of the Forms & Fields page at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844 on the pull request.
- [ ] AC-6: Unit specs pin `kind`, the publish rule for tiles and the counts.
- [ ] AC-7: `examples.json` holds no record for `form/field/picker`, `date/selectorContainer` or `fields`.
