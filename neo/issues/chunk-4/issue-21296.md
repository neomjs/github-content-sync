# The Task Board group publishes the renewed board with its cover

> **Re-cut 18 Nov, after #21140's list re-cut** (its body, "The list"). Timeline is no group: its example becomes a Grid & Table card, "a time axis in any grid", after #21315, in that lane. Kanban Board is now Task Board, with id and route unchanged. The board was renewed (#21297), so its old cover is void. Pull request #21301 is closed unmerged. The previous body is in this issue's edit history.

## Context

A leaf of #21140, for the Engine 14 cut on Mon 23 Nov. The Task Board group has `board/basic` as its only entry, and "Sprint Board" is one of Start here's twelve cards. A group appears with its first published record, and a record publishes only with a cover, so the group shows nothing until `board-basic` has one.

## The Problem

`board-basic` carries `image: null`. The recipe on #21301's branch frames the board before its renew: the table beside the board, which #21297 moved off the first desktop screen. #21301 also adds a `/examples/timeline` route and group, which the re-cut removes.

## The Architectural Reality

- `buildScripts/docs/captureExamples.mjs` captures the families that `apps/portal/resources/data/example-captures.json` declares (640 × 360 CSS px at device scale 2, `devmode/<id>-14.jpg`). The bytes go to `neomjs/pages`.
- #21325 (Julia's shell) re-cuts the group list, the titles and `seo.json`. This leaf lands on it.
- Design authority: #21140's body, design 7: "Every cover and crop is taken in the dark theme, and a title says what the example shows, never a class name."

## The Fix

1. A recipe for `board-basic` on the renewed board, dark, framing the columns and their cards. `board` joins the manifest's families.
2. The record names its cover, and the bytes go to `neomjs/pages` first.
3. `seo.json` is regenerated on top of #21325.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The Task Board group page shows the board's card with its cover, and Start here's Sprint Board card shows the same cover.
- [ ] The cover shows the renewed board's five columns and their cards, dark, and obeys #20679's frame rule.
- [ ] One script run reproduces the cover, and its checksum matches the bytes in the linked `neomjs/pages` pull request.
- [ ] `seo.json` passes `buildScripts/docs/seo/verify.mjs`, and the section's unit and e2e specs pass.
- [ ] **Look:** the pull request shows the cover at card width and the Task Board page, at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844.

## Out of Scope

The timeline's cover and record (Grid & Table, after #21315). The planning families' new demonstrations (#21140's Scheduler, Gantt and Task Board rows). The shell (#21325).
