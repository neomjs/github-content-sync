# The Kanban Board and Timeline groups publish with their covers

## Context

Split from #21146 by its intake ([comment](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21146#issuecomment-6967931120)). A leaf of #21140, for the Engine 14 cut on Mon 23 Nov.

On `dev` at `74238ddcc5` (dev server, Production build), the examples overview reads "43 live examples in 16 groups", and neither the Kanban Board nor the Timeline is among them. They are two of the four families Engine 14 adds (`ROADMAP.md`, cornerstone 5). On Fri 20 Nov the section is read as a whole, by two readers without session context and then by the operator.

## The Problem

- A group appears while one of its records publishes a card, and a record publishes only with a cover (#21124). `board-basic` and `timeline-basic` are their groups' only records, and both carry `image: null`.
- No cover leaf names them: #20679 (Grid & Table), #21120 (Components), #21121 and #21246 (Forms, Multi-Window, Calendar, Scheduler, Gantt). Only #21146's tile criterion reaches them, and that ticket waits on the slate's rows for its eleven groups.
- `timeline-basic` is ready: #21248 fixed its zero-height bars on 17 Nov and left the cover. `board-basic` is renewed by the slate (#21142, renew row 2); Grace files that leaf, and its pull request re-shoots this cover.

## The Architectural Reality

- `buildScripts/docs/captureExamples.mjs` captures the families that `apps/portal/resources/data/example-captures.json` declares: a 640×360 CSS-pixel region at device scale 2, a JPEG of at most 150 kB, written as `devmode/<id>-14.jpg`. Each covered record of a declared family needs a recipe.
- The script captures whatever `dist/development/css` holds, so the themes are rebuilt before a run (Vega's defect-note of 18 Nov).
- A recipe's `theme` sets the browser's color scheme, which only the Portal reads. An example opens in the first theme its `neo-config.json` lists: board and timeline open in `neo-theme-neo-light`.
- `Portal.view.examples.MainContainer#familyDefinitions`: `timeline` names `tile: 'timeline-basic'`. `board` names neither a `tile` nor a `sentence`, so its card would carry the title alone.
- `MainContainerController#publishGroups` takes a group's image from its `tile` record, else from its first card.
- `apps/portal/resources/data/seo.json` gains a route only when regenerated (`buildScripts/docs/seo/`), as #21247 did for Gantt and Scheduler.
- Design authority: #20679's frame rule (the example's own surface, legible at card width, no browser or OS chrome) and each record's `feature` line.

## The Fix

1. Recipes for `timeline-basic` and `board-basic`; `timeline` and `board` join the manifest's families.
2. Both records name their covers. The bytes go to `neomjs/pages` under `resources_pub/website/examples/devmode/`.
3. `board` names `tile: 'board-basic'` and a sentence.
4. `seo.json` is regenerated, and the section's specs (`MainContainerController.spec.mjs`, `ExamplesCatalogNL.spec.mjs`) list the two groups and the new totals.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The overview on the branch lists Kanban Board and Timeline with their covers, and its headline counts two more examples and two more groups than its base.
- [ ] `timeline-basic`'s cover shows the teams with their interval bars at row height. `board-basic`'s shows the board's cards and the table over the same store, as its `feature` line says. Both obey #20679's frame rule.
- [ ] One script run reproduces both covers, and the run's checksums match the bytes in the linked `neomjs/pages` pull request.
- [ ] `seo.json` carries `/examples/board` and `/examples/timeline`, and `buildScripts/docs/seo/verify.mjs` passes.
- [ ] The section's unit and e2e specs pass with the two groups.
- [ ] **Look:** the pull request shows both covers at card width and the overview's first screen with the two groups. The design seat reads it before merge. *(Amended 18 Nov, 04:28Z, from "before review": the design seat wakes after 10:40Z, so the code review runs beside the read, and Friday's read keeps both.)*

## Out of Scope

The board's renew (slate row 2), whose pull request re-shoots `board-basic`. The covers of other groups (#21121, #21146). Captions beyond `board`'s new sentence.

## Avoided Traps

- **Waiting for the board's renew:** the Kanban Board group would miss Friday's read, and a recipe re-run costs minutes.
- **A dark capture of an example that opens light:** the cover would promise a look the visitor does not get.
