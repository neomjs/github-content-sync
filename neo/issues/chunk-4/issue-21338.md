# A sign-up form validates as you type and lists what is wrong

Please comment to claim before starting; check current assignees and linked PRs.

## Context

#21140's content table, Forms & Fields, new for Engine 14: "＋ **Validation**: a sign-up form that validates as you type, blocks submit and lists what is wrong." Lane epic #21329.

## The Problem

A visitor asks: how does a Neo form tell the user what is wrong, and when? No example answers it. Form-level validation appears only as the form app's page marks, and no entry shows a submit being blocked and its errors listed.

## The Architectural Reality

- `form.Container` has `validate()` (`src/form/Container.mjs:552`), `isValid()` (`:321`), `getSubmitValues()` (`:253`) and `reset()` (`:358`).
- Fields carry `required` and their own checks: the email field's address rule, and `minLength` and `maxLength` on text fields.
- Design authority: n/a, because this is a new example and calls no existing behaviour wrong.

## The Fix

A new example under `examples/form/`: a sign-up form (name, email, password and its repeat, a terms checkbox). It validates each field as the visitor types, blocks *Sign up* while anything is invalid, lists the problems beside the button, and shows the submitted values when it passes. Add a record and a caption that names the question.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: Each field shows its error as the visitor types or leaves it.
- [ ] AC-2: *Sign up* stays blocked while a field is invalid, and the form lists what is wrong.
- [ ] AC-3: A valid form shows its submitted values.
- [ ] AC-4: The example works at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, in both themes, by keyboard.
- [ ] AC-5: A record with a caption, and frames at both sizes on the pull request.

## Out of Scope

- The address-and-payment form and the form app (sibling leaves under #21329).
