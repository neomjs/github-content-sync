# A torn-out center pane empties its window and comes home to a side band

Owner: Vega, after #21363. Not curated for contributors: the empty-center behavior needs a design read before a fix is built.

## Context

#21363 adds a "the panes refill their window" check to the tear-out matrix (`test/playwright/e2e/dashboard/TearOutMatrixRows4To7NL.spec.mjs`). Measured headed on 2026-11-18 (macOS, real Chrome, 1280 × 720, Demo B at `examples/dashboard/crossWindow`), tearing out Workbench, the pane that fills the center:

- **After the drop:** the main window's dock panes cover 280 × 59 at (8, 118). Before the gesture they covered 1264 × 594. The Inspector / Timeline / Console band sits in the window's top-left corner, and the rest of the window is empty.
- **After the vessel closes and the pane returns:** 280 × 117 in the same corner. The document reads `root: edge-zone {right: side-tabs}` with `side-tabs.items: [inspector, timeline, console, workbench]`: Workbench came home into the right band, and no center exists.

The existing row-7 checks (`homes: 1`, `rendered: 1`) pass on both states.

## The Problem

Two defects in one journey, tearing out the main pane and bringing it back:

1. **The window empties.** `LayoutAdapter.projectEdgeZoneNode` (`src/dashboard/dock/projection/LayoutAdapter.mjs:900-978`) pushes a sole middle item as a row of the edge-zone's vbox (`middleItems.length === 1` → `rows.push(middleItems[0])`). A right band without a center is then a 280 px band of natural height in the vbox's top-left, away from its edge and filling nothing.
2. **The pane comes home to the wrong place.** The detach removes the emptied center node (`workbench-tabs`), and the return appends the pane to the existing right band instead of rebuilding the center.

## The Architectural Reality

Design authority: `WorkspaceDocument.captureNodeHome` (`src/dashboard/dock/model/WorkspaceDocument.mjs:516`): "Captures a node's own position well enough to REBUILD it, not merely to find it." Its JSDoc adds: "An edge-zone parent needs no anchor: an edge-zone node survives losing every zone, so its id and the zone key remain resolvable." The center slot of `root` is resolvable at return, so the return should rebuild the center node there. For the empty window, `LayoutAdapter.projectEdgeBand` gives a band "a fixed cross-extent … instead of flexing against the center"; no record says what a window shows while it has no center, which is the design fork in The Fix.

- Placement capture at detach: `Workspace.mjs:1281` (`WorkspaceDocument.captureItemPlacement`); the fold on return: `Operations.foldPlacements` (`src/dashboard/dock/model/Operations.mjs:305`) with the host's `resolveDockReturnDescriptor`.
- ADR 0029 §2.7, reintegration row: the item re-enters via its `fallbackTarget` hint.

## The Fix

1. The return rebuilds the center node from its captured home (`{parentId: 'root', slot: 'center'}`) when the original node is gone.
2. A window without a center keeps its bands at their edges. The fork for the design read: (a) an empty center well (flex 1, a drop target) while the bands stay docked; (b) the sole band expands to fill the window. Recommendation: (a), the way IDE docking keeps its document area.
