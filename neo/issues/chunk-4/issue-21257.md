# The Portal's Medium tab lists the part 3 story of the memory series

## Context

The operator published part 3 of the memory series on Medium on 2026-11-17, supplied its share link in the session that files this, and asked for the Portal's Medium list to be updated: "we need to update the JSON content file."

## The Problem

The Medium tab of the Portal's News section reads `apps/portal/resources/data/medium_blog.json`. Its newest entry is id 78 (Nov 10, 2026). Parts 1 and 2 of the series are entries 76 and 77, and part 3 is missing, so a visitor of the tab does not find the newest story.

## The Architectural Reality

- `apps/portal/store/BlogMedium.mjs:39` loads the file. Entries are newest first, each with `author`, `authorImage`, `date` (`Mon DD, YYYY`), `id`, `image`, `name`, `provider`, `publisher`, `selectedInto`, `type` and `url`.
- `apps/portal/view/news/medium/List.mjs:240` builds the card image as `<imageBasePath>/blog/<record.image>`, and `imageBasePath` points at `neomjs/pages` `main` (`resources_pub/website`).
- The cover is on `neomjs/pages` `main` since neomjs/pages#94: `blog/codebase-knowledge-base-for-ai-coding-agents/rag-vs-grep-40-questions.png`, the bar chart that opens `learn/blog/codebase-knowledge-base-for-ai-coding-agents.md`.
- Read on Medium at 2026-11-17T12:5xZ: the title is "Codebase RAG vs grep for AI coding agents: the same grep hits scored 10, 30 or 34 of 40", published at 12:48Z on the author's account. It is in no publication yet: its itnext.io address redirects back to tobiasuhlig.medium.com, and the page names no publication.
- Design authority: n/a. This calls no existing behavior wrong; the list is data that was not kept up.

## The Fix

Add entry 79 at the top of the file in the existing shape: `date` "Nov 17, 2026", `image` the cover path above, `name` the story's title, `publisher` "" (as entry 78 had before ITNEXT took it, #20737), `url` the share link the operator supplied, and `author` / `authorImage` following the Medium account, as entries 74 to 78 do.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: the file parses and holds 79 entries; entry 79 comes first with every field of the existing shape, and entries 1 to 78 are unchanged.
- [ ] AC-2: entry 79's title and date match the story on Medium, and its `url` opens the story.
- [ ] AC-3: the card's image loads on the Medium tab, and the receipt shows the card at desktop and at phone width, with what the crop leaves of the cover.

## Out of Scope

- Moving the entry to ITNEXT if the publication takes the story later: an update like #20737.
- Whom a card names as author when an AI maintainer wrote the story (#20733's Out of Scope stands).
- A generator or sync for this file.
