# Every open Discussion gets a read and a disposition

## Context

The open set is the ideas backlog. Right now nobody can tell a live design question from a shipped one whose thread was never closed. The prioritizing the operator asked for needs that split first.

## The rules

These are adapted from neomjs/neo#15000 (operator rules, 2026-10-17, comment 6244102341) and the Ideation Sandbox closure audit (`.agents/skills/ideation-sandbox/audits/discussion-lifecycle-closure.md`):

- **Read by domain**, by the peer who knows the substrate. Age is not a staleness signal, and nothing is bulk-closed by age.
- **The marker is never the state.** `RESOLVED_TO_AC` in a body can be a graduation criterion, a prerequisite or a resolution (neomjs/neo#13808). Read the thread, then check what its cited tickets actually shipped.
- **Each verdict is a dated comment on the Discussion** and ends one of five ways:
  - **close · resolved**: it shipped, or every Open Question has a terminal disposition. Cite the tickets or PRs.
  - **close · outdated**: a later decision, ADR or code reality overtook it. Cite that.
  - **park**: valid, but not now. The Discussion stays open, and the comment names the reason and the trigger that brings it back.
  - **holds**: live and worth driving. Name the line it serves (Engine 13.3, Fleet Manager v1, Brain Golden Path v2) or say "no line".
  - **graduate**: rare, and only through the consensus gate (`ideation-sandbox-workflow.md` §6). A sweep never graduates by default.
- **Authorship holds.** An author closes their own Discussion. A reader proposes the verdict in a comment, and the author acts on it. Operator-authored Discussions (`op`) are the operator's call.

## The instrument

- **This body.** Verdicts live on the Discussions, never as comments here.
- **Claiming:** claim a slice, or part of one, with one comment. Tick a box once its Discussion carries the verdict, and append the verdict word.
- **`lead:` tags** are mechanical. They come from a grep of the `github-content-sync` corpus (`bd8428ef`, 2026-11-05 10:53Z) for issues and PRs citing each Discussion. **A lead is not a verdict:**
  - "every graduation-cite closed" means "check whether it shipped";
  - "no comments, no citing ticket" means "check whether anyone still needs it".
- **Author tags:** `op` is the operator, `external` is an outside contributor, and any other name is a maintainer.
- **Closing:** a seat in Claude Code auto mode can't close a Discussion yet. The raw GraphQL close is denied as an external write, and `manage_discussion` has no close action; neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1488 adds one. Until then, a ticked `close` verdict stays open unless its author closes it in the UI.
