# Undo of a popup's native close reopens the window but not the pane

## Context

`WorkstationNL.spec.mjs:2604` ("pop-out uses the Group cursor to return and replay the same live pane") is in #19657's intermittent set. It fails at line 2704 with the same assertion at both screens: "undo reopens the pane". Dev runs: [38306161797](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/actions/runs/38306161797) at 1920 and [38309244286](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/actions/runs/38309244286) at 2560.

Observed, on hosted probes from an unmerged branch:
- [Run 38311600237](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/actions/runs/38311600237): alone 2/4, within shard 8 2/2. When red, the pane never appears, not even after 30 s. On a green run the pane shows about 1.1 s after the undo.
- [Run 38313928256](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/actions/runs/38313928256): alone 5/6. At the failure:
  - The pane `neo-component-16` reads `mounted: false`. Its parents `neo-container-7` and `neo-tab-container-2` are mounted, in window `63426de9…`, which is not the reopened popup.
  - `#neo-component-16` is in neither the popup's DOM nor the main page's DOM.
  - The popup (`?workspace=workstation-vessel:commits`, host `neo-dock-workspace-2`, `disconnected: false`) renders its tab container and the `neo-dock-stack-handle-commits` handle, but no pane.
  - The Group document is the popup-owned one: the spec's `toEqual(detached)` at 2702 passed.
  - `readNativeLifecycle(...).owners` is `{}`, and `lastVesselOpen.stage` is `granted`.
- In the dev log of the red run, the reopened window connected 0.66 s after the undo. Then nothing reached the pane for the next 4.4 s.

Unknown: whether `63426de9…` is the main window, and the order in which the main and popup participants project during this undo.

## The Problem

After a native close returned the pane to main (#19273), Group undo restores the popup-owned document and re-embodies the window. Sometimes the live pane instance stays under its old tab card, unmounted, and never moves into the popup's stack. The user sees an empty popup with a tab handle, and the pane is gone from both windows. Redo and further undo then work against a pane that is in neither place.

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/dashboard/dock/window/NativeVesselTransaction.mjs` `replayWorkspaceReturn`: the undo calls `embodyWorkspace` (reserve, then `Neo.Main.windowOpen`) before `project()`.
- `src/dashboard/dock/Workspace.mjs` `projectDockCommit` → `projectDockZoneDocument`: each participant projects its own document. `preserveItemIds` keeps panes that sibling documents own.
- `apps/workstation/view/WorkspaceController.mjs` `onWindowConnect` → `mountTopologyWorkspace`: adopts the window when it connects.
- Inference, not yet verified: the main participant's projection and the popup participant's projection or adoption can land in either order. In one order, the shared pane stays under main's card.

Design authority: `NativeVesselTransaction.replayWorkspaceReturn` — "Re-embodies undo before projection and closes redo after projection settles". The e2e contract at `WorkstationNL.spec.mjs:2704` asserts the same: "undo reopens the pane".

## The Fix

Trace the order of the two participants' projections and the window adoption in this undo. Then make the pane's move into the reopened popup independent of which lands first. The test's 5 s budget is not the defect: the pane never arrives.
