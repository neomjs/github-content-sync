# A child update merged into a parent flight is dropped when their windowIds differ

## Context

Found while diagnosing why #19917's RealWorld v1 fix sometimes left the *Popular Tags* sidebar empty although `/api/tags` answered 200: 2 of about 16 natural runs, and 6 of 12 under a later diagnosis harness. A read-only diagnosis of the interleaving traced it to the engine; the app supplies the trigger.

## The Problem

A child's update can merge into a parent flight that will never render it, and the child is still acknowledged as done:

1. The parent (`HomeComponent`) is mounted, gets new data, and starts `executeVdomUpdate`. It sets `isVdomUpdating` and yields at `src/mixin/VdomLifecycle.mjs:274` (`setTimeout(1)`); `needsVdomUpdate` stays true until the payload is built (`:610`).
2. Inside that yield, the child (the tag list) updates. `mergeIntoParentUpdate` (`:965-966`) sees the parent's `needsVdomUpdate` and registers the child as merged, sets the child's own flag, and returns (`:1323-1325`).
3. `collectScopes(parent)` marks the child collected and claims its promises (`:321-322`), then recurses. `collectScopes(child)` returns at `:298` because the child's `windowId` (`null`) differs from the parent's, so the child gets no scope and no payload, and its flag stays set.
4. The parent's depth-1 payload reduces the mounted child to a `neoIgnore` placeholder (`src/util/vdom/TreeBuilder.mjs:74-80`).
5. `resolveVdomUpdate` settles the child's claimed callbacks (`src/manager/VDomUpdate.mjs:224-226`). A `promiseUpdate()` on the child resolves while its vnode still holds the old content.

End state: the child has `needsVdomUpdate` set, an old vnode, a newer vdom, and no merged, post-update or in-flight entry left to run it. Nothing re-runs it until something calls `update()` again. The change is silently lost and reported as done.

**The trigger:** RealWorld v1's `HomeComponent` creates `TagListComponent` in `onConstructed` with only `parentId`. Containers forward `windowId` only to their `items` (`src/container/Base.mjs:319-333`, `:393`), so the tag list keeps `windowId: null`. On `dev` the inline vdom embed (#19917) hides the drop, since the tag list rides the parent's payload as plain vdom.

## The Architectural Reality

- Merging and collection disagree about eligibility. The merge (`:965`) does not check the window, and collection (`:298`) refuses on it after the merge has already marked and claimed the child.
- The engine's own components forward or pass `windowId` to the children they create: `grid.Row` (#18291), `src/functional/component/Base.mjs:137-143`, the Time trigger, the grid and table header buttons, the Chip field and the calendar week view.

## The Fix

1. Engine: in `mergeIntoParentUpdate` (`:965`), merge only when `parent.windowId === me.windowId`. A mismatched child then runs its own flight, at the cost of one extra worker round trip. The diagnosis ran 2,804 engine-relevant unit specs green both with and without this guard.
2. Name the remaining gap: a `windowId` that changes after a merge was accepted would still be dropped at `:298`. Closing that means collection never marks or claims a merged child it will skip, and hands it back as its own flight. That touches what `VdomLifecycle.spec.mjs` ~961 pins, so it is decided in this ticket's PR or split out with a reason.
3. App (#19917): `HomeComponent` forwards its `windowId` to the tag list and passes `windowId`/`appName` when it creates the previews.
