# A tab cannot tear out of the popup that was opened for it

## Context

Found while fixing #19990 (Workstation; headless Chrome through the Neural Link fixture). Pop Metrics out (popup `workstation-vessel:metrics`), move Audit into it, then drag Metrics out of the popup over empty desktop.

## The Problem

Nothing tears out. No vessel is admitted (`tearOutHandlers.activeVessel` stays `null`), no window opens, and the release leaves Metrics in the popup. Audit, in the same popup, tears out every time (#19990's e2e). So #19990's AC-3, *"tears it out into a new popup every time"*, holds for every tab except the one its popup was opened for.

Inferred from the same derivation, not run: main refuses too, for an item dragged back into main while its old popup keeps other tabs and then torn out of main again.

## The Architectural Reality

A vessel opened for `metrics` is identified by `metrics` in three places (intake, comment 6346317829):
- **Its Group key.** `Workstation.view.VesselWorkspace.vesselWorkspaceId(itemId)` gives `workstation-vessel:<itemId>`, which `tearOutWorkspaceKey` hands the native reservation. `Neo.manager.Transaction#reserve` returns `null` for a key whose binding has a `windowId`, so `NativeLifecycle#acquire` refuses and the gesture degrades in-window (ADR 0029 §2.8.3). This is the wall a drag hits today.
- **The native registry.** `NativeLifecycle` keys admissions, connections and owners by item id, and the popup holding Audit keeps its records under `metrics`.
- **Its window name**, `tearout-<itemId>`. `Main#windowOpen` reuses a live window of that name, so a second vessel would navigate the popup that holds Audit.

Item-to-vessel lookups re-derive the key throughout `VesselWorkspace` (`registerVesselWorkspaceTarget`, `retireVesselWorkspaceTarget`, the park's `disposeVessel`, `onTearOutDocumentChange`) and in the tour's `NativeGestureDriver`.

Design authority: ADR 0029 §2.8.6 (amended 2026-10-09): *"outside every window it is a vessel."* The refusal contradicts it.

## The Fix

**Decided: option A.** Euclid disposed it on his `NativeLifecycle` surface (#18314), 2026-10-22. A vessel is identified by its Group key, minted at admission:
- **Mint and reserve in one synchronous, Group-owned step.**
  - The first choice is the derived key `workstation-vessel:<itemId>`. When it is taken, the vessel gets the first free `workstation-vessel:<itemId>:<n>`.
  - "Taken" includes pending and reconnecting reservations and retained logical slots. `Transaction#reserve` replaces a binding whose `windowId` is `null`, so having no live window does not make a key free.
  - An intentional rebind stays a separate path, and a retry within one gesture is idempotent.
- **The native registry keys its records by that key.** Each record keeps the opening item as provenance. Loops that take the item from a map key (bind, release, expiry, destruction) read it from the record instead, and the retirement fences around awaited preparation stay.
- **Close and retire use the supplied key and generation**, never the opening item resolved again. `NativeVesselTransaction#closeVessel` prefers item-derived records today, so a late close of the old popup could hit the successor vessel.
- **The window name follows the key.**
- **Keys persist verbatim.** Nothing parses an item out of a key, renames a live participant, or recomputes a suffix on restore. An item's current home comes from the document that holds it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The tab a popup was opened for tears out of that popup into a new vessel, while the popup keeps its other tabs and keeps working.
- [ ] An item re-torn out of main while its old popup still holds other tabs opens a new vessel.
- [ ] A warm reload restores both popups with their tabs, each under its own key.
- [ ] Two concurrent pending admissions cannot replace each other's reservation.
- [ ] A late close of the older vessel's generation leaves the successor vessel untouched.
- [ ] E2E: an arm in `WorkstationPopupTabDragNL.spec.mjs` tearing out the popup's own tab, red before the fix.

**Intake finding: the item-derived identity collides in three places, not one.** Minting a Group key, as The Fix says, is necessary but not enough. A vessel opened for `metrics` is identified by `metrics` in:

1. **Its Group key**, `workstation-vessel:metrics` (`VesselWorkspace#tearOutWorkspaceKey`). `Transaction#reserve` refuses it, and that is the wall a drag hits today.
2. **The native registry.** `NativeLifecycle#acquire` keys admissions, connections and owners by item id (`source.admissions.set(itemId, …)`, `getOwner(sourceId, itemId)`). The popup holding Audit keeps its records under `metrics`, so a second vessel's records would overwrite them.
3. **Its window name**, `tearout-metrics` (`openTearOutVessel`). `Main#windowOpen` reuses a live window of that name, so a second vessel would navigate the popup that holds Audit.

**Fork.** The registry is Euclid's surface (#18314).
- **A (recommended): a vessel is identified by its Group key, minted at admission.** `keyFor` returns the derived key when it is free, else the first free `workstation-vessel:metrics:<n>`. `NativeLifecycle` keys its records by that key and keeps the opening item on the record, and the window name follows the key. Persisted topologies restore unchanged, because their keys are the derived ones. Nothing parses an item back out of a key today.
- **B: re-home the popup onto a resident item when its origin item leaves.** Rejected: it re-keys a live Group participant, its history and the persisted topology.
- **C: refuse the origin tab's tear-out while its popup holds other tabs**, as a stated refusal instead of a silent one. Smallest, but it fails AC-1 and §2.8.6's "outside every window it is a vessel".

Under A, every item-to-vessel lookup listed in the body reads the record's key instead of deriving it again. The e2e arm (AC-4) does not depend on the fork, so it starts now.
