# Staged dock projection strips neo-dock-maximized before overflow partition

## Context

Surfaced during the `#20022` **AC-1** `v13.3.1` release-gate audit of the two single-test hosted CI reds seen on `dev` this week ([run `39852869392`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/actions/runs/39852869392), commit `998f35a3bf`, shard `e2e-brain (8/10)` — `WorkstationMaximizeTabReorderNL.spec.mjs:168`, *"switches active tab via click and reorders overflowed tabs via drag while maximized"*). Full Playwright trace diagnosis (`1-trace.trace` DOM snapshots + `test.trace` call timeline) is recorded on `#20022` ([`#20022#issuecomment-6354226190`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20022#issuecomment-6354226190)) with lead disposition in [`#20022#issuecomment-6354251452`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20022#issuecomment-6354251452) placing this ticket in milestone `v13.3.1` as a candidate whose blocking status the operator's walk decides.

## The Problem

In `test/playwright/e2e/workstation/WorkstationMaximizeTabReorderNL.spec.mjs:168-225`, while the `heavy-tabs` dock node (`#neo-tab-container-3`, `8` tabs) is maximized:
1. The test clicks `#neo-tab-header-button-5` (`activity`, `line 191`, `ts = 31289.49 ms`).
2. `Workspace#onDockActiveIndexChange` (`src/dashboard/dock/Workspace.mjs:1972-1976`) commits `{operation: 'setActiveItem', tabsNodeId: 'heavy-tabs', itemId: 'activity'}`. Because `Operations.changeClassFor('setActiveItem')` is `'topology'` (`src/dashboard/dock/model/Operations.mjs:89`), `Workspace#getRefreshOptions` (`Workspace.mjs:2122-2138`) returns `{}` and `refreshDockWorkspace()` runs a full staged shell replacement (`neo-container-2` → `neo-container-16`) rather than `reconcileStableTopology`.
3. **Part A — Product transient in `Reconciler.moveRetainedTabChrome` (`src/dashboard/dock/projection/Reconciler.mjs:711, 908-918`):**
   - `Maximize#onBeforeDockZoneDocumentChange` (`src/dashboard/dock/plugin/Maximize.mjs:242-249`) rightly classifies `setActiveItem` on the maximized node as a neutral in-pane operation (`isNeutralChange === true`) and keeps `maximizedNodeId = 'heavy-tabs'`.
   - However, during the staged shell swap (`Snapshot 203`, `ts = 31530.00 ms`), `Reconciler.moveRetainedTabChrome` (`Reconciler.mjs:908-918`) overwrites `tab.cls` with `[...(plan.config.cls || []), ...Reconciler.configDerivedClasses(tab)]`, **stripping `'neo-dock-maximized'`** from `#neo-tab-container-3` while leaving the four inline rect keys in `tab.wrapperStyle`.
   - Without `'neo-dock-maximized'` (`position: fixed`), `#neo-tab-container-3` drops back into flow layout at its docked split ratio (`flex: 0.4 1 0%`).
   - Still inside `Reconciler.reconcileProjection` (`Reconciler.mjs:711`) — **before** `Workspace#refreshDockWorkspace` (`Workspace.mjs:2621-2628`) calls `Maximize#syncDockProjection()` (`Maximize.mjs:855-868`) — the reconciler executes `await Promise.all(overflowPlugins.map(plugin => plugin.project(true)))`.
   - `Overflow#project(true)` measures `#neo-tab-header-toolbar-4` at the narrow un-maximized docked width and **overflows 7 of the 8 tab buttons (`removeDom: true`) — including `#neo-tab-header-button-10` (`console`) — leaving only `#neo-tab-header-button-5` (`activity`) mounted in the strip** (`Snapshots 224–261`, `ts = 31703.71–32029.82 ms`). When `Maximize#syncDockProjection()` subsequently calls `applyPresentation('heavy-tabs', {animate: false})` (`Maximize.mjs:868`), it restores `'neo-dock-maximized'` without re-projecting `Overflow`, leaving the maximized strip collapsed to 1 visible tab and 7 overflowed tabs (and evicting `#neo-tab-header-button-10` mid-drag).
4. **Part B — Premature post-click settle gate in `WorkstationMaximizeTabReorderNL.spec.mjs:192-199`:**
   - `dockModel.nodes['heavy-tabs'].activeItemId` updates synchronously at commit time (`31377 ms`) while `refreshDockWorkspace()` is still awaiting `flip?.captureFirst()` (`Workspace.mjs:2533`).
   - Because `me.animationSuppressedBars` is `[]` until `onProjectionStaged` runs inside `Reconciler.reconcileProjection` (`Reconciler.mjs:561`, `Snapshot 184` at `ts = 31454.36 ms`), the spec's `expect.poll` (`activeItemId === 'activity' && animationSuppressedBars.length === 0`) resolves at `31384.62 ms` (`70 ms` before `onProjectionStaged` adds `neo-no-animation`), and `not.toHaveClass(/neo-no-animation/)` (`31426.73 ms`), `waitOverflowIdle` (`31431.02 ms`), and `flipSettled` (`31441.95 ms`) all complete before the staged shell replacement even starts.

## The Architectural Reality

Design authority: `src/dashboard/dock/plugin/Maximize.mjs:21-28, 231-237` (*"Input contract while a node is maximized: in-strip tab reordering stays live... operations confined to the node itself (activating one of its tabs; closing, reordering or adding an item within it) defer to the re-projection rule instead, which re-applies onto the surviving node"*) and `#19986` / `#19996` (maximized strip overflow capacity).

- `src/dashboard/dock/projection/Reconciler.mjs:66-69, 908-918`: `configDerivedClasses` intentionally avoids a blind union with `tab.cls` so a cleared maximize cannot stick, because `Maximize#afterSetMaximizedNodeId` (`Maximize.mjs:277-278`) chains `clearPresentation` asynchronously on `transitionTail`. However, for a surviving maximized node (`Maximize#onBeforeDockZoneDocumentChange` kept `maximizedNodeId === nodeId`, or `Maximize#resetPresentation` runs synchronously on instant clear), stripping `'neo-dock-maximized'` in `moveRetainedTabChrome` causes `Reconciler.mjs:711` (`overflowPlugins.map(plugin => plugin.project(true))`) to measure and partition the strip at its un-maximized docked width.
- `test/playwright/e2e/workstation/WorkstationMaximizeTabReorderNL.spec.mjs:192-199`: must await the workspace's `refreshPromise` / `projectionCycle` (and settled overflow partition, per `#20020` / PR `#20021`) before starting `dragTabHorizontally`.

## The Fix

1. **Preserve or sync `'neo-dock-maximized'` across staged projection before `Overflow#project(true)` runs (`src/dashboard/dock/projection/Reconciler.mjs` / `src/dashboard/dock/plugin/Maximize.mjs`):**
   - Ensure that when a maximized tabs node survives a neutral operation (`maximizedNodeId === nodeId`), `'neo-dock-maximized'` is retained on `tab.cls` through `Reconciler.moveRetainedTabChrome` (while `Maximize#onBeforeDockZoneDocumentChange` synchronously strips `'neo-dock-maximized'` via `resetPresentation({animate: false})` when `maximizedNodeId` is cleared before reconciliation), OR ensure collaborator projection sync (`syncDockProjection` + `tabContainer.promiseUpdate()`) completes before `overflowPlugins.map(plugin => plugin.project(true))` partitions the strip.
2. **Tighten the post-click settle gate in `test/playwright/e2e/workstation/WorkstationMaximizeTabReorderNL.spec.mjs:192-199`:**
   - Include `!ws.refreshPromise && !ws.projectionCycle` (and settled maximized-strip overflow count `overflowCount === 0`) in the post-click `expect.poll` gate so `dragTabHorizontally` never begins while the `setActiveItem('activity')` projection is still staging.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Activating, reordering, adding, or closing a tab inside a maximized dock tabs node (`Maximize#isNeutralChange === true`) never causes `Overflow#project(true)` during `Reconciler.reconcileProjection` to partition the strip at its un-maximized docked width or evict visible headers into the overflow menu.
- [ ] Non-neutral dock operations that clear `maximizedNodeId` before reconciliation continue to remove `'neo-dock-maximized'` terminally without sticking across `moveRetainedTabChrome`.
- [ ] `WorkstationMaximizeTabReorderNL.spec.mjs:192-199` awaits settled `refreshPromise` / `projectionCycle` after clicking `activity` before starting `dragTabHorizontally`.
- [ ] Unit coverage (`test/playwright/unit/dashboard/Maximize.spec.mjs`) and `test/playwright/e2e/workstation/WorkstationMaximizeTabReorderNL.spec.mjs` (`3/3`) pass.

## Out of Scope

- Changing `Operations.changeClassFor('setActiveItem')` globally outside the maximize/reconciler continuity seam unless verified safe across all dock consumers.

## Avoided Traps

- Fixing only the spec's post-click settle gate (`WorkstationMaximizeTabReorderNL.spec.mjs:192-199`) would still fail if `Reconciler.moveRetainedTabChrome` strips `'neo-dock-maximized'` and `Reconciler.mjs:711` overflows 7 of the 8 tabs into `removeDom: true` before `Maximize#syncDockProjection()` runs. Both halves are required.
