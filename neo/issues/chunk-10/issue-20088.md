# SEO generator emits doubled /news/blog/blog/<slug> paths in sitemap.xml

## Context

While auditing `https://neomjs.com/sitemap.xml` and `buildScripts/docs/seo/generate.mjs` (`2026-10-24`) to diagnose Google Search Console indexing failures with `@tobiu`, we discovered that all 20 internal blog routes in `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` carry a doubled `/news/blog/blog/<slug>` path prefix.

**Sweep Attestations (`ticket-create §1a`):**
- `Live latest-open sweep:` checked latest 20 open issues in `neomjs/neo` (`#19145`–`#20086`) and `neomjs/middleware-v2` (`#9`–`#34`) at `2026-10-24T01:07:10Z`; no equivalent open ticket found.
- `A2A in-flight claim sweep:` checked latest 30 messages (`status: 'all'`); no competing claim on `buildScripts/docs/seo/generate.mjs`.
- `MC sweep:` `query_raw_memories({query: "middleware-v2 Googlebot Search Console sitemap SsrService canonical SEO"})`, 10 results; surfaced `7530ffa3-5d88-4ebe-980a-f91fe667082f` (`2026-10-16`), which noticed `/news/blog/blog/<slug>` in passing after `#19503` without filing the double-prefix bug in `getBlogRoutes()`.
- `Own-assignment sweep:` 0 open issues assigned to `@neo-gemini-julia` in `neomjs/neo`.

## The Problem

1. In `apps/portal/resources/data/blog.json`, every internal blog entry stores its `id` with a leading `blog/` prefix, e.g.:
   ```json
   "id": "blog/where-an-agent-institution-sits"
   ```
2. In `buildScripts/docs/seo/generate.mjs:649` (`getBlogRoutes`), the generator prepends `/news/blog/` directly onto `post.id`:
   ```js
   routes.push(`/news/blog/${post.id}`);
   ```
3. As a result, `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` emit **doubled** `/news/blog/blog/<slug>` URLs for all 20 internal blog posts (verified live on `https://neomjs.com/sitemap.xml`: `https://neomjs.com/news/blog/blog/where-an-agent-institution-sits`, `https://neomjs.com/news/blog/blog/a-workspace-that-can-leave-its-window`, etc.), while clean `/news/blog/<slug>` URLs return `404`.

## The Architectural Reality

- `Design authority: "Extracts paths for Neo-hosted blog posts (those without a 'publisher' property, meaning they are hosted natively inside the Portal App rather than on external platforms like Medium)." (buildScripts/docs/seo/generate.mjs:630-635)`
- Let's compare how the Portal UI routes blog posts vs `generate.mjs`:
  - In `apps/portal/resources/data/blog.json`, `"id": "blog/<slug>"` is used by the tree/list store, whereas `generate.mjs:649` assumed `post.id` was a bare `<slug>` without `blog/` (or prepended `/news/blog/` instead of `/news/`).
  - We must verify whether the Portal app's route handler (`apps/portal/view/news/...`) expects `/news/blog/<slug>` and normalize `post.id.replace(/^blog\//, '')` in `generate.mjs` (and ensure portal routing and `generate.mjs` agree on `/news/blog/<slug>`).

## The Fix

1. In `buildScripts/docs/seo/generate.mjs` (`getBlogRoutes` and `generateLlmsTxt`):
   - Normalize `const slug = post.id.replace(/^blog\//, '');` before constructing `/news/blog/${slug}` so `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` emit canonical `/news/blog/<slug>` URLs instead of `/news/blog/blog/<slug>`.
2. Verify that the Portal app's route handler for `/news/blog/:slug` resolves `blog/${slug}` in `blog.json` so `SsrService` in `neomjs/middleware-v2` renders the blog post body at `/news/blog/<slug>`.
