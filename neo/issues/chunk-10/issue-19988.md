# An in-window tab drag sends a strip header flying to the window's top-left

## Context

Found in manual testing of `apps/workstation` on `dev` (2026-10-22): resorting tab headers in the pane that holds 12 tabs. In @tobiu's screen recording (about 39.4–41.6 s), "Runtime Health Envelope" is dragged and the pointer rises toward the workspace's top edge. Meanwhile the only other visible header, "Distributed Trace Explorer", leaves its strip. It rises above its row, is clipped by the pane's top border, disappears, and reappears above the strip further left. The headers "fly away" toward the top-left.

A further frame from the same drag (the operator's screenshot follows in a comment) shows "Priority Alert Observatory" being dragged inside its pane. Meanwhile the other header's teal active-tab underline sits outside the pane, over the neighbouring pane's header to the left.

## Measurements

Headless Chromium, 2200×1000, at `8bb3574d0f` (its `src/`, `apps/` and `resources/scss/` are identical to `dev` at `84b711878a`). A `requestAnimationFrame` sampler records every tab button's rect against the strip box.

| gesture | tab buttons outside the strip during the drag |
|---|---|
| 2nd visible tab, 180 px left, in-strip | none |
| 2nd visible tab, into the pane body and back | none |
| 2nd visible tab, 45 px rise above the strip and back, leftwards | none |
| **3rd visible tab ("Workspace Topology Inspector"), 120 px right, 45 px rise and back** | **126 frames, 2 of 2 runs**: the button is absolutely positioned and travels from the strip (y 105) to **(−180, 0)**, the window's top-left corner offset by its own width |

The reproduction moves the dragged tab's own strip button; the recording moves its sibling. Both are strip headers absolutely positioned and sent toward the top-left during an in-window drag.

## The Problem

During a tab drag the strip's `SortZone` positions its items absolutely, freezing their rects for the gesture. A 45 px rise takes the roughly 32 px proxy fully out of the 33 px strip. That is the base `container.SortZone`'s boundary-exit condition (`detachThreshold`, then `isWindowDragging` and `dragBoundaryExit`), and the dock's `TabSortZone` layers its window-drag embodiment on top (`startWindowDrag` / `endWindowDrag`). That this path is entered during the reproducing gesture is inferred, not traced.

At least one header gets coordinates that are not its strip slot, and a CSS transition makes the move a visible flight across the window. Which write does it is not yet traced.

**Design authority:** the dock `TabSortZone#startWindowDrag` docblock. *"No dashboard layout re-flow: a tab strip's geometry is owned by the committed model projection, which this gesture has not touched."* A header leaving its strip while the gesture is still in-window contradicts that.

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/draggable/container/SortZone.mjs`: the absolute-positioning setup for sortable items, the boundary-exit / window-drag transition (`detachThreshold`, `reattachThreshold`, `dragBoundaryExit`), and the cleanup that restores item styles.
- `src/dashboard/dock/interaction/TabSortZone.mjs`: `startWindowDrag` / `endWindowDrag` (in-window proxy hidden or restored, base reorder parked or un-parked), plus the cross-zone events on every move.
- Prior art: a returned tab header kept absolute drag positioning (`top: 0`) outside its strip after a drag, on 2026-10-07 in another consumer. That is the same item-styles seam, after the drag rather than during it.

## The Fix

First trace which write sends the button to `(−180, 0)`: the base SortZone's park or window-drag path, or the dock's `startWindowDrag`. Then keep every strip header inside its strip slot for the whole in-window gesture. Hiding a header that is not shown (e.g. the dragged original) must not move it across the window, and its transition must not animate it there.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] During any in-window tab drag, including rises out of the strip and returns, no tab button's rect leaves the strip box, and no header animates across the window.
- [ ] After the drop, every header sits in its committed slot with no residual absolute positioning.
- [ ] **E2E:** a new spec under `test/playwright/e2e/workstation/` (e.g. `WorkstationTabDragHeadersStayNL.spec.mjs`) drags the 12-tab pane's 3rd visible tab with the reproducing gesture (120 px right, a 45 px rise above the strip, back down) using a real pointer. It samples every frame and asserts all tab buttons stay inside the strip. It must be red on `dev` (126 frames outside, `(−180, 0)`) and green with the fix, and it joins the hosted Workstation tier (#19655).
- [ ] Existing tab sort-zone and dock drag specs stay green.

## Out of Scope

- Cross-pane and edge-preview behaviour of a docked pane's tab drag, which is legitimate in a docked pane. For the maximized case see #19986.
