# Overflow control on an unfocused pane needs two clicks to open its menu

## Context

Found by the gate-3 non-author walk of `apps/workstation` on the v13.3.1 candidate `e2f77dd0fb` (receipt on #20022, finding 2). Ordinary pointer input in headed Chrome, 1440×900, standalone Workstation. First recorded as a design note; promoted to a milestone ticket by the operator's ruling of 2026-10-23 (~14:00 CEST): the version handed to the external tester carries no known bug.

## The Problem

The `Priority Alert Observatory` strip holds more tabs than fit, so it shows the overflow control (`…`, title "More tabs"). With the pane **unfocused**, one click on that control:

1. focuses the pane — the focus-gated action rail (pin, reload, pop out, maximize, close) renders in the strip;
2. the strip re-partitions for the rail's width — `Resident Activity Timeline` moves into overflow and the control itself moves ≈ 100 px to the left, out from under the pointer;
3. **no menu opens** (checked at 250 ms and 1.1 s after the click; no element with a menu/overflow class was visible).

A second click on the control at its new position opens the menu with the hidden tabs. Observed 2/2 in the walk (sessions a: gestures 06 and 09/10). A user sees their click do something unrelated (the pane highlights, a tab vanishes into overflow) and the affordance they clicked slide away.

## The Architectural Reality (inference, to be verified by the owner)

- The header action rail is focus-gated (#17875 lineage; #19983 added the hover-reveal option, off by default here), so the first pointer-down on anything in an unfocused strip focuses the pane and the rail appears in the same strip.
- The tab overflow plugin partitions on the strip's free width; the rail takes ~120 px, so the partition changes under the pointer and the control is re-laid out. #20033 / #20039 settled the e2e oracle for the partition at menu open; they did not touch the first-click behaviour.
- The click that focused the pane does not reach the overflow control's open handler (either the control is re-rendered before `click` lands, or focus handling consumes it).

## The Fix (prescription, owner's call)

One click opens the menu, focused or not. Two shapes, both acceptable:

- open the menu from the same click after the re-partition settles (anchor the menu to the control's new position); or
- reserve the rail's width in the strip's partition (or anchor the overflow control at the strip's right edge independent of the rail), so nothing moves under the pointer and the click lands on the control it was aimed at.

Keep the focused-strip behaviour unchanged (one click opens today).

## Shipped shape (PR #20052)

A third shape, not one of the two above: the toolbar can hold a focus-gated reveal for a named reason, and the overflow plugin holds it from the control's mousedown through the click and while its menu is open, so nothing in the strip moves until the user is done with the menu; the owed reveal lands when the menu closes. Two other shapes were built, measured and withdrawn: reserving the rail's span in the partition (a 180 px single-tab strip truncated its only tab, a 220 px two-tab strip hid a tab that fit) and placing the contribution after the focus-gated actions (it contradicted the toolbar's documented contract and the frozen rail order a spec pins; the hold made it unnecessary). AC-1/AC-2 are the new e2e arm (red on e2f77dd0fb, green at head); AC-3 the overflow/header-action specs; AC-4 the full local battery, receipt on the PR.
