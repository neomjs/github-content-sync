# DockMotionNL moveItem e2e reads the moved pane at its old position

## Context

`test/playwright/e2e/dashboard/DockMotionNL.spec.mjs` › *a committed moveItem is FLIP-bracketed: the pane travels between real positions around the structural commit* fails intermittently in the hosted e2e-brain tier, always with the same message:

> the moved pane must land at a different position (from 1,70 to 1,70)

| Run | Branch, head | In-job retry |
|---|---|---|
| `39876017299` | `agent/20026-departed-window-last-resort`, `654edbceac` | passed |
| `39963349591` | `agent/20056-feed-keeps-scroll`, `df39847e22` | passed |
| `40007469729` | push to `dev`, `da49631cf6` | failed too |
| `40046025369` | PR #20087, `788d84e26e` | failed too; a job re-run passed |

These are at least four sightings in the last 120 runs of "E2E (Brain-gated tier)", back to 2026-10-22 14:56Z. I scanned the failed runs and the earlier attempts of re-run runs. A job whose in-job retry passed and stayed green would not show up, so this is a lower bound. The earliest sighting is 2026-10-23 00:06Z.

In every sighting, each assertion before this one passed: the operation applied, the document holds the item in its target node, and the witness recorded more than five frames, each with a non-zero width.

## The Problem

- **Cost:** each red takes a diagnosis before someone re-runs it. On PR #20087 it held the reviewer seat until the re-run, and on `dev` it reds the push. Under #19655's terminal predicate, a red in this tier blocks merge readiness, so a flake here blocks PRs that are fine.
- **Not order-bound:** the in-job retry runs in a fresh worker without the file's earlier tests, and it failed in two of the four sightings. The sightings also sat in different shard lineups, before and after the duration shards (`0ab667195e`).
- **No local repro so far:** on macOS at `788d84e26e`, the spec passed 7/7 alone and 9/9 in its CI shard's order.

## The Architectural Reality

- **The witness:** every frame, `startMotionSampler` records the first element carrying `dock-flip-item-<id>` (`document.querySelector`). `Workspace#flipMarkerPrefix` is stamped on every plain pane config, and the FLIP addon correlates panes by that class.
- **The bracket:** this test calls `expectSignalBracket(page)` with the default `SIGNAL`, which matches any `.neo-dashboard-dock-animating` element. Several producers raise that class:
  - the Workspace's FLIP play (`MotionSignal.enter(me)` before `flip.play`, `MotionSignal.leave(me)` when it settles);
  - `TabEnterButton`;
  - `RevealOverlay`;
  - `Maximize`;
  - the strip overflow projection (`LayoutAdapter`'s `overflowProjectionStart` / `overflowProjectionIdle`).
- **Prior art in this file:** #19893 diagnosed the same shape on the *rail-click reveal SLIDES* test. The any-element bracket opened and closed on another producer's signal, and `readMotionSamples` stopped the witness before the motion under test was observed. PR #19894 gave that test a narrowed `selector` and a settle. The moveItem test still brackets any element and arms its witness without a settle.
- **Design authority:** the spec's docblock says committed dock operations "move real pixels", and that the signal "brackets each motion".

## The Fix

1. **Read the recordings before fixing.** Two artifacts hold `trace.zip` and `error-context.md` for both failed attempts: `test-results-e2e-brain-40046025369-shard-5` (177 MB) and `test-results-e2e-brain-40007469729-shard-2` (73 MB). Both expire 2027-01-21/22. The trace shows when each producer's signal entered and left, against the moment the moved pane's DOM changed. Each hypothesis below needs that V-B-A:
   - **H2 (prior art #19893):** the bracket closes on another producer's signal before the FLIP play raises its own, so the witness stops while the pane is still at its old position. This is test-only: bracket the Workspace's own FLIP signal, and settle earlier motion before arming the witness, as #19894 did for the reveal.
   - **H1:** two elements carry the marker, and a stale one at the old position comes first in DOM order. Whether that is a product defect (a leftover pane) or a witness defect depends on what the stale element is.
   - **H3:** the projection never moved the pane on that runner. This would be a product defect in the dock projection.
2. **Fix the owner the recordings convict**, red-first on the recorded condition. If no recording separates the hypotheses, reproduce under CPU throttling first, since slow runners are the suspected condition.
