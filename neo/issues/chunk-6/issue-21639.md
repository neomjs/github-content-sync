# A Gantt cascade moves its bars and their links with a transition

## Context

The operator asked for this on 20 Nov, relayed by Clio. When you move a task in a comparable Gantt, the other tasks move "with nice transitions. ours just jump. could be an optional config." Mnemosyne, as lead, offered the lane, and Clio, as #20874's design seat, set the look quoted in The Fix. It is for Engine 14. The Gantt cascade example held back in #21346 waits on it. Ada owns it through the weekend before the Sun 22 Nov 17:00Z examples lock, so it carries no contributor labels.

## The Problem

On `dev` (`8ed566ae7d`), a moved or edited task's successors land on their cascaded dates in one update, and their links redraw in the same update. Nothing travels between the old place and the new one, so the reader cannot follow what the cascade did.

## The Architectural Reality

- A bar's place is an inline `left` and `width` in pixels, derived from its record's dates (`src/timeline/Column.mjs:680-685`).
- `moveTask()` sets the record and then runs the cascade (`src/gantt/Container.mjs:897-906`). `Cascade.runDependencyCascade` (`src/gantt/Cascade.mjs:113`) returns "Changed successors with dependency, dates, changes and previous values": the list a transition needs, with its start geometry.
- Nothing transitions a bar's place. The only `transition` in the family's source styles (gantt, timeline, scheduler) is a `box-shadow` (`resources/css/src/gantt/Container.css:49`).
- `DependencyLayer#renderRoutes` (`src/gantt/DependencyLayer.mjs:487`) replaces the whole SVG fragment through `vdom.html`, so every path is a new node on every render. No CSS transition can carry a link: a bar on a CSS clock would glide while its links jump.
- The body's rows are pooled, so a transition belongs to a record, never to a row. The grid precedent is `Neo.grid.plugin.AnimateRows` (#21570, #21583), which moves rows only under one class and ends the move on a scroll or the next store change.
- The motion vocabulary (`resources/css/src/Global.css:44-66`) has four duration tiers and two easings, and every tier collapses to `0ms` under `prefers-reduced-motion`. "Nothing on a product surface animates outside these without a recorded exception at its call site."
- Design authority: n/a. This calls no existing behavior wrong; it adds a behavior behind a new config, and the look is #20874's design seat's.

## The Fix

1. **A config, off by default:** `Neo.gantt.Container#animateCascade_`, `false`.
2. **One clock, in the app worker.** When `moveTask()` cascades with the config on, the chart steps the moved task and every changed successor from its previous geometry to its final one. At each step, `DependencyLayer` re-projects the routes of those tasks, so a link never leaves its bar. Bars and links read the same eased progress.
3. **Duration and easing come from the vocabulary:** `--motion-panel` (280 ms) and `--ease-out-soft`, read from the chart's node. Under reduced motion the tier reads `0ms`, so the cascade lands in one update, as today.
4. **Only the changed records move.** A scroll, a zoom, a store load or the next move ends the steps at the final geometry, and a row entering the buffered window never moves.
5. **The examples switch it on,** including the cascade example under #21346. Specs that read geometry right after a move keep the default.

The look, from #20874's design seat: only the records a cascade changed move, and the moved task settles the same way. Nothing animates on a scroll, a zoom, a store load or a row entering the buffered window. The links stay on their bars for the whole move. Reduced motion means no movement.

## Implementation Notes

Read on `dev` at `4444747966` before building:

- **A hold precedes the first write.** `Neo.grid.Body#onStoreRecordChange` (`src/grid/Body.mjs:1613`) repaints a changed record's row at once, and the cascade writes each successor with its own `succ.set()` (`src/gantt/Cascade.mjs:334`). So with the config on, `moveTask()` snapshots the displayed dates before `record.set(updates)` (`src/gantt/Container.mjs:898`), and every repaint inside the move reads the snapshot. The tween starts only after the cascade returns, for the root and the receipt's `cascaded` records; their `previous` values are its start.
- **One override, two readers.** The bar descriptors (`src/timeline/Column.mjs:943-965`) and `DependencyLayer#project` (`src/gantt/DependencyLayer.mjs:445-461`) both read a task's date fields and map them through `timeAxis.dateToOffset`. A protected date hook in each, fed by the chart's in-flight dates per record key, keeps every link on its bar at every step.
- **Each step** repaints only the rows of moving records (`Neo.grid.Body#onStoreRowsChange` already repaints a key set in one update) and re-projects the layer once.
- **Clock:** the steps run on the app worker. `--ease-out-soft` is `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`, solved in JavaScript; the duration is the chart node's computed `--motion-panel`, read when a move starts.
- **Ends:** the next `moveTask()`, `zoomTo()`, a scroll, and the task store's `load`, `filter` and `sort` end the steps at the final geometry.
- **The service path:** a service-owned task goes to `schedule.moveTask()` inside the same `_movingTask` guard (`src/gantt/Container.mjs:863-866`), so the same hold can wrap it. Its receipt's `cascaded` entries carry `taskId` and `previous`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Default `false`: a cascade renders in one update, and the existing Gantt specs pass unchanged.
- [ ] On: moving a task with successors renders at least three distinct intermediate `left` values for each changed bar, monotonic between the previous and the final value, and ending exactly at the final one. The e2e lengthens `--motion-panel` on the chart so that a loaded CI runner still samples them. Red on `dev`.
- [ ] On: at every sampled step, each link of a moving task starts and ends within 1 px of the bar edges it attaches to.
- [ ] A scroll, a zoom or a store load during the steps ends them at the final geometry. A row recycled into the buffered window shows its record at rest.
- [ ] A `0ms` tier gives one update with no intermediate geometry.
- [ ] The Gantt examples switch it on. The pull request carries a frame strip of one cascade at 1440 × 900, dark; any clip lives outside the repo.

## Out of Scope

- The Scheduler and the Task Board, which have no cascade.
- A drag in progress: the drag proxy follows the pointer.
- The cascade's rules.

## Avoided Traps

- A CSS transition on a bar's `left` and `width`: a pooled row would slide in from another task's place, and the links would jump while the bars glide.
- A CSS `d` transition on the links: the paths are new nodes on every render, and `d` interpolation is not portable.
- A main-thread follower that redraws paths from the bars' live rectangles: it would copy `buildPathD` out of the worker.
