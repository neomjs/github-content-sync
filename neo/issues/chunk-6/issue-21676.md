## Context

`@tobiu` vetoed `#21609` (`D+S` — Drop+Supersede, [`IC_kwDODSospM8AAAABpEcIxg`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/pull/21609#issuecomment-7051086022)) after inspecting `/examples` cards: disabling mouse selection hid five visual and interaction regressions rather than restoring the original `Hover × Selected` chromatic state grammar, fixing the moving-target `"Open live ↗"` CTA, pruning obsolete browser labels, or keeping covers visible. [Discussion #21670](https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/21670) converged unanimously across all three active model families (`Gemini` `@neo-gemini-julia`, `Claude Opus` `@neo-opus-ada` + `@neo-opus-vega`, and `GPT` `@neo-gpt-euclid`).

## The Problem

1. **Lost orthogonal `(Hover × Selected)` cover gradient (`apps/portal/view/examples/List.mjs:327-337`, `resources/css/src/apps/portal/examples/List.css:67-75`):** `getBackgroundImageStyle()` writes `url(...), linear-gradient(var(--portal-examples-list-gradient-start), var(--portal-examples-list-gradient-end))`, which sits invisible behind `url(...)` because `List.css` dropped `@property` declarations and `background-blend-mode: overlay` from `6fea520d4d:resources/scss/src/apps/portal/examples/List.scss:1-47`.
2. **Hover and Select mapped `1:1` to the same drawer (`List.css:158-174`)**, hiding ~84%–94% of covers behind an opaque slab (`List.css:79`).
3. **Moving-target `"Open live ↗"` CTA (`List.css:49, 146-174`):** Entering a card from below expanded `.portal-example-more` under `.portal-example-title-row`, pushing `"Open live ↗"` ~120 px away from the cursor, while `translateY(-2px)` shifted every hitbox by 2 px.
4. **Zero-entropy browser string (`List.mjs:241-244`):** `0 / 126` records in `apps/portal/resources/data/examples.json` override `"Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari"`, whereas `9 / 126` explicitly qualify `['Desktop', 'Mobile']` (`2 / 9` also have `sharedWorkers`).
5. **Link focus vs card selection (`src/list/Base.mjs:355`, `src/main/addon/Navigator.mjs:128-136, 193-259, 468-470`):** Enabling `selectOnFocus: true` sets `autoClick: true`, firing a synthetic click on card `B`'s wrapper during `focusin` before `B`'s native `<a>` click runs; meanwhile default `Navigator` steps `ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown` by `±1` instead of crossing rows in multi-column CSS grids.

## The Architectural Reality

- `Design authority: "the OLD exaples list => it had a blue gradient for HOVERED items visually mixing into the cover image. it had a green background gradient for selected items. an item could have both: hovered and selected, leading to a new custom gradient. of course we need BOTH states and not hacking around... the idea that a list item gets a cover that expands is not bad. but it can not be 1:1 the same for hover and select... i enter an item with the mouse from the bottom, since i want to click the link. the details expand to the top, the link moves AWAY from the cursor. serious UX fail. all examples have support for all browsers at this point. showing them => fail. hiding 80% of a cover for details.... not nice."` (`@tobiu` veto on `#21609` + [`IC_kwDODSospM8AAAABpEcIxg`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/pull/21609#issuecomment-7051086022), plus `@tobiu` clarification relayed in [`IC_kwDODSospM8AAAABpFueYw`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21676#issuecomment-7052435043): *"i only challenged the team to do better"* — present distinct 4-state visual candidates on top of the converged interaction contract rather than treating the 2025 palette as a literal restoration order).
- **Named container query (`container: examples-list / inline-size` on `.portal-examples-list.neo-list`):** Overview (`padding: 0`), GroupPage (`padding-inline: 24px`), SearchResults (`padding: 0`), and standalone (`padding-inline: 16px`) apply different horizontal paddings, while `.content` (`List.css:62`) declares its own inner `container-type: inline-size`. Naming `container: examples-list / inline-size` on `ul.portal-examples-list` and querying `@container examples-list (min-width: 704px)` (`2 × 340px + 24px gap` inside `ul`'s content box, equivalent to `736px` on a `32px`-padded wrapper) switches modes at the exact 1-column ↔ 2-column boundary (`900 px` and `641 px` sidebar layouts stay 1-column).
- **Shared Portal hover token (`DC_kwDODSospM4BLeWS`):** Source `OQ1`'s blue hover stop from `var(--sem-color-border-focus)` (`var(--purple-400)` = `#3E63DD` in `theme-neo-dark` & `theme-neo-light`), matching `#21674` glass cards.
- **Single arrow-key owner (`DC_kwDODSospM4BLeWS`, `DC_kwDODSospM4BLeXo`):** Configure `navigator: {workerKeys: ['ArrowDown', 'ArrowLeft', 'ArrowRight', 'ArrowUp']}` and `selectOnFocus: false` on `Portal.view.examples.List`; `Portal.view.examples.ListModel` owns body-click toggle (ignoring `tagName === 'a'` in `data.path`), `Escape` (`deselectAll()`), `Left`/`Right` (`±1`), and `Up`/`Down` geometric row traversal via `view.getDomRect()` + `Neo.main.addon.Navigator.navigateTo` (handling uniform 3-column, 2-column, `leadCard: true` `2×2` hero, and 1-column layouts).
