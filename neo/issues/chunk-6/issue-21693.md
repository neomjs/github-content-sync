# A zoom keeps the date at the timeline's left edge

## Context

`Neo.scheduler.Container#zoomTo` changes the scale and leaves the horizontal scroll offset in pixels, so a planner who scrolled to a date and switches the zoom level lands on another date. Ada measured it on `examples/gantt/zoom` (defect-note `4213c8f9`: Week scrolled to Mon 29 Mar → Day shows Tue 9 Mar, 6,048 px before and after). The Gantt inherits `zoomTo`, so every zoom control in the scheduler and Gantt examples does the same. #21617 (the scheduler zoom example) asks exactly this question of a planner, and its step 1 files the jump as an engine ticket that blocks it: this one.

## The Problem

Reproduced on `dev` at `4b444e4e7a` in `examples/scheduler/bigData` (1440 × 900): at Week, the region scrolled to 20,000 px; after Day the region still sits at 20,000 px, and the date at the visible timeline's left edge moves from 6 Apr 09:06 to 2 Feb. The new spec `test/playwright/e2e/scheduler/ZoomAnchor.spec.mjs` ("a zoom keeps the date at the timeline's left edge, both ways") fails on `dev` with `the region scrolled with the scale`.

## The Architectural Reality

- `zoomTo(presetOrConfig)` (`src/scheduler/Container.mjs:1325`) sets `majorTickUnit` / `majorTickStep` and `timeAxis.set(axisConfig)`; nothing reads or writes the scroll position. `Neo.gantt.Container#zoomTo` (`src/gantt/Container.mjs:691`) calls it and redraws the links.
- The scroll position belongs to the grid's horizontal scrollbar (`Neo.grid.HorizontalScrollbar`); the engine scrolls it with `Neo.main.DomAccess.scrollTo({direction: 'left', id: horizontalScrollbar.id, …})`, as `Neo.grid.Container#scrollByColumns` does. Its spacer takes a new width in its own update (`afterSetCenterWidth`), so a scroll sent before that update lands is clamped to the old width.
- The worker already knows the edge: `timelineColumn.scrollLeft` (center-content pixels, the header toolbar's value) less the timeline's `x` in `body.columnPositions` is the axis offset at the visible left edge; `timeAxis.offsetToDate` / `dateToOffset` convert. The `x` matters: `examples/scheduler/bigData` scrolls a 128 px "Team · Site" column before the timeline.
- Design authority: #21346 ("what stays in view on a zoom is an engine gap") and #21617, Avoided Traps ("A zoom that loses the visitor's place without saying so … the engine ticket fixes it"). No ADR, guide or JSDoc states what `zoomTo` does to the scroll position (searched `zoomTo` / `zoomPresets` JSDoc and `learn/guides/datahandling/Scheduler.md` and `Gantt.md`).

## The Fix

In `src/scheduler/Container.mjs`:

1. `zoomTo` reads the date at the visible timeline's left edge before `timeAxis.set`, and afterwards scrolls the region by as far as that date moved on the new scale, so it stays at the edge. While the timeline's start is in view (offset ≤ 0), nothing scrolls: an unscrolled plan opens on its first day at every level, as today.
2. A protected `scrollTimelineTo(value)` sends the scroll after `horizontalScrollbar.promiseUpdate()`, so the new width has landed.

The left edge, not the centre: a centre anchor clamps at the start of a short plan (a week of sessions at Week), so a round trip Hours → Week → Hours loses the place; the left edge only clamps at the far end. It also keeps an unscrolled plan on its first day, which `test/playwright/e2e/gantt/ZoomNL.spec.mjs` asserts at every level.

| Target Surface | Source of Authority | Proposed Behavior | Fallback | Docs | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Neo.scheduler.Container#zoomTo` | `src/scheduler/Container.mjs:1325` | keeps the date at the visible timeline's left edge; returns the axis as today | no scroll while unmounted or while the timeline's start is in view | JSDoc `@description` | `ZoomAnchor.spec.mjs` |
| `Neo.scheduler.Container#scrollTimelineTo` (new, protected) | `Neo.grid.Container#scrollByColumns` (same scrollbar route) | scrolls the region once the scrollbar carries its width | nothing without a horizontal scrollbar | JSDoc | `ZoomAnchor.spec.mjs` |

- [ ] AC-4: `zoomTo` still returns the axis synchronously (`Neo.gantt.Container#zoomTo` reads it).

## Out of Scope

- A zoom by wheel or pinch around the pointer.
- A centre anchor or a caller-chosen anchor date.
- A center column with a measured (flex or unset) width before the timeline: its width lands after a DOM read, later than the scrollbar update this waits for.
- The timeline windows that ignore the columns before it (#21684).
