# A board example moves several selected cards in one drag

## Context

A leaf of #21346, the planning examples under #21140. The visitor's question: can a triager pick ten cards and move them at once?

No contributor labels: the capability is read from the source ([the check on `#21346`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21346#issuecomment-6982869553)) and has not been driven in a browser. A maintainer builds it, and the drive is the first step.

## The Problem

Sorting an inbox card by card is the slow part of a board. The engine's board has a selection model with ranges, and its drag carries the selection; no example shows either.

## The Architectural Reality

- `selectionModel_` (`src/board/Container.mjs:150`, `selection-board`): `src/board/SelectionModel.mjs`, with `singleSelect: false` (`:47`), a range by Shift (`onKeyDownShiftDown`, `:728`) and select-all (`onKeyDownSelectAll`, `:703`). `src/board/SortZone.mjs` drags the selection.
- The keyboard moves one card, not a selection (the source check linked above).
- Template: `examples/board/basic` (not edited). Specs for the family: `test/playwright/e2e/board/`.
- Shared files stay out of this pull request (`#21539`: every examples pull request that edits them makes the others stale): the catalog's data, its two specs and `test/playwright/externalBrainSelection.mjs` belong to #21613, which takes the record below as written.
- Design authority: n/a — a new example; it calls no existing behaviour wrong.

## The Fix

1. **Drive it first** on a scratch copy of the base example: a selection by click, by Shift and by the modifier key, select-all, and a drag of five cards into another column. A defect becomes an engine ticket, linked here as a blocker.
2. **A new folder `examples/board/multiSelect`:** a bug triage. Forty reports in Inbox with a severity tag, and three empty columns: This sprint, Later, Won't fix. A line reads "n selected" with a Clear button. The data ships with the example, in a store of models.
3. **The record, for #21613:** id `board-multi-select`, name "Select many, move once", `feature` "Cards selected by click, Shift and a modifier key, dragged together into another column", `lookFor` "Click a report, Shift-click one further down, then drag the selection into This sprint. Both headers' counts follow." Cover: the Inbox with five cards selected and the line "5 selected".

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Cards are selected by click, by Shift-click as a range, by the modifier key one at a time, and all at once; the pull request names the keys per platform. Escape clears the selection.
- [ ] A drag that starts on any selected card moves all of them, in their order, and the dragged proxy shows how many.
- [ ] The line "n selected" and both headers' counts follow every change.
- [ ] A spec under `test/playwright/e2e/board/` asserts the stage and the order of a moved selection.
- [ ] No console error. 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844. Every theme the base example's `neo-config.json` lists.
- [ ] **Look:** the first frame at both sizes, in the dark theme, is on the pull request beside `board/basic`'s, read cold by one reader of the builder's seat. A selected card at rest, on hover and with focus, and the proxy of a five-card drag are shown; a selection can be read at card size, in the dark and in the light theme.
- [ ] The pull request edits no shared file.

## Out of Scope

Engine changes. Moving a selection by keyboard, an engine gap named on #21346. A selection across lanes.

## Avoided Traps

- **A selection shown by a faint border only.** It has to read on the cover.
- **Three reports in the Inbox.** A range needs a long column.

## Intake · claimed

**Verdict:** accept, with a fork on two ACs. The parent epic carries Maryam's review ([issuecomment-6983738611](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/21346#issuecomment-6983738611)).

**Drive** (dev `4b444e4e7a`, `examples/board/basic`, headless Chromium, 1440 × 900, dark): a click and a Shift-click selected five Backlog cards. The drag carried all five into Review, and the console stayed clean. Two ACs do not hold on the engine as it stands:

1. **"in their order"**: the held card lands first. The selection in board order was Design, Build, Review, Test, Refine. A drag started on Review landed as Review, Design, Build, Test, Refine. That is the documented rule: `SortZone.mjs:410-412` returns `[leadRecord, ...otherSelectedRecords]`, committed lead-first at `:790-798`. Board order survives only when the drag starts on the first selected card.
2. **"the proxy shows how many"**: a five-card drag shows the held card plus four clones and no number. The `+N` badge counts only the cards beyond five (`SortZone.mjs:390-394`, pinned by `SortZone.spec.mjs:802`).

**Fork.** This ticket's Out of Scope keeps engine changes out of its PR, so there are two ways to close the gap:
- **(a) Engine follow-up, as a blocker of this ticket.** A selection lands as one block at the placeholder in board order. The badge shows the total for any multi-selection, as a desktop file drag does. The stack's look stays as it is.
- **(b) Keep the engine and amend the two ACs.** They would read "the held card first, the rest in board order" and "the stack, plus `+N` beyond five".

I recommend (a). Dragging a triage column's middle card should not silently reorder its priorities, and four stacked clones can't be counted at card size. @neo-fable-clio, the ACs are yours to call. I'm not waiting on either answer: the example itself (the data, "n selected" + Clear, the columns, the frames) doesn't depend on the fork, and I'm building it now.
