# Run the Brain-gated e2e tier on every Engine PR, blocking

## Context

Phase 2 of epic #19655, graduated from D#19599. This leaf is blocked by #19656, the `dev`-push burn-in. It starts only once that burn-in's receipts show hosted time per shard within the ceiling, together with a green streak on `dev` in which every red is dispositioned.

## The Problem

The Brain-gated tier exists to catch dock and multi-window regressions before merge. After #19559, a regression sat on `dev` for about 14 hours. The tier helps only if it runs on PRs and blocks when it fails. A non-required check is not an alternative, because current consumers (`validateMergeReady` and the review audit) already treat any red as blocking.

## The Architectural Reality

- **The impact boundary:** `classify-test-scope.yml` already holds the E2E impact boundary: `src/`, navigated apps and examples, themes and build scripts, Playwright support, harness apps, dependencies, and the workflow and classifier themselves. `externalBrainSelection.mjs` classifies fixture dependence, not transitive impact.
- **Merge readiness:** Brain's `validateMergeReady` reports `nonRequiredFailures` as blocking, and `compareRequiredAndEmittedContexts` reports absent required contexts.
- **The job:** the burn-in job from #19656 supplies provisioning, the Brain-tip resolution and the receipt fields.

## The Fix

- Add `pull_request` to the job from #19656. The job always reports, and chooses full or smoke inside itself.
- **Full tier** when the E2E impact boundary matches, or when grid, AI-client or NL fixture/spec/config files change. **Smoke subset** otherwise.
- The summary records the selected population, so a green smoke is never read as full-tier evidence.
- A red blocks exactly as any red does. No `continue-on-error`.
- `WhiteboxE2E.md` states that the tier gates Engine PRs.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The job runs on every `pull_request` to `dev`, always reports, and selects full or smoke inside itself.
- [ ] The full tier runs when the E2E impact boundary matches or grid, AI-client or NL fixture/spec/config files change; otherwise a smoke subset runs. The summary records the selected population.
- [ ] A deliberately failing run, carried through the real reporting path, stays red and is refused by `validateMergeReady`. No `continue-on-error` exists on the path.
- [ ] The PR links #19656's burn-in receipt on #19655: hosted time per shard within the ceiling, and a green streak on `dev` with every red dispositioned (#17844's set included).
- [ ] `WhiteboxE2E.md` states that the tier gates Engine PRs.

## Out of Scope

Provisioning and receipts (#19656), option B (D#18775), and headed-only specs (local, at the cut).

## Avoided Traps

- **Masking reds with `continue-on-error`** or an advisory contract the consumers haven't adopted.
- **A path-filtered required check:** it strands PRs, as neomjs/neo-agent-brain#972 did.
