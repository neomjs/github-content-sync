# Animated roster sorting moves cards twice before settling

## Context and problem

Operator report, 2026-10-13, in the running Fleet Manager: switching the roster cards between **Online first** and **Name** produces two visible moves. Cards first travel to an apparent default/unsorted arrangement, wait for that transition, then travel to the requested order. The operator reports this on changes generally; the sort switch is the concrete reproducer.

Expected: one sort intent moves surviving cards directly from their displayed positions to the requested order. Installed candidate/pins and a recorded frame timeline have not yet been captured. Store clearing and the intermediate arrangement being genuinely “unsorted” are hypotheses, not observed Store facts.

## Architectural reality

Current source checked at Engine `fe6ab42c75675a007bd9d7390fdc09afb4493f74` and Institution `e5aa4238b7acba14ec5383bfd300ce2f2b8e8af2`:

1. Institution `apps/agentos/view/fleet/roster/Controller.mjs:166–177` replaces `store.sorters` once. It does not clear/refill the Store.
2. Engine `src/data/Store.mjs:1269–1274` emits coarse `load` during `sort`. This is intentional; #16559 / #16616 preserve load-only consumers.
3. `src/list/Base.mjs:387–392,623–648,878–886` binds both events; the load handler synchronously rebuilds VDOM in current Store order.
4. `src/list/plugin/Animate.mjs:739–770` then maps `previousItems` indices onto `vdom.cn`, assuming those nodes still have the previous order. Its timer at `:777–784` calls `createItems()` after `transitionDuration` (default 500 ms).

This sequence strongly explains an incorrect first target followed by a settlement correction. It is source evidence, **not an independent rendered reproduction**.

Design authority: the roster List's JSDoc says “a sort MOVES the surviving instances (translate transition)”; #17553 requires stable cards without rebuilding/flashing. The operator identifies the intermediate detour as a defect. Institution's record-keyed pool/DOM identity from #99 must remain intact.

## The fix

First capture the event/record/node/transform timeline on the ordinary Store → animated component-list path. Correct ownership of the sort presentation in the shared list/Animate layer so it uses the actual displayed identity/order and settlement does not launch another visible move. Do not suppress Store's documented load-on-sort contract or add a Fleet-only timer workaround.
