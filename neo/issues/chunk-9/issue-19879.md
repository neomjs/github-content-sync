# FiveBeat scene 5's hosted stack return aims outside the main window

## Context

`WorkstationFiveBeatNL` › *scene 5 — the signature: full journey in ONE run* is the flagship journey's own scene. On hosted CI it fails often, it is neither quarantined nor ticketed, and it reddens unrelated PRs: dependabot bumps, a spec-only fix, and three `dev` pushes.

**Measured** (every hosted `E2E (Brain-gated tier)` run since 2026-10-17, shard 10/10 job logs per attempt, test-level marks):

| sample | failed | passed |
|---|---|---|
| 32 shard-10 jobs on 29 heads, excluding `neo#19871` | **20 of 44 test attempts** | 24 |
| jobs red (both attempts failed) | **8** (incl. `dev` `74ed8d1d`, `18adbb52`, `a4eafc17`) | — |
| first failure → retry also failed | 8 of 12 | — |

Retries fail together, so the cause is tied to the runner, not to timing.

## The Problem

Every failure stops at the same assertion: the stack-return step, after the vessel-dock beat, reports `main workspace did not reach one semantic + rendered stack-return claim`. The claim trace shows the main window as a candidate with `intersects: false`, so the gesture never claimed main at all.

Here is what the failure JSON records (run `39322381695`, attempt 2):

| fact | value |
|---|---|
| `aimed.rect` (main's `right-top-tabs`, client coords) | x **2304**, y **121**, w **220**, h **240** |
| `windows.main.innerRect` | x 10, y 10, w **1280**, h 800 (the spec's emulated viewport) |

A node inside a 1280-wide viewport should not be measured at x 2304. Two readings fit these numbers, and the evidence does not yet decide between them:

- **Uniform 2× scale:** all four values are exactly twice a plausible in-viewport rect (1152 / 60.5 / 110 / 120).
- **Horizontal offset only:** y 121 and h 240 are plausible as they are for a top-right tabs node in an 800-px viewport, and a 220-px zone is also what the quarantined `showcase dwell previews align with their zones` test measures (*"the tab-into preview reads 110 px against its 220 px zone"*, where 110 may be an edge-half preview, not a scale). Then only x is wrong: the dock layout overflows or is translated.

Neither reading has been observed. Either way the aim leaves main, and the claim correctly refuses.

## The Architectural Reality

- `apps/workstation/tour/NativeGestureDriver.mjs` `executeStackReturnStep` (~l. 755–775) aims at `WindowManager.get(me.windowId).innerRect.{x,y}` + the centre of `returnHost.getDomRect([returnNode.id], me.windowId)`. It mixes window geometry with a DOM rect and assumes both use one CSS-pixel space.
- `src/manager/DragCoordinator.mjs` claim candidates (~l. 990) test `Window.get(windowId).innerRect.intersects(screenX, screenY)`. So a doubled DOM rect sends the pointer outside main, and no zone is asked.
- The spec emulates `viewport 1280×800` and `screen 1920×1080` in ordinary mode (`WorkstationFiveBeatNL.spec.mjs` l. 75–76, 507–514).

## The Fix

Diagnose first, then fix the coordinate space at the place the scale enters:

1. **Instrument:** at aim time, the stack-return proof records main's `devicePixelRatio`, `visualViewport.scale`, `innerWidth`/`innerHeight`, the document's and the dock root's `clientWidth`/`scrollWidth`/`scrollLeft`, and the raw `getBoundingClientRect()` of the return node, beside `innerRect`. A failing hosted run then tells the two readings apart: a scale shows in the ratios, an offset in the scroll widths.
2. **Fix by observed cause:** the aim and the claim must agree on one coordinate space and on one laid-out main. Fix the producer of the wrong value (lost emulation, a scaled document, a twice-converted rect, or an overflowing layout), not the test's tolerance.
3. If the same cause explains the showcase-dwell quarantine, lift that quarantine in the same PR.
