# Grid: a row reached by keyboard leaves the view once its neighbours are measured

## Context

Found while building the acceptance journey of epic #19658 (#19666), on its 100k-row example, which is not on `dev` yet. At 1400×900, "Jump to row 50000" (`view.scrollByRows(0, 49999)`, the keyboard navigation path) settles with rows 49955–49986 rendered, the last of them already below the view. Row 50000 is not even mounted, and it stays that way for the 2 s sampled. #19663's author saw the same independently: `scrollByRows(0, 299)` and `(0, 64)` intermittently never displayed the requested record, and left the jump and final-row boundary to #19666.

## The Problem

In content-sized mode, `Body#scrollByRows` scrolls down so the target's bottom meets the view's bottom, at offsets computed from the estimate for rows not measured yet. The rows then render and are measured. `grid.View`'s private `#changeRowHeights` (#19664) keeps the row at the top edge still. So every row between the top edge and the target that grows from the 32px estimate (to 62–119px in the rows sampled on the example) pushes the target further down.

The navigated row is the one the user is looking at, but the correction anchors another one.

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/grid/Body.mjs`, `scrollByRows`: the keyboard navigation path of `selection.grid.RowModel` and `CellModel`, and of the example's jump field. In content-sized mode, scrolling up puts the row's top at the view's top. Scrolling down puts its bottom at the view's bottom, unless the row is taller than the view, which shows its top.
- `src/grid/View.mjs`, `#changeRowHeights`: the one place height changes move the scroll offset, as `scrollCorrection` plus `scrollEpoch` in the next update's meta (#19664).
- `View#scrollTop` follows every scroll that reaches the App worker (`View#syncBodies`), the navigation's own included.
- Design authority: `scrollByRows`' stated alignment ("its bottom meets the viewport bottom") and the epic's predicate that keyboard navigation and the final row work.

## The Fix

- `View#scrollRowIntoView(index, edge)` takes over the content-sized branch of `scrollByRows`. It scrolls to the aligned offset and keeps the row there as its navigation target.
- While a landed navigation target exists, `#changeRowHeights` moves the scroll offset so that the target meets its edge again, instead of holding the row at the top edge.
- A scroll from elsewhere ends the target, as does a store load or a target beyond the store's count.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] After a jump down to an unmeasured row, the row ends fully visible at the view's bottom edge, and stays there in every frame once its scroll has landed. This is an e2e on a fixture that learns heights after the first render.
- [ ] Rows that change above a landed navigation target keep it at its edge. A later scroll from elsewhere returns the correction to the row at the top edge. This is a unit spec.
- [ ] `AnchorCorrection`, `KeyboardNavigationScroll` and `WrapTextMeasure` (unit and e2e) stay green.

## Out of Scope

- Measuring the jump target before its first paint (#19663 leaves it out).
- Fixed-height grids, whose `scrollByRows` is unchanged.

## Avoided Traps

- **Re-running `scrollByRows` after the heights settle:** the target would visibly jump away and back.
- **Anchoring the target before its scroll lands:** the App worker's offset still describes the old position, so the relative correction would land elsewhere.
