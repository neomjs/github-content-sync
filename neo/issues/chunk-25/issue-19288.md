# Headless Chrome's 800 px screen clamps every script-moved popup

## Context

Several of #17844's census rows are classed environment-bound ("headed-only"). Measured today, at least one of them is bound by a single launch default: headless Chrome gives every window an **800×600 screen**, and the window manager clamps a script-moved popup into it. A test's CDP placement is not clamped, so a spec can stage a target popup off that screen, and the product's own `window.moveTo` then cannot follow it there.

## The Problem

`DemoBPerspectivesNL` stalls in its cross-window step. The conversion park moves the torn-out vessel to the target's origin with `Neo.Main.windowNativeMoveTo(822, 22)`. `#moveWindow` polls 6 × 50 ms, sees the popup at x = 320 and answers `false`, so the park is refused (`refusedAt: 'move'`) and re-proposed about every 670 ms. The tour's step never completes, and the spec's 10 s hosting poll reports "the tour has not reached its cross-window step". A main-thread trail of `parkWindowDrag` / `windowNativeMoveTo` / `windowNativeFocus` recorded the loop.

x = 320 is 800 − 480. A standalone probe with the e2e channel (`chrome`, headless) opens a 480×360 popup and calls `moveTo`:

| launch | `screen.width` | `moveTo(900, 20)` lands at | `moveTo(1400, 20)` lands at |
|---|---|---|---|
| default | 800 | 320, 20 | 320, 20 |
| `--screen-info={1920x1080}` | 1920 | 900, 20 | 1400, 20 |
| `--window-size=1920,1080` | 800 | not moved | not moved |

With the overlay `--screen-info={1920x1080}`, `DemoBPerspectivesNL` passes the hosting poll: the popup hosts the live Workbench pane. It then fails at the next assertion, "worker truth must show the original instance mounted once into the popup", which is a separate question for the next owner.

## The Architectural Reality

- The e2e browser's arguments are declared once in `test/playwright/e2e/utils/gpuIntent.mjs`: `BASE_LAUNCH_ARGS`, shared by `PRESENTING_LAUNCH_ARGS` and `ENGINE_LAUNCH_ARGS`. `playwright.config.e2e.mjs` launches the local `chrome` channel with `activeLaunchArgs()`.
- Specs that set `contextOptions.screen` change only what `window.screen` reports; the window manager still clamps to the real headless screen.
- `Neo.Main#moveWindow` verifies the requested coordinates, which is correct: the clamp is real, and the product reports it truthfully.

## The Fix

Give the headless browser a realistic screen: add `--screen-info={1920x1080}` to `BASE_LAUNCH_ARGS`. The switch belongs to Chrome's headless mode; this ticket must confirm that a headed run ignores it before relying on that.
