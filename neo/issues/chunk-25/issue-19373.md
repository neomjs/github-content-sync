# Conversion-out re-show: measure the resume rect's plane on real chrome

## Context

Split from #19372 (its AC-3). #19372 fixes the TERMINAL restore: the park now records the vessel's content rect, so a refused handoff or a cancelled conversion re-shows the frame the park took the window from. The NON-terminal path — the pointer leaves the target while the vessel is parked — re-shows through a different rect, and its plane is not settled by source reading alone.

## The Problem

`apps/workstation/view/VesselWorkspace.mjs:520` resumes at `data.logicalRect ?? data.record?.sourceRect`. `logicalRect` is the coordinator's `logicalSourceRect` (`src/manager/DragCoordinator.mjs:1040–1044`): `{x: screenX − offsetX, y: screenY − offsetY, width/height: proxyRect}` — the pointer minus the grab offset. Two readings of that origin disagree:

- as the proxy's content origin in screen coordinates — then `toFrameOrigin` (`src/dashboard/dock/window/NativeVesselTransaction.mjs:710`) converts it correctly;
- as the frame origin the window follow uses — #19225's own observation: the dragged vessel follows the pointer with its OUTER origin at pointer minus the grab offset, its content one title bar lower — then the resume hands `resumeWindowDrag` an origin one chrome too high, and whether the addon's follow corrects it on the next pointer frame is unmeasured.

The fallback, `record.sourceRect`, is the FRAME sample since #19225 (48591f31b6) and is converted wrongly for certain; it is reached only when `logicalRect` is absent.

## The Architectural Reality

- Terminal restores read `preConversionRect` — content since #19372. Non-terminal re-shows read the out-event's live rect (the design record's `resumeWindowDrag(widgetName, proxyRect)` semantics: the popup resumes under the pointer).
- The resume route is the addon's (`Neo.main.addon.DragDrop.resumeWindowDrag`, `NativeVesselTransaction.mjs:756–758`): the drag is live, the addon owns the gesture, and a direct route move beside it would be a second writer — so the final position is the addon's to settle, which is why this needs a measurement, not a derivation.
- Stage: a pointer-driven vessel-over-vessel drag on real chrome. `DemoBCrossWindowDragNL` has been 2 of 3 red since 2026-09-09; `WorkstationNativeTitlebarDragNL` / `WorkstationNativePopupOverPopupNL` drive the native path only. The measurement needs a torn-out tab dragged by pointer across windows on a real display (headed).

## The Fix — a measurement first

1. On real chrome, headed: tear a tab out, drag it by pointer over a second vessel until the conversion parks the source, drag out again, read the `restore` receipt (`rect`, `frame`, `terminal: false`), the addon's resume data and the window's final `screenX/Y` against the pointer position. Post the receipt here.
2. If the resumed window sits one chrome above where the pointer holds it: hand the content plane in at the conversion-out site (the logical rect shifted by the source window's chrome, or the window's live inner rect) — the rule #19372 applied to the record — with a red-first unit arm beside #19372's.
3. If it sits where the pointer holds it: record that here and close the branch question.
4. Either way, the fallback `record.sourceRect` moves to the content anchor (`resolveVesselRestoreAnchor`, #19372): it is a frame rect by construction.
