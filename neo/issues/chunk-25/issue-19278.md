# Under a hand drag the parked vessel covers the target popup's drop zones

## Context

The operator's hand test on his own display (screen recording, 2026-09-26): a tab torn out and carried over the Metrics popup converts, and the pane leaves the vessel, but the vessel stays **on top of** the target. The target's drop zones and its target-side proxy render under it, and the release commits the vessel as its own popup. The frame-by-frame reading and the fork are on #19186 ("Operator recording, 2026-09-26 11:56").

The ADR 0029 §2.8.6 record's author ruled there at 10:11Z: option 1. The vessel parks clear of the target, inside the visible work area, with zones-before-park as the belt, in one PR, accepted on a hand receipt from the operator's display. This is that PR's ticket.

No automated witness reproduces the failure: the headed runs grant or emulate focus (every receipt reads `refocused: true`), and none captures the compositor. The mechanism below is read from the recording. The operator's `lastVesselParkReceipt` is still wanted (#19186).

## The Problem

§2.8.6 parks the vessel by z-order. It moves the vessel onto the target's frame origin and raises the target with `focus()`, checked by a `document.hasFocus()` poll. Under a real OS mouse drag, macOS Chrome does not raise the target on `focus()`. The poll still admits, so the conversion goes ahead with every affordance it produces hidden under the vessel. A better raise is not available: no raise primitive works under a drag, and the record forbids re-acquisition through `window.open('', name)`.

A second gap: while the park is pending or refused, the target receives no frame at all, so the hand sees no zones until the park admits.

## The Architectural Reality

- Two park bodies run the same focus → move → refocus sequence:
  - `src/dashboard/dock/window/NativeVesselTransaction.mjs`, the engine default (`effectsFor`, park): `windowNativeFocus`, an optional `windowNativeResizeTo`, `DragDrop.parkWindowDrag` to the target's origin, then a refocus that compensates when refused. Demo B parks through it.
  - `apps/workstation/view/VesselWorkspace.mjs#parkTearOutVessel`, the Workstation's own. The pointer path (`nativeTitlebar: false`) parks through `DragDrop.parkWindowDrag` with `parkSize` and `restoreRect`; the native title-bar path moves through `windowNativeMoveTo`.
- The usable display bounds already reach the App worker. `Main#getWindowData()` returns `screen.availLeft/availTop/availWidth/availHeight`, and `Placement.resolvePosition` clamps a popup into them, which is the precedent for a pure static.
- `src/manager/DragCoordinator.mjs#onDragMove` sends the target a transition-owned frame only when `transition.engage && transition.commitEligible && claimed.zone`. `TabSortZone#resolveRemoteDragTransition` answers `engage: false` while `record.transitioning`, so a pending park reaches no target.
- Readers of `lastVesselParkReceipt.refocused`: `test/playwright/unit/apps/workstation/Workspace.spec.mjs`, `test/playwright/unit/examples/dashboard/crossWindow/DemoBWorkspace.spec.mjs` and `test/playwright/e2e/dashboard/DemoBCrossWindowDragNL.spec.mjs`.
- #19248 (PR #19254) makes the target-side proxy the tab header and keeps the pane in the vessel until the drop. Once the vessel parks clear, nothing but the vessel's own frame covers the target.

## The Fix

This implements the amendment text drafted on #19186 ("§2.8.6 amendment text") for the pointer path:

1. **Park position.** One engine static beside `Placement.resolvePosition` answers the parked frame's origin. It takes the corner of the target display's work area farthest from the target's inner rect, clamps the frame inside the work area, and reports whether the parked frame is clear of the target's inner rect. Both park bodies use it. The shrink stays as today and runs first.
2. **No focus step.** The pointer path drops both focus calls and the `hasFocus()` poll. The receipt records `cleared` instead of `focused` / `refocused`, and a refused move still compensates.
3. **Zones before park.** A transition-owned frame whose claim stands while the park is pending or refused reaches `onRemoteDragMove` with `embodyProxy: false` and stays commit-ineligible, so the target renders its zones from the claim. The header proxy still embodies only after the park admits.
4. **ADR 0029 §2.8.6** carries the amendment, signed by its author.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 (unit, red-first): the park-position static answers the corner farthest from the target, clamped inside the work area, with `cleared: true` whenever some corner is clear. A target spanning the work area yields `cleared: false`, and missing bounds yield `null`.
- AC-2 (unit): on the pointer path neither park body calls a focus route. The vessel moves to the static's origin, the receipt carries `cleared`, and a refused move compensates as today.
- AC-3 (unit): a transition-owned frame with a standing claim and a pending park reaches the target with `embodyProxy: false`, and a drop on that frame commits nothing.
- AC-4 (docs): ADR 0029 §2.8.6 carries the amendment from #19186, signed by the record's author.
- AC-5 (hand, on the operator's display, at the PR head; carries #19248's AC-3): a tab torn out over a popup parks clear of it. The target shows its drop zones and the tab-header-sized proxy, whose bounding box is about the source tab button's, and the drop commits the pane. Receipt: `lastVesselParkReceipt` with `cleared`, plus the operator's confirmation.

## Out of Scope

- The native title-bar path. It parks only after the OS releases the drag, when `focus()` raises, and the operator called it good as it is. It keeps target-cover unless the record's author rules otherwise.
- The bounded `disposeVessel` retry, which the ruling ships alone (#19186).
