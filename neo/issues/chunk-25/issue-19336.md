# SharedCanvas renderers keep drawing while their window is hidden

## Context

Grace measured this on the Fleet Manager's Home field ([neomjs/neo-agent-institution#360, motion observation](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-institution/pull/360#issuecomment-5916457647), 2026-09-30). Under `useSharedWorkers` the canvas worker has no `requestAnimationFrame` (`undefined` in a SharedWorker), so the renderer's loop runs on `setTimeout(1000 / 60)`, and nothing pauses it while the window is hidden. Measurement 11 in [D#18730](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/18730) (2026-09-21) left the same question open: rAF throttles itself in a hidden document, a worker's timer does not. Unmeasured: Playwright's Chromium never reported the page hidden, so no probe has watched a hidden window's canvas worker yet.

## The Problem

An animating `Neo.app.SharedCanvas` renderer keeps stepping and drawing while nobody can see it, for example in a minimized Fleet Manager or a background portal tab. The engine records the premise itself: "a hidden document throttles its own timers, not a worker's or its messages" (`src/worker/HiddenTick.mjs:4`).

## The Architectural Reality

Read at dev `7ff8467baa`:

- `Neo.app.SharedCanvas#pause` / `#resume` (`src/app/SharedCanvas.mjs:249`, `:304`) forward to the renderer. Their only callers are the portal hero's `deactivate()` / `activate()` (`apps/portal/view/home/parts/hero/Container.mjs:52`, `:44`), which pause the hero while its part is out of view. Nothing pauses on the window's visibility.
- `Neo.canvas.Base#pause` sets `isPaused`, `canRender` honours it, and `#resume` restarts the loop (`src/canvas/Base.mjs:212`, `:142`, `:226`).
- `Neo.worker.App#onVisibilityChange` (`src/worker/App.mjs:840`) fires the window's app `visibilitychange` with `{hidden, windowId}`. `Neo.component.MagicMoveText` subscribes to it (`src/component/MagicMoveText.mjs:224`).
- `SharedCanvas` hosts: `Neo.app.header.Canvas`; the portal's hero, footer, timeline and services canvases; the Institution's `ObservatoryCanvas` and Home canvas.

## The Fix

`Neo.app.SharedCanvas` subscribes to its app's `visibilitychange` once its canvas is ready, after `MagicMoveText`'s precedent: pause while hidden, resume when visible. The subscription follows the host into another window and ends on destroy. A hidden window and a host's own `pause()` are two separate reasons: the renderer runs only when neither holds, so a portal hero paused out of view stays paused when its window comes back.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1 The app's `visibilitychange` with `hidden: true` pauses the host's renderer, and `hidden: false` resumes it. The subscription follows the host into another window, and a destroyed host no longer listens. Unit arm with the renderer stubbed.
- [ ] AC-2 A host paused for its own reason stays paused across a hide and show: the portal hero, deactivated, then hidden, then shown, is still paused.
- [ ] AC-3 No engine or portal `SharedCanvas` renderer jumps on its first frame after a resume.
- [ ] AC-4 *(post-merge, once the Institution's engine pin includes it)* With the installed Fleet Manager's window minimized, Home's `getStats().frames` does not advance.

## Out of Scope

- `requestAnimationFrame` in SharedWorkers, and Electron's background throttling.
- Renderers hosted outside `SharedCanvas`, such as the Sparkline under `Neo.component.Canvas`: the same loop behind a different host.
- The open presenter and grouping questions of D#18730.

## Avoided Traps

- **Pausing from each app's view:** every host would repeat the same listener, and the next `SharedCanvas` would miss it. The engine owns the renderer's lifecycle.
- **One pause flag for both reasons:** a visibility `resume()` would wake a host that paused itself, as the portal hero does while out of view.
