# Calendar week and day views get an all-day row

## Context

`Neo.calendar.model.Event` declares an `allDay` field (`src/calendar/model/Event.mjs:22`). No module under `src/calendar` reads it. The calendars people use every day show all-day and multi-day events as bars in a row between the day headers and the time grid; ours has no such row.

## The Problem

- An event with `allDay: true` is drawn like a timed one. `week.Component#updateEvents` (`src/calendar/view/week/Component.mjs:910`) places every record of a day by its start and end hours, at the column's full width, so an event from midnight to midnight fills the column and covers that day's timed events.
- An event that spans more than one day appears on no day at all. `Neo.calendar.store.Events#getDayRecords(date)` returns a record only when it starts and ends on that date; the rule is pinned by `test/playwright/unit/calendar/EventsStore.spec.mjs` (#19140). A three-day conference cannot be shown in any view.

## The Architectural Reality

- **Week view** (`src/calendar/view/week/Component.mjs`): the vdom holds a `neo-header-row` with one item per day above the `neo-c-w-column-timeaxis-container`. Column and header descriptors are built together with date-scoped ids (lines 596 to 627). The view keeps buffer columns on both sides and re-uses them while it scrolls horizontally (lines 800 to 860). `updateEvents()` fills each day column from `eventStore.getDayRecords(date)`.
- **Day view** (`src/calendar/view/DayComponent.mjs`) extends the week view with `columnsVisible: 1`, so one implementation serves both.
- **Store** (`src/calendar/store/Events.mjs`): `getDayRecords` is also called by the month and the year view and keeps its pinned rule.
- **Editing** (`src/calendar/view/EditEventContainer.mjs`): fields for title, calendar, start time and end time; none for `allDay`.
- **Dragging** (`week/EventDragZone.mjs`, `week/plugin/DragDrop.mjs`, `week/plugin/EventResizable.mjs`) acts on `.neo-event` nodes inside day columns.
- **Styles:** `resources/scss/src/calendar/view/week/Component.scss` plus the theme folders. Events take their colour from the calendar record through a `neo-<color>` class.
- **Specs:** `test/playwright/unit/calendar/` holds `EventsStore.spec.mjs` and `WeekView.spec.mjs`.

Design authority: n/a — the ticket adds a view for a field the model already declares and calls no existing behaviour wrong; `getDayRecords` keeps the rule #19140 pinned.

## The Fix

- **Store:** a second lookup beside `getDayRecords` that returns the records with `allDay: true` whose days overlap a date range. A pure read.
- **Week and day view:** an all-day row between the header row and the time grid.
  - Each all-day record is one bar from its first to its last day, clipped to the rendered columns, with its title, in its calendar's colour.
  - Bars that overlap stack in lanes. The row is as tall as its lanes and is not rendered when the range holds no all-day event.
  - Bars follow the same column buffering as the day columns, so they stay aligned while the view scrolls.
  - All-day records are no longer drawn in the time grid.
- **Edit form:** an "All day" checkbox that writes `allDay` and hides the two time fields while it is checked.
- **Example:** `examples/calendar/basic/data/events.json` gains one all-day event of a single day and one of three days.

Lane packing is a pure function. #20876 brings an interval packer under `Neo.timeline`; if it is on `dev` when this work starts, the row uses it. If not, the function stays replaceable.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The new store lookup returns the all-day records that overlap a date range and nothing else. `getDayRecords` is unchanged and its spec passes untouched.
- [ ] In the week and in the day view an all-day record renders as one bar across its days in the all-day row and is absent from the time grid.
- [ ] Two overlapping all-day records stack in two lanes. With no all-day record in the rendered range the row is not in the DOM.
- [ ] After the week view scrolls by a week in either direction, each bar still starts and ends on its own day columns.
- [ ] The edit form sets and clears `allDay`, and the view follows the record change.
- [ ] `WeekView.spec.mjs` covers the row for: no all-day record, one single-day record, one record over three days, two that overlap.
- [ ] The pull request carries a before and an after screenshot of `examples/calendar/basic` in the week and in the day view.

## Out of Scope

- Dragging or resizing an all-day bar.
- The month and the year view, which still skip multi-day events.
- A "+n more" overflow for many lanes, recurring events, time zones.
- Moving the calendar onto the timeline family's time axis; that is deferred in `ROADMAP.md`.

## Avoided Traps

- **Changing `getDayRecords` to return multi-day events.** Three views call it and a spec pins it; the timed views would start drawing a three-day block in every column.
- **A bar per day for a multi-day event.** It reads as three events and breaks the title.
- **A view-owned list of all-day events.** The row projects the store like the columns do, so a second window on the same store follows an edit.

### @neo-fable-mnemosyne - 2026-11-13T22:07:40Z

**What follows this ticket, so the three items are in one place.** None of it changes the scope here; each becomes its own ticket once this one is on `dev`, because all three edit the same views and store.

1. **The month and the year view.** Both read `eventStore.getDayRecords(date)` (`src/calendar/view/month/Component.mjs:563`, `src/calendar/view/YearComponent.mjs:661`), so a multi-day event still appears on no day there. The range lookup this ticket adds is the read they need.
2. **Dragging and resizing an all-day bar.** The drag zone and the resize plugin act on `.neo-event` nodes inside day columns; a bar in the all-day row moves and grows by whole days.
3. **The week view on the timeline's axis.** `ROADMAP.md` dates that move after the next cut. The axis alone is not the whole move: the calendar keeps its own event store and model (`calendarId`), and `Neo.timeline.model.Event` carries `resourceId`, with `Neo.timeline.store.Events#getRangeRecords` as the read. Nothing under `src/calendar` imports from `src/timeline` today. One ticket for the axis and the store keeps the engine from carrying two event models past that move.

I file the three when this one is merged, and review the pull request for this ticket if its author wants a second reader.
