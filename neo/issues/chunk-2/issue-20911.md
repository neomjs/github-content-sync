# Row geometry: a row's extra height lies below its cells

**Blocked by:** #20681 (the extra height this ticket renders below the cells).

## Context

#20681 gives content-sized rows an extra height per row: a row's slot becomes its measured height (or the estimate) plus its extra, and the offsets follow. The extra is room for something that is not a cell, such as an inline form or a notes strip under a row. #20681 leaves "any component that renders the extra part" out of scope. But the row itself still renders at the full slot height, so its cells fill the extra.

## The Problem

- `Row#createVdom` sets the row's inline height to `rowGeometry.heightAt(rowIndex)` (`src/grid/Row.mjs:432`), and `.neo-grid-cell` takes `height: inherit` with `align-items: center` (`resources/scss/src/grid/Body.scss:54–64`). A row measured at 32px with an extra of 300px renders 332px-tall cells in every body, with their text centered 166px down.
- The row's box (`contain: strict`, `Body.scss:42–46`) covers the extra too, so a click there is a click on that row (`Body#onRowClick`, `src/grid/Body.mjs:1521–1535`), and hover paints it.
- A consumer cannot correct this from outside. `Body#getRowClass` returns classes only (`Body.mjs:1378–1380`), and a body builds every row from the fixed `Neo.grid.Row` class (`Body.mjs:792–802`).

## The Architectural Reality

- `RowGeometry` is the one height owner of a view, and a row's slot is `[offsetAt(i), offsetAt(i + 1))`. `Row#hasStaleGeometry` compares `style.height` with `heightAt(rowIndex)` (`Row.mjs:743–754`).
- Design authority: n/a — adds a capability. Rows without an extra and fixed-height grids are unchanged.

## The Fix

- `RowGeometry#extraHeightAt(index)`: the extra of the row at an index, `0` without one, kept by the same passes as #20681's extras.
- `Row#createVdom` renders the row at `heightAt(rowIndex) - extraHeightAt(rowIndex)`. Its transform stays at `offsetAt(rowIndex)`, so the extra is empty room below the row, inside its slot.
- `Row#hasStaleGeometry` compares the same height.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A record measured at 32px with an extra of 300px renders its row at `height: 32px`, and the next row's offset is 332 (unit).
- [ ] `hasStaleGeometry()` is `false` right after that render and `true` once the record's measured height changes. A change of only its extra leaves its own row current while the rows below move (unit).
- [ ] With locked start and end columns, the cells of a row with an extra keep the row's height without the extra in all three bodies, and a click inside the extra fires no `rowClick` (component or e2e spec).
- [ ] Fixed-height grids and content-sized rows without extras render as before (the existing `RowGeometry` and content-sized specs, unit and e2e, stay green).

## Out of Scope

- What renders inside the extra, and which rows get one (#20681's own scope).
