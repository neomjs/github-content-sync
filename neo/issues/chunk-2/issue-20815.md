# Portal transitions bypass the motion vocabulary and reduced motion

## Context

#14780 ratified the product motion standards. Durations and easings come from `resources/scss/_motion.scss`, whose reduced-motion block collapses them to 0 ms, and a duration or easing literal at a call site is a contract violation. The Portal's stylesheets predate that rule and never adopted it. This surfaced while reviewing #20813, which touches the Examples List's transitions.

## The Problem

Read on `dev` `095f7ccfbe`:

- `--portal-transition-duration` is a 300 ms literal in `resources/scss/theme-neo-dark/apps/portal/Viewport.scss:6` and its light twin. It aliases no `--motion-*` tier, so the reduced-motion collapse never reaches it.
- 30 `transition` declarations under `resources/scss/src/apps/portal/` carry a duration or easing literal: `news/medium/List.scss` 11, `examples/List.scss` 10, `Viewport.scss` 4, `services/Container.scss` 2, and one each in `news/videos/List.scss`, `news/videos/Container.scss` and `news/medium/Container.scss`. Most pair `var(--portal-transition-duration)` with an `ease-out` literal.
- No Portal stylesheet reads `var(--motion-…)`.

A visitor who asks for reduced motion still gets every Portal transition.

## The Architectural Reality

- `resources/scss/_motion.scss` is the vocabulary: `--motion-fast` (120 ms, hover and press micro-feedback), `--motion-base` (200 ms, state transitions), `--motion-panel` (280 ms, panel and pane movement), `--motion-stage` (400 ms), a stagger, and two easings, `--ease-out-soft` and `--ease-in-soft`. Its reduced-motion block sets the tiers to 0 ms.
- `.agents/skills/pr-review/audits/demo-surface-motion-audit.md` gates the lines a pull request touches on a product surface. Lines no pull request touches keep the debt, which is why this ticket exists.
- `--portal-transition-duration` drives the Portal's layout transitions (padding, sizes) and its hover colour changes alike.

## The Fix

1. Alias `--portal-transition-duration` to a motion tier in both theme `Viewport.scss` files, so the reduced-motion collapse reaches it. Layout movement fits `--motion-panel`.
2. Replace each easing literal under `resources/scss/src/apps/portal/` with `var(--ease-out-soft)`, or `var(--ease-in-soft)` for an exit.
3. Move hover colour changes to `--motion-fast`, the tier for micro-feedback, instead of the layout duration.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] No `transition` declaration under `resources/scss/src/apps/portal/` carries a duration or easing literal; each reads `var(--…)` tokens only.
- [ ] `--portal-transition-duration` resolves to a `--motion-*` tier in both themes.
- [ ] With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` emulated in the browser, one Portal transition's computed `transition-duration` is `0s` (an E2E assertion, or a receipt in the PR body).

## Out of Scope

- New animations or choreography in the Portal.
- The motion lint that would retire the audit (#14780's retirement trigger).
- Stylesheets outside `resources/scss/src/apps/portal/` and the two theme `Viewport.scss` files.

## Avoided Traps

- **Deleting the Portal token.** Many rules read `--portal-transition-duration`; aliasing it keeps them and moves the timing into the vocabulary.
- **One tier for everything.** Hover feedback and layout movement are different tiers in `_motion.scss`.
