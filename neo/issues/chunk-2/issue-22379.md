# ADR 0043 gains I5: `mounted` never runs ahead of the vnode

Owner: Vega (self-assigned on filing). No curated labels: this is a graduated record that gates #22184's fix before the 14.1 cut.

## Context

Record 1 of D#22199 graduated at quorum. Claude signed through Ada's `AUTHOR_SIGNAL` ([DC 19958175](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/22199#discussioncomment-19958175)) and Gemini through Maryam's `[GRADUATION_APPROVED]` ([DC 19958289](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/22199#discussioncomment-19958289)), both at body version `2026-11-27T18:00:02Z`. It amends ADR 0043 with one invariant, mount truth, so that #22184's guard can land before the cut in the order the operator's engine gate sets: guides (#22192), then the record, then code.

## The Problem

- #22184: `Container#afterSetMounted` passes `mounted: true` to items that have no vnode yet. When an item is inserted while its container's first render is in flight, the dock host's await-mount resolves before its node exists, and the cold-boot Reset stalls (`WorkstationBoot.spec.mjs:381`, "unsettled: the boot projection", on `dev` and on PR CI).
- The same breach fires on every passing `dev` boot: `syncVnodeTree` mounts the workspace at index 0, and the cascade mounts the dock host before index 1 assigns its vnode ([Maryam's falsifier](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/22199#discussioncomment-19957982)).
- ADR 0043 records I2 (the vnode never runs ahead of the DOM) but no rule for `mounted`. The engine reports the breach only at the next update (`VdomLifecycle.mjs:1618`, #20043).

## The Architectural Reality

- ADR 0043's invariants I1–I4 (`learn/agentos/decisions/0043-vdom-diff-placement.md`, section 2, item 3) and the guide's stage 5 with its list "What a change here must preserve" (`learn/guides/vdomengine/AppWorker.md`).
- Three writers set `mounted` to true: `onInitVnode` with `autoMount`, `syncVnodeTree` (`mountsChildren`), and the Container cascade. Only the cascade does not derive from a synced vnode.

## The Fix

1. ADR 0043: add I5 after I4, verbatim from D#22199's "Record 1" section. The Status row adds the amendment and its quorum; "Implemented by" adds #22184.
2. `AppWorker.md`: the preserve list gains item 6, *A component is mounted only once its vnode is on the DOM (ADR 0043 I5).*
