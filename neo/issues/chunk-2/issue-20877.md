# A scheduler: resources by time on the multi-body grid

## Context

Leaf of #20874. It adds the scheduler on top of the timeline family and is the base of the Gantt.

## The Problem

The engine cannot show who or what is booked when. An app that plans rooms, machines or people writes its own resource rows and time track, and its bookings are not records a second view can follow.

## The Architectural Reality

- The scheduler is a grid container: resources are rows of the locked body, and the center body holds one timeline column whose cells render the bookings of that resource (`src/grid` multi-body, the timeline leaf's column and axis).
- Bookings are records of the timeline's event store; overlapping bookings of one resource are packed into lanes.
- Move, resize and keyboard rescheduling commit one record change.
- Styles under `resources/scss/src/scheduler` plus theme folders; this leaf brings the two neo themes.
- Guards: file size, ticket archaeology, class-module-scope lint, JSDoc with `@summary`.

## The Fix

Add the family under `Neo.scheduler`: the container and its event drag zone. The implementation exists and lands in one pull request with its unit specs, its styles and one example, `examples/scheduler/basic`, listed in the examples index.
