# The home's first screen holds the category line and names three doors

Leaf of #20682. It builds forks S1, S2 and S6 as decided there ([comment 6879039842](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20682#issuecomment-6879039842)), for the cut of Mon Nov 23.

## Context

The 13.5 gate reads: "someone who has never seen Neo can say after the home section what it is and who it is for". The steward's design read of Nov 10 ([comment 6756557699](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20682#issuecomment-6756557699)) measured the first screen on neomjs.com (v13.4.0, headed Chromium, 1440×900 and 390×844).

## The Problem

A visitor's first ten seconds:

- **The headline shows five different phrases.** Each of the 28 stands 1.42 s and morphs 0.58 s. "An Agent Institution", the category, gets the same 1.4 s as "Conversational UIs".
- **Nothing says whom Neo is for.** The three audiences of the README's "Who This Is For" do not appear on the page.
- **"Get started" opens `/learn`**, which lands on the 60 KB essay "What Is Neo.mjs?". `/learn/gettingstarted/Setup` exists and is not linked from the first screen.

## The Architectural Reality

- `apps/portal/view/home/parts/hero/Content.mjs`: a vbox with the logo, a `MagicMoveText` (`reference: 'magic-move'`, 28 `cycleTexts`, `autoCycle` switched in `activate()` and `deactivate()`), the hero sentence as `h3`, and the button group (`Get started` with `route: '/learn'`, `View on GitHub`).
- `src/component/MagicMoveText.mjs`: `autoCycleInterval_: 2000`, `transitionTime_: 500`. It has no config for a longer first stand.
- The band under the buttons is empty, about 180 px at 1440×900.
- The hero sentence is the README's. `apps/portal/index.html` and `seo.json` carry the same words (`learn/agentos/decisions/0018-neo-identity-source-of-truth-model.md`).
- The app-work gate of `AGENTS.md` applies to `apps/**`: read the siblings first, styles stay in SCSS.

## The Fix

1. **The category line holds.** On arrival "An Agent Institution" stands still for about five seconds. Then the rotation runs as today; the other 27 phrases and their timing do not change. Whether the hold is a `MagicMoveText` config or lives in the hero is the builder's choice; an engine config needs its own JSDoc and a unit spec.
2. **Three doors under the buttons**, one line each, in the Portal's own type:

   | Door | Candidate line | Opens |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | **Building an application?** A complete engine for every desktop and mobile web app: 461 classes, 100,000-row grids. `npx neo-app@latest` | scrolls to the Body's first page |
   | 2 | **Running coding agents or an agent team?** Persistent memory, review across model families, a record anyone can read. | scrolls to "The Agent OS Brain" |
   | 3 | **Studying the model?** What Is Neo.mjs? | `/learn/benefits/Introduction` |

   The copy is a candidate. Its numbers are the README's on the day of the build, and the steward settles the wording on the pull request.
3. **"Get started" opens `/learn/gettingstarted/Setup`.**
4. **On a phone** the doors are whole on the first screen, or they are the first thing after the fold.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Measured the way the design read measured it (every animation frame, a cold and a warm load): the first phrase stands still 5 s ± 0.5 s from first visible, and the later phrases keep 1.4 s ± 0.1 s.
- [ ] The three doors are text in the DOM, each opens its target, and no target answers 404.
- [ ] "Get started" opens `/learn/gettingstarted/Setup`. A unit spec under `test/playwright/unit/apps/portal/view/home/` pins that route and the three targets.
- [ ] **Look.** At 1440×900 the logo, the category line, the hero sentence, both buttons and all three doors stand above the fold, with at least 24 px under the last door, and no door wraps beyond two lines. At 390×844 the fold cuts no door, and each door is at least 44 px high to the touch. The doors read as the page's own: its type and its blue, no cards, no boxes. The design seat reads the frames with a cold reader who sees the first screen for ten seconds and is asked what Neo is and whom it is for.
- [ ] The pull request shows before and after at both viewports and in both themes, captured from the running Portal on the branch. The operator selects on these frames.
- [ ] The hero sentence, the 28 phrases and `apps/portal/index.html` are byte-identical to `dev`.

## Out of Scope

The pages below the first screen (S3, S4 and S5 of #20682 get their own leaves). "Meet the Team" (S7), which is the operator's word. The Slack and Discord links: they stay (the operator, Nov 14). The snapshot's markup and metadata (#20671). A new headline, and any change to the hero sentence.

## Avoided Traps

- **A new tagline.** The trouble is the time the category gets, not its wording.
- **Doors as cards with icons.** The first screen has no room for them, and the page speaks in type on a canvas.
- **A niche as door 1** ("for trading platforms"). The README's claim is every application, with extreme scale on top.
- **A hold that slows the later phrases.** The rotation is the page's signature.
