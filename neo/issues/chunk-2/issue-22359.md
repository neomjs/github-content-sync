# A Material light and dark theme on the design system's roles

Owned work, so no curated labels: it is in the 14.1 cut, it is blocked on leaves of the design system, and a checked draft exists at the filing seat.

## Context

`ROADMAP.md` (Engine 14.1): "A Material light and dark theme and the first adaptive mobile components [...] ship in this cut only if the design system and the gesture events are merged with time to spare, and lead the next one otherwise." ADR 0044 §4 adds that the pair does not wait for the palette track. The record's second revalidation trigger is this ticket's question: can a second theme say its language by re-valuing the role names and adding per-widget files?

On a specimen the answer is yes: the [gap list](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295#issuecomment-7218019616) against Material 3's token export, the [first specimen](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295#issuecomment-7218165243), and the [design seat's decisions](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295#issuecomment-7218629885), which took three names into the role list and made a palette's lightness numbers the theme's own. This ticket lands the pair on running widgets.

## The Problem

Neo ships no theme in a second design language. The roadmap names one for this cut or the next, and until it runs, ADR 0044's central promise (every theme defines the same names, so an override written once works on every theme) rests on hand-written markup.

## The Architectural Reality

- **Owner.** ADR 0044, decision 5: `resources/css/theme-<name>/Global.css` holds palette and roles, light and dark in one file; `theme-<name>/<pkg>/<Cmp>.css` exists only where the theme deviates; component variables are declared once, in `theme-base` (decision 4).
- **What exists.** `dev` at `7cb40d9bfe` has five theme folders (`theme-cyberpunk`, `theme-dark`, `theme-light`, `theme-neo-dark`, `theme-neo-light`), no `theme-base`, and no `Global.css` that declares a palette. The role list, theme Neo's `Global.css` and `check-theme-palette` arrive with the first blocker (its pull request is a draft at `24d1ecce37`); `theme-base` starts with the second.
- **How an app uses a theme.** It lists theme classes in `neo-config.json` (`"themes": [...]`) and puts one on a container; `Neo.button.ThemeSwitcher` switches between the listed ones. The theme build writes `resources/theme-map.json` from each folder's `Global.css`, so the map needs no entry by hand.
- **Where a theme class is still named by hand.** `git grep -n neo-theme-cyberpunk 7cb40d9bfe -- src buildScripts examples/ConfigurationViewport.mjs` names the newest theme in two label maps (`src/button/ThemeSwitcher.mjs`, `examples/ConfigurationViewport.mjs`), four theme-to-scheme maps (`src/component/Canvas.mjs`, and `AmChart.mjs`, `Mermaid.mjs`, `MonacoEditor.mjs` under `src/component/wrapper/`), the equality run's theme list (`buildScripts/util/themeEqualityMatrix.mjs`) and the build's option list (`buildScripts/README.md`). An example offers a theme only if its own `neo-config.json` lists it.
- **The gap that sets the scope.** With `theme-base`, a theme is one `Global.css` plus deviations. The specimen names four cues by which a page reads as Material. The tinted neutral and the tonal containers come from `Global.css`; the pill button and the filled field need a per-widget file each.
- **I5 has no guard yet.** ADR 0044 gives the comparison of names between themes to a guard of migration step 2. No script under `buildScripts/` compares the names two `Global.css` files declare, at `7cb40d9bfe` or at `24d1ecce37`: the `check-theme-*` scripts and the census were read by their summaries, and a search for a missing or added role or palette name matches only the pair check's own "a role is missing" line. This pair is that guard's first second theme. Decided by the design seat on 27 Nov: the comparison goes into `check-theme-palette` and lands with the first blocker.

## The Fix

**1. `resources/css/theme-material/Global.css`,** classes `neo-theme-material-light` and `neo-theme-material-dark`, declaring the same 194 names as theme Neo's file at `24d1ecce37` (85 palette names, 108 roles, `--neo-color-scheme`). The complete draft is in the first comment.

The eleven lightness numbers are Material's own tones:

| Grade | 50 | 100 | 200 | 300 | 400 | 500 | 600 | 700 | 800 | 900 | 950 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Material tone | 98 | 94 | 90 | 80 | 70 | 40 | 30 | 22 | 17 | 12 | 6 |
| Lightness (OKLCH) | 0.984 | 0.954 | 0.916 | 0.835 | 0.745 | 0.496 | 0.405 | 0.330 | 0.286 | 0.245 | 0.187 |

- **Ramps.** `neutral`, `accent`, `brand` and `danger` are Material 3's baseline neutral, primary, tertiary and error ramps (`material-components/material-web`, tokens `v0_192`), resampled at the table's lightness. Material defines one status ramp; `warning`, `success` and `info` take the error ramp's chroma on hues 70, 150 and 240.
- **Regions,** by tone, light | dark: canvas 98 | 6, surface 94 | 12, surface-raised 94 | 17, field 90 | 22. Dark paints Material's ladder to the digit: 0.187, 0.245, 0.286 and 0.330, with 0.405 for a selection.
- **Fills.** Accent 40 | 80, with white | tone 22 on it; hover and press 30 | 70; the tinted containers and the selection 90 | 30, where the dark selection reads `neutral`.
- **Text.** Default 6 | 90, muted 30 | 80, subtle 40 | 70, accent and status text 40 | 80.
- **Borders.** Default 80 | 30, subtle 90 | 22, strong 40 | 70.
- **Scales.** Control heights 32, 40 and 56 px; radii 4, 8, 12 and 28 px and full; Roboto; Material's state layers (8 % and 12 %) as `shade` and `tint`; its durations and easing.

**Why this table.** Four candidates were rendered beside Material 3's published values. Eleven grades cannot hold Material's three light region levels, its three fill tones, three middle tones and five dark levels at once. This table gives up tones 60 and 50 and is the only candidate that paints the dark ladder exactly. With three dark levels, as in the first specimen, a filled field has no visible fill on a card or in a dialog. Giving up tone 60 alone leaves either the card on the dialog's level, or the field without a fill in a dialog.

**What it costs,** each read from the frame against the published value (OKLCH lightness):

| Where | Draft | Material 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Light: hover and press of a filled button | 0.405, darker than the fill | 0.537, lighter |
| Light: the strong border | 0.495 | 0.567 |
| Light: a dialog | 0.954, the card's level | 0.933 |
| Dark: hover and press of a filled button | 0.745 | 0.796 |
| Dark: the strong border | 0.745 | 0.657 |
| Dark: a selected row or chip | 0.406, grey (chroma 0.010) | 0.401, tinted (chroma 0.034) |

The design seat read the frame on 27 Nov and reads none of them as a loss ([her read](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22359#issuecomment-7221262505)); the dark selection is the one she reads again on running widgets. If a frame of running widgets reads one of these as a loss, the table is cut again in the same pull request. A twelfth grade is not an option: eleven is the system's.

**2. Per-widget files, only for the two cues `Global.css` cannot carry:** the button's pill and the filled field (the field background with a bottom indicator, at the large control height). Their variable names are `theme-base`'s at that time.

**3. Registration.** The pair joins every place that names a theme class by hand (the list above, re-run on the pull request's base), with the labels `Material Light` and `Material Dark`.

**4. One shipped example offers the pair** through its `ThemeSwitcher`, and its page loads Roboto.

## Out of Scope

- The adaptive mobile components and the gesture events of Discussion `#22259`, the capstone's other half.
- The exact seven-step shape scale, the 11 px label, label tracking and an eighth slot for Material's secondary ramp: left out by the design seat's decisions until a frame misses them.
- Icons. `src` names Font Awesome classes, and a theme cannot change a glyph set.
- A font loader. A theme names a family and the page loads it.
- Per-widget refinements beyond the two cues. Each gets a leaf when a frame misses it.
- The literal radii in structural files (`#22353`) and product palettes.
- I5's guard itself. It lands with the first blocker.

## Avoided Traps

- **Wrapping Material Web Components.** This is a theme of Neo's own widgets; the closed `#5106` removed the Material component dependencies.
- **Generating the file at build time from Material's token export.** ADR 0044 rejects a generated token source: CSS is the source. The draft's generator stays out of the repository.
- **`color-mix()` for Material's state layers.** A role holds no mix, so a script can measure every pair; hover and press are grades.
- **Landing `Global.css` before `theme-base` covers the packages.** The theme map would list a class under which no component variable resolves.
