# From five merged pull requests a contributor holds two curated claims

## Context

Leaf of #20892. A contributor's standing lowers one cost for them: from five merged pull requests they hold two open curated claims at once in this repository, where everyone else holds one.

## The Problem

The claim responder treats a tenth-time contributor like a first-time one. A contributor who delivers within hours and wants a second issue while the first is in review is refused: `decideClaim` returns the "you already hold" reply as soon as one other curated issue is assigned to them.

## The Architectural Reality

- `buildScripts/util/claimResponse.mjs`, `decideClaim` (`:415`): `otherHeld` is the claimant's other open curated issues, and `if (otherHeld.length > 0)` refuses. The limit is that literal.
- `.github/workflows/claim-responder.yml` computes `heldByClaimant` from the issues assigned to the claimant and passes it in. The rule is therefore per repository.
- `decideReply` (`:474`) is the legacy entry that `neo-agent-brain` and `neo-agent-institution` call after a sparse checkout of this module.
- `CLAIM_WINDOW_DAYS = 3` (`:41`) is the claim window. It does not change here.
- Standing comes from `buildScripts/util/contributorStanding.mjs` (#20893): the rung `returning` starts at five merged pull requests.

## The Fix

1. `contributorStanding.mjs` exports `claimLimit(count)`: 1 below the `returning` rung, 2 from it.
2. `decideClaim` takes `mergedCount` (default 0) and refuses only when `otherHeld.length >= claimLimit(mergedCount)`. With the default it behaves as today, so the two legacy callers are untouched.
3. `claim-responder.yml` fetches the count with one search (`org:neomjs is:pr is:merged author:<login>`) and passes it beside `heldByClaimant`. The module stays pure.
4. The refusal says the rule in one sentence. Below five merges: "You already hold #{A}; from five merged pull requests you can hold two at once." At the limit of two: "You already hold #{A} and #{B}."
5. The merge comment of #20893 gains one sentence in its standing line at the `returning` rung: "You can now hold two curated claims at once in this repository." It ships here, in the same change as the rule, so the comment never promises a claim that `decideClaim` refuses.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: `claimLimit` with spec arms at 0, 4, 5 and 250.
- [ ] AC-2: `decideClaim` assigns a second curated issue to a claimant with five merges who holds one, and refuses a third; with four merges it refuses the second. Spec arms for each, and one for the default `mergedCount`.
- [ ] AC-3: the two refusal sentences read as above; a claimant below five learns what would change it.
- [ ] AC-4: `CLAIM_WINDOW_DAYS` and `decideReply`'s signature are unchanged. The ticket applies to `neomjs/neo` only; the other repositories keep one claim until they move from `decideReply` to `decideClaim`.
- [ ] AC-5: a failed count query falls back to `mergedCount = 0` with a warning; the claim is still answered.
- [ ] AC-6: the merge comment carries the sentence of fix 5 at the `returning` rung and at no other; a spec arm covers both.

## Out of Scope

- A longer claim window for anyone. A claim that delivers does so within hours, so a longer window only lengthens a silent hold.
- The other repositories' claim responders.
- The guide's sentence about the rule (the guide leaf of #20892).

## Avoided Traps

- **Reading standing inside the module.** The workflow fetches the count and passes it in, as it does with `heldByClaimant`, so the module needs no network in its spec.
- **Two claims across repositories.** The held issues are read per repository, so "two at once" is per repository too.
