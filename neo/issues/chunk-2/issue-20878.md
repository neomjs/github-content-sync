# A Gantt chart: a task tree with bars, dependencies and a cascade

## Context

Leaf of #20874. It adds the Gantt on top of the scheduler and the timeline.

## The Problem

The engine cannot show a plan: tasks in a hierarchy, their bars on a time axis, and what follows what. A tree grid shows the hierarchy and nothing of time or order.

## The Architectural Reality

- The Gantt's container extends the scheduler's and replaces the resource rows with a tree column over a task store (`src/grid/column/Tree`).
- Dependencies are records of their own store (from, to, type, lag) and are drawn as arrows in an overlay layer above the center body.
- A move or a resize of a task runs a topological cascade over the four dependency types (finish to start, start to start, finish to finish, start to finish) with lag, reports cycles instead of masking them, and can compute in working time through the timeline's calendar.
- A package outside this repository can attach a schedule service to the container; the view works without one.
- The container is the largest module of the four families. It meets the file-size guard (1,000 code lines) by a split, or it is enrolled in `buildScripts/util/file-size-baseline.json` with its reason.
- Styles under `resources/scss/src/gantt` plus theme folders; this leaf brings the two neo themes.

## The Fix

Add the family under `Neo.gantt`: the container, its tree column, the dependency layer, the dependency model and store, the task model and store, and its event drag zone. The implementation exists and lands in one pull request with its unit specs, its styles and one example, `examples/gantt/basic`, listed in the examples index.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The classes exist under `Neo.gantt` and appear in the output of `npm run generate-docs-json`.
- [ ] The family's unit specs pass in the engine's unit configuration, among them: a predecessor's move cascades to its successor, once in wall-clock time and once in working time; a cycle is reported.
- [ ] `examples/gantt/basic` runs in the four environments and is listed in the examples index; a linked task edited there keeps its bar and both arrow ends after the example reloads its records.
- [ ] A dependency arrow can be marked through the layer config `highlightedDependencyIds`. The class, the data attribute and the arrowhead marker it renders are named for that state (`highlighted`) and for no particular analysis; which arrows are marked is the business of the caller.
- [ ] Styles exist for `theme-neo-dark` and `theme-neo-light`.
- [ ] **Look:** the pull request carries a frame of `examples/gantt/basic` in each of the two themes, one of them with the control that starts a new dependency visible on a bar. The epic's design read (#20874) passes on them: no control in the example's own chrome is left in its default style, and every affordance of the family (bars, arrows, the control on a bar that starts a new dependency, the tree column) reads as designed in both themes.
- [ ] The file-size guard passes by a split or a recorded baseline entry; the ticket-archaeology and lint guards pass.
