# A component `touchend` listener runs twice and the tap loses its click

Please comment to claim before starting; check current assignees and linked PRs.

## Context

On a touch device, adding an ordinary `touchend` DOM listener to a component makes one tap invoke the handler twice, and the tap's native `click` no longer arrives. Emmy measured it in touch-enabled Chrome on the stock Helix. Without the listener, one native tap produced one `touchend` and one `click`. With the listener, the handler ran twice and the click was gone. Reproduction: https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/20308#discussioncomment-19930940

## The Problem

Two delivery paths serve the same event:

- When `Neo.config.hasTouchEvents` is true, Main registers `touchend` and `touchcancel` on `document.body` (`src/main/DomEvents.mjs:26-33`) and forwards each event to the App worker.
- The App-side registry's `globalDomEvents` (`src/manager/DomEvent.mjs:35-60`) lists `touchmove` and `touchstart` but not `touchend` or `touchcancel`. A component listener for either is therefore also mounted as a local native listener (`manager/DomEvent.mjs:495`, sent through `addDomListener` at `:522-526` to `src/main/DomEvents.mjs:220`).

The same `touchend` reaches the component twice. The click loss is observed; confirming its exact mechanism is part of this ticket.

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/main/DomEvents.mjs`: `touchEvents` is applied to the body on touch devices only: `touchcancel`, `touchend`, `touchenter`, `touchleave`, `touchmove`, `touchstart`.
- `src/manager/DomEvent.mjs`: `globalDomEvents` names the events Main already delivers, so no local listener is mounted for them. It includes `touchmove`/`touchstart` and misses `touchend`/`touchcancel`.
- Design authority: n/a — a reproducible defect (duplicate delivery, lost click), see Context.

## The Fix

Add `touchcancel` and `touchend` to `globalDomEvents` in `src/manager/DomEvent.mjs`, matching their body registration on Main, so a component listener is served by the body path only. Then confirm the click survives; if it does not, trace the remaining cause before widening the change.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] With a component `touchend` listener, one tap invokes it exactly once.
- [ ] The tap still produces exactly one native `click`.
- [ ] A component `touchcancel` listener is invoked once per cancel.
- [ ] A spec covers the tap case and fails on `dev`.

## Out of Scope

- `touchenter`/`touchleave`: current browsers do not fire them.
- Gesture recognition and the body listener's passive option (D#22259).
- The partial-lift `NaN` on the Helix: #22263.

## Avoided Traps

- Dropping `touchend` from Main's body list instead: `TouchDomEvents` relies on that listener to reset its anchor and to forward the event.
