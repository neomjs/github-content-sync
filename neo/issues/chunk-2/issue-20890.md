# The timeline reads to a person: editor, header, long bars, today, links

## Context

Leaf of #20874. Seen in the design read of the first example that uses the timeline family (`examples/scheduler/basic`, #20888), in `neo-theme-neo-light` and `neo-theme-neo-dark`. Points 1 to 3 sit in the timeline family on `dev` (`c19cd4934a`), not in that pull request. Points 4 and 5 were added on Nov 14 from the Look read of the Workstation's planning arrangement (#20910, comment 6870773988), where a schedule stands above a release plan seeded around today; both sit in the families on `dev` (`32fb6cb32e`).

## The Problem

1. **The editor asks for ISO strings.** Double-click a bar: "Start date" and "End date" are text fields holding `2026-11-09T02:00`. A person has to type a timestamp.
2. **The header's date row prints an ISO day key.** With no `majorFormatter`, a day reads `2026-11-09`.
3. **A long bar loses its name when its start scrolls out.** At the hour zoom, a booking that began before the visible range is a coloured block without a label, and the header's date row is empty until the next midnight scrolls in.
4. **Nothing marks today.** A plan seeded around today shows 23 or 35 day columns of equal weight, in the header and in the body. The reader has to know the date to find where the plan stands.
5. **A dependency line runs through a bar's first letter.** In the Workstation's release plan five labels are crossed: Design, Booking flow design, Account page design, Launch, Beta on phones. It happens wherever a successor starts on its predecessor's end date.

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/timeline/EventEditor.mjs:227–238`: both date items are `Neo.form.field.Text`.
- `src/timeline/TimeAxisHeader.mjs:483–516`: `formatMajorTick()` falls back to `d.toISOString().slice(0, 10)`.
- `resources/scss/src/timeline/Column.scss:80`: `.neo-timeline-bar-label` is a plain flex child; nothing keeps it inside the visible part of its bar.
- The axis already formats with `Intl.DateTimeFormat` and knows its `timeZone` (`src/timeline/TimeAxis.mjs:32`), and `dateToOffset()` already answers where an instant stands.
- No member of `src/timeline`, `src/scheduler` or `src/gantt` names today or the current time (a search for `today`, `currentTime` and `nowIndicator` finds only `src/calendar`).
- `resources/scss/src/gantt/Container.scss:108–116`: `.neo-gantt-dependency-layer` has `z-index: 4`; `resources/scss/src/timeline/Column.scss:34–50`: `.neo-timeline-bar` has `z-index: 1`, and its label starts 8 px inside the bar. So every link is drawn over every bar.
- `src/gantt/DependencyLayer.mjs:314–323` (`buildPathD()`): a finish-to-start link whose target is less than two stubs (24 px) to the right leaves its source at `r1 + 12`. When the successor starts where the predecessor ends, that vertical run stands 12 px inside every bar that starts on that date, which is where the label's first letter is.
- Design authority: n/a — a look and wording choice; no record makes the ISO forms, the missing mark or the stacking intended, and the family's JSDoc names `majorFormatter` only as an override.

## The Fix

1. The editor edits start and end with the engine's date and time fields (`Neo.form.field.Date`, `Neo.form.field.Time`) in the axis' time zone, and still commits through the owner's `commitTimelineEdit()` once.
2. The default major label is built with `Intl.DateTimeFormat` in the axis' time zone and locale (a day reads like "Mon 9 Nov"; week and month units get their own default). `majorFormatter` keeps overriding it.
3. A bar's label stays inside the visible part of the bar while the bar is partly scrolled out, and the header's date row always names the day of the leftmost visible tick.
4. The timeline family marks the current time: a line through the body at `dateToOffset(now)` and a mark on that tick in the header, for the scheduler and the Gantt chart alike. The instant is a config that defaults to the clock, so a spec and a seeded example can set it. Outside the visible range nothing is drawn.
5. A dependency line passes behind a bar instead of over it: the bars stack above the dependency layer, and the arrowhead still ends at the successor's edge. A route that never enters a bar meets the criterion as well; the frame decides, not the mechanism.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The editor shows a date and a time control for start and for end; an edit is one record change (the existing editor spec stays green, extended for the new fields).
- [ ] With no `majorFormatter`, no ISO day key appears in the header; a unit spec pins the default label per unit and time zone.
- [ ] At the hour zoom of `examples/scheduler/basic`, scrolled to 18:00, every visible bar shows its title and the header shows the day.
- [ ] With the current time inside the visible range, the scheduler and the Gantt chart show its line and its header mark; a unit spec pins the line's offset for a set instant and time zone, and one arm shows that nothing is drawn outside the range.
- [ ] In a plan where a successor starts on its predecessor's end date, no dependency line is drawn over a bar's label, and every arrowhead is visible; the existing dependency specs stay green.
- [ ] **Look:** frames of the editor, the header, a partly scrolled-out bar, the today line and a crossed-label case before and after, in both neo themes in the pull request; the epic's design read (#20874) passes on them.
- [ ] Unit specs pass in the engine's unit configuration (`npm run test-unit`), and `npm run build-themes` compiles.

## Out of Scope

Recurrence, all-day events and a date-range picker. The scheduler's own container, and the Gantt's beyond its dependency layer's stacking and route. A shared axis between two charts (the Workstation's two ranges are its own pull request's, #20910).
