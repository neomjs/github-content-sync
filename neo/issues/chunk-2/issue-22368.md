# theme-base: bind form's 12 files to roles and add track/handle roles

Owned by `@neo-gemini-maryam` on the Engine `14.1` design-system critical path (`#22295` Step 6, row `form`); carries no contributor labels.

## Context

Step 6 row `form` of Epic #22295 ([Step 6 map](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295#issuecomment-7217835670)), incorporating all seven rulings from the acting design seat's read ([#22295#issuecomment-7223146905](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295#issuecomment-7223146905)) of the initial audit ([#22295#issuecomment-7222792625](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295#issuecomment-7222792625)) plus Rule 22 ([#22368#issuecomment-7230586503](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22368#issuecomment-7230586503)). Blocked by #22351 (the `button` pilot), #22349 (the `--neo-` scripted rename), and #22350 (`theme-neo/Global.css`).

## The Problem

Measured across `resources/css/theme-neo-light/form/**` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/form/**` (`12` files, `119` custom properties each):

1. **Duplication & scheme drift:** `119` declarations are maintained twice (`83` differ between light and dark; `75` read retiring `--sem-*` or hue-ramp tokens).
2. **Sub-floor field & control contrast:** Resting field borders (`#ddd` · `#253974`) paint **`1.36:1` · `1.68:1`** on `background-field` (`< 3:1` floor). Switch's off-track (`#bdbdbd` · `#43495c`) paints `1.80:1` · `2.14:1` on canvas and its light knob on off-track paints `1.88:1`, while `Range`'s rail has no non-disabled track role.
3. **Shorthand, double-attenuation & untyped error-copy escapes:**
   - `--textfield-input-font` and `--selectfield-input-font` use a CSS `font` shorthand (`400 13px/17px Arial`), violating ADR 0044 decision 3 and hardcoding `Arial`.
   - Placeholder/hint (`0.6`) and disabled (`0.5`) opacities stack on top of `text-subtle` / `text-disabled` roles, dropping contrast below `2.5:1`.
   - `src/form/field/Text.css:170,431` and `CheckBox.css:42,59,64,91` paint 11px error copy using border-typed variables (`-border-color-invalid`, `-border-color-alert`), escaping the `4.5:1` text floor in `check-theme-palette`.

## The Architectural Reality

- Design authority: *"Every component variable is declared once, in `theme-base`, bound to roles."* (ADR 0044, decision 4); *"A role enters with its reader and leaves when it has none... Type is one variable per property, never a `font` shorthand: the `revert` contract in `src/Global.css` needs it."* (ADR 0044, decision 3); *"every custom property declared under `resources/css` starts with `--neo-`, and its second segment is `palette`, a category or a component."* ([#22295#issuecomment-7222605243](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295#issuecomment-7222605243)).
- None of `form`'s 11 component stems (`fieldset`, `checkboxfield`, `combobox`, `fileuploadfield`, `pickerfield`, `rangefield`, `searchfield`, `selectfield`, `switchfield`, `textfield`, `trigger`) collides with the 13 role categories; every name below gains `--neo-` when #22349 lands.
- Classic (`theme-light`, `theme-dark`) and Cyberpunk (`theme-cyberpunk`) sit in `neo.theme` above `neo.base` and hold zero variant re-valuing cycles under `form/**`.
- Named density debt ([#22295#issuecomment-7223146905](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/2223146905) item 7): `--neo-textfield-input-height` and `--neo-selectfield-input-height` (`25px` inner in a `27px` box, coupled to `trigger/Base.css:9`, `Search.css:10`, and `DomAccess.mjs:1117`) stay apart until the first density scope.

## The Fix

1. **Four reader-backed roles enter `resources/css/theme-neo/Global.css` + `buildScripts/util/check-theme-palette.mjs` (`3:1` floor) + `learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md`:**
   - `--neo-color-background-track: light-dark(var(--neo-palette-neutral-500), var(--neo-palette-neutral-600))` (`#7a839c` · `#666f89`; `3.62:1` · `3.83:1` on canvas) — Switch off-track & Range rail.
   - `--neo-color-background-track-hover: light-dark(var(--neo-palette-neutral-600), var(--neo-palette-neutral-500))` (`#666f89` · `#7a839c`) — Switch off-track hover.
   - `--neo-color-background-handle: var(--neo-palette-white)` (`#fff` · `#fff`; `3.78:1` · `5.00:1` on `background-track`, `5.21:1` on `background-accent`) — Switch knob & on-icon ground.
   - `--neo-color-border-strong-hover: light-dark(var(--neo-palette-neutral-600), var(--neo-palette-neutral-500))` (`5.00:1` · `5.04:1` on `background-field`, `1.31:1` step over `border-strong`, Rule 22 in [#22368#issuecomment-7230586503](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22368#issuecomment-7230586503)) — `Text` and `Select` hover borders.
2. **Split input `font` shorthands, separate error/alert copy, & expose per-side field hooks (`135` `theme-base` variables across `12` files, `0` raw colour literals):**
   - Replace `--neo-{textfield,selectfield}-input-font` with `-input-font-family`, `-input-font-size`, `-input-font-weight`, and `-input-line-height` in `src/form/field/{Text,ComboBox,Select}.css`, `theme-base`, `theme-{light,dark,cyberpunk}` (preserving today's classic/Cyberpunk values for `0` diff), `examples/desktop/basic/Notes.css:7`, and `examples/stateProvider/advanced/MainContainer.css:312`.
   - Add `--neo-textfield-error-color` (`text-danger`) and `--neo-textfield-alert-color` (`text-warning`) for `src/form/field/Text.css:170,431` and `CheckBox.css:42,59,64,91` (with matching classic/Cyberpunk declarations so AC-5 holds at `0` diff).
   - Expose per-side border-width hooks (`--neo-textfield-border-{top,inline,bottom}-width`, `--neo-textfield-border-bottom-width-active`, `--neo-selectfield-border-{top,inline,bottom}-width`, `--neo-selectfield-border-bottom-width-focus`) and `--neo-selectfield-picker-border-radius` so second-language themes (#22359) express bottom-indicator filled fields through variables alone, and guard hover border rules in `src/form/field/{Text,Select}.css` so invalid/disabled/readonly states beat hover while `Select` retains invalid-over-focus border colour.
3. **Delete `resources/css/theme-neo-{light,dark}/form/**` (`24` files)** and update `test/playwright/component/form/field/Chip.spec.mjs:318` to load `theme-neo/Global.css` + `theme-base/form/field/Text.css`.

## Out of Scope

- Moving classic or Cyberpunk `form` files onto roles (Step 10).
- Changing `25px` field inner height onto `control-height-*` (tracked as density-scope debt).
- App/example-owned CSS variables outside the two split `--textfield-input-font` overrides in `Notes.css` and `stateProvider/advanced/MainContainer.css`.

## Avoided Traps

- **Keeping `0.5` / `0.6` opacity on top of `text-disabled` / `text-subtle`:** double-attenuates contrast below `2.5:1`.
- **Keeping `font` shorthands in component variables:** breaks the per-property `revert` contract in `src/Global.css` (ADR 0044 decision 3).
- **Leaving Switch knob/track raw hex literals in `theme-base`:** violates ADR 0044 I1 (`no raw colour value outside the palette`).

**Acting design seat: a field's hover gets a step. One grade on the border, through a new role.**

Read on the frames of `ecb07244f8` and at head `0808577243`. `Full name` at rest and hovered `Country` paint the same border in both schemes, because `--neo-textfield-border-color-hover` binds `border-strong`, the rest role. Before the move, theme Neo's dark had a one-grade step and its light had none.

**Decided:**

- A new role, `--neo-color-border-strong-hover: light-dark(var(--neo-palette-neutral-600), var(--neo-palette-neutral-500))`. These are the grades of `background-track-hover`.
- Its readers: `--neo-textfield-border-color-hover` and `--neo-selectfield-border-color-hover`.

**Measured** with the palette check's `contrastRatio`, light · dark:

| Border | On the field fill | Against the rest border |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| rest, `border-strong` | 3.80 · 3.84 | |
| hover, one grade | 5.00 · 5.04 | 1.31 · 1.31 |
| hover, two grades | 6.85 · 7.17 | 1.80 · 1.87 |

The rest border stays `border-strong`: nothing inside a field's shape names it, so its edge keeps the 3:1 floor. Rule 4 of #22295 says so since tonight, and takes the outlined button off that role.

One grade is a step you see when the pointer arrives. Two grades outweigh the focus border, which sits at the luminance of `neutral-600` in both schemes and is told apart by its hue and its outline. The fill stays out of it: the border is the field's affordance, and a hover fill would need a variable the engine does not have.

The hover rule sets its border colour with `!important` (`src/form/field/Text.css:98`), and the invalid rule at `:124` has none.
