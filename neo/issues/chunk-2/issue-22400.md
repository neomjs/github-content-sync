# Deliver component gesture events and shared Neural Link replay for v14.1

Terminal predicate: In v14.1, an app author can subscribe to gestures on a component, a peer and a Playwright test can replay the same contact sequence, and the observed input and app outcome agree on the real Gallery, Helix and Circle examples while the surrounding page and DevIndex grid keep their native and intentional scrolling behavior.

## Context

The existing Engine ships native touch forwarding, per-node `domListeners`, drag phases and synthetic contact materialization. It lacks a shared semantic gesture lifecycle with explicit ownership. Two contacts can coexist after the browser has already claimed a pan. A successful dispatch alone also cannot tell a peer whether the application acted. The G2 decision attaches the contract to the subscribing component and the owning window. [Decision and author signal](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/22259#discussioncomment-19961906) · [Three-provider replay](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/22259#discussioncomment-19961766)

Decision Record: REQUIRED. Grace offered the Engine input/replay ADR. The reviewed ADR gates the public gesture API. Independent regression coverage for current Engine behavior can land earlier.

## Intended Solution

**Subscription and ownership.** Expose the six gesture families to ordinary declarative component listeners. Use terminal event names for discrete recognition and the established `drag:start/move/end/cancel` convention for phased movement. Component structural CSS declares browser policy before contact. Recognize and track active contacts on Main only on subscribed paths, including logical component ancestry. Keep a window/component/node registration lifetime with explicit removal on listener release, unmount and window departure. Gesture cancellation releases pending timers/claims and suppresses the application commit. App owns selection, form/record changes and component state. Raw inputs remain available to opt-in consumers. Budget Main→App traffic for raw-only, semantic-only, both and no subscribers; idle recognizers add no traffic. [Registration and teardown review](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/22259#discussioncomment-19961870)

**Contact and gesture behavior.** Keep active and changed contact identities, local CSS-pixel coordinates, elapsed milliseconds, velocity in px/ms, relative scale starting at 1, and signed degrees starting at 0. Press and repeated/chorded tap arbitrate with native activation, context menus and hold-to-drag. Continuous pan, simultaneous pinch/rotate, terminal directional swipe and flick share contact-lifetime rules. Rebase on partial lift and preserve the browser’s claim on a late join. Drawing may opt into device-provided pressure, pen geometry and high-rate samples. Components own snap, refresh, reveal, Undo and inertia. For a recycled slot tie actions to the original record’s identity and binding lifetime; cancellation cannot mutate a replacement record.

**Replay.** Use one bounded, timed, serializable trace and named gesture presets at both entry points. Resolve the target and geometry in its owning window. Isolate contact labels per request, validate legal starts/lifts/cancellation and clean up on interruption. Return provider, observed contacts, trust and terminal state, then inspect the App’s separate outcome. Provide Engine synthesis without attaching a debugger and browser-generated input when an appropriate provider is available. Refuse unsupported requirements. The Engine owns event construction, geometry, cleanup, and the fixture adapter. Brain exposes the public write-locked MCP action and capability/refusal contract. The checked-in witness and public MCP receipt exercise the existing raw paths; production semantic recognition has its own verification. [Cross-repository review](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/22259#discussioncomment-19961921)

**Consumers and physical acceptance.** Gallery selects one spatial neighbor per accepted swipe. Helix maps horizontal gestures to rotation and pinch to depth while vertical movement can scroll the page. Change Helix-specific structural policy and its old per-path JS cancellation together. Circle rotates about its existing bounded angle and index, supports both lift orders and permits item dragging. A second finger joining a browser-owned pan cannot start a rotation. Existing visible/keyboard/wheel controls remain usable. Compare page scroll, page pinch, both pre-contact listeners with and without default DragDrop, quick native grid strokes and held JS drag with intentional inertia on actual devices. Hold-to-drag retains its pre-contact non-passive window listener for its 200 ms delayed claim. Grid-wide no-panning requires a monitor that starts JS scrolling at contact. Match workload, power mode and travel on the DevIndex grid, including component cells. Physical iOS/Android results and message/frame measurements decide default browser-policy changes. [Quick-stroke evidence](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/22259#discussioncomment-19949561) · [Physical controls](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/22259#discussioncomment-19961924)

**Release evidence by gesture family.** Record the policy, recognition, motion and commit owners for each exercised family. Pair pan/swipe with actual Gallery selection, pinch/rotate with the actual Circle and Helix outcomes, and tap/press with native activation and the held-drag conflict. A recognizer-only unit pass leaves its consumer row open. The public replay action and fixture additionally require their own provider receipts. [Epic review and release checks](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22400#issuecomment-7228831035)
