# ROADMAP's 14.1 section cuts scope the operator always planned to ship

Owned, so no curated labels: the operator's direction to me, and I file and fix it.

## Context

The operator, in chat on 27 Nov, first: "my gut feeling: 75-100 missing PRs to reach v14.1. design system is in place, new themes definitely not material dark and light, apps/workstation light and dark theme, FM theme. native mobile events like hammer.js not done. more components for mobile not done. new examples for mobile not done. and this is not even everything. drive meaningful lanes please." Then, on the 14.1 scope: "IT WAS ALWAYS PLANNED TO FULLY INCLUDE IT. INSTEAD OF DRIVING IT, THE TEAM TRIES TO REDUCE SCOPES AND IDLES OUT FOR HOURS."

## The Problem

`ROADMAP.md`'s 14.1 section, which every seat plans against, says the opposite in two places:
- `:25`: "The cut is planned for Sunday 29 or Monday 30 November and takes what has passed its gate by then; the rest of an open track leads the next release."
- `:51`: "**Capstone, shipped on green foundations only.** A Material light and dark theme and the first adaptive mobile components … ship in this cut only if the design system and the gesture events are merged with time to spare, and lead the next one otherwise."

The Workstation and Fleet Manager themes and new mobile examples are not in the section at all.

## The Architectural Reality

Design authority: the operator's two sentences above. The cut date and the scope are his to set.

- #22237 set the 14.1 roadmap and #22278 named the design system and touch input. Both are closed; the time-boxed capstone clause is theirs.
- `:43` already treats a product look as a palette of a theme ("a product look such as Workstation's or Fleet's is a palette of a theme"). The two product themes therefore fit the design system's model as written.

## The Fix

In `ROADMAP.md`'s 14.1 section:
- `:25`: 14.1 ships its full scope. No track leads the next release by default, and the operator sets the cut date.
- `:51`: replace the time-boxed capstone with the full set, built in parallel with the foundations. The set: Material light and dark; Workstation light and dark; the Fleet Manager theme; gesture events; more mobile components, starting with a sheet that swipes and a floating action button; new mobile examples. Components start on today's Engine primitives and move to gesture events as those land.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `ROADMAP.md`'s 14.1 section names the full scope above and keeps no "only if … time to spare" or "leads the next release" clause for it.
- [ ] The cut date is left to the operator; no date is invented.
- [ ] `check-relative-links` passes.

## Out of Scope

- Re-planning other tracks, the release gate's bug set, or the milestone's issues.
- The lanes themselves, which seats claim on their own epics: #22295, #22359, D#22259, and the mobile components epic I file next.
