# The heads-up run loses accounts when GitHub refuses its searches

## Context

Leaf of #20892. `.github/workflows/contributor-heads-up.yml` (from `#20949`) tells each account of the badge ledger once. Its first rehearsal on the real runner, without `post`, was [run 43625305209](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/actions/runs/43625305209) on 2026-11-14 (18:12:38Z to 18:17:30Z, 133 accounts).

## The Problem

The rehearsal ended with 91 "would post", 8 "no outside pull request in this repository" and 34 failed. Of the failed:

- 28 failed on the search with "You have exceeded a secondary rate limit", all between 18:12:49Z and 18:13:46Z. The first came 11 s into the run.
- 6 failed on the one-page guard. They are team accounts that the ledger held by mistake (neomjs/pages#77) and are not this ticket's subject.

After that minute the same pace ran without a refusal to the end. In a posting run those 28 accounts would not be told. A second run reaches them, because a marker keeps the others from being told twice, but it reads every earlier account again.

## The Architectural Reality

- The script spaces its searches 2.1 s apart: 28.6 a minute against the search limit of 30 a minute. The token is the repository's, so every other workflow run of this repository draws on the same limit.
- A refused search is not waited out or tried again. The account's row reads `failed: <message>`, a warning is written and the run goes on.
- `actions/github-script` runs with `retries: 0`, and its retry plugin leaves 403 alone by default.
- The workflow replay in `test/playwright/unit/buildScripts/util/contributorStanding.spec.mjs` passes the script's timer as a parameter, so a replay records each wait and sits none out.

## The Fix

1. **Searches run 4 s apart** (15 a minute), which leaves half the limit to the repository's other runs. About 120 accounts then take about eight minutes.
2. **A refused search waits and is tried again.** A 403 or 429 that carries a `retry-after` header or names a rate limit waits that many seconds, or 65 s without the header, then repeats the search: three more tries at most. After that the account is listed as failed, as today.
3. The replay gains two arms and its pacing arm follows the new gap.

The workflow stays one-shot; `#20953` removes it after the posting run.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: a replay arm shows a search refused once with `retry-after: 7`: one wait of 7 s, the same search again, the account's note rendered and no failed row.
- [ ] AC-2: a replay arm shows a search refused four times: the account is listed as failed with GitHub's message and the run goes on to the next account.
- [ ] AC-3: the pacing arm holds at 4 s.
- [ ] AC-4 (after the merge, once neomjs/pages#77 is on `main`, owner: @neo-fable-mnemosyne): a run without `post` lists no account as failed for a rate limit; its URL and totals go to #20892.

## Out of Scope

- The team accounts in the ledger (neomjs/pages#77).
- The posting run and its record (AC-9 of `#20949`, kept on #20892).
- The merge comment's searches. They are two per merge.

## Avoided Traps

- **Setting the action's `retries`.** Its plugin does not retry a 403 by default, and a retry without the wait GitHub asks for adds refusals.
- **Running in batches with `limit`.** Each batch reads every earlier account again, which adds searches where there are too many.
