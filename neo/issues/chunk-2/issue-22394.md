# Motion roles move from src/Global.css into each theme's Global.css

On the Engine `14.1` design-system critical path (#22295): it gates the Material pair (#22359), the first theme whose motion differs. It carries no contributor labels for that reason.

unowned-rationale: measured and ready to build; offered to the maintainers by broadcast. The acting design seat keeps her week for the binding-table reads of step 6.

## Context

Epic #22295's open list asks which sheet owns the values of the seven motion roles. Measured on `dev` at `5ff17dedc8`, after #22352 (`255e64098f`): each sheet goes through the build's `cascadeLayerPlugin`, and the values are read in Chromium on an element of each theme class, with `page.emulateMedia({reducedMotion})`. The first measurement, at the head of #22352, is on #22359 ([comment](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22359#issuecomment-7227564006)); the re-run on `dev` is unchanged.

## The Problem

`buildScripts/util/themeCssLayers.mjs` puts `src/*` in `neo.rules` and a theme's `Global.css` in `neo.roles`, the weakest engine layer. `resources/css/src/Global.css` declares the seven motion roles for every element with a theme class, so it wins. A theme that says `--neo-duration-fast: 90ms` computes `120ms`, in each of the five theme classes.

Theme Neo's seven values equal the ones in `src`, so nothing is wrong on screen today. The Material pair's values (100, 200, 300 and 500 ms, and its own curves) would never apply.

## The Architectural Reality

Design authority: ADR 0044 §2, decision 3: "**Motion keeps today's vocabulary** under role names. Its reduced-motion collapse stays in `src`, after every theme layer, so no theme can undo it."

The collapse stays in `src`. The values are roles, and each theme declares its roles. The comment above the block in `src/Global.css` still says the opposite ("declared once here for every theme root … a per-theme copy would be a divergence trap"); it was written with the vocabulary in `#14780`. Since step 5 the per-theme copy exists by rule: `[names]` in `check-theme-palette` makes every palette declare the same role names, and `theme-neo/Global.css` declares the seven.

`src` sheets read eight role names without a fallback: the scheme, the font smoothing, four durations and two easings. 162 of the 183 reads of a motion role under `resources/css` carry no fallback, and `--neo-duration-stagger` has no reader in CSS. `theme-dark`, `theme-light` and `theme-cyberpunk` declare the first two names and none of the six motion names.

## The Fix

1. `resources/css/src/Global.css`: the seven unconditional declarations leave the `:where([class^="neo-theme-"], [class*=" neo-theme-"])` rule. The reduced-motion block with its five `0ms` durations stays. The comment says what is true afterwards.
2. Every folder that `create-theme` copies as a base declares the seven in its `Global.css`, with today's values: `theme-dark`, `theme-light`, `theme-cyberpunk`, and the two old Neo folders `theme-neo-dark` and `theme-neo-light`. `theme-neo` has them.
3. A guard fails a theme class that lacks a role which a `src` sheet reads without a fallback. A declaration under a condition does not count as the theme's value. Owner: `buildScripts/util/check-theme-var-references.mjs`. Its summary promises this, and its definitions "count wherever they appear", so the collapse in `src` satisfies it today.

| Arm | Normal | `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` |
|---|---|---|
| End state, each of the five classes | `120ms` | `0ms` |
| End state, the theme says `90ms` | `90ms` | `0ms` |
| Step 1 alone, the three themes without a palette | no value for any of the seven | durations `0ms`, easings without a value |

Step 3 belongs to this leaf: on the tree with step 1 alone, `check-theme-var-references`, `check-theme-palette`, `check-theme-property-prefix` and `check-theme-value-files` all exit 0.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1 `src/Global.css` declares no `--neo-duration-*` or `--neo-ease-*` name outside the reduced-motion query.
- [ ] AC-2 Without a re-valued theme, each of the five theme classes computes what it computes on `dev` at `5ff17dedc8`, in both motion preferences.
- [ ] AC-3 A theme that declares `--neo-duration-fast: 90ms` computes `90ms`, and `0ms` under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- [ ] AC-4 The guard is red on the tree with step 1 alone, naming three classes and six names each, and green on the finished tree. A declaration under a condition does not satisfy it.
- [ ] AC-5 A theme generated from each base that `create-theme` offers declares the seven (`createTheme.spec.mjs`).
- [ ] AC-6 One sentence reaches the holder of the 14.1 notes: a theme of your own declares the seven motion roles in its `Global.css`.

## Out of Scope

- A new motion value for a shipped theme: every value stays as it is.
- The Material pair's values (#22359).
- Other roles for `theme-dark`, `theme-light` and `theme-cyberpunk` (step 10 of the epic).

## Avoided Traps

- **One shared default in a roles-layer sheet.** It would be a second kind of `Global.css` for one category pair.
- **A list of theme classes in `src/Global.css` that still receive defaults.** The list would need every future theme.
- **`!important` on the theme's values.** It would also let a theme undo the collapse.
