# ROADMAP: the next cut is Engine 14, with a fifth cornerstone

## Context

`ROADMAP.md` holds the next release's scope and is the session intake for contributors and agents. Its "Next" section describes Engine 13.5 with four cornerstones.

On 2026-11-13 the epic #20874 was filed: a board, a scheduler and a Gantt chart join the engine's component families on one shared time axis, in five leaves. The maintainers decided the same day that the cut of Monday 2026-11-23 carries these families and is planned as 14.0.0. The repository owner names the release on the reading of Friday 2026-11-20.

## The Problem

- The roadmap names neither the epic nor the version. Whoever starts there does not find the largest block of the window. Until today the epic and its leaves were not in the milestone either.
- The intake paragraph invites "a one-PR leaf under one of the cornerstone epics". The four family leaves of #20874 are contributed by the maintainers in one pull request each; a contributor who reads the intake and starts a board from scratch loses the work.
- A major version has a cost the section does not state: `create-app` writes `"neo.mjs": "^13.4.0"` into every new app (`tasks/createPackageJson.mjs:50` in `neomjs/create-app`, read at `95aa784`), so `npx neo-app` installs 14 only after a create-app release.

## The Architectural Reality

- `ROADMAP.md`, section "Next: Engine 13.5 — The Front Door": the heading, the thesis paragraph, the gate, the cornerstone table (four rows), "Carried in the milestone", "How it runs", "Deferred" and "Session intake".
- #20874's body opens with a `Terminal predicate:` line. The table's "Done signal" column summarizes that line for each row.
- The `update-roadmap` workflow's core rule: a roadmap is a thesis, the cornerstones and an explicit deferred set; the full work set is the milestone's linked items and is never listed in prose.
- Milestone 14 holds #20874 and its five leaves since 2026-11-13T15:06Z. The roadmap refers to the milestone by number, never by title.

Design authority: n/a — the ticket calls no existing behaviour wrong; it brings a planning document in step with a filed epic and a release decision.

## The Fix

In `ROADMAP.md` only:

- The heading and the thesis paragraph name Engine 14 and say why: three planning views and their time axis join the component families.
- One paragraph says how the number is confirmed: the four families are on `dev` by Thursday 2026-11-19, 24:00 UTC; on Friday 2026-11-20 a fresh `npx neo-app` app on a packed build of `dev` builds its themes, moves a card, reschedules a booking, edits a linked task and finds its own records again after a reload; a family that misses rides the next cut; create-app is released on cut day, after the engine is on npm.
- The cornerstone table gains row 5: #20874, steward Clio, a done signal that summarizes the epic's terminal predicate.
- "Deferred" gains the calendar's move onto the shared time axis and server-backed loading for the new families.
- "Session intake" says that the four family leaves are maintainer lanes, and that themes, examples and guides open under the epic as each family reaches `dev`.

## Out of Scope

- The gate sentence and the cadence paragraph's throughput figure.
- The epic's leaves, their owners and their bodies.
- create-app's own change and the release notes; both belong to the cut.

## Avoided Traps

- **A second "Next" section for 14 beside 13.5.** The roadmap holds one next release; a run replaces the scope, it never appends one.
- **Listing the five leaves in prose.** The milestone holds them, and a prose list goes stale with the first split.
- **A fallback name written into the roadmap.** The number is confirmed on the reading; the roadmap says what follows for a family that misses, not what the release is then called.
