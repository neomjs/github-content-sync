# Recognize continuous pan and terminal directional swipe

## Context

[Gallery’s browser experiment](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/20308#discussioncomment-19930940) shows a horizontal release can advance one neighbor while vertical movement remains native. [GridDragScroll](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/5ff17dedc8/src/main/addon/GridDragScroll.mjs) already owns held dragging and intentional inertia. The missing reusable behavior is motion and release recognition for subscribed components.

## The Fix

Implement phased pan and a single terminal swipe/flick on the shared contact lifecycle. The ADR binds axis/direction, local start/current center, step and total translation, elapsed time, and velocity units. The consumer declares its policy before contact and chooses its application action.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1 Distinguish a continuous pan, a slow drag, and a qualifying directional swipe by the configured thresholds. Preserve finite values for equal timestamps and make short interrupted paths cancellable.
- [ ] AC-2 On an opted-in surface observe start, move and one terminal phase. Native scroll or a canceled contact produces no swipe commit. Report a direction and velocity for a successful terminal recognition.
- [ ] AC-3 Test the subscribed and unsubscribed paths. Confirm no second motion writer or inertia loop is introduced into the existing Grid drag path.
