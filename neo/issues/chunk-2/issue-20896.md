# GraphScene's wheel zooms toward the pointer

## Context

`Neo.canvas.GraphScene` (`src/canvas/GraphScene.mjs`) draws a graph on a WebGL2 canvas inside the canvas worker, with an orbit camera: a primary-button drag orbits, the wheel zooms, and `flyTo` eases the camera to a node. Its first consumer is the Fleet Manager's Observatory in `neomjs/neo-agent-institution`, which draws a read of about 141,000 nodes.

Two walks of that view by its operator ended on the same sentence. On 2026-10-16: "at maximum zoom-in the nodes are still almost pixels". On 2026-11-13, after the near level had shipped (#20312): "deepest zoom level: nodes still 'pixels' => not helpful to see what is there." The second walk's receipt is [neomjs/neo-agent-institution#485](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-institution/issues/485#issuecomment-6862535972).

## The Problem

The wheel changes one number. `onWheel` scales `camera.dist` and leaves `camera.target` alone, and no gesture moves the target; only `flyTo` does. So a viewer who wheels in travels toward the centre of the scene's bounding sphere, wherever the pointer is. At the wheel's limit the frame shows what lies around that centre, and the cluster the viewer was pointing at has left it.

Measured on the 2026-11-13 walk through `readStats`: the distance went from 5.546 to 0.556 (the limit, `zoom.min` 0.1) and `camera.target` was identical before and after.

The near level's marks and names exist at that limit, so a viewer meets them only at a place they did not choose.

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/canvas/GraphScene.mjs`: `onWheel` (`:1222`) is the whole wheel behaviour; the camera is `{dist, pitch, target, yaw, touched}`; `pick({x, y})` already answers the node under a canvas position; `flyTo` and `static flightStep` are the precedent for moving the target and for a pure, testable camera helper.
- `src/main/DomEvents.mjs` (`:928` onward) builds the wheel report the worker receives.
- Design authority: n/a — this adds a motion and calls no existing behaviour wrong. The consumer's design read asks for it in these words: "the wheel zooms toward the pointer, not the sphere's centre" ([neomjs/neo-agent-institution#312](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-institution/issues/312#issuecomment-6222093228)). #20312 and #20286 both list it under Out of Scope as a later leaf.

## The Fix

When the wheel turns, the point of the scene under the pointer stays under the pointer while the distance changes. `camera.target` moves together with `camera.dist`, so that the world point on the target's depth plane that projects to the pointer keeps its canvas position. Zooming out reverses the move. With the pointer at the canvas centre the behaviour equals today's.

A pure static helper beside `flightStep` computes the new target from the camera, the pointer's canvas position and the old and new distance, so it is tested without WebGL. A config turns the behaviour off for a consumer that wants the centre zoom back.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A unit arm on the helper: with the pointer at the centre the target does not move; with the pointer off the centre, the world point under it projects to the same canvas position before and after a step, zooming in and zooming out (red on `dev` first).
- [ ] A unit arm: `zoom.min` and `zoom.max` still clamp the distance, and at a clamp the target stops moving.
- [ ] The wheel still ends a flight, sets `camera.touched` and requests one frame.
- [ ] The wheel report reaches `onWheel` with the pointer's canvas position. If it does not carry one today, `src/main/DomEvents.mjs` adds it.
- [ ] The JSDoc of `onWheel`, of the helper and of the class header's camera paragraph says what the wheel does.
- [ ] (post-merge, consumer) On the installed Fleet Manager the operator wheels toward a well he points at and reaches its nodes; the receipt goes on neomjs/neo-agent-institution#312.

## Out of Scope

- A double-click that flies to the picked node, a pan gesture, keyboard zoom and the canvas hover halo. Each is its own leaf.
- The near level's sizes, names and thresholds.

## Avoided Traps

- **Flying to the nearest node under the pointer on each wheel step.** The camera would jump from node to node. Zooming toward the pointer's point on the target plane is continuous and needs no pick.
- **Doing it in the consumer.** The camera lives in the canvas worker; a round trip to the App Worker per wheel step trails the frame, the reason #20286 kept labels in the worker.
