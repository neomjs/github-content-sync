# Add Portal home AudienceDoors stage and shared glass-card tokens

Graduated leaf of #20682 from [Discussion #20950](https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/20950) (`Portal Home First & Second Screen Design: Iconic Hero vs. Audience Doors Stage`), sequenced after #20946 (PR #20965) for the `2026-11-23` Engine 14 front-door cut.

## Context

The Engine 14 gate (`ROADMAP.md` Cornerstone #3, #20682) requires that someone who has never seen Neo can say after the home section what Neo is, who it is for, and what to do next, and that each audience in `README.md` (`## Who This Is For`, `README.md:168-176`) finds its door with one existing receipted proof and one next step.

`Fork S2` (`#20682#issuecomment-6879039842`) originally placed those three doors on Screen 1 directly beneath `[Get started]` and `[View on GitHub]`. Live-rendering that prescription exposed five compounding hierarchy, measure, figure-ground, copy-repetition, and negative-space failures (halted pre-commit on operator Lead-Designer review; documented in [Discussion #20950 §1](https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/20950)). Across two `/peer-role` divergence reads (`DC_kwDODSospM4BK1FG` by `@neo-opus-vega`, `DC_kwDODSospM4BK1Kd` by `@neo-fable-clio`), an 8-point `§5.2` `STEP_BACK` sweep (`DC_kwDODSospM4BK1g6` by `@neo-opus-vega`), and 3-family graduation consensus (`gemini` + `claude` `DC_kwDODSospM4BK1o9` + `gpt` `DC_kwDODSospM4BK1u9`), `Fork S2` on Screen 1 was withdrawn (#20946 narrowed to `S1 + S6` in PR #20965) and the audience doors converged into this dedicated Screen 2 stage leaf.

## The Problem

On `dev` today:

1. **No audience routing or Institution ↔ Engine bridge on Home:** Screen 1 (`apps/portal/view/home/parts/hero/Content.mjs`) presents `MagicMoveText` + the `README.md` hero sentence (`"Neo.mjs is an agent institution..."`), then Screen 2 jumps straight into `Portal.view.home.parts.Colors` (`"Amazing Potential · Shared Socket Data"`). Neither screen tells a newcomer whom Neo is for or bridges the Agent Institution on Screen 1 to the multi-threaded Application Engine (`src/`) and Agent OS (`neomjs/neo-agent-brain`) below.
2. **Existing `.portal-glass-card` door styling is trapped under Connect:** `apps/portal/view/services/Container.mjs:72-155` already establishes Portal's door-card anatomy (`.portal-glass-card`: frosted `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, `border-radius: 16px`, headline + paragraph + link row), with its first card already titled `"Build an application"`, but `resources/scss/src/apps/portal/services/Container.scss:3,34-104` nests `.portal-glass-card` inside `.portal-services-component` and `resources/scss/theme-neo-{dark,light}/apps/portal/services/Container.scss:1-11` name its theme tokens `--portal-services-*`.
3. **Home parts have no stable `reference` keys, and the `840px` snap-deck boundary is a single-file literal:** `apps/portal/view/home/MainContainer.mjs:52-60` mounts sections without `reference` keys, while `resources/scss/src/apps/portal/home/MainContainer.scss:4-9` enables `scroll-snap-type: y mandatory` above `840px` (`min-height: 100%; scroll-snap-align: center` in `home/parts/BaseContainer.scss:1-3`) and unsets snap at `<= 840px`. At narrow snap-active viewports (`900×700`), a taller wrapped 3-card stage with `scroll-snap-align: center` can clip its top/bottom edges unless guarded.

## The Architectural Reality

- **Per-class CSS loading (`src/main/addon/Stylesheet.mjs:94-120` & `buildScripts/build/themes.mjs:181`):** Neo loads one CSS file per instantiated class (`Portal.view.Viewport` → `apps/portal/Viewport.css`), while `_partial.scss` files (leading underscore) are skipped from standalone CSS output and consumed via `@use`. `resources/scss/src/apps/portal/Viewport.scss:57-125` + `resources/scss/theme-neo-{dark,light}/apps/portal/Viewport.scss:1-7` already host Portal's cross-view shared selectors (`.portal-shared-background`, `.portal-content-box`) inside `.neo-viewport`. Lifting `.portal-glass-card` and its renamed `--portal-glass-card-*` tokens into `Viewport.scss` makes them available to both `Portal.view.services.Container` and `Portal.view.home.parts.AudienceDoors` with zero extra network requests and zero orphan SCSS files.
- **Structural Pre-Flight (Stage 1 Fast-Path):**
  > *"Pre-Flight (structural fast-path): authoring `apps/portal/view/home/parts/AudienceDoors.mjs` matches sibling pattern of `apps/portal/view/home/parts/Features.mjs` and `apps/portal/view/home/parts/AiToolchain.mjs` in `apps/portal/view/home/parts/` (extending `Portal.view.home.parts.BaseContainer` and mounted in `Portal.view.home.MainContainer`); sibling-file-lift applies; no novel directory choice."*
- **Home deck & breakpoint source:** Extracting `$portal-home-snap-max-width: 840px` into `resources/scss/src/apps/portal/home/_breakpoints.scss` lets `MainContainer.scss`, `AudienceDoors.scss`, and any `>= 841px` Screen 1 candidate row share one compile-time breakpoint source.
- **ADR 0018 identity boundary:** Screen 1's `.neo-h3` hero sentence and 28 `MagicMoveText` phrases remain byte-identical (`learn/agentos/decisions/0018-neo-identity-source-of-truth-model.md`), and `#20946` (PR #20965) owns `hero/Content.mjs` until merged.

## Avoided Traps

- **Orphan SCSS file without a mounted Neo class:** `Neo.main.addon.Stylesheet#addThemeFiles` (`src/main/addon/Stylesheet.mjs:94-120`) loads CSS 1-to-1 per instantiated class; placing `.portal-glass-card` in an unmounted `shared/GlassCard.scss` would silently ship unstyled cards in dev/dist/esm, whereas `Viewport.scss` (`Portal.view.Viewport`) already hosts `.portal-content-box` and `.portal-shared-background` across every Portal route.
- **Keeping `--portal-services-*` token names for a Portal-wide card:** Leaves Home coupled to Connect's namespace (`DC_kwDODSospM4BK1o9` #2).
- **Prompting a scroll before asking Screen 1 discovery questions:** Contaminates Option A's matrix falsifier ("no cue that the page continues", `DC_kwDODSospM4BK1u9`).
- **Hardcoding item indices or pixel scroll offsets:** Silently breaks as soon as `S3` reorders the Body sections (`STEP_BACK` #3).
- **Asymmetric 2-column split hero (Option D) or 3 prose rows under hero CTAs (`Fork S2`):** Both falsified on rendered frames (`D#20950` §1, §3.5).
