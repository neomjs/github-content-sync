# Gantt moveTask becomes a template over protected edit seams

Slice B of #22306's criterion 3, as Clio's [design comment](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22306#issuecomment-7222112719) cuts it. Slice A merged as #22367. Owned, so no curated labels: I build it, and Clio reads the seams before the cross-family review.

## Context

`@tobiu`'s audit rated `src/gantt` 3 of 10 as Engine classes (#22306). One named reason: a subclass cannot change the clamp, the minimum duration or how the service is consulted without copying `moveTask`. At `8d0276127c` that method is 275 lines with eight exits (`src/gantt/Container.mjs:965-1239`), and Container holds 7 of the package's 15 `#private` methods.

## The Problem

- `moveTask` resolves the dates, builds the updates, refuses, consults the service, writes and publishes in one body.
- `rescheduleWorkingTime` (`:1386-1476`) repeats the write and publish tail (`:1444-1475`).
- `_movingTask` is set at four sites (`:1177`, `:1210`, `:1419`, `:1444`), each with its own `try`/`finally`.
- `#ensureKeyboardLinkStatus` (`:790`), `#motion` (`:921`), `#moveCascade` (`:934`), `#publishServiceResult` (`:1282`), `#publishTaskChange` (`:1310`, eight positional parameters), `#setKeyboardLinkSource` (`:1493`) and `#usesService` (`:1579`) are out of reach for subclasses, `Neo.overwrites` and `get_method_source`.

## The Architectural Reality

Design authority: Clio's slice B on #22306: "`moveTask` (…) keeps its signature, its return values and its events. Its body becomes six `@protected` calls". Her rule 1: "Policy is a seam, mechanism is wrapped."

- Flag-window precedent: `data.Store#asContinuation(fn)` (`src/data/Store.mjs:511`) runs `fn` under a context and restores the previous one, also when `fn` throws.
- Seam shape precedent: slice A's `Cascade#cascadeTask(succId, {…})` and `computeRequiredStartMs({…})` take one destructured object.
- `_movingTask` is read only by the chart's own store listeners (`:354`, `:447`, `:454`).

## The Fix

`moveTask` builds one `edit` object (`axis`, `changes`, `meta`, `record`, `useWorking` and the five field names, with today's fallbacks) and runs these steps:

| Step | `@protected` seam | Lines today | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| resolve the dates | `resolveTaskDates(edit)` | 976-1099 | `[startMs, endMs]`, or null |
| ↳ minimum duration | `getMinimumTaskDuration({axis, record})` | 1012 | milliseconds |
| ↳ working-time rules | `fitTaskToWorkingTime(span, context)` | 1027-1061 | a span |
| ↳ clamp to the axis | `clampTaskToAxis(span, context)` | 1063-1093 | a span |
| build the updates | `buildTaskUpdates({...edit, span})` | 1101-1149, 1159-1168 | the changed fields |
| refuse | `refuseTaskEdit({...edit, updates})` | 1155-1157, 1170-1173 | a refusal receipt, or null |
| consult the service | `isServiceTask(record, service)`, then `consultScheduleService({...edit, updates})` | 1174-1198 | the service's receipt |
| write | `writeTaskEdit({...edit, updates})` | 1200-1223 | `{cascaded, cycles, previous}` |
| publish | `publishTaskEdit({…, moved})` | 1225-1238 | `{updates, cascaded, cycles}` |

- **The consult step is a gate plus a call.** A service can return any value, so a single seam's "nothing" cannot mean "the chart writes". `rescheduleWorkingTime` uses the same gate per entry.
- **One writer of `_movingTask`:** `asTaskWrite(fn)`, shaped like `asContinuation`. The write seam and both service calls run through it. `writeTaskChanges(entries, batch)` writes `{record, changes}` entries in one `updateRows()` batch or record by record, for `moveTask`'s rollup path and for `rescheduleWorkingTime`.
- **`rescheduleWorkingTime`** writes through `writeTaskChanges` and publishes through `publishTaskEdit` with `moved: true`, as it does today; its copy of the tail goes.
- **The seven `#private` methods:** `usesService`, `publishServiceResult`, `moveCascade` and `ensureKeyboardLinkStatus` become documented `@protected` methods; `#motion` becomes `ensureCascadeMotion`, like `ensureDependencyLayer`; `publishTaskChange` takes one object. `#keyboardLinkSource` becomes a reactive `keyboardLinkSource_`: its `beforeSet` adds the bar's row, its `afterSet` writes the status text and redraws both rows (rule 2's first exception).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `moveTask`'s body is the template above; its signature, return values and events are unchanged.
- [ ] `rescheduleWorkingTime` writes through `writeTaskChanges` and publishes through `publishTaskEdit`; its write and publish tail is gone.
- [ ] `_movingTask` is set only in `asTaskWrite`.
- [ ] `grep -nE '^\s+#[a-zA-Z]+\(' src/gantt/Container.mjs` prints nothing, and `keyboardLinkSource_`'s hooks carry the status text and the row redraws.
- [ ] The Gantt unit, component and e2e specs that reach `moveTask` or `rescheduleWorkingTime` pass with their assertions unchanged.
- [ ] A differential probe runs the same random edits on base and head and compares returns, `taskChange` payloads and store state: no difference.
- [ ] New arm: a chart subclass that overrides `getMinimumTaskDuration` and `clampTaskToAxis` lands an edit differently, with no copy of `moveTask`.

## Out of Scope

- Criterion 4: replacing `_movingTask`, the `|| 'startDate'` fallbacks and the English strings.
- Slice C: the other eight `#private` methods, the statics called by class name, the two placement seams of Clio's [ruling](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22306#issuecomment-7223915139), and the subclass spec file.
- The relay `schedule` flag, the fourth slice.

## Avoided Traps

- **One write shared by planning the live cascade in pending mode:** pending mode adds rollup edges and filters derived parents, so a store without `rollupParents` would cascade differently.
- **One consult seam whose "nothing" means "the chart writes":** a service that returns `null` would be written twice.
