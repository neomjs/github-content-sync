# Lifting one of two fingers turns Helix rotation and depth into NaN

Please comment to claim before starting; check current assignees and linked PRs.

## Context

A two-finger touch leaves the Helix permanently broken. With browser-generated, trusted touch input in Chrome: put down contact 13, add contact 27, lift only 27, then move 13 again. The App worker then reports `rotationAngle` and `translateZ` as `NaN`, and every later move keeps them `NaN`. Emmy reproduced it on the stock example and published a reusable recipe (Playwright + CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`, no phone needed): https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/22259#discussioncomment-19931568. It was first found on https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/20308#discussioncomment-19930940.

## The Problem

`Neo.main.mixin.TouchDomEvents` tracks a single contact without its identifier. `onTouchEnd` and `onTouchCancel` clear `lastTouch` on every `touchend`, including a partial lift while another finger is still down. The next `onTouchMove` finds no `lastTouch`, forwards the event without `deltaX`/`deltaY`, and only then re-anchors. `Helix.onTouchMove` multiplies the missing deltas and `onMouseWheel` adds the result into `rotationAngle` and `translateZ`; `NaN` then absorbs every later delta.

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/main/mixin/TouchDomEvents.mjs`: `onTouchCancel`/`onTouchEnd` reset `firstTouch`/`lastTouch` unconditionally; `onTouchMove` computes deltas from `event.touches[0]` against `lastTouch`, with no contact identity.
- `src/component/Helix.mjs`: `onTouchMove(data)` scales `data.deltaX`/`data.deltaY` and calls `onMouseWheel`, which writes `_rotationAngle` and `_translateZ` (silent updates) from them.
- Design authority: n/a — a reproducible defect (non-finite geometry), see Context.

## The Fix

1. `TouchDomEvents`: keep the tracked contact by `identifier`. On `touchend`/`touchcancel`, clear the anchor only when `event.touches.length === 0`. When the tracked contact lifts while others remain, re-anchor to a remaining contact without emitting a delta for that transition; `onTouchMove` computes deltas against the same identifier.
2. `Helix.onTouchMove`: ignore an event whose deltas are not finite, so a missing delta can never poison `rotationAngle`/`translateZ`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] With the recipe above (two contacts down, lift one, move the other), `rotationAngle` and `translateZ` stay finite, and the first move after the lift moves them by no more than one move's delta.
- [ ] A one-finger drag still rotates and zooms the Helix as before.
- [ ] A spec reproduces the partial lift and fails on `dev`; a Playwright test can drive it with `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`, as the recipe does.

## Out of Scope

- A gesture recognizer, pinch or rotate semantics, or a public touch API: D#22259 owns those.
- The body-level `touchmove` listener's passive option (proposed as a measured experiment on D#22259).
- The double `touchend` delivery to component listeners: #22264.

## Avoided Traps

- Guarding only in Helix: other consumers of the forwarded deltas would still receive events without them.
- Re-anchoring to `touches[0]` after a lift: the order of `touches` is not tied to the tracked finger; use `identifier`.
