# Calendar week numbers drift by a week with time zone and clock

Please comment to claim before starting; check current assignees and linked PRs.

## Context

The calendar's Year view labels each week row with `DateUtil.getWeekOfYear(weekDate)` (`src/calendar/view/YearComponent.mjs:628`). Rendered on `dev` (`4d5236b292`) in headless Chromium, `examples/calendar/year` with `weekStartDay: 1`, November 2026:

| Row starts | Shown | ISO 8601 |
|---|---|---|
| Mon Oct 26 | 45 | 44 |
| Mon Nov 2 | 46 | 45 |
| Mon Nov 9 | 47 | 46 |
| Mon Nov 16 | 48 | 47 |
| Mon Nov 23 | 49 | 48 |
| Mon Nov 30 | 50 | 49 |

With the default `weekStartDay: 0` the labels are right until the evening west of UTC: run with the view's own arithmetic in `America/New_York`, a calendar opened at 23:30 labels every row with the next week (46–50 instead of 45–49).

## The Problem

Two defects meet.

1. `getWeekOfYear` (`src/util/Date.mjs:114-128`) takes the weekday from `getUTCDay()` and steps with `setUTCDate`, while callers pass local dates, so the answer depends on the zone and the clock. In Node, Monday 2026-11-23 (ISO week 48):

| `TZ` | 00:30 | 08:30 | 12:00 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Europe/Berlin` | 47 | 48 | 48 |
| `Asia/Tokyo` | 47 | 47 | 48 |
| `America/New_York` | 48 | 48 | 48 |

   Sunday 2026-11-29 at 23:30 in `America/New_York` gives 49 (ISO 48).
2. The Year view asks for the week of the row's first day plus seven days (`YearComponent.mjs:617-618`, `:632`). For a Sunday row that lands on the next Sunday, which shares an ISO week with the row's Monday to Saturday; for a Monday row it lands on the next Monday, one week too far. The two errors cancel only for Monday rows east of UTC in the small hours.

## The Architectural Reality

- `test/playwright/unit/util/Date.spec.mjs:59-67` pins `getWeekOfYear` with `new Date(Date.UTC(...))` inputs, which give the same answer in every zone. No case passes a local date, which is what the calendar does.
- The timezone runner exists (`#19005`): `test/playwright/playwright.config.unit-tz.mjs` runs `util/Date.spec.mjs` under `Pacific/Kiritimati` (+14), and CI also under `Pacific/Niue` (−11) via `NEO_UNIT_TZ`.
- `weekStartDay` comes from the calendar's state provider (`src/calendar/view/MainContainerStateProvider.mjs:104`, default `0`).

## The Fix

1. `getWeekOfYear`: compute the ISO week of the date's local calendar day (`getFullYear`, `getMonth`, `getDate`, `getDay`), so neither the zone nor the time of day matters, and say so in its JSDoc.
2. `YearComponent`: label each row with the ISO week of its fourth day (row start plus three days). That is the week holding most of the row's days, for any `weekStartDay`; for Sunday rows it matches today's labels.
3. Specs: local-date cases in `Date.spec.mjs` at 00:30 and 23:30, and a Year view case for `weekStartDay` 0 and 1, at the level you choose (a unit test of a label helper, or a component spec).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] New `Date.spec.mjs` cases with local dates at 00:30 and 23:30 fail on `dev` under `playwright.config.unit-tz.mjs` (both `Pacific/Kiritimati` and `NEO_UNIT_TZ=Pacific/Niue`) and pass on the head (receipt in the PR).
- [ ] The Year view labels November 2026 with 44–49 for `weekStartDay: 1` and 45–49 for `weekStartDay: 0`, independent of zone and clock; a spec pins both.
- [ ] The existing `Date.spec.mjs` cases still pass.
- [ ] Both unit configs pass: `playwright.config.unit.mjs` and `playwright.config.unit-tz.mjs`.

## Out of Scope

- Week numbering other than ISO 8601 (for example US week numbers); `getWeekOfYear` stays ISO.
- Other calendar views and `DateSelector`.
- The local/UTC conversions `#19005` already covers, such as `convertToyyyymmdd`.
