# A live proxy's drag end paints its item at the layout start for one frame

## Context

At the end of a live-proxy sort drag, the dragged item is painted at the start of its owner for one frame before it reaches its slot.

- **Measurement** (Vega's, not re-measured here): a `SortZone` consumer with a `DragProxyContainer`, at `17bdb13f` (the head of PR #20862), on a horizontal zone in headless Chrome. The item was at the owner's start edge for one frame, then at its slot, in 8 of 8 gesture ends (two runs, two themes, drop and Escape). A screencast frame shows it painted. After Escape, the cancelled item is drawn over the first item. After a drop, the drop slot is empty for a frame.
- **Mechanism:** read in the code at `1b12232214` and `17bdb13f` and set out below. PR #20862 changes only the restore index, so `dev` paints the same frame.

## The Problem

`SortZone#processDragEnd` ends a live-proxy drag in three steps:

1. It awaits `Neo.applyDeltas` with the restore batch: a `moveNode` of the dragged component back into the owner, and a `removeNode` of the placeholder (`src/draggable/container/SortZone.mjs:668-695` on `dev`).
2. Only then does it clear `left`, `top` and `position` on the pinned items through `wrapperStyle` (`:709-730`).
3. A committed drop then calls `moveTo()` (`:739-756`).

After step 1, every pinned sibling is still `position: absolute`. The restored component is therefore the only in-flow child and lays out at the layout's start, whatever index it was restored to. The un-pin reaches the DOM through a worker round trip, so at least one frame paints that in-between state.

## The Architectural Reality

- **Restore:** a manual delta batch, so it bypasses the vdom.
- **Un-pin:** ordinary component updates (`src/component/Base.mjs`: `afterSetWrapperStyle` → `updateStyle` → `update()`). These reach the DOM through a round trip, not within a frame; #20057 recorded the same path for the re-show at this terminal.
- **Pinned set:** `getPinnedItems` decides which siblings stay pinned during the drag (#20605). The un-pin loop covers `pinnedItems || sortableItems`.
- **Since PR #20862,** the restore returns a committed drop to its old owner slot. `moveTo()`'s vdom update then carries it to the new slot, which is one more round trip after the un-pin.

## The Fix

Make the restore and the un-pin one paint. Either:
- send the pinned items' style resets in the same `Neo.applyDeltas` batch as the restore, keeping their `wrapperStyle` configs in step without a second update, or
- hold the restore until it can ride in the same batch as the un-pin.

The owner picks the mechanism. The invariant: no frame shows the restored item in flow while its siblings are still pinned. No new config.

Correct `processDragEnd`'s JSDoc step 1 in the same change. It says the deltas move "the DOM nodes to their final positions". Since PR #20862 they return the component to its current owner slot, and a committed drop reaches its final slot through `moveTo()`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] On a drop and on Escape, no sampled frame shows the dragged item at the owner's start. The witness runs in a real browser and samples every frame, following the sampler in `test/playwright/e2e/workstation/WorkstationTabReorderNoFlashNL.spec.mjs`. It is red on `dev` and green after.
- [ ] A committed drop to another slot shows no frame at its old slot.
- [ ] The restored DOM order still equals the owner's item order on both terminals, including with an excluded rendered sibling: the #20861 unit witness stays green.
- [ ] A committed drop still commits exactly once through `moveTo()`, and a cancel commits nothing.

## Out of Scope

- The restore index (#20861 / PR #20862).
- Window-drag and remote-drag transfer.
- Scroll geometry.
- Focus restore (#20057).

## Avoided Traps

- **A consumer-side workaround,** such as hiding the item until the un-pin lands: every consumer would copy the terminal lifecycle that #20605 / #20611 expose through hooks.
- **A fixed wait between restore and un-pin:** another clock, and the kind of race #20057 removed.
