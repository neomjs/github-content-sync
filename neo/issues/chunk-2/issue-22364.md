## Context

The 14.1 gate says that every issue open with the `bug` label on a shipped Engine or Portal surface is fixed on `dev`, or deferred in that section with its reason. The operator decides outstanding deferrals at the cut, on Sun 29 or Mon 30 Nov. The section links only the live `bug` query, so nothing records a disposition per open bug. Sophie, who drives 14.1 readiness for the operator, asked where that record is (A2A, 2026-11-27 15:32Z).

## The Problem

- Since 2026-11-24, 30 `bug` issues have closed as completed ([query](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20label%3Abug%20is%3Aclosed%20closed%3A%3E%3D2026-11-24)). Seven were open at 15:34Z; #22371 and #22376 joined the set by 17:36Z.
- Without a row per open bug, the operator decides from scattered threads. Prior art says he prefers including a fix over deferring it in a cut window (Grace's diary, 2026-11-06). So a deferral has to say why the fix cannot land, not merely that it has not yet.

## The Architectural Reality

- The authority is `ROADMAP.md` `### Release gate`. Deferrals live in that section, so the cut's record is an edit to it.
- That section already routes the scheduling pair, #20747 and #20871, to D#22199. #22184's fix waits on D#22199 too. It changes the VDOM update path, and the operator's engine gate orders such a change guides → ADR → code. So three of the rows wait on one Discussion. Vega proposes splitting it ([D#22199 comment](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/22199#discussioncomment-19957354)). Record 1, mount truth as an ADR 0043 amendment, would be decided before the cut and would let #22184's guard land. Scheduling would follow as Record 2.
