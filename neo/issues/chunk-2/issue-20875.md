# A board family: columns, swimlanes and cards on one store

## Context

Leaf of #20874. It adds the first of four families: a kanban board. It depends on none of the others.

## The Problem

The engine has no board. An app that tracks work in columns builds one from lists and drag zones by hand, and the result owns its data, so a second window or an agent cannot follow a move.

## The Architectural Reality

- Sibling families: `src/grid` and `src/calendar`. Where a module lands inside `src/board` (for example a `model` or `store` subfolder as in `src/calendar`) is decided by the structural pre-flight in the pull request.
- Data: one `data.Store` of card records; a column is a predicate over a field of those records, never a list of its own.
- Drag: reuse the `Neo.draggable` sort zones and the per-window drag coordinator on the main thread (`Neo.main.addon.DragDrop`). It keeps one registration per drag root and one active zone per gesture, so the card zone and the column zone register side by side and keep distinct identities.
- Styles: `resources/scss/src/board` plus one folder per theme. The engine ships five themes; this leaf brings `theme-neo-dark` and `theme-neo-light`, and the other three are a leaf of their own.
- Guards the pull request meets: the file-size guard (`buildScripts/util/check-file-sizes.mjs`, 1,000 code lines), the ticket-archaeology check (no ticket numbers in source comments), the class-module-scope lint, and JSDoc with `@summary` on every class and method.

## The Fix

Add the family under `Neo.board`: a container, a column and its body, a lane row for swimlanes, a card with its items, a card model, a card editor, a selection model for cards, and two sort zones (cards and columns). The implementation exists and lands in one pull request with its unit specs, its styles for the two neo themes and one example, `examples/board/basic`, listed in the examples index.
