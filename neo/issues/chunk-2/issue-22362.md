# Engine E2E's 18 shards average 322 s planned and hit the 6-minute ceiling

Please comment to claim before starting; check current assignees and linked PRs.

unowned-rationale: Ready for a contributor; maintainers provide planning and review.

## Context

Three `e2e-engine` shards passed 100% of their selected tests on standard hosted runners and still failed the workflow's six-minute step ceiling within 24 hours:
- `e2e-engine (8/18)` on #22224 (`2026-11-26`): `71 passed` in `359s`, killed during teardown.
- `e2e-engine (7/18)` on #22344 ([run 46001646106](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/actions/runs/46001646106)): `82 passed` in `361s` (`##[error]The action 'Run e2e (engine tier)' has timed out after 6 minutes.`).
- `e2e-engine (9/18)` on #22358 ([run 46019416079, job 140575115241](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/actions/runs/46019416079/job/140575115241)): `117 passed (6.0m)`, `Benchmark completed in 362s`, `##[error]The action 'Run e2e (engine tier)' has timed out after 6 minutes.`.

## The Problem

Since #22115 raised `FULL_SHARDS.engine` from `15` to `18` at `327` specs (`5,341 s` total, `~297 s` per shard), the Engine E2E tier has grown to `338` executed specs (`333` measured in `buildScripts/util/e2eEngineDurations.json` totaling `5,595 s` plus `5` unmeasured specs planned at the 90th percentile `40.4 s` = **`5,797 s` planned total**).

Divided across `18` shards, `5,797 s / 18 = 322 s` planned per shard — past the `300 s` recount threshold documented on `FULL_SHARDS`, leaving less than `38 s` of margin beneath the `360 s` step timeout for normal hosted-runner variance.

## The Architectural Reality

- `FULL_SHARDS = {engine: 18}` in `buildScripts/util/e2eCiSelection.mjs:223` sizes partial PR selections proportionally and caps full runs; `.github/workflows/test.yml:501-502` holds the matching full-run fallback matrix (`[1..18]` and `[18]`), guarded by `test/playwright/unit/buildScripts/e2eCiSelection.spec.mjs` (*"the engine workflow's full matrix is FULL_SHARDS"*).
- `Design authority:` `"A shard planned at the run step's six-minute ceiling times out on ordinary runner variance, so the count keeps the plan near five minutes: recount once {@link ENGINE_DURATIONS} totals more than 300 s per shard."` (`buildScripts/util/e2eCiSelection.mjs:218-220`).
- Raising `FULL_SHARDS.engine` from `18` to `20` brings the planned load to `5,797 s / 20 = 290 s` per shard (restoring `#22115`'s `< 300 s` target while staying within the 20-slot concurrency guideline). Five recently landed specs (`HelixTouchNL.spec.mjs`, `EventSimulatorTouchRealm.spec.mjs`, `DemoBPopOutTwoThreadNL.spec.mjs`, `GridDragScrollTouchNL.spec.mjs`, `DirectVdomReplyNL.spec.mjs`) are also not yet in `e2eEngineDurations.json`.

## The Fix

1. Update `FULL_SHARDS.engine` in `buildScripts/util/e2eCiSelection.mjs` from `18` to `20` (or the smallest shard count keeping every shard's planned load `≤ 300 s` after refreshing `buildScripts/util/e2eEngineDurations.json` from recent full `dev` runs via `--engine-durations`).
2. Update the matching fallback matrix defaults in `.github/workflows/test.yml` (`L501-L502`) and any prose/workflow references checked by the unit suite (`learn/guides/testing/WhiteboxE2E.md`, `.github/workflows/classify-test-scope.yml` if applicable).
