# theme-base: bind grid's 13 files (64 variables) to roles

Owned by `@neo-gemini-julia` on the Engine `14.1` design-system critical path (`#22295` Step 6, row `grid`); carries no contributor labels.

## Context

Step 6 row `grid` of Epic #22295 ([Step 6 map](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295#issuecomment-7217835670)), filed per the acting design seat's leaf protocol ([#22295#issuecomment-7223146905](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22295#issuecomment-7223146905)) and updated post-#22390 (`28c9e53cf8`, which added `grid/column/component/GitHubOrgs.css` and two corner-radius variables across `VerticalScrollbar.css` and `header/plugin/Menu.css`) and post-design-read ([#22389#issuecomment-7227989668](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22389#issuecomment-7227989668)). Blocked by #22351 (the `button` pilot) and #22350 (`theme-neo/Global.css`, merged).

## The Problem

Measured across `resources/css/theme-neo-light/grid/**` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/grid/**` on `dev@68823a94c7` (`14` CSS files, `67` custom-property declarations per theme, comments stripped; positive control `--neo-grid-cell-background-color-hover`):

1. **Duplication, retiring-token reads & cross-component coupling:** `67` declarations are maintained twice (`36` differ between light and dark; `15` read retiring `--neo-sem-*` or `--neo-purple-*` tokens). Four declarations read sibling component variables directly (`--neo-tree-helper-line-color`, `--neo-grid-header-button-border-right-color`, and `-hover` reading `var(--neo-grid-container-border-color)`, and Neo Light's `--neo-grid-header-button-padding` reading `var(--neo-button-padding)`), violating ADR 0044 decision 1.
2. **Legacy 2019 classic-light holdover & scheme layout jump in `grid/header` and `grid/footer`:** Neo Light's `header/Button.css` and `footer/Toolbar.css` still carry classic-light's `#5d83a7` slate-blue fill with `#fff` text, `11px` font size, `600` weight, `justify-content: flex-end`, and `var(--neo-button-padding)`, whereas Neo Dark uses `#182449` (`--neo-purple-900`), `#f0f1f4` text, `13px`, `400`, `start`, and `0 10px`. Switching between Neo Light and Neo Dark jumps column-header horizontal alignment (`flex-end` ↔ `start`), padding, font size, and font weight.
3. **Sub-floor contrast, invisible dark cell selection & raw colour literals:** Neo Light's `--neo-icon-link-color-hover` reads `--neo-sem-color-fg-primary-highlighted` (`rgb(35.19%, 96.97%, 74.26%)` = `#5af7bd`, **`1.37:1`** on white — ADR 0044 §5); in Dark, `background-selected` and `background-accent-subtle` both resolve to `accent-900` (`#142457`, **`1.00:1`**), making an active `CellModel`/`CellRowModel` cell invisible inside its companion row/column; and `--neo-grid-cell-ismodified-color` (`orange`), `--neo-grid-scrollbar-thumb-color` (`rgba(...)`), `--neo-grid-cell-animated-*-background-color` (`rgb(... / 60%)`), and `--neo-grid-cell-progress-box-shadow` (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`) hold raw colour literals forbidden by ADR 0044 I1.

## The Architectural Reality

- `Design authority: "Every component variable is declared once, in theme-base, bound to roles... One exception to 'roles only': a component may mix a role with transparent for a translucent treatment of its own, as the desktop window's glass does."` (`learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md` §2 decision 4; I1; Epic #22295 package-move rules 1–18).
- **Pinned-column opacity invariant (`resources/css/src/grid/Body.css:85-88,112-114` & `header/Button.css:851,872-875`):** `.neo-grid-row` paints no background; center `.neo-grid-cell` and `.neo-grid-header-button` elements translate horizontally underneath `.neo-locked-start` and `.neo-locked-end` (`z-index: 5`). On `:hover`, both `.neo-grid-cell` and `.neo-grid-header-button` replace their resting `background-color`. Every cell and header fill (rest, `even`, hover, band, selected) MUST resolve to an **opaque** colour role.
- **Reader & classic/Cyberpunk audit (`dev@68823a94c7`):** All `67` variables have `>= 1` reader in `resources/css/src/grid/**` (`0` unread). Classic (`theme-light`, `theme-dark`) declare all `67` variables in `neo.theme` above `neo.base`; `theme-cyberpunk` declares `49` of `67` (missing `18` across `AnimatedCurrency`, `Icon`, `IconLink`, `Progress`, `footer/Toolbar`, and `Menu`), requiring `check-theme-var-references.mjs` to skip unshadowed `theme-base` role reads on pre-Step-10 partial themes with a Step-10 `[burn-down]` gate. Workstation's `15` `--neo-grid-*` overrides in `resources/css/src/apps/workstation/Viewport.css` sit in `neo.apps` above `neo.base`, keeping `WorkstationPaletteBridgeNL` green.

## The Fix

1. Create `resources/css/theme-base/grid/**` (`14` files) declaring all `67` variables once under `[class*="neo-theme-"]`, and delete `resources/css/theme-neo-light/grid/**` and `resources/css/theme-neo-dark/grid/**` (`28` files).
2. Step `--neo-color-background-selected` in `resources/css/theme-neo/Global.css` from `accent-900` to `accent-800` (`#233c8f`) in dark (`1.49:1` on `accent-subtle`, `8.83:1` default text; Epic #22295 rule 15), and add `[BACKGROUND + 'selected', BACKGROUND + 'accent-subtle', 1.1]` (`38` pairs) to `buildScripts/util/check-theme-palette.mjs`.
3. Extend `buildScripts/util/check-theme-var-references.mjs` with `PRE_ROLE_THEMES` + Step-10 `[burn-down]` so partial pre-Step-10 themes (`theme-cyberpunk` missing `18` `grid` variables) do not fail on unshadowed `theme-base` role reads before Step 10.

## Out of Scope

- Moving `theme-light`, `theme-dark`, or `theme-cyberpunk` `grid` sheets onto roles (Step 10).
- Renaming app- or example-owned custom properties in `examples/grid/**` (Step 6 example-group row `examples/grid`).

## Avoided Traps

- **Binding row or header hover to translucent `--neo-color-background-neutral-hover`:** `.neo-locked-start` and `.neo-locked-end` cells and headers sit at `z-index: 5` over horizontally scrolling center columns and replace `background-color` on `:hover`; a `5%` alpha fill leaks scrolling text underneath.
- **Keeping raw `rgba()` / `rgb()` / named-colour literals (`orange`, `rgba(0,0,0,.25)`) in `theme-base`:** violates ADR 0044 I1; replaced with `border-warning`, `--neo-color-shadow`, and ADR 0044 decision 4 `color-mix(..., transparent)` expressions.
- **Leaving cross-component `var(--neo-grid-container-border-color)` or `var(--neo-button-padding)` reads in `theme-base`:** violates ADR 0044 decision 1 ("a component variable reads roles only").
