# The guide explains standing and the badge; the template asks the model

## Context

Leaf of #20892. Once a merged outside pull request gets its comment (#20893) and the badges are served (neomjs/pages#73), the contributor guide has to say what a standing is, and the pull request template gets one line the reviews have been missing.

## The Problem

- `CONTRIBUTING.md` says nothing about standing, the rungs or the badge, so the comment at the merge would be the only place a contributor learns of them, and only after the fact.
- The claim sentence of the guide says "one open curated claim at a time". From five merged pull requests that becomes two (the claim leaf of #20892).
- The guide asks for the model behind an AI-authored pull request (section 5); `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md` does not. Of 82 outside pull requests since July, 25 name no model and none states the reasoning effort, so a reviewer cannot set the depth of a review by what wrote the change. Since 2026-11-14 the lab a pull request names also decides who reviews it: a maintainer on a model of another lab (neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1702).

## The Architectural Reality

- `CONTRIBUTING.md`, section "Contribute to the Neo.mjs code base", item 3 ("Claim before you start") holds the claim sentence. #20889 rewrites the section before it and adds one sentence at the labels; this ticket follows it in the same file.
- `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md` ends with "**Other information:**".
- The merge comment's words are constants of `buildScripts/util/contributorStanding.mjs` (#20893); its badge paragraph hands over the card's line only.
- `npm run check-relative-links` checks the guide's relative links.

## The Fix

1. **A section "Your standing" in `CONTRIBUTING.md`**, after item 3: the seven rungs with the merges each needs (Contributor 1, Returning Contributor 5, Bronze 10, Silver 25, Gold 50, Diamond 100, Core 250, counted across the organisation's public repositories); what the comment at a merge says; the badge in its three forms (card, plate, medal) and the account's record at `https://neomjs.com/badges/<login>-<id>/`, where the three lines stand ready to copy; what the badge proves (it is generated from GitHub's merge record and names the account); and two plain facts: deleting the line removes the badge from a profile, and an account can ask in an issue to be left out of the records.
2. **The claim sentence** says: one open curated claim at a time, two from five merged pull requests, and a claim lasts three days.
3. ~~The comment links the section.~~ Dropped on 2026-11-13: the comment links the account's record, which carries the three forms.
4. **The template** gains one line under "Other information": `**AI disclosure:**`, followed by a hint in an HTML comment that asks for the model, the agent and the reasoning effort and names no model. The line is asked and never required. The review routing reads it (neomjs/neo-agent-brain#1702), so its label is the one that reader knows, and a hint that named a model would mark every pull request whose author left the line empty. Section 5 of the guide ("Transparency") gains one sentence on what the answer is used for: a pull request that names its model is reviewed by a maintainer on a model of another lab, one that names none by any maintainer. Restated on 2026-11-14: the line was filed as `Model, agent and reasoning effort:`, which that reader would not have read.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1: the section exists with the seven rungs, the three forms, the record's address pattern, what the badge proves and both plain facts; every address in it answers 200.
- [ ] AC-2: the claim sentence states the three numbers (one, two from five, three days) and agrees with the claim responder on `dev`.
- [ ] ~~AC-3: the merge comment's badge paragraph links the section, with a spec arm for the rendered link.~~ Dropped with fix 3.
- [ ] AC-4: the template carries the `**AI disclosure:**` line, its hint names no model, no check requires the line, and section 5 of the guide says what the answer is used for.
- [ ] AC-5: `npm run check-relative-links` passes.

## Out of Scope

- The guide's doors and the label descriptions (#20889).
- The other repositories' guides.
- A promise of anything beyond the badge.

## Avoided Traps

- **Linting the model line.** A required field gets filled with noise; an asked one gets an answer from those who have one.
- **A rung's number in an address or an alt text.** A threshold may move; the names stay.
