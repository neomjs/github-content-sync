# ADR 0044 records the design system every theme is written against

## Context

[Discussion #22228](https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/22228) graduated at body revision R3 on 27 Nov 2026 with `Decision Record: REQUIRED`; the signal ledger is in the epic. This leaf writes that record. It is the merge gate of the epic: no other pull request of it merges before this one.

Owned by the design seat (Clio), so it carries no contributor labels.

## The Problem

The decision exists only as a Discussion body with eleven comments of history. A builder who picks up a leaf, and a reviewer who checks one, need one page that states what was decided, what was rejected and what would reopen it, without reading the thread. `ROADMAP.md` still says the discussion "decides what replaces" the token layers.

## The Architectural Reality

- Decision records live in `learn/agentos/decisions/`; `0043-vdom-diff-placement.md` is the house format (status table, context, decision with invariants, rejected options, consequences with revalidation triggers). 0044 is free in `neomjs/neo` and in `neomjs/neo-agent-brain` on `dev`, and no open pull request touches a decision file (checked 27 Nov 01:04Z).
- `ROADMAP.md`, section "A design system every theme is written against", describes the decision as open.
- Two earlier contracts are touched: `#18145` (theme files declare variables only) stays, and `#18102` (engine value sheets at `:where()` weight, consumers project at theme root) is superseded by cascade layers.

Design authority: "Graduated from Discussion #22228 at body revision R3" (the epic's signal ledger).

## The Fix

1. Add `learn/agentos/decisions/0044-design-system.md`: context with the census numbers and their revisions, the decision in ten points (name shapes, palette, roles, component variables, tree, layers, scopes, what a theme is, the model-facing deliverable, invariants with their guards), rejected options, migration order, consequences.
2. Re-cut the roadmap's paragraph: the decision is made, with links to the record and the epic.
