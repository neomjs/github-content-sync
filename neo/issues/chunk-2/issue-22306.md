## The Problem & Architectural Reality (Design Scope After Pass-1 Mechanical Leaf)

1. **`DependencyLayer` Overlay Wired by Hand in Three Places (`Container.mjs:238-286,382,626-656`, `Body.mjs:48-55`):**
   - `ensureDependencyLayer()` calls `Neo.create` with a hand-built ID (`Container.mjs:630`), spreads `...me.dependencyLayerConfig` instead of `[isDescriptor]` `merge: 'deep'`, pushes `createVdomReference()` into `body.getVdomRoot().cn` in two places (`Container.mjs:649`, `Body.mjs:48`), mirrors 10 configs via individual `afterSet*` property writes AND re-sends all 10 in `syncDependencyLayer()` (`layer.set({...})` at `L382`), and destroys by hand. **Pre-code gate:** record who owns the overlay's inputs before writing code so those 10 duplicate hooks either collapse cleanly or bind declaratively.
2. **Monolithic Un-overridable Methods & Un-instantiated `Cascade` Static Bag (`Container.mjs`, `Cascade.mjs`):**
   - Four methods account for `925 / 3,965` lines (`moveTask` `287` LOC, `Cascade.runDependencyCascade` `270` LOC, `Cascade.planWorkingTimeSchedule` `251` LOC, `DependencyLayer#project` `117` LOC), with `runDependencyCascade` and `planWorkingTimeSchedule` duplicating the queue and cycle-break loop. Subclasses cannot customize clamping, minimum duration, or service consultation without copying `moveTask`.
   - `Neo.gantt.Cascade` (`Cascade.mjs:13`) `extends Base` yet is never instantiated; its static methods take `chart` first while `Container.mjs:471-479` wraps `Cascade.*` back in one-liners (`core-idiom-audit.md` check 5: registration is not dispatch), and `Column.mjs:25` (`DependencyLayer.milestoneHalfSize`) + `EventDragZone.mjs:196` (`DependencyStore.getFinishToStartRefusal`) hardcode class names.
3. **15 `#private` Methods Blocking Subclassing & `Neo.overwrites` (`ExtendingNeoClasses.md`):**
   - Behavior in `#private` methods (`Container.mjs`: 7; `DependencyStore.mjs`: 4; `TaskStore.mjs`: 2; `CascadeMotion.mjs`: 2) is unreachable by subclasses, mixins, `Neo.overwrites`, and Neural Link `get_method_source` (`CODING_GUIDELINES.md §8`), alongside `45` `#private` fields (`4.1`/file vs `0.6` across `src/`). Internal state handles belong in `#private` fields (`#rippleTimeoutId` precedent); overridable behavior and subclass-facing state belong on `@protected` methods and reactive/protected members.
4. **Closure/Flag State, Java Mutators, Model Spread & Defensive Non-Idioms:**
   - `Container.mjs:97,160-179`: five store listener closures close over a local `stale` variable while `_movingTask` (named like a reactive backing field) is toggled across four `try/finally` sites; `drawnSpans` is one mutable `Map` shared by reference across four classes.
   - `EventDragZone.mjs:20,22,235-252`: plain fields `linkTarget = null` and `taskPreviewRecord = null` mutated via Java-style `setLinkTarget()` / `setTaskPreviewRecord()` instead of reactive `linkTarget_` / `taskPreviewRecord_` configs.
   - `TaskModel.mjs:2,31-58`: spreads `...timelineFields` instead of extending `Neo.timeline.model.Event` / `Model#fields_` (`merge: 'deepArrays'`).
   - **24+ lines of defensive guards against shapes the class system rules out:** `me.startDateField || 'startDate'`, `rowHeight || 48`, `this.store?.isDerivedParent?.(record)`, `typeof ... getFinishToStartRefusal === 'function'`.
   - **Hardcoded English UI strings in render logic:** 6 strings across 8 call sites (`Column.mjs:68,73`, `Container.mjs:420,496,662,748,1022,1039`) with no config seam for customization/i18n.
   - **Preserved mid-life unsubscribe invariant (`@neo-gpt-emmy`, `D#22305#discussioncomment-19938652`):** `Base#destroy` covers subscriber destruction only; replacing `timeAxis` (`Container.mjs:343`) or ending a drag gesture (`EventDragZone.mjs:278`) while the subscriber remains alive MUST continue to dispose the old subscription immediately.

## The Fix

1. **Settle & Record `DependencyLayer` Input Ownership First:** Post/fold the ownership decision for `DependencyLayer` inputs + lifecycle, convert `dependencyLayerConfig` (and declarative child hooks) to `[isDescriptor]` (`merge: 'deep'`), eliminate double-syncing `afterSet*` + `layer.set({...})` writes, and unify VDOM mounting.
2. **Decompose Monoliths & Open Extension Seams:** Factor `moveTask` and the duplicated `Cascade` queue/cycle-break loop into focused `@protected` overridable seams; replace `Cascade`'s un-instantiated `extends Base` static-bag round-trip and hardcoded `DependencyLayer.*` / `DependencyStore.*` calls with overridable class/instance dispatch; convert all 15 `#private` methods to documented `@protected` methods (retaining `#private` fields strictly for internal handles).
3. **Idiomatic Reactivity, Model Inheritance & Config-Backed Strings:** Convert `linkTarget` / `taskPreviewRecord` to reactive `linkTarget_` / `taskPreviewRecord_` configs; align `TaskModel` with `Neo.timeline.model.Event` / `Model#fields_` inheritance; replace closure/flag state (`stale`, `_movingTask`) and impossible-shape `||` / `typeof` guards with clean class state; expose the 6 hardcoded English UI strings as overridable configs; and preserve mid-life `timeAxis`/gesture unsubscribe.

### Decision (pre-code gate): the layer owns its inputs, the chart writes each one once

**Read for this, in full, at `eed5718b0f`:** all eleven files of `src/gantt` (4,538 lines after the mechanical pass), `src/scheduler/Container.mjs`, `src/Neo.mjs`, `src/core/Base.mjs`, `src/state/Provider.mjs`, `.github/CODING_GUIDELINES.md`, `DeclarativeConfigMerging.md`. **What the code does today**

- The layer has eleven reactive inputs. Three re-project when they change (`axisLeft`, `scrollLeft`, `highlightedDependencyIds`: `DependencyLayer.mjs:193-234`). The other eight store the value and wait, so the chart follows each write with `syncDependencyLayer()`, which sends ten keys again and then calls `project()` (`Container.mjs:1434-1454`).
- Two writers disagree on one input: `afterSetViewportWidth` sends the configured width (`:477-480`), the ten-key set sends `timelineColumn.effectiveViewportWidth` (`:1448`). It works because the hook calls the second one next.
- The parent class already has the mechanism: `static timelineRelays` and `relayTimelineConfig()` forward a container config to the column, its header and the drag zone, from generated hooks (`scheduler/Container.mjs:80-104`, `:465-492`, `:1435-1442`). The Gantt overrides four of those generated hooks and `afterSetTimeAxis` by hand to add the layer.
- The sibling child reacts by itself: every input hook of `timeline.Column` re-renders its rows (`timeline/Column.mjs:377-525`).
- The Engine has two shapes for an owned child component. The common one is a `<name>Config` spread into a `Neo.create` inside `construct` or a lazy `ensure…` method: 60 spread lines in 33 files under `src`, three of them in the Gantt's parent (`eventDragZoneConfig`, `editorConfig`, `timelineColumnConfig`), and the grid builds its view this way (`grid/Container.mjs:113`, `:374`). The newer composites declare that config with a deep-merge descriptor (`desktop/Container.mjs:92`, `app/content/Container.mjs:89`). The layer follows the first shape without the descriptor, and `ensureDependencyLayer()` mounts as well as creates, so the vdom reference is pushed from two places (`:727-731`, `Body.mjs:33-37`).

**Decision**

1. **Inputs.** The layer's reactive configs are its only inputs, and each has exactly one writer:

| Input | Whose value | The one writer |
|---|---|---|
| `bufferPx`, `startDateField`, `endDateField`, `timeAxis`, `rowHeight`, `taskStore`, `dependencyStore`, and the plumbing pair `appName`, `windowId` | a chart config | the relay table, which gains a `layer` hop beside `column`; a config the scheduler does not relay calls the relay from its own hook |
| `axisLeft`, `scrollLeft`, `viewportWidth` | the timeline column's geometry | one chart method, called from the scroll and column-position subscriptions |
| `highlightedDependencyIds` | the registered analysis | one chart method, which `syncDependencyLayer()` calls |
| `drawnSpans` | the cascade motion | passed once, with the creation config |

2. **Reaction.** The layer re-projects when one of its inputs changes, as the column does. The chart keeps the one decision only the writer can make: when the data inside the stores has settled (`_movingTask`, `isUpdatingRows`, `schedule.isPublishing`: `:306-322`, `:396-431`). `syncDependencyLayer()` shrinks to geometry, highlights and `project()`.
3. **Lifecycle.** `dependencyLayerConfig` gets the deep-merge descriptor, so an app or a subclass overrides one key or the class (`{bufferPx: 800}`, `{module: MyLayer}`). `ensureDependencyLayer()` stays the lazy creator, as `ensureEventDragZone()` is in the parent, and only creates. The body mounts its overlay in the one place that rebuilds its root (`Body#createRowPool`), and the chart stops writing into the body's vdom. The chart destroys the layer in `destroy()`, as today.
4. **State providers.** None inside the package. An app binds the chart's reactive configs, and one relay hop carries them to the layer; a provider between the two would hold a second copy of each value. (The calendar is the other engine pattern: its settings live only in a provider, `calendar/view/MainContainer.mjs:153`, so there the provider is the single source.)

**Rejected: the layer reads its chart** through one `chart` config, as `CascadeMotion` does. The ten hooks would stay, each as a "something changed" call; six constructions in two spec files draw the layer from plain configs without a chart, which is what makes its projection testable; and the layer's state would stop being visible on the layer.

**What goes:** the ten-key re-send, every hook's `syncDependencyLayer()` call, the hand-written layer line of the nine mirrored values, the chart's own push of the vdom reference, the field write after creation. The pull request reports the measured line counts.

**Proof in the pull request:** per input, a spec that writes the chart config alone and asserts the arrows; a write count per input during one cascade (one); the standalone layer specs unchanged. One lead to settle there, not here: the chart subscribes to no width, the column does (`timeline/Column.mjs:589-611`), so a resize of the center body alone may leave the layer's window stale today.

**Slicing.** Three pull requests in this order: the overlay (criterion 2, `#22343`, which blocks this ticket), the extension seams (criterion 3, filed when `#22343` merges), the idioms (criterion 4, on this ticket).

**Design seat: the per-task seam is a placement seam** (answer to Grace's challenge in her review of PR #22367, slice A, which is merged)

**Her reading is the one I meant, and my design said it too loosely.** "Two per-task seams" names the policy of where a task lands, not the whole visit. Rule 1 decides it: a method a subclass replaces takes its inputs and returns its result. A seam that also carries the pass's writes (`succ.set`, `staged.set`, `nextSpans`, `pending`) is mechanism with a policy inside, so push-only or keep-float means copying about a hundred lines.

**The seam**
- One per pass, because the two policies differ: the cascade of an edit lands a successor on its required start; the plan of a calendar switch compares two modes and keeps hand-placed float.
- In: the task, what the pass computed for it (the required start with its governing edge, the current span, the duration). Out: `[startMs, endMs]`, or nothing when the task stays.
- It writes nothing. The walk's visitor keeps every write, the staging and the result entry.

**Where it lands.** Slice A stays as merged: it is behaviour-identical, and its gate was the per-edge seam. The two placement seams go into slice C, whose cascade arm is the proof: a subclass changes where a task lands and touches no write.
