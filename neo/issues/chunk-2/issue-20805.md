# A curated claim holds for three days, not seven

## Context

A claim on a curated issue reserves it for seven days from the claimant's latest comment (`CLAIM_WINDOW_DAYS`, `buildScripts/util/claimResponse.mjs:40`; `CONTRIBUTING.md:134–135`). Seven days suited a contributor who wrote the change by hand.

Measured on 2026-11-12 over the outside claims that were assigned on curated issues created since the responder went live on 2026-09-25:

| Read | Value |
|---|---|
| Outside claims that were assigned | 25 |
| Claims that became a pull request by the claimant | 11 |
| Claim to pull request | median 0.6 h, third quartile 3.9 h, maximum 7.7 h |
| Within 24 hours | 11 of 11 |
| Claims without a pull request by the claimant | 14 |
| Of those: still held (0.5 to 2.0 days old) / released / the issue closed by someone else's pull request | 4 / 2 / 8 |

## The Problem

- Every claim that delivered did so within eight hours. In this sample a claim that is silent after a day has never delivered.
- A silent claim holds its issue for seven days. Maintainer seats leave an assigned issue alone, so the work waits.

## The Architectural Reality

- `buildScripts/util/claimResponse.mjs:40` holds the constant; the JSDoc at `:10` and `:37` says seven. `earlierClaim`, `staleAssignees` and `renderReply` read the constant.
- `test/playwright/unit/buildScripts/util/claimResponse.spec.mjs` hard-codes "7-day" in a title (`:242`) and six days as still holding (`:262`).
- `CONTRIBUTING.md:134–135` says seven days three times. `.github/workflows/claim-responder.yml:3` says it in a comment.
- The Brain and the Fleet Manager check this module out from `dev`, so the constant reaches them with the merge. Their guides carry the same sentence.

Design authority: `buildScripts/util/claimResponse.mjs:36–37`, "How long a claim holds an issue for later claimants after its claimant's latest comment, mirroring CONTRIBUTING's seven days for a silent claimant." The measurement is the reason to change the number. The operator proposed three days on 2026-11-12 as a peer's input; the pull request's review decides.

## The Fix

- `CLAIM_WINDOW_DAYS = 3`. The JSDoc and the workflow's comment follow.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md:134–135` says three days.
- The spec's holding and lapsing cases derive their days from the constant instead of the literals.

Why three and not one: a person who claims on a Friday evening and works on Sunday still holds the issue on Monday, and any comment restarts the window.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `CLAIM_WINDOW_DAYS` is 3, and no `7` or "seven" about the claim window remains in the module, the workflow's comment, the guide or the spec's titles.
- [ ] The spec's holding and lapsing cases derive from the constant, and `npm run test-unit -- test/playwright/unit/buildScripts/util/claimResponse.spec.mjs` passes.
- [ ] The reply an assigned claimant receives says three days.
- [ ] Post-merge, not this pull request: the same sentence in the Brain's and the Fleet Manager's guide.

## Out of Scope

- A longer window as standing for a returning contributor.
- The one-claim cap (#20750).

## Avoided Traps

- **One day.** The measurement would allow it for an agent. A person who works at a weekend would lose the claim.
