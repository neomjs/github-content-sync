# ADR 0043 I5: a vnode alone is no proof that Main wrote it

Owner: Vega (self-assigned on filing). A correction of my own text in #22379 / #22380.

## Context

ADR 0043 I5 merged at `0d8a0c9ed4` (#22380). Its strong rule holds: a component is mounted only once Main has written its vnode. The sentence that operationalizes it does not: *"A container passes `mounted: true` only to items whose vnode exists."*

## The Problem

Euclid ran #22381's exact head (`ec3088451e`) in Chromium and found two supported paths. In both, a child prepared by a vnode-only render (`initVnode(false)`) has a vnode, is absent from its container's landed tree, and is still mounted by the cascade with no DOM in Main:
1. a prepared floating leaf added silently while the container's first render is held (the removal sweep exempts it);
2. a prepared leaf added from `parent.on('vnodeInitialized', …)`, synchronously after the vnode sync.

The controls behave: an ordinary late child stays unmounted until a later render draws it, and a child included before capture mounts normally. The sentence therefore names the wrong evidence, and the leaf that implements it (#22381) inherits that scope.

## The Architectural Reality

- `learn/agentos/decisions/0043-vdom-diff-placement.md`, section 2, item 3, I5.
- The container's landed tree is what `onInitVnode` and `syncVnodeTree` already read: the child ids `ComponentManager.getChildIds(vnode)` returns from the synced vnode.

## The Fix

In I5, replace the two sentences *"A container passes `mounted: true` only to items whose vnode exists. `vnodeInitialized` is no proof of that: it stays true after an unmount clears the vnode."* with:

> A container passes `mounted: true` only to items its landed tree contains. Neither a vnode nor `vnodeInitialized` proves that: a vnode-only render (`initVnode(false)`) leaves a vnode Main never wrote, and `vnodeInitialized` stays true after an unmount clears the vnode.

Record the correction in the Status row. Proposed on D#22199 ([DC 19959083](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/22199#discussioncomment-19959083)), for confirmation by Record 1's author and signers.
