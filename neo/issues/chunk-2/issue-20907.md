# The scheduler's default drag shows nothing until release

## Context

Leaf of #20874. Found while reviewing #20898.

A scheduler that is created without `eventDragZoneConfig` gives no feedback between press and release: the bar jumps on drop. `examples/scheduler/basic` passes `eventDragZoneConfig: {useProxy: true, moveInMainThread: false}` (`examples/scheduler/basic/Viewport.mjs:73`), which is why its journey and its frames show a preview.

## The Problem

A default that every consumer has to override is the wrong default, and the bar has no dragging state without it.

Evidence, with its limits: on `dev` the family passes `useProxy: false` when it creates its zone (`src/scheduler/Container.mjs:658`), and `Neo.timeline.draggable.EventDragZone#dragMove` moves a proxy and nothing else. The symptom was measured on the Gantt, which shared that path before #20898 repaired its own zone: twelve pointer moves left the bar's rect unchanged and no proxy was in the document. For the scheduler it is read from the source and not measured yet.

## The Architectural Reality

- `Neo.scheduler.EventDragZone` extends `Neo.timeline.draggable.EventDragZone`, whose config has `useProxy: false`.
- The preview's look exists: when `zone.useProxy` is set, `Neo.scheduler.Container#onBarDragStart` builds the proxy from the bar's title and its `neo-scheduler-bar-*` class, styled as `.neo-scheduler-drag-preview` in `resources/scss/src/scheduler/Container.scss`.
- #20898 moves the scheduler's default into `getEventDragZoneDefaults()` without changing it, and sets `useProxy: true, moveInMainThread: false` on `Neo.gantt.EventDragZone`. That is the precedent.
- Design authority: "Every affordance. […] each has a designed rest, hover, active and dragging state in both themes." (the design read of #20874, [comment](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20874#issuecomment-6859971579)).

## The Fix

Turn the preview on by default for the scheduler family, keep `eventDragZoneConfig` as the override, and remove the opt-in from `examples/scheduler/basic` so the example shows the default.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A scheduler created without `eventDragZoneConfig` shows a preview that follows the pointer during a move and during a resize. A test reads it mid-gesture, before release.
- [ ] `examples/scheduler/basic` no longer passes `useProxy` or `moveInMainThread`, and its journey passes.
- [ ] Escape and a failed drag start leave no preview behind.
- [ ] The unit specs that stub the drag addon pass (the Gantt's needed `setDragProxyElement` in the stub).
- [ ] **Look:** the pull request carries a mid-drag frame of the example in `theme-neo-dark` and in `theme-neo-light`.

## Out of Scope

The Gantt's zone, which #20898 repairs. A new look for the preview.
