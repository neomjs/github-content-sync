# Engine 14.1 release notes, written for the weekend cut

## Context

Engine 14.1 is the weekend minor (milestone v14.1; roadmap set by #22237). `buildScripts/release/publish.mjs prepare` refuses to cut without `.github/RELEASE_NOTES/v14.1.0.md`. At 2026-11-26T20:1xZ no notes ticket, file or owner existed: #22237 closed without one, and the cut owner of 14.0 confirmed that they are not holding the 14.1 notes. The operator asked that the 14.1 notes carry concrete performance benefits; the measured ones are in #22218's decision record (#22215 turned the VDom worker off by default).

## The Problem

A minor's notes are easy to under-write: one pass at the cut, a changelog with a title on top. This window has a measured performance result and a defined milestone, and a reader should learn from the notes what they can now do and what got faster on which device. The performance numbers also come from different populations: physical iPhone AB/BA repeats in two power-mode cohorts, desktop 120 Hz checkerboard runs, and other runtime measurements. A number quoted without its arm, endpoint and denominator says more than was measured.

## The Architectural Reality

- `.github/RELEASE_NOTES/v<version>.md` is the staging note `publish.mjs prepare` requires; `.agents/skills/release-notes` governs its writing.
- The scope comes from the corpus since 14.0.0 (GitHub Release published 2026-11-23T23:08:01Z), re-run against live GitHub at the cut head.
- 13.4.0's notes (#20496) show a minor's notes as one leaf; 14.0's (#21032) are the current shape.
- Design authority: n/a — calls no existing behavior wrong.

## The Fix

- Write `.github/RELEASE_NOTES/v14.1.0.md` from what has merged by the cut. Name chapters by what a reader can now do, built from the milestone's delivered items and the mined arcs.
- Write a performance chapter from #22218's records. Each figure (iPhone scroll p95, 120 Hz checkerboard, blank fling runs, CPU, GC) names its arm, endpoint, power-mode cohort and denominator, and links the record that measured it.
- Name the window's outside contributors by script, as the 14.0 notes did.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `.github/RELEASE_NOTES/v14.1.0.md` is on `dev` before `publish.mjs prepare` runs for 14.1.
- [ ] Every performance figure names its arm, endpoint, cohort and denominator, and links its record.
- [ ] Every chapter claim links its primary (PR or issue), and the counts are regenerated at the cut head.
- [ ] The window's outside contributors are named.
