# Portal: a dead Blog route, two framework labels, Connect's own links

Leaf of #20682. One pull request for three small defects its design read found on Nov 10 ([comment 6756557699](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20682#issuecomment-6756557699)) and promised as first issues. They were never filed. The fourth of that list, removing the Slack and Discord links, is withdrawn: the operator ruled on Nov 14 that both stay. A fourth point follows from that ruling: Connect names the two rooms.

## Context

Each point was read again on `dev` at `2f38667e` on 2026-11-14, unless it says otherwise.

## The Problem

1. **The footer's Blog button opens a route that does not exist.** `apps/portal/view/home/FooterContainer.mjs:75` has `route: '/blog'`. `apps/portal/view/ViewportController.mjs` knows `/news` and `/news/{*itemId}`; the blog list lives at `/news/blog`.
2. **Two places put Neo among frameworks.** The project's writing rule is that Neo is an application engine, never a framework. `apps/portal/resources/images/workers-focus.svg` labels the engine "Framework", and `apps/portal/view/home/parts/Features.mjs:62` ends a card with "(other frameworks)".
3. **Connect hides the site from GitHub's referrer lists and repeats a link** (`apps/portal/view/services/Container.mjs`):
   - links to our own GitHub pages carry `rel: 'noopener noreferrer'` (lines 86, 107, 126, 129, 148), so a visit from neomjs.com arrives at GitHub without its source;
   - "Ask in the discussions" (line 147) opens the discussions list, the same target as the third card's "Join the discussion" (line 125);
   - read on Nov 10 and not re-read today: the intro above the cards names three of the four cards.
4. **Connect does not name the two chat rooms.** `apps/portal/view/services/Container.mjs` links GitHub and LinkedIn only. Slack and Discord stand in the home's footer, and Discord in the header, whose icons are hidden on a phone on every route but home (`apps/portal/view/ViewportController.mjs`, `updateHeaderToolbar`: `hidden = activeIndex !== 0 && vertical`). That rule is intentional and stays (the operator, Nov 14: no space for a second toolbar row), so Connect is where a phone visitor has to find the rooms, and today finds neither.

## The Architectural Reality

- The footer's buttons and the Connect cards are plain item configs in their views; routes resolve in `ViewportController.mjs`'s `routes` map.
- The diagram is a static SVG under `apps/portal/resources/images/`; its text is in the file.
- The app-work gate of `AGENTS.md` applies to `apps/**`: read a sibling view first, styles stay in SCSS.

## The Fix

1. The Blog button's route becomes `/news/blog`.
2. The SVG's label reads "Engine". The card's bracket names what it compares with and drops "other" (candidate: "(conventional frameworks)").
3. On Connect: links to `github.com/neomjs` keep `noopener` and drop `noreferrer`; "Ask in the discussions" opens the new-post form of the Q&A category; the intro names all four cards.
4. Connect offers Slack and Discord beside the discussions, with the invite links the footer uses (`apps/portal/view/home/FooterContainer.mjs`).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The footer's Blog button opens the blog list, and a unit spec pins its route.
- [ ] `git grep -n -i "framework" -- apps/portal` returns no line that calls Neo a framework or lists it among "other frameworks".
- [ ] On Connect, every link to `github.com/neomjs` has `rel: 'noopener'`; "Ask in the discussions" resolves to the new-post form of the Q&A category; the intro names four cards.
- [ ] Connect shows a Slack and a Discord link at 390×844 and at 1440×900, and each opens the invite the footer uses.
- [ ] The pull request shows the footer, the diagram and Connect before and after.

## Out of Scope

The Slack and Discord links in the header, the footer, `apps/portal/index.html` and the README (they stay: the operator's ruling of Nov 14, #20682 comment 6879039842). The home's first screen (#20946). Links to other sites, which keep `noreferrer`. The header on a phone: its hidden icon group is intentional and does not change. The contributing guide and the issue chooser: #20889.
