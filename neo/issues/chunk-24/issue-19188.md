# A window beat can show an empty black vessel with nothing inside

## Context

Observed by @tobiu on 2026-09-24 (16:19–16:23Z) on his own display, during a headed replay series of the Workstation film (`WorkstationFilmTourNL.spec.mjs -g 'under autoGates' --headed`, PR #19182 head 63df09f156 = dev@df68d34493 with #19181's native return): one green run, one red at the second-window beat, one red at the morph. The operator escalated the sighting; this ticket promotes the defect-note (Memory Core `b5912c84ec357a47`).

## The Problem

**Observation:** one drag produced a popup with a black background and nothing else in it — no pane, no tab strip.

**Series facts (from the runs' popup probe):** every created vessel closed again in all three runs (3/3, 2/2, 3/3), so the black vessel was transient, not an orphan; the first run created only two vessels because the tour stopped at its failed conversion. Which beat produced the black vessel and for how long it stayed black is unmeasured — the probe records created / loaded / closed, not what a vessel painted.

**Inference — three candidates, none proven:**
1. a born vessel painted before its document and pane materialised (the tear-out's mid-gesture birth, or the morph's birth under its 1500 ms hold);
2. the morph's retiring vessel painted after its pane had left (the re-entry cancels the tear-out, the vessel retires);
3. a vessel whose theme background never applied, so the body fell to a dark default (the class of `VesselPlaceholder`'s own history: an unaddressed component inherits the user-agent ground).

## The Architectural Reality

- Vessel birth and retirement: `src/dashboard/dock/window/TearOut.mjs` (the vessel is born mid-gesture, `birthHold` in the film's morph, `previewNodeId`), `VesselPark.mjs`, `VesselConversion.mjs`; the popup boots its own Workstation app from the `?popout=` / `?tearout=` URL and adopts the opener's theme at birth (`#18672`: an adopted instance takes its container's theme).
- The film's window beats: `GestureDriver#executeTearOutStep` (tear-out; the morph = tear-out + re-entry), `executeCrossWindowDockStep` (the second window), `NativeGestureDriver#executeNativeReturnStep` (the stack's return; the vessel closes after the transfer).

## The Fix

1. **Instrument first:** a per-frame popup probe on a headed film series on the film-stage display (`NEO_FILM_DISPLAY_BOUNDS`, `filmStage.mjs#placeNativeWindow`) — for every popup: created, loaded, first paint with the body's computed background colour and the pane count, and the same read every 100 ms until close; the receipt names the beat, the vessel and the duration of any frame painted without pane content.
2. **Classify** against the three candidates from the receipt.
3. **Repair at the owner:** a vessel is not shown before its pane is mounted and its theme background applies (hide-before-paint at birth), and a retiring vessel stays hidden once its pane has left — whichever the receipt names.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1 The instrumented headed series names the beat, the vessel and the duration of the black frame (or records five runs without one, which closes this as not reproduced with the receipt attached).
- [ ] AC-2 After the fix no popup paints a frame without its pane content and its theme background — the witness reads each popup's first painted background and child count — headed 5/5 on the film-stage display.
- [ ] AC-3 The film replay is green headed 3/3 at the PR head on the film-stage display.
- [ ] AC-4 The PR body names the mechanism.
