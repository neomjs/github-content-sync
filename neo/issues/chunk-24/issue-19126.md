# The film's opening is one purposeful rearrangement — pop-out, drop zones, the native return, rails, a resize — and the picture sets the pace

## Context

Sub of #15252. The operator's pacing steer (2026-09-23 13:5xZ, relayed by @neo-gpt-emmy): little text fits into 22 seconds while the UI can show a lot — rails, pin/unpin, splitters, pop-outs, a native popup dragged back across animated drop zones; the Qt-like drop-zone motion is part of the attraction. Our earlier plan let speech set scene length: #19112 / PR #19115 grew a 10 s stationary born hold and #19123 / PR #19124 (withdrawn) a 7 s post-receipt pause from measured utterances. The plan inverts here: the picture sets the pace, and a short benefit exchange rides several actions. The coauthored lines and their measured seconds (attempt `20260923T133000Z-opening-02`, in the production record) stay valid as the voice's durations, not as holds.

## The Problem

The merged screenplay parks the picture: a 10 s hold on a born vessel and pauses sized to lines. And three of the attractions the operator named have no scene-owned interaction in the film at all: the edge rails (reveal, pin, unpin), the native popup dragged back over the main window's animated drop zones, and a splitter resize with its live conserved-pair preview. A tour that narrates them without performing them would be an edit trick.

## The Architectural Reality

- Cues the film's controller handles today: `overflow`, `scroll`, `canvas-update`, `cross-zone-showcase`, `theme`, `gate`, `tear-out`, `convert-while-dragging`, `stack-return`, `perspective-capture`, `perspective-restore`, `undo`, `redo` (`apps/workstation/tour/cueVocabulary.mjs`). Gesture executors: `executeTearOutStep`, `executeCrossWindowDockStep`, `executeStackReturnStep` (`NativeGestureDriver.mjs`) and `executeCrossZoneShowcaseStep` (`GestureDriver.mjs`).
- The missing beats are already witnessed outside the film: the native titlebar return in `WorkstationNativeTitlebarDragNL.spec.mjs` (`Browser.setWindowBounds` moves with a settle poll; the hold is the gesture, #18119; the drop reads the zone under the popup's corner, #18059); rail reveal and pin in `DockPinCollapseNL.spec.mjs` and the dense control's reveal arms; the splitter's live preview (#17849) under a real pointer drag in the dense tour's `resizeSplit through the real boundary` beat.
- `TourRunner` starts a cue and its pause concurrently and awaits host settlement; `pause` steps hold settled results (#19112's ledger). Holds inside a gesture (`birthDwellMs`) are the driver's option and default to zero.
- The film witness (`WorkstationFilmTourNL.spec.mjs`) replays the continuous journey; the unit spec pins the order and the budget sum.

## The Fix

One purposeful rearrangement, one persistent session, in this order:

1. **Cold open (≤ 2 s):** the living room; a splitter drag is the first motion.
2. **Pop-out:** Metrics leaves past the window's edge, is born mid-gesture and committed; the born hold shrinks to what it takes to read "that is a window" (≤ 1.5 s). The morph (return before release) leaves the opening.
3. **Second window:** Commits converts while dragging into the vessel; the drop-zone previews glow inside a real OS window; commit — two windows composing.
4. **The native return:** the vessel dragged by its OS frame back over the main window; root edge chips and previews animate as it crosses them; the hold is the gesture; the stack comes home and the empty vessel closes. New cue `native-return` with an executor that mirrors the witness's move helper.
5. **Rails:** a pane pins to its edge rail from its header action; a click on the rail tab slides the reveal in; the pin brings it back. New cue `rail`, scene-owned, real clicks.
6. **Resize:** a splitter drag with the live preview; the layout settles.
7. **Perspectives, undo, redo** fast, then the living signature.

Speech spans actions and never parks them: "Meet Workstation." over the cold open; "Let's give the charts their own window." over the pop-out; "Now I can keep them in view while I work." over the second window; "And when I'm done —" / "— it all comes home." over the native return. Pauses are only as long as an outcome takes to read (≤ 1 s); silence where the motion carries it. Process: a **silent choreography rehearsal** first — the headless replay's per-beat durations judge the pace — then the voice is chosen, cut and placed over that flow.
