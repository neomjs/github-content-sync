# A born vessel's content sits one title bar below its drag pointer

## Context

Measured 2026-09-25 on the film-stage display (external, 3360×1890, pin 2632,100,1320,920) in the take's capture mode (no viewport emulation) at the head of PR for #19220 (vessels born 320×240 outer / 320×173 inner): the film's second-window beat still fails with `target vessel did not reach one parked semantic + rendered claim`. The driver's snapshot at the failed step: `claimCount 1, winnerStableId workstation-vessel:metrics, converted false, engaged false, parkReceipt null` — the target claims the pointer, the conversion never proposes. Receipts: `test-results/dock-wow-demo/glitch-probe/2026-09-25/capture-mode/fix-head/`; the same beat is green under the tour's emulated 1280×800 viewport, ten runs today.

Geometry at the last frame: target outer top 1148, dragged source outer top 1286, both 320×240 outer with a 67 px title bar. The pointer (aimed at the target's inner centre) sits 15 px below the source's outer top, inside its title bar; the source's content starts 52 px below the pointer.

## The Problem

Observation: the dragged vessel follows the pointer with its outer origin at pointer minus the grab offset (15 px, the grab inside the tab), while its content starts one title bar lower. Under emulation a popup's metrics carry no title bar (outer equals inner), so the same follow lands the pointer inside the content; on a real window it lands in the chrome. Inference: `VesselConversion` measures inner rects — overlap divided by the smaller extent per axis, `min(rx, ry)`, entry at 0.55. With both inner rects 173 tall and offset 138 px, the y overlap is 35 px, 0.20; the outer rects would overlap 0.43. So a real window converts only when the user drags much deeper into the target than the film, or a person, expects — the vessel the user holds is one title bar lower than the pane they grabbed.

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/main/addon/DragDrop.mjs` (~:548 and ~:598): the window follows `screenX − offsetX`, `screenY − offsetY` through `moveTo`, which speaks the outer frame; the offset is measured from the dragged element's rect in the source window (~:666).
- `apps/workstation/view/VesselWorkspace.mjs#openTearOutVessel` (~:1131): the birth `top` is the proxy's screen y (proxy y + the main window's chrome + `screenTop`), used as the popup's outer top; the popup's own chrome is unknown before birth.
- `src/dashboard/dock/window/VesselConversion.mjs#sample`: inner rects, `axisRatio` over the smaller extent, `composeRatios = min(rx, ry)`.
- `apps/workstation/tour/NativeGestureDriver.mjs#executeCrossWindowDockStep`: the pointer goes to the target's inner centre and waits for `remoteSnapshot.ready`.
- `Main#windowOpen` after #19220: the popup's total size is the request; the content is the request minus the platform's title bar.

## The Fix

1. Name the content origin: the follow places the CONTENT at pointer minus the grab offset — subtract the popup's own `outerHeight − innerHeight` (readable from the same-origin popup once laid out; 0 under emulation, so the tour keeps its geometry) on every follow frame, and correct the birth position the same way on the first frame after the popup has a frame. Owner: the follow in `DragDrop`, with the birth `top` in the host reading the same chrome once known.
2. Unit arm on the follow with a fake window whose `outerHeight − innerHeight` is 67: the moved origin is the pointer minus the offset minus the chrome; with 0 it is unchanged (the emulated case).
3. Witness: the capture-mode compose beat settles on the film stage (one headed run), and the receipt shows the pointer inside the source's content at the grab offset.
