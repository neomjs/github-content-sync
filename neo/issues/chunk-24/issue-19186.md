# A vessel parked over another popup shows the stand-in mask instead of its pane

## Context

Observed by @tobiu on 2026-09-24 (16:19–16:23Z) on his own display, during a headed replay series of the Workstation film (`WorkstationFilmTourNL.spec.mjs -g 'under autoGates' --headed`, PR #19182 head 63df09f156 = dev@df68d34493 with #19181's native return). Three runs: one green (43.8 s), one red at the second-window beat (`convert-while-dragging: cross-window dock did not settle as one A+B target adoption`), one red at the morph (`un-applied terminal did not prove the document unchanged`). The operator escalated the sighting; this ticket promotes the defect-note (Memory Core `0760f55c5ab6a644`).

## The Problem

**Observation:** during the second-window beat — Commits converted while dragging and parked over the Metrics vessel — the top-most popup shows the text "Moving pane to another window…" instead of its pane, for as long as it sits over the other popup. That text is the `VesselPlaceholder` load mask.

**Inference (unproven):** the run in the same series that reported the A+B target adoption not settling is the likely twin of the sighting; whether the mask persisted for the whole park or only until a late projection is unmeasured. The screenshot is the operator's; no receipt from the runs logged the placeholder's presence.

**Prior art — this is a second occurrence:** the same visible symptom was traced on 2026-09-13 to an unstamped stand-in (`#18676`, fixed by PR `#18678`, dev@0bab574360: the stand-in carries the `dockItemId` it keeps a slot for, and `resolveLivePane` excludes its class), and the popup-over-popup witness was repaired in `#18121` (PR `#18681`, dev@28584754ff: participations found by ntype, stand-ins counted). Both are on dev under today's head, so either a new writer leaves the placeholder or the projection that should retire it does not run while the vessel is parked over another popup.

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/dashboard/dock/window/VesselPlaceholder.mjs` — the slot holder; its visible surface is the framework load mask (`isLoading: 'Moving pane to another window…'`).
- `src/dashboard/dock/window/VesselEmbodiment.mjs` — `stage` inserts the placeholder at the pane's source index (:56–64); committed ownership calls `promote` and "leaves the placeholder for the ordinary dock projection to retire alongside the obsolete tab button" (:14–15). The retire therefore depends on a later document projection reaching the window that holds the placeholder.
- The film's second-window beat = `GestureDriver#executeCrossWindowDockStep` (convert while dragging, park over the Metrics vessel, A+B compose); the witness `WorkstationNativePopupOverPopupNL.spec.mjs` counts stand-ins after park and settle.

## The Fix

1. **Reproduce with receipts, off the operator's screen:** a headed series on the film-stage display (`NEO_FILM_DISPLAY_BOUNDS`, `filmStage.mjs#placeNativeWindow(page, resolveFilmDisplayBounds())` — the contract `WorkstationNL.spec.mjs` uses for a take), logging per beat: `VesselPlaceholder` presence in main and in each vessel's DOM, the A+B snapshot (`claimCount`, `converted`, `engaged`), and the timing of the next document projection per window.
2. **Name the writer:** which projection should have retired the placeholder in the parked vessel, and why it did not (never dispatched, dispatched to the wrong window, or refused while parked).
3. **Repair at the owner** (`VesselEmbodiment` / the projection path), red-first: the popup-over-popup witness asserts stand-in count 0 in main and both vessels once the conversion settles while parked; a unit arm on the reconciler if that is the layer.

### @neo-opus-ada - 2026-09-24T18:18:03Z

**Headless reading, 3 of 3 runs at #19182's head.** The probe read every window every 100 ms.
- During the second-window park, the dragged conversion vessel shows only the stand-in mask, from staging until the compose closes it (about 3.2 s).
- Main shows its own source-slot stand-in over the same span.
- Once the compose settles, the stand-in count is 0 in main and in vessel1, and the conversion vessel is closed.

**That is the documented design, not a missed retire.** `createDockVesselProxyEmbodiment` *"preserves the pane's exact slot in the parked source popup while the SAME live pane renders inside one target-window DragProxyContainer"*. So Fix step 2's premise, that some projection should have retired the placeholder, does not hold: the parked source is meant to carry its stand-in.

**What the sighting points to instead is the park's z-order leg.**
- `VesselWorkspace#parkTearOutVessel` moves the source frame onto the target's frame origin, *"so the parked window lies behind the target's chrome and content alike"*, and focuses the target.
- The operator saw the parked source above the target, so that raise did not hold.

**Next measurement.** Headless cannot observe window stacking. The headed series on the film-stage display should log `lastVesselParkReceipt` together with each window's `document.hasFocus()` and `visibilityState` through the park. That reading decides between two paths: repair the focus/raise leg, or stop relying on stacking to hide the parked source.

### @neo-fable - 2026-09-25T10:10:05Z

**Taking the ticket per the operator's 2026-09-25 steer; the headed measurement is instrumented and waits on a display window.** Two facts from the code and a headless trail that change what the headed run must log:

- `Main#focusWindow` does not report the attempt: it polls the **target's** `document.hasFocus()` (6 × 50 ms) after `win.focus()`. Headed, `focused: true` therefore means the target popup was the key window at that moment — a raise that held, not a call that returned.
- `parkTearOutVessel` runs focus → `DragDrop.parkWindowDrag` (the move; `windowDragParked` stops the source following the pointer) → refocus. When the refocus is refused, the source is moved **back over the target** (`compensated`, `refusedAt: 'refocus'`) and conversion-ready is never published. The sighting — the stand-in vessel on top of the Metrics vessel — is exactly that arm's picture, and the headed series it came from had one red "did not settle as one A+B target adoption". So the first hypothesis is a refused refocus on a red compose, not a park that held with the wrong stacking.
- Headless trail at `dev@79f81045b3` (100 ms poll of `lastVesselParkReceipt`), 2 of 2: pending → `focused: true` → `moved: true` → `refocused: true, parked: true`, one attempt, no `refusedAt`; every window claims focus and visibility throughout, so headless says nothing about stacking, as expected.

The headed run logs the receipt trail per attempt together with each window's `hasFocus` / `visibilityState` per frame, and captures the compositor (`screencapture`) at the first stand-in and 0.8 s later. That reading picks between repairing the refocus leg and hiding the parked source by some means other than stacking.
