# After the A+B compose the merged vessel's tab strip has a gap before its first tab

## Context

Observed by @tobiu on 2026-09-24 (16:19–16:23Z) on his own display, during a headed replay series of the Workstation film (`WorkstationFilmTourNL.spec.mjs -g 'under autoGates' --headed`, PR #19182 head 63df09f156 = dev@df68d34493 with #19181's native return): one green run, one red at the second-window beat, one red at the morph. The operator escalated the sighting; this ticket promotes the defect-note (Memory Core `f0e886135f6d2bdd`).

## The Problem

**Observation:** after the A+B compose (Commits docked into the Metrics vessel), the merged vessel's tab strip shows a gap before its first visible tab — "Commits" sits after empty space where the Metrics tab should be.

**Prior art — this is a second occurrence:** on 2026-09-13 the same picture was traced (Emmy, from a saved trace) to the first tab button being *retained with its text span emptied* — a ~56 CSS px blank where the Metrics label had been — not a removed tab; owned by `#18674` and closed on 2026-09-14 by PR `#18684` (dev@66bb5e078d, "prepare live headers for whole-stack return"). That fix is on dev under today's head.

**Inference (unproven):** today's gap is either that retained-button state re-exposed by the whole-stack native return (`#19181` moves the merged stack home in the beat right after the compose) or the compose's own projection lag — `VesselEmbodiment#promote` leaves "the obsolete tab button for the ordinary dock projection to retire" (VesselEmbodiment.mjs:14–15), so a projection that never reaches the vessel leaves a button without its pane. Which one is unmeasured.

## The Architectural Reality

- The vessel's tab strip is the dock TabContainer's header toolbar projected from the vessel's document; `#18684` prepared live headers so a whole stack can return with its buttons intact.
- `src/dashboard/dock/window/VesselEmbodiment.mjs` — staging inserts a `VesselPlaceholder` at the pane's index and the obsolete tab button is retired by the next projection, not by `promote`.
- The film's compose = `GestureDriver#executeCrossWindowDockStep`'s A+B target adoption; the witness reads the target vessel's document, not its painted strip.

## The Fix

1. **Reproduce with receipts, off the operator's screen:** a headed series on the film-stage display (`NEO_FILM_DISPLAY_BOUNDS`, `filmStage.mjs#placeNativeWindow`), reading the merged vessel's tab strip at the compose settle and at the next projection: button count, each button's text node and rect, `hidden` flags, and the strip's first visible button offset from its left edge.
2. **Discriminate** a retained empty-label button (the `#18674` class) from a hidden / overflowed first tab and from a placeholder slot.
3. **Repair at the header projection**, red-first: the witness asserts that after the compose the first visible button starts at the strip's left edge and every button carries its label.
