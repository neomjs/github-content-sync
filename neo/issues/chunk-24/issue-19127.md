# The film gets a scene-owned rail beat: a pane collapses to its edge rail, the rail tab reveals it, the pin brings it home — driven by real pointer input

## Context

Sub of #19126 (the visual-first opening; the operator's steer of 2026-09-23 13:5xZ: rails, pin/unpin and the drop-zone motion are part of the attraction, and they need scene-owned interactions, not narration). The film's cue vocabulary has no rail cue; the dense control already proves the interaction with a human's clicks (`WorkstationNL.spec.mjs` › the unpin action, the rail tab, the reveal overlay, its `Pin`).

## The Problem

The Workstation film cannot show its rails. Nothing in `apps/workstation/tour/` drives the header's `unpin` action, a rail tab or the reveal's pin, so the take could only narrate a rail — an edit trick the steer rules out.

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/dashboard/dock/interaction/Rail.mjs` renders a rail tab per auto-hidden item as a real button whose `handler` is `onTabClick` (`:427`, `:777`), which hands the item to `RevealStateMachine#tabClick` (`:785`); the reveal's pin is `RevealOverlay`'s toolbar action (`neo-dashboard-dock-reveal-pin`, `aria-label` `Pin`, `:189–195`) routed through `revealPinRequested` → the rail's `onRevealPinRequested` (`Rail.mjs:350`).
- The pane's header projects the engine's `pin` action (`enableDockPinAction`, default on; `aria-label` `unpin` while the item is pinned), focus-gated like the other five, through `tab.Container#headerActions` (`src/tab/Container.mjs:327`); the dock Workspace routes the intent and commits `setItemAutoHidden`. A focus-gated action is mounted only while its subject holds real focus (`toolbar.Base` follows the subject's `containsFocus`, kept by `manager.Focus`) — a synthetic mouse event moves no focus.
- `apps/workstation/tour/GestureDriver.mjs` owns in-window scripted input: `runGesture` (dispatched input settles before cleanup releases it), `simulateEvent` (real synthetic mouse events through `InteractionService`), the film cursor dot, `waitFor`/`trap`. `executeCrossZoneShowcaseStep` is the sibling to mirror; `TourController#executeCue` dispatches cues (`apps/workstation/view/TourController.mjs:287`), and the unit spec pins the vocabulary list exactly.

## The Fix

A `rail` cue and `GestureDriver#executeRailStep({itemId, sourceNodeId}, options)`: click the pane's tab and take real focus by name, walk the cursor to the header's `unpin` action and click it, wait for the committed document (`items[itemId].autoHidden === true`) and the rail tab, walk to the rail tab and click, wait for the reveal overlay to be visible with the item, walk to the reveal's pin and click, wait for `autoHidden === false` and the pane back in its home node. Receipts `{collapsed, revealed, restored, edge}` plus `documentBefore`/`documentAfter` with `documentsUnchanged` reported. Cancellation and destruction release input through the existing `runGesture` cleanup; a missing action, rail tab or overlay fails with a named error and no residue. The film gains a `film-rails` scene at the position #19126 names, with `filmPace` options and the cursor dot; the unit vocabulary arm and the budget pin move in the same change.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1 `rail` is a handled cue; `executeRailStep` performs unpin → reveal → pin back through real pointer input and returns the three phase receipts with the pane home in its own node afterwards. *Restated 2026-09-23 from "the document round-tripped byte-identical": byte-identity is reported in the receipt (`documentsUnchanged`), not required — the pin back may leave `pinned` or the active item different from the moment before the fold.*
- [ ] AC-2 Unit lifecycle controls: destroy during the reveal releases input exactly once and leaves no cursor; a rail tab or overlay that never appears fails with a named error, no residue.
- [ ] AC-3 The film carries a `film-rails` scene (budget ≤ 8 s, pauses only to read the reveal), and the headless replay (`WorkstationFilmTourNL.spec.mjs -g 'under autoGates'`) asserts the rail receipts at the PR head on the seat's own bridge.
- [ ] AC-4 The vocabulary arm pins the new cue; the pacing pin moves with the scene budget inside the envelope.
