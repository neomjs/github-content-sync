# A lone Portal example leaves two empty desktop columns

## Context

Ada reported that a three-column Examples group can finish with one regular card and two empty tracks. I reproduced the unbalanced row on the actual Portal using the merged catalog at `d6233acbcd`.

## The Problem

At desktop width, Workers & Platform has four demonstration cards, Lists & Galleries has seven and Multi-Window has ten. All three end with a card in only the first column. The rendered counts include the cross-listed cards. A 21-card Grid & Table group fills its last row already.

## The Architectural Reality

`GroupPage` enables a lead card. `List.css` gives wide group pages three columns and the first card a two-by-two span. The orphan is a regular card on the row after a complete preceding row. `ListModel#stepRow` already chooses keyboard destinations using live card rectangles.

Design authority: #21140 establishes the three-column Examples layout at 1440 px. Preserve the two-row lead and the existing card navigation.

## The Fix

At the wide three-column breakpoint, give the lone final demonstration a landscape row. Keep its cover at one-third of the row and place the existing footer beside it. Preserve the cover’s detail disclosure and scope the rule to demonstration cards on group pages.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The lone final card fills the row with 4, 7 or 10 rendered demonstrations. Its cover keeps the existing aspect ratio and the lead retains its measured bounds.
- [ ] ArrowDown still selects and focuses the last card, with details and its launch cue available.
- [ ] Two-column and phone layouts preserve the existing measurements. A complete three-column row and component tile lists keep their current layout.
- [ ] Compare the whole tab before and after with loaded covers and disclose any visual trade-off.

## Out of Scope

Changing catalog membership, replacing cover images, and changing mobile navigation.
