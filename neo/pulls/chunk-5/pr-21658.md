Resolves #21653

*On Air at Eight* gives the visitor ten seconds of opening titles, a late guest and a ninety-second recorded segment. Dragging the film into the first slot changes the presenter’s cue and shows ten seconds of clearance on the transmission timeline. Taking the cue switches the program picture. Leaving the guest first puts dead air on the channel.

![The broadcast desk and rundown](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/8d373e17b0ee6d1a8bdff4e927367e49f9784f06/apps/portal/resources/images/examples/on-air-at-eight-14.jpg?raw=true)
