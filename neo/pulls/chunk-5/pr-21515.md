A new example under examples/form/checkout puts Country, ZipCode, Phone, Currency and IBAN in one form: a shipping address and a payment. The postal code's format, the phone hint and the amount's currency follow the chosen country (Austria, Germany, Switzerland). The IBAN's format is checked for the country its first two letters name, where IBANField knows one, and its check digits for every country. Check order in the inspector validates the form and shows the submitted values; Reset also clears the form.

The postal code gets its countryCode from the example's country change handler: ZipCodeField.countryField reads a `record` from the combo box's change event, which now carries the chosen record as `value`, so that link leaves the postal code without a country.
