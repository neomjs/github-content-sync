Resolves #21682

A timeline bar's `aria-label` now names its dates the way the header does: `TimeAxisHeader.formatWall` in the column's new reactive `locale_` config (default `Neo.config.locale`), in the axis zone. A bar from midnight to midnight names its days up to `lastWallMoment` (so a one-day bar reads `Survey the site (Nov 9)`), any other bar adds hours and minutes and reads up to `endDate` itself (`Nov 10, 08:00 – 13:00`), and a bar without length names one moment (`Inspection (Nov 16)`, `Handover (Nov 17, 14:00)`). The title stays first and the `barKey` fallback for an untitled bar is unchanged. Public behavior: only the accessible name of bars changes (Scheduler, Gantt and timeline examples); no migration.

Evidence: L2 (unit spec `test/playwright/unit/timeline/Column.spec.mjs`, run locally) -> L2 required (AC-6 names the unit spec).
