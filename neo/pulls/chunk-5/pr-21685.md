Resolves #21684

A timeline whose region also scrolls other columns before it now measures from its own left edge. `Neo.timeline.Column` and `Neo.timeline.TimeAxisHeader` get an `axisLeft` getter, the timeline's x in the center body's `columnPositions`, and subtract it when they window bars and ticks. `Neo.gantt.DependencyLayer` gets an `axisLeft_` config: the layer is drawn from it and windows with it, and `Neo.gantt.Container` keeps it current when a column move changes the positions. `scrollLeft` keeps its documented meaning (center-content pixels) on all three classes, and with nothing before the timeline `axisLeft` is 0, so every existing layout computes what it did.

Evidence: L3 (headless Chromium e2e on `gantt/basic`, red on `dev`, green at head) → L3 required (every AC is a browser observation). Residual: none.
