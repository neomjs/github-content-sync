Resolves #21619

🌿 A visitor can no longer wonder whether the scheduler's left side is a real table: it sorts four columns and every job stays in its row.

Related: #21346 · #21140 · #21613

Adds `examples/scheduler/resourceColumns`: a field-service week of 24 technicians whose name, skill, region and booked hours stand as four locked, sortable resource columns beside their jobs. Booked hours are summed from the jobs themselves, so the column cannot disagree with the bars. Below 720px the grid's `responsiveLockPolicy` keeps the name column alone locked, and the plan opens at Monday 08:00 through the grid's horizontal scrollbar: a phone shows names beside jobs, and the toolbar says to swipe right for the table. The styles take the timeline's and the scheduler's theme tokens, so all five themes style the example without theme files. No engine change, no shared file.

Evidence: L3 (Chromium at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, five themes; `ResourceColumns.spec.mjs` in `e2e-engine`; the frames and cold reads below) → L3 required (AC-1 to AC-7 are rendered layout, sorting and look). Residual: none for #21619; publication belongs to #21613.

## Deltas from ticket

- Jobs are half or whole days (08:00–12:00, 12:30–16:30, 08:00–16:00), so the booked hours run from 4 to 40 and sort into a clear ladder. Bars do not move or resize: the hours are summed once, and editing is out of scope.
- The plan opens at Monday 08:00 instead of midnight. On a phone this is what puts jobs on the first frame: the policy moves Skill, Region and Booked into the scrolling region ahead of the plan, and the first two cold reads graded that table-only phone frame 2 and 3 of 10. The policy can unlock a column but not hide one, and a header button's `hidden` leaves the body's cells in place, so the example scrolls instead.
- The drive found no engine defect, so nothing blocks this ticket.
