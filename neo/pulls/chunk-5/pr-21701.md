Resolves #21626

`examples/gantt/taskColumns`: a festival's production plan, 29 tasks in five phases, with seven columns beside the chart: outline number, the task tree, the owner as initials in a badge of their own colour, start, finish, days, and percent done as a short bar. A `TaskModel` subclass in the example's folder adds `outline`, `owner` and a calculated `days`, and the now line marks the day the progress was reported. At 1100 px or less a `responsiveLockPolicy` keeps only the tree locked, so on a phone the other six columns scroll with the chart.

#21685 is on `dev` and merged into this branch at `1c46f6274d`, so the diff is this example’s own (12 files). #21692, the clip the scrolled frame below lacks, landed on `dev` with PR #21699 (`9f0dd82f75`). `7a4c2402a3`: the phone-width checks scroll the timeline into view first. At 390 × 844 the grid mounts the six loose columns (to x = 390) and the timeline, which starts at x = 502, only once it scrolls into view; the checks had found the survey bar in a transient mount before the layout settled. CI's failing load (run 44862877504) had every stylesheet by 466 ms, so the 15 s wait of `8b9f907f87` treated the wrong cause, and it is gone.
