Resolves #21180

`examples/grid/tree` replaces the 250 px, 7-row file tree with a full-stage three-level operating budget (`52` nodes: `4` departments → `12` teams → `36` budget items) across seven columns (`Name` `tree`, `Budget` `currency`, `Spent` `currency`, `Used` `progress`, `Seats`, `Owner`, `Status`). Leaf rows hold the only authored figures; `buildRollupData()` and `MainStore#onRecordChange` compute every team and department's `headcount`, `budget`, `spent`, and `utilization` from its children so edits cannot leave parent totals stale. Opens with the first level expanded (`16` visible rows), toggles nodes by click and `ArrowRight` / `ArrowLeft`, switches between `neo-theme-neo-light` and `neo-theme-neo-dark` (honoring `?theme=` and `prefersDarkTheme`), and resets sorters, expansion, and data via `Reset`.

Evidence: L3 (unit, component, and Playwright E2E specs green locally; before/after frames at `1440 × 900` and `390 × 844`; two-pass deterministic cover capture; two swapped-order cold readers) → L3 required (AC-1..AC-7).
