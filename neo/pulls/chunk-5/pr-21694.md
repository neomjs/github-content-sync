Adds `examples/scheduler/liveUpdates`, an airport gate operations scheduler (`14` gates across Piers A–C and `60` flights over `06:00–12:00 UTC`) backed by `Neo.data.Store` (`GateStore`, `FlightStore`) and `Neo.data.Model` (`GateModel`, `FlightModel`). A deterministic Mulberry32 generator (`?seed=19257`, `1s` cadence, no network requests) slips flights, lands new arrivals into free turnaround slots, and frees gates while advancing changed-flight highlights from at-rest (`.is-feed-fresh`) to `.is-feed-fading` via `setSilent` before each primary store mutation, preserving horizontal/vertical scroll and skipping any bar currently held in `eventDragZone`.
