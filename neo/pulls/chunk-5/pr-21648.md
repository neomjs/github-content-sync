Resolves #21639

`Neo.gantt.Container#animateCascade` (off by default) moves the tasks a cascade changed from their previous to their final dates over the chart's `--motion-panel` with `--ease-out-soft`, both read from its node. Bars and links read one in-flight span per task (`Container#drawnSpans`), stepped by the new `Neo.gantt.CascadeMotion` on the App Worker, so a link never leaves its bar. The records hold their final dates from the start. Reduced motion (the `0ms` tier), a scroll, a zoom, a store load, filter or sort, and the next move land at once. Both Gantt examples switch it on.

Evidence: L3 (headless Chrome e2e at the head, red on `dev`; unit specs) → L3 required (AC-2, AC-3 and AC-5 are browser observations). Residual: none.

CI jobs: +0 — the e2e spec joins the existing `gantt` selection, the unit spec joins `unit`.

## Deltas from ticket
- **No pre-write hold.** The rows the cascade's writes repaint send their update only after a task boundary (`VdomLifecycle` awaits `timeout(1)` before it collects), so `CascadeMotion#start()` repainting the moved tasks at their previous spans inside the same synchronous edit is what the browser shows first. The e2e's monotonic check would catch a final-then-back flash.
- **A dragged or resized task stays where its preview dropped it,** and its successors travel. Moving it from its old place replayed what the preview had already shown (seen in the first capture). Keyboard, editor and API edits move the edited task too, as the ticket's look says.
- The easing is read from `--ease-out-soft` with the duration, so no easing literal sits in JavaScript. A failed read lands in one update.
- A running move also lands on a store `sort` or `mutate`, and when the config is switched off.
- `e2e/gantt/BasicNL.spec.mjs` sets `animateCascade:false`: it measures bars right after moves, and its resize grab missed a still-moving handle.
- `test/playwright/externalBrainSelection.mjs` lists the new spec: like its siblings, it drives the page's own App Worker.
