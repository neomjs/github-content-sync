## What changes

- **`/examples` opens an overview:** the headline counts what the selected build publishes ("40 live examples in 14 groups" in production), the action tests its first claim, three groups to start with, the banded index, and every group's card by band. On a phone the start cards give way, so the index follows the build control.
- **`Portal.view.examples.GroupIndex`:** one component bound to the new groups store (`Portal.store.ExampleGroups` of `Portal.model.ExampleGroup`), in four presentations: the overview, the upright index beside a group page's cards, the crumb's sheet, and the link to the next group. Entries are links in the tab order; the open group carries `aria-current="page"`.
- **Counts by home record**, so they add up to the headline. A group that only borrows cards shows them in parentheses, explained under the index.
- **Phone, below 640 px:** a group page keeps a crumb whose group button opens the full index as a sheet. It takes focus, closes on Escape or a followed link, returns focus to its button, and the button carries `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
- **The desktop control (phase 2):** `list` puts the banded index upright beside the heading and the cards, and it ends above the fold of a 720 px viewport. `switcher` shows the crumb at every width: its group button is a pill that names how many groups the build publishes, and a link to the next group ends the cards. With either, no card is narrower than 410 px: two columns of 562 px beside the index at 1440×900, three of about 450 px with the switcher.
- **`TabContainer` becomes `MainContainer`:** the section is a card layout over the overview and one page per published group, no longer a tab container. Inactive cards stay mounted, so each page keeps its scroll position (a browser keeps it across `display: none`, and loses it across removal).
- **The overview replaces the Featured list**, as on the board. The `featured` flag has no reader after this; dropping it from `examples.json` waits for the open catalog pull requests, so it conflicts with none of them.
