## Deltas from ticket
- **`test/playwright/dedicatedWorkerNeuralLink.mjs` (`createDedicatedWorkerAppConnection#invokeWorker`):** Live `NEO_AGENTOS_RUNTIME_ROOT=""` execution of `rendering/ButtonBaseNL.spec.mjs` and `tab/TabHeaderActionsNL.spec.mjs` surfaced that `ComponentService#getDomRect` returns a `DOMRect` whose coordinates live on prototype getters serialized via `DOMRect.prototype.toJSON()`. Over the WebSocket Bridge (`Client#send` → `JSON.stringify`), `.toJSON()` runs in the App Worker realm; over Playwright's `worker.evaluate` (CDP `returnByValue`), prototype getters and `.toJSON()` are stripped to `{}`. Wrapping the worker evaluation return in `result === undefined ? undefined : JSON.parse(JSON.stringify(result))` inside the App Worker callback restores wire parity with `Client#send` and is pinned in `test/playwright/unit/test/dedicatedWorkerNeuralLink.spec.mjs`.
- **`.github/workflows/test-e2e.yml` (`e2e-engine` shard count `4 → 8`):** Scaling `e2e-engine` from `65` to `109` specs (`+44` specs) caused 4-shard runs to hit the 5-minute step ceiling (`test-e2e.yml:9-12`: *"the ceiling is five minutes of test time per shard... A shard nearing it gets a sibling, never a higher ceiling"*). Bumped `e2e-engine` matrix shards from `4` to `8` (`~13.6` specs/shard; `e2e-engine-gate` aggregates all shards without changing branch-protection check names).
- **`test/playwright/e2e/rendering/StoreLoadingNL.spec.mjs`:** Over the ~1ms off-socket `worker.evaluate` adapter, `store.sort` resolved in the worker before its VDOM reorder reached the main thread and before `Gallery#afterSetMounted`'s `me.timeout(300)` (`offsetHeight` read + initial `selectOnMount`) fired.
