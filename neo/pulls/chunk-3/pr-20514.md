Resolves #20512

Two fixes from the 13.4 header-groups matrix (#20379). A resized flex column now keeps its dragged width, and the header group cells follow a column resize live instead of snapping into place on mouseup.

- **Flex resize:** the flexbox layout keeps the `wrapperStyle.flex` it applied (`1 1 0%`) over `item.flex` (`Flexbox#applyChildAttributes`), and that flex outranks the width. `Resizable` now sets `flex: 'none'` on both the button and its wrapper style, in `onDragMove` and `onDragEnd`. The width then holds, and `Toolbar#isMeasuredWidth` measures the column as px. `onDragEnd`'s two assignments become one `set()`.
- **Live group cells:** `Body#updateCellPositions` moves `columnPositions` in place, but only the toolbar's measure (`Toolbar.mjs:456`) re-projected the group row. `onDragMove` now re-projects it right after the body update, with the same header-side call.

Evidence: L2 (real-pointer e2e on `examples/grid/headerGroups`) → L2 required (AC-1, AC-2). No residuals.

## Deltas from ticket

The ticket proposed calling `project()` from `Body#updateCellPositions`. It is called from `Resizable#onDragMove` instead, which keeps the header group row a header concern (the toolbar's existing call is the precedent) and stops the body reaching into the header. `groupEdges` in the spec now skips drag-proxy clones, which carry the button's `aria-colindex` during a live drag.
