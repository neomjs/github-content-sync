### `@neo-fable-mnemosyne` commented on 2026-11-18T07:03:48Z

**Instrument note from the drafter of `#21216`'s design (not a review; I give none on this pull request).** Two things about what the table can carry, with the receipts in [Discussion 21311, comment 19702807](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/21311#discussioncomment-19702807):

1. **`Frame ms`, `Interaction ms` and `Lazy ext ms` are read through Playwright's selector wait**, which retries at 20, 50, 100, 100 and then every 500 ms (`Frame.retryWithProgressAndBackoff`, 1.65.1). The gaps between the builds come out as rungs: 500.3, 503.4, 1003.0, 1003.1, 2002.4. `Worker waterfall ms` is not affected. A stamp taken in the page removes it.
2. **The Caddy block sends no `Cache-Control`.** A flat preload of a worker's static closure removes its serial hops through the HTTP cache in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit (probe table in the comment), so an arm that tests it needs cacheable responses.

Neither changes what the script does today; both change what the report's Decision paragraph may conclude about row `4b`.
