Resolves #22385

`Neo.isEqual` now treats objects with different own keys as unequal, `NaN` as equal to `NaN` (also as the time value of invalid dates), and terminates on cyclic objects and arrays. So a config set from `{a: undefined}` to `{b: undefined}` now runs its `afterSet` and stores the new value. `compareObjects` uses `Object.keys` plus `Object.hasOwn`, `isEqual` and `compareDates` check `Number.isNaN` only after `===` fails (so `0` and `-0` stay equal), and `compareArrays`/`compareObjects` pass a `WeakMap` of the pairs in progress through the recursion. `compareFunctions` is untouched.

Evidence: L2 (unit specs, run locally and in CI) → L2 required (every AC of #22385 is unit-level). Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket
A small `Compare.isComparing(item1, item2, seen)` helper records the pairs in progress. The pairs stay recorded after a nested comparison returns: any unequal pair makes the whole comparison false at once, so a recorded pair never hides a difference.
