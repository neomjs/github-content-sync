Resolves #20974

Grid and table `bulkUpdateRecords` now forward whole-record payloads to `Store.updateRows` as one batch. A shared adapter keeps declared writable fields, including nested values, while omitting keys, calculated fields and undeclared fields. The table body follows `rowsChange`, rebuilds changed rendered rows once, and sends one update; ordinary record edits and batch selection notifications retain their handling.

Evidence: L3 (live colors and finance cell updates in local Chrome) → L3 required (AC4 browser-rendered live updates). No residuals.
