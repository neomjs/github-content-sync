Resolves #19538

Since #19533 a pointer drag never parks: the window of the group that claims the pointer carries the drag as the source's tab-header proxy, and leaving it opens a fresh vessel. This deletes everything only the retired pointer park reached, and changes no behaviour.

**Removed:**
- **Conversion:** `VesselConversion` and its spec; `TabSortZone`'s sensor, replay and conversion branches; the `dockVesselConversion*` events, listeners and options in `LayoutAdapter` and both hosts.
- **Pointer park:** Workstation's corner placement and shrink, `NativeVesselTransaction`'s pointer `parkVessel`, `disposeVessel` and `resolveClearPark`, the re-show's extent restore, and `VesselPark`'s convert-out (`onConversionOut`, `pendingOut`).
- **Demo B's park family:** its `VesselPark`, effects and listeners; Demo B has no native title-bar path.
- **`DragCoordinator`'s transition protocol** shrinks to what the claim resolver answers. `resolveRemoteDragTransition({draggedItem, pointerInTarget})` returns `null`, `{engage: false}` or `{engage: true, proxyRect}`. `retain`, `preview`, `commitEligible`, `replayAfterTransition` and `activeTargetCommitEligible` are gone: no source returned them.
- **Re-show addon calls:** `NativeVesselTransaction`'s re-show no longer asks the `DragDrop` addon first (`resumeWindowDrag` and the orphan-recovery probe and ack). Only a parked pointer drag could answer them, so they always fell through to the route move.
- **The skipped size matrix** of `WorkstationHumanPopupOverlapNL`, with its exclusive helpers.

**Kept:** the native title-bar park (`nativeVesselParkHandlers`: focus, move, refocus behind the target, re-show on refusal, dispose on commit), B's claim resolver, and `TearOut`'s exit wait.

Evidence: L2/L3, unit plus headless e2e. This is a deletion with no behaviour change, so no L4 step is owed. The native title-bar arms that need a headed wide display skip at their unchanged environment gates headless (counts below).
