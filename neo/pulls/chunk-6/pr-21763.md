Resolves #21747

The timeline's editor now marks the part of a bound that changed when it refuses a save: the time field when only the time moved since the editor opened, else the date field. That is the rule `validateBound` already follows for a bound that does not parse. One method, `EventEditor#getBoundErrorField()`, serves both refusals in `save()`: an end before the start, and an owner refusal (`commitTimelineEdit`) on a bound. The scheduler's minimum-duration refusals from #21724 now sit under the time field a person changed.

Evidence: L3 (unit specs plus a headless Chromium e2e) → L2 required (AC-1..AC-4 name field states a unit spec reads). Residual: none.
