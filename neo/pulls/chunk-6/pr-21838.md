Resolves #21601

🌿 After this, an unanswered header measurement can no longer keep a newer layout off the screen.

A cold-boot Reset committed the Feed and never rendered it. The projection before it waited on a tab strip's natural-width read with no bound (`Reconciler.mjs`, both overflow recaptures), and every later commit queued behind that wait. Sophie's hold-and-release control (#21601, `issuecomment-7069886552`) reproduced the hosted signature exactly by holding that one read.

Now each commit creates its refresh's `AbortController` at the enqueue boundary (`Workspace#projectDockZoneDocument`) and aborts the one before it, whether that refresh is in flight or still queued; a repair or a parked replay binds its own. The reconciler hands the signal to the overflow recaptures it starts. The overflow pass (`Neo.tab.plugin.Overflow#project`) races each await against the abort, hands its strip back as the last split left it, releases its latch, and leaves any queued re-run to the projection that superseded it. An answer that comes later writes no cache and no split. A superseded refresh still adopts its landed document, but it skips `releaseDeclaredPanes` and the presentation hooks, so an obsolete catalog cannot retire a pane the newer document restarts. The fence is Sophie's design from the ticket thread. The branch also stamps each step before the reconcile (`hostMounted`, `captured`, `released`), so a future miss names its phase.

Evidence: L3 (the real Workstation in local Chromium with the overflow read held through Reset, plus the unit tiers) → L3 required (AC-1; AC-2's local half). AC-2's three consecutive hosted `components` runs are this head's CI plus two reruns, listed under Test Evidence before review.

size-guard-growth: src/dashboard/dock/Workspace.mjs — 12 code lines (1073 → 1085): the three stamps before the reconcile, and the supersession fence (one AbortController per commit, created at the enqueue boundary where it also aborts the one before it, the signal handed to the reconciler, and the skip that keeps a superseded catalog from releasing panes). No consumer line to delete: no caller tracked a refresh's supersession before.
