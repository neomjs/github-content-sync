Resolves #21624

`examples/gantt/taskEditor`: a website relaunch planned in sixteen linked tasks over four phases, each with its percent done drawn in its bar. A task opens in the timeline's editor by double-click or by Enter on its bar, and the toolbar names both. A later end moves the task and every task linked after it, a saved percent fills the bar, an end before the start is refused with its message, and Escape or Cancel changes nothing and hands focus back to the task.
