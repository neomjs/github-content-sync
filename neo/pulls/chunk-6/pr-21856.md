Resolves #21539

🌿 After this, merging one group's examples leaves every other group's PR as mergeable as it was.

The examples catalog was three shared files: every record in `examples.json`, every capture recipe and the tile list in `example-captures.json`, and the generated `seo.json`, so each examples PR conflicted with every other. Now a group owns its files: `apps/portal/resources/data/examples/<group>.json`, with `index.json` naming the groups, and `example-captures/<family>.json` beside it. The tiles are the catalog's `kind: 'component'` records, each with its capture size as `tileSize`. The catalog interleaves the groups, through the overview's featured cards and the cards a page borrows through `alsoIn`, so each record carries `order` and both readers sort by it: the catalog Store's pipeline reads the index and then the group files in parallel through `Portal.connection.ExampleCatalog`, inside the Store's own load, and `buildScripts/util/exampleCatalog.mjs` reads them for `captureExamples`, `seoJson` and the specs. The catalog spec's literals that every examples PR edited (the counts table, the record count, the complete id order, each page's ids) derive from the records by the view's rule now. `seo.json` stays out of examples PRs: no PR check reads it, and the cut's `prepare` regenerates it (#21809).

Evidence: L3 (local: comparisons with `dev` through the real readers, `git merge-tree` over all 210 group pairs for cards and for tiles with controls, a spec rehearsal that adds a record to every group, held-read Store controls, and the Portal's Examples page served from this head) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-3). No residuals.

## Deltas from ticket

- The design comment expected a per-group split to keep "the same records in the same order". A plain concatenation does not: the catalog interleaves the groups (51 runs over 19), and it would move the overview's lead card from Workstation to the Meritocracy Index and reorder 11 of 21 pages. Each record's `order`, its old position times ten, keeps both; a record without one comes last, so appending stays the default.
- The tile list also lived in the shared capture file. Its 51 entries were exactly the catalog's 51 component records, so the tiles derive from them, and a sized tile's `{width, height}` moved onto its record as `tileSize`.
- The capture plan runs in catalog order, recipes and tiles alike. The single manifest's authored sequence cannot survive files per family without a position key on every recipe, and the sequence changes no capture: each runs in a fresh browser context and writes a file named by its id.
- AC-3's spec scope reached past the count lines: the spec pinned every page's ids and the complete order. Those derive from the records now; the curated places stay as checks (Apps leads with Workstation, Forms & Fields with the form app, Start here's twelve), and the publish rule has its own arm.
- Part 3 needed no new gate: no PR job compares `seo.json` with the generator (the unit specs read its titles, `verify.mjs` runs outside CI), and `prepare` regenerates it at the cut since #21809. The README tells examples PRs to leave it alone.
- The Contract Ledger of what this keeps and changes is on #21539.
