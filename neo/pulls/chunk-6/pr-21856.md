Resolves #21539

🌿 After this, merging one group's examples leaves every other group's PR as mergeable as it was.

The examples catalog was three shared files: every record in `examples.json`, every capture recipe in `example-captures.json`, and the generated `seo.json`, so each examples PR conflicted with every other. Now a group owns its files: `apps/portal/resources/data/examples/<group>.json`, with `index.json` naming the groups, and `example-captures/<family>.json` beside it. The catalog interleaves the groups, through the overview's featured cards and the cards a page borrows through `alsoIn`, so each record carries `order` and both readers sort by it: `Portal.store.Examples#load()` reads the index and then the group files in parallel, and `buildScripts/util/exampleCatalog.mjs` reads them for `captureExamples`, `seoJson` and the specs. The catalog spec's literals that every examples PR edited (the counts table, the record count, the complete id order, each page's ids) derive from the records by the view's rule now. `seo.json` stays out of examples PRs: no PR check reads it, and the cut's `prepare` regenerates it (#21809).

Evidence: L3 (local: a before/after comparison through the real readers, `git merge-tree` over all 210 group pairs with a control, a spec rehearsal that adds a record to every group, and the Portal's Examples page served from this head) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-3). No residuals.

## Deltas from ticket

- The design comment expected a per-group split to keep "the same records in the same order". A plain concatenation does not: the catalog interleaves the groups (51 runs over 19), and it would move the overview's lead card from Workstation to the Meritocracy Index and reorder 11 of 21 pages. Each record's `order`, its old position times ten, keeps both; a record without one comes last, so appending stays the default.
- AC-3's spec scope reached past the count lines: the spec pinned every page's ids and the complete order. Those derive from the records now; the curated places stay as checks (Apps leads with Workstation, Forms & Fields with the form app, Start here's twelve).
- Part 3 needed no new gate: no PR job compares `seo.json` with the generator (the unit specs read its titles, `verify.mjs` runs outside CI), and `prepare` regenerates it at the cut since #21809. The README tells examples PRs to leave it alone.
