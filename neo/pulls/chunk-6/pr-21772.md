Resolves #21679

Moves the bottom header seam in timeline-bearing grids off `.neo-grid-header-toolbar` onto the header items themselves (`box-shadow: inset 0 -1px var(--neo-timeline-border-color)` on `.neo-timeline-header` and `border-bottom-color: var(--grid-container-border-color)` on sibling plain/tree `.neo-grid-header-button` items, both at rest and on `:hover`), so `.neo-timeline-now` in the minor row covers the timeline's bottom edge and meets the first row's segment with `0px` gap while every column's bottom edge stays aligned on the same pixel row without shifting any toolbar or body.

Evidence: L3 (rendered-rect E2E across Gantt, Scheduler, and plain locked-column grids + 1440 × 900 and 2× DPR seam frames in `neo-dark` and `neo-light`) → L3 required (AC-1 through AC-5).

Related: #21671, #21672, #20890, #20874

## Deltas from ticket

- Post-filing commit `06768e6409` (`#21702` / `#21714`) added `&:hover { ... border-bottom-color: var(--neo-timeline-border-color); box-shadow: none; }` on `.neo-timeline-header.neo-grid-header-button`, and `scheduler/Container.css:6` sets `--grid-header-button-border-hover-color: transparent` on plain header buttons (`Button.css:50`). Both `:hover` rules carry the bottom-edge styles (`inset 0 -1px` on `.neo-timeline-header:hover` and `border-bottom-color: var(--grid-container-border-color)` on `.neo-grid-header-button:not(.neo-timeline-header):hover`), pinned by hover assertions in `NowLine.spec.mjs`.
