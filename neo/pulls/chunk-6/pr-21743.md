Resolves #21627

`examples/board/bigData` is a support desk of 10,000 tickets in five columns, with 6,200 in New. Each column renders a window of about a hundred cards (`maxRenderedCards` 100, `overscan` 10) and moves it as its body scrolls. The DOM therefore holds at most 550 cards, whatever the store holds. A counter in the toolbar reads the store's count and adds up the cards the columns render, so it follows every scroll and drop. The subtitle names the move to try: scroll New to its end and drag its last ticket into Triage. The tickets ship with the example as a seeded store of models.

Evidence: L3 (Playwright e2e in headless Chromium, with real pointer input) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-3, AC-5, AC-6). AC-4 is the profile below, and AC-7 is the frames and the cold look. Residual: none.

## CI red: diagnosed, waits for #21756

`e2e-engine (6/15)` failed on `1b36bd9571` twice, rerun included, at `BigData.spec.mjs:106`: "no blank stretch in New after the drop". Locally the spec passes 3 of 3, also at 4× CPU throttle.

**Cause (engine, #21745):** at the drop, `DeltaUpdates#flushLayoutHeals` toggles the cards root's `display` after its `moveBefore()` and forces a layout. New's body has no content for that layout, so its `scrollTop` clamps to the top spacer plus padding minus the viewport: one window above the end, where the viewport shows only spacer until the window re-projects. In `board/basic` the same drop ends 907 px above the end on dev. The fix and its red-first are on #21756.

**Merge order:** #21627 is now `blocked_by` #21745. Once #21756 merges, I rebase this branch and add the end-position assertion from my handover (New still at its end after the drop).
