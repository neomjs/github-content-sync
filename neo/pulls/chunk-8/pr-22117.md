Resolves #21974

A flight now collects the merge owners parked under the roots it carries. When `VDomUpdate.mergedCallbackMap` holds more owners than the flight has collected, each parked owner is walked up its rendered parents (`items` membership, same window, within the root's update depth), and an owner under a carried root joins the flight. A held reconcile flight therefore no longer strands their callbacks. Stranded, they left a cold-boot Reset unsettled: `WorkstationBoot.spec.mjs:349`/`:381`, four CI sightings on 23 Nov.

Evidence: L3 (Emmy's real Workstation cold-boot and Reset journeys in headed Chromium on the candidate bytes; the unit control red on dev and green here) → L3 required (AC-2). Residual: none.
