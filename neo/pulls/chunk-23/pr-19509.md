Resolves #19502

A sparse depth-2 ancestor can expand a dirty grandchild through its allowlist, while the old depth-only coverage check still emits the grandchild separately. The filter now uses actual sparse membership to remove that duplicate payload. Roots absorbed beyond the ancestor's nominal depth retain their in-flight protection and callback ownership until adoption; removing those too let a later update promise settle on an earlier vnode. Dense-depth behavior and clean-sibling pruning are preserved.

Evidence: L3 (local Chrome, real App → VDom → Main → App with dedicated and shared workers) → L3 required. The Neural Link E2E spec is outside Engine-only CI selection; the local receipt below is required.

*   **Inputs Read Before Patch:**

*   **Expected Solution Shape:** "Covered" must mean the ancestor's payload reaches the child root's whole scope, not just the root. A sparse ancestor covers an allowlisted root whose own payload is shallow (depth 1) or itself sparse. A dense root carries silent descendants that a sparse payload prunes, so it stays emitted; dense means depth -1, or depth > 1 without its own allowlist, which includes `denseUpdate`. Absorbed roots beyond nominal depth keep their flight. The fix needs no TreeBuilder change and no traversal, and both the unit and browser specs get an arm with a dense root.
*   **Patch Verdict:** It matches on ownership and contradicts on one condition. `parentIds ? parentIds.has(id) : depthCovers` absorbs any allowlisted root, dense or not. The flight retention, `absorbedScopes` into `protectedRoots` and `inFlightScopes.has(childId)` in the callback hand-off, is the shape I expected.
*   **Premise Coherence:** Coheres with verify-before-assert. Your busy-Main control rejected the predicate-only repair, and the same discipline catches this one: a control built on a production dense producer.
