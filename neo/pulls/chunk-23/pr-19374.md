Resolves #19372

🌿 The park now remembers where the content was, so the window that comes back no longer forgets its own title bar.

Since 48591f31b6 (#19225) the conversion samples the dragged vessel's FRAME — right for the admission, which reads the park against the outer frame — but both park call sites handed that same frame rect to `VesselPark` as the restore anchor, and the terminal re-show still converts its rect as CONTENT (`toFrameOrigin`, the contract #18515 fixed and `Workspace.spec` pins). Every refused native handoff, and every non-commit terminal of a parked conversion, re-showed the window one title bar above where the user left it, while the receipt said `admitted: true`.

The fix is one host helper, `VesselWorkspace#resolveVesselRestoreAnchor` (the window's inner rect), used by both `onConversionIn` call sites; `resolveVesselConversionSourceRect` is byte-identical, so the admission keeps the frame. `VesselPark#onConversionIn`'s docblock now names the plane of `sourceRect`.

Change class: fix — behavior change at the terminal restore only; the admission sample and the transaction are untouched.
agent-preflight: unit tier + the two dock specs locally; the e2e witness is Neural-Link-bound and runs outside CI by construction (`NEO_AGENTOS_RUNTIME_ROOT`).

Evidence: L3 (the #18532 witness arm on real 67 px chrome, headless Chrome on a real display, four arms green at this head) → L3 required (AC-1). Residual: none; AC-3 was split to #19373 before this PR (ticket body edited in place).
