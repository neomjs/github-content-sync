- Tiles sort by name. Catalog order put Combo box, Date field and Date selector first, because they used to be cards; a visitor scans 28 field types faster as an index. Cards keep the catalog's order.
- A tile's caption carries the card's open-live cue as its arrow alone (#21140 design 7: a card and a tile show at rest that they open a running example). The cold read below found it missing.
- Names follow #21140's design 7 ("Date field", not `form.field.Date`). Two choices to check: `form.field.Range` is "Range slider", and the functional entries read "Function component", "Hosting a class component" and "Nested templates".

## Cold look

One fresh reader (a front-end engineer evaluating component libraries, given only the frames) graded Forms & Fields 7/10 as proposed against 3/10 today: "names great, previews weak". In their words, it reads as "a gallery of 27 form components, alphabetical, each a plain name under a sample of the real control". What I did with each point:

- No sign that a tile opens a live example: fixed here, the arrow above.
- "Preview scale varies": Combo box, IBAN, Email and Search read as "~5px mush", Form layout is unreadable. The crops frame a wide field at its full width, so a 176 px tile shrinks its text to about 6 px. That is the tile recipe's, not this page's; noted in a defect-note and next on my lane.
- Radio button reads like a checkbox, and File upload is "a muddy brown bar": both are the neo dark theme's drawing of those fields, noted in defect-notes.
- The light theme keeps tiles charcoal: by design, every crop is a dark-theme stage.
- The count line ("1 from other groups: which one?"), the footnote about counts in parentheses, the loud build control, and a single card leaving a hole beside it on More components belong to the shell, not to this PR; relayed to its owners.
