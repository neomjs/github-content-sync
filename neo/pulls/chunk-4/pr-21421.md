Resolves #21419

HeaderGroups' pending-start release arm no longer fails CI on a drop's transient state. `groupEdges` now reads `NaN` leaf edges for a group cell whose leaf is momentarily absent, instead of throwing. Previously the throw ended `expect.poll` at once, on two of the three CI failures since #21373. A relayout wait that fails now polls for 10 s more and reports whether the cells healed. The next failure therefore names a stalled re-projection or a slow runner, which the third failure could not.

Evidence: L3 (a CI-run e2e spec; a red-first transient-leaf arm and 32 throttled runs, both outside CI) → L3 required (AC-1 and AC-2 change the CI-run spec). Residual: the 60-run CI watch in Post-Merge Validation.

Change class: zero-delta (`test`). agent-preflight: not hosted here; the pre-commit and pre-push guards ran.

## AC Evidence
| AC-1 | `groupEdges` returns `NaN` edges for an absent leaf, so `groupOrder` and every alignment poll keep polling. Outside CI: the transient-leaf arm in Test Evidence, red on base and green here |
| AC-2 | `expectRelaidOut` catches its failed edge poll, polls for 10 s more and throws with both readings plus `healed` or `still off`. Outside CI: Test Evidence shows both outcomes |
| AC-3 | Outside CI: both pending-start arms pass 32 of 32 at 8× CPU throttling with four workers, at this head's spec |

## Deltas from ticket
- **`NaN`, not `null`.** The ticket said `null`, but `left - null` is `left`, so a cell near x = 0 would read as aligned. `NaN` fails all fifteen existing `Math.abs(left - leafLeft) < 2` comparisons unchanged. In the failure message's JSON, `NaN` prints as `null`.
- **A second poll, not a sleep.** The ticket said "reads the edges again 10 s later". The `fixed sleeps` guard rejects a bare `waitForTimeout(10000)`, and the first push failed it. A second 10 s poll answers the same question and returns early when the cells heal.

## Test Evidence
- **Red-first, AC-1.** A scratch arm, never committed, strips one leaf's `aria-colindex` for 800 ms while it polls `groupOrder` and then waits on `expectRelaidOut`.
  - Base `267eb2336f` fails in 946 ms with CI's own error: `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'left')`.
  - This head's helpers pass in 1.6 s, by polling through the absence.
- **AC-2, both branches, at `ce47b849ee`.**
  - An absence of 7 s fails at 8.6 s with "Group cells off their leaves, healed within 10 s more: {first…, later…}". The second poll returns as soon as the cells heal.
  - A permanent absence fails at 15.6 s with "… still off within 10 s more: …". The first reading shows Q1's `leafLeft: null` (`NaN` in JSON).
- **AC-3.** The pending-start arms (this head's spec plus a CPU-throttle hook) pass 32 of 32 at 8× with four workers. The full `HeaderGroups.spec.mjs` passes 24 of 24 at this head, unthrottled.
- **Before the fix**, at `267eb2336f`: 48 of 48 throttled runs were green. The CI failures don't reproduce locally, which is why AC-2 exists.
