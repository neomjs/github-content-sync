Resolves #20883

Residual: none.

### Cold look
Two cold readers without session context graded the unlabelled `1440×900` Week + Day frame pairs from 1 to 10 in swapped order:

| State | Reader A (`before` first) | Reader B (`after` first) |
|---|---|---|
| Before (`origin/dev`) | 7 | 7 |
| After (all-day row) | 6 | 7 |

Weakest points named verbatim:
- **Reader A:** on the `after` pair, pushing the default `12:00 AM`-anchored timeline down by the all-day row slices the `04:00 PM Incident review` block horizontally at the `900 px` viewport bottom edge (while `12 AM–9 AM` remain empty); on `before`, 30-minute blue events omit `AM`/`PM`.
- **Reader B:** both pairs open scrolled to `12 AM` leaving half the grid empty overhead; the extra all-day strip exacerbates `04:00 PM` bottom-edge clipping in `after`.

*(Kept `examples/calendar/basic`'s existing `startTime` untouched so `#20883` stays strictly scoped to the all-day row rather than changing example viewport hours alongside it.)*
