Resolves #20575

🌿 A Workstation grid can no longer select a row, or band a header group, in the theme's colors.

Workstation's palette bridge (`resources/scss/src/apps/workstation/Viewport.scss`) gains two mappings:

- **Selection.** All ten grid selection tokens map onto signal washes a step above the hover's 10%: a selected cell 30%, a selected row 22%, the row or column around a selected cell 16%. Text is ink.
- **Header groups.** The header tokens also land on `.neo-grid-header-group-row`. Its cells read the leaf headers' tokens by design (`grid/header/GroupRow.scss`), and the bridge had declared them only on `.neo-grid-header-button`. The glyph rule stays button-only.

The palette-bridge census, `WorkstationPaletteBridgeNL`, carries the ten selection tokens, and its `readPalette` reads `--workstation-ink`.

Evidence: L2 (browser e2e plus a computed-style probe in Workstation Pro) → L2 required. Residual: none.
