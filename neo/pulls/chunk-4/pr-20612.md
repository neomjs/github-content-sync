The shared baseline's guide lint (`neo-agent-skills` 1.2.0, the `Guides` step inside `PR baseline`) now judges Engine's guides, so no Engine job checks out the Brain to lint them. `lint-guides.yml` retires: it cloned `neomjs/neo-agent-brain` at `dev` and ran the Brain's own copy. The guides are judged at the pinned release, so a Brain push can no longer change an Engine PR's verdict. The lint becomes one step in a job every PR already starts, and `lint-guides.yml`'s own runner is gone from the 20 slots the organization shares.
