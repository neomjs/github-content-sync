Resolves #21459

🌿 *No visitor can leave Lists & Galleries thinking a Neo list only shows rows: here one reorders by drag, one animates every sort and one buffers 5,000 records.*

Lists & Galleries gets the rest of #21367's row. A new `examples/list/reorder` puts a release checklist beside a wall of portraits, each a list on its own store, reordered by drag through `Neo.draggable.list.SortZone`; every item shows its place, which changes only once a drop has written the store. `list/animate` publishes as a card, its toolbar wrapping on a phone. `component/carousel` (portrait slides), `list/buffered` (rows read "Record 1 of 5,000") and `list/circle` (sized to its stage) publish as tiles. The group shows six cards and six tiles.

**Merge order:** after #21458 (part 1, its ticket blocks this one) and neomjs/pages#128 (the covers). Declaring the `lists` family in `example-captures.json` needs part 1's four recipes, so this branch carries #21458; once it merges, this diff narrows to part 2 (the commits below). #21463 fixes the `unit` job's red `dialog/Close.spec.mjs:181`, which comes from `dev`.
