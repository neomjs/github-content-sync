Resolves #21472

The Tabs group had one card, a workbench whose three tabs rendered nothing. It now opens on **Browser-Style Tabs** (`examples/tab/browser`): thirty closable tabs in one strip, a "+" header action that opens and activates one more, drag to reorder, and the `Overflow` plugin's menu for the tabs that do not fit. Every behaviour is a `Neo.tab.Container` config (`closable` headers, `headerActions`, `activateInsertedTabs`, `dragResortable`); the controller only answers "+". `tab/container` becomes a tile with content in each tab. The cover and the tile are in neomjs/pages#132.

Evidence: L3 (component, unit and e2e specs in CI; the cover and the tile captured twice to the same bytes; frames released and changed) → L3 required (AC-1..AC-4). Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket

- **`tab/container`'s cards rendered nothing:** `vdom: {html}` on these items never reached the DOM, which is why its tabs were empty on 18 Nov. They now use the `html` config and keep `tabIndex` through `vdom`.
- **Its badge tab is found by `flag`,** like its first tab; it was found by the text `'Tab 3'`, which a rename broke.
- **`captureExamples.mjs`:** a tile needs no recipe in a declared family, since `tabs` now holds `tab-container`. The same line and docblock as #21468, #21458 and #21464, so whichever merges first, the others apply clean.
- **The cover shows the opening strip, not one with a new tab.** After an insert with overflow active, the strip's active-tab indicator slides to a stale position and shows there briefly before it fades. Out of scope, noted in defect-note `a23da6e4`.
- **The browser tabs open on the dark theme** (`neo-theme-neo-dark` first), and their recipe declares `theme: 'dark'`, so the cover follows #21140 Design 7 ("every cover and crop is taken in the dark theme"). Commit `546b802daa`; Euclid's review of #21468 caught the same rule there.
- **`seo.json`** takes the generator's entries: the group's description, the new page, and the tab container's `lastmod`.
