Resolves #21406

🌿 *A cover can no longer catch a canvas before its pixels are there.*

Charts, Canvas & Maps goes from one card of its own to four. The graph scene, sparklines and Mermaid publish with covers. Two of them draw in the canvas worker, so `Neo.component.Canvas` gains `drawn_`, which its root reflects as `data-drawn`. A `Neo.component.Sparkline` sets it once the renderer has answered for its values and a measured size. The new remote `Neo.canvas.GraphScene#whenDrawn()` answers after the owed frame has drawn, and the graph scene example sets `drawn` from it for every scene it sends. The new sparkline example shows six metric cards fed once a second, with switches for the feed, `usePulse` and `useTransition`, and a Restart that always replays the same readings. The new Mermaid example draws four presets from editable text and follows a theme switch.

Evidence: L3 (whole unit suite and the affected e2e specs at the head, red proofs against `dev`'s engine files, headless covers three times each, before/after frames, two cold readers) → L3 required (AC-1..AC-7). Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket

- **The Sparkline stylesheet moves from `grid/column` to `component`.** It was the only Sparkline CSS, so a sparkline outside a grid had no sizing rules. On a 2× screen it drew at twice its box and showed only the top-left quarter of its line.
- **`graph-scene` moves after `open-street-maps`** in `examples.json`, so the group still opens with its map (#21322).
- **The group's sentence names the new cards** (`apps/portal/view/examples/MainContainer.mjs`).
- **The covers:** the Mermaid cover shows the Sequence preset, fitted to its box, because a 640 × 360 crop cannot show source and diagram readably together. The graph scene cover is the dense middle of the default scene, because the canvas keeps a 400 px minimum and the fitted sphere is taller than the crop.
- **`src/canvas/GraphScene.mjs` grows by 7 code lines** (`buildScripts/util/file-size-baseline.json` 1078 → 1085):

size-guard-growth: src/canvas/GraphScene.mjs — +7 code lines for the remote whenDrawn() and its waiter list: only the renderer knows when an owed frame has drawn, and no engine code polled for it before, so nothing is deleted in return

- **`am-charts`** is not published here: its labels draw only with PR #21401 (#21400). The ticket's AC-7 and Out of Scope now say so.
