Resolves #21425

A tile entry in `example-captures.json` may now give its component a capture size (`{path, width, height}`); the recipe applies it to the stage component before it measures, as it already hides a field's other states. 22 field tiles take 220 px and IBAN and the copy-to-clipboard trigger 224 px, so their regions shrink from 348 to 388 px to 268 to 272 px and their 14 px text shows at 9.1 px instead of 6 to 7 px on a 175 px tile. One headless run recaptured all 45; the bytes are on neomjs/pages#118 under the same names, so no catalog record changes.

Evidence: L3 (one recipe run with its report, the recipe's unit spec, frames of Forms & Fields with the new bytes routed in) → L3 required (AC-1 to AC-5). Residual: AC-5's cold read, Residual-Owner: #21140 (the Friday read of the examples section).

## Deltas from ticket

- AC-1 was amended on the ticket after the first runs: a width or height bound does not narrow the lists, File upload, Range, Fieldset, Date selector, Form layout, Nested templates or Sitemap below their own content, and cropping them would break AC-2. Each is captured at its smallest region, listed above.
- The entry also takes a `height`, for components whose 4:3 region their height decides (Text area, Date selector, Form layout, Nested templates, Monaco editor, Sitemap).
