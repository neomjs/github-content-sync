## Deltas from ticket

- **One drag engine.** A group cell's drag runs through its region toolbar's column drag zone as one unit (`SortZone#startUnitDrag`).
  - GroupRow's own engine is deleted: `createDragZone`, the start fence, `moveGroup`, `getGroupDropOrder`.
  - GroupRow now projects the cells, registers each region part with the main thread under its toolbar's zone, and hands the gesture over.
  - The registration gives Escape an owner from a gesture's first frame. The cells turn draggable once it lands.
- **Unit mode in the header `SortZone`.**
  - A unit passes whole units only, by a block rotation of adjacent `swapNeighbours`, so it never lands inside another group.
  - The group row re-projects on every switch. The commit moves the whole run.
  - The proxy and the leaf proxy share `cloneProxyCell`.
  - In @neo-opus-vega's #20632 code: `clampProxyToGroup` has a one-line unit guard inside it, `groupOverflow` is guarded at its call site, and `moveTo` delegates to `moveItem`.
- **Switches follow the dragged unit's edge,** as a column drag's do. A wide group grabbed at its middle lands where its proxy is drawn, not where the pointer is. The e2e helpers now aim by the edge.
- **The container drag zone runs one drag step at a time.** My held-handshake arms found two races in the shared `container/SortZone`: an end could tear down a start still building, and a start could run into the last drag's end, losing its unit.
  - Starts and ends now queue in arrival order (`queueDragStep`, entered through `startDrag`).
  - The latest move made while a start waits is replayed once it's built, so a release still lands where it let go.
  - The end latch is per gesture, and a cancel during a queued start still ends the drag.
  - `dragEndActive` stays a boolean for its readers.
  - This covers leaf drags too. The `DockTabSortZone` unit fake gains the queue dependency.
- **A release during a pending start drops the group where the pointer let go**, now through the replay. An Escape there moves nothing.
- **A drop measures the headers once they have rendered.** After a drop, the header `SortZone` measured right after an un-awaited `update()`. The layout read could overtake the restore of headers the drag had taken out of the flow, so the flex `Dec` took their space as its width: 528 px rendered, 678 or 828 px measured. The group cells and body columns then stayed misaligned until the next resize. Hosted CI failed `HeaderGroups.spec.mjs:723` on both earlier heads. The drop now measures through the toolbar's `measureAfterUpdate()`, and leaf drops share the path.
- **Downstream consumer:** `neomjs/neo-pro`'s `board/ColumnSortZone.spec.mjs:352` pins the engine's `SortZone#onDragStart` and `#processDragEnd` source (engine SHA `bc8a8c1bcb`). This PR changes `onDragStart` (the `resolveDraggedItem` line), so that pin goes red once this lands, unless neo-pro #65 retires the fork and its pin first. @neo-gemini-julia is notified. A ColumnSortZone that extends the engine zone after #65 inherits the queue.
