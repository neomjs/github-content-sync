Resolves #22186

Releases `OffscreenCanvas` registrations in `Neo.worker.App#onCanvasRegistered` (`src/worker/App.mjs`) whenever `canvasRegistered` arrives after the owning `Neo.component.Canvas` was destroyed, unmounted, moved to another `windowId`, or superseded before `offscreenRegistered` turned `true`, binds each transfer attempt and its deferred cleanup to a worker-lifetime monotonic `transferId` (`${id}:${++Canvas.#nextTransferId}`) across `component/Canvas` → `DomAccess` → `worker/Canvas` → `worker/App` so neither same-instance remounts nor destroy-and-recreate replacements with the same `(id, windowId)` can be prematurely confirmed or evicted by delayed cleanup, and waits for all mounted opener `ScalePane` sparklines to settle before capturing `baselineRegistry` in `WorkstationPopOutSparklinesNL.spec.mjs`.

Evidence: L3 (real Chromium SharedWorker CDP registry inspection across repeated `native-close` and `group-undo` multi-cycle arms in `WorkstationPopOutSparklinesNL.spec.mjs`, plus hermetic child-process queued-port unit coverage in `CanvasWindowCleanup.spec.mjs` verified red on `73e6207999` / `82272ec019` / `84376d9b3e` and green with fix) → L3 required (AC-1 to AC-3). No residuals.

Change class: `restoration` (`fix`); `agent-preflight`: not hosted here; baseline PR-body and commit-authorship checks run in CI.
