Resolves #22121

Arrow keys now cross between a group page's demonstration cards and its component tiles, and the page keeps one selected card.
- ArrowDown past the last card row enters the tiles, on the row facing the card. ArrowUp past the first tile row comes back.
- At the page's outer edges the card stays where it is.
- Selecting a card or tile with the pointer clears the ring in the other section.
- The search results behave the same way, since they stack one list per kind per group.

**How.** `ListModel` already hands ArrowLeft on the first card to its owner as `leaveStart`. It now hands a row step it cannot take to the owner as `leaveRow`, with the card's horizontal span.
- `MainContainerController#onListLeaveRow` enters the next list on the same page that has cards, through the new `ListModel#enterRow`. That method picks the card in the row nearest the span, by the rule `stepRow` already uses; one private helper now serves both.
- `onListSelect` clears the page's other lists when one of them selects.
- `getPageLists` resolves a list's own page, so a selection on another page never clears the one on screen.

Evidence: L3 (browser e2e on a local Portal at 1440×900 and 900×800, red with `dev`'s sources) → L3 required (AC-4). Residual: none.
