## AC Evidence

- **Reuse of the existing reflection, tokens and keyframes.** The `.preview-image-wrap` pseudo-element rules in `resources/css/src/apps/portal/examples/List.css` were hoisted, unchanged, into one compound selector shared with `.portal-examples-tiles .neo-list-item .preview-image`, and the hover rule gained a tile arm. - **4:3 crop kept.** The e2e asserts `|ratio - 4/3| < 0.02` while hovered.
- **Caption and launch icon stay in place.** The e2e captures the `.portal-example-title-row` and `.portal-example-open-cue` bounding boxes before hover and asserts deep equality after hover.
- **Selection and pointer cues stay distinguishable.** The e2e selects the tile, hovers it, and asserts the selection light (`--portal-examples-list-light-select`) still rises from the details while the glint rests on the crop.
- **Reduced motion.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the e2e asserts the static cue (reflection at opacity 1, glint duration 0) and that the first tap on the cue still opens the example (popup or same-tab, URL contains `/examples/`).
- **Coverage.** The Checkbox tile on Forms and the first tile of another group (More components) are both exercised, in `neo-theme-neo-dark` and `neo-theme-neo-light`.
