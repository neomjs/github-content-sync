Resolves #20026

A message addressed to a departed window no longer reaches another window when it carries no routing key. `sendMessage`'s last resort now skips a known-departed destination, as its `appName` step already did. The send resolves no port, and `promiseMessage` rejects `NEO_DEAD_PORT`, which `isDeparture` settles through `reason.destination`. #19067's invariant now holds for the key-less `updateVdom` that `App#applyDeltas` and `Component#unmount` send.

Not intended for the v13.3.1 cut unless its lead wants it: the defect is latent in today's flows (see Test Evidence).

Evidence: L2 (unit arms on the routing cascade; e2e drop census outside CI) → L2 required (AC-1, AC-2); AC-3 by CI's tiers.
