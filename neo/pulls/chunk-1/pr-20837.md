### The tier decisions

- **Layout movement keeps the alias** (`--portal-transition-duration` → `--motion-panel`), per the ticket: padding, heights, margins, the gradient crossfade, the cube's `transform`. The alias is what makes the reduced-motion collapse reach all of them at once, and keeping it means the many rules that read it need no edit.
- **Hover and colour feedback → `--motion-fast`** (`_motion.scss`: "hover/press micro-feedback"): card lift (transform + box-shadow + border-color), link colours, the video play button's background, the medium list's author link.
- **Two deliberate duration changes** worth naming in review: the cube layout was `800ms` and now follows `--motion-panel` (280 ms); its face crossfade was `50ms` and now uses `--motion-fast` (120 ms). Both are the ticket's rule rather than a literal kept alive, but they are visible timing changes and I would rather flag them than hide them.
- `ease-in-out` on the cube → `var(--ease-out-soft)`: the transition is a re-layout that settles, not an exit, and the vocabulary has no third easing.
