Resolves #20095

A revealed item's toolbar is now its header. It carries the item's pop-out, gated exactly as the docked header gates it. Using it pops the railed item out through the docked pop-out's own admission path, measured at the reveal's pane. The item detaches still `autoHidden`, so closing its window returns it to its rail. An app-held reveal pops out through the same entry (`Workspace#popOutRailItem`). Every reveal end now names its cause on `dockRailRevealChange`, so an application holding a reveal can tell a person's dismissal from the item leaving its rail.

The reveal's pop-out is the docked pop-out's own transaction (`HeaderActions#popOutDockItem`: measure, admit, hand off, detach, witness), entered from the reveal. Only the hand-off is new: a rail destroys a cached, config-built reveal pane when its item leaves, and by then that pane is inside the vessel. So the rail yields it, alive, before the detach (`Rail#yieldRevealPane`), and the tear-out holds it as the handle adoption moves (`TearOut#capturePane(itemId, pane)`). A workspace that declined the HeaderActions plugin binds no reveal pop-out.

size-guard-growth: src/dashboard/dock/Workspace.mjs — five lines: the public seam an app-held reveal pops out through (AC-3), a one-line delegate like `recreateDockPane`, and its two projection wires; the transaction lives in the HeaderActions plugin

Evidence: L2 (component and unit specs in headless Chromium) → L2 required (AC-1 through AC-5). The real-window leg is the drag tear-out's e2e (`DemoBDockTearOutNL`), which this click enters through the same pair. Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket
- **AC-2 narrowed in the body before this PR.** The reveal's pin restores a railed item, the inverse of the docked header's pin, so it keeps its own `restorable` policy. Maximize is a node's action, which a railed item has no node for. The authority is Clio's design read on Institution #791 (Q1 (a): "pin, pop-out, maximize where the item has them").
- **Cause vocabulary.** The ticket named the person, the application and the item. A retarget also ends the item it leaves, so a tab click, a hover dwell and an application `hold` onto another item carry `tab`, `hover` and `hold`.
- **No deletion in the engine.** The consumer lines this lets go are in the Institution follow-up: `pinRevealedDetail` and its six journeys' calls, plus the cockpit's own dismissal flag, which reads `cause` instead.
