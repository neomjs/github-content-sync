Resolves #20835

A target update within one window could escape its queued common ancestor while another leaf's reply was pending, diff the moved subtree alone and insert a second copy of an existing node. Pre-collection merging now retains effective full-depth work under a pending full-depth ancestor. It includes work carried through a shallow intermediate component while preserving independent narrow payloads.

This covers the dispatched-leaf remainder in #20835. The original #20764 repair and its completed ticket #20762 remain intact.
