Resolves #22153

Locks board column drags horizontally (`moveVertical: false`), replays pre-delay pointer travel when `Pointer`'s arming timer fires, syncs mid-gesture mounted drag proxies to the latest pointer coordinates in `DragDrop#setDragProxyElement`, clears `dragComponent` inside `SortZone#processDragEnd` on `dragQueue` while passing `dragStartPending` moves through `ColumnSortZone#onDragMove`, and checks `data.targetPath` alongside `data.path` for `ignoreDragSelector`.

Evidence: L3 (local Playwright unit, component, and headless Chromium E2E across Board examples and Portal Home `Planning`) → L3 required (AC-1 through AC-4).
