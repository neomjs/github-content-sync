### 🔬 Depth Floor

- **Challenge**: In `src/canvas/Base.mjs:300-314`, `1 << data.button` is evaluated whenever `typeof data.button === 'number'` and `button` is neither `1` nor `2`. In W3C Pointer/Mouse events, chorded or synthetic move/cancel payloads can carry `button: -1`, where `1 << -1` shifts by `31` (`-2147483648` in JS); if `data.buttons` is omitted on a synthetic `down` or `up` report, `pressedButtons` acquires bit 31 (or `up` fails to clear bits `0..30`). Guarding `button >= 0 && button < 31` inside a shared helper eliminates both the shift edge case and the duplicated ternary.
