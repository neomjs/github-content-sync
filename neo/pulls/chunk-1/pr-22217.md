Resolves #22216

Awaits `Neo.applyDeltas(windowId, deltas)` on Main before invoking `afterCreate` (`onInitVnode(vnode, autoMount)`) in `VdomLifecycle#createVnodeTree` when `autoMount` runs with `useVdomWorker: false` (enforcing ADR 0043 §2.3 Invariant I2, skipping destroyed initiators, and restoring `app.fire('mounted')`), stores `import('../vdom/Helper.mjs')` on `App#vdomHelperPromise` and awaits it in `onLoadApplication` and `createVnodeTree`, and updates `VesselClosesMidFirstRenderNL.spec.mjs` to hold the vessel's initial mount frame in both worker and two-thread arms.

Evidence: L3 (local Chromium Component + E2E two-thread `useVdomWorker: false` verification across all failing ADR 0043 §2.4 `(a)` witnesses + isolated unit proof) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-3, AC-4).
