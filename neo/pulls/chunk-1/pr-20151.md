Resolves #20146

GridViewFocus's region loop never blurred between clicks. Regions two and three therefore passed their pointer-stamp poll on region one's stamp while their own `DomAccess.focus` was still in flight. The blur arm then raced that request: the View was re-focused and re-stamped 1–6 ms after `blur()`, and "blur clears the pointer modality class" failed whenever the first poll read landed after it.

Each region after the first now starts unfocused (blur, then wait for the stamp to clear), so its poll can pass only on its own click's stamp. The keyboard arm also waits for its click's stamp before ArrowDown. Test-only, one file.

Evidence: L2 (local whitebox e2e on a private `NEO_NL_PORT`; a review-only event-log probe and a review-only 4× CDP throttle) → L2 required (AC-1–AC-4). Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket
- **AC-1's instrument is an event log, not a throttle.** It observes the re-stamp in every run, where a throttle only raises the chance of the failure.
- **AC-3's "loaded host":** this host was not measurably loaded during these runs (25–31 s per batch, against 37–45 s in the failing base batches). The 4× page throttle stands in for a slow runner. By construction, load now changes only how long a poll waits, not whether it passes.
