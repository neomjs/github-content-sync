Resolves #20849

A pending overdrag scroll or polling delay could resume after cancellation and modify a newer drag on the same SortZone. The move now checks the identity of its already-captured rectangle snapshot after both scroll awaits and before polling again. Replaced work returns without touching the new gesture; normal same-snapshot scrolling and swapping continue. No generation field, queue or consumer override is added.

Evidence: L3 (native pointer → App/VDOM/Main state and rendered-position controls) → L3 required. No residual acceptance is deferred.
