Resolves #19966

Hiding a `document.body`-rooted component while one of its updates was in flight left its children marked `mounted` under an unmounted root: the late landing re-marked them. The next show found nothing to change, so no child's mount hook ran, and a grid header menu reopened after an `Escape` inside that window had no Navigator on its value list. `syncVnodeTree()` now mounts children only while the root's DOM is live (`me.mounted || !me.vdom.removeDom`). A late landing still hands out its vnodes, and the root's next mount mounts the children.

Evidence: L3 (local headless Chromium, `playwright.config.e2e.mjs`: a deterministic arm red on base and green at head, and the `#19928` arm under 8 workers) → L3 required (AC-2, AC-4).

## Deltas from ticket

- **The e2e arm closes after the payload leaves.** A close inside `executeVdomUpdate`'s macrotask yield, before the payload is collected, ships the root's `removeDom`, mounts nothing, and passed on base. The arm closes right after `Neo.vdom.Helper.updateBatch` posts the payload, the order an `Escape` takes in the flake.
