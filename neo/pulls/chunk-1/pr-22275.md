Resolves #22271

Releases `Neo.main.addon.GridDragScroll`'s touch timer, document listeners, and tracking state without starting momentum on `touchcancel` / touch `pointercancel` (in both monitoring and active drag phases), on uncancelable compositor-claimed `touchmove` events, and on multi-contact partial lift, while pinning each single-contact stream to its owning `touch.identifier` so secondary contacts cannot overwrite live drag bookkeeping.

Evidence: L3 (live Chromium CDP multi-touch E2E on OrderReviewDesk across macOS + Linux containers + unit suite) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-3). No pre-merge residuals.

## Deltas from ticket
- Wired touch `pointercancel` alongside `touchcancel` in both monitoring (`onMonitorEnd`) and active drag (`onDragCancel`), and aborted monitoring/active drag when a `touchmove` arrives with `event.cancelable === false`: on Linux Chrome 158 (`e2e-engine` shard 3), CDP `touchCancel` and browser compositor scroll takeover emit `pointercancel` (and mark subsequent `touchmove` events uncancelable) without necessarily delivering `touchcancel`, which previously left `monitorDrag` / `activeDrag` armed or double-scrolling alongside native scroll.
- Called `event.preventDefault()` on the arming `touchmove` in `GridDragScroll#onMonitorMove` when promoting into `startDrag`, preventing Blink's compositor from locking in parallel native scrolling on threshold-crossing moves.
- Added `GridDragScroll#destroy` cleanup and rebased cleanly onto `dev` (`c51aa6828c`) atop `#22274`.
