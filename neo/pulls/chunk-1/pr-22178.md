Resolves #21974

Collects descendant `mergedCallbackMap` owners whose render subtrees are expanded by a covering ancestor VDOM flight via an O(1) `VDomUpdate#descendantMergedOwnerMap` reverse ancestor index, and preserves `needsVdomUpdate` on pruned sibling containers during sparse `depth > 1` updates so neither `Reconciler#reconcileShellStaged` (`host.promiseUpdate()`) nor sparse-pruned containers lose their owed callback settlement.

What shipped:
- **`src/manager/VDomUpdate.mjs` & `src/manager/Component.mjs`:** indexes `mergedCallbackMap` owners under their `parentId` ancestor chains in `descendantMergedOwnerMap` (mirroring `descendantInFlightMap`), refreshed on `parentId` change / unregister only when `mergedCallbackMap` is non-empty, giving O(1) `getParkedOwnersUnder(rootId)` lookup with zero global scans across unrelated trees.
- **`src/mixin/VdomLifecycle.mjs` (`executeVdomUpdate`, `getVdomUpdatePayload`):** collects same-window, non-updating, render-reachable (`parent.items` and `!vdom.removeDom`) parked merge owners covered by each collected root's payload (and transfers callbacks onto the covering root when an unmounted owner has `!owner.vnode`), and passes sparse `mergedChildIds` into `afterSetNeedsVdomUpdate`.
- **`src/container/Base.mjs` (`afterSetNeedsVdomUpdate`):** skips clearing `_needsVdomUpdate` on mounted items pruned out of a sparse `mergedChildIds` payload so their own owed flights still run.

Evidence: L3 (real Chromium SharedWorker component + Engine E2E verification across `WorkstationBoot.spec.mjs`, `DockReload`, `DockLock`, `ShallowUpdateFlags.spec.mjs`, `SparseUpdatesNL.spec.mjs`, plus hermetic unit specs) → L3 required (AC-1 to AC-3). No residuals.

### 🔬 Depth Floor

**Challenge:** the index is kept coherent by convention. `mergedCallbackMap` is a public `Collection`, and every writer now has to go through `registerMerged` / `removeMergedOwner`. The parked-owner loop then uses an index entry without checking `mergedCallbackMap.get(ownerId)`. A stale entry therefore makes a live component a collected scope, and building its payload resets its `_updateDepth`. Production writers are all converted today, so this is not blocking.
