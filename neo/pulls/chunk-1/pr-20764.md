Resolves #20762

Closes the pre-collection `timeout(1)` merge/wake gap in `Neo.manager.VDomUpdate` + `Neo.mixin.VdomLifecycle`, continues `isParentUpdating` past non-colliding intermediate scopes to covering in-flight ancestors, and guards `syncVnodeTree` / `unmountRemovedChildren` against unmounting components reparented into a container while an older pre-insert VDOM flight is in transit.

Evidence: L3 (VDOM unit suite, `SyncVnodeTreeProfile` hot-path budget, component suite, and multi-worker `YieldedMoveCoalescingNL` / `MountHookIsolation` / `KeyboardNavigationScroll` E2E specs) → L3 required (all four close-target ACs).

## Deltas from ticket
- **`VDomUpdate.hasDispatchedDescendants(ownerId, excludeId)` (`src/manager/VDomUpdate.mjs`, `src/mixin/VdomLifecycle.mjs#mergeIntoParentUpdate`)**: Wake-time `mergeIntoParentUpdate(me.parentId, 1, me.id)` requires `VDomUpdate.isAwaitingCollection(parent.id) || !VDomUpdate.hasDispatchedDescendants(parent.id, wakingChildId)` so a waking sibling does not fold into an ancestor that is already blocked behind another sibling's dispatched worker batch (`YieldedUpdateMerge.spec.mjs:401`), while still folding cleanly when all in-flight descendants are pre-collection or waking in the same window.
- **Wake-time `postUpdateQueueMap` / `preUpdateMap` retirement drain (`src/mixin/VdomLifecycle.mjs#executeVdomUpdate`)**: When a pre-collection initiator yields at wake time (`merged || isParentUpdating`), detaches and runs queued `postUpdates` and `preUpdate` hooks after clearing `isVdomUpdating` (mirroring `resolveVdomUpdate`) and refreshes `mergeIntoParentUpdate` so silent `updateDepth` escalations made inside `preUpdate` (e.g. `grid.View#createViewData`) propagate into `mergedCallbackMap` before ancestor collection.
- **Full ancestor collision walk (`src/mixin/VdomLifecycle.mjs#isParentUpdating`)**: Removed the early `return false` when an intermediate `parent.isVdomUpdating` scope does not collide at its own `updateDepth` (e.g. an absorbed intermediate container registered in flight at `depth: 1` inside a covering ancestor batch at `depth: -1`), ensuring descendants continue walking up `parentId` and park in the covering ancestor's `postUpdateQueueMap` instead of dispatching an overlapping `updateBatch` against the same pre-batch `vnode`.
