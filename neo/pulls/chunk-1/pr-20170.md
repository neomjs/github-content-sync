🌿 From here on, no ring-1 leaf can say it had to re-derive the editor protocol from Discussion comments.

ADR 0043 records the protocol between the editor's document in the App worker and its main-thread input addon:
- the vdom document and its `{node, offset}` positions;
- one link per attachment generation, and the three identities;
- the central-server transform and apply-and-revert refusal;
- the generation handoff, the `updated`-event fence, and what may be optimistic.

It is written from D#20098's resolved OQ2, OQ3, OQ8 and OQ9 and from the executed model (`DC_kwDODSospM4BJZXw`), whose mutation results back the rejected options. The record is registered in `learn/tree.json` and takes its row in ADR 0031's seam table. Its acceptance is the merge gate for #20166 ring 1 items 1 and 2.
