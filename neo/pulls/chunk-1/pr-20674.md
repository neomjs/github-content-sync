Resolves #20672

🌿 Two readers of one grid can no longer disagree on what a cell holds.

`Neo.grid.Container` gains `getCellValues({dataFields, mode, records})` and a `dataFields` getter, and `toCsv()` becomes the walk's first serializer. A reader outside the grid gets typed values for exactly the records and columns it names. `toCsv()` loses its inline record read, its copy of `column.getRecordValue` and its object branch: 12 code lines leave `src`, 32 arrive.

Related: #20667

Evidence: L3 (unit specs, plus the CSV download e2e in local Chrome) → L3 required (AC-7's e2e half). No residuals.

Change class: capability (`feat`). agent-preflight: not hosted here, so the class is declared and unverified.

Intake, self-authored in this session: `Prescription checked: src/grid/Container.mjs — owns the concern`. It holds the region arrays, the store and the columns, and already owns the serializer. The per-cell read stays with the column's `getRecordValue` and `exportRenderer`.

## Deltas from ticket

- **The walk looks its columns up in the region arrays, not in `grid.columns`.** My first draft used `columns.get(dataField)`. A comparison against the old `toCsv()` showed that it loses a column whose `dataField` changed at runtime, because the collection keeps a column under the name it was created with. The old `toCsv()` handled that case, so the walk has to. The collection's defect is out of scope, noted in `#20673`.
- **One test beyond the ACs** pins that case: "a column pointed at another field at runtime is read under its new dataField".

## Test Evidence

- **The e2e, outside CI** (it needs a Brain checkout): `env -u NEO_MCP_REMOTE_TOKEN NEO_NL_PORT=8294 NEO_AGENTOS_RUNTIME_ROOT=<brain checkout> npx playwright test -c test/playwright/playwright.config.e2e.mjs test/playwright/e2e/grid/GridExportCsvNL.spec.mjs --workers=1`: 1 passed at 69214894f4.
- **Old against new, in a throwaway spec that is not committed.** It holds the old `toCsv()`, copied verbatim from `dev`, and runs it beside the new one on a grid of 20,000 seeded rows × 10 columns. The data mixes strings with delimiters, quotes, line breaks and formula prefixes, numbers including negatives and `NaN`, dates, `null`, objects and arrays, and two `exportRenderer`s that return an object, nothing, a negative number or formula-like text. The two outputs are byte-identical for `{}`, `{delimiter: ';'}`, `{escapeFormulas: false}`, `{header: false}`, a tab delimiter without guard and header (1.85 to 1.89 MB each), and for a sorted and filtered store.
- **The case that failed first**, in the same spec: after `column.dataField = 't'`, the first draft returned `[[null]]` and the CSV `"S\r\n"`, where the old `toCsv()` returns `"S\r\ntwo"`. The shipped walk returns `[["two"]]` and the old CSV.
- **Timing**, same spec, 100,000 rows × 10 columns, median of 5: `toCsv()` took 315 ms before and 321 ms after. `getCellValues()` alone took 216 ms.
