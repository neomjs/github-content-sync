Resolves #20086

An application can now reveal a railed item over the dock layout and hold it open for as long as its own state needs it. The reveal leaves focus where it was, a press beside it no longer closes it, and the Workspace reports when the person ends it with `Escape` or the item's own tab. This is the entry neomjs/neo-agent-institution#791 needs: a card selection shows the Agent detail over the roster, instead of opening a column that moves every card.

- **`RevealStateMachine`** gains `revealed-held`, entered by `hold(itemId)` and left by `release(itemId)`. Pointer, focus, hover and outside inputs leave it alone. `escape()`, the item's own tab and `itemCleared` end it, as they end every reveal.
- **`Rail#reveal` / `#dismissReveal`** drive the held state for an item on that rail, and the outside-pointer listener stays off while it is held. `RevealOverlay.VISIBLE_STATES` draws it.
- **`Workspace#revealRailItem` / `#dismissRailReveal`** find the rail through `forEachDockRail`. The Workspace relays every rail reveal change as its own `dockRailRevealChange`, threaded through the projection context beside `onDockZoneDocumentChange`.
- **Docs:** ADR 0029 §2.7 gains the app-held reveal row, and its Status gains the amendment line. `DockLayoutsFeatures.md` names the new reveal beside the click and hover reveals.

Prescription checked: `src/dashboard/dock/interaction/RevealStateMachine.mjs` owns the concern. The machine owns every reveal input's policy, and `Rail.mjs` owns the listener and the focus move that policy gates. No consumer-side layer reaches either.

Evidence: L2 (unit + component tier in headless Chromium, red-first on `dev` sources) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-3; AC-4 is docs). Residual: none.
