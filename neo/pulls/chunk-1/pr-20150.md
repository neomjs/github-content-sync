Resolves #20090

The moveItem test now brackets the Workspace's own FLIP signal (`.neo-dock-workspace` plus `.neo-dashboard-dock-animating`) instead of any element that carries the class.

The move also re-projects two tab strips, and their header toolbars raise and drop the same class within about 25 ms. On a slow runner that closed the bracket before the Workspace's FLIP began. The witness then stopped with the pane still at its old position ("from 1,70 to 1,70"). The shape and the fix are the same as #19894's for the reveal overlay.

Evidence: L2 (local whitebox e2e, 8× CDP CPU throttle, private `NEO_NL_PORT`) → L2 required (AC-1, AC-2); AC-3 is hosted runs of this PR. Residual: none.
