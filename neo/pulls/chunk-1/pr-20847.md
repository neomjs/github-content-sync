Resolves #20846

🌿 A store can no longer insist that every row it holds is one of its leaves.

`Store#modelOf(item)` (protected) answers the model a raw row derives its fields from and becomes a record of. It returns `this.model` unless a subclass overrides it. The four places a store turns a raw row into values or a record ask it: `createRecord()`, `hydrateRecord()`, `resolveField()` and `resolveRow()`.

A store that projects rows of its own shape answers those rows with their own model. The first such store is Pro's grouping store, with its group rows (neomjs/neo-pro#185). The leaf fields' `convert` and `type` then stop parsing computed aggregates.

Change class: capability (`feat`). It adds a protected seam, and the default changes no store.

size-guard-growth: src/data/Store.mjs — the seam's 4 code lines take the file from 1000 to 1004; without it, each store holding rows of another shape would duplicate hydrateRecord's map replacement and TreeStore's healing

Nothing is deleted, because the seam prevents a duplication rather than replacing one: the first consumer, neomjs/neo-pro#185, overrides one 3-line method.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms on the real `Store` and `TreeStore`) → L2 required (every AC is unit-observable). Residual: none.
