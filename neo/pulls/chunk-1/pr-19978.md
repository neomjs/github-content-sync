Resolves #19977

A column drop's layout restore clamps the header toolbar's `scrollLeft` to 0 for a moment. Since `#19957`, `GridHorizontalScrollSync#onHeaderScroll` routed that clamp into the scrollbar, and the whole grid lost its horizontal scroll. In `HeaderCellRectSync.spec.mjs`, pass 2 then pressed its header off-screen. Now:

- `onHeaderScroll()` routes a header scroll only while the header can hold the scrollbar's position. A header that cannot reach it was clamped by layout, not scrolled.
- The drop re-applies the scroll truth once the toolbar's own update has landed (`await owner.promiseUpdate()`), not after a fixed 30 ms.

Evidence: L3 (local headless Chromium, `playwright.config.e2e.mjs`: a deterministic clamp arm red on base and green at head, and the flaky arm under 6 workers) → L3 required (AC-3, AC-4).
