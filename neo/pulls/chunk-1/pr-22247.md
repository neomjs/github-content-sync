Resolves #22234

Adds `Neo.button.Segmented` (`ntype: 'segmented-button'`), a single-choice segmented button group with one bindable value. The public name describes the UI control that applications request. It extends `Neo.toolbar.Base` to share keyboard navigation. Arrow keys move focus and Enter or Space selects the focused option. Updating the choices retains surviving button instances and their selection when possible. The Board card-design example uses the control in place of its three button handlers and manual pressed-state loop.

Evidence: L3 (real Chromium pointer, keyboard, disabled-choice, programmatic, five-theme and two-viewport Board journeys; unit state and binding checks) → L3 required.

## Deltas from ticket

An enclosing keyboard-enabled toolbar could handle the same ArrowRight after the inner control had already moved focus, pulling focus back to its outer sibling. Navigator now leaves a key consumed by a nested subscription alone. A Chromium component regression exercises that case through the normal subscription API.
