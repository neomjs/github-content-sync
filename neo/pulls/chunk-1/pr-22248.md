Resolves #22245

Re-syncs the centre grid header toolbar after column drag-end/cancel through `GridHorizontalScrollSync#sync` (`ScrollManager#syncHorizontalScroll`) instead of writing the App Worker's cached `owner.scrollLeft` directly via `DomAccess.scrollTo`. Reading the live Main-thread `.neo-grid-horizontal-scrollbar` `scrollLeft` and updating `registration.headerScrollLeft` preserves `#12929`'s reflow-clamp recovery while preventing a late drag-end restore from overwriting a newer Main-thread scroll and triggering an `onHeaderScroll` rollback onto the scrollbar.

Evidence: L3 (Playwright unit, browser component, and Whitebox E2E suites) → L3 required (AC-1..AC-4).
