## Deltas from ticket
- `PopupViewport#setAppRecord(record)` (paired with `PopupViewport#setReturnHandler(handler)` in `WindowTearOut`) projects the hosted app's colored icon tile (`.neo-icon.neo-desktop-app-window-icon`, compounded at top level in `AppWindow.css` so `color` and `font-size` outrank `component/Icon.css`) and title (`.neo-desktop-popup-title`) on the left of `desktopPopupToolbar` with `Back to desktop` anchored on the right, keeping window identity when multiple apps pop out.
- `.neo-viewport.neo-desktop-popup-viewport .neo-container` sets `--container-background-color: transparent` so child containers stamped with `.neo-theme-*` by `Container#afterSetTheme` during live theme switches do not re-bind opaque `#fff` or `#0d0f12` over the glass canvas.
