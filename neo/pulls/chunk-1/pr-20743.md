Resolves #20741

Sets `me.needsVdomUpdate = true` when `Neo.mixin.VdomLifecycle#updateVdom` defers execution to `isParentUpdating`, `isChildUpdating`, or `themeFilesLoaded` (leaving `!mounted` untouched), so descendant updates triggered before the deferred post-update runs merge into the pending ancestor cycle via `mergeIntoParentUpdate` instead of launching disjoint VDOM flights. Also invokes `beforeExecuteVdomUpdate` before dispatch and `afterExecuteVdomUpdate` across live (`!root.isDestroyed`) co-emitted disjoint roots in `executeVdomUpdate` (`emittedRoots`) after destruction and reply-completeness checks, and moves `Neo.grid.View`'s content-sized row measurement trigger from `afterExecuteVdomUpdate()` to `afterVnodeSync(deltas)` (`deltas !== null`) so structural covering-ancestor renders (`updateDepth: -1`) still schedule row measurement when absorbing `View`.

Change class: `restoration` -> `fix` (`agent-preflight`: not hosted here; baseline `check-whitespace`, `check-shorthand`, `check-jsdoc-types`, `check-block-alignment`, `check-parse`, `check-derived-domain`, `check-fixed-sleeps`, `check-content-root-derivations`, `check-class-module-scope`, `check-engine-brain-boundary`, `check-file-sizes`, `check-spec-retirement`, `generate-docs-json`, `neo-agent-skills-ticket-archaeology`, `neo-agent-skills-secrets` ran).

**Post-merge challenge, asked for by the operator on 2026-11-11.** Measured on this PR's parent `c7e1974483` and its merge commit `5f407d6956` with one counting spec in the unit harness. The full tables, the method and the spec are on #20747.

**Verdict: keep the merge, and close what it opened.**

- **It does not add operations.** Worker batches fall (4 to 2, 7 to 2, 30 to 20); payloads, bytes and deltas are equal or lower; the split move becomes one `moveNode`. A grid with content-sized rows mounts in 9 batches and 104 deltas instead of 18 and 115.
- **It fixes a starvation nobody claimed.** With two children updating back to back, one parent update waited 152 ms on the base, until the test stopped the children. Here it lands in under 2 ms.
- **It costs isolation.** Once an ancestor has yielded, every update below it waits for the flight in the air, including updates that share no node with it: five disjoint leaf updates landed at 3 ms on the base and at 19 ms here, behind a 16 ms flight. The guide `AsymmetricUpdates.md` names exactly this isolation as the default, and this PR did not touch the guide.
- **A wedged flight now freezes more.** With a flight that never settles, a sibling, a cousin and their branch kept updating on the base. Here all three are parked under the root as soon as the root asks for one update. Round 1 named this as a hypothesis; it was answered with code pointers and not run. The failure and destroy paths do release (run: they hold).
- **`await component.set({})` on a yielded component** settles with the deferred flight (1.3 ms before, 17.8 ms here).
- **Content-sized grids** run one measure pass per covering render, in memory, with no main-thread call. `View#afterVnodeSync`'s `deltas &&` is always true for an update render.

No spec holds any of this, in either direction. A prototype of "yield only on a collision" lost a child's second request through `Container#afterSetNeedsVdomUpdate`, so that is no quick repair.
