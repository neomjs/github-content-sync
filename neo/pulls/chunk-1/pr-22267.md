Resolves #22264

Adds `touchcancel` and `touchend` to `globalDomEvents` in `Neo.manager.DomEvent` (matching Main's body-level `touchEvents` registration) so ordinary component touch listeners neither double-fire through a duplicate local listener nor lose the tap's synthesized native `click` via `DomEvents#domEventListener`'s `default:` `event.preventDefault()`.

Evidence: L2 (unit and touch-enabled Chromium component tests via Playwright `tap()` and CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`) → L2 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-3, AC-4).

|---|---|
| AC-1 | CI-covered: `test/playwright/component/main/TouchEndTapClick.spec.mjs` and `test/playwright/unit/manager/domEvent/TouchGlobalEvents.spec.mjs` (one native touch tap invokes a component `touchend` listener once). |

## Deltas from ticket
- Confirmed the exact click-loss mechanism requested in the ticket: because `touchend` was absent from `globalDomEvents`, `DomEvent#mountDomListeners` attached a local `{name: 'touchend', handler: 'domEventListener'}` listener on Main; on a non-scrolling tap, `touchend.cancelable === true`, so `DomEvents#domEventListener`'s `default:` branch called `event.preventDefault()` (`src/main/DomEvents.mjs:305-307`), suppressing the browser's synthesized `click` while `touchend` still bubbled to `document.body`'s `onTouchEnd` for a second worker dispatch.
