## Deltas from ticket

- Hoisting `GroupPage.css`'s `24 px` (`16 px` phone) inline padding from inner children onto `.portal-examples-group-page` fixed a second container-query split-brain at `1366×768`: on `dev`, `.portal-examples-group-page` measured `1102 px >= 1100 px` (forcing 3 sub-minimum `335.3 px` columns via `List.css:62`) while `.neo-list-item:first-child` queried `examples-list` at `1054 px < 1100 px` (losing its `2×2` lead span).
- Accounting for `alsoIn` membership, the six `3n + 4` groups in `devmode` are `Forms & Fields` (4), `Dialogs & Overlays` (4), `Buttons, Menus & Toolbars` (4), `Lists & Galleries` (7), `Scheduler` (13), and `Apps` (10); `Workers & Platform` (5) and `Multi-Window` (12) include `alsoIn` entries, and `expectCardLayout` now enforces `docked` `1×1` layout across every group.
