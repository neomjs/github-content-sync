Resolves #19953

The grid's filter summary now sits in the search controls' row instead of on a row of its own. Checking the first header filter, or unchecking the last, no longer pushes the grid and its open value menu down by 52px. This came from outside feedback on the 13.3.0 showcase.

- **`src/grid/SearchToolbar.mjs`:** the toolbar is one wrapping row (`hbox`, `wrap`, `gap: 8px`) instead of a `vbox`.
  - The controls take `flex: '1 1 24rem'` and grow.
  - The summary takes `flex: '0 1 auto'`, so its chips and *Clear filters* sit at the row's right edge.
  - The summary wraps below the controls only when the row is too narrow, which keeps #19794's narrow-width reachability.
  - The entries are content-sized (`0 1 auto`), so a chip and *Clear* stay on one line. With the old `12rem` basis, the summary measured 279px wide and still broke into two 48px lines.
- **`resources/scss/src/grid/SearchToolbar.scss`:** drops the `width: 100%` that forced each part onto its own row and the summary's `margin-top`, and matches the entries' `flex`.

Evidence: L3 (browser e2e on the showcase, red on `dev`, green here) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-3). AC-2's narrow-width arm is in the Neural Link tier, so CI's `e2e-brain` runs it. Locally the Neural Link bridge refused authentication.
