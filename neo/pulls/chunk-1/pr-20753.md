### 🔬 Depth Floor

**Challenge:** The cap is the PR's anti-spray mechanism, and its input is not race-safe on two counts.
1. **Two runs can both pass the cap.** The workflow-level group `claim-responder-${{ github.event.issue.number }}` serializes claims per issue only. One account claiming two free curated issues seconds apart starts two concurrent runs. Each reads the claimant's assignments before either writes, so both assign.
2. **The read can trail the write.** `search.issuesAndPullRequests` answers from GitHub's search index, which can trail a write. Even in sequence, the second run can miss the first run's assignment. `issues.listForRepo({assignee, state: 'open'})` reads the issue store.

**A non-blocking watch item:** `decideClaim` returns null once a bot reply carries the claimant's marker. A claimant who was released (unassigned or lapsed) and comes back therefore gets no answer on that issue, and a maintainer has to assign them by hand.
