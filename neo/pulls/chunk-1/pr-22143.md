**Glance:** The flake was a +96 px scroll-snap shift. A bare `Tab` from outside the active stage, followed by `node.focus()`, let the browser scroll to the newly focused card before the position assertion (`expect(focused.position).toEqual(rest.position)`, `HomeAudienceDoorsNL.spec.mjs:532`). The fix awaits the spec's own `waitForHomeStylesAndFonts` (line 70) before measuring. It then focuses `cards.nth(1)` with `preventScroll: true` and reaches the first card with `Shift+Tab`, so keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`) still lands on the card the assertion reads, without leaving its row. I looked for a weakened assertion and found none: the position comparison and the focus target are unchanged. Exact-head CI is green at `2146315ce5` (32 pass), and the author's 10/10 repeat run covers both themes.
