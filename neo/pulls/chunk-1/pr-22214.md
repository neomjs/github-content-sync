## Deltas from ticket
- **`buildScripts/util/fileServer.mjs` (`findMount`) & `apps/portal/neo-config.json`:** Scoped `apps/portal/neo-config.json` `/examples/...` exclusion to trailing-slash paths and non-catalog 2-segment demos, and gated hash-app slashless yield in `fileServer.mjs` on `rootClaimsPath` so the `21` catalog groups serve Portal (`200`) while non-catalog demos (`/examples/dialog?theme=dark`) preserve `301 /examples/dialog/?theme=dark` (`fileServer.spec.mjs`, `RouteWindowIdAudit.spec.mjs`).
- **`src/Neo.mjs`, `src/DefaultConfig.mjs` & `src/main/DomEvents.mjs` (`resolveRouterBasePath`):** Adopted `<base href>`'s pathname as `Neo.config.routerBasePath` when `'pathname'` mode is mounted under that sub-path (`--no-spa-root`) without an explicit `routerBasePath` override in `neo-config.json`, preserving root mounts and explicit `routerBasePath: ''` overrides (`DomEvents.spec.mjs`, `PathnameRoutingNL.spec.mjs`).

- **`src/Main.mjs` (`redirectTo`) & `src/controller/Application.mjs` (`afterSetMainView`):** Delegated `#/...` targets in `Main#redirectTo` to `DomEvents#setRoute` in `'pathname'` mode so `<base href>` does not turn internal hash redirects into document reloads (`core/PathnameRouting.spec.mjs`), and read per-window `Neo.windowConfigs[windowId].hash` for initial deep-route render delay in `SharedWorker` mode.
- **`test/playwright/dedicatedWorkerNeuralLink.mjs` (`NAVIGATION_URL`):** Widened the pre-navigation SharedWorker CDP release matcher from `.html`/`/` to non-asset clean pathnames (`/learn/...`, `/docs/Neo.component.Base`; pinned in `sharedWorkerAppWorker.spec.mjs`).
