Resolves #20688
Related: #20671, #20700

Adds `getSeoJson()` and `--format seo-json` to `buildScripts/docs/seo/generate.mjs` (backed by `buildScripts/docs/seo/seoJson.mjs` and wired into `buildScripts/docs/rebuildContentIndexesAndSeo.mjs`, populating `apps/portal/resources/data/seo.json` with 160 total route entries at head: 10 curated top-level routes preserved directly from `apps/portal/resources/data/seo.json` plus 150 generated deep routes across 125 `/learn/*`, 24 `/news/blog/*`, and 1 `/news/releases/*` in the engine checkout), extends `Neo.main.addon.DocumentHead#update` to support `tags: Object[]` and unambiguous named `ldJson` (`{name, value}`) wrappers (including `value: null` removal) without overwriting `data-schema-name="neo-organism"` or misclassifying plain Schema.org objects, and updates `Portal.service.Seo` to emit route-specific `canonicalUrl` (`https://neomjs.com/` for `/home`), `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:url`, and `data-schema-name="neo-route"` `ld+json` on exact route matches while clearing `neo-route` on parent-segment fallback hits.

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit suites covering `generate.mjs`, `seoJson.mjs`, `rebuildContentIndexesAndSeo.mjs`, `DocumentHead.mjs`, and `Portal.service.Seo`) → L2 required (AC-1 through AC-4).
