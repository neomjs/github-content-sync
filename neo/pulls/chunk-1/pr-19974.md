Resolves #19973

A floating component added as a container's item renders inside its parent's node, so hiding it (`removeDom` plus the parent's update) removes its DOM. `unmountRemovedChildren()` skipped every floating child, so the hidden one kept `mounted: true` with no DOM until a deferred unmount. A show inside that window ran no mount hooks, and the reopened menu in `SpawnAnimation.spec.mjs` never aligned. `unmountRemovedChildren()` now tests a floating child too when its own vdom carries `removeDom`.

Evidence: L3 (local headless Chromium, `playwright.config.component.mjs`: a deterministic browser arm red on base and green at head, and the flaky arm under 6 workers at head) → L3 required (AC-2, AC-4).
