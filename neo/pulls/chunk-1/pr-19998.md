Resolves #19991

The 100k Matrix pane froze its App Worker when no ancestor gave it a height. Its view measured the body's full scroll height as the viewport (5,000,050 px), so `createRowPool` created 99,970 Rows. The batch `push(...newRows)` then threw past V8's argument limit after every one of them had registered its id. Each later pass re-created those ids, which produced the `[Row, Row]` flood. The measured chain is in the [intake comment](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/19991#issuecomment-6339978228).

- `resources/scss/src/grid/Container.scss`: `max-height: 100vh`. A grid is never taller than its window, so the measurement stays a real viewport and the grid still scrolls inside it.
- `Neo.grid.Body#createRowPool`: one `push` per created Row, so no batch can hit the argument limit and every registered row sits in the pool.

Evidence: L3 (headless Chrome, Workstation on this checkout: red on `dev`, green here) → L3 required (AC-1). AC-2 is a unit contract (L2).

## Deltas from ticket
- The ticket's "clamp `availableRows`" is a CSS bound instead: it makes the measurement correct at its source, needs no second main-thread read, and leaves a grid that still scrolls.
- **`100vh` census, per Ada's challenge.** No grid changes height. The census covered 17 app/example pages with grids (colors, email, finance, legit, workstation and the 12 grid examples) plus the Portal Grids guide's 6 live previews, at 1440×900, 1280×720 and 375×667. With the cap neutralised, no grid container exceeds its window, and no page document scrolls. The guide previews are 344 px inside their preview panel. Consumers outside this repo were not measured.
