Resolves #20699

A grid whose fixed-height rows reach past the browser's maximum scroll height now scrolls to its last row. The shape is pages, not scale; the reasons are in [the intake](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20699#issuecomment-6766690775).

- **The split:** a new View-owned `Neo.grid.ScrollPages` divides the logical range into pages over the physical one.
- **Inside a page:** scrolling stays 1:1 with the compositor.
- **Crossing into another page:** the logical offset stays put. The physical offset moves through the View's existing scroll correction (`scrollCorrection` plus `scrollEpoch`), in the update that renders the moved rows.
- **The range:** every logical offset maps inside the physical range, at any extent/cap ratio. A page's offset grows by less than a page, so near the end of a long range the offsets that a page's rows cannot reach go to the next page.
- **Thumb drags and jumps:** a thumb drag picks its page in proportion to the thumb's position. A jump to an end of the physical range, a move of more than a viewport, picks the first or the last page. A scroll that the browser stops at an end keeps the logical offset.
- **The cap:** `Neo.main.DomAccess#getMaxScrollHeight` measures it once per window, and the view stays 1% below it.

Evidence: L2 (unit: the page math, plus a mounted grid with a lowered cap) and L3 (whitebox e2e over 2,000,000 rows, local Chrome) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-4). The Firefox and WebKit witness is below. Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket
- **Content-sized rows past the cap stop at the cap,** with a named error, rather than keeping today's range. Gecko drops a taller scroll range to 1 px, as measured below, so in Firefox today's range is no range at all.
- **The cap probe is a binary search.** Because of that same collapse, a "huge child, read `scrollHeight`" probe returns 1 in Gecko.
- **The two paths share one correction queue.** A private correction queue in `grid.View` was split out of the anchor correction, and page crossings and keyboard jumps use it too.
- **The scroll manager follows the page.** When a jump or a thumb drag moves the logical offset, `syncBodies` hands the new offset to the scroll manager. Without that, its buffered scroll-end re-sync landed mid-range (the e2e found this; the unit spec now asserts it).
- **A thumb drag owns the scroll offset**, after Ada's read of the correction channel.
  - **The race:** a crossing's correction could be in flight when the thumb is pressed. Landing during the drag, it moved the offset under the thumb by a page step, and the worker paged the drag's reports one step off.
  - **The guard:** `GridRowScrollPinning` holds a correction that lands during a drag, once its batch commits. It adopts the correction's epoch and lets the pin hold the moved rows. The release applies the held correction, unless the thumb moved after it (Ada's second read: a drop alone left the next wheel scroll one page off), or a later render for another place retired it (Euclid's read). The View pages a drag from the offset the main thread reported.
  - **The contract change:** the addon spec's race arms keep their end state (1,380). The correction now applies at the release, not under the thumb.
- **A store load resets a paged grid through `View#scrollToOffset(0)`.** `Body#onStoreLoad` used a main-thread scroll to 0, which needed the first page to answer any report at 0. With the ends gated on jumps, the view moves the rows and the offset to the top together.
- **Two `Row` test fakes gained members.** `RowCellIdMigration` and `TreeRowAria` now carry `getRowOffset` and `pageOffset`, which `Row#createVdom` reads.
