Resolves #20722

🌿 A selection model's rules have a home of their own, so nobody has to borrow a component's stylesheet for them.

`Neo.selection.Model` gains a reactive `windowId`. `register()` takes it from the view and follows it with `observeConfig`, `afterSetWindowId` inserts the model's theme files, and `unregister()` stops following. It is the shape `plugin.Base`, `layout.Base` and `grid.column.Base` already have. The engine's own models ship no theme files, so nothing loads for them.

Evidence: L3 (unit arms in CI, and one real-browser run of a model class with a theme file) → L3 required (AC-5).
