## Deltas from ticket
- Updated `test/playwright/e2e/portal/PathnameRoutingNL.spec.mjs:248` (`aliasRedirect` `/apps/portal/?utm_source=test`) from `301` to `307` to match AC-3, and added a live browser hash-mode `spaRoot` boot + in-document route transition test (`globalThis.sameDocument === true`) in `test/playwright/e2e/core/PathnameRouting.spec.mjs` alongside the Node `fileServer.spec.mjs` fixture.
- Fixed `<base href="/<spaRoot>/">` hash-navigation document reloads in `src/main/DomEvents.mjs` (`handlePathnameLinkClick`) and `src/Main.mjs` (`redirectTo`): when a hash-mode app runs at `/` under `spaRoot` with `<base href="/<spaRoot>/">`, clicking `<a href="#/…">` or calling `Neo.Main.redirectTo({url: '#/…'})` previously resolved against `document.baseURI` to `/<spaRoot>/#/…`, triggering a `307` round-trip and full-page reload (caught in CI by `PathnameRouting.spec.mjs`). Both paths now update `location.hash` in place without reloading the document or workers (`DomEvents.spec.mjs` + `PathnameRouting.spec.mjs`).
- Scanned uncommented HTML slices via index bounds (`hasBaseHref`) in `buildScripts/util/fileServer.mjs` instead of `.replace(/<!--[\s\S]*?-->/g, '')` (`CodeQL` `js/incomplete-multi-character-sanitization`).
