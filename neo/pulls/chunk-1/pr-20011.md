Resolves #19983

Adds an opt-in hover trigger (`showOnHover_` on `Neo.toolbar.Base` and `headerActionsShowOnHover_` on `Neo.dashboard.dock.Workspace`, threaded through `LayoutAdapter.project` and `Reconciler`) that reveals a dock pane's focus-gated header actions on pointer hover while `Neo.tab.plugin.Overflow` reserves the full revealed action-cluster span up front so hovering into and out of a dense tab strip never shifts its overflow partition.
