Resolves #22125

Releases departed popup `OffscreenCanvas` surfaces and per-window `canvasWindowMap` / `Sparkline` / `canvas.Base` renderer bindings across both native window close and Group Undo returns while keeping surviving opener canvases live and painting.

Root cause: under Group Undo, the travelling Feed pane moves back to the opener while the popup is still connected, whereas closing the popup via its native window control retires the popup's port first (`App#removePort`), leaving recycled popup-only cell IDs in `Neo.worker.Canvas#map` and leaving the departed popup's `windowId` slots inside `canvasWindowMap[nodeId]` even after the returning Feed sparklines re-register in the opener. In addition, a cross-window container move with `removeFromPreviousParent === false` resets only the root container's `_mounted` silently, leaving pooled row cells `mounted === true` across the window hop or triggering transfer before the destination window's `<canvas>` DOM delta lands.

What shipped:
- **`src/worker/App.mjs` (`removePort`, `onCanvasRegistered`):** relays `{action: 'releaseWindowCanvases', canvasGroup, windowId}` to the shared group's Canvas worker whenever a member window's last App Worker port retires while the group remains active, and gates `onCanvasRegistered` on matching `windowId`.
- **`src/worker/Canvas.mjs`:** scopes `unregisterCanvas({nodeId, windowId})` per window (preserving or promoting surviving window surfaces in `map[nodeId]` while deleting empty entries), adds `releaseWindowCanvases` / `onReleaseWindowCanvases` + `removePort` cleanup, drops late `registerCanvasDirect` transfers from departed `windowId`s, and carries `windowId` on `canvasRegistered`.
- **`src/main/DomAccess.mjs` & `src/component/{Canvas,Sparkline}.mjs` & `src/canvas/{Base,Sparkline}.mjs`:** reports transfer outcome from `transferCanvasToWorker`, tracks `#mountGeneration` on `Canvas` to cancel superseded mounts, retries briefly on `'missing-node'` while the destination window's DOM delta lands, re-registers when `windowId` moves while a descendant remains `mounted === true`, and guards `Sparkline#unregister` + `canvas.Base#clearGraph` / `waitForCanvas` by `(canvas, windowId)` + generation token.

Evidence: L3 (real Chromium SharedWorker CDP registry inspection + Neural Link pixel-paint verification across 3 consecutive pop-out/return cycles for both `native-close` and `group-undo` in `WorkstationPopOutSparklinesNL.spec.mjs`, plus hermetic unit specs) → L3 required (AC-1 to AC-3). No residuals.
