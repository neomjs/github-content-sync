Resolves #19986

Gates dock drag hit-testing (`resolveHoverTarget` and `resolveEdgeRailHover`) inside `Neo.dashboard.SortZone` behind `findMaximizedPaneId(dockCoordinator.document)` when a dock pane is maximized (`maximizedPaneId !== null`), restricting valid drop targets to the maximized pane itself (`resolved.paneId === maximizedPaneId`) and returning `null` for edge rails and background panes covered by the `.neo-dashboard-dock-maximized` overlay (`z-index: 40`).

Evidence: L4 (hosted `E2E (Brain-gated tier)` and `Engine Tests/unit` green at `975046247c`; local `DashboardSortZoneMaximizeGuard.spec.mjs` 3/3 and `WorkstationMaximizedTabDragNL.spec.mjs` 1/1 green) → L4 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-3, AC-4). Residual: none.

## AC Evidence
| AC-1 | `src/dashboard/SortZone.mjs` (`resolveHoverTarget`, `resolveEdgeRailHover`) + `test/playwright/unit/dashboard/DashboardSortZoneMaximizeGuard.spec.mjs` (`3/3` pass) — hovering coordinates outside the maximized pane's tab strip returns `null` (no preview, no `activeHoverTarget`), while hovering the maximized pane's own strip resolves `{paneId: maximizedPaneId, zone: 'tab'}`. |
| AC-2 | `test/playwright/e2e/workstation/WorkstationMaximizedTabDragNL.spec.mjs` — dragging `Throughput` to `(900, 700)` while Center Dock is maximized leaves `maximizedPaneId: 'workstation-center'`, `dockpane-5` does not exist, and `Throughput` remains in `workstation-center`. |
| AC-3 | `test/playwright/e2e/workstation/WorkstationMaximizedTabDragNL.spec.mjs` — dragging `Throughput` past `Latency` inside the maximized Center Dock tab strip reorders tabs to `['Latency', 'Throughput', 'Allocation']` while `maximizedPaneId` stays `'workstation-center'`. |
| AC-4 | `test/playwright/e2e/workstation/WorkstationMaximizedTabDragNL.spec.mjs` (`1/1` pass) + `test/playwright/e2e/workstation/WorkstationMaximizeRestoreNL.spec.mjs` (`1/1` pass) — restoring the maximized pane restores normal cross-pane dock drag targets. |

## Deltas from ticket
None substantive — uses `findMaximizedPaneId(dockCoordinator.document)` from `src/dashboard/dock/model/Queries.mjs` directly inside `SortZone#resolveHoverTarget` and `SortZone#resolveEdgeRailHover` as specified by the ticket's design authority.
