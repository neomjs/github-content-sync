Resolves #19830

Destroying a functional component now ends its render flight, as it already did for a classic one. #14923's cancellation boundary moves from `component.Base#destroy` to the top of `component.Abstract#destroy`: `rejectCallbacks`, `unregisterInFlightUpdate` and `triggerPostUpdates`. `Abstract` mixes in `VdomLifecycle`, which registers the flights, and both component families extend it. A functional component destroyed mid-flight therefore no longer leaves its in-flight entry, its wedge watchdog or its place in each ancestor's `descendantInFlightMap`. Its parent's next update settles, and no false "vdom update wedged" report follows, which removes the late watchdog error behind the `ViewLoadingReprojection.spec.mjs` unit flake. For classic components the destroy order is unchanged: Base's own teardown, then the boundary, then `Abstract`'s DOM-event and registry teardown.

Evidence: L2 (a unit arm drives a real functional flight through `VdomLifecycle`, holds its batch, and removes the child from a mounted container mid-flight) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-3 are registry and promise contracts at the `VDomUpdate` boundary; AC-4 is the suites). Residual: none.

agent-preflight: not hosted here; the pre-commit hooks ran. Change class: restoration (`fix`).
Prescription checked: `src/component/Abstract.mjs` owns the concern. It mixes in `VdomLifecycle`, the flight registrar, and its `destroy()` is the one both component families reach. Duplicating the calls in `FunctionalBase` would fork the boundary, and a manager-side unregister hook would couple two managers.

## Deltas from ticket
- The arm removes the child the way an app does, with `parent.remove(child)` on a **mounted** parent. An unmounted parent defers its own update, so it can never yield to the child. A bare `child.destroy()` leaves the parent's vdom naming the dead child, and the parent's next update then fails in `createDeltas`.
- `Abstract#destroy` gained an `@summary`, as the commit gate requires for a modified method, and reads `id` once.
- Out of scope, noted in a defect-note: `FunctionalBase#destroy` forwards its documented `updateParentVdom` to `Abstract#destroy`, which takes no parameters. Container removal is unaffected, because `removeAt` splices the vdom itself.
