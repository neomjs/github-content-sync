Resolves #19982

Maximize and restore resize a dock pane through a DockFlip. The node takes its new layout box at once, while an inverse transform plays it from the old rect. The resize-driven overflow pass lands inside that window. It read the visual box (`getDomRect`) and partitioned the strip for a width the pane no longer had, and since a settling transform fires no further resize, the stale split stayed until the next activation. `Overflow#project()` now reads the natural widths and the strip extent through `getLayoutRect()`, which transforms never affect, so every DockFlip window (maximize, restore, any other animated resize) partitions for the settled size.

Evidence: L3 (headless Chromium: CI's `e2e-brain` tier runs the new spec at head; the red on base is a local run) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-4, AC-5).

## Deltas from ticket

- The coordinate arm needed no size-arm-only branch. The header toolbar is `position: static` and its actions are its children, so both read `offsetLeft` against the same offset parent: probed live, that is the dock edge zone at rest and the fixed tab container when maximized. A comment at the read records that contract.
- Headless at 1440×900 the stale maximized pane shows 5 of 12 headers; the operator saw 2. Where the pass lands in the FLIP decides the count, which is why the oracle compares against a forced re-projection rather than an expected number.
- The unit stubs follow the source: `Overflow.spec.mjs` and `DockLayoutAdapter.spec.mjs` stub `getLayoutRect` now.
