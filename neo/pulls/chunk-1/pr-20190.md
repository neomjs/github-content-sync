Resolves #20188

Moves `role: 'grid'`, `'aria-colcount'`, and `'aria-rowcount'` from `.neo-grid-container`'s outer node onto an inner `getAriaRoot()` wrapper around `headerWrapper` and `view` (`src/grid/Container.mjs`), leaving `searchToolbar` as a sibling outside the ARIA `grid` subtree. In `Neo.grid.header.Button#wrapColumnMenuRoot`, moves `role: 'columnheader'`, `'aria-colindex'`, `'aria-description'`, and `'aria-sort'` from the inner sort `<button>` onto the `.neo-grid-menu-wrapper` `<div>` (with `'aria-labelledby': this.id`), keeping both the sort button and the column menu trigger inside a single `columnheader` across initial construction, runtime `columnMenu` wrapping, sorting, filtering, and column index updates.

`agent-preflight`: not hosted here; baseline unit, component, and e2e grid suites ran (`change-class: restoration`).
