Resolves #20017

A warm reload no longer leaves the remounted Workstation panes hidden.

**Cause.** If the reload landed while a staged dock projection was running, the projection failed on its dead port. The refresh queue then went on projecting into the departed window, where nothing renders. The Group's adoption of the root into the next window moved the host mid-projection. The host's next `promiseUpdate()` parked while the host was mounted but had no vnode, and no flight ever claimed it. The projection hung, and its staged shell stayed `visibility: hidden` over the panes it had taken. The trace is on the ticket: https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/20017#issuecomment-6359629749

**Fix.** `Workspace#refreshDockWorkspace` no longer projects into a departed window. It parks the newest such projection as `departedProjection`, and `afterSetMounted` runs it on the next mount, chained like any re-projection. It still stands down once a newer commit has been scheduled. The chain shape that `onDockProjectionFailed` used inline is now `chainProjection()`, shared by both callers.

The engine hole underneath is captured as a defect-note for triage after the cut, not patched here: a `promiseUpdate()` parked on a mounted, vnode-less component that no flight claims.

size-guard-growth: src/dashboard/dock/Workspace.mjs — +12 code lines for the departed-window park and its replay on mount; the repair's inline chain (−11) became the shared `chainProjection`, so the only net growth is the park itself.

Evidence: L3 (local Chromium e2e: the warm-F5 arm alone, red on dev, green on head; unit arm red-first) → L3 required (AC-2, AC-4 are local runs). See Test Evidence.
