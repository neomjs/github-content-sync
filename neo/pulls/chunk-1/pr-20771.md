Explicitly retiring a bound tear-out vessel via `Neo.manager.transaction.NativeLifecycle#retire` now detaches the pane synchronously via `effects.unbind` while skipping (or immediately releasing) the `readmit: true` hold across all three arrival orders between `retire()`'s `close` promise and window `disconnect`, handing the committed item straight to `effects.released` with `awaitingClosure = false`.

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit suite covering all three `retire`/`disconnect` arrival orders, unowned `readmit` disconnect retention, and dock workspace/vessel transaction suites) → L2 required (all `#20770` close-target ACs are unit-verifiable on `NativeLifecycle`). No residuals.

## Deltas from ticket
- Preserved `releaseData: data` on `held` in `NativeLifecycle#releaseSource`, fell back to `admission.releaseData` inside `NativeLifecycle#releaseHeld` when `data` is omitted/empty, and cleared `envelope.releasedBinding.awaitingClosure = false` in `NativeLifecycle#releaseHeld` whenever `this.isCurrentRelease(envelope)` holds, ensuring `retire()` (and `result.closed` / lease expiry) delivers the original `{windowId, releasedBinding, ...}` release envelope to `effects.released` and ends `awaitingClosure`.
