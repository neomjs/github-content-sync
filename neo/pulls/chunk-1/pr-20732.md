Resolves #20729

`Picker#afterSetMounted` now closes an open picker with `hidePicker()`. The close passes the `hidden` config, so `hiddenChange` reaches `pickerIsMounted` and ComboBox's `aria-expanded`.

Before, `picker.hide()` set `_hidden` silently. The field still believed its picker was open, every later close was a no-op, and `onKeyDownEscape` swallowed Escape for good. That was #20719's `CellEditingPickerProjection` red.

Evidence: L2 (unit, red-first) and L3 (whitebox e2e forced-order probe, local Chrome) → L2 required (AC-1). AC-2 runs in CI's `e2e-engine` tier. Residual: none.
