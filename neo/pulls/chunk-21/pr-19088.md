Resolves #19087

`findUnresolvableImports` now judges its resolved-path identity arm on `path.relative(root, resolved)`, and `esmodules.mjs` passes its build root. An engine built in place inside a consumer's `node_modules/neo.mjs` — how `neomjs/pages` deploys — no longer flags every import in its own tree. Pages' `build-all` gets past `dist/esm` again. The specifier arm is unchanged, and the resolved arm still catches an engine reached below or beside the root.

Evidence: L3 (pages' own layout built locally, with a same-tree A/B) plus L2 unit arms with red-first and mutation controls → L3 required (AC-3 is a pages-layout receipt). The deploy itself is #19047 AC-4's.
