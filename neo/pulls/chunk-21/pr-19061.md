Resolves #19056

A teardown call nobody awaits, such as an addon `unregister` or a canvas transfer, can land after its window closed. It no longer reads as a worker defect. `promiseMessage` now names the destination and window on its `NEO_DEAD_PORT` rejection. The unhandled-rejection mirror moves into `worker.Base#onUnhandledRejection`, which settles a rejection only when it is `NEO_DEAD_PORT` **and** `isWindowDeparted` confirms the window was there and left. Every other rejection is mirrored as before, and a caller that awaits or catches still receives its rejection.

Evidence: L3 (the four affected NL spec files, run whole on the exact head, headless Chromium with the external Brain runtime) → L3 required (AC-3). Residual: none on the close target.
