## Deltas from ticket
- The runner stays document-tier: a gate is a host-side cue (accretion defence — the app already had the settlement seam the ticket wanted to add to the engine).
- Scene order: the morph (Metrics leaves and re-enters) now precedes the committed tear-out of the same pane, so tear-out → conversion → return stay contiguous as the five-beat witness proves them, and the film gains the "same pane, one more time: this time it stays" beat.
- Three measured driver preconditions shape the screenplay and are recorded as defect-notes: a tab folded into a tab bar's overflow menu is no drag handle (`did not arm` on Traces and on Audit as third tab of the narrow right group); a twelve-tab group re-enters without re-arming its reattach; and the stack return aims at the torn-out pane's stored home — my showcase had emptied that home, so the return fell back to the first tabs node (the 100k grid) and never settled in four runs. The showcase now docks Commits into that home group instead.
- The re-entry terminal reports `reentered: true` rather than `cancelled`; the controller accepts both as legitimate un-applied settlements.
- `perspective-capture` passes the perspective name as the record's `layoutId` — `capturePerspective` refuses without one.
- Two read-only oracles for whitebox specs: `getTourState()` (the bridge resolves property paths, not a method call mid-path) and `getCueLog()` (cue trail without proof documents, cue errors, vessel states, pending-ness probes) — the latter is what named each stalled beat.
