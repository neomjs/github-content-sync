Fixes #19062 (implements the recommended fix).

## Change
`getMaxPages()` in `src/toolbar/Paging.mjs` now returns `Math.max(1, Math.ceil(totalCount / pageSize))`. With `totalCount: 0`, `maxPages` is 1, so `currentPage === maxPages === 1`: all four nav buttons disable, last assigns 1, next's guard is a no-op, text reads `Page 1 / 1`. Non-empty maths unchanged (all four readers are inside this class).

## Note on spec
#19049 (still open) records the as-is empty-store values. I left its file untouched to avoid stepping on that PR — happy to update its empty-store arm to the decided behavior here, or in a follow-up once it merges. Guided by the acceptance criteria: reverting this one-liner turns the future updated arm red by construction (`toBe(1)` vs `toBe(0)`).

## Validation

- Full playwright suite not run locally (needs browsers); relying on CI.
