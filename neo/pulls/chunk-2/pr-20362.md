Resolves #20338

Ruled-out e2e tiers no longer boot runners just to report a skip.

- **Engine tier:**
  - The `e2e-engine` shards take a job-level `if` on `run_e2e`.
  - The required `e2e-engine` gate now also needs the classifier. It passes skipped shards only when the classifier succeeded and ruled the tier out, and it still requires every shard green when the tier ran.
- **Brain tier:**
  - `e2e-brain` takes a job-level `if` on `e2e_brain_tier != 'none'`. It is not a required context.
  - The skip reason and each changed file's boundary rule move to the classifier's step summary.
- **Brain tip:** the `brain` job booted a runner for a `git ls-remote`. It folds into the classifier behind a `resolve_brain_tip` input, and `brain_sha` is resolved only when the tier runs.

**Measured:** replayed with the current classifier over the 346 PRs merged from 2026-10-13 to 2026-11-05, 92 rule out both tiers. The change removes 806 job-starts, 2.33 per PR:
- 368 engine shards;
- 92 `none` Brain jobs;
- 346 Brain tip jobs.

Evidence: L2 (workflow-shape arms + the gate script executed under `bash -e` across 7 inputs) → L3 required for AC-1 and AC-4 live (a docs-only PR after merge). Residual: AC-1, AC-4, Residual-Owner: #20328.

Prescription checked: the three workflows own the concern, and `classify-test-scope.yml` stays the one shared owner of scope.

## Deltas from ticket

- **Components moved out of this leaf** (body edit, under Out of Scope). Its three required shard contexts need an aggregate gate plus the operator's ruleset swap. That is the same decision as neomjs/neo-agent-skills#281.

- **Engine steps keep only the head check.** They drop their now-redundant `run_e2e` condition and keep the head check that protects reruns.
