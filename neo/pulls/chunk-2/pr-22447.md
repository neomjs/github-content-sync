Recognizes single, repeated and chorded taps and timed presses on subscribed nodes. Main tracks both families on the existing contact owner. App filters each tap callback by its declared counts. Native button clicks remain the activation path for touch and keyboard. Press yields when the existing pointer sensor arms a drag.

`tap` observes each qualifying release. `Gesture` owns its 250 ms and 8 CSS-pixel tap defaults, its 350 ms and 24 CSS-pixel repeat limits, and its 500 ms and 8 CSS-pixel press defaults. A tap streak stays on the same registered node and contact count. The highest-priority `press:start` listener supplies any configured overrides. A matching context menu may arrive while the contact is held or immediately after release.
