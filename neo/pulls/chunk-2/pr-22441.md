Resolves #22419

`Neo.sheet.Base` is a persistent bottom sheet: a container fixed to the window's bottom edge that rests at `snapPoints` (px Numbers or CSS lengths such as `'50dvh'`), with `snapIndex` as its reactive state. Its inline height is its snap point from the first render, with the first and last points as its bounds.

`Neo.sheet.Handle` is the seam that #22421 reuses. It is a focusable `role="separator"`. A vertical drag resizes its `target` on the main thread through `Neo.main.draggable.Resize`, the Splitter's live-resize controller, and the release reports the height and the velocity of the release stroke Main measured. The sheet resolves the point with the static `Handle.resolveSnapIndex()` and settles it: App moves to a changed point, and Main restores an unchanged one.

Without a pointer, Arrow keys, Home and End step on the focused handle, and a header button beside the handle expands one point at a time and collapses from the top. The sheet's role bindings sit once in `theme-base/sheet/`. `examples/sheet/base/` lists thirty places over a drawn map. This is the second leaf of #22414.

Evidence: L3 (the live example in Chromium at 390 × 844: pointer drags through Main's sensor, keys, the button, a first-paint DOM probe) → L3 required (AC-2 drag receipt, AC-5 first paint, AC-7 frames). Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket

- **`Neo.main.draggable.Resize` measures the release velocity**, and `drag:end` carries it as `resizeVelocity` (additive). App receives no `drag:move` while Main resizes, and `drag:start` carries the press at least 100 ms before the arm, so in the first browser run every flick settled as a slow release. The velocity is that of the release stroke: walking back from the lift, the stroke ends where the pointer reversed or had rested 40 ms (`velocityRestTime`). A step within 1 px (`velocitySlop`) counts as rest. Splitter and DockSplitter ignore the new field, and their specs pass.
- **The handle is a `role="separator"`** (the APG window splitter), not a button: it carries a value, and the arrow keys move it.
- **From the cold reads:**
  - a 48 px toggle target;
  - a top border bound to `--neo-sheet-border-color`;
  - the handle's focus ring drawn inside the bar;
  - in Round 2, the handle and the toggle in one row.
- **The role bindings live once in `theme-base/sheet/`**, not as two identical copies in the Neo themes, so a role-only theme and a nested theme scope resolve them too. The classic themes keep their own values.
- **theme-equality** gains a `sheet` ledger row and a harness cell.
- **An unsettled release** is restored by Main when the next drag starts.
- **Touch:** until `pan` (#22405) replaces the sensor here, a touch drag on the handle arms after the sensor's 200 ms hold, as the ticket's Out of Scope records.
