Resolves #22401

ADR 0045 is the Decision Record of D#22259 at G2. It binds what the gesture leaves need before the public event API lands:
- six families as `<family>:<phase>` events, kept out of `globalDomEvents`;
- per-node `register`/`unregister` on a lazily loaded Main addon, in the ResizeObserver shape;
- teardown that cancels without commit;
- browser ownership after a late join;
- one coalesced semantic stream, measured beside the raw one;
- R2 replay whose `engine` and `browser` providers name what they prove.

`learn/tree.json` lists the ADR under Decisions.
