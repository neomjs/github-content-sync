Resolves #22463

The `dialog` example's Edit another contact / Cancel / Save row is now the dialog's docked footer, `headers: [{dock: 'bottom', cls: ['neo-footer-toolbar', 'neo-toolbar'], ...}]`, as `AddUserDialog` and `board/CardEditor` dock theirs. It runs edge to edge under the dialog's top rule, as the title bar runs above the body, instead of sitting in the body's `1rem` padding. The example sheet loses the padding override that the old placement needed. No engine file changes.

Evidence: L3 (computed geometry and frames against neomjs.com and `dev`, the Tab walk, and the dialog e2e specs ×5) → L3 required (AC-1 to AC-3).

## Deltas from ticket

- The toolbar drops its own `gap`. The dialog's footer rule already spaces the buttons (`--neo-dialog-footer-button-margin-left`).
- The footer buttons take the dialog's footer size (`--neo-dialog-footer-button-height`, `control-height-small`), as `AddUserDialog`'s do. They are smaller than the body-row buttons were.
