Horizontal pan rotates Helix and pinch changes its depth. On a phone, a vertical stroke over the stock Helix scrolls the page and the existing controls sit below the stage. Mouse wheel, keyboard navigation and the range fields keep their existing paths.

Evidence: L3 (stock Helix in Chromium at 390 × 844, including trusted touch input and both shared replay providers through an isolated Neural Link Bridge).

## Deltas from ticket
Helix batches pending pan and pinch into one geometry refresh on an animation frame from its owning window. The stock example uses its existing range-field bounds for gesture input. It also gives the stage a keyboard focus target and stacks its controls below the stage on narrow screens.

## Contract Ledger
| Surface | Authority | Observed contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Helix semantic input | ADR 0045 and Engine pan/pinch recognition | Horizontal steps rotate at the previous touch gain. Changes in pinch scale drive depth. Accepted terminal steps are retained. Cancel discards the active gesture’s unpainted delta. |
| `Helix.gestureBounds` | Helix consumer | Optional inclusive `[min, max]` for rotation or depth during gesture input. The stock example uses the ranges already exposed by its controls. |
| `Main.nextAnimationFrame` | Owning Main window | Resolves with that window’s next browser animation timestamp. Helix discards stale requests when its mount or window is retired. |
| Vertical touch policy | Helix CSS and the browser | The stage declares `pan-y`. The body touch handler forwards raw listeners without the previous Helix-specific exception. |
