Resolves #19872

`WorkstationPopOutHoverSyncNL` now judges a scroll that opens a burst of its own. It stops the Workstation Feed's producer, whose 500 ms ticks scroll the grid back to the top. It then waits until the App Worker's grid body has synced the warm-up's scroll and closed its burst, and only then puts the counters on. It asserts exactly one `suspendHover` and one `resumeHover` in the popup. Before this, the judged scroll had never sent a suspend of its own. Every green was a feed-tick burst, or the warm-up's suspend landing after the counters.

Evidence: L3 (real-browser Playwright Chromium NL runs, local, own Bridge on `NEO_NL_PORT=8191`) → L3 required (AC1–AC4); AC5 is hosted `e2e-brain` on this PR. Residual: none.
Change class: zero-delta (`test`). agent-preflight: not hosted here; the baseline `PR body` and `Substrate size` jobs run in CI.

An instrumented run located the mechanism. It logged popup scroll events, `neo-is-scrolling` toggles, addon calls and wheel marks on one clock, and trapped `scrollTop`, `scrollTo` and `focus` with stacks. Its findings:

- A burst is usually open when the popup loads, and both wheels land inside it.
- That burst's `resumeHover` is the first call the counters see, ~245 ms after load.
- The suspend the old `arrayContaining` matched came later, from `DomAccess.scrollTo({top: 0})` at 455, 955 and 1453 ms. The stack runs through the worker's remote call, and the origin is `grid.Body#onStoreLoad` on each Feed batch.

The Feed's tick behavior itself is out of scope, noted in defect-note `MESSAGE:5ba8a0de`.

## Deltas from ticket

None substantive. The ticket body was corrected in place once the instrumented run showed the tick bursts; it now prescribes this shape.

## Evolution

My first model was that the warm-up's own suspend raced the counters. A run with 50 ms before the counters should then have gone red every time, but it stayed 5/5 green. The instrumentation that followed found the Feed ticks, and only stopping them turns the old spec red.
