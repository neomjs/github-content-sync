The coding-guidelines guard now inspects runtime class declarations and expressions before checking their members. It requires a recognized `Neo.setupClass` registration and rejects absent ancestry, unbound superclass names, and direct members of unbound `globalThis`, `self`, or `window`. The documented class rule covers inheritance, configs, construction and registration.

Evidence: L2 (old Translation source rejection, 22 focused tests, real staged CLI controls, and the full base-verified scan).

## Deltas from ticket

The scope resolver honors shadowed bindings, including `Neo` in loops and switches, and follows local superclass bindings. Imported names and normal Neo namespace ancestry remain supported. The superclass check uses scope resolution for unbound names and host globals. The class and its setup call share an execution context, with provably dormant literal branches excluded. Registered local factories remain covered. Imported or computed ancestry remains a review concern.
