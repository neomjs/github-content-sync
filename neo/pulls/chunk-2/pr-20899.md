Resolves #20897

`Neo.grid.Container#afterSetWindowId` now forwards a new `windowId` to every column, beside its ScrollManager. Two kinds of grid now hand their window to their columns: a grid built before it joins a window, which is how every dock pane is built, and a grid moved into another window. A column class that ships its own SCSS loads it there.
