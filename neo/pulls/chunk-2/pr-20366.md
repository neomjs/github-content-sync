Resolves #20364

Widens `Neo.grid.Row#applyRendererOutput` to accept any `Neo.component.Abstract` instance (so `Neo.functional.component.Base` cell components register in `row.components[column.dataField]` and emit `{componentId}` VDOM references) and brings `Neo.table.Body` to lifecycle parity with `Neo.grid.Row` by relaying `afterSetMounted` to `createdCellComponents` and passing `component: me.createdCellComponents?.get(cellId)` into `column.renderer`.
