Resolves #22372

`moveTask` keeps its signature, return values and events. Its body is now a template over `@protected` seams: `resolveTaskDates` (with `getMinimumTaskDuration`, `fitTaskToWorkingTime` and `clampTaskToAxis`), `buildTaskUpdates`, `refuseTaskEdit`, `isServiceTask` with `consultScheduleService`, `writeTaskEdit` and `publishTaskEdit`. `moveTask` reads the schedule service once, so the service that admits an edit is the one that decides it. `rescheduleWorkingTime` writes and publishes through the same seams, and `asTaskWrite(fn)` is the only writer of `_movingTask`. Container's seven `#private` methods are now `@protected` methods, and `#keyboardLinkSource` is the reactive `keyboardLinkSource_`. The coding-guidelines baseline drops Container's seven retired entries.

Evidence: L2 (a base-vs-head differential probe with mutants; the specs and guards run locally) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-7).

CI jobs: none added.

## Deltas from ticket

- `edit` also carries `prevSpan`, the task's span before the edit: the date and the update seams both compare against it.
- `writeTaskChanges` runs inside `asTaskWrite` itself, so `rescheduleWorkingTime` needs no window of its own. `asTaskWrite` restores the previous state instead of `false`, as `asContinuation` does; that differs only for a nested write, which no path makes today.
- `publishTaskChange` takes `span` (`[start, end]`) instead of `startMs` and `endMs`. The two objects the seams pass have typedefs, `GanttTaskEdit` and `GanttSpanRequest`.
- The subclass arm sits in `Inheritance.spec.mjs`, the package's subclass spec, rather than a new file.
- From Emmy's review, three more changes:
  - `moveTask` reads the service once and passes it to `refuseTaskEdit`, `isServiceTask` and `consultScheduleService`, as the base held one service through admission.
  - `keyboardLinkSource` set by config arms once the live region exists, through `construct()`, and `checkKeyboardLinkSource` binds it to its row when the first layout renders that row. Before, the check took the missing row for a recycled one.
  - `GanttSpanRequest` carries `record`.
