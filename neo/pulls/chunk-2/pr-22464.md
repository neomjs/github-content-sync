Frame critique:
- **Resolved defects vs base:** On `dev` (`f2ffa3bb1c`), `<mark>` read out-of-gamut `--neo-green-50` / `--neo-green-900` HSL values that clamped to solid `#fff` in Light (vanishing completely on raised cards) and `#000` in Dark; binding `--neo-global-mark-background-color` to `--neo-color-background-brand-subtle` (`oklch(0.960 0.060 161.0)` · `oklch(0.280 0.060 161.0)`) with `text-strong` (`19.10:1 · 14.25:1`) gives marked runs a legible emerald tint clearly separated from `accent-subtle` inline `<code>` pills and status warnings. In Dark, heading `<code>` (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`) moves off slightly dimmed `--neo-white-off` (`#DDDDEE`) to match `#fff` `text-strong`, and inline `<code>` chips move onto `background-accent-subtle` + `text-default` (`13.20:1`). As noted by `@neo-fable-mnemosyne` in `#22444#issuecomment-7230852891`, `border-subtle` on `accent-subtle` inline `<code>` sits at `1.00:1 · 1.01:1` (matching legacy `1.05:1 · 1.09:1`), left to Theme Neo's pre-cut read rather than this row.

## Cold look
Both schemes now render a coherent document foundation from roles alone: headings (`text-strong`) anchor the hierarchy over `text-default` body paragraphs on `background-canvas`, inline `<code>` (`background-accent-subtle`) and `<mark>` (`background-brand-subtle`) carry distinct cool-indigo and emerald tints on both canvas and raised cards, and any new role-based theme (starting with Material, #22359) inherits the complete global page surface and element typography without carrying legacy sheets.
