Related: #22400
Refs #22401

Adds the window-local subscription and contact lifecycle for the six opt-in gesture families. Main selects a recognition owner per family, tracks physical touch membership after native cancellation, and sends admitted phases through App's existing logical listener path. The stock raw touch stream stays intact.

Evidence: L3 (trusted Chromium input and actual App and Main transport, including a SharedWorker popup).

## Deltas from ticket

A detached node can retain the final native touch event outside the document path. Reacquisition requires a fresh start whose Touch list consists entirely of new contacts. The App Worker fences phases by the registration generation and retires state at the final-port boundary. Family-specific recognition, motion and replay remain in their own leaves.

### 🔬 Depth Floor

- **Challenge**:
  1. **Last-contact position on `cancel` / final lift for family recognizers (`src/main/addon/Gesture.mjs:376-405`, non-blocking foresight for recognizer leaves):** `cancelClaim` sets `claim.frame = {changed, contacts: [], ...}` before firing `contact` (`kind: 'cancel'`) and before its fallback `publish(claim, '${family}:cancel')`. Since ADR 0045 Decision 3 (clarified in #22412) requires every terminal event (`end`, `cancel`, `tap`, `swipe`) to report finite `x, y` at the last focal point, family recognizers (or a small `lastContacts` / `x, y` field on `claim`) should retain the prior frame's coordinates rather than reading `claim.frame.contacts` on `cancel`.
  2. **Delayed `start` pointer capture (`src/main/addon/Gesture.mjs:493-498`, non-blocking foresight for `press`):** `capturePointer` calls `node.setPointerCapture(sequence.pointerId)` when a phased gesture publishes `start`. For immediate `pan:start` on `pointermove` the pointer is guaranteed active, whereas a timer-driven `press:start` on mouse/pen should guard against `DOMException` (`NotFoundError`) if a race triggers the timer right as the pointer lifts.
