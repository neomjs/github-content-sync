Resolves #21065

Launcher icons now reorder within their group, by drag and drop or by keyboard, and each account keeps its own arrangement.

- The apps store sorts by a new `order` field, so the launcher and the start menu follow one sort.
- The launcher drags through `Neo.draggable.list.SortZone` (#21078). Ctrl+Shift+Left or Right (Cmd+Shift on macOS) moves the focused icon through the same `moveRecord()` commit.
- Each order write (one `updateRows()` batch) saves the signed-in account's arrangement to `localStorage`, and signing in applies it before the desktop renders.

This branch carries #21077 (`3a1dec8924`), #21070 (`40620aa25e`, `92714caa50`, `a38389bc2e`), #21073 (`b260c7c202`) and #21078 (`829f7e8551`) until they merge. The reorder change is `b77c0d8f01`.

Evidence: L3 (Chromium on the local dev server, both themes) → L3 required (AC-1 and AC-3 are browser claims). Residual: an OS-level mouse drag (the drag reached the page's Pointer sensor as dispatched pointer events; see #21078), Residual-Owner: #21061.

## Deltas from ticket

- The keys are Ctrl+Shift+Left/Right (Cmd+Shift on macOS). The Navigator walks this grid with Up/Down, so Left/Right are free, and the modifiers keep clear of the browser's Alt+Arrow history keys.
- Persistence follows the store, not the zone: the controller saves on the apps store's `rowsChange`, so the drag, the keys and any later writer share one save path. An arrangement lists each group's app keys; an app it does not list follows in catalog order.
- `neo-config.json` adds the `LocalStorage` main-thread addon.
- The drag proxy carries the tile styles under `webdesktop-launcher-proxy`, and the placeholder fades.
