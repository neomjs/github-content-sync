Resolves #4386

The Phone field accepts international formats such as `+49 (151) 1234 567` and `+49(151)1234567`. Parenthesized groups must contain digits and close correctly; existing slash, dot, hyphen and space forms remain supported. Validation still uses `Text.validate()`, including blank/required handling, caller overrides and clearing errors after an edit.

Evidence: L1 field-validation and regex behavior. No browser or worker-transport change is claimed.
