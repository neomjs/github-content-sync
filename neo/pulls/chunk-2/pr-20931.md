> Moving a WebSocket into a browser SharedWorker collapses 3 open windows onto 1 TCP connection—yet fanning raw frames out over MessagePort or BroadcastChannel still leaves 3 windows paying 3x frame deserializations and holding 3 duplicate store copies on the same machine.
