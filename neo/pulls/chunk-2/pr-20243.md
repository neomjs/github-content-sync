Resolves #4817

Lifts the reactive `required_` config (`false`), `afterSetRequired(value, oldValue)` hook (`oldValue !== undefined && this.validate(false)`), and `required: me.required` `toJSON()` serialization into `Neo.form.field.Base`, removing the duplicated definitions from `Neo.form.field.CheckBox` and `Neo.form.field.Text` (plus the shadowed duplicate `toJSON()` method in `CheckBox.mjs`) for a net `-22` line reduction across `src/form/field/` while making `Neo.form.field.FileUpload#isValid()`'s existing `me.required` check reactive.

Evidence: L3 (Playwright unit tests in `test/playwright/unit/form/field/BaseRequired.spec.mjs` and full `test/playwright/unit/form` suite) → L3 required (reactive config inheritance, `afterSetRequired` validation trigger across `Base`, `CheckBox`, `Text`, and `FileUpload`, and `toJSON()` serialization).
