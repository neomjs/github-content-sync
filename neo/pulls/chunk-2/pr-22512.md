Resolves #22508

The density toggle on `examples/board/cardDesigns` paints its labels at the button skin's size again: `14px`, and `13px` below 600px wide, as 14.0 does. The example drops its two `--neo-button-text-font-size` overrides and a weight override that equalled the skin's semibold. PR #22247 had carried the example's old element-level font size into that variable, which is the one the label reads.

Evidence: L3 (the painted label read on the released site, `dev` and head, both schemes, both widths) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-2).
