Resolves #1069
Related: #15000

Wires horizontal `scroll` events on `.neo-c-w-scrollcontainer` into `Neo.calendar.view.week.Component` infinite-scroll column paging (`onScroll` -> `onWheel`), assigns deterministic column/header VDOM IDs in `createColumnAndHeader`, and restores the main-thread `scrollContainerId` handshake in `Neo.calendar.view.week.plugin.DragDrop#getEventDragZone` and `Neo.calendar.view.week.EventDragZone#dragStart`.

## Deltas from ticket
- Restored the `scrollContainerId` main-thread handshake in `EventDragZone#dragStart` (`Neo.main.addon.DragDrop.setConfigs`) and `week/plugin/DragDrop#getEventDragZone` (`config.scrollContainerId`), which had drifted after `#1245` (`bed4ea72f3`), and initialized `clientX`/`clientY` in `Neo.main.addon.DragDrop#onDragStart`.
- Populated deterministic column and header VDOM IDs (`getColumnId(date)`, `getColumnHeaderId(date)`) in `WeekComponent#createColumnAndHeader` to match `updateHeader()`.
