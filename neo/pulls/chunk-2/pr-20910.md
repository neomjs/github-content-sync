## Deltas from ticket
- **Reset to default restores the arrangement the workspace is in.** It was hard-coded to the shipped document, which with two arrangements leaves the documents and the `dock.perspective` name disagreeing. A switch is `resetTopology(name)`: one Group row carries the documents and the arrangement's name. Undo therefore returns the previous arrangement, a save records it, and the arrangement's panes come back from their windows. A tour enters through `shipped`.
- **The shared store keeps no sorter.** `moveEvent` sorts a store that has sorters, a sort fires `load`, and a board re-projects every column on `load`.
- **Completion is `progress`.** That is the field the board's card editor writes; the schedule reads it through `percentDoneField`.
- **The release plan sits one level above the work items.** It holds phases and deliverables, on the Gantt chart's own stores as the ticket's Out of Scope keeps them, so no task appears twice with diverging dates. The plan's day is the UTC day, as in the families' examples.
- **The e2e spec joins the CI engine tier** (`RUN_PATHS`, `ADAPTER_ELIGIBLE_SPECS`). It drives the page's own SharedWorker and passes off-socket.
