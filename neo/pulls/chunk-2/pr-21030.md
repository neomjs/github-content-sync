Resolves #21029

`RecordFactory#applyPreparedFields()` now admits a record when its store's `getRow()` answers it, instead of `get()`. `getRow()` is the lookup `Store#updateRows()` finds a batch's rows with, and the one `Store#shouldReapplySorters` already reads for the rows a store holds. A store that holds a row `get()` does not answer, such as one its filters hide in a structure of its own, now has that record written inside the batch's one transaction: a refused batch leaves it as it was and publishes nothing. A record the store does not hold is refused as before.

Evidence: L2 (unit, headless Node; red-first on `dev`) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-4 are unit ACs).
