Resolves #21064

Web desktop apps now open in windows. A double-click on a launcher icon, or Enter on a focused one, opens the app in a `Neo.dialog.Base` that grows out of the icon. The window is created on first use through a lazy `import()` and kept afterwards. The controller passes `appName` and `windowId`, plus a state provider that holds the app and names the desktop's provider as its parent. The window's content therefore binds `app` from its own provider and `account` from the parent ("Granted by view-mail to Alex Morgan").

The taskbar lists open windows from a `tasks` store:

- Minimizing keeps a task, and closing removes it.
- Clicking a task restores its window or raises it to the front.

The start menu opens apps the same way. A revoked permission closes that app's window, even a minimized one. Signing out closes every window.

#21068 and #21072 merged on 2026-11-16. This branch now carries #21077 (`3a1dec8924`) and #21070 (`40620aa25e`, `92714caa50`, `a38389bc2e`) until they merge. The windows change is `b260c7c202`.

## Deltas from ticket

- The start menu reaches the controller through each app record's `handler` field (`model/App.mjs`), because `menu.List` calls a chosen item's `handler` at whatever level it sits. The launcher is unaffected.
- Revoked and signed-out windows hide without animation, because their launcher icon may already be gone.
- New windows open centered and stack exactly on top of each other; cascading their position is left as polish.
