Resolves #22476

Theme Neo's eight tooltip variables now live once in `theme-base/tooltip/Base.css` and read roles. This is the `tooltip` row of #22295's step 6, as rule 24 decides:
- The bubble reads `background-accent` with `text-on-accent`. `accent-600` is the shipped `#3e63dd`.
- Border and shadow stay `none`.
- The box reads the small control: `radius-small`, `control-height-small`, `control-font-size-medium`, `space-0-5` and `space-2`.

Both theme-Neo tooltip sheets are deleted. The classic themes keep their own declarations. No painted value moves.

Evidence: L3 (computed-style equality across all five themes, and a hovered tooltip read on the released site, `dev` and head) → L3 required (AC-2, AC-3).

## Deltas from ticket

- **A component spec pinned the deleted sheet's literal.** `test/playwright/component/tooltip/ThemeProjection.spec.mjs` read `--neo-tooltip-font-size` as the old `14px` (CI red at `23c3f4b740`). Its control now asserts that the token equals its role, `--neo-control-font-size-medium`. Its docblock now names the mechanism that decides the contest: an unlayered projection beats every cascade layer. The `:where()` specificity it described no longer decides it.
- **A guide named a deleted sheet.** The cascade-layer example in `learn/guides/uibuildingblocks/StylingAndTheming.md` quoted `theme-neo-dark/tooltip/Base.css` in `neo.theme`. It now quotes this row's `theme-base` sheet in `neo.base`. The point it makes, that an application's `neo.apps` sheet wins at any weight, is unchanged.
