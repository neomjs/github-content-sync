An attributed gesture replay now uses the same scoped write lease as other Engine client writes. App resolves a live component owner from the Main document’s target path. Main binds the replay to that path and cancels the matching replay on writer departure. App releases the departed writer’s lease after the contact cleanup settles.

Evidence: L3 (real Engine App and Main, Chromium, and two distinct sessions on an isolated Neural Link Bridge).
