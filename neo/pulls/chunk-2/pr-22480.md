`Neo.toolbar.Navigation` uses one native route link per destination. On a phone it forms a bottom bar, and on a wide window it forms a rail. The owning window’s history controls the current marker. The app keeps ownership of its destination views.

Evidence: L3 (real route, navigation history, focus, draft, safe area and rendered example in headless Chromium) → L3 required.
