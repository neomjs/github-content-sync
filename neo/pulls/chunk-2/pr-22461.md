Resolves #22440

🌿 A tab's look used to live in two per-scheme copies no other theme could reach. Now it reads the roles every theme defines.

`resources/css/theme-base/tab/Container.css`, `Strip.css` and `header/Button.css` declare the 58 tab variables once on `[class*="neo-theme-"]`, on #22420's template: 22 role reads that keep their value, 8 that move one (the [binding table](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/22440)'s seven, plus `16px` → `1rem`), 23 keywords and 5 listed literals (the strip height, the three paddings' block parts, the close radius). The six `theme-neo-{light,dark}/tab/` sheets are deleted. Cyberpunk declared only the close radius, so two theme-base literals would have reached it (a 3px strip, uppercase labels); it re-declares both as `initial`, which leaves every reader on the fallback it had before. The equality matrix's `tab-container` cell now measures `.neo-tab-body-container`, so the content border's move is measured rather than only declared.

Decision Record impact: aligned-with `adr:0044-design-system` (decision 6, migration step 6).

Evidence: L3 (the cut gate in a fresh `neo-app` workspace installed from this branch's `npm pack`, red on a `dev` pack; computed-style equality across 5 themes and both scopes; frame pairs) → L3 required (AC-4 needs a generated workspace). Residual: none.

agent-preflight: not hosted here; the PR baseline's 22 guards ran locally on the head, all exit 0.

## Deltas from ticket

- The ticket said the classic themes keep their own values. Light and dark do (57 of 58 each; the one they lack, `--neo-tab-button-color-pressed`, is a role read that resolves nowhere in them, as before). Cyberpunk declared only the close radius: `theme-cyberpunk/tab/Strip.css` (new) and `tab/header/Button.css` set `--neo-tab-strip-height` and `--neo-tab-button-text-transform` to `initial`. `0` and `none` would have looked right, but `src/code/LivePreview.css` computes `margin-top: calc(var(--neo-tab-strip-height) * (-1) - 1px)`, so `0` would move cyberpunk's live preview strip by 1px outside the matrix.
- The equality matrix gains one part (`.neo-tab-body-container`, +1,200 comparisons).
- Two specs named the old sheets or tokens: `button/Pressed.spec.mjs` links the tab sheet through `themeSheetHrefs`, and `dashboard/DockInlineHeaderPaint.spec.mjs` probes `--neo-color-background-surface-raised`, `--neo-color-border-default` (both compute as `oklch()`) and `--neo-control-height-medium`.
- The close radius stays the listed literal `2px`, open for the design seat as the ticket asks.
