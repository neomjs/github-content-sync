Resolves #22502

Cyberpunk's tooltip paints a bubble again. `theme-cyberpunk/tooltip/Base.css` declares the 8 tooltip variables from cyberpunk's own palette:
- **Fill and text:** its panel (`--neo-cyber-alt-background-color`) and its text colour;
- **Edge:** the cyan edge and glow its dialog wears;
- **Corners:** square, like its buttons;
- **Box:** the classic tooltip's `14px` / `24px` / `2px 8px 0`.

Cyberpunk declares none of the theme Neo roles that `theme-base` binds the tooltip to, so until step 10 it keeps its own sheet, as the classic themes do.

Evidence: L3 (computed-style equality across all five themes, plus the hovered tooltip on a shipped surface, merged `dev` vs head) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-2).
