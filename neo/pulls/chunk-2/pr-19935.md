RealWorld v1 renders with its Conduit theme again. Its home view shows one *Popular Tags* sidebar with every tag, and its article page renders: body, tags, one comment form, each comment once, and the article's date in both meta blocks. All changes are in `apps/realworld`, plus one e2e spec and its `RUN_PATHS` entry.

Evidence: L3 (browser e2e with the RealWorld API and showdown served from fixtures, plus a local run against the live jsDelivr theme and avatar) → L3 required (AC-1..AC-4). Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket

- **#19922's app trigger.** Views were created with only a `parentId` and got their `windowId` when the container inserted them, after `onConstructed()` had created the tag list. The tag list kept `windowId: null`, and its update during the home view's flight was dropped. Views now take the container's `appName` and `windowId` at creation, and so does every child they create. The engine side stays in #19922.
- **Two article-view defects, found while proving AC-3; `dev` fails the same way.**
  - `afterSetTagList()` wrote to `cn[1]` of the body node's empty children while the body's html was still on its way from the main thread. The hole at `cn[0]` made the next update throw in `TreeBuilder`, so the article view never rendered. The slot now starts as a `removeDom` placeholder.
  - Comments were embedded by their own vdom and rendered twice. They are now embedded by reference, like the tag list and the comment form.
- The article actions showed the template's placeholder date, "January 20th"; both meta blocks now show the article's date.
