## Deltas from ticket

The CSS build compiles widget variable declarations to native `@scope` rules bounded by the registered theme classes. A container that changes only a role re-evaluates the active theme’s widget values. The next named theme starts its own subtree. Scope limits update when a theme is added, renamed or removed in watch mode. The authored split is preserved: structural rules live in `src` and theme files declare variables. JavaScript consumers that already need a theme name, such as floating tooltips or drag proxies, skip the role-scope marker.

Explicitly themed container items keep their choice when the parent switches through the same theme and back. Ordinary items still inherit. The consumer includes the standalone ownership repair through `ede81759ed`. An ntype-created Monaco editor keeps its raw null theme at construction. A detached Web Desktop body follows its owner after return, while an explicitly themed body keeps its own palette. An already constructed, unmanaged instance that matches its first container retains its previous adoption behavior.

### Design read: Material Dark, selected and hovered rows

**Take the darker pair: selected `accent-600`, hovered `accent-700`.**

Read on [your two-row preview](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/c77431a714055c8bf39e94ff5bbea4f1e51d1ae1/review-evidence/22359/grid-dark-predictive-comparison.png) and its receipt, with the palette at `8cc518ce09`; the ratios are computed from those values with the palette guard's `contrastRatio`.

| | Row text on the fill | Selected against hovered | Fill against the plain surface |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current: selected `500` (`#6750a4`), hovered `600` (`#4e3589`) | 5.00 on the selected row, 7.43 on the hovered one | 1.49 | 2.53 / 1.70 |
| Darker: selected `600`, hovered `700` (`#392073`) | 7.43 on the selected row, 10.01 on the hovered one | 1.35 | 1.70 / 1.26 |
| Neo Dark, same frame | 8.83 on the selected row | 1.49 | |

1. **The chosen row should not be the hardest row to read.** Row text is 12.65:1 on the plain surface and 5.00:1 on `accent-500`. It passes its floor, and it is still the one line a user has just picked. On `600` it reads 7.43.
2. **A dark scheme pays for a lit fill by the row.** One selected row at `500` is fine. A multi-selection is a wall at half lightness on a surface at a quarter. `600` keeps the hue and gives the wall back to the surface.
3. **The step between selected and hovered survives.** It drops from 1.49 to 1.35. In your frame Neo Dark's 1.49 reads at once, so 1.35 needs the integrated frame to confirm it. If it reads flat there, raise the selection back before you lighten the hover: the hovered row is the one that sits 1.26 from the surface, and hue is doing most of its work.

One thing that is not the palette's: the hovered row is this heavy in both themes because the grid binds row hover to `--neo-color-background-accent-subtle`, a tinted accent fill. That binding belongs to #22431 and I am not reopening it here; it only means the palette is the right lever for this question.

**Limit.** Two rows at rest are not a working grid. AC-7's integrated frame, with a multi-selection and a hover inside it, stays the place for the final read.
