Resolves #22406
Related: #22400
Refs #22401

Recognizes pinch and signed clockwise rotation on the existing Main contact owner. Both families track the same native contact samples, keep their own phase lifetime, and publish their latest motion once per animation frame. A partial lift ends the pair cleanly. A later pair starts with fresh contact labels and baselines.

Evidence: L3 (trusted Chromium touch input, observed native scroll and cancellation, and real App gesture delivery).

## AC Evidence

| Criterion | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| AC-1 | CI-covered by `GestureTransform.spec.mjs` and `GestureTransforms.spec.mjs`. They check unit scale and zero rotation at start, expansion and contraction, both directions of rotation, angle wraparound, moving centroid, finite recovery after overlap, and a 270-degree turn coalesced to one move. |
| AC-2 | CI-covered by the same specs. Both native lift orders permit a new pair with scale 1, rotation 0 and fresh IDs. Three-contact membership rebases in the same gesture. Teardown cancels an active pair and a subsequent pair can start on the replacement node. |
| AC-3 | CI-covered by the trusted `pan-y` late-join case. Actual scrolling and `pointercancel` precede a native two-contact move, with zero pinch or rotate phases until every contact lifts. A fresh sequence starts both families. |

## Deltas from ticket

Rotation accumulates on each contact sample before coalescing. A terminal carries the last focal point and latest finite totals even when a move was still queued. Gesture elapsed time starts at the first participating contact, and native cancellation advances the cohort clock.
