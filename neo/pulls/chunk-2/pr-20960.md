Resolves #20959

The heads-up run's searches are 4 s apart instead of 2.1 s, and a search that GitHub refuses for a rate limit waits `retry-after` seconds, or 65 s without the header, and is tried again, three more times at most. The first rehearsal on the real runner ([run 43625305209](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/actions/runs/43625305209), without `post`) had lost 28 of 133 accounts to a refused search within its first minute.

Nothing was posted by that rehearsal, and this change posts nothing either: the workflow runs by hand only.

Evidence: L2 (the workflow replayed against a stand-in GitHub that refuses a search) → L3 required (AC-4: a run on the real runner without a rate-limit failure). Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: #20892.

Change class: restoration. `agent-preflight`: not hosted here; the shared baseline runs in CI.

Related: #20892, #20953
