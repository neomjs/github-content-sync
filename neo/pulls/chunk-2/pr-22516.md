### 🔬 Depth Floor

**Challenge OR documented search (per guide §7.1):**

- **Challenge**: three non-blocking weaknesses.
  - `swipeNavigation` is read once, in `construct()`, so setting it later does nothing: no listener, no `touch-action`. An app that decides on swipe after a responsive check gets a silent no-op. The body's ledger calls it a creation-time opt-in, and that is fine for 14.1. A reactive `swipeNavigation_` with an `afterSet` that adds and removes the listener and class would be the Neo shape when a reader needs it.
