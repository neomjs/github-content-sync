Resolves #22483

🌿 A step-6 move could split or drop a variable while the 14.1 codemod still sent app authors to the old target. Now the map records what each name became, and no run writes a dead one.

`loadRenameMap()` takes three entry shapes: `{to}`, `{split: [≥ 2 --neo- names], reason}` and `{dropped: true, reason}`. The schema is the one the codemod's owner, Julia, set. `rewriteContent()` rewrites only `to`. `runThemeVariableRename()` reports each remaining split or dropped name with its record (`unmigrated`), and the CLI exits 1 on one, with and without `--check`. The spec's CONTROL test now asserts that every `to` and `split` name is declared or read under `resources/css` or `src`, dashless `setCssVariable` keys included.

Evidence: L2 (the unit spec, its new arm red on the old codemod, and the live tree checked by the CONTROL test) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-4 are unit-level). Residual: none.
