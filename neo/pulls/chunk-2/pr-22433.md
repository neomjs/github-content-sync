Adds the registered Engine replay RPC for bounded, timed touch traces in one owning Main window. Explicit frames and the hold, swipe, pinch and rotate presets share one validator. Main pins the original target, observes its dispatched TouchEvents, and shares the atomic input lock with drag.

Evidence: L3 (real Chromium TouchEvents and App outcome through the registered RPC in two SharedWorker windows).

| AC-2 | CI-covered by the same Chromium spec. The eight-frame witness, a timed hold and a two-contact transform produce observed events. Timeout, same-ID replacement and window loss interrupt the replay without reporting completion. |

## Deltas from ticket

A mixed frame processes arrivals before releases so a same-time contact replacement keeps the sequence open. Main anchors CSS coordinates to the initial target border box. App distinguishes an unavailable Main receipt after a port loss from an observed empty event list.
