**Other information:**

- `src/list/ValuePicker.mjs`: pluralize next to the count the method already reads.
- `test/playwright/unit/list/ValuePicker.spec.mjs`: one new case covering counts 0, 1 and 2 on the mounted rows (AC-1, AC-2).
- `test/playwright/e2e/grid/HeaderMenuStateNL.spec.mjs`: the twelve `1 matches` expectations become `1 match` (AC-3).
