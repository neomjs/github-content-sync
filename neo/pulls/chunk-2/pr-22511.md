Resolves #22504

🌿 The dock drew every drag in literals that only theme Neo's two copies carried. Now each mark reads a role, and a theme built on Neo can no longer drag a panel over a drop target its roles never named.

The 23 dashboard variables are declared once in `resources/css/theme-base/dashboard/`, bound to roles per #22504's table: the drop preview's accept, reject and signal, the arrival outline, the drag proxy, the drop chips and a detached window's placeholder. The six `theme-neo-{light,dark}/dashboard/` sheets are deleted. The classic themes declare all 23 themselves and keep their values.

The visible change is the teal. Accept, signal, the arrival outline and the proxy's edge now read `border-accent`, a blue like the one `theme-light` and `theme-dark` already paint, and reject reads `border-danger`. `border-accent` is one grade (accent-600) in both schemes, so these marks paint alike in light and dark: 4.98 · 3.69 on `background-surface`. Four radii take the nearest step of the shape scale (6 → 8 and 3 → 4 px), and the insertion guide keeps its listed `2px`.

Evidence: L3 (whole-page frames of a parked drag and a specimen of the real dock classes, from built trees in Chromium; the equality run across 5 themes; the dock e2e specs) → L3 required (AC-3 asks for rendered frames). Residual: none.
