Adds continuous pan and a single directional swipe at release on the existing Main contact owner. Pan retains its total while contact membership changes and sums steps between published animation frames. Swipe reads the actual lift position and qualifies against recent release velocity. The browser's existing contact claim suppresses a swipe after native scrolling takes over.
