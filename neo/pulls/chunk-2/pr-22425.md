Resolves #22395

Enforces `Global.css`-only scope for every `palette`- and `role`-shaped CSS custom property (`kindOf` from `themeNameShapes.mjs`) in `buildScripts/util/check-theme-palette.mjs` over AST declarations and `@property` registrations, refuses conditional palette or colour-role declarations for every theme class (with or without a palette), restricts `src/Global.css` scale-role declarations (motion roles included) to rules where every effective selector arm is narrower than a bare theme or under a condition, and adds `npm run theme-contrast -- <fg> <bg> [--theme <theme>]` (`measureThemePairContrast`) sharing `measurePairRatio` with `checkPalette`.

Related: #22295, #22394
