The project can use elapsed time or its own working calendar. In the Community Garden Plan example, drag from a task’s finish dot to another task to link them. The plan reopens with the edited dates and links.

## Contract ledger

`moveTask()` clamps the edited task to the visible axis. Its successors take their required dates even beyond that axis; their records, the result and `taskChange` agree. Cycles are reported separately. Keyboard, pointer and editor operations use this writer. An optional external scheduling service can handle an edit by task key and return a committed, pending or refused result. The ordinary chart runs with its task store and dependency store alone. The chart also accepts caller-selected bar and arrow highlights.

## Deltas from ticket

The grid body and cascade arithmetic have their own modules. The example uses the existing Calendar catalog family and one browser storage entry for the tasks and their dependencies.
