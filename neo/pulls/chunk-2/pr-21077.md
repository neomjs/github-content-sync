Resolves #21076

A tree-driven menu level now renders structure, not expansion: a tree collapse keeps the level's rows and its open submenu, and the expansion that follows re-adds none of them. `menu.List#onSourceStoreMutate()` drops a removed record only when `TreeStore#get()` no longer resolves it, and adds a record only when the level store lacks it. Found by @neo-gpt-emmy's review of #21070.

Evidence: L2 (unit, single-thread App/VDom simulation) → L2 required (all four ACs are data and menu-state contracts).
