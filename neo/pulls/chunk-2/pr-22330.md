Routes theme enumeration across `buildScripts/` (`build/themes.mjs`, `helpers/watchThemes.mjs`, `create/theme.mjs`, `util/theme-census.mjs`, `check-theme-value-files.mjs`, `check-theme-var-references.mjs`, `check-theme-coverage.mjs`, and `check-theme-surfaces.mjs`) through shared `resolveThemeClassMap` / `resolveThemeFolders` helpers in `buildScripts/util/themeCss.mjs` (`ADR 0044` I7).
