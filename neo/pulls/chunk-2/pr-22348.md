Resolves #22343

The Gantt's dependency layer now re-projects when one of its own inputs changes, and the chart writes each input in one place: nine mirrored values through the scheduler's relay table (`static timelineRelays` gains a `layer` target, read at the layer's creation and on every later change), the three geometry inputs through `syncDependencyLayerGeometry()`. `syncDependencyLayer()` no longer re-sends ten keys, `dependencyLayerConfig` merges deep, the center body is the single mount path, and a resize of the center body or of a locked region moves the layer's window. A `set()` on the layer projects once for all its inputs. Decision record: `#22306`, comment 7216849463.

Evidence: L3 (headless Chromium: the ten `examples/gantt` pages in dev mode, each against a `dev` control, and a drag of the locked Task column's edge) → L3 required (AC-5 and AC-8 read a browser). No residuals. agent-preflight: not hosted here; the pre-commit guards and the Gantt, scheduler, timeline and grid unit specs ran.

## Deltas from ticket

- **The config became reactive.** A descriptor on a plain config merges between classes only: `core.Base#setFields` assigns a config without a trailing underscore before `mergeConfig` runs, so an instance config replaced the subclass default. `dependencyLayerConfig_` merges at both levels, as `launcherConfig_` does in `desktop.Container`. The asymmetry itself is out of scope, noted in a `defect-note`.
- **An override of a mirrored input now survives.** On `dev`, `dependencyLayerConfig: {bufferPx: 800}` is lost at the first `syncDependencyLayer()`, which re-sends the chart's value. Here it holds until the chart's own `bufferPx` changes; the deep-merge arm asserts both.
- **The resize lead was a defect, on three sources.** On `dev` a resize of the center body leaves the layer's window where it was. The chart now observes the body's `containerWidth` and the horizontal scrollbar's two margins. Those carry the locked regions' widths and outlive the locked bodies, which come and go with their columns.
- **Creation reads the relay table.** `getDependencyLayerInputs()` builds the layer's first values from `timelineRelays`, so a subclass that points a relay at another layer config moves the first value with it. `drawnSpans` is set after `dependencyLayerConfig`, as `dev` assigned it after creation.
- **A relayed input travels with the highlights.** The relay hop writes a chart config in one `set()` with the registered analysis' highlights, and the layer projects once per `set()`. A store, axis or field swap under an analysis therefore projects once, as on `dev`. The hooks that invalidate the analysis do so before they relay.
- **Lines grew.** What goes: the ten-key re-send, eight `syncDependencyLayer()` calls in config hooks, one hook whole, the hand-written creation list, and the chart's push into the body's vdom. What comes: the layer's eight input hooks and its batch, the relay table and three extracted methods, each with its JSDoc.
- **Stage 2 of the intake is the reviewer's.** I wrote the ticket and the fix in one session, so my prescription check on `#22343` (comment 7217057207) is not independent: is the relay table the right owner of the nine writes, and the body the right place to mount?
