### 🔬 Depth Floor

**Challenge OR documented search (per guide §7.1):**

- **Challenge**: Once a short leftward swipe (`24 <= travel < threshold`) settles in `SwipeActions#onPanEnd` (`src/list/plugin/SwipeActions.mjs:179-188`), `this.distance` snaps to `this.threshold` and `#gestureId` clears, but `onPanStart` (`SwipeActions.mjs:149`) rejects any non-leftward stroke (`travel <= Math.abs(data.deltaY)` with `travel = -data.deltaX`) and no outside-tap or `Escape` listener clears the preview. As a result, a user who reveals `Archive` cannot swipe the row back closed to the right or tap elsewhere to dismiss it — it stays exposed until another leftward touch pan begins, an action commits, the list unmounts, or scrolling recycles that slot. Safe for `#22422`'s scope, and worth adding rightward-close / tap-away dismissal as a follow-on polish when a second surface adopts `SwipeActions`. Also note `core-idiom-audit.md` check 8 (`let me = this` when `this` is used `> 2` times in `src/` / `examples/`): `SwipeActions#onOwnerConstructed`, `onPanStart`, `onPanEnd`, `commit`, and `destroy` use direct `this` 5–8 times each.
