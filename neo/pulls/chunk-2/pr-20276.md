Resolves #20275

Adds `Neo.collection.Sorter.getValue(item, property)` to resolve dotted property paths on `Neo.data.Record` instances and plain objects, lifts `sortArray(arr)` into `Neo.collection.Base` (eliminating the duplicate `Neo.data.TreeStore#sortArray`), and updates `Neo.data.Store#resolveField` so Turbo Mode (`autoInitRecords: false`) soft-hydrates complex root fields of dotted paths without stamping flat keys onto raw items.

Evidence: L2 (Playwright Chromium unit suite covering AC-1..AC-4) → L2 required (AC-1..AC-4). Residual: none.
