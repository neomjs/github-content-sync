Resolves #22451

A button's border reads `border-default` again, the role 14.0 read through `--sem-color-border-default`. The Button pilot (#22420) bound it to `border-strong`, which reads near-black on a row of buttons. This follows rule 4 of #22295 as the design seat rewrote it: a boundary owes 3:1 only where it identifies the control, and a button's label identifies it. Six declarations in `theme-base/button/Base.css` move together: the plain and secondary borders at rest, hover and active. A field keeps `border-strong`.

Evidence: L3 (computed styles against the released site and against `dev`, plus theme-equality across all five themes) → L3 required (AC-2, AC-3).
