Resolves #19295

`GraphScene` draws a clustered scene through a foveated level of detail, in its own WebGL2 renderer with no external library.
- A scene may carry `clusters`, one cluster id per node.
- `setScene` derives the cluster centroids, one contiguous edge range per cluster, and one bundle line per connected cluster pair.
- The camera distance against the fitted distance picks the level:
  - **far** draws the bundles over the centroids.
  - **mid** draws the nodes and the bundles.
  - **near** adds the own edges of the `lod.nearClusters` clusters nearest the camera target.
- A level change only moves draw ranges; it uploads nothing.
- A scene without `clusters` draws as before.

The example gains a 100k-node, 1M-edge, 64-cluster scene ("100k clustered") and a timed lap over the levels ("LOD lap").

Evidence: L3 (unit arms red on dev first; the example's lap measured in headless branded Chrome on hardware GL, page visible) → L3 required (AC-6). Residual: none; the visible-window read on the operator's display is under Post-Merge Validation.
