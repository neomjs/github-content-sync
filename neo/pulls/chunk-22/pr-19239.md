Resolves #19238

ADR 0034 §2.3 gains item 9: the packaged shell's credential window is a native password field, sandboxed and script-free in a window of its own. A window-only preload hands the value to main once, on submit, and the window is destroyed on every settle path. The cockpit's renderer and the App Worker still never hold a credential. Item 8 points to item 9. The anti-anchor row and §2.3's heading scope the renderer-readable ban to everything outside that window. §2.3's own falsifier requires this amendment before neomjs/neo-agent-institution#227 implements it.

Evidence: L3 for the one measured sentence in item 9 → L1 required (documentation). The L3 run was an Electron 43.1.0 probe on darwin, driving #227's `harness/credentialPrompt.mjs`:
- While the field had focus, `ioreg -l -w 0` showed `kCGSSessionSecureInputPID` = the probe's main PID; after the window settled, no holder.
- The app menu's Paste item is role `paste` with `CommandOrControl+V`.

Decision Record impact: amends ADR 0034 §2.3 (items 8–9, the anti-anchor row, the heading).
