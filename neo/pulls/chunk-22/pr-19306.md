Resolves #19305

`DomEvents` coalesces `mousemove` into the next animation frame but sent every other mouse event at once. A press that followed a jump in the same frame therefore reached the app worker as press-then-move. `Neo.canvas.Base#updateMouseState` derives movement from the last reported position, so the press carried the whole jump with `buttons: 1`, and `GraphScene` orbited on it. In the probe on engine `a50ae57ce8`, one click swung the camera from yaw 0.7 to 3.1. Now `flushPendingMouseMove` sends the waiting move before a press, release, click, double click, context menu, wheel, enter or leave. Moves on their own still send once per frame.
