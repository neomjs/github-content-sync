Resolves #19103

A root without a live opener now gets its own canvas group, and with it its own canvas SharedWorker (`neomjs-canvas-worker-<group>`). A second unrelated root therefore no longer kills the first root's renderer. The app worker keeps one channel per group and routes every canvas-bound message by `windowId` → group, with replies going back over the channel their request arrived on. Remote proxies wait for the caller's group to be ready and reject with `NEO_WORKER_START_FAILED` when its worker fails to load or stays silent. Canvas components `await whenCanvasReady()` instead of polling for proxies, and every app-side canvas remote call carries `windowId`.

Evidence: L3 (real Chromium, real SharedWorkers and dedicated workers, e2e plus locally run Brain-gated NL witnesses) → L3 required (the product-witness ACs). No residuals.

## Deltas from ticket
- Replies to canvas-originated requests (e.g. its `registerRemote`) name no window. They now return over their arrival channel: the message is stamped with its group, and `App#assignPort` carries the stamp into the reply. The first two-root run caught these as unroutable.
- The shared app worker's renderer proxies exist once any group registers them. Canvas components therefore talk to their renderer only after their own group adopted the canvas (`offscreenRegistered`). The failed-group arm caught this.
- `Neo.canvas.Header#updateConfig` drops `windowId` before `set()`.
- The failed-group arm runs on `apps/colors` (lazy canvas start). A canvas worker created at boot and failing to load holds the root's whole boot, as it already does on `dev`: out of scope, noted in defect-note `d0ae7252bc511b1b`.
