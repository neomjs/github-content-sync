> **Stacked on #19254** (the tab-header proxy, #19248). Until #19254 merges, this diff also carries its commits. The #19278 delta is the two commits after `5b4a8095f1`. Merge #19254 first.

Under a hand drag the parked vessel stayed on top of the target popup and covered its drop zones and the target-side proxy (the operator's recording, #19186). The park hid the vessel behind the target by z-order, and `focus()` raises nothing during a real OS drag. The vessel now parks clear of the target, in the corner of the target display's work area that covers it least, and nothing is focused. While the park is still pending, the target already shows its drop zones.

- `NativeVesselTransaction.resolveClearPark` (new static): the corner of the work area whose frame overlaps the target's inner rect least (the farthest among equals), clamped inside the work area, plus `cleared`. The engine's default park (Demo B) and the Workstation's pointer park both use it, reading the display bounds from `Main#getWindowData` for the target window. Neither focuses; the receipt records `cleared`. The native title-bar park keeps its focus chain.
- `TabSortZone#resolveRemoteDragTransition` marks a standing claim that the park has not admitted (pending or refused) with `preview: true`.
- `DragCoordinator#onDragMove` sends such a frame with `embodyProxy: false`, never commit-eligible, so the target renders its zones and a drop on it leaves.
- ADR 0029 §2.8.6 carries the target-clear binding and the zones before the park, following Clio's ruling and Mnemo's draft on #19186.

## Deltas from ticket
- **The static lives on `NativeVesselTransaction`,** not beside `Placement.resolvePosition`: the transaction owns the park, and the Workstation's park already calls its statics.
- **`preview` is the source's mark, not the coordinator's inference.** Only the transition owner knows whether a pending transition is a convert-in to this target. A pending convert-out, or the A→B wait for a vessel still parked in A, stays unmarked.
- **The native title-bar park keeps target-cover.** It parks only after the OS released the drag, when focus is granted. Clio has this as a fork to confirm.
- **The "fits" precondition stays** for a position-only transaction (Demo B): a source that cannot be shrunk still refuses, as before.
