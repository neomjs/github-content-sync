Resolves #19311

A tooltip whose target was left inside the show delay appeared a moment later and stayed. The leave now clears the pending show. `addTimeout` also passed the timer handle to the name-keyed `clearTimeout`, which cleared nothing, so a delay armed twice leaked its first timer past any hide. It now clears its predecessor by name.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms on the class's own methods with real timers; the tooltip component specs in a real browser) → L2 required.
