Resolves #859

A human operator can now hold, find and answer the A2A Tasks addressed to them: the Memory Core half of the operator's inbox (neomjs/neo-agent-institution#551, row 4).

- **Assignment.** A direct Task to a registered human is assigned to that human, from the identity's own `accountType`; a caller-supplied `task.assignee` still grants nothing. Broadcast cohorts stay agents. System and unclassified targets stay unassigned.
- **Answer.** The human assignee may leave `InputRequired` for `Working` or `Completed` through `transition_task` on the original message. An agent assignee still waits for its originator. Another human, an inbox reader and a system identity are refused. A direct human Task stored before this, with a `null` assignee, gains its owner from its one `SENT_TO` edge on the first authorized transition.
- **Find.** `list_messages` gains `taskStates` and `taskOrder: 'priority-age'`.
  - The Task view filters before the page, so `totalCount` counts exactly that population; a question behind any amount of newer traffic is counted and listed.
  - The count and the page read one SQLite snapshot, and each row's `task` comes from the SQL that matched it. A write landing between the reads, or between the match and the row, cannot make the page disagree with its count or its filter.
  - Invalid states, an empty list, or `taskOrder` without `taskStates` are refused, never answered as an empty or unfiltered page. A Task read marks nothing read.

`get_message` is unchanged: the human reads its own Task's body; another human cannot.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms over an isolated graph, red-first per arm on dev `166f17f`, plus a mutation run on the same-read override) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-6 are service behavior; AC-7's installed question/reply witness is the consumer's). Residual: the cockpit consumer and its installed walk, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#551
