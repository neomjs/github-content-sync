Resolves #761

Supersedes #804, which delivered an escalation design #761 never ratified. Its review ([5400413001](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-brain/pull/804#pullrequestreview-5400413001), [disposition](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-brain/pull/804#issuecomment-5968528311)) split the leaf. This PR is the reduced slice:
- the seat that newly holds a pull request's next action is woken once;
- the escalations (unowned, dead route, silent holder) move to #807;
- the split is recorded on [neomjs/neo#19122](https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/19122#discussioncomment-18729871), and the reused source follows the [salvage map](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-brain/issues/761#issuecomment-5968481942).

After each pulse, a round does three things:
- plans the wakes from the snapshot and a ledger of holding episodes;
- writes the ledger beside `open-work.json`;
- sends each wake as a task-bearing `add_message` from the viewer the plane verified at boot.

A seat whose route reads `unreachable` in `who_is_online`'s identity projection is skipped and retried each round. That projection skips a dead route; it never certifies that a particular wake was delivered.

The path is off by default (`fleet.openWorkWakes`, `NEO_FLEET_OPEN_WORK_WAKES`) and plane-mode only. Switched on, rounds stay quiet until the producer's retained day passes the switch-on bounds.

Evidence: L2 (unit: the planner, the gate and the round, over an in-memory ledger, an injected sender and an injected routes read) → L4 required for AC-7's falsifier (live wakes on the installed Fleet over a week). Residual: AC7, Residual-Owner: #759.

## Deltas from ticket
- **Scope.** AC-4, AC-5's escalation half and AC-7's escalation falsifier are #807's, per the amended #761.
  - #804's escalation records, counts and log lines are gone.
  - The saved ledger holds only `{holding, records, pulse, gate}`, and an arm pins that.
- **The operator is not woken.** An approved, green, mergeable head is reached through OQ5's awaiting-merge chip (Institution #449, merged); the operator has no wake route.
- **The rotation** is the registry's seats on the PR's repository, sorted, turning by PR number.
- **The gate is judged every round, not once.** A day outside the bounds keeps rounds quiet until it leaves the retained window. The bounds come from the first observe-only day (920 pulses):
  - a complete pulse costs at most 10 points at the 95th percentile (seen: 2–3);
  - a seat's PRs change at most 60 times in any clock hour (seen: 27).
- **At most once.** The ledger is written before the first send. A failed send is logged and never retried. A ledger older than the retained day baselines again.
