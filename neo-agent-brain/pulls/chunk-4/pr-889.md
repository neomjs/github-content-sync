Resolves #888

Related: #459

The GitHub Workflow sync no longer derives Portal indexes and SEO.
- `SyncService.rebuildContentIndexesAndSeo` and the `deriveContent` option are removed.
- `emitGeneratedContentAndDerive` is now `emitGeneratedContent`.
- The auto-push allowlist drops the three `apps/portal/*` paths that only the derive wrote.

The step ran only in the manual modes. The one scheduled caller (`--corpus-only`) never derived, and the Portal's deploy now derives from the published corpus. Moving the Brain's `neo.mjs` pin past neomjs/neo#19408 can no longer break the sync.

Evidence: L2 (unit specs over the emitter, the CLI wiring and the source order) → L2 required (AC-1 is a source predicate; AC-2 to AC-4 are spec- and lint-covered). Residual: none.

Intake: self-authored this session. Prescription checked: `ai/services/github-workflow/SyncService.mjs` — the better owner of Portal derivation is the pages deploy's step 7 (neomjs/pages#14). SyncService keeps emission, so removing the step is the fix, not a symptom patch.

## Deltas from ticket
- **The auto-push allowlist** drops `apps/portal/resources/data/`, `apps/portal/sitemap.xml` and `apps/portal/llms.txt`. Only the derive wrote them. If they stayed, the allowlist would commit whatever another process left there as sync output. The Stage2 allowlist arm now asserts that no Portal path remains.
- **Two Stage2 arms are rewritten, not deleted.** The persist-before-push-check ordering keeps its property without the derive. The auto-push retry arms now count passes with `pullRuns`.
- **RechunkOrdering is anchored on the aggregate verdict.** A re-chunk placed after the verdict would skip the facets that advanced whenever one facet fails. Its provenance paragraph moved to the commit message, per the ticket-archaeology lint.
