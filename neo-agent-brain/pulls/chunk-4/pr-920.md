Resolves #919

🌿 An approval the producer saw one reviewer give can no longer appear under the author's name, and a push answering a change request can no longer pass silently.

A verdict transition now carries `by`: the reviewers observed moving the decision. A reviewer moved it when their standing opinion became the new decision, or when the same opinion moved onto the current head (a re-approval). Both reads must hold every standing opinion (`normalizePullRequest` now keeps `opinionsComplete`). Otherwise, like a dismissal or a branch rule, the change names no one. When exactly one reviewer moved a verdict, that reviewer is the event's actor. Otherwise the actor stays the PR's author, and the reviewers ride in `payload.transition.by`. A push records the verdict it landed on, and the PR lane shows a push only when that verdict was `CHANGES_REQUESTED`. Every other push, CI and review-request change stays off the lane, as before. The consumed contract is #919's Contract Ledger.

Evidence: L2 (unit tests over the reducer, the PR-lane events, and reducer to event) → L3 required for the installed Activity tab showing these words, which needs the cockpit consumer neomjs/neo-agent-institution#593 too. Residual: that installed reading, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#593

## Deltas from ticket
- `head` joins `PR_LANE_TRANSITION_KINDS`, filtered to pushes whose landed-on verdict is `CHANGES_REQUESTED`. The ticket left the mechanism open.
- A push carries the verdict from before the push, the one it answered. If the reviewer approves within the same pulse, the lane shows both the push and the approval.
- Attribution needs complete opinions on both reads (review RA-1). A cut or pre-upgrade read keeps the author as actor rather than guessing.
