Resolves #880

🌿 A bench the operator records on a seat's identity node can no longer be overruled by our roster file in the heartbeat or issue focus.

A leaf of epic #875. The swarm heartbeat's targets and issue focus's owner findings now take a seat's participation from the AgentIdentity node rows `who_is_online` reads, never from `identityRoots.mjs`. An operator without our roster gets their own seats' benches, and a bench recorded on the node (#28) reaches both readers. #880's Contract Ledger declares each consumer's behavior.
- **One read for every plane-side reader:** `ai/graph/agentIdentityParticipation.mjs` (`readAgentIdentityNodes`, `participationStatusOf`, `participationByIdentity`). The wake daemon's own copy moved there from its `queries.mjs` and `wakeTargetEligibility.mjs`. The index-backed query, and the rule that a node without a status is active, now each live in one place.
- **Heartbeat:** `resolveTargets` takes a `participationProvider`, and the module-load map from the roots is gone. Every discovered target, self included, passes the same gate. `SwarmHeartbeatService` and `checkAllAgentIdle` supply the graph read. A read that throws propagates:
  - the heartbeat service names it and pulses nobody that cycle (the next interval reads again);
  - the idle check fails, rather than judge an unread team idle.
- **Issue focus:** `buildWorkGraphStallFindings` reads the nodes through the in-process graph store unless records are handed in. A benched owner is marked only from its node, and the evidence names the node (`AgentIdentity:@id:status`). An epic awaiting resolution is graded by what its owners' nodes answered:
  - `verified-stall` only when every owner's node reads inactive;
  - `source-degraded` when the store cannot answer;
  - `candidate-stall` when an owner has no node.

**Not in this leaf:** two roots readers remain, and neither reads participation: `active-local-team`'s team membership and issue focus's stale-assignment maintainer list. #875 needs its own disposition of both before it closes.

Evidence: L2 (unit, with in-memory graph stores and the real resolver). The heartbeat and issue focus on a running plane are not claimed.

## Deltas from ticket
