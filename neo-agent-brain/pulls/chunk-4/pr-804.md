Resolves #761

The open-work producer now wakes the seat that newly holds a pull request's next action. After each pulse, a round does three things:
- plans the wakes from the snapshot and a ledger of holding episodes;
- writes the ledger beside `open-work.json`;
- sends each wake as a task-bearing `add_message` from the viewer the plane verified at boot.

The path is off by default (`fleet.openWorkWakes`, `NEO_FLEET_OPEN_WORK_WAKES`) and runs only in plane mode. Switched on, every round stays quiet until the producer's retained day passes the switch-on bounds: it records who holds what and wakes no one.

Evidence: L2 (unit: the planner, the gate and the round, over an in-memory ledger, an injected sender and an injected routes read) → L4 required for AC-7's falsifiers (live wakes on the installed Fleet, recorded over a week). Residual: AC7, Residual-Owner: #759.

## Deltas from ticket
- **No lead is woken (AC-4, AC-5).** No code knows a lead. An unowned PR, a dead route and a silent holder are escalation records instead: logged, counted, and the last 50 kept in the ledger. Proposed on the [intake comment](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-brain/issues/761#issuecomment-5967090179), which has no reply yet.
- **The operator is not woken.** An approved, green, mergeable head belongs to the awaiting-merge list (Institution #449), not a pager.
- **The rotation** is the registry's seats on the PR's repository, sorted, and it turns by PR number.
- **The gate is judged every round, not once.** A day outside the bounds keeps rounds quiet until that day leaves the retained window. The bounds come from the first observe-only day (920 pulses over 16 h):
  - a complete pulse costs at most 10 points at the 95th percentile (seen: 2–3);
  - a seat's PRs change at most 60 times in any clock hour (seen: 27).
- **At most once.** The ledger is written before the first send, and a failed send is logged, not retried. A ledger older than the retained day baselines again, so switching back on wakes no backlog.
