Resolves #956

The placement probe now tells the reader *we could not look* from *we looked and it does not fit*, and says what to do about either. Every uncertainty entry carries the reader's own word plus a `cause` and a `nextStep` from one vocabulary; a preset verdict carries a `kind` — `observed` (a fit, or a refusal with its measured reason) or `unverified` (a read did not answer; the verdict is the preset's fit on the observed consumers alone, never an affirmative fit) — with the same two sentences; the recipe's placement step reads `ok · unverified` instead of `failed · no supported preset fits this host` when the host did not answer, and `failed` only when observed refusals leave no preset. The Docker sentences come only from the Docker readers' own error text; a generic failed read never becomes a Docker diagnosis.

Evidence: L1 (pure modules under injected readers, the unit tier) → L1 required (AC-1–AC-4 are unit-pinned by the ticket; AC-5 is post-merge by the ticket's own words). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#351.

## Deltas from ticket
- `recommendPlacement` gains an `unverified` bucket and per-row `kind` / `cause` / `nextStep`, so the recipe's status is computed from the verdicts rather than re-derived from the budget (the intake note on #956).
- The probe exposes `host.observedAvailableBytes` and `host.missingReaders`: the arithmetic an unverified verdict is reasoned on, and the readers it names — the ticket's "the preset's fit on the observed facts" needs a number the incomplete budget did not carry.
- A measured shortfall on the observed consumers alone is an observed refusal even while a reader is missing (`host-short-observed`), so "never blocks" cannot turn into "always fits" through a missing reader.
- The vocabulary is two tables, not one: the uncertainty classes (per reader, `<fact>` expanded) and the observed-refusal classes (`<n>` the measured gibibytes); both are exported constants with the JSDoc table beside them.
