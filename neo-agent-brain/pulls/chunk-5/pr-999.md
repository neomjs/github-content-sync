Resolves #417

🌿 After one run, nobody can say the Knowledge Base answered from a copy of the conversation frozen in August.

`ai/scripts/maintenance/retireLegacyConversationRows.mjs` is a dry-run-first CLI beside its maintenance siblings. Under the heavy-maintenance lease, it selects the default stamp's (`neo-shared/neo`) rows whose `type` is `ticket`, `pull` or `discussion`. It refuses before deleting when a selected row lives outside `resources/content/`, or when the replacement is not established. The replacement is the configured tenant repository that is the corpus source, and it needs an accepted manifest from its configured extraction identity plus conversation rows of its own. With `--apply` and the confirmation token, it deletes the selection in pages and then proves the preserved count. An interruption once deletion has begun exits non-zero with the partial receipt. The plane run itself is #417's Post-Merge Validation.

Evidence: L3 (the exact head ran as a dry run against the plane's live store inside the orchestrator container, plus the unit arms) → L3 required (AC-1, AC-3). Residual: the plane run and its receipts (#417's Post-Merge Validation), Residual-Owner: #64, whose AC-6 already owns the corpus tenant's activation receipts.
