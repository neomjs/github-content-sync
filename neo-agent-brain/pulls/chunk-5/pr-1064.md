## Deltas from ticket

Server-originated requests receive temporary bridge IDs so a restarted server's reused request ID cannot consume an old local reply. Replies and cancellation IDs translate back to the issuing generation. Replacement handshakes preserve the local client's negotiated protocol and capabilities and time out after 10 seconds. Dispatched request errors carry `data.outcome: unknown`; calls waiting for a handshake are never sent if it fails.

Prescription checked: `ai/mcp/client/stdioToStreamableHttp.mjs` owns the adapter; the previous fixed-transport closure is replaced in place. No server/session service, SDK internals, config or launcher changes. Logical session continuity remains [D19401](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/19401).

### 🔬 Depth Floor

- **Challenge**: In `localTransport.onmessage` (`ai/mcp/client/stdioToStreamableHttp.mjs:365-369`), any non-request message (`!isRequest`, covering both client notifications and client responses to server-originated requests) whose `generation.transport.send(message)` rejects with a non-terminal error (such as a transient `502`) calls `retire(current, error)`, whereas a regular request failing with `502` answers only that request and keeps `current`. Because JSON-RPC notifications and responses have no error-reply channel back to the local client, retiring `current` on a dropped client notification/response is the right conservative fence (`// Lost notifications cannot be answered. Renew before accepting future traffic.`). Similarly, if the initial session ever dropped in the narrow window after the initial `InitializeResult` (`initialized = true`) and before the client's first `notifications/initialized` arrived, `ensureRemote()` would send `notifications/initialized` at handshake completion and then forward the client's `notifications/initialized` once more—benign because `notifications/initialized` is idempotent on MCP servers. Both behaviors are sound and non-blocking.
- **Documented search**: *"I actively looked for (1) accidental replay of dispatched `tools/call` writes across `retire()` / `ensureRemote()`, (2) race conditions between late `onmessage`/`onerror`/`onclose` callbacks from a retired `StreamableHTTPClientTransport` and an active replacement handshake, and (3) bearer token reflection in `onError`/`onRecovered` stderr diagnostics, and found no concerns."*
