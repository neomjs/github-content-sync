Resolves #1133

The `#15704` stale-replay arm slept a fixed 250 ms after delivery, then read the daemon log and watermark once. Both are written after the dispatch, so under CI contention the window expires first. That failed 2 of 3 CI attempts on #1126 (run 39107522011), whose change cannot reach the daemon. The arm now polls the observed state, as the spec already does elsewhere: the suppression line, then both message ids in the durable watermark. Every assertion after that is unchanged.

Evidence: L2 (the arm locally plus a delay mutation in the daemon) → L2 required (a unit property). Residual: none.
