Resolves #1058

#1018's attribution re-keyed a row's `AGENT_MEMORY` node and nothing else. A row whose graph projection is still REM's legacy `memory:<uuid>` node kept tenant `shared` beside its re-keyed Chroma row. That is 497 rows on the canonical plane, and the reconciler refuses to converge them. The graph pass now moves that projection with its row: from the shared mirror to the identity on an apply, and back on a reverse. The projection only mirrors its row, so its prior owner is known and the journal keeps its format. A projection that holds any other owner is refused, counted, and makes the run exit non-zero. A `memory:` node without the row's `chromaId` is curated ontology and stays untouched.

Prescription checked: `ai/scripts/migrations/attributePreIdentityMemories.mjs` owns the concern. The migration owns the attribution's write set and its reverse; the reconciler's tenant rule fails closed on purpose, and the ingestor projects canonical nodes only.
