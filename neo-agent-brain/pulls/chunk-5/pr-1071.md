Resolves #1070

A version-2 summary receipt can omit a zero `unclassifiedSourceCount`, while Chroma's merging upsert retains an extra projected count. Recovery then repeats the same verification failure. New synthesis now publishes the numeric count including zero; replay materializes only that historical v2 omission as zero, leaving the durable envelope unchanged. Already-absent/zero projections remain no-ops. Explicit counts, v1 frozen keys, ownership refusal and Dream overlays retain their existing boundaries.

Evidence: L3 (isolated SQLite plus real SDK/disposable-Chroma recovery and second-pass convergence) meets the source-repair ACs. The existing #64 owns the observation after normal deployment; no live data repair or restart was performed.
