Resolves #1153

Related: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#732

Tracks all live sockets per `agentId` in `Bridge` (`agentSockets: Map<agentId, Set<WebSocket>>`) so closing a newer socket promotes the most-recent remaining open socket back into `Bridge.agents` instead of deleting the seat's registration and dropping App RPC replies, delivers `broadcastToAgents` frames to all open sockets for an `agentId`, makes `ConnectionService.ensureBridgeAndConnect()` short-circuit when already open and coalesce concurrent in-flight calls, and logs WebSocket `(code, reason)` on disconnect.

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit specs exercising Bridge multi-socket lifecycle, ConnectionService connect coalescing, and close logging) → L2 required (all close-target ACs are unit-verifiable in CI). Residual: none.
