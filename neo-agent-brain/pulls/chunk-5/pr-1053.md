Resolves #1046

The roster read its lanes from the viewer's own mailbox page. A human viewer never receives an `AGENT:*` broadcast, so every card read "no lane claimed". The lane record now folds the composer's own team observation: the non-stamping observer read, asked once a minute per viewer, never the caller's page or selector. It is `wired` only at team breadth. A narrower policy still folds what it admits and reports the A2A source degraded with the reason. Retained claims belong to the observation's `admissionKey`: a changed admission retires them, and a failed observation lends none. The forge's lane facts are read over every synced issue: each login's newest lane-claim comment and newest assignment among the open issues. The roster takes each seat's newest explicit claim across both sources, unless a newer A2A release ended it. With no standing claim, its open assignment fills the line as inferred, without a claim time.

Evidence: L3 (unit and integration specs: the composer, roster and wiring over fakes; the real A2A and PR/lane adapters end to end; the real MailboxService over SQLite through the host binding and through the MCP tool a plane serves) → L4 required for AC-5 (the installed Fleet Manager's roster). ## Deltas from ticket
- The [intake](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-brain/issues/1046#issuecomment-6199205161) folded the observation beside the ordinary page. The observation answers a superset of that page whenever it answers, so the held page, its fence and `readHeldA2ASnapshot` are deleted; `readHeldLaneSnapshot` replaces them. The observation is rate-limited because a mailbox page costs about a second on a team plane (#740).
- AC-2's admitted sources are two capability facts: `capabilities.lane` (A2A, with `policy`) and `capabilities.forgeLane`, mirrored per row as `sources.lane` and `sources.forgeLane`. `sources.lane.source` stays `memory-core:mailbox`, the only literal the Institution's `SourceHealth` accepts on that axis. Rows gain `laneSource: {source, confidence}`.
- Forge facts are the PR/lane slot's new `lanes` (`createIssueLaneFacts`), not its events. PR transitions crowd claims out of the newest 50 events, and synced comment blocks carry no id, so the event adapter never emitted a corpus comment claim at all. A closing issue ends its claim and its assignment.
- Assignment credits every assignee, per [Vega's fork answer](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-brain/issues/1046#issuecomment-6199462208), and has no claim time: `updatedAt` moves with every comment.
- A plane that predates `lanes` reads `forgeLane` degraded, "the PR/lane read answered no lane facts". The Institution card is unchanged.
- The saved lane record carries its `admissionKey`. After a restart it lends nothing until an observation under that key arrives, and a record saved without one lends nothing ([Euclid's RA-1](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-brain/pull/1053#pullrequestreview-5539107606)).
