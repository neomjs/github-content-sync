### 🔬 Depth Floor

- **Challenge** (optional simplification): Stage 4 and Stage 5 clocks are transitions by the ADR's own table. Stage 4 is "Transition time into that actionable state"; Stage 5 is "Message `sentAt`". Yet `admitClaimedTask` and `admitDirectMessage` still require the source to declare `clockBasis` (`normalizeLifecycleClock(task)`, `normalizeLifecycleClock({...message, actionableSince: message.sentAt})`). Every Memory Core reader will repeat the constant `'transition'`, and a reader that forgets it throws for the whole frontier. The predicates own those semantics and could stamp `transition` themselves. Declarations stay mandatory only where the basis genuinely varies: PR stages 1–3.
- **Documented search:** I actively looked for three gaps and found none.
  - **Undeclared clocks on records nobody consumes.** Only admitted clocks are validated; the per-request map stores `undefined` for a bare login, and that is resolved only for the consuming agent.
  - **A reason bypassing the bound at read time.** The reader runs the same validator.
  - **Pulses resetting age.** AC-3's producer arm keeps the clock while capture time moves.
