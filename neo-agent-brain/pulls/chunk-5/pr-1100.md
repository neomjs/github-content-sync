Resolves #459
Related: neomjs/neo#17416, neomjs/neo#19158, #443, #417, #457

Removes all `resources/content` defaults and fallbacks derived from `projectRoot`, the working directory, or `__dirname` across Brain configs, readers, and diagnostics (`ai/configBase.mjs`, `ai/mcp/server/github-workflow/configBase.mjs`, `wireFleetActivityReadSource.mjs`, `LocalFileService.mjs`, `SyncService.mjs`, `GoldenPathSynthesizer.mjs`, `ConceptDiscoveryService.mjs`, `IssueIngestor.mjs`, `detectTruncatedTimelines.mjs`, `audit-discussion-lifecycle.mjs`), updates `QueryService#inferSourceType` to classify one-segment origin-qualified corpus paths (`^([^/]+\/)?(archive\/)?(issues|discussions|pulls|release-notes)\/`) without relying on a `resources/content/` prefix, and updates `SearchService#resolveLocalReferenceRoot` so it no longer maps `resources/content/` back to `aiConfig.projectRoot`.

**Red-first state of KB source typing (AC-2):** Prior to this PR, `QueryService#inferSourceType` matched `source.startsWith('resources/content/issues/')` (and sibling `resources/content/...` prefixes) with a `match?.type ?? 'raw'` fallthrough (`QueryService.mjs:75`), so one-segment origin-qualified corpus paths (`neo/issues/chunk-1/issue-1.md`, `neo/archive/pulls/v12.0.0/chunk-1/pr-2.md`, `neo/discussions/chunk-1/discussion-3.md`, `neo/release-notes/chunk-1/v12.0.0.md`) fell through to `'raw'` instead of `'ticket'`, `'pull'`, `'discussion'`, and `'release'` (while two-segment `neomjs/neo-agent-brain/...` paths typed `'raw'` both before and after this PR). Separately, `SearchService`'s change is in `resolveLocalReferenceRoot` (`SearchService.mjs:214-219`), whose unit arm (`SearchService.spec.mjs:115`) cannot discriminate where `neoRootDir === projectRoot`.

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit suites across all reader/config/sync/KB surfaces + `audit-discussion-lifecycle.mjs --self-test` + ADR-0019 lint guards) → L2 required (all 4 close-target ACs are unit- and lint-verifiable in CI).
