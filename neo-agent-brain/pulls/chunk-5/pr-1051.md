*   **Patch Verdict:** Matches. Evidence:
    - `openLaunchAdmissionJournal` publishes the successor's fence before its state, so a crash between the two reads `handoff-replayed`. A clean exit is `phase: handoff` at the same epoch, and `close()` alone is `unclean-issuer-exit`.
    - `writeFileAtomicSync({fsync: true})` flushes the scratch file and the directory, and throws when it cannot. Record reads use `O_NOFOLLOW`, a UID and 0600 check, and a dev/ino/mtime/ctime re-stat.
    - `recover()` re-adopts every generation as `STALE` first, and `REVOKED` stays revoked. It restores `ACTIVE` only after the seat, identity, origin, runtime-pin, definition-hash and live-process checks pass, plus a capability re-read whose SHA-256 must equal the recorded `capabilityHash`. It then listens on the exact recorded origin, and a bind conflict leaves it unavailable.
    - `performHandoff` refuses pending Starts, drains in-flight proofs and awaits `server.close` before the durable publish.
    - `fleetMcpLauncher` realpath-matches the signed `generationRoot` to its own root.
    - The signing key moves from that direct env read to a declarative `fleet.signingKey` leaf read at its use site (ADR-0019 §5.1–§5.2, an improvement).
