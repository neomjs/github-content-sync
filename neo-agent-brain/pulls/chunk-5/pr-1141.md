Resolves #1140

A seat freshly started from Fleet Manager now binds its `bridge-token` launch credential owner to `this.getRegistry()` (`FleetRegistryService` in production) instead of the `this.registry` unit-test seam (`null` in production), and `createBridgeTokenOwner` derives `storeRoot: storeRoot(registry)` at construction time so a missing or mismatched registry fails fast at `Start` rather than refusing `neo-mjs-neural-link` with `(credential-missing, bridge-token)` at launch redemption.

Evidence: L2 (unit tests in `launchCredentialOwners.spec.mjs` and `FleetLifecycleService.spec.mjs`) → L2 required (AC-1 and AC-2 are unit-observable). No residuals.
