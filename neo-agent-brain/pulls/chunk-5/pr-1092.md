- [ ] **RA-1: Fix the Proxy receiver at its owner, and keep the snapshot on the instance.**
  - The cause: `makeSafe` (`ai/services/shared/serviceProxy.mjs`) traps `get` with `Reflect.get(target, prop, target)` but has no `set` trap. A setter called through `SDK.*` therefore runs with `this` set to the Proxy, and a private-field write throws.

- [ ] **RA-2: One shared snapshot seam, not a copy per service.**
  - The duplication: VectorService pastes `isPlainSnapshotObject` and the accessor / validator / `#getConfig()` set from TES, and the copies have already diverged: TES's validator recurses, VectorService's does not.
  - The fix: move the shared parts (`isPlainSnapshotObject`, `cloneConfigSubtree`, the validate-then-assign step) into `ai/services/shared/`, beside `serviceProxy.mjs` and `secretCarrier.mjs`. Each service keeps only its thin accessor and its leaf set.
  - Why now: the second copy is where the extraction belongs. #1091 would otherwise paste it into every service its 53 files touch.
- [ ] **RA-3: Scope `cloneConfigSubtree`'s premise to the pin, and name its retirement trigger.**
