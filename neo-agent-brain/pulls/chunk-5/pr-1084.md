Resolves #1083

🌿 *A route the receiver keeps declining can no longer call itself reachable on the strength of a delivery from weeks ago.*

The AC-7 audit on #503 found that ticket's own subscription reading `reachable` through 29 consecutive skips, 18 days after its last delivery. Skips stay transparent to the failure streak, as Eos designed it: a skip never counts as a failure and never erases one. What changes is the delivery's shelf life. A run of `STALE_SKIP_RUN` (3) skips, newer than every conclusion, retires the delivery as evidence. The route then reads `unknown`, never `unreachable`, and the verdict carries the run as the additive `consecutiveSkips`. A delivery now also counts as an attempt, so `lastAttemptedAt` no longer predates `lastDeliveredAt`.

Evidence: L1 (a pure projection; unit arms red-first against `dev`'s module) → L1 required (a projection rule; its deployed reading is AC-5). Residual: AC-5, post-merge. Residual-Owner: @neo-opus-vega.
