Resolves #1078

The continuity path now says what it did. On quit, `devFleetServer`'s `cleanShutdown` logs the handoff's start before its first await (the running seats, the per-stage bound), then `sealed after <ms>` or `not completed after <ms>: Fleet continuity refused: <stage>` — the service already named the stage; the log dropped it. On boot, the successor logs what it inherited: the previous issuer's exit (`unclean-issuer-exit`, `handoff-replayed`, `handed-off` or `none`), its origin, and each retained generation with the check that kept it stale. `McpLaunchAdmissionService` keeps that as `continuityRecord`, names every per-seat recovery check instead of throwing bare errors, and projects a `detail` beside the unchanged `reason` on `statusOf()` — a running seat this process holds no generation for reads `stale · issuer-replaced · unclean-issuer-exit`, a retained generation reads the check it failed.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms on the admission service over a journal fixture; the dev server's quit and boot lines are code-read, not run — the installed quit is the host's act) → L4 required (AC-3: an FM Quit → relaunch with nine running seats, the reason read from the log and the roster). Residual: AC-3, Residual-Owner: #1081 (the budget leaf split out of #1078, blocked by it; its AC-4 is the same FM Quit → relaunch witness).
