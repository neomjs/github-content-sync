Resolves #1019

Two parts close the silent zero of `query_recent_turns` for other identities, its third sighting after June, July and 2026-10-14's misread of Gemini's trail:

1. **On a `team` deployment**, a public-summary read of another identity without `memorySharing` now takes the `team` policy. That is the read every caller is already entitled to there, per the operator: *"we have team sharing mode enabled … you should get results"*.
2. **When an owner-scoped page for another identity is still empty**, for example under an explicit `private`, or on `private`/`legacy` deployments, the response carries a `hint` naming the restriction and the next step.

Unchanged:
- recovery of one's own identity stays tenant-scoped;
- full or private projections stay `private`;
- on `private` and `legacy` deployments, the no-parameter path stays exactly #741's;
- `scope` stays the refusal marker.

Evidence: L1 (unit, real in-memory SQLite graph plus #741's isolated private/legacy deployment probe) → L1 required (every close-target AC is a method-contract AC). Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket
- **`hint`, not `scope`:** `fleetRecentTurnsSource.mjs` reads any `scope` as a refusal. The hint goes only on empty pages, because `userId` is the tenant and tenant-mates are real.
- **The `team`-default read (commit 2)** was added at the operator's direction. It amends #741's default-path line and #12671's AC-7a for `team` deployments only, and #1019's body records the amendment. Two #741 tests changed with intent kept:
  - AC7a now asserts isolation under explicit `private` and the deployment-default read.
  - The unreadable-WAL case now separates recovery (self read, degrades) from shared reads (explicit or implicit, fail).
- **`legacy` deployments are deliberately not widened:** only `team` declares peers' summaries shared.
- **Euclid's owned-surface read (commit 3):**
  - The implicit-`team` posture now resolves before the absent-graph shortcut, so an implicit foreign read fails like the explicit one instead of returning a silent zero. #741's absent-graph test now asserts the soft fallback for recovery (self) only.
  - The `legacy` hint names untagged rows too.
