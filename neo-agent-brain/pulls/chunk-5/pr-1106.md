Resolves #1101
Related: neomjs/neo#17416, neomjs/neo#19158, #1100, #1103

The GitHub sync CLI now runs corpus emission only. `npm run ai:sync-github-workflow` and `--emit-only` refuse before the lease or any request, naming `--corpus-only`. `--corpus-only` and `--refetch-issues` are unchanged, including the literals the corpus publisher greps for.

The in-engine delivery is gone:
- `runFullSync`, plus the auto-push and commit-rebase-push path with its recovery and allowlist;
- `IssueSyncer.pushToGitHub`, its helpers, and the two GraphQL operations only it used;
- the push's `pushFailures` bookkeeping and the `pushToRepoAfterSync` leaf;
- the dev-branch guard and an orphan migration.

`PrimaryRepoSyncService` loses its metadata-reset step, and its KB-relevance prefix narrows to the concepts root the KB still reads. The net diff is +240/−1665.

Evidence: L2 (unit and CLI-child arms, plus the full Brain unit suite on `dev` and on this branch with failure sets diffed) → L2 required (AC-1…AC-6 are CLI, source and doc properties). Residual: none.

Prescription checked: `ai/scripts/maintenance/syncGithubWorkflow.mjs` and `ai/services/github-workflow/SyncService.mjs` own the concern. The CLI is the only entrypoint of the retired modes, and the service owns the delivery, so deleting at both removes the behavior. A flag or config switch would keep it alive.

Rebased onto `dev` `623a0109`, after #1100, #1104 and #1103 merged. #1100's emission guard stays, but it now reads `#getConfig().issueSync.contentRoot` directly. Its `contentRoot` option is gone (AC-7): no production caller passed it, and Stage2 now gets its root from the `configSnapshot` seam. #1103 had moved `pushToGitHub` and `#scanLocalFiles` onto its `#getConfig()` seam. Both stay deleted here, and `IssueSyncer.spec.mjs` keeps #1103's `findSharedConfigMutations` import.
