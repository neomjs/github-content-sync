Resolves #1824

🌿 A wake that started its turn can no longer hold every later wake behind it.

When the recipient hook never redeemed a wake, its slot stayed `unknown` until the seat's session changed, and every ordinary wake waited behind it. `readCodexWakeReceipt()` now reports `turn-unacknowledged` once the exact wake text started its queue turn and that turn completed or aborted with no hook acknowledgement. With no claim for the nonce, the fleet reader asks for that state, and `deliverPrimarySessionWake()` settles the slot as delivered with `acknowledged: false`. The flag reaches the receiver record through the spool result, where #503 can read it. Nothing is withdrawn or re-sent.

Evidence: L2 (unit and receiver-integration specs through injected Codex transports; ten mutations) → L3 required (AC-5: a real unacknowledged wake on a live seat). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: #503.
