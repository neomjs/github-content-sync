Resolves #493

Self-heals wrong LM Studio residents autonomously under `warm-provider` instead of printing operator-addressed `lms unload` / `lms load` instructions. Restores `unloadLmsModel`, replaces a pinned resident at the wrong shape or a diagnosed foreign resident answering the role's requests in `ensureLmsModelsLoaded` (`replaced` / `heal-exhausted` with a persisted per-role failure counter at `orchestrator.recoveryActuator.lmsReplacementFailuresPath`), retires `replacement-required` and operator imperatives across readiness and `DeploymentStateBridgeService`, and wires bridge `providerModelIdentity.state = 'mismatch'` plus readiness shape mismatches through `wrong-resident` residency facts (`diagnosedWrongResidents`) into `warm-provider`.

Evidence: L2 (injected CLI `execFileFn` / actuator recorders + real temp-directory failure-counter persistence in CI) → L2 required (AC-1..AC-6 specify injected recorder and `brain-unit.yml` unit coverage). No residuals.
