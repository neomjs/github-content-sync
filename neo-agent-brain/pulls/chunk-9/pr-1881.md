Resolves #1840

Stops the host wake receiver (`ai/daemons/wake/receiver.mjs`) from re-dispatching, rewriting, and re-logging spool-blocked pending native wakes on every `drain()` pass, deduplicates unprobeable `contextGate` fail-open warnings to once per record, and coalesces concurrent `drain()` triggers (`POST /wake` bursts, `scheduleRotationRetry()`, periodic timer) to at most one trailing pass behind an active pass while keeping staged `codex-desktop` batch candidates (`#1878`) re-probed before commit.

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit suite including 300-record spool contention + real `stagePrimaryWake` settlement + concurrent drain coalescing) → L4 required (AC4 post-deploy 1-minute host `com.neomjs.agent-os-wake` CPU share and `launchd.err.log` rate). Residual: AC4, Residual-Owner: #1373.
