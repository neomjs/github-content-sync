Resolves #1810

A GitHub rename releases the old login for anyone to register. `classifyAuthorTrust` gave every roster `formerLogins` entry its resident's trust tier before any standing check, so whoever registered a released login would be classified like the renamed seat and skip the hostile-content sanitizer. Now a former-login match keeps the resident's tier only when the author shows org standing: `authorAssociation` is OWNER, MEMBER or COLLABORATOR, or the login is in the injected collaborator set. Without standing it falls through to `EXTERNAL`. Current logins are unchanged.

Evidence: L2 (unit: the pure classifier and its conversation-level caller, red-first on `dev` source) → L2 required (all four ACs are unit-level).

## AC Evidence

| AC | Proof |
| --- | --- |
| AC-1 | `a former login without org standing is a stranger` — every roster former login with no association, `NONE`, `CONTRIBUTOR`, `FIRST_TIME_CONTRIBUTOR` or `FIRST_TIMER` classifies `EXTERNAL`, and `isExternalTier` returns true. At the conversation level, `a formerLogins comment without org standing is quarantined like any stranger` (`ConversationTrust.spec.mjs`). |
| AC-2 | `a former login with org standing keeps the owning resident trust tier` — MEMBER, OWNER (case-insensitive) and COLLABORATOR, plus a collaborator-set entry, each keep `peer-trusted`. |
| AC-3 | Existing rows unchanged: current logins (with and without association), non-rostered org members, untrusted associations and unknown logins. The new stranger test also asserts that a current login needs no corroboration. |
| AC-4 | The `classifyAuthorTrust` docblock's resolution order now has the former-login step with its standing condition and the reason. The two map docblocks say why they are split. |

CI's `unit` gate at `43c81b42` found one caller spec pinning the old behavior: the #1279 test gave a former-login reply no association and expected `peer-trusted`. `2e7f5ea9` gives that reply MEMBER standing and adds the stranger test above. `discussionRoutingDisposition.mjs` falls back to `classifyAuthorTrust(author)` without an association, so a historical former-login author there now reads untrusted. That is the intended fail-closed direction.
