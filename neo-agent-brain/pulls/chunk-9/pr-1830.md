Resolves #1812

Memory Core binds each verified provider subject `{authProvider, providerBaseUrl, providerUserId}` to the one `AgentIdentity` it acts as, in a protected relation outside Nodes/Edges. Selection by subject ships dormant. Until the cutover leaf (#1814) records the activation, requests keep today's login selection, and only identities created at first admission gain a binding. Once it is active, every request that carries a provider subject or may create an identity selects by subject, lookup-only planes included. A bound subject keeps its identity whatever its login spells, and that identity's bare id keys the request's reads and writes. Every conflict fails closed by name:
- an incomplete subject;
- a login naming another subject's identity;
- an unbound legacy identity;
- an unbound subject the founding record maps;
- an unbound subject whose source creates no identity;
- an activation the graph cannot read.

Bindings survive identity migrations and every graph-bundle restore path, which restores the relation as one unit. An administrative endpoint move keeps the endpoints it leaves, so family history keyed by an earlier subject tuple stays reachable. The plane-local `ai/scripts/maintenance/subjectBindings.mjs` (`list`, `bind`, `seed`) is the only administrative writer.

Evidence: L2 (spec-driven contract tests on real SQLite through the real request path; selection ships dormant) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-7, AC-10 and AC-11 are contract properties of dormant code; activating them on a live plane is the cutover leaf #1814's).
