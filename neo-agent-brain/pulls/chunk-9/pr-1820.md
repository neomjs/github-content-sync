Resolves #1806

Fixed the three compounding `QueryService.mjs` retrieval and ranking regressions measured in `#1800` so domain-matching source files are no longer buried by unrelated guides or multi-chunk `src/core/Base.mjs` accumulation, and Engine source/guide paths resolve into `node_modules/neo.mjs/`.

- **Gated `guideMatch` & `conceptMatch` on non-stopword matches (capped per guide):** Stopped awarding `queryScoreWeights.guideMatch` (`+50`) / `conceptMatch` (`+15`) for every word longer than two characters regardless of relevance. Words in `guideStopWords` no longer score on guides/concepts, path/filename/name matches are checked per domain keyword, chunk-content fallback triggers at most once per chunk, and cumulative `guideMatch` per guide `sourcePath` is capped at `guideMatch * 3` (`150`).
- **Bounded `baseFileBonus` & `inheritanceBoost` (attenuated `src/core/Base.mjs`):** Awarded `baseFileBonus` once per distinct `sourcePath`, applied `inheritanceChain` boosts once per distinct child source, skipped `src/core/Base.mjs` inheritance boosts from `ai/` (`brain-source` / `ai-infrastructure`) children unless `core/base` / `neo.core.base` is queried explicitly, capped `src/core/Base.mjs` cumulative inheritance boost from Engine children at `Math.floor(inheritanceBoost * 0.5)` (`40`), and capped specific ancestor classes at `inheritanceBoost * 6` (`480`).
- **Extended lexical rescue to `node_modules/neo.mjs/` (`resolveRescueTarget` & `getLexicalRescueRoots`):** Resolved path hints, filename hints (fallback to `node_modules/neo.mjs/{src,learn}` when `neoRootDir` returns zero filename matches), `learn/tree.json` guide-title rescues (tightened word threshold and stop-word list `'neo'`, `'for'`, `'from'`, `'into'` so multi-word ADR/guide titles require specific overlap), and code-term rescue indexing into `node_modules/neo.mjs/src` with Engine `src/*` paths classified as `type: 'src'`.

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit specs + live 92,257-chunk Chroma 49-query ranking benchmark) → L2 required (all ACs verify scoring, rescue, and benchmark rank thresholds directly). No residuals.

## Deltas from Ticket

- In addition to gating `guideMatch` per non-stopword match, capped cumulative `guideMatch` per guide `sourcePath` (`guideMatch * 3`) so single multi-chunk Engine guides (`WorkingWithVDom.md`, etc.) do not crowd out specific parent-class answers (`E272` moved into Top-5, lifting `parent-class` to `5/5`).
- Tightened `addGuideTitleRescues` required hit count (`titleWords.length <= 3 ? titleWords.length : titleWords.length - 1`) and excluded generic word `'neo'` so exposing `node_modules/neo.mjs/learn/tree.json` does not falsely rescue unrelated `Neo Identity ...` titles on generic Engine/Brain questions.
- Preserved `inferSourceType` synchronous behavior and queried `node_modules/neo.mjs/{src,learn}` in `addFilenameHintRescues` only when `neoRootDir` returns zero basename matches.
