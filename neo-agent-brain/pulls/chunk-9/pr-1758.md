Resolves #1756
Related: #1430, #1733

Closes the `[minReclaimableBuildCacheBytes, buildCacheWarmFloorBytes + minReclaimableBuildCacheBytes)` dead band in `ContainerHealthDiagnosisService.diagnoseEngineDisk` by reading `AiConfig.orchestrator.diskPressure.buildCacheWarmFloorBytes` (`2 GiB`) directly at the use site so sustained Docker / data-mount disk pressure routes to `prune-build-cache` only when `reclaimableBuildCacheBytes - buildCacheWarmFloorBytes >= minReclaimableBuildCacheBytes` (`1 GiB`), and otherwise routes to `record`. Extracts `DiskPressureDefectService` beside `ContainerHealthControllerService` and unifies `Orchestrator.publishDefectNote` so unrelieved disk pressure (`record` or `prune-build-cache` returning `alarmOnly` / `prune-freed-nothing`) emits one quiet `defect-note:` to `AGENT:*` per persisted episode (`disk-pressure-defect-episode.json`) with a stable subject fingerprint and per-class readings (`usedPercent`, `availableBytes`, `images`, `volumes`, `buildCache`) in the body, followed by `defect-note: [recovered]` once a fresh valid `statfs` sample drops below `AiConfig.orchestrator.diskPressure.percent`.

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit specs exercising warm-floor dead-band routing against the live `AiConfig` leaf, `DiskPressureDefectService` persisted episode dedupe across restarts, and `defectObservationFold` `red` -> `recovered` -> `red` folding across distinct readings; sandbox ceiling: container-plane cut requires operator rollout) -> L4 required (AC3). Residual: AC3, Residual-Owner: #1733.
