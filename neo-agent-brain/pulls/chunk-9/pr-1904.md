Resolves #1899
Related: #759, neomjs/neo#21964

Gates already-requested unapproved reviewer routing (`openWorkReducer`, `openWorkHolder#holderOf`, `wireFleetOpenWorkWakes`) and formal review approval (`PullRequestService#managePrReview` on `action: 'create', state: 'APPROVED'`) on live head/base mergeability per graduated neomjs/neo#21955:

1. **Producer normalization, Fleet holder & wakes (`openWorkQueries.mjs`, `openWorkReducer.mjs`, `openWorkHolder.mjs`, `wireFleetOpenWorkWakes.mjs`):** Selects `commit.status.contexts.context` and separates `review-admission/mergeability` (`row.reviewAdmission`) from genuine test CI (`row.ci` and `failedChecksOf`). For non-draft, unapproved green heads with an existing `requested` reviewer, `MERGEABLE` (with passing or absent admission status) retains normal reviewer selection, `CONFLICTING` routes `rebase` (`rotation` with empty IDs on eligible outside PRs, `author` on seat-owned/unowned PRs) without removing GitHub `reviewRequests`, and `UNKNOWN`/missing mergeability or a non-success `review-admission/mergeability` status yields `{role: 'unknown', kind: null, ids: []}` without assigning CI repair until resolved.
2. **Managed approval (`PullRequestService.mjs`, `mutations.mjs`, `openapi.yaml`):** Repeats `readReviewAdmissionMergeability` before `addPullRequestReview` on `APPROVED` creates, refusing head-drifted observations first (`PR_MERGEABILITY_UNAVAILABLE`, including when a different head conflicts), reviewed-head conflicts (`PR_MERGE_CONFLICT`), and pending/unreadable/transport-failed observations (`PR_MERGEABILITY_UNAVAILABLE`, retaining the last valid observed `headSha`/`baseSha` across retry transport failures) with zero mutations, then binds the outgoing `addPullRequestReview` call to the verified `commitOID` while leaving `COMMENT`, `REQUEST_CHANGES`, and review-body updates on their existing paths (separate read from subsequent base moves).

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit suites across `openWorkReducer`, `openWorkHolder`, `fleetOpenWorkSource`, `openWorkWakes`, `wireFleetOpenWorkWakes`, `PullRequestService`, `PullRequestServiceReviewers`, and OpenAPI/MCP compliance) → L2 required (pure reducer, selector, wake planner, projection, and service/OpenAPI contract).
