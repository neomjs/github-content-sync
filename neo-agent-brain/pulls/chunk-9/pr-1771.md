Resolves #1770

A sunset rotation now waits for the host's first opening instead of expiring a minute after its accept. Under the plane's signed hold (`holdUntil`, the receipt's `sentAt` plus `windowMs`), the hold alone is the rotation's deadline (`rotationDeadline`). A locked screen defers `screen-locked` without calling the adapter, so nothing activates or types (`readScreenLocked()`: `ioreg -n Root -d 1`, `CGSSessionScreenIsLocked`, no privacy grant). A person at the keyboard defers `human-input-active`, as before. Neither ends the wait any more. Lost activations, the one defer that takes the front from a person, are capped at 5 (`ROTATION_MAX_ACTIVATION_LOSSES`, failure `session-rotate-front-lost`). An envelope without a valid hold keeps the 60 s of wall-clock time an older plane was promised. The fresh-session script asserts the front again and compares an absolute cutoff right before Cmd+N, after the holder check. The holder fence records the seat's holder on the first pass and checks it on every later one, so a session started by hand fails the rotation `session-rotate-superseded`. When it records a rotation `incomplete`, the plane posts one `defect-note:` through `publishDefectNote`. The note reports the missing successor marker and asks for the seat's window to be checked before anyone starts a session by hand.

## Deltas from ticket
- A rotation that is still locked is skipped at the top of the drain, before it is claimed: one `ioreg` read per pass and no record write, so an overnight lock causes no file churn. The first locked pass (which records the holder) and the first pass after the unlock run the whole path. Arm: "#1770: while the lock holds, a waiting rotation is neither claimed nor rewritten".
- `isRotationExpired` and `rotationDeadline` take the receiver record instead of `acceptedAt`, and their three callers moved with them. `rotationDeadline` reads no clock.
- `check-block-alignment --fix` realigned one neighbouring `const` block in `buildOsascriptArgs`.
- The `incomplete` note's hand-start prompt says the session was started by hand. The plane's own prompt says the plane opened it, which would misstate a person's start. The operator caught it on a manual restart.
- The note's subject and body name what the plane observes: no successor marker. It asks for the seat's window to be checked first, because a session can open without sending its marker.
- The fresh-session script takes an absolute cutoff (`freshSessionCutoffSec`, Unix seconds) instead of a duration. It reads the clock after the holder check, right before Cmd+N, and refuses at or past the cutoff.
- The adapter re-reads the lock at its last boundary and after a failed activation. A lock there defers `screen-locked`.
- The script asserts the front again right before the cutoff check (`"before fresh session"`), so a lock that engages after a successful activation is caught before Cmd+N. A loss there is retry-safe (`isPreSessionFailure`), because only the tab shortcut precedes it and that opens no session. The holder and cutoff refusals stay final.
