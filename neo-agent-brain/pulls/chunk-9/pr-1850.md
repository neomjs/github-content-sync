Resolves #1849

The open-work rotation now reads which seats show no sign they can act from the round's presence answer: presence `dark`, and a wake that is not `reachable`. It picks such a seat last. An admitted rotation slot moves on from one as soon as another seat qualifies, and keeps its episode. It moves on only from a wake the plane took, and never back to a seat it already woke or tried: a failed submission may have reached its seat. With every seat absent, the hashed pick stands as before. Holder wakes (author, reviewer, merge) are untouched. On neomjs/neo#21445, the triage would have reached a live seat instead of going three times to a seat dark since 16 Nov.

## Deltas from ticket

- When its ledger holds an admitted rotation slot, the round reads the absent seats before its first pass, as `deliveriesOf` does for silent holders. A re-pick plans no new action, so the first pass has to see who is absent. The route map's 5-minute cache bounds the extra presence reads.
- Names: `absentSeats` (planner), `readAbsent` (round), and `absentOf` (the presence reader, beside `undeliverableOf`).
- Ledger: from Euclid's review, a handoff records the seats a slot woke (`handedFrom`, which the validator accepts on rotation slots only), and the new wake counts its submissions from zero.
  - A spent slot has no receipt, so it stays put with its count.
  - A slot that moves on after a failed submission records that seat too, because the plane may have taken the wake before its answer was lost. So neither A → B → A nor A → B (answer lost) → C → B can replay a first receipt.
