Resolves #321

Related: #304 (the human producer's silent losses — complementary, untouched)

The defect ledger gets its machine producer. An orchestrator task, `ci-failure-ingest`, reads the repository's CI runs since its receipt, and for every failed job files one `defect-note:` per failing test the run-and-job thread does not hold yet — the surface is the test (`<spec path> › <title path>`), the symptom is the first `Error:` line of the failure block matched on the full test identity, the run and job are the thread. A job that ran its suite green is candidate recovery evidence: a CI-filed record that has been quiet for a day recovers under the newest green run of its job **when that job's log names the record's test with a pass mark**; a job's colour says nothing about one test, and a reporter that names no passing tests can prove nothing. The fold records the distinct threads a fingerprint was sighted under and the promotion trigger accepts two threads from one reporter as independent — a machine producer is one identity, so the run is its independence; the digest is unchanged.

Admission is per observation and completeness is stated: a write interrupted after its first note resumes at the second; the mailbox is paged to its end or the tick certifies no recovery; a run listing that exhausted its page bound leaves the unread remainder as a continuation slice in the receipt, drained before the next window, and an open continuation certifies no recovery; runs still running are carried as pending and finished by id; an epilogue shorter than its own count files nothing.

Evidence: L3 (the real Actions REST client, parser and note builder over the live `neomjs/neo` history of the last twenty hours, dry run against an empty mailbox; the shipped fold and grammar asserted in the specs, never a copy; every recovery, admission and completeness boundary exercised by injected controls) → L3 required (AC-3). AC-8 is post-merge by the ticket's own definition and is listed under Post-Merge Validation. Stated bound, not a residual: records sighted by a job whose reporter names no passing tests (`unit`'s `github` reporter) cannot recover automatically — AC-4 says so; neomjs/neo#18361 (filed, mine) adds the `list` reporter beside `github` on the engine's unit job and lifts it.

## Deltas from ticket

- **The trigger changes, by evidence.** `independentSecondOccurrence` required two distinct reporters; a machine producer files as one identity per orchestrator, so no CI-only record could ever qualify. The fold records `threads` and the trigger accepts two threads from one reporter. A human echoing their own note without a thread stays an echo. (Ticket ledger and AC-6 aligned in the body.)
- **Recovery waits a day AND needs the test observed passing.** "Subsequently passes" is read as "the affected test is named passing by a newer green run of its job after the quiet window": the quiet window keeps flakes open long enough for the digest; the pass mark in the evidence job's log is what makes a green job evidence about *this* test rather than about a file or a branch. Jobs whose reporter names no passes (the `unit` job's `github` reporter) cannot recover records — stated in AC-4; neomjs/neo#18361 adds the `list` reporter beside `github` on the engine's unit job. (AC-4 rewritten in the body.)
- **Admission is per observation, not per job**, so an interrupted write is lossless. (AC-9 added.)
- **Completeness is stated:** paged mailbox reads with a cap that suppresses recovery when hit; running runs pending in the receipt; a bounded run listing leaves a continuation slice (receipt version 2 — a version-1 receipt is read as version 2 with nothing pending and no continuation); an epilogue shorter than its count files nothing; blocks matched by full identity. (AC-10 added and widened; AC-5 widened.)
- **The source is the job log, not a report.** CI uploads `test-results/` only on failure and the artifact carries `error-context.md` files only; both reporters CI runs print the same epilogue and failure blocks, so one grammar serves `unit` and `components`, and the `list` reporter's `✓` lines are the pass evidence.
- **Cost, measured.** A tick lists jobs only for failed runs and for green runs of a workflow with an open CI-filed record; pending runs add one read each; a recovery candidate adds one log read per evidence job; a continuation adds one bounded listing per tick.
