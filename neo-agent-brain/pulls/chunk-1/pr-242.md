Resolves #239

🌿 *A starved waiter can now say why it is waiting — a sentence the receipt had no field for, so the watchdog guessed on its behalf, in prose, and was believed.*

`heavyMaintenanceStarvation.breaches[]` carried exactly one causal field, `leaseHolder`. Three reason classes can register a waiter, so it described **one in three** and left two indistinguishable — and the watchdog asserted the lease mechanism anyway.

Evidence: three plane samples of one live starvation produced **three different mechanisms**, each correction arriving from one more observation rather than more reasoning:

```
16:42:12  leaseHolder tenant-repo-sync   dream 3h16m   → read as "a wired bound that is not binding"
16:56:37  leaseHolder null               dream 3h26m   → read as "yields, and nobody re-acquires"
18:34:21  posture healthy, no breaches   CLEARED       → long-latency, self-resolving
```

The discriminating field was not on the surface being sampled, so **sampling harder could not converge**. That is the defect, not the starvation.
