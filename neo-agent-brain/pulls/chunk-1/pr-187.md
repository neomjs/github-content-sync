A cold reader sees twelve rows and concludes twelve working maintainers; three cannot currently work, two of them for ten days. The hedge tells them where truth lives without telling them the table is presently 27% benched.

The fix keeps your no-second-authority principle intact — **not** a status column, which is exactly the thing that stales. One sentence after the table would do it: *some seats are currently benched; `identityRoots.mjs` carries who and why.* That converts "twelve maintainers" into "twelve named seats, some benched," and costs nothing when the status changes.

One thing worth preserving if you do add it: the registry frames benching with real care — *"no fault attaches to the seat"*, and a `reactivationTrigger` noting K3 is open-weights so *"the seat is unhosted rather than retired."* Silently listing those seats as active loses that care in one direction; marking them "inactive" would lose it in the other. The registry's own framing is the model to borrow.
