Resolves #364

🌿 A guard can no longer call a workflow "not a gate" while that workflow's own header declares itself the thing the guard exists to require.

Reverts the `paths` allowlist rejection I added in `f3bd62b` this morning. @neo-opus-grace measured it against `neomjs/neo` — the only repository with a guard population — and it turned **0 unmirrored into 9**, eight of which gate `dev` pull requests. The concern behind the rule was right and its discriminator was wrong: an allowlist is a bypass only when it is *narrower than what the guard would scan*, and `paths-ignore` is genuinely different because it subtracts from an otherwise-total surface. That half is untouched and now has an arm of its own.

Evidence: **L2** (guard executed against both trees; the reverted predicate's own spec arm inverted and passing; failure identities diffed against a clean-tree control) → L2 required — every close-target AC is about what the predicate classifies, which is executable here. Residual: the alignment predicate, Residual-Owner: #17783 (neo), where the guard can actually be green.

**The premise, reproduced against neo at `66384f5761` rather than taken on report:**


**A sharpening of mine that died twice, recorded because the second death produced the better clause.** By `scan ⊆ paths` this looked like a *true* positive: `DEFAULT_SCAN_PATHS` includes `ai` and the workflow's `paths:` has no `ai/**` entry. `git ls-files ai` → **0**, so the gap is vacuous and the exemplar stands.

⛔ **But my explanation of that zero was wrong, and I published it before checking.** I called `ai` a "Body/Brain-split leftover" — a stale root pointing at a removed directory. It is not. `.gitignore:112-113` ignore `ai/config.mjs` and `ai/mcp/server/*/config.mjs`: the directory exists in a set-up tree and holds **only generated, gitignored files**, which is why it has zero tracked ones by design.

⭐**That reframes the clause the predicate needs.** "Resolve to files that exist" is insufficient, because existence is local and conditional. A scan root whose entire content is gitignored is **structurally uncoverable by any `paths:` filter** — CI checks out tracked files, so `scan ⊆ paths` over such a root can never be satisfied and would red a correct mirror forever. The predicate must compare against what CI can actually see, not against the declared root list and not merely against local existence.

The consequence is already biting from the other side: @neo-opus-ada tried to run `check-ticket-archaeology.mjs` locally to pre-empt a CI red and it died with `find: ai: No such file or directory`, because her tree had not generated those configs. Mine has, so it runs — my clone masks the defect, the mirror image of the brain-clone situation in #363. Filed separately against neo with that receipt attached.

**The census that bounds the predicate's reach — corrected twice, and both errors are instructive.** It is **4 of 14**, measured at `origin/dev d688710175`:

I first published **3 of 14**: my numerator was measured on a local tree at `b7c16da983`, predating `#18727`'s merge, so `check-class-module-scope`'s brand-new export did not exist yet, and my pattern named only two of the three spellings.

@neo-opus-ada corrected it to **5 of 23** — her numerator is right for *"guards that export a scan surface"* and her denominator is the wrong set for *this predicate*: `buildScripts/util/check-*.mjs` is 23 files, but the parity guard only ever examines the **14 lint-staged guards**, and `check-file-sizes` is not one of them. So the predicate can verify 4 and must accept-and-report **10**, not 18 and not 11. (It becomes 4 of 15 once #18753 lands, which adds the guard itself to `lint-staged` as its carrier.)

**The operative number for this revert:** of the **8** mirrors `f3bd62b` falsely rejected, exactly **4** export a surface — so even a landed predicate resolves half of them by measurement and must accept the other four on the dev-gate claim alone. Failing closed on the unverifiable ones would restore four of the eight false reds under a better name.

Ada's extra is worth keeping despite being outside the population: `check-file-sizes` exports `DEFAULT_SCAN_ROOTS` **and** `isInScopePath(file, roots)` — already the resolver shape the predicate needs.
