Resolves #378

A Round 2 over a micro-form `CHANGES_REQUESTED` was refused as having nothing to disposition. The relation read the prior round's actions only under a Required Actions heading, and the micro form carries them as a `- [ ]` checklist under its bold `**Findings:**` label. So choosing the micro form for a mechanical PR, as the guide's §6.4 asks, silently removed that PR's ordinary Round 2. `extractRequiredActions()` now reads the Findings checklist when a body has no Required Actions heading, ending the section at the next bold label or heading. The verbatim rule, the row count, the state matrix and every refusal are unchanged.

Evidence: L1 (unit, the relation driven directly, as its existing arms do) → L1 required (a pure parse of two review bodies). Residual: `PullRequestService.spec.mjs` is outside `brain-unit.yml`'s fixed smoke list (#201), so these arms run locally, not in CI.
