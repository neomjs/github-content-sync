Resolves #1949

Enforces `### 📊 Final Evaluation Metrics` (`6` numeric `0–100` scores + `1` categorical `[EFFORT_PROFILE]`: `Quick Win | Heavy Lift | Maintenance | Architectural Pillar`, or `N/A — Cycle 1 was Micro-Review` verified against the cited Round-1 review) in `PullRequestService` (`validatePrReviewBody` and `managePrReview`) in lockstep with `neomjs/neo-agent-skills#480`, and adds `resolveEffectivePrEvaluationMetrics` (`PullRequestService#resolveEffectiveEvaluationMetrics`) so post-fix Round-2 `APPROVED` metrics supersede pre-fix Round-1 `CHANGES_REQUESTED` scores with legacy fallback for historical approvals. Refs `neomjs/neo-agent-skills#480`, `neomjs/neo#22305`.
