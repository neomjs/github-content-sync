Resolves #1935

Four SQL lookups of a message's own `SENT_TO AGENT:*` edge now name `idx_edges_source`. With `source` correlated and `target` constant, the planner took `idx_edges_target` and walked all 27,676 broadcast edges once per row. That walk was both 2026-11-25 `mc-server` stalls: `list_messages` with `directOnly` and an `involves-me` observer.

- `mailboxObservation.mjs`: the `directOnly` broadcast check, and the correlated `legacyBroadcast` check that the `own` and non-team `all` scopes add. Both now read `Edges … INDEXED BY idx_edges_source` through one `ownEdges` helper. The legacy candidate branch keeps its scan of every broadcast by target.
- `MailboxService.mjs`: `getWakeDeliverySeries`' two sums share one pinned `isBroadcast` check (the duplicated literal is gone), and `stampBroadcastCohortEdge` reads its edge back by source.

Rows, counts and pages are unchanged; only the plans move. `INDEXED BY` fails `prepare` loudly if the index is ever renamed, rather than falling back to the walk.

Evidence: L2 (plan assertions on the Brain's own schema, red on the unpatched source) plus an L3 read-only receipt on the live store → L2 required (AC-1–AC-3 are unit). Residual: PMV-1, Residual-Owner: #1843.
