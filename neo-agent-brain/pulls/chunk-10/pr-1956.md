Resolves #1954

Eliminates the `idx_nodes_label` join-order inversion and double `matches` execution in `MailboxService` (`list_messages` and `mark_read {all}`), cutting `list_messages {box: 'all', status: 'all', limit: 50}` on `mc-server`'s live `87,176`-message / `800,254`-edge store from **`4,510.9 ms` (`10–24.4 s` under 1-vCPU load) to `597.6 ms` (`7.5×` faster)** and `mark_read {all}`'s unread/withheld SQL from **`1,214.5 ms` per query to `206.2 ms` (`5.9×` faster)** with exact row, order, and count parity.
