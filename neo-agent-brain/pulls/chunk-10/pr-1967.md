Resolves #1966
Related: neomjs/neo#22400

Neural Link now advertises `drive_gesture` through MCP. It takes an explicit App Worker session, owning window, managed target and provider, with either contact frames or a named preset. The Engine executes the replay and owns the writer grant and cleanup. A required browser provider receives the typed unavailable receipt when a driver is absent.

Evidence: L3 (real public stdio MCP clients, isolated Bridge, exact Engine Helix candidate `036e01626f`, real Chromium and independent App Worker geometry).

## AC Evidence
| AC | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| AC-1 | The real public stdio spec replays eight contact transitions through the mounted Helix. It checks active and changed IDs, `trusted: false`, ordered `[cancel, end]`, actual DOM deliveries, and independently observed rotation and depth. |
| AC-2 | The live spec observes zero contacts and unchanged geometry for the unsupported browser provider, an invalid transition, and a blank session. With two real stdio clients, the contender is denied while a contact is held. Closing the owner emits `touchcancel` and the contender can then replay. A 10.25 s hold completes through the public action. |
| AC-3 | The live spec verifies `tools/list` and `get_mcp_tool_handbook`. The focused Brain units cover the OpenAPI shapes, capability inventory, request forwarding, provider integrity and per-call deadline. |

## Deltas from ticket
A blank session is refused before the ordinary connection resolver can select the sole live App. The action gives the Engine up to 31 seconds of execution budget in addition to the existing transport deadline. Ordinary RPCs retain their configured deadline.

## Contract Ledger
| Surface | Authority | Verified behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `drive_gesture` | Engine ADR 0045 and the public Neural Link capability table | One explicit session/window/target/provider and exactly one input form. The Engine validates contact lifecycles. The public operation is write-locked and hidden from the read-only projection. |
| Successful replay and refusal | Engine gesture receipt and the MCP error formatter | Observed Engine success passes through. For an Engine refusal, MCP exposes a readable error and preserves the original typed receipt. An incompatible provider or trust signal fails closed. |
| Writer ownership | Engine and the Bridge-stamped caller | The owning writer keeps control until its outstanding contact is cancelled. A competing caller cannot dispatch before that boundary. |
| Execution and transport deadlines | Engine replay limit and Brain's existing ConnectionService | The bounded Engine budget applies to this RPC only. A hold longer than the ordinary transport deadline reaches its terminal receipt. |
