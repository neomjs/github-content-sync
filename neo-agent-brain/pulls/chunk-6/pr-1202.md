Resolves #1201

The Fleet's seat lifecycle states are one exported contract: `neo-agent-brain/fleet-contract` now carries `SEAT_LIFECYCLE_STATES` (`running · stopping · stopped · failed · unavailable`), `SEAT_LIFECYCLE_READ_ONLY_STATES` (`exited`) and `SEAT_LIFECYCLE_ROW_FALLBACK` (`unknown`), one line per word saying what it means and which control a cockpit offers. The journal codec and the cockpit row producer read the contract instead of their literals, and a Start whose `spawn` throws now leaves a `failed` record that names why in `failureReason`, as every other failed record does. A client that imports the contract learns a new word at its pin bump, before an install shows it — neomjs/neo-agent-institution#749 is that client. The ticket's Contract Ledger ships as written, all four rows.

Evidence: L2 (unit: the contract snapshot, an acorn scan of every seat-record write in `FleetLifecycleService.mjs`, the codec's exact set, the producer's fallback word, the spawn-failure record) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-4 are source contracts). Residual: AC-5 (the FM's `sourceHealth.spec` imports the contract at the next Brain pin bump), Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#749.
