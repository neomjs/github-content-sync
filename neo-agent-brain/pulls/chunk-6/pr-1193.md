Resolves #1144
Related: #1203

Provisions the Neural Link Bridge's `NEO_FLEET_BRIDGE_PUBLIC_KEY` directly from `FleetRegistryService`'s `signing.key` custody at Fleet boot (`settleFleetBridge` in `devFleetServer.mjs`), while leaving orchestrator task definitions untouched so non-custodian orchestrator roles never mint a competing `signing.key` or override a launcher-supplied `NEO_FLEET_BRIDGE_PUBLIC_KEY`. Classifies older (`1008` on `/?role=verifier`), unkeyed/foreign-keyed/protocol-mismatched, or spawn-failing Bridges on the NL port into one named Fleet boot state (`bridge-outdated`, `bridge-unkeyed`, `bridge-unavailable`) with exact causes and literal next steps covering both supervised (`host-edge` / `launchd`) and Fleet-started topologies. Direct Bridge launch in `settleFleetBridge` ships as a declared interim (routing stdio to `<fleet.dataDir>/logs/neural-link-bridge-stdio.log` and handling ChildProcess `'error'` events as `bridge-unavailable`) until #1203 moves continuous Bridge supervision (mid-session exit and runtime-generation replacement) to the Fleet-supervised `host-edge` role.

Evidence: L3 (real detached `run-bridge.mjs` child spawned from `settleFleetBridge` with `NEO_FLEET_BRIDGE_PUBLIC_KEY` unset in operator env, verified via live `createBridgeTokenOwner.prove` WebSocket handshake and `probeBridgeVerifier` against old/unkeyed WebSocket servers) -> L3 required (AC-1, AC-2). Residual: none.
