Instruments the shadow-only community-steward experiment authorized by `#3` (`#1311`, `reportId: dd78a28718cf0db9a526876c271053c674e713c9dc4b0fe99882f410ee7f2664`, `optionDispositions.J.disposition = 'shadow-only steward experiment authorized; activation thresholds unset until its window'`) via `communityStewardWakeLease.mjs`, `CommunityActivityService.{acquireStewardLease,releaseStewardLease,evaluateStewardWake}`, and `ai/mcp/server/memory-core/configBase.mjs` (`communityStewardWake.*` leaves). `resolveValidatedStewardWakePolicy` reads `memoryCoreConfig.communityStewardWake` directly at the use site (`ADR-0019 §5.1, B5`) with no caller override parameters.
