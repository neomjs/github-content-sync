Resolves #1255

Replaces the default `onStuck = () => process.exit(1)` in `startWakeReceiver` (`ai/daemons/wake/receiver.mjs`) with `() => process.kill(process.pid, 'SIGKILL')` and forwards `stepBoundsMs` and `watchdogIntervalMs` to `createWakeReceiver` so a stuck `libuv` threadpool step (`reload`, `accept`, `drain`) terminates immediately after `liveness.stuck()`'s synchronous `writeFileAtomicSync` flush instead of deadlocking inside `uv__threadpool_cleanup` -> `uv_thread_join`.

Prescription checked: `ai/daemons/wake/receiver.mjs` — owns the concern.

Evidence: L1 (unit spec in `test/playwright/unit/ai/daemons/wake/receiver.spec.mjs` exercising `startWakeReceiver`'s watchdog-to-`liveness.stuck`-to-`SIGKILL` sequence) → L1 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-3). Residual: none.

Micro-review eligible: single-behavior restoration — 12-line change in `ai/daemons/wake/receiver.mjs` plus one unit test in `test/playwright/unit/ai/daemons/wake/receiver.spec.mjs`.
