## Summary

Closes the three trust-classification blind spots surfaced during the first `/peer-role` cycle on `neomjs/neo` Discussion `#20111` (`DC_kwDODSospM4BJLuI`) and promoted from `@neo-fable-clio`'s defect note (`MESSAGE:8d7e2228-41e9-44a3-a912-966d365f5b20`), serving as the trust-tiers leaf of Epic `#875` (*Runtime identity facts come from the plane's graph, not identityRoots*):

1. **`MemoryService.resolveMemoryTrustTier` (`ai/services/memory-core/MemoryService.mjs:406-508`):**
   - Queries the live `GraphService` `AgentIdentity` node first (resolving `formerLogins` aliases via `resolveAgentIdentityAlias`), so runtime/auto-provisioned seats such as `@neo-gemini-maryam` resolve their graph-stamped `properties.trustTier` (`'internal-authored'`) instead of `'unclassified'` while keeping `@neo-gemini-maryam` absent from `ai/graph/identityRoots.mjs` (`#700#issuecomment-6240532059`).
   - Indexes both `identity.id` and normalized `identity.properties.formerLogins` (`@neo-gemini-pro`, `@neo-gemini-3-1-pro`, `@neo-fable`, `@neo-gpt`, `@neo-opus-4-7`, `@neo-claude-opus`) in `MemoryService.identityTrustTiers` so historical memories written before an identity rename resolve to `'peer-trusted'`.
2. **`classifyAuthorTrust` (`ai/services/shared/contentTrust/authorTrustClassifier.mjs:40-115`):**
   - Indexes `identity.properties.formerLogins` alongside `identity.properties.githubLogin` in `KNOWN_TIER_BY_LOGIN`.
   - Accepts optional `authorAssociation` and classifies `OWNER`, `MEMBER`, and `COLLABORATOR` as `TRUST_TIERS.REPO_TRUSTED` when the login is not already in `KNOWN_TIER_BY_LOGIN`, while keeping `CONTRIBUTOR`, `FIRST_TIME_CONTRIBUTOR`, and `NONE` fail-closed at `TRUST_TIERS.EXTERNAL`.
3. **`conversationTrust.mjs` & GitHub GraphQL Conversation/Sync Queries (`discussionQueries.mjs`, `issueQueries.mjs`, `pullRequestQueries.mjs`):**
   - Selects `authorAssociation` alongside `author { login }` across all 12 discussion, issue, and PR conversation/sync GraphQL queries and forwards `authorAssociation: node.authorAssociation` in `projectNode` (`conversationTrust.mjs:47-50`), preventing non-rostered org members (`@neo-gemini-maryam`) from being classified as `external` or having first-party `github.com` URLs rewritten to `[QUARANTINED_URL: github.com]`.
