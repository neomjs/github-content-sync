Resolves #1257

Extends `wakeSubscriptionStatusPolicy`, `readActiveWakeSubscriptionIdentities`, and `WakeSubscriptionService` so withdrawn (`status = 'degraded'`) wake routes are recognized by route-key lookup, reactivated in place on `subscribe()` and `bootstrap()` while preserving their server-issued `signingKey` and clearing `WebhookDeliveryService` degraded state, and included in `_reconcileDuplicateSubscriptions` (`active` preferred over `degraded`, newest kept within each canonical route group).

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit tests against SQLite + in-memory GraphService and WebhookDeliveryService) → L2 required (AC-1 through AC-4). Residual: none, Residual-Owner: none.

| AC-2 | CI-covered: `test/playwright/unit/ai/services/memory-core/WakeSubscriptionService.spec.mjs` (`reconciler retires degraded duplicates alongside active routes within the same canonical route key (#1257)`) |
