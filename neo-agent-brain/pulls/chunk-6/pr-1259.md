Resolves #1257

Extends `wakeSubscriptionStatusPolicy`, `readActiveWakeSubscriptionIdentities`, and `WakeSubscriptionService` so withdrawn (`status = 'degraded'`) wake routes are recognized by route-key lookup, reactivated in place on `subscribe()` and `bootstrap()` while preserving their server-issued `signingKey` and clearing `WebhookDeliveryService` degraded state, and included in `_reconcileDuplicateSubscriptions` (`active` preferred over `degraded`, newest kept within each canonical route group).

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit tests against SQLite + in-memory GraphService and WebhookDeliveryService) → L2 required (AC-1 through AC-4). Residual: none, Residual-Owner: none.

## AC Evidence
| AC-1 | CI-covered: `test/playwright/unit/ai/services/memory-core/WakeSubscriptionService.spec.mjs` (`bootstrap reuses and reactivates an existing degraded route in place while preserving signingKey (#1257)`, `subscribe reuses and reactivates a degraded route in place without rotating its signingKey (#1257)`) |
| AC-2 | CI-covered: `test/playwright/unit/ai/services/memory-core/WakeSubscriptionService.spec.mjs` (`reconciler retires degraded duplicates alongside active routes within the same canonical route key (#1257)`) |

## Deltas from ticket
- Also runs `_reconcileDuplicateSubscriptions(owner)` inside `subscribe()` (matching `bootstrap()`) and clears `WebhookDeliveryService.clearDegraded(subscriptionId)` inside `_retireSubscription()` so retired duplicate IDs do not linger in `WebhookDeliveryService` sets.
- Preserves keyless legacy rows as keyless on reuse rather than minting a `signingKey` inside `_refreshExistingSubscriptionRoute()`, because `subscribe()` / `bootstrap()` return `{status: 'existing'}` without returning a newly minted key (per `rotateKey()`'s contract forbidding silent key rotation on existing-row reuse).
