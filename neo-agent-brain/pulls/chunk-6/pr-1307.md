## Deltas from ticket
Resolved the `2026-09-20` intake evidence directly inside `CommunityActivityService.claimTask` and `MailboxService.transitionTask`: rather than calling `MailboxService.addMessage` (which writes an asynchronous JSONL WAL record and stamps `sharedEntity: true`), `claimTask` executes one `IMMEDIATE` SQLite transaction across `mc_community_task_binding`, `Nodes`, `Edges`, and `GraphLog` with `sharedEntity: false, userId: normalizeUserId(tenantId), tenantId`, and `MailboxService.transitionTask` enforces tenant-private visibility (`storedState.sharedEntity === false || storedState.tenantId != null`) before exposing or mutating the cached `MESSAGE` node. Added `CommunityActivityService` to `GRAPH_NODE_WRITER_INVENTORY` in `GraphService.mjs`.
