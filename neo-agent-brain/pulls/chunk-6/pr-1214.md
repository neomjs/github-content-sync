Resolves #1081

Give Fleet quit request drain (`devFleetServer.mjs` `cleanShutdown`) and continuity handoff (`McpLaunchAdmissionService#performHandoff`) one shared shutdown budget (`AiConfig.fleet.shutdownBudgetMs`, default `10000` ms, `NEO_FLEET_SHUTDOWN_BUDGET_MS`), while keeping `proofTimeoutMs` (`10000` ms) for individual credential proofs and logging `(budget <budgetMs> ms)` beside elapsed handoff seal and refusal times.

Evidence: L1 (Playwright unit tests in openWorkBoot.spec.mjs and McpLaunchAdmissionService.spec.mjs) → L4 required (AC-3, AC-4 live Desktop quit with 9 running seats). Residual: AC-3, AC-4, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#732.

## AC Evidence
| AC-1 | `test/playwright/unit/ai/services/fleet/openWorkBoot.spec.mjs` (`under a 300 ms shutdownBudgetMs with a drain + handoff that would take longer, cleanShutdown logs Fleet continuity refused: deadline and exits (#1081)`) |
| AC-2 | `test/playwright/unit/ai/services/fleet/McpLaunchAdmissionService.spec.mjs` (`performHandoff race deadline follows deadlineMs while credential proofs retain proofTimeoutMs (#1081)`) |
| AC-3 | Residual — Institution sibling PR [`neomjs/neo-agent-institution#757`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-institution/pull/757) (Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#756) |
| AC-4 | Residual — Post-merge live 9-seat quit validation (Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#732) |

## Deltas from ticket
- Instead of `McpLaunchAdmissionService#performHandoff` importing `AiConfig` and starting a second independent timer for `AiConfig.fleet.shutdownBudgetMs` alongside `cleanShutdown`, `cleanShutdown` reads `AiConfig.fleet.shutdownBudgetMs` once at quit start (`fallback: proofTimeoutMs` when non-positive), drains requests under `Math.min(proofTimeoutMs, remaining())`, and passes the remaining operational scalar `deadlineMs: remaining()` through `handoffRuntimeCustody` → `handoff` → `performHandoff` (ADR-0019 §5.4 rung (iii)). `performHandoff`'s single stage-naming deadline bounds the handoff and preserves the stage on timeout (`refused at <stage>: deadline` / `Fleet continuity refused: deadline at <stage>.`). Proposed Contract Ledger and `AC-2` restatement to `@neo-fable-clio` on [`#1081#issuecomment-6275599446`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-brain/issues/1081#issuecomment-6275599446).
