Resolves #1250

A `wakeSuppressed: true` direct message to a peer is now refused at acceptance with the rule in the error, whatever its subject, tag, priority or task: a peer address always wakes (the operator, 2026-10-24), because an idled-out seat reads nothing until its wake. The guard keys on the address and the principal classes. A `human` or `system` sender, a `human` or `system` recipient, and a seat's message to itself keep the pre-rule arms (the tag allow-list, `[alert]` subjects, then the high-priority / task / actionable-subject refusals), so the session-sunset self-DM, operator steering, the KB alerting daemon's audit records under `@system` and `requestPublish` custody to the operator are unchanged; `AGENT:*` is untouched. The lead-role baton's tag exemption is retired — the new lead is exactly a seat that may have idled out — with the decision in the guard's JSDoc and mirrored on [neo-agent-skills#205](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-skills/issues/205#issuecomment-6418592971). The `wakeSuppressed` description in `openapi.yaml` and the discipline paragraph in `learn/agentos/A2A.md` state the rule instead of recommending quiet acks.

Evidence: L2 (unit tier: the acceptance seam exercised through real `addMessage` calls under seeded principal classes) → L2 required (every AC is an acceptance-time behaviour). Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket
- **The recipient's principal class is part of the rule.** A quiet message to the human operator or to a system identity is accepted: the operator has no wake route an idled seat depends on, and `requestPublish` (#1263, merged after the ticket was written) addresses the operator quietly by design — without this arm the rule would refuse publication custody. The recipient class is read the same way as the sender's, so `resolveSenderPrincipalClass` became `resolvePrincipalClass` (it already served the reviewer and self lookups).
- **A `system` sender is exempt by class** (the ticket's ledger row): the KB alerting daemon's `deliveryMode: 'audit'` to one peer stays accepted under its default `@system` sender. Under a seat identity (`NEO_AGENT_IDENTITY`) such an audit DM is refused and the daemon logs it — noted, not changed here.
- The `requestPublish` tags stay in the allow-list (#1263's surface); the recipient arm already covers them, so their removal belongs to that lane.
