Resolves #1221

The FM's awaiting-merge list now shows an approved PR from a repository that runs no CI. `holderOf()` gave the operator's merge chip only to a green head, so neo-stage (hosted CI paused) and pages (no workflows), whose heads report no check rollup, fell through to `none`. Every green, red and pending path keeps its holder.

- **Challenge**: While `fleetOpenWorkSource.mjs:94` populates `seat(id).reviewing` directly from `row.requested` regardless of `ci`, `holderOf()`'s `rebase` (`:50`), `review` (`:54`), and `route-review` (`:58`) rules remain gated on `ci === 'green'`.
