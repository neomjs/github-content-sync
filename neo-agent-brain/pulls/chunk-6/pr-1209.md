Resolves #1208

A Fleet that must start its Neural Link Bridge boots again. The started Bridge's stdio log now resolves through `registry.getDataDir()`, the registry's own resolver (the override, else `AiConfig.fleet.dataDir`), instead of `registry.dataDir`, the test override production never sets. The resolution moved inside the start's `try`, so a log that cannot be placed reports `bridge-unavailable` (`FLEET_DATA_DIR_MISSING`) and Fleet boot continues.

Before this fix, at `dev` `883278aa`, every Fleet boot with nothing answering on its NL port exited 1. That includes every fresh install's FM, and any FM start while the host-edge Bridge is down.

Evidence: L2 (unit: `settleFleetBridge` with a production-shaped registry and the singleton's override cleared) → L3 required (AC-3, a packaged rehearsal on a candidate carrying the fix). Residual: AC-3, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#752.
