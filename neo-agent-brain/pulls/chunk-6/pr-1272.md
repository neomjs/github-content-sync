Resolves #1252

Adds `projection: 'compact' | 'full'` (default `'compact'`), `neighborLimit` (default `12`), and `types` to `get_context_frontier` (`MemoryService.getContextFrontier`, `GraphService.getContextFrontier`, and `ai/mcp/server/memory-core/openapi.yaml`). In `'compact'` mode, `get_context_frontier` returns `totalNeighbors`, `neighborCounts` by node label, `topology` (`frontier: {id, type, name}` and the top `neighborLimit` `strategicNeighbors` by weight without `description`), and the top `neighborLimit` `semanticContexts` trimmed to `{nodeId, name, weightedScore, trustTier}` plus the first 300 characters of `content` (`< 6,000` characters on a 446-neighbor fixture). `'full'` preserves today's complete topology and untruncated `semanticContexts`, and `types` filters neighbors by node label in either projection.

Consumer census across `neo-agent-brain` and skills: internal callers `GoldenPathSynthesizer.mjs:1579` (`{depth: 1}`) and `StorageRouter.mjs:179` (`()`) call `GraphService.getContextFrontier` directly (where the no-argument / `depth`-only path is unchanged), and the two skill mentions (`epic-review-workflow.md`, `memory-mining-protocol.md`) read `get_context_frontier` bare without reading `description` or `metadata`.

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit suite + OpenAPI service-parity lint) → L4 required (AC-5 live post-merge bare `get_context_frontier` seat recovery receipt). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: #1253.
