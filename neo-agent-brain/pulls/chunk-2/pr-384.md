Resolves #372

Tri-Vector told the model to use closed vocabularies while sending unrestricted strings to its structured-output decoder. Its TEST_GAP filter then treated the model's self-reported confidence as admission evidence. The current shared gap cohort would pass that score gate in 99.23% of cases.

The prompt, schema and writer now share the existing node/relationship vocabularies and closed stability/logical-layer sets. The writer retains explicit catch-all behavior for legacy input. TEST_GAP admission keeps its existing structural-node and precise test-file rules and no longer reads self-confidence. The extractor stops requesting, reducing or newly writing that field; historical stored scores remain untouched.

Evidence: L1 (real extractor/provider-schema capture, Ajv validation and isolated graph-store tests) → L3 required for AC-4 (deployed REM graph readback). Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: #253.

Change class: restoration → fix. Aligned with ADRs 0023 and 0024: no routing boost, new symbol index or expansion of the fourteen extracted node types.
