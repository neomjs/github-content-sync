Resolves #448
🌿 *A failed projection cycle can no longer reach the orchestrator log as nothing but an exit code.*

Both children keep their `mc-server-<date>.log` line and now also write one stderr line: the label, `error.code`, the message, and git's stderr when a GitMirror error carries it (already redacted). That is the channel `ProcessSupervisorService#writeChildStderr` re-logs into the orchestrator log, at ERROR for an unprefixed line. `projectCoreCorpus` handles its failure inside `runProjectCoreCorpus` through the existing `output`/`exit` seams. `aggregate-temporal-summary` keeps its direct `main()` and exports `reportAggregationFailure(error, {output, exit})`, which its entry guard calls. On the local plane the 22-hour projection failure would have read `… KB_GITMIRROR_FILE_READ_FAILED — GitMirror failed to read a revision file — git: fatal: path '_index.json' does not exist in '18b473b6e8'` instead of `exited with code 1`.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms through the entry seams; no plane) → no deployed AC. The supervisor half (stderr → orchestrator log) is covered by its own specs. Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket
The temporal-summary shape changed during implementation, and #448's Fix and Avoided Traps say so in place. I first injected its service as a seam, like `runProjectCoreCorpus`. That hid the `runCycle` chain from `lint-script-plane`'s static walk, and the lint then reported the entry's known authority conflict (`…::temporal-summary::authority-conflict-in-plane`) as stale although it is unchanged at runtime. So only the failure report is extracted.
