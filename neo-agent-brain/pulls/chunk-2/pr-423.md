Resolves #282

Shared core `kbSync` now extracts the installed Engine and Brain from separate repository-bound profiles. Each reader has its own revision and hierarchy identity; both profiles compile before publication, then produce two hash-checked JSONL artifacts behind one manifest. Embedding uses separate `(tenantId, repoSlug)` calls with `deleteStale: false`, preserving legacy rows for their owned retirement tickets. The mutable `SourceRegistry` remains for its other legacy consumers.

Evidence: L3 (real installed Engine pin plus clean Brain checkout extraction into revision-stamped artifacts; local unit and MCP/vector controls) → L3 required (AC-1 through AC-7). No live KB re-embed or activation is claimed; the later row-retirement transactions are owned by `#417` and `#419`.

Micro-review eligible: no — the shared Knowledge Base writer and its repository-ownership contract change.
