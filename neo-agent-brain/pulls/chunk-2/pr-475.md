Resolves #472
🌿 *The ADRs can no longer tell a fresh agent that concepts belong in a repository.*

neomjs/neo#19096 graduated option E′ into #471. This PR records that decision in the ADRs and the ontology guide:
- **The source.** Each plane's store is the one concept source, and the explanation is the node's `description`.
- **Admission.** Projections admit rows whose `validated` is not `false`, so a seedless plane has no KB concepts until one is validated.
- **ADR 0023 §2.5** replaces its content-SSOT target.
- **ADR 0024** §2.6 and §2.7 describe the store and an optional seed, and §6 drops the open content-as-SSOT item.
- **`ConceptOntology.md`** stops describing a version-controlled, git-reviewed ontology.
