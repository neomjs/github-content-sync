## Deltas from ticket

The accepted [peer fold](https://github.com/neomjs/neo-agent-brain/issues/401#issuecomment-5759891819) governs the cut: fixed Graph origin, private staging, Compose election and independent KB ingestion. No configurable origin switch or shared KB mount is added.

Index validation additionally rejects non-array/invalid JSON and selected paths that cross the origin/facet boundary or traverse directories. The validated head index is reused for materialization; its selected rows are also the comparison input for facet invalidation.

The owning spec is added to the executing `brain-unit.yml` smoke selection. The workflow's collection step alone does not execute it; full retained-suite selection remains #201.
