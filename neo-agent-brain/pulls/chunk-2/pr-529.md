Resolves #528

The OpenCode wake plant now stamps the seat identity the `opencode-server` reader requires. Until now it rewrote the seat envelope without `agentIdentity` on every `session.created` and every restored session's first update. `readOpenCodeEnvelope` refuses such an envelope ("opencode-server envelope requires 'agentIdentity'"). That is the refusal behind 307 of 348 dispatches to @neo-kimi-phoebe's route (#513), and the expiry of any hand-healed OpenCode envelope.

- **The plant stamps the identity.**
  - It stamps the identity in the `@handle` spelling (the boot hook's rule), taking `NEO_AGENT_IDENTITY` from the seat's launch env first.
  - Else it carries over the identity of the envelope it replaces, which covers a Dock launch that bypasses the seat wrapper.
  - With neither, it writes nothing and logs why, leaving the existing envelope in place. An envelope the reader must refuse is worse than none.
  - Identity is the one field it reads from disk: it is per-seat and never changes. Coordinates are still never adopted.
  - The env read is a module-level helper, the Kimi hook's `readAgentIdentity` shape. The plant runs inside OpenCode, where no AiConfig exists to read the `stopHook.projection.agentId` leaf from (ADR 0019 read).
- **The contract is declared once.** `localWakeAdapters.mjs` exports `OPENCODE_SEAT_ENVELOPE_FIELDS`, and `readOpenCodeEnvelope` validates against it. The plant runs outside the repo and cannot import it, so a spec binds them.
- **Both producers are pinned to the real reader.** `opencodeSeatEnvelopeParity.spec.mjs` runs the plant's real `writeEnvelope` and the boot-hook script the generator emits through the real adapter (`dispatchLocalWake`: the reader plus `assertOpenCodeEnvelopeOwner`). The daemon's "two producers, one contract" JSDoc names both.
- **No owner epoch.** This envelope carries no `pid`/`pidStartedAt`; those legs belong to the `kimi-pull-bridge` envelope `consumeWakeOutbox` reads. The JSDoc says so. That corrects #503's reframed AC-1 and makes its "which process is `pid`" question inapplicable here.
- **CI.** The plant's spec declares its seat identity. The three wake specs join `brain-unit.yml`'s run list; none of them ran in CI before.

Evidence: L2 (both producers' real output through the real adapter; CI-like env) → L3 required for AC-4 (a live OpenCode seat's dispatch recorded `delivered` after a plant rewrite). Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: #503.
