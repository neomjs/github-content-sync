Resolves #694

`learn/agentos/OwnAgentTeam.md` now says where a teammate's identity comes from — the credential the seat presents (`auth.mode`: `oidc` · `gitlab-pat` · `github-pat` · `local-bearer` · `seat-token`, validated by `AuthService.mjs`), with the `AgentIdentity` node created on first contact under the resolved provisioning policy (`auth.autoProvisionIdentitySources`: the PAT modes by default, an override widens or empties it), looked up — never created — under `oidc` / `seat-token`, no teammate at all under possession-only `local-bearer`, and the write guard reading no roster (#665) — and demotes `ai/graph/identityRoots.mjs` to what it is: the Neo team's optional roster, which other teams do not have and do not need. *(Fact corrected after review RA-1 — the first body said "provisioned at the first authenticated request" for every mode.)* *Define Identity Roots* became *Where Identity Comes From* with *The optional roster* under it; *Seed The Graph* is marked roster-teams-only; *Verify A Teammate* checks the credential path and names the roster as never a blocker; *Bring Up The Team* starts with the seat's own credential and carries the roster as one optional step; *Source Anchors* gains `configBase.mjs` / `AuthService.mjs` / `assertExpectedIdentity.mjs`. The operator's rulings of 2026-08-21 and 2026-10-01, written where an outside team reads them.
