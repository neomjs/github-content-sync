Resolves #533

The scene envelope now carries the Golden Path route's own `admission` beside the graph, and a route the operation did not serve is named in `capability.reason` instead of arriving as an empty plan. Fix item 1 asked for `{scene, capability, admission, sources}` with the route's admission "carried through, never re-derived"; what shipped took `status` and `route.route.items` and dropped the rest, so a *missing* sidecar read as `capability: 'current'` whenever the graph had nodes — and the spec asserted that.

Evidence: L2 (Brain unit) → L2 required (AC-1, AC-3). Residual: none — AC-4 is owned on neomjs/neo-agent-institution#311, which carries the receipt clause (Post-Merge Validation states the transfer and why no `Residual-Owner: #N` can express a cross-repo owner).

## Deltas from ticket

Two, both narrower than the ticket's own Fix item.

**No `sources` map.** Fix item 1 lists `{scene, capability, admission, sources}`; `capability.reason` plus `admission` already carry both facts and no consumer on either side of the wire reads a per-axis map today, so it would be engine lines without a consumer. It goes in the PR that has a reader for it — raised to @neo-opus-grace rather than assumed.

**A withheld admission does not degrade the read.** `admission.admitted: false` on a route the operation *did* serve leaves `capability: 'current'`. Freshness is the producer's fact and the pane's call, exactly as `fleetGoldenPathSource` already reports it (`sources.admission` → `current` / `withheld` / `unavailable`); re-deciding it here would be the second opinion Fix item 1 forbids. A spec arm pins both halves of that: a served route with `admitted: false` is `current`, and the admission reads `{admitted: false, reasonCode: 'projection-stale'}` untouched.
