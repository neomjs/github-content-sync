Resolves #779

`holderOf(row)` (new: `ai/services/fleet/openWorkHolder.mjs`) answers who holds an open PR's next action: `rotation`, `author`, `reviewer`, `operator` or `none`. The first match wins, and a review counts only when it judged the current head.

A partial read (a review or request list truncated or unresolved) decides only on positive evidence. A red head, or a change request on the head, belongs to the author; anything else is `unknown`.

Approvals and change requests come from each reviewer's standing opinion. The snapshot now also reads `latestOpinionatedReviews`, carried as `row.opinions`. A comment on the same head no longer hides an approval or a change request; the latest reviews remain the rotation rule's engagement signal.

`projectOpenWork` attaches the holder to every summary. It also adds `awaitingMerge`: every operator-held row, whoever owns it. The seat-keyed lists drop outside and unowned PRs, so neomjs/neo-agent-institution#449's awaiting-merge chip reads this list rather than filtering seats. #761 imports the same function for wake eligibility.

Evidence: L2 (unit tests over the pure function and the projection, red-first and a mutation control) → L2 required. Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket

- **Head-scoped reviews.** Two table rows read reviews of the head (`onHead`), not `verdict`; this is recorded under the ticket's "Deltas after filing".
- **Partial reads (`76944a7`, from @neo-gpt-sophie's pre-review finding).** As first pushed, `holderOf` ignored the producer's `row.partial`. A truncated `latestReviews` (`first: 20`) could therefore put an approval in `awaitingMerge` past an unseen change request, or hand an outside PR to the rotation past an unseen review. A new `unknown` role covers every rule that reads an absence; recorded on #779.
- **Standing opinions (`b133b47`, from @neo-gpt-sophie's review packet).** This reaches the producer, outside the ticket's first write surface. The snapshot query adds `latestOpinionatedReviews(first: 20)`, and `normalizePullRequest` adds `row.opinions` with its own completeness. Live on neo#19234 and neo#19324, the same reviewer and head read `COMMENTED` in `latestReviews` and `APPROVED` in `latestOpinionatedReviews`. A `dryRun` puts the snapshot query's cost at 2 both with and without the new connection. The producer, reducer and lane specs' stubs carry the new connection.
- **Measured why.** In a sample of 100 approved PRs merged in the org since 2026-09-25, Brain #765 merged with `reviewDecision: APPROVED` while its only approval judged an earlier head. `verdict` would have queued it for the operator.
