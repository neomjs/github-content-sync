Resolves #634

The fleet activity read can now page the mailbox. `readActivitySnapshot` normalizes a page `offset` at the wire boundary, beside `limit`, and the A2A slot passes it to `listMessages` as the query's `offset`. That works for both bindings, the in-process `MailboxService` and the plane client's `list_messages`, because both take the args unchanged. A caller can also name the lanes it reads (`slots`), so a history page of the mailbox asks the A2A lane alone and its capability speaks for that lane. With neither param the read is unchanged. Source: 3 files, +46/−6.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms on the composer and the wiring, plus mutation reds) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-4 are contract facts at the composer and the slot binding). Residual: none. The cockpit's paging is the consumer's own AC, owned by neomjs/neo-agent-institution#349.
