Resolves #766

A seat's hooks now reach the plane as the seat.

- **The new leaves** are `AiConfig.seat.planeBase` (`NEO_SEAT_PLANE_BASE`) and `AiConfig.seat.planeBearer` (`NEO_MCP_REMOTE_TOKEN`).
- **One reader:** the hooks read them through a single runtime-resident module, `ai/scripts/lifecycle/hooks/seatConfig.mjs`. It replaces three byte-identical `readPlaneConfig` copies (Claude and Kimi turn presence, Codex `codex-context`) and `wakeArmingHook.readSeatConfig`.
- **The injection:** `start` injects the seat's placed endpoint as a new reserved slot, beside the plane credential it already injected. A Fleet-launched seat therefore carries both. The Fleet passes the endpoint as `startAgentProvisioned`'s `remotePlan.endpoint`, for tenant and plane alike.
- **`fleet.planeBase` / `fleet.planeBearer`** stay the transport's alone.

Evidence: L2 (unit specs drive child-process reads of the real config, the spawn env of the start path, and the projected hook bytes; an agent cannot launch a seat through the installed Fleet) → L4 required for #766's PMV-1, a Fleet-launched Claude seat that arms at SessionStart.
