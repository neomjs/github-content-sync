Resolves #713

The two Gemini model leaves gain env bindings: `NEO_GEMINI_MODEL` and `NEO_GEMINI_EMBEDDING_MODEL`. The defaults are unchanged. This means a deployment, and the hosted preset (#686), can name another Gemini chat or embedding model the same way it does for the other providers.

- **`ai/configBase.mjs`:** `modelName: leaf('gemini-3.5-flash', 'NEO_GEMINI_MODEL', 'string')` and `embeddingModel: leaf('gemini-embedding-001', 'NEO_GEMINI_EMBEDDING_MODEL', 'string')`. The names follow `NEO_<PROVIDER>_MODEL` and `NEO_<PROVIDER>_EMBEDDING_MODEL`, like the Ollama and OpenAI-compatible leaves.
- **The binding takes effect.** The readers already use the leaf at the use site:
  - `MemoryService` (`:1942`) and `SessionService` (`:193`) hand `aiConfig.modelName` to the Gemini provider at creation;
  - `TextEmbeddingService` and `embeddingProviders.mjs` read `aiConfig.embeddingModel`.
- **Compose** passes both names through wherever the sibling provider model names pass: three services in `docker-compose.yml`, and the dev profile's provider block.
- **The census** in `config-leaf-parity.json` classifies both as optional overrides, beside their provider siblings, and moves from 74 keys to 76.
- **`ModelProviders.md`** names the two variables and their defaults.

ADR 0019 was read in full before this touch, as AC-2 asks. The change follows §2.1 and §5.2: the leaf owns env-override-with-default. There is no `process.env` read in source, no export, and no alias.

Evidence: L2 (unit, red-first, and the SSOT guards). Residual: none. #713's former AC-3 is now #686's AC-4 (below).
