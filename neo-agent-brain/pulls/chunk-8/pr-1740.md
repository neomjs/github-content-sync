Resolves #1739

A sunset receipt now carries what its rotation needs:
- **Amendments fold in, and an open rotation completes first.** A handover sent while a rotation is open, and before its first successor marker, folds into that receipt. An open rotation is decided before any later receipt.
- **The writer comes from the receipt.** The `add_message` tool hands `addMessage` the request's Memory Core session as a server-owned option, and a sunset handover to self keeps it as `originSessionId`. A writer whose session changed mid-turn closes its own receipt.
- **The count comes from the receipt.** The read takes the summary lane's stamp, else the subject's `focus items: N, N placed`, else `'missing'`. Heavy maintenance no longer holds a sunset.
- **After a successor's marker, a stamp never releases a live turn (Rounds 1–2).**
  - A handover sent after a rotation's successor marker is attributed by the seat's newest saver, as before this PR. Its stamp is recorded but cannot release a live turn.
  - With no attributed close, it stays pending (`writer-close-pending`, logged), with nothing sent, until a close lands or its window passes.
  - No marker age decides it.

Evidence: L2 (rotation, mailbox and tool-boundary arms, each run against the source before its fix and at the head; the arms that check a fix fail before it, the controls pass on both) → L4 required (AC-11: a live Claude sunset of one of the three shapes, which needs a plane cut). Residual: AC-11, Residual-Owner: #1373.

## Deltas from ticket
- **The stamp enters at the tool boundary, not in the service.**
  - `SessionService` statically reaches `StorageRouter` and the chat-model provider, so importing it into `MailboxService` would widen a module graph the host-barrel spec pins.
  - `addMessageTool` passes `SessionService.currentSessionId` as a second-argument option, the way `listMessagesTool` passes `recordSeen`.
- **The fold keys on send time and the first successor marker (Round 1).** A handover sent after the marker is not an amendment: it waits while the open rotation completes, then gets its own disposition.
- **No window decides a post-successor receipt (Round 2).** The read returns `writerClosedAt`, and `decideRotation` keeps such a receipt pending until it is set. No config input is added.
- **#1401's absent-count wait is retired.**
  - Its arm is rewritten as AC-7, and `decideRotation` loses `receipt-count-pending`.
  - `parseFocusItemsReceipt` moves into the pure `sunsetReceipt.mjs`, and the summary lane imports it from there.
- **Known limits, recorded in #1739's Out of Scope (D#19401).**
  - A reconnect inside a successor's sunset turn leaves the receipt pending until its window passes.
  - A late writer that shares the successor's Memory Core id cannot be told from the successor.
