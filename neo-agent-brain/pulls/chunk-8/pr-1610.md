Resolves #1609

Every open-work message now asks to wake the seat it names. Until now, `repair-ci` on a red head, `route-review` on a green one and the other kinds arrived with the wake off. The plane stamps the viewer as their sender, and for a human sender an omitted `wakeSuppressed` defaults to quiet. A seat waiting on CI therefore learned the result only at its next turn, and the harness's PR monitor wakes only on failures. `wakeMessageOf` now sets `wakeSuppressed: false`. The mailbox guard allows a sender to elect a wake, and the sender question stays with #1607.

Evidence: L2 (a unit spec drives a real round through a red head and then a green one, and reads what it sends) → L2 required (AC-1). Residual: AC-2, Residual-Owner: #759.
