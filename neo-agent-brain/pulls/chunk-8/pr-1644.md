Resolves #1619

A merge now wakes the seats whose tickets it unblocked. For each merge it has just observed, the open-work producer reads the issues the merge closes, the open issues those block, and each one's assignees and remaining blockers. An issue left with no open blocker becomes one `unblocked` wake per assignee seat. The wake goes out as `@system` through the plane intake, with the issue as its `unblocks` token. The author's `post-merge` wake is unchanged.

Evidence: L2 (unit specs over the read's resolution, the producer pulse, the planner, the host round and the intake) → L3 required (AC-3, a real merge after the plane cut and the FM update). Residual: AC-3, Residual-Owner: #759.
