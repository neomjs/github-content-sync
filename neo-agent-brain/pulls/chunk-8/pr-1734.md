Resolves #1732

🌿 *Being older than the WAL no longer locks a message away from the seat that sent it.*

`rebuildMessageEdges.mjs` now links `SENT_BY` and `SENT_TO` from a `MESSAGE` node's own `from` / `to` where the graph lacks them:
- the identity is normalized as the projection normalizes it (`normalizeMailboxIdentityForComparison`);
- a route is linked only from a node labelled `MESSAGE`, from a single string, to a target of a kind the projection routes to (senders `AgentIdentity` or `System`, destinations `AgentIdentity` or `BroadcastSentinel`); anything else counts as `invalid`;
- one route per message and type, which the insert itself enforces, so a route another writer adds meanwhile stays the only one (counted `present`);
- a broadcast route carries `broadcastCohort: 'legacy-unknown'`, the projection's stamp without a recipient snapshot;
- `DELIVERED_TO` is not rebuilt, because the snapshot is not on the node.

The default `--types` grows to six.

This is not one-shot logic (`pull-request` §6.2). The script is the standing repair that rebuilds edges from node fields, and a carrier older than the WAL has no other repair, today or after a later sever.

Evidence: L2 (unit: the rebuild against a fixture graph, and admission through `canReadMessage` over the rebuilt edges; the plane's graph is out of a PR's reach) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-4). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: #1733.
