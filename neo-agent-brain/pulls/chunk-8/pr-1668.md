Resolves #1667

A seat Start no longer waits forever for the runtime-reference lease. `waitForRuntimeGenerationReferences` takes an opt-in `deadlineMs` and then throws `RUNTIME_REFERENCES_HELD`, naming the holder's owner, pid, start and last progress, never its token. `FleetControlBridge.startOutcome` answers that refusal on the wire as a domain rejection with its code; unnamed errors stay sanitized. The Start passes 20 s as a bound on the lease wait itself, longer than a healthy retirement pass. The wait begins after repository preparation and dependency installation, so the cockpit's 30 s round trip shows the refusal when those finished within about 10 s. Every other waiter keeps waiting until release: boot recovery here, plus the installer and host acquisition in Institution.

Evidence: L2 (unit specs with a real file lease held by a live pid, a Start through `startAgentProvisioned`, and a Start through `dispatchFleetRequest` → `FleetControlBridge`) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-3). The cockpit check is under Post-Merge Validation.
