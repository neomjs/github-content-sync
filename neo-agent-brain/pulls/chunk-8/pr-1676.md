Resolves #762

A seat's wake digest now names its own open work: its open pull requests (CI, verdict, who holds the next action) and the reviews requested of it.

- **Publication.** After each pulse the host publishes the producer's projection to the plane through `publish_open_work_copy`. The verb is admitted from the Fleet's human operator only, through the gate the open-work wake intake already uses. Entries are keyed by the verified publisher and replaced whole per publisher.
- **Rendering.** `pollDigest` renders the recipient's entry after the events. A stale, unavailable or untracked copy is named as such, never as no work. Open work is state, so it never makes a poll pending; the push path's digests are unchanged.
- **Direct read.** A seat can also read its own entry, as at session start, with `read_own_open_work`.

Evidence: L1 (unit) → L1 required (#762's four ACs are unit). Residual: none; the live witness after the plane cut is Post-Merge Validation.
