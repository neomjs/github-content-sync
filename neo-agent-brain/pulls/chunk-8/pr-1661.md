Resolves #1658

Claude Desktop seats stop being frozen on the build they were installed with. The Fleet finds new Claude builds through Homebrew's public cask record, used only as a pointer: a candidate is downloaded only when its URL is Anthropic's versioned release zip for the record's own version and commit, and its bytes must match the record's SHA-256 before anything is extracted. Admission then proves the bundle as it always did (Team ID, codesign, Gatekeeper, probe). `fleet.harnessGenerations.claudeDesktop` defaults to `true`, and the host's updater policy decides which updater owns Claude: with the policy, seats start from the newest admitted generation and the Fleet acquires on its 6-hour pass; without it, seats start from `/Applications`, whose own updater keeps it current. The download half every acquisition needs lives once, in `acquireHarnessArchive.mjs`.

Evidence: L2 (unit specs with fetch, extract and admission seams; a read-only live check of the real cask record through the branch's code) → L4 required for AC-7 (an FM build carrying this, a real download, a restarted seat). Residual: AC-7, Residual-Owner: #1531.

## Deltas from ticket

- The ticket first named `claudeDesktopUpdate.mjs` a third sibling of `antigravityUpdate.mjs` and `codexDesktopUpdate.mjs`. Both already carry their own copies of the vendor-independent half (run chain, rejection memo, streaming digest check, `ditto`, the single `.app`, even `compareDottedVersions`), so a third copy failed the maintainer test. That half is `acquireHarnessArchive.mjs` now, #1660 deletes the two older copies, and the ticket's Fix is amended to match.
- `FleetLifecycleService#assertHarnessHostGate` is gone. The host gate is part of the opt-in read (`harnessGenerationOwnership`), so a Start, the acquisition job and the model catalog read one answer, and the acquisition context carries the gate's `reason`.
- `seatJourney.spec.mjs` pins `NEO_FLEET_HARNESS_GENERATIONS_CLAUDE_DESKTOP=false` for its fleet server, as it already pins the Codex binary: on a host with the policy, the default would otherwise move its stand-in onto generations.
