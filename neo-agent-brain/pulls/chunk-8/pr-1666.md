Resolves #1665

The Fleet stops keeping every vendor build forever. After each family's acquisition pass, `retireHarnessGenerations` removes every admitted copy except the selected generation, its rollback and the ones a seat holds: every seat record's pin and every lease of the family's seats on disk (ADR 0034 §2.5 item 5). A lease that cannot be read keeps everything and names the seat. A retired copy's ledger entry stays, stamped `retiredAt`, so the build is never selected or fetched again; admitting it again clears the stamp. Retirement runs on the family's admission chain, so no admission lands between its check and a removal.

Stacked on #1664 (and so on #1661): it extends `wireFleetHarnessAcquisition.mjs` and `FleetLifecycleService.mjs` as those PRs change them, and it retargets as they merge.

Evidence: L2 (unit specs over real store copies) → L4 required for AC-6 (an FM build carrying this, its first pass on the live Antigravity store). Residual: AC-6, Residual-Owner: #1531.

## Deltas from ticket

- A family whose acquisition fails (a feed that is down) still retires: what the store holds does not depend on the feed.
- `materializeHarnessGeneration` clears `retiredAt` when it restores a retired build's copy. Without that, a build admitted again would read as retired with its copy present.
