Survivor validation now proves every surviving seat at once and applies each seat's outcome as it settles. The survivor stage costs its slowest seat instead of the sum, so one slow credential proof no longer runs out the 8 s budget and leaves every seat stale, as it did at 2026-11-12T17:46Z. ## Deltas from ticket
- Two tests pinned the serial order on purpose, and concurrency removes it. "real pins seal by their record…" now reads its two witness lines order-free. "one seat whose Neural Link proof fails…" now asserts on whichever seat's connection the fixture Bridge refused.
