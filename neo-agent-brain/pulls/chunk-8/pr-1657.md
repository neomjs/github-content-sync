Resolves #1653

🌿 The rotation stops handing work to a seat that can only wait for its reset.

The open-work rotation now passes over a seat whose plan is exhausted, until the instant its full binding window resets, the way it already passes over an unreachable seat. That instant comes from the latest `resetsAt` among the seat's full binding windows, read from the same verbose presence answer as the route map and with its pool's newest windows. A full window without a `resetsAt`, a stale reading or an unobserved one starts no wait. The roster and its hash offset stay whole, so a waiting seat hands its pick to its successor. Work a seat holds as reviewer or author, and pending merges, still route through `routeOf` alone. Participation is never written.

Evidence: L2 (unit arms with a clock against the real planner, the capacity reader, and the wired round fed one injected presence answer) → L3 required (AC-4: a rotation wake on the plane after a cut). Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: #1508.
