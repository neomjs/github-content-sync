Resolves #1642

An outside contributor's PR pushed past a seat's review now wakes that seat for Round 2 when its new head goes green, instead of waking a rotation seat with `triage-outside`. `holderOf` stops reading an outside PR as untouched once a native seat has reviewed an earlier head. A red new head stays the author's, and a pending one waits. A standing change request or approval outranks an earlier comment, also after its seat has reviewed the current head, and a seat's change request joins any review a maintainer requested. A seat that has reviewed the current head holds nothing until the next push. A login without a seat, a bot's review, run approval and a seat's own PR keep their routes.

Evidence: L2 (pure holder rules and a planner journey) → L2 required (AC-1 to AC-3 are unit ACs). Residual: AC-4, Residual-Owner: #759.
