Resolves #1678

A failed open-work wake send is now retried on later rounds. The plane answers a repeated admission of one wake with its first receipt, so a retry after a lost answer still wakes its holder only once.

- **Plane.** `admitOpenWorkWake` keeps a bounded record of admissions by task id. A repeat answers the first receipt, even one arriving while the first write is still in flight. A failed write keeps nothing, so its retry writes. The record lives in the plane's process and holds 2,000 admissions.
- **Host.** A send that throws re-arms its slot as `pending` with an `attempts` count, and the next round submits it again, up to five attempts. A refusal from the plane is never retried. A failed merge wake returns to the pending merges, and a rotation re-pick keeps its slot's count.

Evidence: L1 (unit) → L1 required (#1678's AC-1 to AC-3 are unit). Residual: AC-4, the host log after the plane cut and the install, Residual-Owner: #759.
