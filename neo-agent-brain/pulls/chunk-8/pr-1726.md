Resolves #1722

A sunset's rotation now waits only on the turns that could have written its receipt. `readSunsetReceipt` used to take the latest expiry over every active turn-presence interval begun by the receipt's time. A turn that ends without `add_memory` stays active for its whole TTL, so it held the rotation after the sunset turn had closed. Now an interval begun by the receipt's time counts only if it progressed at or after the receipt, provided any did. An older interval that went quiet before the receipt holds nothing. Without such progress, every interval begun by the receipt's time counts, as before.

Evidence: L2 (the lane over an in-memory graph, plus the real `TurnPresenceService` writer and `add_memory`) → L4 required for AC-3 (a live Claude sunset; it can only run after the cut). Residual: AC-3, Residual-Owner: #1373

## Evolution
The first head took the newest interval started by the receipt's time as its writer. That holds for one session, but a seat can run two sessions, and a later turn of the other session that closed before the receipt then released the rotation while the writer was still active. Progress since the receipt is evidence the interval was alive when the receipt was written. Its absence keeps the old conservative read.
