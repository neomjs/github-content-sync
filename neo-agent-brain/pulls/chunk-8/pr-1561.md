Resolves #1559

Removes the duplicate `requestPublish` (`$ref: '#/components/schemas/RequestPublishPayload'`) advertisement and output-only `revision` (`$ref: '#/components/schemas/RequestPublishRevision'`) and `publication` (`$ref: '#/components/schemas/RequestPublishPublicationState'`) declarations from `add_message`'s input `properties.task.properties` in `ai/mcp/server/memory-core/openapi.yaml`, while preserving top-level `properties.requestPublish` (`line 2620`), runtime acceptance of `task.requestPublish` under `additionalProperties: true` (`MailboxService.mjs:3415-3425`, where top-level `requestPublish` wins when both are supplied), and `'200'` response `properties.{revision, publication}` (`lines 2686–2689`). Passing `task.revision` or `task.publication` to `MailboxService#addMessage` remains refused at runtime (`MailboxService.mjs:144,3416-3421`, `refused: unknown-field`). Removing those three nested `$ref`s under `task.properties` reduces `add_message.inputSchema` from `5,718 B` to `1,830 B` (`-3,888 B` measured via `openApiValidator` / `SwaggerParser` + `stripSchemaDescriptions` on `memory-core/openapi.yaml`; `3,646 B -> 1,724 B` in Antigravity's projected `add_message.json`; and `14,471 B -> 6,657 B` / `-7.8 KB` on the described schema in `get_mcp_tool_handbook`). Graduated from `neomjs/neo` Discussion `#20680` (Track 2 — Option G).

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit specs exercising `memory-core/openapi.yaml` schema structure, `ToolService` compact/handbook schema projection, and `MailboxService.addMessage` runtime `refused: unknown-field` enforcement) → L2 required (AC-1, AC-2, AC-3).
