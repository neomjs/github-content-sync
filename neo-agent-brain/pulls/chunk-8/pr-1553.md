Resolves #1552

A Start refused before spawn, such as a Codex Desktop Start whose capability probe refuses the installed build, leaves a record with no process and no `runtimeStart`. `handoffRuntimeCustody` read that record as a legacy seat and refused the whole continuity handoff. The 2026-11-09 FM update therefore exited unclean, and the running seats recovered `stale`.

`verifySeats` now passes over a record that claims no process: no pid, no child handle, not adopted, and no unresolved ownership or cleanup. A seat that does claim a process without a generation-bound Start still refuses, as #1166 pinned.

Evidence: L2 (CI: unit specs drive the real `publishUnavailable` writer and the real handoff) → L3 required (AC-3: an installed FM update). Residual: AC-3, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#732.
