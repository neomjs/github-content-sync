Resolves #1713

A named `progress` or `terminal` on a closed turn interval is now the documented no-op. The `turnId` the Claude PostToolUse hook has sent since #1686 therefore no longer re-opens the turn that `add_memory` just closed. A named progress past `expiresAt` still joins its own turn. With this, the rotation's live-turn guard releases at the sunset turn's `add_memory` instead of an hour later.

Evidence: L2 (the real `start → add_memory → named progress` sequence and `readSunsetReceipt`, both on the spec's graph store) → L3 required for AC-5 (a live census and the next Claude sunset on the cut plane). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: #1712.

Prescription checked: `ai/services/memory-core/TurnPresenceService.mjs` owns the concern. Its JSDoc states the no-op contract, and the hook and the rotation reader are its consumers.
