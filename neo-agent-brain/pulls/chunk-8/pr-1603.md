Resolves #1601

Projects the three Brain-owned non-secret token-replacement fields onto the Fleet wire so neomjs/neo-agent-institution#902 can render stopped-seat token replacement without touching credential bytes or decrypting stores on roster polls: `FleetControlBridge#getAgent(id)` adds `planeCredential` (`'none' | 'same-as-forge' | 'distinct' | 'unreadable'`, read at the bound-plane endpoint shared with `FleetManager#replaceSeatCredential` and compared inside Brain over SHA-256 digests via `crypto.timingSafeEqual` while `listAgents` stays registry-only), `wireFleetOpenWorkSource` parses `GitHub-Authentication-Token-Expiration` (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS UTC`, `±HHMM`, or ISO-8601 UTC accepted defensively, `null` for anything else) into ISO-8601 UTC without date guessing, and `openWorkProducer` + `projectOpenWork` retain and project frozen `credentialRefusal` and `tokenExpiresAt` on `fleetOpenWork.seats[id]` even when a seat holds zero open pull-request rows.

Related: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#902

Evidence: L2 (encrypted fixture-store wire dispatch + GraphQL header/refusal producer unit suites) → L2 required (AC-1..AC-4 unit, AC-5 contract-ledger review). No residuals.
