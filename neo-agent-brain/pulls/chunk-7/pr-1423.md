Resolves #1422

Fixes two unit-test harness defects in `neomjs/neo-agent-brain`:
1. `test/playwright/unit/ai/services/knowledge-base/DatabaseService.sync.spec.mjs` (`checkoutRevision`): resolves `git -C root rev-parse HEAD` before falling back to `process.env.GITHUB_SHA`, matching `resolveCleanBrainCheckoutRevision` in `imageRevisionReader.mjs:34-67` so `.github/workflows/brain-unit.yml` `suite (base)` (`git checkout --detach HEAD^1`) stops failing with `KB_IMAGE_READER_BRAIN_IDENTITY_INVALID`.
2. `test/playwright/unit/ai/daemons/wake/receiver.spec.mjs`: separates the `stepBoundsMs` ceiling for real `fsync`-backed steps (`accept: 2000, reload: 2000, drain: 2000`) from `dispatch: 250` (with `accept: 250` passed only to the mocked `accept: never` arm) and adds a `300ms` `state.transition` delay on the stuck-`dispatch` arm so Linux `ext4` `fsync()` stalls during `'drain'` cannot preempt `'dispatch'`.

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit suite execution in sandbox) → L2 required (AC-1 and AC-2 are unit-test assertions verified in CI).
