🌿 Unsayable after this: a scheduled lane that runs on every poll and leaves no trace.

The data-integrity sweep gets the task-state envelope its three in-process siblings already have. That is one inert `buildTaskDefinitions` entry, with no `pidFileName` file and no spawn.
