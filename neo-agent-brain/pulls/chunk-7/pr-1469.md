Resolves #1468

The roster, the cockpit's first read, no longer waits out a slow plane. In plane mode its wake and presence axes are each a plane call that only the MCP SDK's 60 s bounds, so a loaded plane after a relaunch held the whole answer past the cockpit's 10 s budget (neomjs/neo-agent-institution#841). `FleetManager`'s two roster producers now bound their plane reads by the new `AiConfig.fleet.rosterAxisTimeoutMs` leaf (4 s). An axis still reading reports pending through its adapter's own fail-honest path while the call runs on, and a call that settles after the bound logs its axis and duration. The plane `who_is_online` reader shares one call between overlapping reads.

Evidence: L2 (unit: the producers, the roster DTO and the reader on the Brain's unit setup) → L4 required (AC-5: an installed cold relaunch). Residual: AC-5, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#841.

## Deltas from ticket

- The bound sits in `FleetManager.fleetWakeStatus()` and `fleetPresenceStatus()`, not in `fleetRoster` itself. Only `fleetRoster` calls them, and they hold the adapter options a pending snapshot needs. `seatParticipation`, which gates Start, reads presence directly and stays unbounded.
- The bound is an AiConfig leaf (ADR 0019 §2: a declared literal is AiConfig's), read at the use site as each producer's `timeoutMs` default. Specs pass a 50 ms scalar to the operation (§5.4 rung iii) and never write AiConfig.
- The wake capability keeps its adapter's aggregate reason ("subscription reader failed for 1 agent(s)"). Its rows name the wait.
- Intake: self-authored this session. Prescription checked: `ai/services/fleet/FleetManager.mjs` owns the concern. The plane client keeps its 60 s, as the ticket's Out of Scope says: a shorter per-call bound would fail reads that answer at 17–25 s under load.
