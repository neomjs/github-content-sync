Resolves #1519

🌿 A release that ships on a weekend no longer waits until Monday to reach the Brain.

`.github/dependabot.yml` moves both ecosystems, npm and github-actions, from `interval: daily` (Monday to Friday) to `interval: cron` with `cronjob: "30 4 * * *"`. That runs every day at 04:30 UTC, the hour the scheduled job already used, so the organization's Dependabot runs stay staggered on the shared runners. A two-line header note says why. Majors, cooldown (`neo-agent-skills` excluded) and grouping are unchanged. It's the twin of neomjs/neo#20607, which opened three Dependabot PRs within two minutes of merging.
