Resolves #312

Exposes the already-computed hybrid re-ranker sort key (`compositeScore`, rounded to 6 decimals) on every re-ranked row of `query_raw_memories` (`detail: 'full'` and `detail: 'summary'`) and `query_summaries` across all downstream filters (`live` tombstones, `filteredIndices`, fast exit, and `needsPostFilter`), and surfaces `reRanked: Boolean(searchResult._reRanked)` on both response envelopes (flat and `conceptWalk: true`) while keeping `relevanceScore` and `ContextAssembler` output unchanged.

Evidence: L1 (unit specs in `StorageRouterDegraded.spec.mjs` exercising `StorageRouter.injectQueryReRanker`, `MemoryService.queryMemories`, `SummaryService.querySummaries`, `ContextAssembler.prototype.formatSummaryContext`, and `openapi.yaml`) → L1 required (AC-1 through AC-6).
