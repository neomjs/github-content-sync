Resolves #1350

🌿 *A corpus whose head has not moved can no longer pay for a full replay.*

`buildIngestEnvelope` gave every non-delta-safe profile a full materialization on any linear advance, including one that changed no file. The corpus tenant's two extractors, `ConversationCorpusSource` and `ReleaseNotesCorpusSource`, are both non-delta-safe, so `github-content-sync` re-ingested its whole corpus every ~31 min at an unchanged head. Such a profile now goes full only when the diff is non-empty. An empty advance builds the zero-delta incremental envelope that delta-safe tenants already build, and `selectAssignments` hands a non-delta-safe extractor no assignments for it. Identity changes and failed or pending checkpoints still reach the builder with a null base (`requiresTenantRepoCheckpointRevalidation`) and replay in full.

Evidence: L2 (unit spec, a mutation run, and the real builder on the real corpus) → L3 required only for the plane readings under Post-Merge Validation (a plane running this head).
