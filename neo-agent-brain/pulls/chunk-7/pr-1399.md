Resolves #1385

Supersedes an open backup retry streak in the deployment-state snapshot projection (`doesReceiptSupersedeBackupStreak` / `reconcileBackupRetryStateWithReceipt` in `ai/daemons/orchestrator/scheduling/backup.mjs`, applied in `DeploymentStateBridgeService#collectMaintenanceSnapshot`) when a fresh positive-evidence restorable receipt (`backup.status === 'success'`, `integrity.restorable === true`, `offHostSync.status` in `['success', 'disabled']`, `durability.posture !== 'unmet'`, `!durability.configErrorCode`) completed after `retryState.streakStartedAtMs` and within the overdue bound (`now - finishedAt <= backupIntervalMs + Math.max(0, retryWindowMs)`). Projects `retry.phase: 'healthy'` with `ledgerPhase`, `supersededBy: {bundleName, finishedAt}`, and the receipt's `lastSuccessAt` / `lastSuccessAgeMs`; `describeBackupMaintenanceHealth` reads that reconciled projection so `backup-retry-exhausted`, `backup-retry-open`, and `backup-success-overdue` clear without mutating `task-state.json` or duplicating the rule, while stale, non-qualifying, or conflicted receipts (`#17785`) remain degraded.

Evidence: L2 (Playwright unit specs covering retry projection, overdue age bound, health reason codes, bridge maintenance snapshot, and Fleet System/Tasks projections) → L4 required (AC-3 installed FM backup lane line after plane cut). Residual: AC-3, Residual-Owner: neomjs/neo-agent-institution#516.
