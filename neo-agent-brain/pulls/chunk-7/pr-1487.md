Resolves #1485

🌿 *A plane can no longer count our team as its own: its candidates are the agents its nodes name.*

Four readers took "who exists" from `identityRoots.IDENTITIES`. Each now reads the plane's AgentIdentity nodes, through two helpers in `ai/graph/agentIdentityParticipation.mjs`:
- `agentIdentitiesOf()` returns the nodes whose `accountType` is `agent`.
- `memberLoginsOf()` returns the logins of the agents and people.

A node that records no account type is not a member. Our own plane holds three such nodes: `@agent-a`, `@agent-b` and `@neo-test-writer` (defect-note b75b1883).

- **Heartbeat `active-local-team`**, which is also `checkAllAgentIdle`'s default team, takes the plane's agents from an injected `teamProvider`.
- **Stale-assignment maintainers** come from the nodes. `buildStaleAssignmentCandidates` reads the plane when no maintainers are passed, the same way its stall-inference sibling does.
- **IssueIngestor** reads its internal authors once per ingest run, not at import.
- **The open-work seat lookup** resolves author lines and logins through the plane's `who_is_online` rows. It reuses the pulse's one presence read.

Evidence: L1 (unit arms, each with nodes that are on no roster of ours) → L1 required for AC-1 and AC-2. AC-3 needs our live plane, so it runs post-merge.
