Resolves #1445

🌿 *After this, recall cannot find a message that `get_message` would refuse to open.*

`query_messages` answers a free-text query with the A2A messages the caller may open, nearest first.
- **One admission rule.** `canReadMessage` moves out of `getMessage` into `helpers/messageAdmission.mjs`, and `admissionWhere` is its Chroma set form.
- **Filter, then re-check.** The index is pre-filtered with `admissionWhere`, so the top-k holds admitted rows only. Each hit is then re-checked with `canReadMessage` against its graph.
- **Graph content.** Content comes from the graph, so a retracted message answers with its placeholder.
- **Mapper.** It now stores canonical identities and counts a known broadcast's audience. The count is what admits a known-zero broadcast to everyone, as `getMessage` does.

Related: #35 (parent), #1439 (the index writer), #1440 (history backfill).

Evidence: L3 (unit specs at `f9934b74`, plus the AC-3 falsifier against a throwaway Chroma 1.5.9 with real qwen3 embeddings) → L3 required (AC-3 names a real Chroma). No residuals.

  - Unfiltered, foreign rows fill the top 5 for 10 of 10 phrasings.
  - With `admissionWhere('@bob')`, every phrasing returns exactly bob's five admitted rows (direct, delivered broadcast, legacy, known-zero, own send) and no foreign one.

- **Challenge**: Two non-blocking boundary invariants worth recording for downstream consumers (`#1440` backfill and future maintenance):
