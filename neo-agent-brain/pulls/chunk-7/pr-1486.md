Resolves #1484

🌿 *A fresh plane can no longer boot holding our team: who exists there arrives with whoever signs in.*

The boot seed now holds two anchors and two roots, the ones every plane's mailbox needs: `@system` and `AGENT:*`. Both are declared once, in `ai/graph/mailboxRoots.mjs`.

Boot provisions no team identity:
- Our team's plane gets its roster from `seedAgentIdentities.mjs`.
- Another operator's agents get their nodes at their first authenticated request.

The trust taxonomy moves to `ai/graph/trustTiers.mjs`, so the new module needs nothing from the roster. The roster re-exports the taxonomy and lists the same two root objects. The roster generator now anchors on `BROADCAST_SENTINEL`.

`GraphService.provisionMissingIdentityRoots()` had no runtime caller, so it is deleted, together with GraphService's roster import.

Evidence: L1 (unit arms on a fresh graph, red on dev's code) → L1 required for AC-1 to AC-3. AC-4 needs our live plane, so it runs post-merge.

## Deltas from ticket
- The ticket was re-scoped in place before this PR: the four candidate readers moved to #1485.
- `GRAPH_BOOT_SEED_VERSION` goes from 1 to 2. A restore target booted empty under v1 still holds our roster, so the freshness proof now refuses it and names v2 as the manifest version it compared against. Booting it again does not help: boot only adds missing seeds and never removes the roster, so a restore needs a new empty target initialized under v2. A populated graph is never cleared to pass the proof.
