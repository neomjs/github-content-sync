Resolves #1382

🌿 *A passing canary can no longer buy a full re-materialization every sweep.*

A recovery generation that deferred again folded back into its episode under a new consumption key, so the next sweep probed afresh, got a healthy canary and minted another generation. On the canonical plane that was a full 56,730-file materialization every ~2 min with `embeddings=0`, checkpoint held. The awaiting-recovery cohort now admits only episodes that have not consumed a generation: an episode gets one bypass, and after it repeats its outcome the ordinary backoff alone retries the repo. The canary and the full-band proof stop for it too.

Evidence: L2 (unit specs, red on `dev` first) → L3 required for AC-2 only (a plane running this head). Residual: AC-2, Residual-Owner: #64.

## Deltas from ticket
- One bypass per episode rather than one per backoff interval. The ordinary cadence is due at exactly the moment a re-opened bypass would be, so retry timing is the same, and no canary or proof work is spent after the first failed generation.
- The #16692 spec pinned the old re-arm ("consumption history rotates the gate key immediately"). It now pins the spent bypass and the ordinary retry. The crashed-retry spec now reads `still-failing` instead of `recovery-probe-backoff`, because no canary runs for a spent episode.
- The probe key loses its `lastConsumedGenerationId || 'initial'` branch, which can no longer be reached.
- The repository's block-alignment fixer realigned two untouched blocks in the file.
- #1382 was split: fix 2 is deferred with evidence, and fix 3 moved to #1403.
